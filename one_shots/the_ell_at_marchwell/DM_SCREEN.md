## SIX RULES

1. **Most rooms mean nothing.** Describe beautifully, never mention again.
2. **Horror is arithmetic, not gore.**
3. **Printed stat blocks, run as written.** Quirks never touch numbers.
4. **Theatre-of-mind.** Battle maps for fights only.
5. **Never say "it doesn't work."** Every spell functions — just *here*.
6. **Time is soft.** Track rests, not hours.

**Chain-click** = a soft double click, like a chain taken up. Every fold.
Never a warning; always a receipt.

---

## THE FOLD RULE

**Identical things are one thing with several mouths.**

| Set size | Result |
|---|---|
| **2 identical doorways** | Deterministic. In one, out the other. Reliable. |
| **3+** | Random — roll among the other mouths. Unpredictable to *everyone*, including the Ell. |

- **Deface** a frame → removes it from the set (5-set → 2-set = shortcut)
- **Copy** a mark → adds to a set (deliberate long-distance link)
- **Unique room** → in no set → **rest-safe**
- **Hesper only folds through 2-sets.** That's why she's never where you want.

## RESTING

**Long rest requires a room the Ell can tell apart.** Otherwise: fold at the
edge of sleep, wake scattered, short-rest benefit only.

Qualifies if: **they cut a mark** (chalk doesn't work — it gets copied) ·
**one-of-a-kind object in it** (anchor, gallows, well, milestone) · **someone
else already marked it** (Ambry's squat, Hesper's camps).

**Table ruling, said once at the first rest:**
> *"I won't tell you in advance whether a rest will be long or short. It isn't
> random — it depends on something — and there may be a cost either way."*

**SAFETY VALVE:** after **2 failed long rests**, they find a century-old camp
with a name knife-cut into the doorframe. Don't explain it. (Ambry backs it up
if needed: *"sleep where somebody's cut something."*)

### Rest cost

| Rest # | What turns up later |
|---|---|
| 1st | A copy of something they own — *theirs*, down to the repair |
| 2nd | A voice: whoever slept deepest, saying what they *would* say |
| 3rd | A copy of a PC, doing a routine, calm, not hostile, nothing inside it |
| 4th+ | Combinations. They should be afraid to sleep. |

*Past cap: +15 HP to Hesper each.*

## THE FINALE

**Watched Door:** the exit exists only while **2+ conscious creatures can see
it.** Watching is **passive** — never make players spend actions staring.
Vanishes = unbroken plaster. Anyone in the doorway is shunted back, prone,
never killed.

**Hesper's plan:** she can't out-damage six PCs and doesn't try. **She only has
to stop the last one.** She breaks sightlines — folds into them, *Close The
Way* (bonus action, un-doors a route they're using), copies body-block.

**The ratchet:** every PC who leaves weakens the ones still in. 6 is
comfortable; the 4th one out leaves two people holding her off — and two is
the minimum to keep the door existing at all.

**The answer (don't hand it over): a doorway with a body in it cannot
vanish.** Wedges/crowbars don't work — it must be *occupied*. Hesper
demonstrates it in round 1.

**Her Fold:** once/turn, no action, part of movement, **must enter and exit a
doorway.** Counter it by blocking a door, defacing a frame, or fighting her
away from the doors.

### Endings
- **Kill her, walk out.** She was never on the list. Costs them someone they liked.
- **Close the file** — answer *HOW BIG IS A HOME?* honestly, out loud. No roll, no Charisma check. Bad faith doesn't land. Ends the instrument everywhere; Hesper gets four seconds in an orchard, in wind.
- **The ugly one** — they don't count, and come back six but not the same six.

## MAGIC — quick answers

*Misty step* obeys the fold rule · *Locate object* points nine ways · *Sending*
answers **in your own voice** · *Speak with dead* gives courteous answers about
a house it never saw · *Detect magic* = one seamless aura, "conjuration the way
a map is conjuration" · *Augury* always weal **and** woe · *Dispel* has nothing
to dispel · digging = more plaster, then notation

## IF THEY ASK

**Who built it?** Nobody. A *surveyor*, not an architect. It measured and left.
**Do we meet it?** No. Nothing to fight. Gone longer than there are words for.
**Where's the machine?** There is none. **The Ell is the instrument.**
**How do we know it's real?** They watch it work 3×: the scratch cutting itself
(S1), their chalk copied (S2), the line drawn in the floor recording them (S5).
**The arrows and voices?** Them. It files every feature and reprints it. They've
been following themselves. The oblex was reading the same file.
**Why the gallows in the nursery?** No reason. Filed under *things in places*.
**Why can't Hesper leave?** Different house, different door, never on any list.
