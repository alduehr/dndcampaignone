# Highwater — Pacing Sheet

Non-canon, standalone — see [`../README.md`](../README.md) and
[`HIGHWATER.md`](HIGHWATER.md).

**This is the page you run from.** The adventure is a 3-hour real-time
countdown that should end *close*. This sheet gives you the clock rules,
the checkpoints, the announcements, every time cost in one place, the
levers that speed up or slow down the table, and the Gauge handout.

---

## 1. Setup (Before You Start The Clock)

- [ ] Phone or kitchen timer set to **3:00:00**, visible to the table.
- [ ] The **Gauge handout** (§2) printed or on a whiteboard, with room to
      write the **Penalty Tally**.
- [ ] Map handout: the **player-safe sketch** from `MAP_SPEC.md` §8.5.
- [ ] Your DM maps: Crest, Gallery, Sluice (`MAP_SPEC.md` §§2–4).
- [ ] Scaling row chosen (`PARTY_AND_LEVEL_SCALING.md`).
- [ ] Scratch paper for the **gate state**: Gate 1 ☐ Gate 3 ☐ Weir-Gate ☐
      Diversion Mouth clear ☐ main plug clear ☐ Gate 2 ☐, beacon ☐,
      stoplogs ☐, early-gates bonus ☐, Ivo's location ____.
- [ ] Run the **20-minute Lowmill setup** first. **Start the timer when the
      party reaches the road's end at the dam** (the Keeper's House door).

## 3. The Clock Rules (one paragraph each)

**Effective time.** Effective time left = timer − Penalty Tally. Marks,
checkpoints, and deadlines all use effective time. Check it whenever you add
to the tally; if a Mark is crossed, announce it at once.

**What goes on the tally.** Only time the fiction skips: rests, slow labor,
failed group-check rolls, wrong levers. Anything the table plays out live is
already paid for by the timer — never charge twice. The full list is §6.

**The deadline.** Gate 2 must be open with **0:10 or more** of effective time
left. **When Gate 2 opens, stop the timer** and write down the effective time.
The finale is played in rounds; the frozen reading decides the outcome and
tunes the finale (§5). **0:01–0:09** = the Notch tears (the dam holds but the
valley floods). **Hits 0:00** = the Breach (§8).

**Where the water goes.** When Gate 2 opens: Weir-Gate open *and* Diversion
Mouth clear → the channel (good ending). Otherwise, main plug clear → down the
valley (bad ending). **Neither exit clear → Water Hammer, the leaf jams, no
Draw-Down: don't stop the timer.** The party has 10 minutes of effective time
to open the channel (wheel + shutter; the pressure clears the mud, and
anyone in the Cache is swept); otherwise the plug blows down the valley on
its own. Stop the timer when either happens.

## 4. Run Order And Checkpoints

Targets are **effective time left** at average pace for four characters,
assuming they take one short rest somewhere and fail a few rolls. "Slack" is
how far off they can drift before you pull a lever (§5).

| # | Milestone | Target | Slack | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M0 | **Timer starts**; party at the road's end | **3:00** | — | Announce the clock; hand over the Gauge. |
| M1 | **E1 resolved**; Ivo found (or skipped); party inside | **2:40** | ±10 | If E1 is still running at 2:35, the crayfish break and dive. |
| M2 | **Control Gallery**; Flood Chart read; plan reformed | **2:30** | ±10 | *Mark 1.* Read the chart aloud, plainly. |
| M3 | **Gear Hall (E2)** resolved | **2:15** | ±10 | Talking is ~5 minutes; fighting ~12. |
| M4 | **Forge (E3)**; gear retrieved | **1:55** | ±10 | *Mark 2 at 2:00* (corridor collapse). |
| M5 | **Master Gear installed**; levers live | **1:45** | ±10 | Remind them (via Ivo or the Pressure Board) that Gates 1 and 3 can open now. |
| M6 | **Sluice Level**; Pressure Board read; heading for the Outfall | **1:35** | ±10 | *Mark 3 at 1:30* floods the Tailrace — this is the first real squeeze. |
| M7 | **Diversion Mouth cleared** (or Flush, or plan to dig from the Cache side) | **1:15** | ±10 | |
| M8 | **At the Old Overflow Door** | **1:05** | ±10 | *Mark 4 at 1:00.* |
| M9 | **Cache resolved (E6)** | **0:50** | ±10 | Talking ~8 minutes; fighting ~15. |
| M10 | **Great Wheel turned**; channel open | **0:40** | ±5 | *Mark 5 at 0:30.* Make sure nobody stays in the Cache. |
| M11 | **Levers pulled 1 → 3 → 2**; Gate 2 opens; **stop the timer** | **0:15–0:25** | ±5 | The frozen reading. **Deadline: 0:10.** |
| M12 | **Crest Run + Silt Elemental (E7)** | untimed | — | ~20–25 real minutes, played in rounds. |
| M13 | **Epilogue** | untimed | — | ~10 minutes. |

**Whole session:** ≈ 20 min setup + 2h 35m–3h of clock + ≈ 25 min finale +
10 min epilogue → **about 3 hours 45 minutes to 4 hours.** Book four hours.

**Rule of thumb:** a party at the Valve Hall with more than 1:40 left is
*ahead*. A party that hasn't reached the Old Overflow Door by 0:55 is
*behind*.

## 5. Elastic Dials (Make It Close)

### If the table is BEHIND (more than 10 min past a checkpoint)

Pull these, **in order**, until back on pace. Never announce that you're
helping.

1. **Cut a fight short.** Half the foes break at the end of round 2. E1: the
   crayfish dive. E2: Greaves bolts down the chute. E3: the smoke works at
   once.
2. **Hand over a clue.** Corrin Bale appears with the knock; Ivo remembers
   the stoplogs; the Alcove's draft is obvious.
3. **Drop a success requirement by one.** Great Wheel, gear install, mud.
   (Never touch DCs.)
4. **Point out a shortcut that costs HP.** The Spoil Chute, the East Face
   Ledge, the Flush.
5. **Varrow opens the door himself** and asks to talk.
6. **Ivo at the levers.** If he's in the Control Gallery, he pulls on the
   bell's signal — no walk back up.

### If the table is AHEAD (more than 10 min before a checkpoint)

Pull these, **in order**:

1. **Surge Crack.** An extra groan and a 2d6 hazard in the current room.
2. **Second wave.** Two more mere-claws climb the Crest, or two more quipper
   swarms rise in the Shaft.
3. **A second jam.** Gate 3's hoist is jammed: 2 successes (DC 14 Athletics)
   at the crank on the shelf; costs 5 minutes.
4. **Corrin Bale panics** and runs for the Alcove with the crew's purse,
   starting a chase through the Gate 2 Chamber.
5. **A false fit.** The 18-tooth gear seats on the first bolts and slips
   under load (DC 14 Intelligence to spot before trying it; otherwise a
   failed install).
6. **Early silt.** One silt-spawn climbs the Shaft and harasses the party in
   the Gallery.

### The finale dial (uses the frozen reading)

| Frozen reading | E7 |
|---|---|
| **0:25 or more** | Two extra silt-spawn climb the parapet on round 3. |
| **0:15–0:24** | As written. |
| **0:10–0:14** | The elemental starts at −30 HP. |
| **0:01–0:09** | As written — and the Notch tears at the end of round 8 (the valley floods). |

## 6. Every Time Cost In One Place

**Costs (add to the tally):**

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Short rest | **15** |
| Quick catch-breath (one Hit Die, average) | 2 |
| Taking Ivo along (first level change) | 5 |
| Gear install: each failed roll | 5 |
| Filing the 30-tooth gear to fit | 10 |
| Hand-cranking a gate | 10 (+5 per failed attempt) |
| Mud job: each failed roll | 5 |
| Forcing the Old Overflow Door: each attempt | 2 |
| Great Wheel: each failed roll | 3 |
| Great Wheel slipped (3 failures), reset | 5 |
| Levering the Diversion shutter by hand (cable cut) | 5 |
| Lowering the stoplogs | 5 (+5 per failed attempt) |
| Wrong gate order: Shudder | 10 |
| Wrong gate order: Water Hammer | 20 |
| Gate 3 hoist jam (ahead-dial) | 5 |

**Bonuses (subtract from the tally; the tally may go negative):**

| Action | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Stoplogs lowered | **−15** (once) |
| Gates 1 and 3 opened, in order, before Gate 2 | **−10** (once) |

**Free, but HP for time:**

| Route | Risk |
|---|---|
| Spoil Chute (G4 → S1) | DC 12 Acrobatics or 2d6; one-way |
| Shaft stair below the Gallery | Water weird (E4); a fall into the sump is 5d6 (2d6 after Mark 3) |
| East Face Ledge (fall from the parapet) | 3d6; lands at the East Stair |
| The Flush | DC 15 Str save or 3d10 for anyone in S3/S8; counts as Gate 2 for the Order Rule (smooth only if Gates 1 and 3 are running) |

**Spells that save time** (they remove a cost or a roll; they don't add
bonuses): Knock (the door), Fabricate (the gear), Mending (a cracked gear
only), Control Water (the Tailrace and the Outfall), Water Breathing
(underwater digging and swimming), Stone Shape / Move Earth / Transmute
Rock (the mud), Telekinesis (the wheel), Speak with Animals (E1, E3), Misty
Step / Fly / Spider Climb (shortcuts between levels).

## 7. Announcement Script

| Mark | Say |
|---|---|
| **Start** | *"The water is a hand's breadth under the lip. Odo said three hours. It's three hours."* |
| **1 (2:30)** | *"Somewhere below you, something heavy shifts. The air goes cold and wet. The bottom of the dam is taking water."* |
| **2 (2:00)** | *"A long, grinding groan. Dust sifts from the ceiling. Somewhere west, a section of the corridor lets go."* |
| **3 (1:30)** | *"The beck below the dam has risen over the tailrace mouth. You can hear the tunnel filling."* |
| **4 (1:00)** | *"Water is in the Valve Hall to the chest. The sump pulls like a drain. In the Cache, someone swears."* |
| **5 (0:30)** | *"Water comes over the Spill Notch in a smooth black sheet. The whole dam is humming."* |
| **6 (0:00)** | *"The crest cracks."* (See §8.) |

## 8. Failure Procedure (The Breach)

If effective time hits **0:00** and Gate 2 is not open:

1. **Say it plainly, then keep playing.** *"The crest goes."*
2. **Run five rounds of escape.** Each character on the Sluice Level needs
   two DC 13 Athletics checks to reach a stair; on the Gallery, one; on the
   Crest, none — they run for an abutment. A character who fails twice is
   swept out of the dam (6d10 bludgeoning, DC 15 Con save for half). If that
   drops them to 0, they don't make death saves: the river throws them up on
   the hillside below the dam, unconscious and stable at 0 HP, and the party
   finds them in the epilogue. Nobody dies to this roll; the cost is the
   town. The beacon can still be lit in these five rounds.
3. **Narrate the wave.** Lowmill is destroyed. If the beacon was lit, most of
   the people live; if not, few do.
4. **Epilogue.** Run the Breach outcome from the main file. This is an
   honorable loss.

## 9. End-Of-Session Checklist

- [ ] Stop the clock the moment Gate 2 opens (not when the fight ends).
- [ ] Read the outcome (main file, "Outcomes & Epilogue").
- [ ] Award the **level-up to 6** if the dam holds.
- [ ] Hand out rewards by outcome; scale the town's payment by party size.
- [ ] Note **Corrin Bale**'s fate and what was taken from the Cache.
