# Highwater — Pacing Sheet

Non-canon, standalone — see [`../README.md`](../README.md) and
[`HIGHWATER.md`](HIGHWATER.md).

**This is the page you run from.** The adventure is a 3-hour real-time
countdown that should end *close*. This sheet gives you the checkpoints, the
announcements, the levers that speed up or slow down the table, and the Gauge
handout.

---

## 1. Setup (Before You Start The Clock)

- [ ] Phone or kitchen timer set to **3:00:00**, visible to the table.
- [ ] The **Gauge handout** (§2) printed or on screen.
- [ ] Map handout: the **player-safe sketch** from `MAP_SPEC.md` §8.5.
- [ ] Your DM map: Crest, Gallery, Sluice (`MAP_SPEC.md` §§2–4).
- [ ] Scaling row chosen (`PARTY_AND_LEVEL_SCALING.md`).
- [ ] Sheet of scratch paper to track the **Order Rule** (which gates are open).
- [ ] Run the **20-minute Lowmill setup** first. **Start the timer when the
      party crosses onto the Crest.**

## 3. Run Order And Checkpoints

**Target time remaining** when each milestone should be reached at average
pace. The "slack" column is how far off the table can be before you pull a
lever (§5).

| # | Milestone | Target remaining | Slack | What to watch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M0 | **Timer starts**; party on the Crest Walk | **3:00** | — | Announce the clock; hand over the Gauge. |
| M1 | **E1 resolved**; party enters the dam | **2:45** | ±10 | If still on the Crest at 2:40, have the crayfish retreat to the water. |
| M2 | **Control Gallery reached**; Flood Chart read; plan reformed | **2:30** | ±10 | *Mark 1 hits here.* Read the chart aloud. |
| M3 | **Gear Hall (E2) resolved**; sabotage understood | **2:15** | ±10 | Negotiation shortens this. |
| M4 | **Forge reached (E3)**; gear retrieved | **1:55** | ±10 | *Mark 2 hits at 2:00* (corridor collapse). |
| M5 | **Master Gear installed**; levers live | **1:45** | ±10 | Every failed install roll costs 5 min. |
| M6 | **Valve Hall read**; gate order known; party at the Outfall | **1:30** | ±10 | *Mark 3 hits here.* |
| M7 | **Mud diversion mouth cleared** (or bypassed) | **1:10** | ±10 | Tailrace may be flooded by now. |
| M8 | **At the Old Overflow Door**; knock or break in | **1:00** | ±10 | *Mark 4 hits here.* |
| M9 | **Cache resolved (E6)**; Varrow dealt with | **0:45** | ±10 | Negotiation shortens this. |
| M10 | **Great Wheel turned**; channel open | **0:35** | ±5 | *Mark 5 at 0:30.* |
| M11 | **Levers pulled in order 1→3→2**; Gate 2 opens | **0:20** | ±5 | **True deadline: 0:10.** |
| M12 | **Crest Run + Silt Elemental (E7)** | **0:20 → 0:08** | — | Run ends when Draw-Down completes. |
| M13 | **Finish**; epilogue | **0:05–0:10** | — | Target: "close but not on top." |

**Rule of thumb:** if the party is at the Valve Hall (M6) with more than 1:30
left, they are *ahead*. If they haven't entered the Cache (M8) by 0:50, they
are *behind*.

## 4. Announcement Script (Read Aloud At Each Mark)

| Mark | Say |
|---|---|
| **Start** | *"The water is a hand's breadth under the lip. Odo said three hours. It's three hours."* |
| **1 (2:30)** | *"Somewhere below you, something heavy shifts. The air in the corridor goes cold and wet. The Sluice Level is taking water."* |
| **2 (2:00)** | *"A long, grinding groan. Dust sifts from the ceiling. Somewhere west, a section of the corridor lets go."* |
| **3 (1:30)** | *"The beck below the dam has risen over the tailrace mouth. You can hear the tunnel filling."* |
| **4 (1:00)** | *"Water is in the Valve Hall to the chest. The sump pulls like a drain."* |
| **5 (0:30)** | *"Water comes over the Spill Notch in a smooth black sheet. The whole dam is humming."* |
| **6 (0:00)** | *"The crest cracks."* (See Failure.) |

If the table is slow, announce the Mark *before* they ask. If the table is
fast, hold the announcement until the party acts.

## 5. Elastic Dials (Make It Close)

### If the table is BEHIND (more than 10 min past a checkpoint)

Pull these, **in order**, until back on pace:

1. **Cut a fight.** E1: half the crayfish retreat. E2: Greaves breaks at
   round 1 and the crew flees. E3: only 2 ore-borers.
2. **Hand over a clue.** Ivo's log, a draft from the Alcove, or Corrin Bale
   appears and whispers the knock.
3. **Collapse a DC.** Great Wheel: −1 success. Gear install: −1 success.
   Mud: the small job is automatic.
4. **Offer a shortcut that costs HP.** The Spoil Chute (2d6), the East Face
   Ledge (3d6), the Shaft stair (past the weird).
5. **Varrow surrenders on sight** if the party shows the Flood Chart.
6. **Compress the Run.** Skip two obstacles; they reach the Crest.

### If the table is AHEAD (more than 10 min before a checkpoint)

Pull these, **in order**:

1. **Surge Crack.** An extra groan + 2d6 hazard in the current room.
2. **Second Wave.** A second wave of crayfish at the Crest, or two extra
   quipper swarms at the Shaft.
3. **A second jam.** Gate 3's hoist is jammed too: 2 successes to free it
   (DC 14 Athletics), 5 minutes.
4. **Corrin Bale panics** and tries to flee with the Cache key; a chase.
5. **A false lead.** The 18-tooth gear fits the first bolts; the table must
   realize it will slip under load (DC 14 Intelligence).
6. **Early silt-spawn.** Send 1–2 silt-spawn up the Central Shaft to harass
   the Run.

### The "close" finish — the Crest Run dial

The Crest Run is the **elastic tail**. If the party opens Gate 2 with:

| Gate 2 opens at | Crest Run behavior |
|---|---|
| **> 0:25** | Add the second silt wave (2 silt-spawn at round 3); run a full 8 rounds. |
| **0:20 – 0:25** | Run as written. |
| **0:10 – 0:20** | The elemental arrives already weakened (−20 HP). |
| **< 0:10** | The Draw-Down isn't finished. The dam fails at 0:00 *unless* the party bluffs an extra margin: the Danger Line is a visual mark, and the table can try a final desperate stunt (see Failure). |

## 6. Time Trades (What Costs What)

Use this table whenever the table asks "can I do X to save time?"

| Action | Saves | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| **Spoil Chute** (G4 → S1) | ~4 min | DC 12 Acrobatics or 2d6; one-way |
| **Central Shaft stair** | ~5 min | Water weird encounter (E4); falls 4d6 |
| **East Face Ledge** | ~3 min | 3d6 fall; arrives at the East Stair |
| **Seepage Crawl** | ~2 min | Heavy armor disadvantage; drips |
| **Service Corridor** | ~2 min | Dark, cramped |
| **Culvert Ladder** | ~3 min | 3 min climb; the only dry route after Mark 4 |
| **Knock** (spell) | 8–10 min | Opens the Old Overflow Door at once |
| **Fabricate / Make Whole** | ~10 min | Replaces the broken Master Gear |
| **Mending** | ~5 min | Only on a cracked gear (not shattered) |
| **Control Water** | 10–15 min | Clears the Outfall and the tailrace |
| **Water Breathing** | ~8 min | Swim the flooded tailrace; no checks |
| **Stone Shape / Move Earth** | ~10 min | Clears the mud |
| **Telekinesis / Gust of Wind** | ~5 min | Turns the Great Wheel |
| **Short rest** | — | **−15 min** |
| **Hand-crank a gate** | — | **−10 min per gate** |
| **Wrong gate order** | — | −10 min (Shudder) or −20 min (Water hammer) |
| **Failed install roll** | — | −5 min |
| **Failed mud-dig roll** | — | −5 min |
| **Failed Wheel roll** | — | −3 min |
| **Persuade Varrow** | ~10 min | None; he helps with the wheel |

## 7. Failure Procedure (The Breach)

If the timer hits **0:00** and Gate 2 is not yet fully open and the Draw-Down
has not had its 10 minutes:

1. **Say it plainly, then keep playing.** *"The crest goes."*
2. **Give the party 5 minutes** (a "last stand") to try a desperate stunt:
   light the beacon, hold the Winch House door, or evacuate the crest.
3. **Narrate the wave.** Lowmill is destroyed unless the beacon was lit
   (then the low town is lost but most of the people live).
4. **Epilogue.** Run the Breach outcome from the main file. This is an
   *honorable loss*.

## 8. End-Of-Session Checklist

- [ ] Stop the clock the moment the dam is safe.
- [ ] Read the outcome (main file, "Outcomes & Epilogue").
- [ ] Award the **level-up to 6** if the dam holds.
- [ ] Hand out rewards, including Maren's reward by outcome.
- [ ] Note **Corrin Bale**'s fate (promise kept or not).
- [ ] Note what the party took from the Cache.
