## AI Use

Load when anyone travels north out of the fens into the broken highland above the Sundering Reach. This file covers the Heights as a **physical place**: its geology, its named ruins and workings, its hazards with DCs, and what lives up there. The mining town of Harrowgast is the staging point for everything here. Usable for any story; nothing here depends on a particular plot.

## One-Sentence Identity

A cold, bare-boned highland of split ridges, roofless Concord ruins and abandoned mine-adits, where the weather does most of the killing and the nearest roof is a day's walk downhill.

## What A Traveler Notices First

The fens fall away below and the world goes grey and hard — scree, heather, wind, and the great cleft ridges that gave the whole region its name, cliffs split as though by one ancient blow. Concord ruins stand far more *intact* here than in the lowlands, because the highland never drowned: roofless halls, snapped aqueducts, and the black mouths of adits punched into every hillside. It is colder, clearer, and lonelier than the fens, and the silence up here has weight to it.

## Terrain

Old, hard mountain country worn down to bare bone: heather moor at the lower elevations giving way to scree slopes, exposed rock shelf, and cliff. Water runs fast and clear here rather than black and slow. Snow lies in the high gullies well into summer and returns early.

The ground is honest — you can see where you are going — but it is unforgiving. Scree moves underfoot. Frost has worked every ruin and gallery loose over a century of freeze and thaw, so old stonework and old timber alike cannot be trusted with weight. The mine-workings are extensive, largely unmapped, and open onto the surface in places with no warning at all.

**Getting through it:** the Heights Road climbs from the fens and degrades into a rough track; beyond that, travel is by shepherds' paths, miners' trods, and cairn-lines. Distances look shorter than they walk. The real requirement is not a guide but **equipment** — cold-weather gear, rope, and light — all of which Harrowgast sells at highland prices, and which every local will tell a stranger to buy before setting out.

## Named Features

- **The Sundered Ridge** — the signature landmark: a ridge cleft clean to its root. From the lip, on a clear day, you can see the whole Reach laid out below, down to the flooded basin gleaming at its center. The single best orientation point in the north.
- **The Roofless Hall** — the most intact Concord ruin anywhere in the region: a great hall standing open to the sky, its walls dense with old carved inscription. Scholars come a long way to copy those walls. The lingering dead are common inside it.
- **The Abandoned Adits** — the worked upper tunnels above Harrowgast, where salvage is still dug out of the spoil-heaps and the old galleries. Rockfall country, and contested by rival diggers.
- **The Whisper Scree** — a long scree slope where wind moving through the loose rock makes a sound uncomfortably like talking. Crossing it unsettles almost everyone; it is otherwise simply a bad slope.
- **The Snapped Aqueduct** — a broken Concord aqueduct spanning a gorge. Crossing the standing section saves the better part of a day and is a genuine climb. Cold on the span, even in sun.
- **The Cold Cairns** — highland cairns of Concord make, exposed on the open tops and thoroughly looted. Carved inside; a favorite of scholars, and a favorite ambush spot for whoever is watching the scholars.

## Hazards

| Hazard | Check | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold and exposure** | DC 13 Constitution save after a day in the open without proper highland gear; DC 15 for a night | Failure: 1 level of exhaustion, and it compounds fast. Telegraphed hard in Harrowgast, where everyone sells gear and tells you to buy it. |
| **Rockfall / unstable ruin** | DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) to spot the bad ground first; DC 13 Dexterity save to avoid | Failure: 2d6 bludgeoning, and possibly a blocked gallery behind you. Worst in the adits and inside frost-worked ruins. |
| **Scree slip** | DC 13 Strength (Athletics) to cross a moving slope | Failure: a slide of 20–40 ft., 1d6 damage and scattered gear. Descending scree is worse than climbing it. |
| **Climbing (aqueduct span, cliff, adit shaft)** | DC 13–15 Strength (Athletics) | Failure: a slip, with fall damage scaled to the height. Handholds and exposure are always described before the roll. |
| **Bad air (deep adits)** | DC 12 Constitution save on entering a still gallery | Failure: dizziness and disadvantage on checks until fresh air. Telegraphed by a guttering flame — miners carry a candle for exactly this. |
| **Whitening weather** | DC 14 Wisdom (Survival) to hold direction in cloud, snow, or hill-fog | Failure wastes hours and can walk a traveler onto a cliff edge. Weather closes on the tops within minutes. |

## Fauna And Encounters

**Mundane wildlife (the common case).** The Heights are thin country, but far from empty.

- **Red deer** on the lower slopes — the highland's main game, and the reason hunters come up at all. Harmless; a herd going off all at once means something else moved first.
- **Mountain hare, ptarmigan, and grouse** — the practical food supply for anyone working the tops.
- **Feral goats** among the crags, descended from lost stock. **Goat-like**, CR 0. Harmless, except that a startled one can start a slide above you.
- **Wolves** — **Wolf-like**, CR 1/4, in packs of three to six in hard winters, when they come lower and grow bold. Telegraphed by tracks, kills, and the dogs in Harrowgast going frantic. Pack tactics make them a real threat to a lone traveler on open ground.
- **Eagles and highland raptors** — **Giant Eagle-like**, CR 1, at the largest. Territorial near eyries in spring; not hunters of people, but they will drive off anyone near a nest.
- **Ravens** everywhere, and worth watching — a wheeling group over the scree usually means a carcass, a rockfall, or a body.

**The lingering dead.** They stand about the old workings and the roofless ruins, and there are more of them than any lowlander expects. Run per [`../../04_bestiary/FOLK_UNDEAD.md`](../../04_bestiary/FOLK_UNDEAD.md), but pitched harder than the fen dead — **Specter-like** to **Wraith-like** (AC 12–13, ~22–45 HP), colder in manner and quicker to take offense. Most appear to have been miners. They are still **not hostile by default**; they are simply less forgiving of trespass, and a folk rite that would settle a fen ghost may not settle one of these (DC 15 Religion). Avoidance works. So does leaving.

**People.** The Heights are worked, robbed, and fought over. Scavengers and claim-jumpers rob lone diggers and the occasional traveler — **Bandit-like**, **Scout-like**, or **Thug-like**, CR 1/8–1. **Concord Remnant** salvage crews hold dig-claims at the ruins and cairns — a few scouts and a hired bravo (AC 13–14, ~16–30 HP), who will warn strangers off a claim long before resorting to violence. **Cinder Ledger** buyers keep scouts up here watching who digs what. Lone **Ashen Wardens** pass through working the highland dead, and are usually glad of company. See [`../../04_bestiary/HUMAN_HAZARDS.md`](../../04_bestiary/HUMAN_HAZARDS.md).

## Level Range And Danger

**Level 2–6. Moderate-to-high — the most physically demanding country in the region.** Cold, falls, and rockfall are the real killers; the dead up here are harder than lowland dead, and there is no shortage of people willing to rob a stranger nobody will miss. This is the "come back when you are ready" frontier north of the fens.

**Telegraph it.** Harrowgast folk, the Ashen Wardens, and every shepherd on the road say the same thing: the Heights are no place for the green or the unsupplied, and the weather turns in an hour.

**Retreat:** downhill, always. Following any watercourse down reaches the fen edge eventually, and most named sites are a day or less from Harrowgast.

**Resting:** no safe long rest on the open tops — cold, wind, and no fuel above the heather line. Harrowgast is the only reliable recovery point in the north, and it has no healer worth the name. Arrive prepared or do not arrive.

## Resources

- **Salvage** from the adits and ruins: Concord stone, worked metal, tools, and the relics Cinder Ledger buyers pay well for and ask no questions about.
- **Ore and stone** — the reason Harrowgast exists at all, and still worth digging.
- **The best gear on the frontier** — rope, lamps, picks, cold-weather kit; bought at Harrowgast, at Harrowgast prices.
- **Game and hides** — red deer, hare, and grouse; DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) to hunt.
- **Highland herbs and lichens** — DC 14 Wisdom (Survival); scarce, and prized by apothecaries precisely because they grow nowhere lower.
- **The view from the Sundered Ridge** — worth the climb, and the best way for a newcomer to understand the shape of the whole region.
