## AI Use

Use these tables to improvise a Near or Far Telling on the spot — for a one-shot, a side excursion, or a mishap result from a failed Rite (see `THE_RITE_OF_THE_BROKEN_THRESHOLD.md`). Roll or choose; these are meant to be fast, not exhaustive.

## Quick Reference: Cost and Danger by Tier

| Tier | Cost/DC (see `THE_RITE_OF_THE_BROKEN_THRESHOLD.md`) | Typical Danger | Typical Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Near Telling | 1 shard, 1 hr, 1 exhaustion, DC 15 | Low | Introduce the concept; low-stakes "what if" excursions |
| Far Telling | 3 shards, 8 hr, 1 exhaustion + memory, DC 20 | Moderate-High | Side-quest destinations; recovering something/someone lost |
| The Last Telling | 1 Broken Shard, 24 hr + Between-Hour, 2 exhaustion + memory, DC 25 | High | Campaign-level excursions (see `THE_LAST_TELLING.md`) |

## Near Telling Generator

### Divergence Point (d8)

| d8 | What Changed |
|---|---|
| 1 | A specific person who died in the Kept Telling lived here (or vice versa). |
| 2 | A battle, election, or negotiation went the other way. |
| 3 | A disaster (fire, flood, plague) that struck the Kept Telling was averted here — or one that was averted, struck. |
| 4 | A relationship never happened, or happened when it didn't at home. |
| 5 | A discovery/invention came decades earlier or later. |
| 6 | A settlement was founded somewhere else, or never founded at all. |
| 7 | A faction here made the opposite choice at a known crossroads moment. |
| 8 | Roll twice, combine. |

### Tone (d6)

| d6 | Tone |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Bittersweet — better in some ways, worse in others; nothing simple |
| 3-4 | Wistful — quietly, plausibly nicer; unsettling *because* it's nicer |
| 5 | Tense — the divergence created a new, ongoing problem |
| 6 | Unsettling — small wrongnesses accumulate into real dread |

**Use:** A Near Telling excursion is usually about a specific NPC or PC connection — someone the party wants to see, warn, or learn from. Keep danger low; the point is emotional weight, not combat.

## Far Telling Generator

### Divergence Point (d10)

| d10 | What Changed |
|---|---|
| 1 | A major war here was lost that the Kept Telling won, or the reverse. |
| 2 | A different power altogether rules a region the party knows. |
| 3 | A historical figure the Kept Telling reveres here is reviled, or vice versa. |
| 4 | Geography itself differs — a city drowned, a forest never cleared, a river rerouted. |
| 5 | A faction that doesn't exist at home rules openly here. |
| 6 | A catastrophe far worse than any in the Kept Telling's history occurred and was only partly recovered from. |
| 7 | Magic, faith, or technology developed along a fundamentally different path. |
| 8 | A person the party knows to be dead is, here, a major power — or the reverse: someone powerful at home never rose at all. |
| 9 | The divergence is recent and still unfolding — the party arrives mid-crisis. |
| 10 | Roll twice, combine, and pick the more dramatic contradiction between them. |

### Danger Flavor (d6)

| d6 | Danger |
|---|---|
| 1 | Political — the party is caught between factions, not monsters |
| 2 | Environmental — the divergence broke something about the land itself |
| 3 | Monstrous — the divergence let something loose that didn't happen at home |
| 4 | Social — the party is recognized/mistaken for their Kept Telling counterparts, with consequences |
| 5 | Temporal — time behaves oddly here even by Unmade standards; use sparingly |
| 6 | Combination — roll twice |

**Use:** A Far Telling excursion supports a full side-adventure: retrieve something, rescue someone, learn a historical truth, or simply survive and get out. Danger should be real but always escapable — keep a Return Rite location reachable.

---

## Design Reminder

Neither table should ever produce a Telling darker or more dangerous than the Last Telling by default — the Last Telling is meant to remain the setting's confirmed worst case. If a Far Telling roll threatens to overshadow it, dial the danger flavor down a notch rather than up.
