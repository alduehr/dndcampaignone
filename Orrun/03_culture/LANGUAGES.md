## AI Use

Load to decide what a character can read or understand, especially at old ruins, archives, and shrine-sites. **Concord Script** in particular is a natural gate for historical and archival content in any story set in this world — gaining the ability to read it is a meaningful, usable milestone. All of the languages below are public knowledge; none of this reveals any hidden or campaign-specific truth.

## Design Note

Languages map onto standard 5e language slots; treat them as reskins so any class or background works normally. Variety is kept meaningful, not overwhelming.

## Common Languages

| Language | Maps To (5e) | Speakers | Script |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reach Common** | Common | Most of Orrun | Common letters |
| Regional dialects (e.g. farming, fishing, or trade-town speech) | Dialects of Common | Local to their region | Common letters |
| Standard ancestry languages | Dwarvish, Elvish, etc. | As normal | As normal |

## Regional / Cultural Languages

| Language | Speakers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Mourner's Cant** | Folk death-keepers and grief-tenders | A soft ritual register of Common with death-vocabulary; marks the speaker as devout. Not a full separate language — more a dialect of practice and word choice. |

## Ancient / Dead Languages

| Language | Where Found | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Concord Script** | Old ruins, archive-cairns, shrine-stones, and salvaged relics across the continent | The written language of the old Custodian Concord. A natural gate for historical clues — reading it can unlock old records, shrine mechanisms, and forgotten history. Learnable via scholars who study the Concord, certain informed NPCs, *Comprehend Languages*, or a found primer. |
| **Old Custodial** | The deepest, oldest Concord sites | An older, still rarer ritual dialect used by the Concord's highest-ranking members. Vanishingly rare even among scholars — almost no one alive can read it fluently. A natural late-game or capstone language gate for a story that wants one. |

## Secret / Restricted

| Language | Users | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Gravecaller Knock** | A secretive, widely feared group rumored to speak with the dead | A tapping or knocking code (on doors, coffins, stone) used to identify members to one another. Recognizable as more than random noise with a successful check. |
