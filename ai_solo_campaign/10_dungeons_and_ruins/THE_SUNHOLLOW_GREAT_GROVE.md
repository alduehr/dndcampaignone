## AI Use

Load for far-continent / Ring 2+ play in the Sunmark: the **living sacred grove-heart at Sunhollow**, where the grove-tribes keep an ancient nature-faith whose death-rites *still hold the dead* — the campaign's **hopeful contrast** to the failing north. A mid-high (L8-13) **living-faith exploration/social site** (a sacred lush grove, not a delve) delivering the far **REV_008/REV_010 hopeful echo** (a faith that works — a *consensual, holding* way with the dead is conceivable; synthesis is possible). **CRITICAL guardrail: a living grove-faith, NOT Concord, NOT a node, NOT the keystone, NOT the Court** — it predates and sits apart from the Concord, the Three Thresholds, and the Mourners. Grove-Keeper Sael Sunmark and Sun-Singer Doll are the social frame. Reverence and the grove's leave are the access key.

## One-Sentence Identity

The vast, living holy grove at the heart of the warm south, where an ancient grove-faith tends its dead so well that they *still rest* — a green sanctuary that shows the player, alone in all the continent, a way with the dead that has not failed, and so a hope for what the broken north might become.

## Map Placement

Far-continent, S, in the warm **Sunmark Wilds** between the southern plains and the **Greatspine** (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 48-56, Y 72-82). The **Great Grove at Sunhollow** (the tribes' holy heart), reached via the **Green Roads** through the **Grove-Camps**. The campaign's most peaceful, lush, non-death-haunted region.

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 8-13. Solo danger: moderate-high.** This is **not a combat-dungeon**: the challenges are the deep wilds (getting lost in the sacred forest), earning the grove-keepers' trust and the grove's reverent leave, and the *temptation* others bring (the Ledger wanting to cut/take, the Remnant wanting to study, zealots wanting to "harvest" the grove's secret). The few threats are grove-wardens defending the sacred green and wild beasts. Telegraphed; retreat-rich (the Grove-Camps are havens); a grove-guide negates most wild-risk. Not required; the hopeful echo and a green sanctuary.

## Purpose in the World

A mid-high site that (1) gives the player the campaign's **hopeful contrast** — a faith whose rites *still hold the dead*, proof that a consensual, holding way is possible (REV_008/REV_010 hope), (2) deepens the endgame's synthesis option (Maire's path) by *showing* it can work, (3) offers a distinctive non-dungeon living-faith exploration where reverence and trust are the play, and (4) is a green sanctuary and emotional respite from the death-haunted frontier — **without the keystone, the Court, or the grove ever being a Concord site.** It is hope made a place.

## Access / Entrance Routes

Full physical approach and entrance description: [`Locations/Orrun/06_sites/FAR_CONTINENT_SITES.md`](../../Locations/Orrun/06_sites/FAR_CONTINENT_SITES.md) → **The Great Grove At Sunhollow**. Campaign-specific access routes:

1. **By the grove's leave (the reverent way):** earn Grove-Keeper Sael Sunmark's blessing to enter the holy heart (the proper path).
2. **With Sun-Singer Doll (the seer's way):** the grove-seer guides a trusted, reverent outsider to the heart (the deepest access).
3. **As a pilgrim (the humble way):** join a grove-pilgrimage along the Green Roads.
4. **By force/theft (the forbidden way):** cut, take, or intrude — the grove-wardens and the wilds turn against you, and the keepers bar you (a grave offense).

## Current Occupants

- **Grove-keepers, grove-singers & pilgrims:** tend the living faith and its well-kept dead.
- **Grove-wardens:** defend the sacred green (living foes; warn-then-ward, not slay).
- **The grove's *resting* dead:** at peace — present but not restless (a quiet wonder, NOT undead-threat).
- **(DM) outsiders' temptation:** Ledger cutters, Remnant scholars, a zealot who'd "take" the grove's secret — the real tension.
- **Hollow Court / Concord:** **NOT present, NEVER were.** The grove-faith predates and sits apart from the Concord.

## Factions Involved

- **Grove-tribes (Sael Sunmark):** keep and guard the living faith.
- **Grove-singers (Sun-Singer Doll):** tend the rites and the omens (the hopeful node).
- **Cinder Ledger (edge-trade):** covets the grove's timber/resin and any "secret."
- **Concord Remnant (edge):** wants to *study* why the grove-rites still work.

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The Green Roads (the approach)
- **Description:** the pilgrim-and-trade paths through the warm wilds toward the Grove-Camps.
- **DC / hazards:** lost in the lush forest (Survival DC 14); wild beasts; the cutters' camps.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the grove-faith is *living and confident* — a faith that hasn't failed (REV-context).

### Zone 2: The Grove-Camps (the tribes)
- **Description:** the grove-tribes' settlements at the sacred forest's edge; warm, peaceable, wary of takers.
- **DC / hazards:** social (earning trust; the keepers' caution); no combat unless you threaten the green.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the tribes' dead *rest* — the player sees rites that work (the hopeful beat).

### Zone 3: The Sacred Grove (the living heart)
- **Description:** the great living grove proper; ancient trees, grove-shrines, the well-kept dead at peace.
- **DC / hazards:** reverence (the grove-wardens; the green's regard); the temptation of intruders.
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_SMK_001 (REV_008/REV_010, hopeful):** the grove-faith *holds its dead consensually and well* — living proof that a holding, non-harvesting way with the dead is possible, and that synthesis (not only seal/restart/destroy) is conceivable. **Capped: a hopeful contrast and the synthesis-seed; NEVER the keystone, the harvest, or the Court — the grove does not know the north's crisis exists.**

### Zone 4: The Heart-Tree & the Resting Dead (the wonder)
- **Description:** the grove's oldest heart-tree, around which the tribes' honored dead rest in peace.
- **DC / hazards:** deep reverence (defilement is catastrophe and offense); no undead-threat — the dead are *at peace*.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the *how* of the living rite (Sael/Doll share it only to the deeply trusted; a far REV_010 synthesis-seed) — **a DM gate: this is a living faith's wisdom, NOT a Concord mechanism, NOT the keystone; it informs the endgame's synthesis choice, it is not the lever.**

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_SMK_001 (REV_008/REV_010, hopeful):** the living grove-faith that *holds the dead well* — the synthesis-seed and the campaign's clearest hope.
- The tribes' resting dead (the emotional proof a faith can work).
- **Hard cap:** the Great Grove NEVER delivers REV_005/006/007, the keystone, the harvest, or the Court. It is a *separate, living* faith; its wisdom *informs* the endgame's synthesis option but is never the apex lever or a Concord site.

## Puzzles

**Reverence & Trust (social/reverent, not a lock):** the grove answers to reverence and the keepers' trust.
- **Solutions:** earn Sael Sunmark's leave (honor the grove; a service; humility — Persuasion/Insight DC 15); earn Doll's trust to learn the rite's wisdom (Religion/Insight DC 16); a grove-guide opens the way; protect the grove from takers (the trust-winning path).
- **Failure:** the keepers bar the grove (an offense to mend), not a dead end or cheap death.
- **Costly bypass:** none crude — force/theft only offends and bars; the grove is *living*.
- **Do not reveal:** any Concord/keystone framing — the grove is genuinely outside it.

## Noncombat Options

- **Reverence & pilgrimage:** the entire site is social/reverent by design.
- **Protect the grove:** win trust by defending the green from cutters/zealots.
- **A grove-guide:** negates wild-navigation risk.
- **The wisdom-gift:** the "payoff" is the living rite's wisdom (a synthesis-seed), not loot or a fight.

## Combat Encounters

This is a **near-combat-free site by design** (a living, sacred grove). Combat only arises if the player *threatens the green* or fights the takers:
- **Grove-wardens** — Druid/Scout-like defenders (AC 14-15, ~45-70 HP; warn-then-ward; nature-magic; not slayers — they protect, they don't pursue).
- **The takers (optional)** — Ledger cutters / a zealot band (Bandit/Cultist/Veteran-like; the *moral* fight, defending the grove).
- **Wild beasts (approach)** — standard 5e forest beasts (avoidable).
- Far NPCs (Sael Sunmark, Sun-Singer Doll) per `SUNMARK_NPCS.md` — never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **The Grove-Camps are havens** — always a retreat.
- **No bound undead** — the grove's dead are at peace; nothing to flee at the heart.
- **A grove-guide** makes the wilds safe to leave.

## Treasure and Rewards

- The grove-faith's wisdom — the living rite (a far REV_008/REV_010 synthesis-seed; the endgame-informing prize, C_SMK_001).
- The grove-keepers' friendship and a grove-blessing/charm (a possible minor item / a boon).
- A green sanctuary and safe southern base (if the player protects the grove).
- The campaign's clearest *hope* (a thematic reward).

## Consequences if Ignored

Nothing crisis-critical — the grove tends its dead, oblivious to the north's failing. But the **takers may not be ignorable**: if the player never engages, the Ledger's cutters, a zealot, or a Remnant expedition may *breach* the grove to "take its secret," and the living faith is wounded or its wisdom stolen and misused (a far tragedy, and the synthesis-hope dimmed). The far REV_008/REV_010 echo stays reachable in the NW (nothing dead-ends), but the hope is poorer for the loss.

## Scaling Notes

- **L8:** fewer/weaker wardens and beasts; emphasize reverence and the hopeful beat.
- **L10-11 (intended):** as written.
- **L13:** stronger taker-bands (the moral fight), the deepest wisdom-sharing, full Ledger/Remnant pressure. **The grove stays a living non-Concord faith regardless — never a node, never a path to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the reverence/trust/guide outs are the intended solo path; the site is near-combat-free and the only fights are moral (defending the grove).
