## AI Use

Load when the player presses out onto the drowned basin and into the **upper works of the great shrine beneath Hollowmere** — the central mystery site of the campaign. **This file covers ONLY the player-accessible approach and upper chambers** (the causeway, the basin-shore ruin, the first submerged halls). The deep shrine and the apex truth (the Hollow Court, the harvest mechanism, the endgame choice) live exclusively in `THE_UNDER_SHRINE_APPROACH.md` (**DM-only**) and `DM_ONLY_CANON.md`. **Hard guardrails:** the upper works deliver M2/M5 confirmation and M6 *fragments* (Act 2-3 paced); they **never** reveal REV_007 (the Court). Telegraph lethal danger at every depth so a low-level PC cannot suicide into the endgame. The deep stair down is gated, signposted as drowning-and-dread, and leads to the DM-only file only at Act 4-5.

## One-Sentence Identity

The flooded upper halls of the Concord's greatest shrine, surfacing through the Hollowmere basin — the center every drifting dead is pulled toward, where the player finally stands inside the thing the whole frontier fears.

## Level Range and Solo Danger Rating

**Levels 5-9 for the approach and upper works. Solo danger: high (and deeper = lethal, telegraphed).** The cold water, the dread, the gathered dead, and rival factions make the upper works genuinely dangerous; the **deep stair down is explicitly endgame-tier** and signposted as fatal for a sub-Act-4 party. The approach is survivable and rewarding at L5-9; going past the gate is not.

## Location

The drowned basin Hollowmere sits beside. The shrine's upper structures surface through the flood when the fog thins and the water is low; the player reaches them by boat, by the exposed low-water causeway, or via the basin-shore ruin. Vertically beneath it (off-limits here) lies the Under-Shrine — see the DM-only file.

## Purpose in the World

The campaign's gravitational center, made enterable. The approach (1) gives the strongest direct M2 proof (the player stands in the shrine), (2) confirms M5 at the source (the dead pour in here), (3) exposes M4 (Reke's basin scheme is about *this*), (4) delivers M6 fragments (the upper Script), and (5) **sets up the endgame** by placing the gated deep stair the player will one day descend. It is the hub the whole node-network points to.

## History (Player-Safe)

The great drowned ruin in the basin is plainly Concord work — the largest in the Reach. Hollowmere was founded on its shore. Folk know the Concord built it and that it flooded after the Quietfall; what it *did* is the central unknown. The dead drift toward it; the Compact debates draining the basin "for resettlement."

## History (DM-Only)

This is the **keystone** of the harvest network and, far below, the Hollow Court's seat. The upper works are the shrine's public-facing structure — ritual halls, keeping-galleries, relay-cores — drowned when the basin filled in the staged Quietfall. As the harvest reawakens, the upper works warm: the Script "lights," the gathered dead thicken, the water turns colder and "wrong." **Councilor Reke's drainage scheme is a Hollow Court operation to expose the shrine for reopening** (M4 -> the Court, gated). The upper works yield real proof and real danger but are *not* the Court — the Court is straight down, behind the gate, at Act 4-5. The approach is where the player's investigation physically converges with its target while the apex stays protected by depth and lethal telegraphing.

## First Impression

The fog thins and the basin shows its bones: black water, and rising from it, drowned stone vaster than any building in the Reach — fitted blocks, star-graven arches, a flooded hall whose far end you cannot see. The cold off the water is the cold of an open grave. Pale shapes drift just under the surface, all facing inward, all drawn here. Something below is awake, or waking, and it is patient.

## Access / Entrance Routes

1. **By boat (daylight):** the safest way out to the surfaced upper works; a guide (Old Sashe) or a Hollowmere boatman helps.
2. **The low-water causeway:** when the basin drops, an exposed ruin-causeway lets the player walk out — tide/level-timed (the safety valve and the clock).
3. **The basin-shore ruin (the Surfacing Ruin):** a partly-surfaced wing the player can enter from the shore without a boat; the gentlest entry (this is also the L1-4 *shore* clue site from the encounter tables — the deep entry is the L5-9 escalation).
4. **The Gravecaller way / Reke's drainage works:** factional routes (forbidden contact or following the scheme), each with its own complications.

## Current Occupants and Factions

- **Gathered Remembrances:** the woken dead, drawn here from across the Reach — telegraphed, mournful, dangerous in number.
- **Reke's drainage crews + Hollow Court cutouts:** working to expose/reopen the shrine (M4 -> Court, gated).
- **Concord Remnant scouts:** desperate to study and claim the keystone (Reclamation apex ambition).
- **Gravecaller radicals (Cole Ashby's faction):** trying to reach and *break* the shrine (the Breaking).
- **Ashen Wardens (Othetha):** trying to keep it sealed.
- **Hollow Court:** present only far below, behind the gate — **never deployed in the upper works.**

## Zone / Room List

### Zone 1: The Surfacing Wing (basin shore)
- **Description:** the partly-surfaced wing reachable from the shore; flooded ankle-to-knee deep; the first readable Script.
- **Occupants:** 1-2 drifting Remembrances (telegraphed, parley-able).
- **Hazards:** cold water (Con DC 12); slick stone (Athletics DC 12).
- **Secrets / Clues:** C-M2-1 Script: "the door under the water" (the strongest direct M2 proof — the player reads the shrine's own words).

### Zone 2: The Drowned Causeway Hall
- **Description:** a long flooded processional hall leading inward; the gathered dead thicken here.
- **Occupants:** several drifting Remembrances (a telegraphed crowd; a mass-rite scene, not a slugfest).
- **Hazards:** deep water (Athletics DC 14); the dread aura (Wis DC 14 or shaken); a rising-water cycle (DC 15 to read — the clock that pushes the player out).
- **Secrets / Clues:** the dead here remember being *pulled* (C_SR_005 at the source — REV_001/005 confirmation).

### Zone 3: The Keeping-Galleries (upper)
- **Description:** ringed niche-galleries where the Script says the dead's patterns were kept — drowned and warming.
- **Occupants:** drifting dead; possibly a Remnant scout-team or a Gravecaller cell (faction scene).
- **Hazards:** thin-touch (Con DC 14, reversible); collapsing drowned stone (Perception 14 / Dex 14, 3d6).
- **Secrets / Clues:** C-M6 fragment Script: "here the patterns of the dead were kept for the Keepers" (Act 2-3 fragment — the *what*, never the *why*/Court); a refined keystone-adjacent relic (high-tier M3).

### Zone 4: The Relay-Choir (the warming heart of the upper works)
- **Description:** the great upper relay-core, half-drowned, faintly "lit" and humming as the harvest wakes — the deepest the player should reach pre-endgame.
- **Occupants:** a strong gathered-dead "choir-keeper" Remembrance (parley/rite primary); possibly a converging faction climax (Reke's crew vs. Cole's radicals vs. the player).
- **Hazards:** intense dread (Wis DC 15); thin-touch (Con DC 15); the cold of the deep water.
- **Secrets / Clues:** the warming core proves the machine is *switching on* (M2/M5 apex-of-the-approach inference); the M4 link — evidence Reke's scheme targets *this* (C_SR_016 at the source).

### Zone 5: The Sealed Deep Stair (THE GATE — off-limits)
- **Description:** a vast drowned stair descending into black water and deeper cold, beyond the relay-choir.
- **Occupants:** none visible — only dread, and the sense of something patient far below.
- **Hazards:** **explicitly lethal.** Drowning, killing cold, mind-breaking dread; a sub-Act-4 descent is a telegraphed death.
- **Secrets / Clues:** **This stair leads to `THE_UNDER_SHRINE_APPROACH.md` (DM-only), the endgame, at Act 4-5 only.** In Acts 1-3 it is a gate: the player can see it, dread it, and turn back — they cannot survive it, and the file's apex content is NOT delivered here. Do NOT narrate the Court, the mechanism, or the choice at this stair.

## Exploration Features

- **Standing inside the mystery:** the upper works are the payoff of the whole investigation — every prior node-clue (D03/D05/D09/D14/D16) "points here," and now the player is here.
- **Reading the warming:** Arcana/Religion DC 16 — *the machine is waking, and this is its center* (a powerful, fair inference; still not the Court).
- **The faction convergence:** the upper works are where Reke, the Remnant, the Gravecallers, and the Wardens all converge — a set-piece of competing agendas the player navigates.

## Puzzles

**The Relay-Choir's Closing Word (Zone 4):** the player can briefly *quiet* the warming upper works (slowing the local harvest) by reading the Concord "closing word" (Concord Script DC 16) or performing a true rite (Religion DC 16) — an alternate, non-combat climax that eases the Reach and buys time on the master clock. Failure or force wakes the choir-keeper. This does NOT seal the shrine (that is the endgame); it is a temporary stay.

## Secrets and Clues

- **C-M2-1 / C_SR (network apex-surface):** the player reads the shrine's own Script — the strongest direct M2 proof (REV_001/002).
- **C_SR_005 / C-M5 at the source:** the dead are pulled *here* (REV_001).
- **C-M6 fragment:** the keeping-galleries Script (REV_005, Act 2-3, capped).
- **C_SR_016 at the source:** Reke's scheme targets this shrine (REV_003 -> the Court, gated).
- **NEW: C_SR_039 (REV_001/005, fragment):** the warming relay-choir = the machine switching on at its center (a strong inference clue; still NOT the Court).
- **The Sealed Deep Stair:** foreshadows REV_007 *as dread only* — never delivers it.

## Boss / Climax (variable, capped)

No apex boss in the upper works. The climax is one of:
- **The choir-keeper Remembrance** (Zone 4; ~64 HP; *frightful presence, withering wail, life-drain, summon gathered dead*; **parley Insight/Persuasion DC 16 or true rite Religion DC 16 primary**; bound to the relay-choir — the player can always retreat toward the shore). See STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.
- **The faction convergence** (Reke's crew / Remnant / Gravecallers collide; the player chooses a side or defuses it — can be entirely non-combat).
- **The Closing Word** (a non-combat ritual climax that quiets the upper works).

**Never** stage the Hollow Court here. The deep stair is a gate, not a boss.

## Adversary Profiles

Reference `../13_encounters_and_bestiary/STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Drowned/Gathered Remembrance** (drifting dead; AC 12-13, ~22-40 HP).
- **Choir-Keeper Remembrance** (upper-works climax; ~64 HP; bound to the relay-choir).
- Reke's drainage toughs (Thug/Guard-like) and Hollow Court cutouts (treated as unwitting human agents — Spy-like; **never reveal whom they serve**).
- Cole Ashby's radicals and Remnant scouts (per their NPC entries; recurring — never cheap-killed).

## Noncombat Options

- **Investigation:** read the Script, observe the warming, prove the M4 link — all without a fight.
- **Ritual:** the Closing Word / mass-rite quiets the works and lays the gathered dead.
- **Social/faction:** ally with the Wardens (seal-keepers), expose Reke, or negotiate the converging factions.
- **Stealth:** slip through the upper works between water cycles and faction patrols.

## Solo-Play Safety Valves

- **Warning signs:** cold, dread, the gathered dead, and the rising water all escalate with depth — the player is warned at every step.
- **Retreat:** the shore/boat exit is always reachable; the water cycle *pushes* the player out rather than trapping them.
- **The gate:** the Sealed Deep Stair is signposted as lethal — a non-railroad "do not go further yet."
- **Allies:** Old Sashe (boat/fens), the Wardens (rites, withdrawal), a Mourner (laying the dead).
- **Partial success:** the player can read the proof and leave without confronting the choir-keeper.

## Treasure and Rewards

- A high-tier keystone-adjacent Remembrance relic (best M3 object in the Reach; thin-touch care; every faction wants it).
- A Concord Script rubbing of the shrine's own words (a campaign-defining clue-object).
- A Warden's gratitude / Mourner goodwill if the dead are laid; a temporary easing of the Reach's hauntings if the Closing Word is spoken.
- **No node-treasure from below** — the deep stair is gated.

## Consequences if Ignored

If the player never reaches the upper works, the investigation still resolves via the network nodes and Caradril — nothing dead-ends. But the harvest's reawakening accelerates unopposed at its center: Reke's drainage advances, the gathered dead thicken, the master clock runs, and the eventual reveal lands without the player having stood at the source. The Remnant or the Gravecallers may reach the upper works first, each a worse outcome.

## How Site Changes After Interaction

- **Closing Word spoken / dead laid:** the upper works quiet; the Reach's hauntings ease briefly; the master clock slows (the Court counters over time).
- **Reke exposed at the source:** the basin scheme collapses; the city/Compact reels (feeds C3 / Reke's clock).
- **Faction reaches it first / the player sides with one:** the keystone's fate tilts toward that faction's endgame (track carefully).
- **The player descends the gate prematurely:** telegraphed death — redirect, do not kill cheaply (a near-drowning that throws them back to shore, marked and warned).
