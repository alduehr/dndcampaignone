# /10_dungeons_and_ruins/ — Folder README

## Purpose

This folder contains all authored dungeon, ruin, environmental-dungeon, heist, and adventure-site files for the AI solo campaign.

## Current Status

**Stage 12 complete (2026-06-13).** 23 adventure sites authored — 15 built in Stages 3/4/7, 8 new sites added in Stage 12. Full coverage from Level 1 to Level 20, spanning the Sundering Reach, Ring 1 regions (Ashgarden Vale, Tollwood, Pale Coast), Caradril, and two far-continent anchor sites, plus the DM-only endgame approach.

**For the full site inventory, see [`DUNGEON_INDEX.md`](DUNGEON_INDEX.md).** For investigation/puzzle-focused sites, see [`PUZZLE_DUNGEONS.md`](PUZZLE_DUNGEONS.md). For ruin-type sites, see [`RUIN_INDEX.md`](RUIN_INDEX.md).

## Design Standards

All sites in this folder meet the Stage 12 / `DND_MECHANICS_REQUIREMENTS.md` dungeon standard: level range, solo-danger rating, zone list, trap/hazard DCs, encounter list with stat references, puzzle/clue checks, treasure, boss/climax mechanics, noncombat options, retreat routes, solo-play safety valves, consequences-if-ignored, and state-update triggers.
