## AI Use

Load for far-continent high-tier play: a **volcanic ruin descent** into the fire-mountains of the Emberfell Theocracy, beneath the fortress-temple-city Ashfast and the mining town Cinderhold. An **environmental dungeon** where heat, ash, gas, and lava are the primary threats. It is a **thematic mirror** of the harvest (a fire-cult that "renders" the dead in the volcanoes) and a far REV_002 echo (the "old glass" relic source) — but it is a distinct culture, **NOT part of the harvest conspiracy, NOT a node, NOT the Court.** Use environmental-dungeon mechanics; very high but telegraphed solo danger. A far, optional, treasure-and-corroboration site.

## One-Sentence Identity

A descent into a Concord-touched volcanic ruin beneath the Emberfell fire-theocracy, where the heat itself is the dungeon, a fire-cult guards its rendering-deeps, and "old glass" relics prove the dead-as-substance trade reaches even here.

## Level Range and Solo Danger Rating

**Levels 12-16. Solo danger: very high (environmental).** Lethal heat, toxic gas, unstable lava-rock, and a zealous theocracy make this one of the deadliest sites in the library — but the danger is **environmental and telegraphed**, with clear gear/preparation requirements (heat protection, gas wards) and retreat routes. A far, optional, high-tier site; never on a critical path.

## Location

Far-continent, SE-central: the **Emberfells** volcanic highland (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`). Reached from **Ashfast** (the theocracy's fortress-temple-city) and **Cinderhold** (the volcanic mining town), via the **Ash Roads**. Distant from and unrelated to the NW campaign cluster.

## Purpose in the World

A far high-tier environmental-dungeon and **thematic mirror**: the Emberfell fire-cult "renders" their dead in the volcanoes, an independent culture that *rhymes* with the harvest without being it. It (1) delivers a far REV_002 echo (the "old glass" volcanic relic source — substance and trade exist continent-wide), (2) showcases environmental-survival play at high tier, (3) offers volcanic Concord-touched treasure, and (4) deepens the world's thematic web. **Hard guardrail: a mirror, not the machine — the Emberfell rites are their own faith, NOT the harvest, NOT the Court.**

## History (DM-Only)

The Emberfell Theocracy is an **independent far culture, not part of the harvest conspiracy** (`DM_ONLY_CANON.md`, Full-World section). Its fire-rites are a *thematic mirror* of the harvest — they "render" the dead into fire-glass, which the Ledger and black-marketeers trade as "old glass" (a far REV_002 substance/trade echo). The deeps hold Concord-touched works (the Concord reached even here at its height), but **no node, no keystone, no Court** — the singular live machine is under Hollowmere. Ash-Hierophant Vole Cindra rules zealously; Ember-Sage Mira Cindra is a heretic-scholar who suspects the fire-glass "holds something" (an oblique far REV_002 path). The descent corroborates the *theme and the trade* — never the mechanism's origin or the Court.

## Access / Entrance Routes

Full physical approach and entrance description: `vael/orrun/sites/ashfast-deep`. Campaign-specific access routes:

1. **From Cinderhold (the miners' way):** hire masked guides; brave the mine-deeps toward the forbidden works.
2. **The Ash Roads pilgrim-ways (the zealot's way):** approach via the theocracy (access + scrutiny).
3. **The black-market dig (the smuggler's way):** follow the "old glass" trade down (Ledger-linked; illegal under the Pyre's law).
4. **Over the caldera rim (the reckless way):** the most lethal approach.

## Current Occupants and Factions

- **Emberfell Theocracy (Pyre-Wardens):** guard the forbidden deeps zealously (Sef Embren, enforcer).
- **Cinder-miners & the mining-lord (Doss Ashfast):** work the heat for glass and ore.
- **Ember-Sage Mira Cindra (heretic-scholar):** secretly studies the fire-glass (the oblique REV_002 contact).
- **Cinder Ledger black-marketeers:** smuggling "old glass" out.
- **Fire-touched dead & elementals:** the rendering-deeps' guardians/hazards.
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** No network here; pure thematic mirror.

## Zone / Room List

### Zone 1: The Cinder Mines (Cinderhold)
- **Description:** the worked volcanic mines; heat, masks, miners.
- **Occupants:** miners, the mining-lord's overseers.
- **Hazards:** heat (Con DC 14 staged); gas pockets (Con DC 14 or poisoned); collapse.
- **Secrets / Clues:** "old glass" in the rock (C_FC_004 / REV_002 far echo — the substance and trade exist here too).

### Zone 2: The Forbidden Stair
- **Description:** the theocracy-sealed descent toward the Concord-touched deeps.
- **Occupants:** Pyre-Wardens (Sef Embren); a seal/guard-post.
- **Hazards:** the guards (social/stealth gate); rising heat.
- **Secrets / Clues:** Mira Cindra's hidden notes (oblique REV_002 — "the fire-glass holds something").

### Zone 3: The Rendering-Deeps
- **Description:** where the fire-cult "renders" their dead into fire-glass; lava-channels and ash.
- **Occupants:** fire-touched dead; possibly a Pyre-rite in progress.
- **Hazards:** lethal heat (Con DC 15); lava-rock (Dex DC 15, lava damage on fail); toxic gas (Con DC 15).
- **Secrets / Clues:** the rendering *mirrors* the harvest — a chilling thematic echo (NEW: C_FC_015, REV_002 far echo, **capped: a mirror, not the harvest/Court**).

### Zone 4: The Concord-Touched Vault
- **Description:** an old Concord work the volcano has half-consumed — Script under ash, fire-warded.
- **Occupants:** a fire-elemental guardian or fire-touched Remembrance (boss).
- **Hazards:** the guardian; molten collapse; fire-wards.
- **Secrets / Clues:** Concord Script (far M2/M6 *fragment* — the Concord reached here; **never the keystone/Court**); high-tier fire-glass relics.

### Zone 5: The Magma Throat (off-limits)
- **Description:** the active volcanic deep below the vault.
- **Hazards:** instant-death lava (explicitly telegraphed; a gate, not a trap).
- **Secrets / Clues:** none reachable — the descent bottoms out in living fire. **A dead end downward; never a path to anything.**

## Exploration Features

- **Environmental survival as the pillar:** heat-management, gas-wards, timing lava-cycles — the dungeon is the volcano.
- **The thematic mirror:** seeing a *different* culture commodify/ritualize the dead deepens the campaign's themes.
- **Far faction play:** the theocracy, the heretic-sage, the Ledger smugglers, the miners — a far web.

## Hazards and Traps

| Trap / Hazard | DC | Effect | Bypass |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lethal heat (staged by depth) | Con 14-15 | Exhaustion -> fire damage; lethal deep | Heat protection (gear/magic); timing; don't linger |
| Toxic gas pockets | Con 14-15 | Poisoned; lethal in deep pools | Gas-wards; masks; route around |
| Lava-rock / lava channels | Dex 15 | Lava damage (high); falls | Care; timing the cycles |
| Molten collapse | Perception/Dex 15 | High damage + blocked path | Reroute; a guide |
| Fire-wards (Zone 4) | DC 16 | Fire-blast / alarm guardian | Disarm; read the Script |
| The Magma Throat | — (instant lethal) | Death by lava | **Turn back — telegraphed gate** |

Environmental hazards dominate; preparation (heat/gas protection) is the real "key."

## Puzzles

**The Fire-Wards (Zone 4):** the Concord vault is fire-warded with Script-glyphs.
- **Solutions:** read the Script (Concord Script); quench/route the fire-channels (Survival/Arcana DC 15); a heretic-sage's (Mira Cindra) knowledge.
- **Failure:** rouses the fire-guardian (a telegraphed high-tier fight).
- **Costly bypass:** Ledger smugglers know a way (illegal; a favor owed).

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_FC_004 (REV_002 far echo):** "old glass" volcanic relics — substance/trade reach here (existing far clue).
- **NEW: C_FC_015 (REV_002 far echo):** the fire-cult's rendering *mirrors* the harvest's commodification of the dead — a thematic echo. **Capped: a mirror and a far trade, never the harvest mechanism, never the Court, never the keystone.**
- Far M2/M6 *fragment* Concord Script in the vault (the Concord reached here; never the apex).

## Boss / Climax Mechanics

**A fire-elemental guardian or a fire-touched Remembrance** (Zone 4; see STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES — Caldera Guardian / Fire-touched Remembrance).
- **Environmental-first:** the *real* boss is the volcano — surviving the heat/gas to reach and leave the vault.
- **The guardian:** AC 16-17, ~110-140 HP (L13-15), fire-themed (fire damage, heat aura, magma-grip); bound to the vault (retreat-rich).
- **Non-combat outs:** quench the fire-wards / read the Script to pass; Mira Cindra's aid; reverence to a fire-touched Remembrance.
- **No cheap death:** the lethal heat/lava is telegraphed and gear-gated; the guardian is bound.

## Noncombat Options

- **Environmental mastery:** the core "solution" is surviving the volcano with the right prep — a non-combat triumph.
- **Heretic alliance:** Mira Cindra aids access and reading.
- **Far diplomacy/stealth:** navigate the theocracy, the smugglers, the miners.
- **Quench/read the wards:** bypass the guardian.

## Solo-Play Safety Valves

- **Gear-gated, not ambush-gated:** the lethal heat/gas/lava are telegraphed; the player is warned to prepare (and can retreat to gear up).
- **Havens:** Cinderhold and Ashfast are the retreat points.
- **A guide / the heretic-sage:** reduce navigation and ward risk.
- **The Magma Throat:** signposted instant-death gate (a non-railroad "stop").
- **Not required:** a far, optional site.

## Treasure and Rewards

- High-tier **fire-glass / Concord-volcanic relics and artifacts** (tier-3/4; heat-themed; see `14_treasure_and_artifacts/` when built).
- Far allies/leverage (Mira Cindra, the mining-lord) or a zealot enemy (the theocracy).
- Significant "old glass" value (a moral/economic choice — the substance trade).

## Consequences if Ignored

The theocracy and the Ledger keep the "old glass" trade turning; Mira Cindra's heresy is silenced or driven underground; the far REV_002 echo and the thematic mirror go unwitnessed (REV_002 is fully covered in the NW — nothing dead-ends). A far, optional thread closes quietly.

## How Site Changes After Interaction

- **The deeps explored / old glass exposed:** the far trade is disrupted or implicated; Mira Cindra rises or is endangered.
- **The theocracy crossed:** a far zealot enemy.
- **Allied with the heretic-sage:** a far ally and an oblique REV_002 corroboration in hand.
