## AI Use

Load for Caradril's **criminal / relic-trade dungeon** — the undercroft warren beneath the Ashmarket where the black market in "quiet-coin" (Remembrance relics) is stored, refined, and fenced. This is a **heist/social/stealth site against living foes** (the Salt Syndicate and Ledger fences), with a relic-refining moral core. The city counterpart to the Pellow Grange (D08) and Wreckers' Caves (D13): the cleanest *commercial* proof of REV_002 in the city, told through living villainy. **Guardrails:** M2/M3 demonstrations and the relic trade — never the Concord-sin, never the Court. Combat is the worst option; the design rewards infiltration.

## One-Sentence Identity

A warren of cellars and refining-rooms beneath Caradril's relic market, where the Salt Syndicate and Ledger fences turn the dead into quiet-coin — and an investigator can prove, in coin and ledgers, exactly what is being sold.

## Level Range and Solo Danger Rating

**Levels 6-9. Solo danger: moderate (social/stealth-weighted).** The danger is being caught and made an enemy of the Syndicate, the Ledger, or the Hush — not death. The guards prefer to catch, intimidate, and march intruders out (or to the Watch) over killing in a city. Storming the undercroft is the *failure* path; stealth, social, and faction play are the intended ones.

## Location

Beneath the Ashmarket district of Caradril (relic/salvage trade), connecting via cellars to the Sill and, at its deepest, faintly toward the upper Sunken Wards (a link to D16). Entered from market cellars, a Sill back-alley, or a Syndicate-controlled stair.

## Purpose in the World

The city's *commercial* relic-engine, made enterable. It (1) proves REV_002 in the clearest mercantile form (the quiet-coin trade, refining, and ledgers), (2) exposes the Syndicate-Ledger-Hush relic network, (3) gives a heist/social set-piece at city scale, and (4) ties the Reach relic-finds (Pellow, Wreckers, Deep Adit "old glass") to a continental market through Caradril. A choice-defining site: expose, rob, stop, or join the trade.

## History (DM-Only)

The undercroft is where the **quiet-coin trade** is stored, *refined*, and fenced. Raw Remembrance relics (from the Reach "old glass," the Vale/Coast quarrying, and Caradril's own salvage) are brought here; the Crucible's smelting and certain alchemists *refine* them into higher-value forms — a process that demonstrably destroys some and "concentrates" others (a chilling M3 confirmation). The **Salt Syndicate** runs the warren; the **Cinder Ledger** is the quiet buyer (Vyre's monopoly arm); the **Hush** brokers the secrets. The undercroft is the city's commercial face of the harvest's *aftermarket* — proof someone collects the substance on purpose (REV_002), commercially, at scale. It is **not** a node and holds **no** Concord-sin reveal — only the trade.

## Current Occupants and Factions

- **Salt Syndicate:** runs the warren ("Tidewife" Sorrel's people, or "Mother" Silque's above her).
- **Cinder Ledger fences:** quiet buyers; a Ledger factor or two.
- **The Hush:** a broker presence; couriers.
- **A refiner/alchemist:** works the relics (the moral core).
- **No undead by default** — this is a living-villainy site (a stirred relic-Remembrance is possible as a moral beat).

## Zone / Room List

### Zone 1: The Cellar Stalls (the front)
- **Description:** the public-ish undercroft — "antiquities" cellars; the social gate.
- **Occupants:** a tally-clerk, 1-2 Syndicate runners.
- **Hazards:** social exposure (a wrong word brings scrutiny); a tripline alarm (Perception/Stealth DC 13).
- **Secrets / Clues:** a cloth-covered relic that "holds a dead face" (C-M3 / C_CAR_005 demonstration — REV_002).

### Zone 2: The Counting-Cellar
- **Description:** the trade's records-room.
- **Occupants:** a Ledger fence; a guarded strongbox.
- **Hazards:** a quality lock (Thieves' Tools DC 15); a watcher.
- **Secrets / Clues:** the **trade-ledger** (the prize: relics logged by source — Reach old glass, Vale saint's-bones, Coast sea-relics — bound for the Ledger; C_CAR_017 / REV_002 documentary).

### Zone 3: The Refining-Room
- **Description:** the brazier-room where relics are "refined" (the moral core).
- **Occupants:** a refiner/alchemist + a guard.
- **Hazards:** heat/fumes (Con DC 13); thin-touch near raw/refined relics (Con DC 14, reversible); a stirred relic-Remembrance possible (telegraphed, layable).
- **Secrets / Clues:** the refining *proves the substance* — some relics resist destruction, some "concentrate" the dead (C_CAR_006 analog / REV_002, the chilling commercial confirmation).

### Zone 4: The Deep Cache & Wards-Link
- **Description:** the deepest, wettest cellars where the best stock is hidden and a flooded passage links toward the Sunken Wards (D16).
- **Occupants:** a Syndicate strong-arm; possibly a Reclaimer scout (the Wards-link).
- **Hazards:** flooded footing (Athletics DC 13); thin-touch (Con DC 14).
- **Secrets / Clues:** a refined high-value relic (best city M3 object short of the Wards); the link to D16 (an exploration thread).

## Exploration Features

- **The paper trail:** the trade-ledger is the heart — a documentary REV_002 clue tying the whole regional relic-supply to the Ledger.
- **The refining horror:** watching a relic "refined" is the campaign's clearest *commercial* statement that the dead are a commodity.
- **The faction triangle:** Syndicate vs. Ledger vs. Hush — play them, don't fight them all.

No lethal mechanical traps; the threat is human, reputational, and legal.

## Puzzles

**The Counting-Cellar (social/heist "puzzle"):** getting the trade-ledger out clean.
- **Solutions:** pick the lock and copy it (stealth); turn a Ledger fence or Syndicate clerk (social, Persuasion/Insight DC 14); buy a copy from the Hush (coin + favor); catch a courier above-ground with it.
- **Failure:** detection -> the trade closes ranks; the Syndicate/Ledger become wary/hostile (consequence, not death).
- **Costly bypass:** the Hush sells the ledger's contents outright (expensive; less proof-weight than the original).

## Secrets and Clues

- **C-M3 / C_CAR_005 (REV_002):** the cloth-covered and refined relics demonstrate the substance.
- **NEW: C_CAR_017 (REV_002, documentary):** the trade-ledger — relics logged by source-region, bound for the Ledger; the city's clearest *commercial* REV_002 proof and a continental-supply map.
- **C_CAR_006 analog (REV_002):** the refining proves relics resist destruction / "concentrate" the dead.
- **The Wards-link:** a thread to D16 (the Sunken Wards Deep).

## Boss / Climax Mechanics

No monster boss by design. The "boss" is the **fork** and the **refiner scene**:
- **The fork:** expose the trade (carry the ledger to the Watch/reformers/Mourners — checks the Ledger's city supply, earns allies + a Ledger/Syndicate enemy); rob it (coin, become a hunted thief); stop it (free/refund the stock, lay any stirred dead — a quieter win); join it (sell silence — coin + a Ledger favor at a moral cost).
- **The refiner** is a social/stealth obstacle (a guard or two, Thug/Spy-like), not a fight to the death; the *horror of the refining* is the climax's emotional weight.
- **Caught anywhere:** city consequences (debt, blacklist, Watch attention, a powerful enemy) first; violence last.

## Adversary Profiles

Reference `CARADRIL_ENCOUNTERS.md` and `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Salt Syndicate runner / strong-arm** (Thug/Bandit/Spy-like, AC 13-14, ~16-45 HP; catch-and-intimidate).
- **Ledger fence** (Spy/Noble-like; noncombatant; calls guards).
- **Refiner/alchemist** (Mage-lite, AC 12, ~30 HP; a thin-touch/relic trick, not a slayer).
- **Stirred relic-Remembrance** (specter-like, AC 12, ~22 HP; moral beat; layable).

## Noncombat Options

- **Social:** pose as a buyer; turn a fence/clerk; negotiate the factions.
- **Stealth:** the Sill back-alley; copy the ledger; slip the cache.
- **Investigation:** the paper trail is the win — no fight needed.
- **Faction:** the Hush sells the way in and out; the Watch wants the trade exposed.

## Solo-Play Safety Valves

- **It's a city, not a deathtrap:** capture/ejection/legal trouble is the default cost of failure, not death.
- **Retreat:** market cellars and Sill alleys give many exits.
- **The Hush:** a paid safety valve (intel, a way in, the ledger's contents).
- **Partial success:** prove the trade with one relic and a glimpsed ledger, then walk.

## Treasure and Rewards

- The **trade-ledger** (the real prize — leverage on the Ledger and a continental-supply map).
- A refined high-value Remembrance relic (best city M3 object short of the Wards; thin-touch care).
- Syndicate cache: coin and contraband (~200-300 gp; taking it makes enemies).
- Reformer/Watch/Mourner allies if exposed; a Ledger favor if joined.

## Consequences if Ignored

The quiet-coin trade keeps turning the dead into coin (the Monopoly clock advances at its hub); the Vyre-Quorrin deal is fed with refined relics; the Reach's looted dead flow through unopposed. The city's clearest commercial REV_002 proof and a powerful piece of anti-Ledger leverage go unused (REV_002 reachable elsewhere — nothing dead-ends).

## How Site Changes After Interaction

- **Exposed:** the trade scrambles; reformers/Watch gain; the Ledger/Syndicate retaliate (feeds C1/C3).
- **Robbed:** a hunted-thief status; the Syndicate hunts the player.
- **Stopped/laid:** a quieter win; the stock dispersed/refunded; Mourner goodwill.
- **Joined:** coin + a Ledger favor; the campaign remembers the complicity.
