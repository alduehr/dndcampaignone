## AI Use

Load for far-continent / Ring 2+ play in the Glassmere League: the **Concord Remnant's great League chapter-vault beneath the Reliquary**, the largest Remnant archive outside Caradril, where the worldly river-cities' wealth has bought a deep relic-and-record collection. A mid-high (L9-13) **archive/social-infiltration site** delivering far **REV_002/005 corroboration** (the Remnant's continental scholarship; the relic-as-substance proof at scale). **CRITICAL guardrail: a great Remnant *chapter-vault*, NOT the Sealed Archive, NOT the keystone, NOT the Court.** It corroborates the harvest's history and the substance; the clearest written apex-proof stays in Caradril's Sealed Archive (Act 3 gate). Mareth Senn and Sefwy Holt are the social frame.

## One-Sentence Identity

The deep relic-and-record vaults under the Remnant's grand League chapter-house, where the continent's richest scholars hoard Concord relics and salvaged records — a far, lesser mirror of Caradril's Sealed Archive, guarded by wards, scholars, and the League's own intrigue.

## Map Placement

Far-continent, central, the city of **Glassmere** on the **Glasswater** (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 52-58, Y 50-58). Beneath **the Reliquary** (the Remnant's great League chapter-house), in the banking-quarter shadow of **the Three Bridges**. The "real" urban Orrun past Caradril — Caradril writ several times larger and older.

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 9-13. Solo danger: moderate-high.** This is an *urban archive heist/investigation*, not a monster-crawl: the threats are Remnant scholar-guardians and wards, League intrigue (Holt's bank, the Glass Ear's information), and Concord Script gates — far more social/stealth than combat. Telegraphed; retreat-rich (the city is all around). Not required; corroboration and scholarly treasure.

## Purpose in the World

A mid-high far-corroboration site that (1) proves the Remnant's scholarship and the harvest's history at *continental scale* (REV_002/005 corroboration, the substance-proof in a great collection), (2) makes the League the Remnant's true heartland the player can investigate, ally with, or rob, (3) offers a deep urban archive-infiltration with Script gates and faction intrigue, and (4) shows the Ledger and Remnant operating at full city-power — **without delivering the Sealed Archive's apex-proof or naming the Court.**

## Access / Entrance Routes

Full physical approach and entrance description: `vael/orrun/sites/reliquary-vaults`. Campaign-specific access routes:

1. **As a guest scholar (the open way):** earn League-Factor Oren Glass's or Reliquary-Lector Mareth Senn's leave to study (slow, safe; limited access).
2. **By the back-ledgers (the banking way):** Banker-Magistra Sefwy Holt (the Ledger's rival) can buy you a key — for a price.
3. **By the Glass Ear (the broker way):** the information-master sells the vault's layout and wards.
4. **By stealth (the heist way):** infiltrate the chapter-house and its wards directly (the riskiest, richest path).

## Current Occupants

- **Remnant scholars & Reclaimer agents:** study and guard the collection (turn-back-first, not kill).
- **Archive wards & a vault-guardian:** Concord-ward locks and one bound construct-sentinel.
- **League intrigue:** Holt's agents, the Glass Ear's watchers, rival collectors.
- **(DM) the Mourners' urban old-songs node** (Mother Wenna Lowwater) — an oblique REV_005 folk thread outside the vault.
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** A great chapter-vault; the apex is under Hollowmere.

## Factions Involved

- **Concord Remnant (Mareth Senn, Sabine Ferrant's reach):** own the vault; covet the apex-proof.
- **Cinder Ledger:** the League is its heartland; it wants the relics commercially.
- **Banker-Magistra Sefwy Holt:** the Ledger's banking rival; a lever and a price.
- **The Glass Ear:** sells access and intelligence.

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The Reliquary (the public chapter-house)
- **Description:** the grand Remnant college above-ground; reading-rooms, displays, scholar-guards.
- **DC / hazards:** social/cover (Deception/Insight DC 14); the scholars' suspicion.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the *scale* of the collection — the Remnant is a continental power (REV_005 shape).

### Zone 2: The Reading-Vaults (the guarded stacks)
- **Description:** the restricted lower stacks; ward-locked Concord records.
- **DC / hazards:** ward-glyph locks (Script or Investigation DC 15); a watching scholar.
- **Secrets/Clues:** Concord Script on the harvest's substance (REV_002, scholarly).

### Zone 3: The Relic-Vault (the substance-proof)
- **Description:** the deep relic-strongroom; the collection's prized Remembrance relics.
- **DC / hazards:** a vault-guardian (watchword or fight); the thinning aura of massed relics.
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_GL_001 (REV_002/005):** the Remnant's catalogued proof — relics that *store the dead*, logged with the Concord's harvest-history at continental scale. **Capped: corroborates substance + scale; NEVER the Sealed Archive's apex-proof, the keystone, or the Court.**

### Zone 4: The Lector's Study (the scholarly heart)
- **Description:** Mareth Senn's private study; the chapter's working theories and correspondence.
- **DC / hazards:** social (Senn herself) or stealth; the chapter's correspondence-wards.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the Remnant's *continental ambition* in writing — and a DM gate: the study points *toward Caradril's Sealed Archive* for the real proof, it does NOT contain it.

### Zone 5: The Lost Stacks (treasure)
- **Description:** the deepest, uncatalogued stacks; rare relics and salvaged records.
- **DC / hazards:** ward-traps; a dormant relic's aura.
- **Secrets/Clues:** Concord artifacts and a final REV_002/005 record; the stacks are a closed strongroom (no passage to anything below).

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_GL_001 (REV_002/005):** the Remnant's continental-scale relic-and-harvest catalogue.
- The Mourners' urban old-songs (Mother Wenna Lowwater; oblique REV_005, outside the vault).
- **Hard cap:** the Reliquary Vaults NEVER deliver REV_006/007 in full, the keystone, the harvest mechanism, or the Court. The clearest apex-proof is Caradril's Sealed Archive (Act 3); this vault *points there*.

## Puzzles

**The Ward-Locks & the Cover (Script + social/stealth):** the vault answers to Concord wards and the chapter's trust.
- **Solutions:** read the ward-glyphs (Concord Script DC 15); pose as a guest scholar (Deception/faction DC 14); buy a key (Holt) or the layout (the Glass Ear); a clean stealth route.
- **Failure:** alarm and ejection (and Remnant suspicion), not a dead end or cheap death.
- **Costly bypass:** ally openly with the Remnant (then they watch what you read).
- **Accept clever solutions:** any valid social/stealth/Script approach passes.

## Noncombat Options

- **Guest-scholar access / faction standing:** study the vault lawfully.
- **The Glass Ear / Holt:** buy access without a fight.
- **Stealth:** the intended heist path — avoid every guardian.
- **Watchword:** stand the vault-guardian down with the Script.

## Combat Encounters

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Reclaimer agents / scholar-guards** — Concord Sentinel-Scholar (AC 13-14, ~40-60 HP; warn-then-control; turn-back-first; a mage-type with a Remembrance one-off at high tier).
- **A vault-guardian** — Concord Construct-Guardian (AC 17, ~95-120 HP; **watchword stands it down**; bound to the relic-vault).
- Far NPCs (Mareth Senn, Oren Glass, Sefwy Holt, the Glass Ear) per `GLASSMERE_LEAGUE_NPCS.md` — never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **The city is the retreat** — the player can withdraw into Glassmere any time.
- **Guardian is bound** to the relic-vault — leaving the stacks escapes.
- **Scholars prefer ejection** to killing in their own chapter-house.

## Treasure and Rewards

- Concord relics and rare salvaged records (tier-3; a possible scholarly minor item).
- Far allies/leverage (Mareth Senn, Oren Glass, Holt) or a powerful far enemy.
- A lead pointing toward Caradril's Sealed Archive (the apex-proof's true home).
- Far REV_002/005 corroboration (C_GL_001).

## Consequences if Ignored

The Remnant's League chapter presses its continental Reclamation ambition unchecked (Sabine Ferrant's reach grows); the Ledger commercializes more of the collection; the Glass Ear sells the vault's secrets to the highest bidder. The far REV_002/005 corroboration passes uncollected (it stays reachable in the NW and at the Sealed Archive — nothing dead-ends).

## Scaling Notes

- **L9:** simpler wards (DC 14), no first-round guardian blast; emphasize the social heist.
- **L11 (intended):** as written.
- **L13:** apex wards (DC 16), a mage-scholar pair with Remembrance one-offs, the lost stacks open. **The vault stays a closed chapter-strongroom regardless — never a path to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the access/stealth/watchword outs are the intended solo path; guardians are bound and scholars turn-back-first.
