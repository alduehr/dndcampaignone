## AI Use

Load when the player descends below the upper Sunken Wards (district file `THE_SUNKEN_WARDS.md`) into the **deep wards** — the proper dungeon level beneath Caradril. This is Stage 12's flagship city dungeon: the deepest player-reachable expression of the harvest network outside the Reach, the **back-route to the Sealed Archive**, and a three-faction battleground (Remnant, Hush, Salt Syndicate). **Guardrails:** the deep wards give M2/M3 demonstrations and M6/M9 *fragments* (Act-paced); they faintly touch the Court's continental network but **never name the Hollow Court, never reveal the apex truth, and are NOT the keystone.** Use dungeon mechanical standards; default to retreat-rich, telegraphed solo play.

## One-Sentence Identity

The drowned deep level of the older city beneath Caradril — a dormant Concord relic-network the Remnant has secretly mapped, where the Stilling's century of calm shows its true face and a hidden stair climbs into the Sealed Archive.

## Level Range and Solo Danger Rating

**Levels 6-10 (core 7-9). Solo danger: moderate, telegraphed, retreat-rich** — escalating to **high** only in the deepest Cistern Vault and at a Reclaimer confrontation. The Sill above is always a retreat; a guide (Old Pater Dunk) or the Remnant's map removes the navigation risk; every apparition can be avoided or parleyed. This is the city's real exploration challenge but a *minor* node by design — never a lethal ambush, never the keystone.

## Location

Beneath Caradril, reached from the upper Sunken Wards (the Sealgate stair down from the Sill, or the Remnant's hidden back-way from the Lantern Reach). The deep wards lie below the dry upper galleries described in `THE_SUNKEN_WARDS.md`, where the still black water deepens and the Concord stone grows older and colder.

## Purpose in the World

A dense, repeatable mid-game exploration site that (1) proves the harvest network is **continental** (city M2 fragment), (2) demonstrates Remembrance-as-substance commercially and physically (M3), (3) offers a back-route into the Remnant's Sealed Archive (an alternative to a front-door break-in for `Q_THE_SEALED_ARCHIVE`), and (4) stages the three city-underworld factions against each other so the player's choices ripple into city politics.

## History (DM-Only)

The deep wards are a **minor, long-dormant cousin of the Reach's harvest-network nodes** — a lower Concord relay-level that was part of the same continental machine but never a major node, and has lain still through the **Stilling** (the harvest's century-long pause). As the master harvest reawakens far off in the Reach, the deep wards **faintly warm**: still-water "listening" pools stir, a few drifting Remembrances wake, and the in-situ Concord Script now reads as part of *a network*. The **Concord Remnant** mapped this in secret from the Lantern Reach; one deep stair connects up into the **Sealed Archive** (their true vault). The deep wards are a deliberate *smaller echo* of the Reach's Deep Adit — they corroborate the truth, they are **not** the keystone, and the Hollow Court has no seat here (only the faint network-touch). The Stilling's prosperity is itself the harvest's pause made flesh, but that is a late, earned thematic key (REV_005), never spoken here.

## Access / Entrance Routes

Full approach and entrance description (the three routes down): `vael/orrun/sites/sunken-wards-deep`. Campaign-specific: the Remnant back-way drops the player nearest the Archive stair; the flood-channel route can be learned from the Syndicate or the Hush.

## Current Occupants and Factions

- **Concord Remnant (Reclaimers):** keep the back-way and guard the Archive stair; prefer to *turn back* intruders, not kill.
- **The Salt Syndicate:** uses the dry upper wards and a deep cache for relic and contraband drops.
- **The Hush:** runs couriers and sells the *map* and the *secret* of the back-way (for a price).
- **Drifting Remembrances:** the warming node's woken dead — telegraphed, avoidable, parley-able.
- **Hollow Court:** faint network-touch only; **never present, never named.**

## Zone / Room List

### Zone 1: The Listening Galleries
- **Description:** flooded colonnades where the still-water pools "listen." The transition from the dry upper wards into the deep.
- **Occupants:** none by default; a Syndicate lookout possible at the Sealgate end.
- **Hazards:** flooded crossings (Athletics/Con DC 13); a rising-water cycle in two halls (DC 14 to read the cycle).
- **Secrets / Clues:** Concord Script along the lintels (C_CAR_016 fragment — language-gated).

### Zone 2: The Syndicate Drop
- **Description:** a dry upper vault the Salt Syndicate uses for relic/contraband caches.
- **Occupants:** 1-3 Syndicate runners (avoidable, bribable, or a low-stakes fight).
- **Hazards:** a tripline alarm (Perception DC 13 / Stealth DC 13).
- **Secrets / Clues:** crated "quiet-coin" relics (C-M3 demonstration); a manifest tying the trade to the Ashmarket (link to D17, the Ashmarket Undercroft).

### Zone 3: The Drowned Concourse
- **Description:** the deep wards' main flooded boulevard; the relay-channels run here, and the woken dead drift.
- **Occupants:** 1-2 drifting Remembrances (Drowned Remembrance profile, STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES); telegraphed, parley-able.
- **Hazards:** deep water (Athletics DC 14); cold (Con DC 13); a thin-touch zone near a warming relay (Con DC 14, reversible).
- **Secrets / Clues:** the relay-channel Script "listening to the door under the water in the north" (C_CAR_016 / C-M2 fragment — proof the network is continental, pointing *at the Reach*).

### Zone 4: The Archive Stair (the back-way)
- **Description:** a hidden, dry stair climbing up out of the wards into the lowest level of the Sealed Archive.
- **Occupants:** Reclaimer guards (Concord Sentinel-Scholar profile, STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES); social/avoidable first, combat last.
- **Hazards:** a Concord ward-glyph on the door (the puzzle, below); the Reclaimers themselves.
- **Secrets / Clues:** this *is* the back-route for `Q_THE_SEALED_ARCHIVE` — the deep wards' single greatest prize (access, not gold). The Archive itself holds C-M6-1/C-M9-1 (REV_005/006) but is **Act 3 gated** — reaching it early yields fragments and a locked door, not the apex.

### Zone 5: The Cistern Vault (deepest reach)
- **Description:** the lowest flooded chamber, where a warming relay-stone stands in black water and the strongest woken dead keeps it.
- **Occupants:** 1 strong drifting Remembrance (a "ward-keeper" Remembrance, ~52-60 HP; parley/rite primary).
- **Hazards:** flood-risk (the cistern fills on a slow cycle, DC 15 to read); thin-touch (Con DC 14); deep cold water.
- **Secrets / Clues:** the warming relay-stone is the clearest city proof the **network is alive and continental** (C_CAR_016 deep fragment); a refined Remembrance relic (best M3 object in the city) rests in the keeper's care.

## Exploration Features

- **Reading the warming:** an attentive player (Arcana/Religion DC 15) can tell the deep wards are a *smaller, dormant version of something far larger and to the north* — a striking, fair M2/M5 inference without any reveal.
- **Mapping the wards:** completing a map (for the Remnant or against them) is a non-combat objective with political payoff.
- **The three-faction triangle:** the player can play Remnant, Syndicate, and Hush against each other rather than fight any of them.

## Hazards and Traps

| Trap / Hazard | DC | Effect | Bypass |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flooded crossings | Athletics/Con 13-14 | Exhaustion risk, dropped gear, forced retreat | Rope; Dunk's dry routes; the Remnant's map |
| Rising-water cycle (Zones 1, 5) | DC 14-15 to read | A hall/cistern floods on a slow cycle | Time it; never seals the only exit |
| Collapsing Concord stone | Perception 13 / Dex save 14 | 3d6 bludgeoning + blocked path | Reroute; spot the stress-cracks |
| Thin-touch zones (Zones 3, 5) | Con 14 | Reversible: disadvantage on Death saves until a true rite / shrine long-rest | Don't linger; cleanse via rite |
| Syndicate alarm (Zone 2) | Perception/Stealth 13 | Brings runners / a chase | Stealth; bribe; avoid the cache |
| Archive ward-glyph (Zone 4) | see Puzzles | Forced = a warding apparition | Solve the Script; Remnant key |

No lethal mechanical traps; the danger is water, the woken dead, and the Reclaimers.

## Puzzles

**The Archive Ward-Glyph (Zone 4):**
- **Setup:** the back-way door is sealed by a Concord ward — a star-sequence in Script.
- **What the player sees:** a graven door with a ring of star-marks and a Script verse.
- **Intended solution:** read the Script (Concord Script, Comprehend Languages, or a Remnant reader) and "name the stars in the old order" (Investigation DC 14 with a rubbing).
- **Alternate valid solutions:** a Reclaimer's key (turn or trick a Remnant agent); a Mourner's knowledge of the old star-order; sheer force (Thieves' Tools/Strength DC 17 — but forcing it wakes a warding apparition).
- **Failure consequence:** forcing it rouses a warding Remembrance (one telegraphed fight, not a death-trap).
- **Costly bypass:** buy the solution from the Hush (the Bellman) — costs coin and a favor.
- **Do not reveal:** that the Archive beyond holds the deliberate-Quietfall proof — that is Act 3 and behind further gates.

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_CAR_016 (NEW, REV_002/005 fragment):** the deep wards' warming relay-Script proves the harvest network is **continental and alive**, listening "to the door under the water in the north" (points at the Reach keystone; never names it or the Court).
- **C-M3 / C_CAR_004-005:** the Syndicate quiet-coin relics and the Cistern relay relic demonstrate Remembrance-as-substance (REV_002).
- **The back-way:** physical access to the Sealed Archive (gateway to C-M6-1/C-M9-1, REV_005/006 — **Act 3 gated**).
- **Reclamation evidence:** the Remnant's secret map proves Quorrin's reach and intent.

## Boss / Climax (variable)

No single fixed boss. The climax is one of:
- **The Cistern ward-keeper Remembrance** (~52-60 HP; *chill touch, frightful presence, life-drain, summon 1 drifting dead*; **resolvable by true rite Religion DC 15 or parley Insight/Persuasion DC 15**; bound to the cistern — the player can always retreat). See STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.
- **A Reclaimer confrontation at the Archive stair** (social/avoidable first; the Reclaimers prefer to turn the player back).
- **A three-faction collision** the player triggers or defuses (no combat required).

## Adversary Profiles

Reference `../13_encounters_and_bestiary/STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Drowned Remembrance** (drifting dead; specter/wraith-like, AC 12-13, ~22-40 HP).
- **Ward-Keeper Remembrance** (cistern climax; ~52-60 HP).
- **Concord Sentinel-Scholar / Reclaimer agent** (Spy/Mage-like, AC 13-14, ~40-60 HP; Hold Person, Counterspell, Remembrance one-offs; turn-back-not-kill).
- **Salt Syndicate runner** (Thug/Bandit-like, AC 13-14).

## Noncombat Options

- **Social:** bribe/avoid the Syndicate; turn or talk past the Reclaimers; buy the back-way from the Hush; recruit Old Pater Dunk as guide.
- **Stealth:** the Brass Ferry flood-channel bypasses the upper galleries; time the water cycles; slip past the drifting dead.
- **Puzzle:** open the ward-glyph by reading the Script — no fight needed.
- **Faction:** map the wards *for* the Remnant (strings attached) to be *given* the back-way.
- **Rite:** lay the woken dead and *quiet* the deep wards (a visible win that eases the city's faint unease).

## Solo-Play Safety Valves

- **Warning signs:** cold, dimming lanterns, and "wrong" reflections precede every apparition.
- **Retreat:** the Sealgate stair up to the Sill is always reachable; the deep wards never seal the only exit.
- **Partial success:** the player can read the Script, grab a relic, or find the back-way without going all the way to the Cistern.
- **Ally:** Old Pater Dunk (guide) removes navigation danger; a Mourner contact aids rites.
- **Prep:** the tide/flood cycles are learnable; the player can scout the upper wards first.

## Treasure and Rewards

- 1-2 genuine Remembrance relics (M3; thin-touch care) incl. the refined Cistern relay relic (best city M3 object).
- Salt Syndicate cache: coin and contraband (~150-250 gp; taking it makes a Syndicate enemy).
- A portable Concord Script rubbing (C_CAR_016 fragment, carry to a reader).
- **The back-way to the Sealed Archive** — the real prize for an investigator (worth more than gold).
- A Remnant boon if the player maps the wards for them (or leverage if they steal the map).

## Consequences if Ignored

The deep wards keep warming unobserved; the Remnant keeps its hidden advantage and Archive back-way; the Syndicate keeps its drops. No mystery dead-ends — M2/M3/M6 are reachable elsewhere. But the player misses the easiest route into the Archive and an early confirmation that the crisis is continental, and the Remnant's Reclamation clock advances unopposed in the city.

## How Site Changes After Interaction

- **If mapped/read:** the player gains the city M2 fragment and possibly the back-way; the Remnant tightens security (Reclamation reacts).
- **If the dead are laid:** the deep wards quiet; a small visible easing of the city's unease (the Court will slowly counter).
- **If exposed:** the city is forced to acknowledge the ruin beneath it (a civic shock; reformers gain; the conspiracy scrambles — feeds clock C3 The City's Denial Cracks).
- **If the Syndicate cache is hit:** the Syndicate turns hostile; the Watch may take interest.

## Scaling Notes

- **L6:** fewer/weaker apparitions; simpler hazards; the Cistern keeper ~44 HP; no Reclaimer ambush.
- **L8 (intended):** as written.
- **L10:** more apparitions; a Reclaimer ambush near the Archive stair; the Cistern keeper ~64 HP with a summon each round; a flooded lower hall guarding a better relic. Never a damage-race; always a non-combat path.
- **Solo PC:** single foes or pairs only; the Reclaimers and Syndicate prefer to turn back/bribe; the dead are bound and avoidable.
