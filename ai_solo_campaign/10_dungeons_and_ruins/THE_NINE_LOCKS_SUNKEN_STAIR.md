## AI Use

Load for Ring 2 play in the Verdance Reaches — the player's **first step into the wider continent** past Caradril: a **Concord lock-and-relay vault beneath the great staircase of locks** on the Verdance, where the river-corridor's old works lie half-flooded. A mid-tier (L8-12) **river-node/relay site** delivering **REV_001/005 corroboration** that the harvest network runs *up the Verdance into the continent's interior* — the first proof the network is bigger than the NW cluster. **CRITICAL guardrail: a corridor node, NOT the keystone, NOT the Concord Deep, NOT the Court** — it points *toward* the network's continental spread, never to the heart (which is under Hollowmere, behind the player). Mirrors the Sunken Tollhouse / Greenward Toll-Station at Ring-2 tier. Factor-General Crane and River-Captain Mossa Drenn are the social frame.

## One-Sentence Identity

The flooded Concord lock-works and relay-vault beneath the Verdance's great staircase of nine locks, where the river-corridor's old infrastructure proves the harvest network ran inland up the river — the first node the player finds *outside* the home frontier.

## Map Placement

Far-continent (Ring 2), SE of Caradril, up the **Verdance**, at **the Nine Locks** by the river-town **Marrowfen Stair** (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 40-46, Y 42-48). The Verdance Reaches are the *transitional* corridor — frontier roughness giving way to the older, richer interior. The player's first Ring-2 site.

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 8-12. Solo danger: moderate-high.** A flooded node-crawl: the threats are the lock-works' water (flooding chambers, currents), the drifting dead drawn to the node, and corridor-politics (the Ledger's inland directorate, petty lords, bargefolk). Telegraphed; retreat-rich (Marrowfen Stair is the haven); a bargefolk guide negates most water-risk. Not required; corroboration and the first Ring-2 step.

## Purpose in the World

A mid-tier site that (1) proves the harvest network runs *inland up the Verdance* (REV_001/005 corroboration — the network is continental, spreading from the NW), (2) makes the Verdance Reaches a real Ring-2 destination and the gateway to the wider continent, (3) offers a flooded relay-node mirroring the home-region nodes at higher tier, and (4) shows the crisis *fresher and less denied* here than in the long-suffering Reach — **without the keystone or the Court.** It is the player's bridge from the home frontier into the continent.

## Access / Entrance Routes

Full physical approach and entrance description: `vael/orrun/sites/nine-locks-sunken-stair`. Campaign-specific access routes:

1. **With the bargefolk (the river way):** River-Captain Mossa Drenn or the lock-keepers know the flooded works (negates water-risk).
2. **By the Ledger's leave (the corridor way):** Factor-General Wessel Crane's inland directorate controls the locks; a charter grants access (and watchers).
3. **At low water (the seasonal way):** the drained season bares more of the works.
4. **By stealth (the quiet way):** slip into the lock-vault past the Ledger's lock-keepers.

## Current Occupants

- **Drifting dead drawn to the node:** the corridor's leaked Remembrance, fresher here.
- **A node relay-keeper dead / a ward-glyph lock:** the vault's defenses.
- **Ledger lock-keepers & corridor-guards:** Crane's inland directorate's men.
- **(DM) Remnant scholars:** an inland chapter (Sub-Lector Hadwin Vael) mapping the corridor's nodes.
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** A corridor node; the heart is under Hollowmere, behind the player.

## Factions Involved

- **Cinder Ledger (Crane's inland directorate):** control the locks and the corridor trade.
- **Concord Remnant (inland chapter, Hadwin Vael):** map the corridor's old works.
- **Reachward Compact / petty lords:** the corridor's patchwork authority (toll-politics).
- **Mourners / bargefolk:** the river-folk who *notice* the freshening drift.

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The Lock-Stair (the great works)
- **Description:** the staircase of nine locks; the Ledger's lock-keepers, barge-traffic, corridor-politics.
- **DC / hazards:** social (lock-keepers; charter-politics); slick lock-walls (Dex DC 13).
- **Secrets/Clues:** the locks are *Concord-built* — the old empire's works run up the river (REV_005 shape).

### Zone 2: The Flooded Sub-Locks (the descent)
- **Description:** the half-drowned chambers beneath the locks; old Concord conduits.
- **DC / hazards:** flooding/currents (Athletics DC 14; time the sluice-cycle, Survival DC 15); zero-vis.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the drifting dead gather here, fresher than in the Reach (REV_001).

### Zone 3: The Relay-Vault (the node-proof)
- **Description:** the sealed Concord relay-chamber beneath the locks; the node's heart.
- **DC / hazards:** a ward-glyph lock (Script or Investigation DC 15); a relay-keeper dead.
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_VR_001 (REV_001/005):** the relay-Script shows the node *channels leaked Remembrance basin-ward, down-river toward the NW* — proof the network runs inland up the Verdance and *points home*, not onward. **Capped: corroborates the network's continental spread + its NW-ward pull; NEVER the keystone/Court/Concord Deep.**

### Zone 4: The Drowned Conduit (the dread-gate)
- **Description:** the conduit running down-river out of the vault, vanishing under the Verdance.
- **DC / hazards:** deep water; the strongest drifting dead.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the conduit runs *toward the NW frontier* and floods out — **a DM gate: it confirms the direction (home), but is impassable and ends in flood/silt. It is NOT a tunnel to the keystone; it only points the way the player already came.**

### Zone 5: The Lock-Keepers' Cache (treasure)
- **Description:** the old Concord lock-wardens' stores; salvage and corridor-relics.
- **DC / hazards:** a flooded lock (Thieves' Tools DC 15); a ward.
- **Secrets/Clues:** Concord relics and "old glass"; the cache is a closed strongroom (no passage onward).

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_VR_001 (REV_001/005):** the corridor relay proves the network runs inland and pulls NW-ward (toward home).
- The bargefolk's fresher witness of the drift (oblique REV_001, less-denied here).
- **Hard cap:** the Nine Locks NEVER deliver REV_006/007, the keystone, the harvest mechanism, or the Concord Deep. The node *points down-river toward the NW* — it confirms the heart is *behind* the player, never that it is here or further on.

## Puzzles

**The Sluice-Cycle & the Ward (timing + Script):** the flooded works answer to the lock-cycle; the vault answers to a Concord ward.
- **Solutions:** time the sluice-cycle (Survival DC 15; bargefolk negate it); read the ward-glyph (Concord Script DC 15; a Remnant reader); quiet the relay-keeper by closing-word/rite (Religion/Script DC 15).
- **Failure:** a flooding chamber drives you out (a built-in valve) or rouses the dead (telegraphed), not a dead end.
- **Costly bypass:** let the Ledger or the Remnant open the vault (then contest it).
- **Accept clever solutions:** any valid timing/Script/rite approach passes.

## Noncombat Options

- **Bargefolk guide / sluice-timing:** brave the works safely.
- **Script / closing-word:** clear the vault and quiet the dead without a fight.
- **Corridor diplomacy:** play the Ledger directorate, petty lords, and the Remnant chapter off each other.
- **Stealth:** slip the lock-keepers.

## Combat Encounters

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Corridor drifting dead** — Drowned/Drifting Remembrance (AC 13, ~30-48 HP; rite/parley-able; bound to the node/water).
- **A relay-keeper dead** — Ward/Choir-Keeper Remembrance (AC 13, ~52-64 HP; bound to the relay; closing-word out).
- **Ledger lock-keepers / corridor-guards** — Guard/Spy-like living foes (catch-and-eject; avoidable).
- Far NPCs (Crane, Mossa Drenn, Hadwin Vael) per `VERDANCE_REACHES_NPCS.md` — never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **Haven:** Marrowfen Stair is the always-open retreat.
- **The flooding self-limits** the descent (a built-in valve).
- **Dead are bound** to the node/water — surfacing escapes.

## Treasure and Rewards

- Concord relics, "old glass," and corridor-salvage (tier-2/3; a possible water-warded minor item).
- Far allies (Mossa Drenn's barge; a turned lock-keeper) or a Ledger-directorate rival.
- REV_001/005 corroboration (C_VR_001) — and the directional proof that the heart lies *behind*, in the NW (a key strategic clue).

## Consequences if Ignored

The Ledger's inland directorate runs the corridor's old works unwatched; the freshening drift worsens up the Verdance as the network spreads; the Remnant inland chapter strips the relay-Script. The Ring-2 gateway stays unopened. The REV_001/005 corroboration (and the homeward-pointing direction) passes uncollected (it stays reachable in the NW — nothing dead-ends).

## Scaling Notes

- **L8:** softer dead (~30 HP), simpler ward (DC 14); emphasize the river-works and corridor-politics.
- **L10 (intended):** as written.
- **L12:** stronger relay-keeper (~64 HP), the drowned conduit and cache in play, full corridor-faction tension. **The conduit stays a homeward-pointing dead-end regardless — never a path onward to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the guide/timing/Script outs are the intended solo path; the dead are bound and the flooding self-limits.
