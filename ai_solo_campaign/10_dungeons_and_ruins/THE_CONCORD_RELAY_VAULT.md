## AI Use

Load when the player reaches the buried Concord **relay/signal station** high in the Sunder Heights — a mid-tier (L7-10) Concord installation between the Deep Adit (D05) and Caradril's Sealed Archive in clue-weight. It is the Reach's strongest *node-network* clue site short of the keystone: the place where the harvest network's *communication and command* layer is readable, delivering rich M6/M9 **fragments** (Act 3 paced). **Guardrails:** fragments and a guardian — never the apex, never the Court. Warden-sealed and Remnant-contested. Use dungeon mechanical standards; high but telegraphed solo danger.

## One-Sentence Identity

A buried Concord signal-station beneath the high Heights, where the network's command-Script still waits to be read — and a Warden-set seal and a wakeful Concord guardian stand between the player and the clearest in-Reach proof of the order's true work.

## Level Range and Solo Danger Rating

**Levels 7-10. Solo danger: high (telegraphed).** A wakeful Concord construct-guardian, a sealed lower vault, the thinning aura, Heights cold, and a possible Remnant race make this a real test — but every threat is signposted, the guardian is bound to the vault, and retreat up the adit is always open. Not a low-level dive; the Heights approach and the seal warn the player off.

## Location

High in the Sunder Heights above Harrowgast, reached past the worked adits and the Roofless Hall (a known Heights landmark, `SUNDER_HEIGHTS_SITES.md`). Deeper and higher than the Deep Adit; a true Concord vault rather than a mine-cut node.

## Purpose in the World

The Reach's "command-layer" clue site. Where the Deep Adit shows the *harvesting* and the Whispering Cairn shows the *census*, the Relay-Vault shows the *network's command and the Quietfall's sever-order* — the richest M6/M9 fragments reachable in the Reach (the full proof still living in Caradril's Sealed Archive). It gives an Act-3 player a Reach-side path to REV_005/006 so they are never forced to Caradril, and it is a prime Remnant-contested prize.

## History (Player-Safe)

A sealed Concord cairn-vault high in the Heights, marked with the "dead language," shunned and rumored cursed; the Wardens are said to "keep" it. Scavengers and Remnant scholars covet it; none have opened it.

## History (DM-Only)

A Concord **relay/signal station** — part of the network's command layer, where the harvest's tallies were coordinated and the great shrines "spoke" to one another. It holds the readable order to *sever* the network — the Quietfall's command, in Concord Script (a rich M9 fragment). After the Quietfall the Wardens (seeded by survivors) **sealed it** to keep the network quiet; the resident seal is a Warden work, and a Concord **guardian-construct** (an animated relay-warden of stone and stored Remembrance) still stands its post. As the harvest reawakens, the vault faintly warms and the guardian stirs. The Remnant has located it and is trying to open it for proof and command-knowledge (a Reclamation apex-prize). It is a secondary node and a command-node — **not** the keystone, **not** the Court's seat.

## First Impression

The adit ends at a sealed bronze-and-stone door deep in the cold rock, graven with star-marks and a Warden's later seal laid over the Concord work. Beyond — when it opens — a vaulted chamber of fitted stone, Script running floor to ceiling, and at its center a figure of stone and cold light that turns its head toward you. The air is the thin, wrong cold of a leaking node.

## Access / Entrance Routes

1. **Through the worked adits (Harrowgast):** the standard way, past the mine-workings and the Roofless Hall.
2. **The Remnant's dig (contested):** the Remnant crew may already hold the approach (a social/stealth gate, like the Deep Adit).
3. **A Warden's leave (the clean way):** Warden Othetha or a Heights Warden can grant access and aid against the guardian (the safest, ally-gated route).

## Current Occupants and Factions

- **The Relay-Warden (Concord guardian-construct):** the vault's wakeful guardian (boss).
- **Concord Remnant crew:** racing to open and read the vault (Veska Dunn or a senior Reclaimer).
- **Ashen Wardens:** the seal-keepers; allies or obstacles depending on the player's standing.
- **A bound Heights Remembrance or two:** the relay's "operators," drifting (parley/rite-able).
- **Hollow Court:** network-touch only; **never present, never named.**

## Zone / Room List

### Zone 1: The Sealed Approach
- **Description:** the adit's end at the Warden-sealed Concord door; the social/seal gate.
- **Occupants:** a Remnant forward camp possible (Veska Dunn + bravos).
- **Hazards:** the seal (the puzzle/obstacle); Heights cold (Con DC 13).
- **Secrets / Clues:** the Warden seal itself is a clue — *the Wardens deliberately sealed Concord works* (M6/M9 fragment shape; REV_006).

### Zone 2: The Operators' Hall
- **Description:** the upper vault where the relay's "operators" worked; Script-graven; a drifting bound Remembrance.
- **Occupants:** 1-2 bound Heights Remembrances (relay operators; parley/rite-able).
- **Hazards:** thinning aura (Con DC 14, thin-touch reversible); collapsing stone (Perception/Dex 14, 3d6).
- **Secrets / Clues:** C-M6 fragment Script: "the shrines spoke; the dead were tallied and shared" (the network's command layer, REV_005).

### Zone 3: The Signal-Choir (the warming core)
- **Description:** the relay's central mechanism — a "choir" of relay-stones, faintly warming and humming.
- **Occupants:** the Relay-Warden guardian (boss) stands here.
- **Hazards:** intense thinning aura (Con DC 15); the guardian; dread (Wis DC 14).
- **Secrets / Clues:** the warming core proves the network is *coordinated and reawakening* (M2 apex-of-Reach fragment); the command-Script is read here.

### Zone 4: The Sever-Vault (the Quietfall command)
- **Description:** a sealed inner vault holding the Concord's *severance order* — the readable command that staged the Quietfall.
- **Occupants:** none (the guardian defends the approach); a final Script-plate.
- **Hazards:** the inner seal (a harder Script puzzle); thin-touch (Con DC 15).
- **Secrets / Clues:** **C-M9 fragment (rich):** the network was *deliberately severed* on command — the clearest in-Reach proof of the deliberate Quietfall (REV_006, Act 3 gated). A portable rubbing (the prize).

### Zone 5: The Deep Conduit (off-limits)
- **Description:** a sealed shaft descending toward the network's deeper arteries.
- **Hazards:** explicitly endgame-tier; telegraphed as beyond this tier (a gate, not a trap).
- **Secrets / Clues:** none reachable; foreshadows the network's depth (never the keystone/Court).

## Exploration Features

- **Reading the command layer:** the vault is a *reading* dungeon at heart — the Script is the payoff.
- **The Warden seal as testimony:** noticing *who sealed it and why* (Investigation/History DC 15) is itself a REV_006 fragment.
- **The Remnant race:** a recurring rival dynamic (beat them to the reading, or contest it).

## Puzzles

**The Warden Seal (Zone 1) and the Sever-Vault Seal (Zone 4):** two Concord/Warden lock-glyph puzzles.
- **Setup:** star-sequence Script seals; the inner one harder.
- **Intended solution:** read the Script (Concord Script / Comprehend Languages / a Remnant or Warden reader); name the order (Investigation DC 14 outer / DC 15-16 inner with a rubbing).
- **Alternates:** a Warden's leave (Othetha) opens the outer seal cleanly and aids within; a Mourner's old star-lore; force (Strength/Thieves' Tools DC 17-18 — wakes the guardian hostile and risks a thin-touch burst).
- **Failure consequence:** forcing wakes the Relay-Warden as a hostile rather than a postured guardian.
- **Costly bypass:** let the Remnant open it (then contest the reading).
- **Do not reveal:** the deeper conduit's destination (the keystone) or the Court.

## Secrets and Clues

- **C-M6 fragment / C_CAR_011 analog (REV_005):** the network's command layer — "the shrines spoke; the dead were tallied and shared."
- **C-M9 fragment (rich) (REV_006):** the readable *severance order* — proof the Quietfall was deliberate (Act 3 gated; the clearest in-Reach M9).
- **NEW: C_SR_041 (REV_005/006, fragment):** the Warden seal + command-Script together prove the order was *severed on purpose by people who knew what it was* (a strong inference clue; Act 3; still not the Court's *survival*).
- **The warming core:** M2 fragment (network reawakening, coordinated).

## Boss / Climax Mechanics

**The Relay-Warden** (Concord guardian-construct; see STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES — Concord Construct-Guardian).
- **It is a postured guardian, not a berserker:** it challenges intruders and demands the "watchword." Resolutions:
  - **Watchword (best):** read the Script and speak the Concord challenge-response (Concord Script DC 15) — it stands aside and the player may read freely (non-combat).
  - **Warden's leave:** a Warden ally can "stand it down" (Religion/faction DC 14).
  - **Combat:** AC 17, ~95-120 HP (L8-10), resistant to nonmagical damage, *slam, relay-blast (force/necrotic, recharge), thinning aura*; bound to the Signal-Choir — **the player can always retreat up the adit**; it does not pursue. Tough but beatable; morale: it stops when the intruder leaves or speaks the watchword.
- **No cheap death:** telegraphed, bound, retreat-rich, with two non-combat outs.

## Adversary Profiles

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Concord Construct-Guardian (Relay-Warden)** — AC 17, ~95-120 HP, construct, relay-blast, thinning aura; watchword/leave non-combat outs.
- **Bound Heights Remembrance (relay operator)** — wraith-like, AC 13, ~36-45 HP; parley/rite-able.
- Remnant crew (Veska Dunn — Scout/Mage-lite; bravos — Thug/Scout-like; **recurring, never cheap-killed**).

## Noncombat Options

- **Script-reading:** the whole vault can be cleared by reading (watchword + seals) without a fight.
- **Warden alliance:** Othetha's leave opens the seal and stands down the guardian.
- **Faction:** beat or partner with the Remnant for the reading.
- **Rite:** lay the relay operators.

## Solo-Play Safety Valves

- **Warning signs:** the Warden seal, the cold, the dread, and the guardian's challenge all precede danger.
- **Retreat:** the adit out is always open; the guardian is bound.
- **Ally:** a Warden's leave is the clean, safe route.
- **Partial success:** read the Operators' Hall Script and leave without facing the guardian.
- **Telegraphing:** the Deep Conduit is signposted as endgame-tier.

## Treasure and Rewards

- The **severance-order rubbing** (the prize — a rich, portable M9 fragment; Act 3).
- Concord salvage and a command-relay relic (high-tier M3; thin-touch care).
- A Warden's seal-token / gratitude (if allied); the Remnant pays heavily for the reading (a choice).
- A Concord Script command-primer (aids future Script reading).

## Consequences if Ignored

The Remnant opens and reads the vault, carrying the command-knowledge and the severance-order to Quorrin (a major Reclamation advance toward Act 3-4) and possibly learning to *command* the reawakening network. The Wardens lose a sealed node. The Reach's clearest M9 fragment passes to a faction. (REV_005/006 remain reachable in Caradril — nothing dead-ends.)

## How Site Changes After Interaction

- **Read/allied with Wardens:** the player gains the M9 fragment; the Wardens trust ↑; the seal is re-set.
- **Remnant beaten to it / contested:** `FACTION_STATE.md` (Remnant attitude), `WORLD_CLOCKS.md` (Reclamation).
- **Guardian destroyed vs. answered:** answering quiets the node (a small easing); destroying it leaves the warming core unguarded (the Remnant exploits this).
