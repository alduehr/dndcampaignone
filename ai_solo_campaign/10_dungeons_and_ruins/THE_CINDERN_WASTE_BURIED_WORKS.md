## AI Use

Load for far/late high-tier play in the Emberfell Theocracy: the **buried Concord works beneath the Cindern Waste**, the ash-badland the fire-priests forbid, where old infrastructure lies drowned in volcanic ash and the Ashfast guards its own pre-Concord-adjacent secret. A high-tier (L13-16) **ash-environmental ruin** delivering far **REV_005/006 corroboration** through buried Concord works the theocracy has built its faith *atop* without understanding. **CRITICAL guardrail: buried peripheral Concord works under a separate southern theocracy, NOT the keystone, NOT the Concord Deep, NOT the Court** — the Ashfast's "secret" resonates (a faith guarding old works) but is its own thing; the live machine is under Hollowmere. Complements `THE_EMBERFELL_CALDERA_DESCENT.md` (the caldera; this is the ash-waste). Ash-Hierophant Vole Cindra forbids it; Ember-Sage Mira Cindra (heretic) is the key.

## One-Sentence Identity

The Concord works drowned in volcanic ash beneath the forbidden Cindern Waste, over which the fire-theocracy has built its certain faith without ever knowing what sleeps below — a buried ruin the priests forbid, the heretics covet, and the ash itself defends.

## Map Placement

Far-continent, SE-central, in the **Cindern Waste** ash-badland in the **Emberfells'** rain-shadow (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 66-74, Y 64-72; the world-map "Cindern Waste" forbidden zone). Reached from **Cinderhold** (the volcanic mining town) and the **Forbidden Waste's** edge. A conceptually alien late-game fire-theocracy guarding its own buried secret.

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 13-16. Solo danger: very high.** The ash-waste is the chief threat — choking ash-storms, heat, unstable ground over buried voids, and the theocracy's ban (the Pyre-Wardens hunt waste-trespassers) — alongside the buried works' guardians. Telegraphed (the priests forbid it loudly); retreat-rich only if prepared (Cinderhold is the haven); a heretic-guide or miner negates much waste-risk. Gear- and faction-gated, not ambush-gated. Not required; high corroboration and a forbidden secret.

## Purpose in the World

A high-tier far-corroboration site that (1) proves the Concord's works (and harvest) reached the volcanic south, *buried and forgotten beneath a later faith* (REV_005/006 corroboration), (2) gives the Ashfast a real internal fracture (the orthodox ban vs. the heretics' hunger to dig) the player can exploit, (3) offers an ash-environmental ruin distinct from the caldera-descent, and (4) deepens the motif of *faiths built atop buried truths* (the Ashfast as a thematic mirror of the Wardens/Concord) — **without the keystone or the Court.**

## Access / Entrance Routes

Full physical approach and entrance description: [`Locations/Orrun/06_sites/FAR_CONTINENT_SITES.md`](../../Locations/Orrun/06_sites/FAR_CONTINENT_SITES.md) → **The Cindern Waste Buried Works**. Campaign-specific access routes:

1. **With Ember-Sage Mira Cindra (the heretic's way):** the heretic-scholar knows the safe waste-routes and craves the works (the key access; defies the Pyre).
2. **By a miner's tunnel (the Cinderhold way):** Cinder-Master Doss Ashfast's deep workings break into the buried Concord voids.
3. **Against the ban (the trespasser's way):** brave the forbidden waste and the Pyre-Wardens directly.
4. **Alone in the ash (the suicide way):** the ash-storms and unstable ground kill the unprepared — heavily telegraphed against.

## Current Occupants

- **Ash-drowned Concord guardians & dead:** buried, ash-locked, intact.
- **Pyre-Wardens (Sef Embren's enforcers):** hunt waste-trespassers; enforce the ban.
- **Heretic-diggers (Mira Cindra's circle):** seek the buried works in secret.
- **(DM) Gravecaller / Remnant interest:** drawn by the buried works and the stirring dead.
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** Buried peripheral works under a separate theocracy; the live machine is under Hollowmere.

## Factions Involved

- **Emberfell Theocracy (Vole Cindra; Pyre-Warden Sef Embren):** forbid and guard the waste.
- **Heretics (Ember-Sage Mira Cindra):** covet the buried works (the player's lever).
- **Cinder-Master Doss Ashfast:** his mines break into the works (a trade/access angle).
- **(DM) Concord Remnant / Gravecallers:** want the buried records / the stirring dead.

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The Forbidden Waste-Edge (the ash-approach)
- **Description:** the Cindern Waste's edge; ash-dunes, Pyre-Warden patrols, the ban's wards.
- **DC / hazards:** ash-storm (Con DC 15); unstable ground (Dex DC 14); Pyre-Warden patrols.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the priests forbid the waste — they *guard* something they don't understand (REV_005 shape).

### Zone 2: The Ash-Drowned Entry (into the works)
- **Description:** a Concord structure half-swallowed by ash; a miner's break or a heretic's dig.
- **DC / hazards:** ash-collapse (Perception/Dex DC 15); choke-ash (Con DC 14); the buried dark.
- **Secrets/Clues:** in-situ Concord Script under the ash (REV_005 fragment, Act 3+ gate).

### Zone 3: The Buried Hall (the forbidden proof)
- **Description:** the works' central hall, ash-preserved; the documentary heart.
- **DC / hazards:** ash-locked guardians; Script-traps; the heat.
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_FC_016 (REV_005/006):** the buried works' record — the Concord harvested and the Quietfall was deliberate, in works the theocracy built its faith *atop* unknowing. **Capped: far corroboration of the harvest + planned fall; NEVER the keystone, the live machine, or the Court.**

### Zone 4: The Ember-Heart (guardian-climax)
- **Description:** the works' sealed inner chamber, kept by an ash-and-fire-touched Concord guardian.
- **DC / hazards:** the apex guardian (watchword or a hard fight); heat; ash.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the relay-Script (a far REV_006 fragment) — and a DM gate: the chamber severs into ash and rock, NOT a passage to the keystone or the Concord Deep.

### Zone 5: The Deep Ash-Vaults (treasure)
- **Description:** the deepest ash-drowned vaults; intact Concord relics, the worst air and heat.
- **DC / hazards:** ash/heat/collapse; the strongest guardians.
- **Secrets/Clues:** Concord artifacts and a final REV_005/006 record; the vaults bottom out in ash and bedrock (no downward passage).

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_FC_016 (REV_005/006):** the buried works' harvest + deliberate-Quietfall record.
- The Ashfast's *unknowing* guardianship (the thematic mirror — a faith atop a buried truth).
- **Hard cap:** the Buried Works NEVER deliver REV_007, the keystone, the live machine, or the Court. The Concord reached the south *peripherally*; the Ashfast's secret is its own. The heart is under Hollowmere.

## Puzzles

**The Ash & the Watchword (survival + Script + faction):** the waste answers to survival-craft; the works to a Concord watchword; the ban to the theocracy's fracture.
- **Solutions:** a heretic- or miner-guide negates the ash/navigation; read the Script / name the watchword (Concord Script DC 16; Mira Cindra reads it); exploit the orthodox/heretic split to gain access.
- **Failure:** an ash-storm drives you out (a telegraphed valve) or a guardian/patrol engages (a hard fight), not a cheap death — the ash is signposted as the killer.
- **Costly bypass:** let the heretics or the Remnant dig it open (then contest the records).
- **Do not reveal:** any "the heart is here" implication — these are buried peripheral works.

## Noncombat Options

- **A heretic/miner guide:** negates most ash/navigation risk.
- **Watchword / Script:** clear the works without a fight.
- **Faction-play:** exploit the orthodox-vs-heretic fracture for access.
- **Stealth:** slip the Pyre-Wardens and the guardians.

## Combat Encounters

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Ash/fire-touched Concord guardians** — Concord Construct-Guardian + Caldera-Guardian traits (AC 17-18, ~120-160 HP; immune fire; **watchword stands them down**; bound to the works).
- **Ash-locked frozen-in-ash dead** — Drowned/Drifting Remembrance, ash-preserved (AC 13, ~52-64 HP; rite/parley-able; bound).
- **Pyre-Wardens** — Veteran/Priest-like theocracy enforcers (AC 16, ~60-90 HP; avoidable; faction-negotiable).
- Far NPCs (Vole Cindra, Sef Embren, Mira Cindra, Doss Ashfast) per `EMBERFELL_THEOCRACY_NPCS.md` — never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **Haven:** Cinderhold is the retreat (the ash-waste itself is hostile).
- **Guardians and dead are bound** to the works — leaving escapes them.
- **Ash-storms self-limit** the delve (a telegraphed valve) — but prep against the ash is mandatory.

## Treasure and Rewards

- Intact ash-preserved Concord artifacts (tier-3/4; a great far-southern prize).
- Far REV_005/006 corroboration (C_FC_016) and the Ashfast's secret.
- The heretics' alliance (Mira Cindra) or a powerful theocratic enemy (the Pyre).
- A foothold in the fractured theocracy.

## Consequences if Ignored

The heretics dig the works open without guidance and rouse the guardians (a far catastrophe), or the Pyre buries the secret deeper and burns the heretics; the Remnant or Ledger eventually reaches the intact relics. The far REV_005/006 corroboration passes uncollected (it stays reachable in the NW and Caradril — nothing dead-ends).

## Scaling Notes

- **L13:** soften guardians (~120 HP, no first-round blast); the ash still mandatory-prep; emphasize the buried archive and faction-fracture.
- **L14-15 (intended):** as written.
- **L16:** apex guardians (~160 HP + summons), the deep ash-vaults open, full Pyre/heretic convergence. **The works stay buried peripheral works regardless — never a path to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the guide/watchword/faction outs are the intended solo path; the ash is the gate, guardians are bound and watchword-able. **Mandatory ash-prep is the explicit safety requirement — telegraph it before the player commits.**
