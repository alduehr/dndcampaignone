## AI Use

Load for far-continent / Ring 2+ play in the Saltmere Reaches: the **submerged ruins of the drowned salt-towns** the receding inland sea reveals, dived by Delver Oss and forbidden by the salt-clans. A high-tier (L11-15) **environmental ruin-dive** that delivers a far **pre-Concord echo** of catastrophe and the troubled dead (REV_001/005 corroboration through an *older, separate* fall). **CRITICAL guardrail: the drowned towns are pre-Concord — older than the harvest, NOT the keystone, NOT the Concord Deep, NOT the Court's seat.** They resonate with the theme (the dead went down, an older drowning) without being it. Corroboration and treasure, never the apex.

## One-Sentence Identity

The salt-crusted, half-submerged ruins of towns that "went down" in an older fall the salt-clans hold sacred, where the receding brackish sea bares drowned streets, salt-preserved dead, and a catastrophe older than the Concord.

## Map Placement

Far-continent, S-central interior, in the shrinking **Saltmere** inland sea, ringed by the **Bonepan Flats** and the **Ghostmark Range** (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 55-62, Y 70-78). Reached from **Brackhold** (the largest surviving salt-clan hold) and the receding lake-edge. Far from the NW campaign cluster — a Ring 2+ horizon site.

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 11-15. Solo danger: high.** The chief threats are the dive itself (cold brine, salt-collapse, lost footing on drowned streets), the salt-preserved dead that stir worse of late, and the clan/Gravecaller/Ledger tensions on the surface — not a single boss. Telegraphed and retreat-rich (Brackhold and the lake-edge camps are the havens); a guide (Delver Oss) negates most navigation risk. Not campaign-required; corroboration and treasure.

## Purpose in the World

A late-game far-corroboration and treasure site that (1) proves catastrophe and the troubled dead are **older and wider than the Concord** (REV_001/005 echo via an independent pre-Concord fall), (2) gives the salt-clans and the player a genuine reason to enter the forbidden water (loot, lore, a lost clan-kin), (3) offers a distinctive cold-brine ruin-dive, and (4) deepens the "the world has drowned before" motif — **without ever implying the harvest-heart or the Court is here.**

## Access / Entrance Routes

1. **With Delver Oss (the diver's way):** hire the reviled scavenger; he knows the safe drowned streets (negates most navigation/collapse risk; angers the clans).
2. **By the clans' leave (the sacred way):** earn Matriarch Bryd Saltmere's grudging permission to recover a lost clan-kin or settle the stirring dead — slower, safer socially.
3. **At low water (the seasonal way):** the worst-receded season bares the most ruin but rouses the most dead.
4. **Alone over the flats (the reckless way):** the most dangerous approach; the Bonepan Flats' mirage-country and salt-collapse claim the unguided.

## Current Occupants

- **Salt-preserved drowned dead:** brine-cured Remembrance, stirring more than they have in a century.
- **Delver Oss and rival scavengers:** looting the drowned towns; a flashpoint with the clans.
- **Cinder Ledger salt-and-relic buyers:** quietly purchasing what the divers raise (an oblique REV_002 thread).
- **(DM) Gravecaller-adjacent interest:** the stirring drowned dead draw subtle cult attention Bryd would violently reject.
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** This is an older, separate fall; the live machine is under Hollowmere.

## Factions Involved

- **Salt-clans (Bryd Saltmere):** forbid the dive; hold the drowned dead sacred.
- **Independent scavengers (Oss):** loot the ruins; mistrusted.
- **Cinder Ledger:** buys the salt-cured relics raised here.
- **(DM) Gravecallers:** a quiet, deniable toehold drawn by the stirring dead.

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The Lake-Edge and Bared Streets
- **Description:** the receded shoreline where drowned streets emerge from the salt-crust; scavenger camps and clan-watchers.
- **DC / hazards:** salt-collapse footing (Dex/Athletics DC 14); social friction (clan-watchers vs. scavengers).
- **Secrets/Clues:** the *scale* of the drowning — a whole town, salt-preserved (REV_001/005 echo shape).

### Zone 2: The Brine-Drowned Concourse
- **Description:** the main street, half-flooded with brackish water; salt-stiffened doors and stalls.
- **DC / hazards:** cold-brine immersion (Con DC 14 / exhaustion); zero-visibility silt (Perception disadvantage).
- **Secrets/Clues:** salt-cured household dead, posed as in the moment of drowning.

### Zone 3: The Salt-Choir (the gathered dead)
- **Description:** a drowned temple-square where the town's dead have "gathered," facing inward — the eerie heart of the dive.
- **DC / hazards:** the stirring dead (combat or rite); a Frightful regard (Wis DC 15).
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_SR2_001 (REV_001/005 echo):** the drowned dead *gathered and could not pass* in the old fall — a pre-Concord mirror of the basin's pull, salt-cured and centuries old. **Capped: corroborates the theme; NEVER the keystone/harvest/Court.**

### Zone 4: The Clan-Vaults (treasure)
- **Description:** the old salt-merchants' sunken strongrooms; brine-preserved goods, salt-relics, lost clan-heirlooms.
- **DC / hazards:** flooded locks (Thieves' Tools DC 16); a vault-bound salt-cured guardian-dead.
- **Secrets/Clues:** salt-cured "old glass" (a far REV_002 relic-source, oblique); a lost clan-heirloom (the social-quest prize).

### Zone 5: The Deep Drowned Quarter (the dead end down)
- **Description:** the deepest, never-receded streets — pitch brine, the oldest dead, the best salvage.
- **DC / hazards:** deep cold-brine (Con DC 15); the strongest salt-cured dead.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the oldest fall's record (carved clan-stones, pre-Concord) **and an explicit DM gate: the quarter bottoms out in silt and salt-rock, NOT a passage downward to anything. The drowned towns are a closed older ruin by design — the live machine is under Hollowmere.**

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_SR2_001 (REV_001/005 echo):** the gathered, unable-to-pass drowned dead — a pre-Concord mirror of the harvest's pull.
- Salt-cured relics raised here feed the far Ledger relic-trade (oblique REV_002).
- **Hard cap:** the drowned towns NEVER deliver REV_006/007, the keystone, the harvest mechanism, or the Concord Deep. The "going-down" is an *older, separate* catastrophe-myth.

## Puzzles

**The Sacred-Ground problem (social/navigation, not a lock):** the clans forbid the dive; the dead resent the disturbance.
- **Solutions:** earn Bryd's leave (recover a lost clan-kin / settle the dead — Persuasion/Insight DC 15-16); dive with Oss against the clans (faster, costs clan-standing); reverence the gathered dead to pass the Salt-Choir (Religion/Insight DC 15).
- **Failure:** the clans bar the lake; the gathered dead rouse (a telegraphed high-tier encounter).
- **Costly bypass:** pay Oss richly; accept the clan-feud.
- **Do not reveal:** any "the heart is here" implication — the drowned towns are an older, closed fall.

## Noncombat Options

- **The clans' leave / reverence:** dive with permission and lay the dead by rite (no fight).
- **A guide (Oss):** negates most dive and navigation risk.
- **Recovery quest:** frame the dive as recovering a lost clan-kin (the clan-honored path).
- **Stealth:** dive at low water, avoid both clans and dead.

## Combat Encounters

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Salt-cured drowned dead** — Drowned/Drifting Remembrance, far-scaled (AC 13, ~40-60 HP at this tier; rite/parley-able; bound to the drowned streets).
- **A vault-bound salt-guardian dead** — Ward-Keeper Remembrance, scaled (AC 13, ~64-72 HP; bound to the clan-vault; closing-reverence out).
- Surface NPCs (Bryd Saltmere, Delver Oss) per `SALTMERE_REACHES_NPCS.md` — far powers, never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **Havens:** Brackhold and the lake-edge camps are always-open retreats.
- **The dive self-limits:** cold and air force the player out (a built-in valve).
- **Bound dead cannot leave the drowned streets** — surfacing always escapes.

## Treasure and Rewards

- Salt-cured "old glass" relics (far REV_002 source) and brine-preserved valuables.
- Lost clan-heirlooms (the clan-honored social reward; Bryd's gratitude/alliance).
- Tier-3 salvage and a possible salt-warded minor item (see `14_treasure_and_artifacts/` when built).
- Far lore: the "going-down" pre-Concord catastrophe (REV_001/005 echo).

## Consequences if Ignored

The drowned dead stir worse; a delver (Oss or a rival) wakes a drowned-town the clans can't put back, and Brackhold loses another town to the going-down's superstition and the literal stirring; the Ledger quietly strips the salt-relics. The far REV_001/005 echo passes uncollected (it remains reachable in the NW — nothing dead-ends).

## Scaling Notes

- **L11:** soften the salt-dead (~40 HP); emphasize the dive and lore.
- **L13 (intended):** as written.
- **L15:** stronger gathered dead (~60-72 HP), the deep quarter open, full clan/Gravecaller tension. **The quarter stays a downward dead-end regardless — never a path to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the leave/reverence/guide outs are the intended solo path; the dead are bound and the dive self-limits.
