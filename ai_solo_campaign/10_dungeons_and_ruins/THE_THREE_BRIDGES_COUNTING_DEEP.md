## AI Use

Load for far-continent / Ring 2+ play in the Glassmere League: the **Cinder Ledger's great League counting-deep beneath the Three Bridges banking-quarter**, the bank's continental heart where the relic-and-coin trade is actually run. A mid-tier (L9-12) **social-infiltration / heist site** delivering far **REV_002 corroboration** (the continental relic-supply ledger; the Ledger's true scale and purpose). **CRITICAL guardrail: a bank-vault and paper-trail, NOT the keystone, NOT the Court** — it proves the Ledger commodifies the dead at continental scale, never the harvest-heart. Mirrors Caradril's Counting-Quays / Ledger Keep at greater scale. Sefwy Holt (rival) and the Ledger's League provost are the social frame.

## One-Sentence Identity

The deep counting-vaults under the League's banking-quarter, where the Cinder Ledger runs its continental relic-and-coin empire on paper — a far, larger mirror of the Caradril Ledger Keep, taken not by the sword but by cover, ledgers, and turned clerks.

## Map Placement

Far-continent, central, the city of **Glassmere**, the **Three Bridges** banking-quarter on the **Glasswater** (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 52-58, Y 50-58). Near **the Floor** (the merchant-exchange) and **the Reliquary** (the Remnant chapter, D28). The Ledger's true heartland.

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 9-12. Solo danger: moderate (social/heist).** Almost entirely a *paper-trail and infiltration* site: the threats are Ledger provost-guards, counting-house wards, and League banking-intrigue — combat is the *failure* path. Telegraphed; retreat-rich (the city is around). Not required; corroboration and a continental paper-trail.

## Purpose in the World

A mid-tier far-corroboration site that (1) proves the Ledger commodifies the dead at *continental scale* (REV_002: the relic-supply ledger, logged by source-region from across Orrun), (2) makes the League the Ledger's true power-center the player can investigate or rob, (3) offers a high-stakes urban heist where cover and turned clerks beat force, and (4) reveals the Ledger's monopoly purpose at scale — **without the keystone or the Court.**

## Access / Entrance Routes

Full physical approach and entrance description: `vael/orrun/sites/three-bridges-counting-deep`. Campaign-specific access routes:

1. **As a depositor/client (the cover way):** open an account / pose as a relic-seller to gain the counting-floor.
2. **By a turned clerk (the inside way):** turn an underfactor (the clean path — mirrors Coll Riis in Caradril).
3. **By Holt's rivalry (the banking way):** Banker-Magistra Sefwy Holt funds a quiet audit/raid against her rival.
4. **By stealth (the heist way):** infiltrate the counting-deep and its wards directly.

## Current Occupants

- **Ledger provost-guards & strong-arms:** catch-and-eject, not kill (city muscle).
- **Underfactors & clerks:** the counting-house staff (some turnable).
- **Counting-house wards & a strongroom lock:** the vault's defenses.
- **(DM) the Glass Ear & Holt's agents:** League intrigue around the vault.
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** A bank-vault; the apex is under Hollowmere.

## Factions Involved

- **Cinder Ledger (the League provost; Mother Calla Vyre's continental reach):** run the counting-deep.
- **Banker-Magistra Sefwy Holt:** the Ledger's banking rival; a lever and a funder.
- **Concord Remnant:** wants the relic-trade records too.
- **The Glass Ear:** sells the vault's layout and the clerks' secrets.

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The Counting-Floor (the public bank)
- **Description:** the grand banking-floor; tellers, clients, provost-guards.
- **DC / hazards:** cover (Deception/Insight DC 14); the provost's suspicion.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the *scale* of the trade — a continental bank built on relics (REV_002 shape).

### Zone 2: The Clerks' Hall (the turnable inside)
- **Description:** the underfactors' working hall; the day-ledgers.
- **DC / hazards:** turn a clerk (Persuasion/Insight DC 14-15); a watching provost.
- **Secrets/Clues:** day-ledgers showing relic-purchases by region (REV_002 fragment).

### Zone 3: The Counting-Deep (the supply-ledger)
- **Description:** the deep strongroom-archive; the master trade-records.
- **DC / hazards:** the strongroom lock (Thieves' Tools DC 16, 13 w/ a clerk's key); a vault-strong-arm.
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_GL_002 (REV_002):** the master relic-supply ledger — the dead, *logged as commodity by source-region from across the continent*, bound for the Ledger's refiners. **Capped: proves continental commodification of the substance; NEVER the harvest-mechanism/keystone/Court.**

### Zone 4: The Refiners' Cellar (the substance-horror)
- **Description:** the bank's relic-refining cellar; where raw relics are "concentrated."
- **DC / hazards:** refining-hazard (Con DC 14, thin-touch); a refiner-guard.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the relics *resist destruction / concentrate* — substance-proof (REV_002, the commercial-horror beat); a DM gate: the cellar is a closed workshop, NOT a passage anywhere.

### Zone 5: The Provost's Strongbox (treasure)
- **Description:** the League provost's private strongbox; coin, deeds, leverage.
- **DC / hazards:** a fine lock (Thieves' Tools DC 17); the provost.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the provost's leverage-files (blackmail on League powers) + significant coin; a closed strongbox (no passage).

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_GL_002 (REV_002):** the continental relic-supply ledger (the dead as commodity, logged by region).
- The refining-cellar's substance-proof (REV_002, commercial-horror).
- **Hard cap:** the Counting-Deep NEVER delivers REV_005/006/007, the keystone, the harvest mechanism, or the Court. It proves *commodification and scale*, never the live machine.

## Puzzles

**The Heist (cover + turned clerk + lock):** the vault answers to trust, keys, and intrigue, not force.
- **Solutions:** pose as a client (Deception/faction DC 14); turn an underfactor for a key (Persuasion/Insight DC 14-15); pick the strongroom (Thieves' Tools DC 16); Holt funds an audit; the Glass Ear sells the route.
- **Failure:** alarm and ejection (Ledger suspicion + a debt), not a dead end or cheap death.
- **Costly bypass:** ally with Holt openly (then owe her).
- **Accept clever solutions:** any valid social/heist approach passes.

## Noncombat Options

- **Cover / turned clerk:** the intended clean heist — no fight.
- **Holt / the Glass Ear:** buy access and intel.
- **Stealth:** avoid every guard.
- **The audit:** expose the trade legally through Holt's banking rivalry.

## Combat Encounters

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Ledger provost-guards / strong-arms** — Salt Syndicate Runner/Strong-Arm profile reskinned as bank-muscle (AC 13-14, ~16-45 HP; catch-and-eject; a Spy-like lieutenant at high tier).
- **A vault strong-arm / refiner-guard** — Thug/Veteran-like (AC 14, ~45 HP).
- Far NPCs (the League provost, Sefwy Holt, the Glass Ear) per `GLASSMERE_LEAGUE_NPCS.md` — never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **The city is the retreat** — withdraw into Glassmere any time.
- **No bound monsters** — this is a living-foe heist; the foes prefer ejection to killing.
- **The cover lets you walk out** if you keep it.

## Treasure and Rewards

- Significant coin, deeds, and the provost's leverage-files (blackmail on League powers).
- Far allies/leverage (Holt) or a powerful far enemy (the Ledger's continental arm).
- A continental relic-supply map (C_GL_002 — REV_002 corroboration; a strategic prize).

## Consequences if Ignored

The Ledger's continental relic-trade runs unchecked from its true heart; Holt's rivalry curdles into the Ledger's favor; the refining-horror spreads as more relics are "concentrated." The far REV_002 corroboration passes uncollected (it stays reachable in the NW and Caradril — nothing dead-ends).

- Ledger/Holt outcomes: `FACTION_STATE.md`, `RELATIONSHIPS.md` (the League provost, Sefwy Holt, the Glass Ear).

## Scaling Notes

- **L9:** simpler locks (DC 15), fewer guards; emphasize the cover-heist.
- **L10-11 (intended):** as written.
- **L12:** finer locks (DC 17), a Spy-lieutenant, the provost's strongbox in play. **The vault stays a closed bank-strongroom regardless — never a path to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the cover/turned-clerk/lock outs are the intended solo path; combat is the failure path.
