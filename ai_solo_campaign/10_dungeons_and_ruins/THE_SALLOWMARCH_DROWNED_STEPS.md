## AI Use

Load for far-continent / Ring 2+ play in the Sallowmarch Protectorate: the **submerged pre-Concord causeway-ruin** that runs out to sea off the fever-delta, half-drowned, rumored, and shunned — "the Drowned Steps" of the world-map's forbidden zones. A high-tier (L10-14) **tidal/delta environmental ruin** delivering a far **pre-Concord echo** (REV_001/005 corroboration through *delta marsh-cant and imperial denial*). **CRITICAL guardrail: a pre-Concord causeway, older than the harvest, NOT the keystone, NOT the Concord Deep, NOT the Court** — it echoes "the world drowned before" and never names the campaign secret. Marsh-Healer Doll Fenn and the smuggler "Heron" Maddox are the social frame; the imperial Protectorate denies it exists.

## One-Sentence Identity

The ancient causeway that strides out under the delta's tide toward open sea and nothing anyone will name — a pre-Concord ruin the fever-folk shun, the smugglers use, and the absent crown pretends away, where the drowned dead of an older fall keep its sunken steps.

## Map Placement

Far-continent, S coast, off the **Sallow Marches** delta where the **Mardenflow** meets the **Hollow Gulf** (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 56-62, Y 82-90; the world-map "Drowned Steps" forbidden zone). Reached from **Reedmouth** (the smuggling-village) through the **Fever Channels** of the deep delta. A hot, wet, malarial region under a distant crown's garrison-rule.

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 10-14. Solo danger: high.** A tidal/delta crawl: the threats are the rising tide on the sunken steps, fever and the fetid channels, the older fall's drowned dead, and the imperial garrison's hostility to anyone poking the "Steps that don't exist." Telegraphed (the fever-folk warn; the tide is visible); retreat-rich (Reedmouth and the delta camps are havens); a marsh-guide negates most channel-risk. Not required; corroboration and treasure.

## Purpose in the World

A high-tier far-corroboration site that (1) proves catastrophe and the troubled dead are **older than the Concord** in a third independent place (REV_001/005 echo, like the Saltmere drowned towns and the Marrowdowns barrows), (2) gives the delta-folk and smugglers a reason to brave the Steps (loot, a lost cargo, a stirring the fever-folk fear), (3) offers a distinctive tidal/fever-delta crawl, and (4) shows the crisis under *imperial denial* — a region whose occupiers forbid the very rumor — **without the keystone or the Court.**

## Access / Entrance Routes

1. **With "Heron" Maddox (the smuggler's way):** the reed-boss knows the channel-routes to the Steps (negates channel-risk; outlaw company).
2. **By Doll Fenn's counsel (the healer's way):** the marsh-healer (the delta's true leader) sends the player to settle the stirring drowned dead.
3. **At low tide (the tidal way):** the worst-ebb bares the most steps but strands the slow on the returning tide.
4. **Against the garrison (the defiant way):** the Protectorate forbids the Steps; brave its patrols (adds an imperial-pursuit layer).

## Current Occupants

- **Older-fall drowned dead:** pre-Concord causeway-dead, stirring as the crisis radiates south.
- **"Heron" Maddox's smugglers:** use the Steps' channels as a hidden route.
- **Imperial garrison patrols:** enforce the official denial; hostile to interlopers.
- **(DM) Cinder Ledger buyers** quietly purchasing what the Steps give up (oblique REV_002).
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** A pre-Concord causeway; the live machine is under Hollowmere.

## Factions Involved

- **Delta-folk (Doll Fenn):** shun the Steps; want the stirring dead settled.
- **Smugglers ("Heron" Maddox):** use the channel-routes; mercenary allies.
- **Imperial Protectorate (Vorr Sallow's garrison):** deny and forbid the Steps.
- **(DM) Cinder Ledger:** buys what's raised.

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The Fever Channels (the approach)
- **Description:** the deep delta backwaters threading toward the Steps; reed, fever, smuggler-marks.
- **DC / hazards:** fever exposure (Con DC 14); lost in the channels (Survival DC 15); garrison patrols.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the Steps are *older than the delta's memory* (REV_001 shape).

### Zone 2: The Tidal Causeway (the sunken steps)
- **Description:** the great pre-Concord causeway, awash to the knee at ebb, drowned at flood.
- **DC / hazards:** the rising tide (Survival DC 14 to time; strands the slow); slick steps (Dex DC 13).
- **Secrets/Clues:** the causeway's pre-Concord scale and make (REV_001/005 echo).

### Zone 3: The Drowned Gate-House (the gathered dead)
- **Description:** a half-submerged gate-structure on the causeway where the older dead "gather."
- **DC / hazards:** the drowned dead (rite or combat); a Frightful regard (Wis DC 15).
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_SM_001 (REV_001/005 echo):** the causeway-dead *gathered and could not pass* in an older fall — a pre-Concord mirror of the basin's pull, in delta idiom. **Capped: corroborates the theme; NEVER the keystone/harvest/Court.**

### Zone 4: The Sea-Vault (treasure)
- **Description:** a flooded vault-chamber beneath the gate-house; the causeway-builders' drowned hoard.
- **DC / hazards:** flooded lock (Thieves' Tools DC 16); a vault-bound drowned guardian; the tide.
- **Secrets/Clues:** pre-Concord valuables and "old glass" (oblique REV_002); the vault is sealed below by the sea (no passage onward).

### Zone 5: The Far Steps (the dread-gate down)
- **Description:** the causeway's seaward run, vanishing under deep water toward "nothing anyone will name."
- **DC / hazards:** deep water; the strongest older dead; the open sea.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the far steps end in *open ocean and ruin* — **an explicit DM gate: the Steps run out to sea and stop. They are an older, closed causeway, NOT a passage to the keystone or the Concord Deep. The dread is atmosphere, not a real descent.**

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_SM_001 (REV_001/005 echo):** the gathered, unable-to-pass older causeway-dead.
- Doll Fenn's and the delta-Mourners' marsh-cant on "the old going-under" (oblique REV_005 folk-fragment).
- **Hard cap:** the Drowned Steps NEVER deliver REV_006/007, the keystone, the harvest mechanism, or the Concord Deep. The "going-under" is an *older, separate* fall the imperial crown denies entirely.

## Puzzles

**The Tide & the Channels (navigation/timing, not a lock):** the Steps and channels answer to the tide and marsh-craft.
- **Solutions:** time the ebb (Survival DC 14); a smuggler- or marsh-guide negates the channels; settle the gathered dead by rite (Religion DC 15); avoid the garrison by smuggler-cover.
- **Failure:** stranded by the tide or found by the garrison (a complication/pursuit), not a dead end or cheap death.
- **Costly bypass:** Maddox's boat and route for a price.
- **Do not reveal:** any "the heart is out there" implication — the Steps run out to sea and stop.

## Noncombat Options

- **A guide / timing the tide:** brave the Steps safely.
- **Reverence / rite:** settle the gathered dead without a fight.
- **Smuggler-cover:** evade the garrison.
- **Doll Fenn's counsel:** the healer-sent, delta-honored path.

## Combat Encounters

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Older-fall drowned dead** — Drowned/Drifting Remembrance, far-scaled (AC 13, ~40-56 HP; rite/parley-able; bound to the causeway).
- **A sea-vault drowned guardian** — Ward-Keeper Remembrance, scaled (AC 13, ~64 HP; bound to the vault; closing-reverence out).
- **Garrison patrol** — Guard/Veteran-like living foes (avoid/bribe/stealth preferred).
- Far NPCs (Doll Fenn, "Heron" Maddox, Vorr Sallow's officers) per `SALLOWMARCH_PROTECTORATE_NPCS.md` — never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **Havens:** Reedmouth and the delta camps are the retreats.
- **The tide self-limits** the dive (a built-in valve).
- **Dead are bound** to the causeway — leaving the Steps escapes.

## Treasure and Rewards

- Pre-Concord valuables, "old glass," and a possible tide-warded minor item.
- Doll Fenn's / Maddox's gratitude (a delta base and smuggler-route).
- Far REV_001/005 corroboration (C_SM_001).

## Consequences if Ignored

The older drowned dead stir worse and spill into the Fever Channels; the garrison's denial cracks (or it cracks down harder); a smuggler or Ledger buyer strips the sea-vault. The far REV_001/005 echo passes uncollected (it stays reachable in the NW). The delta endures, as it always has.

## Scaling Notes

- **L10:** soften the dead (~40 HP); emphasize the tide and channels.
- **L12 (intended):** as written.
- **L14:** stronger gathered dead (~56-64 HP), the far steps and sea-vault in play, full garrison/smuggler tension. **The Steps stay a seaward dead-end regardless — never a path to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the guide/tide/rite outs are the intended solo path; the dead are bound and the tide self-limits.
