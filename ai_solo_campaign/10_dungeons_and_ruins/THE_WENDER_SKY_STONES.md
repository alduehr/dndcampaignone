## AI Use

Load for far-continent / Ring 2+ play in the Wender Steppe: an **ancient steppe shrine-circle that is NOT Concord** — older, independent, raised by the horse-clans' forebears, where the wind-singers read the Sky and where "the Sky has gone thin" is the steppe's own oblique sensing of the cosmic wound. A mid-high (L8-13) **non-dungeon exploration/social site** (a sacred open-air ring, not a delve) delivering the campaign's deepest **outside view** and a far, very oblique **REV_009 cosmic echo**. **CRITICAL guardrail: NOT Concord, NOT a node, NOT the keystone, NOT the Court** — the steppe-clans were never harvested and carry no Concord ruins; this is a *separate, older, living* holy site whose seers sense the thinning sky without understanding it. Clan-Speaker Tamur Wend-Khar and Wind-Singer Esha are the social frame. Hospitality-custom is the access key.

## One-Sentence Identity

The wind-worn ring of standing sky-stones the horse-clans' forebears raised long before the Concord, where the wind-singers read omens in the Sky and have begun to sing that "the Sky has gone thin" — a living, non-Concord holy site that senses the world's wound from wholly outside the Concord's shadow.

## Map Placement

Far-continent, N-central, on the open **Wender Steppe** beyond the **Highmark Spine**, S of the **Karran Teeth** (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 48-58, Y 22-32). Near the **Winter-Camp at Cold Springs** and the **Summer-Riding** migration-paths. A cold grass-sea ridden by clans the Concord never tamed — the campaign's clearest "outside view."

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 8-13. Solo danger: moderate-high.** This is **not a combat-dungeon**: the challenges are the steppe itself (cold, distance, getting lost on the trackless grass), earning the clans' hospitality and the wind-singers' trust, and reading the sky-stones' meaning — far more social/exploration than fight. The few threats are raiders (Raid-Lord Borr) and the open land. Telegraphed; retreat-rich (the clan-camps are mobile havens); a clan-rider guide negates most steppe-risk. Not required; the outside view and a cosmic echo.

## Purpose in the World

A mid-high site that (1) gives the player the campaign's deepest **outside view** — a people and a faith *entirely outside* the Concord's history, to whom the whole crisis is a foreign affliction, (2) delivers a far, deeply oblique **REV_009 cosmic echo** ("the Sky has gone thin" — the afterlife's thinning sensed from the far north, never named), (3) offers a distinctive non-dungeon sacred-site exploration where hospitality and omen-reading are the play, and (4) reminds the player the world is **larger than the Concord's shadow** — **without the keystone, the Court, or naming the Quiet Country/harvest.**

## Access / Entrance Routes

Full physical approach and entrance description: `vael/orrun/sites/sky-stones`. Campaign-specific access routes:

1. **By clan-hospitality (the sacred way):** earn a clan's guest-right (hospitality is sacred custom); a guest is escorted to the Sky-Stones.
2. **With Wind-Singer Esha (the seer's way):** the seer takes a trusted outsider to read the stones (the deepest access).
3. **At a gathering (the seasonal way):** visit during the Winter-Camp or a Summer-Riding gathering.
4. **Alone across the grass (the reckless way):** the trackless steppe and raiders claim the unescorted; also *violates* hospitality-custom and offends the clans.

## Current Occupants

- **Wind-singers & clan-pilgrims:** tend and read the living shrine.
- **The Sky-Stones themselves:** ancient, non-Concord, omen-laden (no undead, no guardian — a *living* holy site).
- **(DM, rare) the faintest thinning-echo:** a strange quiet/wrongness the seers sense at the stones — atmosphere, not a monster.
- **Raid-Lord Borr's reavers** (on the approach, not at the sacred ring).
- **Hollow Court / Concord:** **NOT present, NEVER were.** The steppe was never Concord; there is no node here.

## Factions Involved

- **Steppe-clans (Tamur Wend-Khar):** keep the guest-custom and the shrine.
- **Wind-singers (Esha):** read the Sky and the stones (the seer-node).
- **Cinder Ledger (edge-trade):** trades at the Spine-Foot meet; eyes the steppe warily.
- **Raid-Lord Borr:** a steppe reaver, a threat on the open land (not at the shrine).

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The Open Grass (the approach)
- **Description:** the trackless steppe to the shrine; clan-camps, migration-paths, sky.
- **DC / hazards:** lost on the grass (Survival DC 15); cold; Borr's reavers.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the clans know *nothing* of the Concord — the outside view (REV-context).

### Zone 2: The Sky-Stones Ring (the sacred circle)
- **Description:** the great non-Concord standing-ring on a rise; older than any Orrun ruin the player has seen.
- **DC / hazards:** social (guest-custom; the wind-singers' regard); no combat threat.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the stones are *independent and ancient* — proof the world predates and exceeds the Concord (REV-context).

### Zone 3: The Singing-Place (the omens)
- **Description:** the ring's heart, where the wind-singers read the Sky's omens.
- **DC / hazards:** earning the reading (Insight/Persuasion/Religion DC 15-16); the strain of the thinning-sense (Wis DC 14, no damage — a chill).
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_WS_001 (REV_009, far/oblique):** the wind-singers sing that "the Sky has gone thin" and "a far root aches" — the afterlife's thinning, *sensed* from wholly outside the Concord, in steppe sky-idiom. **Capped: a deeply oblique cosmic echo; NEVER names the Quiet Country, the harvest, the keystone, or the Court — the seers sense, they do not know.**

### Zone 4: The Old Cairns (the forebears)
- **Description:** the clan-forebears' burial-cairns near the ring; the steppe's honored dead.
- **DC / hazards:** reverence (disturbing them offends the clans gravely); no undead.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the steppe's dead *rest well* (their non-Concord rites still hold) — a quiet contrast to the failing north; the cairns are closed honored graves (no passage anywhere).

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_WS_001 (REV_009, far/oblique):** "the Sky has gone thin / a far root aches" — the cosmic-thinning sensed from outside.
- The clans' total *unknowing* of the Concord (the outside-view context-beat).
- **Hard cap:** the Sky-Stones NEVER deliver REV_005/006/007, the keystone, the harvest, the Court, or the named Quiet Country. The seers sense a wrongness in the Sky; they do not and cannot know its cause.

## Puzzles

**Hospitality & the Omen-Reading (social/reverence, not a lock):** the shrine answers to guest-custom and the seers' trust.
- **Solutions:** earn guest-right (honor the custom; a gift; a service — Persuasion/Insight DC 15); earn Esha's trust to read the stones (Religion/Insight DC 15-16); a clan-escort opens the way.
- **Failure:** the clans bar the shrine (an offense to mend), not a dead end or cheap death.
- **Costly bypass:** none crude — this is a *living* site; force/theft only offends and bars.
- **Do not reveal:** any Concord/keystone/Court framing — the steppe is genuinely outside it.

## Noncombat Options

- **Hospitality:** the entire site is social/reverent by design.
- **A clan-escort:** negates steppe-navigation risk.
- **Omen-reading:** the "payoff" is a vision/song, not loot or a fight.
- **Outride raiders:** evasion over combat on the open grass.

## Combat Encounters

This is a **near-combat-free site by design** (a living, non-Concord holy place). The only combat is *on the approach*:
- **Borr's reavers** — Scout/Veteran-like mounted raiders (AC 14-15, ~40-55 HP; mounted skirmishers; evadable; flee at half HP).
- No undead, no Concord guardians — there are none here.
- Far NPCs (Tamur Wend-Khar, Esha, Borr) per `WENDER_STEPPE_NPCS.md` — never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **The clan-camps are mobile havens** — always a retreat.
- **No bound foes** at the shrine — there is nothing to flee there.
- **A clan-escort** makes the open steppe safe to leave.

## Treasure and Rewards

- The clans' guest-friendship and a clan-token (passage and aid across the steppe).
- A wind-singer's omen/vision (the far REV_009 cosmic echo — a lore/foreshadowing prize, C_WS_001).
- A steppe-craft gift (a fine horse, a warded charm — a possible minor item).
- The outside-view perspective (a thematic reward, not loot).

## Consequences if Ignored

Nothing crisis-critical — the steppe carries on outside the Concord's whole history, as it always has. The wind-singers go on singing of the thinning Sky to ears that don't understand; the player misses the deepest outside view and the far REV_009 echo (which stays reachable in the NW — nothing dead-ends). Borr's reavers prey on the trade-meets unchecked.

## Scaling Notes

- **L8:** weaker/fewer reavers on approach; emphasize hospitality and the outside view.
- **L10-11 (intended):** as written.
- **L13:** Borr's full war-band on the approach (still evadable), the deepest omen-reading. **The shrine stays a living non-Concord site regardless — never a node, never a path to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the hospitality/escort/evasion outs are the intended solo path; the site is near-combat-free.
