## AI Use

Load for far/late high-tier play in the Highmark Passes: an **ice-locked Concord station among the oldest works** in the cold passes beyond the northern wall, where the most isolated survivors and the oldest, best-preserved Concord infrastructure lie frozen and intact. A high-tier (L12-16) **frozen environmental ruin** delivering far **REV_005/006 corroboration** through *exceptionally well-preserved* Concord records (cold preserves what the south's damp destroyed). **CRITICAL guardrail: an old peripheral station, NOT the keystone, NOT the Concord Deep, NOT the Court** — the ice preserves the *history* better than anywhere, but the live machine is under Hollowmere. Optional, not arc-required (a barrier frontier). Reuse the Warden seed-network logic; this is the far-north cousin of the Reach Relay-Vault. The Wardens' oldest knowledge and a possible isolated survivor-hold are the frame.

## One-Sentence Identity

The frozen Concord station among the oldest works in the high cold passes, where the dry ice has preserved the Concord's records and machinery better than any southern ruin — a place where the old order's history is *legible*, kept by isolated survivors and guarded by the cold itself.

## Map Placement

Far-continent, far N/NE, in the **Highmark Passes** of the **Highmark Spine** (the parent range of the Sunder Heights), beyond the northern wall toward the **Sunder Ocean** (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 30-44, Y 6-14). Reached via the high passes from the Reach/Karran approaches. The oldest Concord works and the most isolated survivors — an optional late frontier, a barrier, not arc-required.

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 12-16. Solo danger: very high.** The cold itself is the chief threat — lethal exposure, ice-collapse, frozen falls — alongside the oldest, best-preserved Concord guardians and the isolation (no home ally for weeks). Telegraphed heavily (the passes warn; survivors counsel); retreat-rich *only if prepared* (an isolated survivor-hold is the sole haven). Gear-gated and prep-gated, not ambush-gated. Not required; high corroboration and the legible Concord history.

## Purpose in the World

A high-tier far-corroboration site that (1) gives the *clearest far-surface* REV_005/006 proof outside Caradril's Sealed Archive — because the cold preserved the Concord's records and machinery intact, (2) ties the far-north's oldest works to the Wardens' seed-network and the deliberate Quietfall, (3) offers a lethal frozen environmental ruin where preparation beats force, and (4) realizes the world-map's "far north past the Highmark Spine" rumor as a real place — **without the keystone or the Court.** A capstone far-frontier site for prepared high-tier play.

## Access / Entrance Routes

Full physical approach and entrance description: `vael/orrun/sites/frozen-works`. Campaign-specific access routes:

1. **Over the high passes (the climber's way):** brave the lethal cold and ice (gear- and prep-gated).
2. **With an isolated survivor-guide (the survivor's way):** a far-north hold-survivor who knows the frozen works (negates much cold/navigation risk — the sole reliable haven-path).
3. **With a Warden / Remnant expedition (the order's way):** the Wardens' oldest knowledge or a Remnant high-expedition reaches the works (access + the order's stakes).
4. **Alone and unprepared (the suicide way):** the cold kills the unready before any fight — heavily telegraphed against.

## Current Occupants

- **The oldest, best-preserved Concord guardians:** intact construct-sentinels, cold-locked but functional.
- **Frozen Concord dead:** ice-preserved Remembrance, intact and aware.
- **Isolated survivor-holds:** the far-north's most remote folk, keepers of old knowledge.
- **(DM) a Warden-network seed / a Remnant high-expedition:** the orders' stakes in the oldest works.
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** A peripheral preserved station; the live machine is under Hollowmere.

## Factions Involved

- **Ashen Wardens (oldest knowledge):** the seed-network's far-north roots; the Wardens know the most here.
- **Concord Remnant (high-expedition):** covets the legible records and intact machinery.
- **Cinder Ledger (edge):** would pay anything for intact relics from here (rarely reaches it).
- **Isolated survivors:** the holds who've kept the works' edge for generations.

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The High Pass (the lethal approach)
- **Description:** the ice-locked passes to the station; survivor-holds, frozen markers.
- **DC / hazards:** lethal cold (Con DC 15, exhaustion/cold damage); ice-collapse (Dex DC 15); whiteout (Survival DC 16).
- **Secrets/Clues:** the works are *old* — the oldest Concord the player has seen (REV_005 shape).

### Zone 2: The Frozen Station-Front (the preserved works)
- **Description:** the Concord station's intact frozen face — machinery, Script, all legible in the ice.
- **DC / hazards:** cold; an outer construct-guardian (watchword or fight).
- **Secrets/Clues:** intact Concord Script — better-preserved than any southern ruin (REV_005/006 fragments, Act 3+ gate).

### Zone 3: The Ice-Vault Archive (the legible proof)
- **Description:** the station's frozen archive — the Concord's records preserved whole by the dry cold.
- **DC / hazards:** archive-guardian; Script-traps; the deep cold.
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_HMP_001 (REV_005/006):** the frozen archive's *intact* record — the clearest far-surface proof of the harvest (REV_005) and the deliberate, planned Quietfall (REV_006), legible because the cold preserved it. **Capped: the clearest far corroboration of the Concord's sin and the planned fall; NEVER the keystone, the live machine, or the Court (which the records do not name as surviving).**

### Zone 4: The Cold-Locked Heart (watchword-guardian)
- **Description:** the station's sealed inner relay-heart, kept by an intact, powerful construct-sentinel.
- **DC / hazards:** the apex construct-guardian (watchword or a hard fight); the thinning aura; the cold.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the relay-Script (a rich far REV_006 severance-fragment) — and a DM gate: the heart severs into ice and rubble, NOT a passage to the keystone or the Concord Deep.

### Zone 5: The Deep Ice (treasure)
- **Description:** the station's deepest frozen vaults; intact Concord machinery and relics, the worst cold.
- **DC / hazards:** lethal deep cold; the strongest guardians.
- **Secrets/Clues:** intact Concord artifacts (the best-preserved in the world) and a final REV_005/006 record; the vaults bottom out in ice and bedrock (no downward passage).

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_HMP_001 (REV_005/006):** the frozen archive's *intact* harvest + deliberate-Quietfall record (the clearest far-surface proof).
- The Wardens' oldest far-north knowledge (a Warden-confession path, gated).
- **Hard cap:** the Frozen Works NEVER deliver REV_007, the keystone, the live machine, or the Court's survival. The cold preserves the *history* better than anywhere — never the present-day apex.

## Puzzles

**The Cold & the Watchword (survival + Script):** the station answers to Concord challenge-responses; the passes answer to survival-craft.
- **Solutions:** read the Script / name the watchword (Concord Script DC 16; a Warden or Remnant reader); a survivor-guide negates the cold/navigation; a Warden's stand-down quiets the guardians (faction DC 15).
- **Failure:** the cold drives you out (a built-in, lethal-telegraphed valve) or rouses a guardian (a hard fight), not a cheap death — the cold is *signposted* as the killer.
- **Costly bypass:** let a Remnant high-expedition open the works (then contest the records).
- **Do not reveal:** any "the heart is here" implication — the works are a preserved peripheral station.

## Noncombat Options

- **A survivor-guide:** negates most cold/navigation risk (the key solo safety valve).
- **Watchword / Warden stand-down:** clear the guardians without a fight.
- **Rite / reverence:** quiet the frozen dead.
- **Read, don't raid:** the archive is the prize; reading it is non-combat.

## Combat Encounters

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Frozen Concord dead** — Drowned/Drifting Remembrance, ice-preserved (AC 13, ~48-64 HP; rite/parley-able; bound to the station).
- **Construct-guardians** — Concord Construct-Guardian, apex-scaled (AC 17-18, ~120-180 HP; **watchword stands them down**; bound to the works; thinning aura).
- Far/Warden NPCs (a survivor-guide; a Warden of the oldest knowledge; a Remnant high-expedition lead) — reuse Warden/far rosters; never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **The sole haven** is an isolated survivor-hold — retreat is possible *if prepared* (the cold is the real gate).
- **Guardians and dead are bound** to the station — leaving escapes them.
- **The cold self-limits** the delve (a built-in, telegraphed valve) — but is itself lethal, so prep is mandatory.

## Treasure and Rewards

- The **best-preserved Concord artifacts in the world** (tier-3/4; the far-frontier's great prize).
- The clearest far-surface REV_005/006 proof (C_HMP_001 — a scholarly/strategic capstone).
- The Wardens' oldest knowledge (a faction-deep reward) or a Remnant rivalry.
- A survivor-hold's loyalty (a far-north base).

## Consequences if Ignored

The oldest works stay frozen and unread; a Remnant high-expedition eventually reaches them and claims the intact records and machinery (a major shift in the Remnant's far ambition), or the isolated survivors' knowledge dies with them. The clearest far REV_005/006 proof passes uncollected (REV_005/006 stay reachable in the NW and Caradril's Sealed Archive — nothing dead-ends).

## Scaling Notes

- **L12:** soften guardians (~120 HP, no first-round blast); the cold still mandatory-prep; emphasize the archive.
- **L14 (intended):** as written.
- **L16:** apex guardians (~180 HP + summons), the deep ice open, full Warden/Remnant convergence. **The works stay a frozen peripheral station regardless — never a path to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the survivor-guide/watchword/Warden outs are the intended solo path; the cold is the gate, the guardians are bound and watchword-able. **Mandatory prep (cold gear) is the explicit safety requirement — telegraph it before the player commits.**
