## AI Use

Load for far-continent / Ring 2+ play in the Marrowdowns: the **ancient pre-Concord barrow-fields** of the chalk downs, "grown restless" as the death-rite crisis radiates south, guarded by shire-custom and robbed by barrow-thieves. A mid-high (L8-12) **barrow-crawl** delivering a far **pre-Concord echo** (REV_001/005 corroboration through *down-country barrow-superstition*). **CRITICAL guardrail: the barrows are pre-Concord — older than the harvest, NOT the keystone, NOT the Concord Deep, NOT the Court.** Mirrors `THE_BARROW_OF_NINE_DOORS.md` at higher tier and in a gentler, orderly region. Shire-Reeve Penmark and Moot-Mother Crale are the social frame.

## One-Sentence Identity

The chalk-down burial-mounds older than the Concord, where the orderly shire's dead have lain quiet for centuries and have only now "grown restless," drawing grave-robbers, the Ledger's buyers, and a player who can read the older fall written in the barrows.

## Map Placement

Far-continent, S-central uplands, the **Marrowdowns** chalk-downs S of the **Greatspine** (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 58-66, Y 60-68). Reached from **Marrowmoot** (the shire-town), **Penmark Hold** (the reeve's manor), or the wool-village **Wether**. A pastoral, orderly mid-continent region — a gentler contrast to the haunted frontier.

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 8-12. Solo danger: moderate-high.** The barrows are a classic crawl — restless barrow-dead, collapse, and a door-glyph puzzle — set in a region where the *social* frame (shire-custom, the reeve's law, the moot's grief-keeper) is half the challenge. Telegraphed (the shire warns; the moot-mother counsels); retreat-rich (Marrowmoot is the haven). Not required; corroboration and treasure.

## Purpose in the World

A mid-high far-corroboration site that (1) proves catastrophe and the troubled dead are **older than the Concord** in a second independent place (REV_001/005 echo, like the Saltmere's drowned towns), (2) gives the orderly shire a real trouble (barrow-robbers + restless dead) the reeve needs help with, (3) offers a classic barrow-crawl with a door-glyph puzzle and a parley-able barrow-lord, and (4) shows the crisis read through *barrow-superstition* — a fourth folk idiom — **without the keystone or the Court.**

## Access / Entrance Routes

Full physical approach and entrance description: `vael/orrun/sites/barrow-fields`. Campaign-specific access routes:

1. **By the reeve's leave (the lawful way):** Penmark hires the player to deal with barrow-robbers / the restlessness — the shire-honored path.
2. **By the moot-mother's counsel (the reverent way):** Senna Crale's old grief-songs guide a respectful entry and the laying of the restless dead.
3. **With the robbers (the grey way):** follow or join barrow-thieves who know the open mounds (fast, costs shire-standing).
4. **Alone across the downs (the quiet way):** the open, low-danger approach; the barrows are not hidden, only forbidden by custom.

## Current Occupants

- **Restless barrow-dead:** pre-Concord down-folk, quiet for centuries, now stirring.
- **Barrow-robbers:** grave-thieves stripping the open mounds (the reeve's chief complaint).
- **Cinder Ledger buyers:** quietly purchasing barrow-goods and "old glass" (oblique REV_002).
- **A barrow-lord:** an ancient, powerful chieftain-dead at the complex's heart — parley-able, NOT the Court.
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** Pre-Concord barrows; the live machine is under Hollowmere.

## Factions Involved

- **Shire-law (Aldous Penmark):** guards the barrows by custom; wants the robbers stopped.
- **Mourner grief-keepers (Senna Crale):** hold the old songs; counsel reverence.
- **Cinder Ledger:** buys barrow-goods; pushes the robbers indirectly.
- **Concord Remnant:** wants to dig the barrows for pre-Concord proof.

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The Open Mounds (the robbers' reach)
- **Description:** the barrow-field's edge, where thieves have cracked the nearer mounds.
- **DC / hazards:** social (robbers; the reeve's law); collapse in dug mounds (Dex DC 13).
- **Secrets/Clues:** the robberies are *waking* the dead — the restlessness has a cause (REV_001 shape).

### Zone 2: The Door-Glyph Mound (the puzzle)
- **Description:** a great sealed barrow whose door bears layered pre-Concord glyphs.
- **DC / hazards:** the glyph-door (see Puzzles); a guardian-dead if forced.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the glyphs record an *older fall* (pre-Concord; REV_001/005 echo).

### Zone 3: The Gathered Barrow (the folk-truth)
- **Description:** a central chamber where the down-folk's dead have "gathered," restless and aware.
- **DC / hazards:** the restless dead (rite or combat); a Frightful regard (Wis DC 14).
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_MD_001 (REV_001/005 echo):** the barrow-dead *gathered and grew restless* as the rites that held them failed — a pre-Concord mirror of the basin's pull, read in down-country idiom. **Capped: corroborates the theme; NEVER the keystone/harvest/Court.**

### Zone 4: The Barrow-Lord's Chamber (parley-boss)
- **Description:** the complex's heart; the ancient chieftain-dead and the richest grave-goods.
- **DC / hazards:** the barrow-lord (parley primary; combat if defiled).
- **Secrets/Clues:** the barrow-lord remembers the *older fall* (oblique REV_001) — and a DM gate: the chamber is a closed barrow-heart, NOT a passage downward to anything.

### Zone 5: The Deep Mounds (treasure)
- **Description:** the oldest, unrobbed mounds; pre-Concord treasure, the strongest dead.
- **DC / hazards:** collapse; the eldest barrow-dead.
- **Secrets/Clues:** pre-Concord grave-goods and a final REV_001/005 carved record; the mounds bottom out in chalk (no downward passage).

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_MD_001 (REV_001/005 echo):** the gathered, restless pre-Concord barrow-dead.
- Senna Crale's old grief-songs (the southern-downs node of the continental old-songs folk-thread; oblique REV_005).
- **Hard cap:** the barrows NEVER deliver REV_006/007, the keystone, the harvest mechanism, or the Concord Deep. This is an *older, separate* fall in down-country idiom.

## Puzzles

**The Glyph-Door (Zone 2) — three valid paths** (mirrors the Barrow of Nine Doors, D06):
- **Solutions:** read the pre-Concord glyphs (Script/History DC 14-15); the moot-mother's star-and-grief lore opens it by song (Religion DC 14); a barrow-robber's stolen key (costly/grey).
- **Failure:** rouses a barrow-guardian (a telegraphed fight), not a dead end.
- **Costly bypass:** dig past it (collapse risk + shire's wrath).
- **Accept clever solutions:** any valid in-fiction approach passes.

## Noncombat Options

- **The reeve's leave / the moot-mother's songs:** enter lawfully and lay the dead by rite.
- **Parley the barrow-lord:** reverence resolves the boss without a fight.
- **Stealth:** slip the robbers and the dead.
- **Social:** stop the robbers by exposing the Ledger's buyers (the reeve's gratitude).

## Combat Encounters

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Restless barrow-dead** — Drowned/Drifting Remembrance, far-scaled to dry barrow (AC 12-13, ~36-52 HP; rite/parley/song-able; bound to the mounds).
- **The barrow-lord** — Ancient Remembrance / Ward-Keeper hybrid (AC 14, ~80-100 HP; **parley/reverence primary**; bound to its chamber).
- **Barrow-robbers** — Bandit/Thug-like living foes (AC 13, ~22-30 HP; catch-and-eject or flee).
- Far NPCs (Penmark, Crale) per `MARROWDOWNS_NPCS.md` — never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **Haven:** Marrowmoot is the always-open retreat.
- **The barrows are open country** — withdrawal is easy.
- **Dead are bound** to the mounds — leaving the field escapes.

## Treasure and Rewards

- Pre-Concord grave-goods (tier-2/3 valuables; a possible minor barrow-warded item).
- The reeve's gratitude and the shire's lawful base + a route south.
- Senna Crale's old songs (lore-prize + far-ally bond).
- Far REV_001/005 corroboration (C_MD_001).

## Consequences if Ignored

The robberies wake more barrows; the restless dead spill onto the downs; the shire's long peace cracks (Penmark's fear realized) and the Ledger strips the barrow-goods, or the Remnant digs the field for pre-Concord proof. The far REV_001/005 echo passes uncollected (it stays reachable in the NW).

## State Update Triggers

- C_MD_001 read/understood: mark in `KNOWN_CLUES.md` (REV_001/005 echo; **never** log REV_007/keystone).
- Shire/robber/Ledger outcomes: `FACTION_STATE.md`, `RELATIONSHIPS.md` (Penmark, Crale).
- Grave-goods/songs: `INVENTORY_AND_REWARDS.md`.

## Scaling Notes

- **L8:** soften the dead (~36 HP) and the barrow-lord (~80 HP); emphasize the glyph-puzzle and social frame.
- **L10 (intended):** as written.
- **L12:** stronger barrow-lord (~100 HP), the deep mounds open, full robber/Ledger/Remnant tension. **The mounds stay closed barrow-hearts regardless — never a path to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the leave/songs/parley outs are the intended solo path; the dead are bound and the country is open.
