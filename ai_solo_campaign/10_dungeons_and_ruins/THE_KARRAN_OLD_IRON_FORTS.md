## AI Use

Load for far-continent / Ring 2+ play in the Karran Marches: the **scattered peripheral Concord forts and deep-mine workings** ("the old iron") the outlaw-and-mining frontier digs into, where "humming old iron" and crews that "come back changed" mark a far edge of the harvest network. A high-tier (L10-14) ruin-and-mine site delivering far **REV_002/005 corroboration through a brutal frontier lens**. **CRITICAL guardrail: peripheral surface-Concord ruins, NOT the keystone, NOT the Concord Deep, NOT the Court** — the network reached even here, thinly, but the heart is under Hollowmere. Mirrors the Reach as a harsher second frontier (`THE_CONCORD_RELAY_VAULT.md` at the rough edge). Iron Brask and Ore-Factor Mully Karr are the social frame.

## One-Sentence Identity

The cold stone forts and deep ore-mines where the old empire's iron still hums, where mining crews dig into peripheral Concord workings and "come back changed," and where a strong-handed outlaw frontier mirrors the player's own.

## Map Placement

Far-continent, NE, in the **Karran Teeth** range (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 70-78, Y 18-28). Reached from **Karran-Gate** (the mining-and-mercenary town), **Brask's Hold** (Iron Brask's fortress), or **the Deep Cuts** (the great ore-mines). A harsher mirror of the Sundering Reach — cold stone, mines, feuds, and the rule of the strong.

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 10-14. Solo danger: high.** A frontier with no law but the strong hand: the threats are the deep mines (collapse, gas, dark), the "humming-iron" Concord workings and their changed crews, and the warlord/factor/Gravecaller tensions on the surface. Telegraphed (the crews' fear is loud); retreat-rich (Karran-Gate and Brask's Hold are havens); a mining-crew guide negates much mine-risk. Not required; corroboration and ore-wealth.

## Purpose in the World

A high-tier far-corroboration site that (1) proves the harvest network reached the far NE periphery (REV_002/005 corroboration: "humming old iron," changed crews, in-situ Script), (2) gives the rough frontier a real trouble (the changed crews, a haunted Deep Cut) the warlord and factor need handled, (3) offers a mine-and-fort dungeon mixing collapse/gas survival with relay-guardian challenge, and (4) mirrors the player's home Reach as a *second, harsher* frontier — **without the keystone or the Court.**

## Access / Entrance Routes

Full physical approach and entrance description: `vael/orrun/sites/old-iron-forts`. Campaign-specific access routes:

1. **Iron Brask's commission (the strong-hand way):** the warlord hires the player to clear a haunted Deep Cut or a humming fort (the frontier-honored path).
2. **Mully Karr's coin (the trade way):** the ore-factor pays the player to make a humming workings safe to mine.
3. **With a mining crew (the deep way):** descend with a crew who know the cuts (negates much risk; the crew may "change").
4. **Alone into the peaks (the reckless way):** the Old Iron forts are scattered and high; the unguided freeze, fall, or get lost.

## Current Occupants

- **"Changed" crews & humming-iron dead:** miners touched by the workings; peripheral Concord dead.
- **A relay-guardian:** a Concord construct-sentinel of a fort's inner workings (watchword-able, NOT the Court).
- **Mining crews & mercenaries:** the Deep Cuts' workforce and Brask's strong-arms.
- **(DM) Gravecaller-adjacent zealots:** drawn by the changed crews and stirring dead.
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** Peripheral surface-Concord ruins; the live machine is under Hollowmere.

## Factions Involved

- **Warlord holds (Iron Brask):** rule the frontier by strength; want the troubles cleared.
- **Cinder Ledger (Mully Karr's edge-trade):** buys "old glass" / humming-iron and wants the cuts mineable.
- **Concord Remnant:** an NE expedition (turnable) maps the peripheral workings.
- **(DM) Gravecallers:** a toehold on the lawless, stirring-dead frontier.

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The Fort-Mouth (the strong-hand reach)
- **Description:** a Concord fort's broken gate, now a mercenary camp and ore-stage.
- **DC / hazards:** social (Brask's men; rival crews); high-peak exposure (Survival DC 14).
- **Secrets/Clues:** the fort is *Concord-built* — the old empire reached the NE (REV_005 shape).

### Zone 2: The Deep Cut (the mine)
- **Description:** the great ore-mine driven into peripheral Concord workings.
- **DC / hazards:** collapse (Perception/Dex DC 15); choke-gas (Con DC 14); the dark.
- **Secrets/Clues:** "humming old iron" — Concord relay-stone the crews fear (REV_002 source).

### Zone 3: The Humming Workings (the changed crews)
- **Description:** the inner Concord workings where the iron "hums" and crews "come back changed."
- **DC / hazards:** the changed/humming-iron dead (rite or combat); thin-touch exposure (Con DC 15).
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_KM_001 (REV_002/005):** the humming iron is harvest-relay infrastructure — it *holds the dead* (REV_002) and the crews' change is exposure to it; in-situ Script confirms the Concord's reach (REV_005 frag). **Capped: corroborates substance + reach; NEVER names the keystone/Court.**

### Zone 4: The Relay-Heart (watchword-guardian)
- **Description:** the fort's sealed inner relay-chamber, kept by a Concord construct-sentinel.
- **DC / hazards:** the relay-guardian (watchword or fight); the thinning aura.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the relay-Script (a far REV_005 fragment) — and a DM gate: the relay severs into rubble, NOT a passage to the Concord Deep or the keystone.

### Zone 5: The Lost Cuts (treasure)
- **Description:** the deepest, abandoned cuts; ore-wealth, Concord salvage, the worst air.
- **DC / hazards:** gas/collapse; the strongest changed dead.
- **Secrets/Clues:** Concord relics, "old glass," ore-wealth; the cuts bottom out in rock (no downward passage to the network).

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_KM_001 (REV_002/005):** the humming iron holds the dead + the Concord reached the NE.
- The changed crews are living evidence of relic-exposure (oblique REV_002 / thin-touch echo).
- **Hard cap:** the Old Iron forts NEVER deliver REV_006/007 (beyond a far Script-fragment), the keystone, the harvest mechanism, or the Concord Deep. The network reached here *peripherally*; the heart is under Hollowmere.

## Puzzles

**The Watchword / the Workings (Script + survival):** the relay-heart answers to a Concord challenge-response; the mine answers to crew-craft.
- **Solutions:** read the Script / name the watchword (Concord Script via a Remnant reader, DC 15-16); a crew-guide negates the mine-hazards; quiet the humming workings by rite (Religion DC 15).
- **Failure:** rouses the relay-guardian or the changed dead (telegraphed), or strands a crew in the dark.
- **Costly bypass:** let the Remnant NE expedition open the relay (then contest it).
- **Do not reveal:** any "the heart is here" implication — these are peripheral forts.

## Noncombat Options

- **Watchword / rite:** clear the relay-heart and quiet the workings without a fight.
- **A crew-guide:** negates most mine-hazards.
- **Frontier diplomacy:** play Brask, Mully Karr, and the Remnant expedition off each other.
- **Stealth:** slip the guardian and the changed dead.

## Combat Encounters

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Changed / humming-iron dead** — Drowned/Drifting Remembrance, far-scaled (AC 13, ~40-56 HP; rite/parley-able; bound to the workings).
- **Relay-guardian** — Concord Construct-Guardian (AC 17, ~95-120 HP; **watchword stands it down**; bound to the relay-heart; thinning aura).
- **Mercenaries / strong-arms** — Thug/Veteran-like living foes (catch-and-eject or flee).
- Far NPCs (Iron Brask, Mully Karr, the Remnant captain) per `KARRAN_MARCHES_NPCS.md` — never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **Havens:** Karran-Gate and Brask's Hold are the retreats.
- **A crew-guide / the watchword:** make withdrawal reliable.
- **Guardian and dead are bound** to the forts/workings — leaving the cut escapes.

## Treasure and Rewards

- Ore-wealth, "old glass," and Concord salvage (tier-3); a possible relay-warded minor item.
- Iron Brask's respect / Mully Karr's trade-favor (a far frontier base).
- Far REV_002/005 corroboration (C_KM_001).

## Consequences if Ignored

The humming workings change more crews; a haunted Deep Cut goes dark and Mully Karr loses the ore (or pushes crews to their deaths for it); Brask's hold weakens; the Remnant NE expedition strips the relay-Script. The Gravecaller toehold deepens on the lawless frontier. The far REV_002/005 echo passes uncollected (it stays reachable in the NW).

## Scaling Notes

- **L10:** soften the dead (~40 HP) and guardian (~95 HP, no first-round blast); emphasize the mine-survival.
- **L12 (intended):** as written.
- **L14:** apex guardian (~120 HP + summons), the lost cuts open, full frontier convergence. **The cuts/relay stay a downward dead-end regardless — never a path to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the watchword/guide/rite outs are the intended solo path; the guardian is bound and watchword-able.
