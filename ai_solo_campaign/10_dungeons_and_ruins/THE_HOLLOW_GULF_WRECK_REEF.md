## AI Use

Load for far-continent / Ring 2+ play in the Hollow Gulf Ports: a **wreck-tangled reef and drowned harbor-quarter** off the great southern ports, where a privateer-and-salvage culture dives a shipwreck-graveyard rich with cargo, foreign relics, and a sunken older harbor. A high-tier (L10-15) **maritime environmental dungeon** that is the campaign's most *normal* far site — adventure, salvage, and rivalry, with only the faintest, most oblique echo of the crisis (a far REV_002 relic-supply thread, denied by the prosperous south). **CRITICAL guardrail: a wreck-reef and salvage-site, NOT a Concord node, NOT the keystone, NOT the Court.** Port-Mistress Ive Calder and Privateer-Captain Roke Mallin are the social frame. Use this as a *relief* site: the world that doesn't know.

## One-Sentence Identity

The shipwreck-graveyard reef and the drowned old harbor-quarter beneath the greatest southern port, dived for cargo, foreign relics, and salvage by a warm, brash maritime people who have never heard of the cold north's troubled dead — and would laugh if they had.

## Map Placement

Far-continent, S coast, off **Calderport** on the **Hollow Gulf** where the **Mardenflow** delta meets the warm **Calm Reach** (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 60-68, Y 84-92). Reached from **Calderport**, the **Mardenmouth** wharves, or the **Foreign Quarter** (overseas traders). The maritime south — the gateway to Surren and the wider world.

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 10-15. Solo danger: high (maritime/environmental).** The threats are the sea itself — wreck-tangle, currents, deep water, reef-predators — plus port-rivalry, privateers, and salvage-claim disputes. **Mostly *not* about the dead:** this is a diving-and-salvage adventure. Telegraphed; retreat-rich (the ports are all around); a salvage-crew or Mallin's ship negates most sea-risk. Not required; treasure and a "the wider world" beat.

## Purpose in the World

A high-tier site that (1) gives the maritime south a genuine adventure (a rich, dangerous wreck-reef and a drowned harbor), (2) shows the player **how much of the world simply doesn't know** about the crisis — a deliberate relief and contrast, (3) offers a distinctive sea-diving environmental dungeon, (4) seeds the *only* far-southern crisis thread as a faint, denied REV_002 relic-supply echo (foreign relics passing through the Ledger's southern arm), and (5) opens the horizon toward Surren and overseas — **without the keystone or the Court.**

## Access / Entrance Routes

Full physical approach and entrance description: `vael/orrun/sites/wreck-reef`. Campaign-specific access routes:

1. **With a salvage-crew (the diver's way):** hire a Calderport wreck-crew (negates most sea-risk; salvage-claim politics).
2. **On Roke Mallin's ship (the privateer's way):** the licensed sea-raider runs the player out (access + a roguish ally/rival).
3. **By Port-Mistress Calder's leave (the lawful way):** a sanctioned salvage-charter (slower, safer, taxed).
4. **Alone in a hired boat (the reckless way):** the reef and currents claim the unguided.

## Current Occupants

- **Salvage-crews & privateers:** working the wreck-reef; salvage-claim rivals.
- **Reef-predators & the sea:** the chief threat — currents, sharks, a great eel, wreck-tangle.
- **Foreign and overseas traders:** Surren-side merchants whose lost cargoes lie here.
- **(DM) the Ledger's southern relic-arm:** quietly buys "old glass" foreign relics that pass through here (the faint REV_002 thread).
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** A wreck-reef; the apex is under Hollowmere, an ocean and a continent away.

## Factions Involved

- **Calderport (Ive Calder):** charters and taxes the salvage; rules the reef-claims.
- **Privateers (Roke Mallin):** run the dives and the raids; mercenary allies.
- **Cinder Ledger (southern arm):** buys foreign relics and salvage.
- **(DM) a worried Mourner / a far-sailing captain:** the rare NPC who's seen the north and takes the faint signs seriously.

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The Wreck-Reef (the salvage-ground)
- **Description:** the shipwreck-graveyard reef; tangled hulls, salvage-buoys, rival crews.
- **DC / hazards:** currents (Athletics DC 14); reef-predators; salvage-claim disputes.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the sheer *scale* of trade lost here — a world of ships and cargo (horizon-beat).

### Zone 2: The Tangled Hulls (the dive)
- **Description:** the diveable wrecks; cargo-holds, drowned cabins, foreign goods.
- **DC / hazards:** wreck-tangle (Athletics/Acrobatics DC 14); air/cold (Con DC 13); a lurking predator.
- **Secrets/Clues:** foreign cargo and relics — and "old glass" among them (the faint REV_002 thread).

### Zone 3: The Drowned Harbor-Quarter (the old ruin)
- **Description:** a sunken older harbor-district the rising sea took generations ago — drowned streets and warehouses.
- **DC / hazards:** deep water; collapse; a reef-eel's lair.
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_HG_001 (REV_002, faint/far):** among the drowned warehouses, a cache of "old glass" foreign relics logged for the Ledger's southern arm — far proof the relic-trade reaches *even here*, denied by the prosperous south. **Capped: a faint southern relic-supply echo; NO meaning, origin, harvest, keystone, or Court — the south does not know what it handles.**

### Zone 4: The Deep Wrecks (treasure)
- **Description:** the reef's deepest, most dangerous wrecks; the richest cargo, the worst currents.
- **DC / hazards:** deep-water/current (Athletics DC 15); the great eel/predator.
- **Secrets/Clues:** rich foreign cargo, overseas curios (Surren-side), a possible saltwater-warded item; the wrecks are a closed graveyard (no passage anywhere).

### Zone 5: The Foreign Hold (the horizon)
- **Description:** a wrecked Surren-side ship's hold; overseas goods, charts, and lore of the wider world.
- **DC / hazards:** the deepest dive; the predator.
- **Secrets/Clues:** charts and curios of Surren and the overseas world (a horizon-beat, the gateway to the wider world) — **a DM gate: this opens the *world*, not the keystone; the apex stays under Hollowmere.**

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_HG_001 (REV_002, faint/far):** the southern relic-supply echo (foreign "old glass" for the Ledger).
- The rare worried Mourner / far-sailing captain who's *seen the north* (an oblique crisis-thread the south denies).
- **Hard cap:** the Wreck-Reef NEVER delivers REV_005/006/007, the keystone, the harvest, or the Court. The south *does not know.* The relic-thread is faint and meaning-less to the locals by design.

## Puzzles

**The Sea & the Salvage (environmental + social, not a lock):** the reef answers to seamanship and the ports answer to charter-politics.
- **Solutions:** a salvage-crew or Mallin's ship negates most sea-risk; time the currents (Survival/Athletics); a sanctioned charter (Calder) avoids claim-disputes; avoid the predator.
- **Failure:** a lost dive, a swept boat, a claim-feud — costs and complications, not a dead end or cheap death.
- **Costly bypass:** pay for the best crew and charter.
- **Do not reveal:** any crisis-significance to the locals — the south genuinely doesn't know.

## Noncombat Options

- **A crew / a charter:** dive safely and lawfully.
- **Avoid the predator:** seamanship over a fight.
- **Port-diplomacy:** settle salvage-claims by negotiation.
- **The horizon-beat:** trade, charts, and overseas lore — pure adventure.

## Combat Encounters

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md` and standard 5e beasts (no Concord adversaries here by design):
- **A reef-predator** — a Large sea-beast (Giant Shark / Reef-Eel-like; AC 13-15, ~70-120 HP; sea-bound; avoidable).
- **Rival salvage-crews / privateers** — Bandit/Veteran-like living foes (catch-and-eject; flee at half HP).
- Far NPCs (Ive Calder, Roke Mallin) per `HOLLOW_GULF_PORTS_NPCS.md` — never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **The ports are the retreat** — surface and sail back any time.
- **No bound undead** — the sea-beast is avoidable and sea-bound.
- **The dive self-limits** (air/cold) — a built-in valve.

## Treasure and Rewards

- Rich foreign cargo, overseas curios, and a possible saltwater-warded minor item (tier-3).
- Charts and lore of Surren and the overseas world (the horizon-prize).
- Far allies (Calder's charter; Mallin's ship) or a far rival.
- A faint REV_002 relic-supply echo (C_HG_001) — strategic, not apex.

## Consequences if Ignored

The wreck-reef is worked by others; the Ledger's southern relic-arm runs unwatched; a rival crew claims the drowned harbor's richest wrecks. Nothing crisis-critical is lost — the south carries on, oblivious, which is the point. The far REV_002 echo stays reachable in the NW (nothing dead-ends).

- C_HG_001 read/understood: mark in `KNOWN_CLUES.md` (faint REV_002 echo; **never** log REV_005/007/keystone).
- Port/privateer outcomes: `FACTION_STATE.md`, `RELATIONSHIPS.md` (Ive Calder, Roke Mallin).
- Cargo/curios/charts: `INVENTORY_AND_REWARDS.md`.

## Scaling Notes

- **L10:** a single weaker predator (~70 HP); shallower wrecks; emphasize salvage-adventure.
- **L12-13 (intended):** as written.
- **L15:** the great eel (~120 HP), the deep wrecks and foreign hold in play, full port-rivalry. **The reef stays a salvage-graveyard regardless — never a Concord site or a path to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the crew/charter/avoid outs are the intended solo path; the predator is avoidable and the dive self-limits.
