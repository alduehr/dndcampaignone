## AI Use

Load for far-continent / Ring 2+ play in the Hethewald Free Holds: the **forbidden deep-wood Concord ruins** the forest-folk shun and keep their old bargains to leave asleep — the Tollwood's "Old Mast" logic writ large. A high-tier (L9-13) ruin-exploration site delivering far **REV_005 corroboration through a folk-pagan lens** (the "grey hands took the dead") and a deep-wood gated presence. **CRITICAL guardrail: surface-Concord ruins, NOT the keystone, NOT the Concord Deep, NOT the Court.** Bram Hethe forbids it; Forest-Mother Onn Greenward's old songs are the oblique key. Mirrors `THE_OLD_MAST.md` at higher tier.

## One-Sentence Identity

The deep, forbidden Concord ruins of the eastern forest, where the free-holds keep ancient bargains to leave the "grey hands' work" asleep, and where the old dead and a forest-bound power stir when the bargains slip.

## Map Placement

Far-continent, E-central, deep in the **Hethewood** along the **Hethe** river (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`, approx. full-grid X 64-72, Y 40-50). Reached from **Hethemoot** (the great gathering free-hold), **Greenward** (Onn's grove-village), or the **Tollreach** outlaw-toll camp. The Tollwood (and its Old Mast, D12) is this forest's far-NW finger.

## Level Range and Solo Danger

**Levels 9-13. Solo danger: high (gated).** Like the Old Mast, the wood itself is the chief threat — the misleading deep forest, the failing-bargain dead, and a forest-bound presence that force fails against. Telegraphed heavily (the holds forbid it; the bargains warn of it); a forest-guide and Onn's songs are the safety valves. Not required; corroboration and a deep-wood challenge.

## Purpose in the World

A high-tier far-corroboration site that (1) proves the Concord and its harvest reached the deep east (REV_005 corroboration via the holds' "grey hands" folk-fragment), (2) gives the free-holds a reason to either guard or open the ruins (a faction flashpoint with the Ledger's logging arm and the Remnant's interest), (3) offers a gated deep-wood challenge where woodcraft and reverence beat force, and (4) shows the crisis read through a *folk-pagan* idiom distinct from the Mourners and the official faith — **without the keystone or the Court.**

## Access / Entrance Routes

1. **Against the holds' law (the forbidden way):** enter without leave — fast, but the wood and the holds both turn against you.
2. **With Bram Hethe's leave (the lawful way):** earn the Speaker's grudging blessing to investigate why the bargains are failing — slower, far safer.
3. **By Onn Greenward's songs (the reverent way):** learn the old grove-songs that keep the dead quiet — the oblique key to passing the presence.
4. **Following a Remnant expedition (the scholarly way):** travel with Remnant scholars seeking the ruins (access + rivalry).

## Current Occupants

- **The failing-bargain dead:** deep-wood Concord dead, stirring as the old bargains slip.
- **A forest-bound presence:** an ancient grove-power (like the Old Mast's), roused by the failing bargains — old, NOT a god, NOT the Court.
- **Remnant scholars & Gravecaller-adjacent zealots:** seeking the ruins for opposite reasons.
- **Cinder Ledger logging crews:** pressing the wood's edge (an oblique trade thread).
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** Surface-Concord ruins; the live machine is under Hollowmere.

## Factions Involved

- **Free-holds (Bram Hethe):** forbid and guard the ruins; keep the bargains.
- **Mourner-kin grief-keepers (Onn Greenward):** hold the old songs (the key).
- **Concord Remnant:** covets the deep-wood ruins and their records.
- **Cinder Ledger:** logging-greed pressing the wood (and quietly buying what's raised).
- **(DM) Gravecallers:** drawn by the stirring deep-wood dead.

## Zone List

### Zone 1: The Marked Wood (the bargain-edge)
- **Description:** the forbidden forest's edge, hung with offering-marks and bargain-tokens the holds maintain.
- **DC / hazards:** the wood misleads (Survival DC 16); breaking a mark rouses the wood.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the bargains themselves — the holds *know* something sleeps here (REV_005 shape).

### Zone 2: The Drowned Holds (the ruins proper)
- **Description:** Concord ruins half-swallowed by the deep wood — toppled halls, root-cracked Script.
- **DC / hazards:** structural collapse (Perception/Dex DC 15); the failing-bargain dead.
- **Secrets/Clues:** in-situ Concord Script on the harvest's reach (REV_005 fragment, Act 3-gated).

### Zone 3: The Grey-Hands Hall (the folk-truth)
- **Description:** the central ruin-hall the songs call where "the grey hands worked" — the documentary/folk heart.
- **DC / hazards:** archive-dead; a Frightful regard (Wis DC 15); the presence's attention.
- **Secrets/Clues:** **C_HW_001 (REV_005):** the deep-wood proof — the "grey hands" (the Concord) took the dead here too, in Script and in the gathered dead. **Capped: corroborates the harvest's *reach*; NEVER the keystone/Court.**

### Zone 4: The Sleeping Grove (the presence)
- **Description:** the oldest heart, where a forest-bound presence sleeps under the failing bargains.
- **DC / hazards:** the presence (reverence or flee — force fails); the deepest misleading wood.
- **Secrets/Clues:** the presence is *older than the Concord's roads* (an M5/REV_001 pre-Concord echo) — **and an explicit DM gate: the grove is a closed older heart, NOT a passage to the keystone or the Concord Deep.**

### Zone 5: The Root-Vaults (treasure)
- **Description:** root-swallowed Concord strongrooms; the deep-wood salvage.
- **DC / hazards:** root-locks (Athletics/Thieves' Tools DC 16); a vault-bound dead.
- **Secrets/Clues:** Concord relics and a final REV_005 Script-fragment; the vaults bottom out in root and rock (no downward passage).

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_HW_001 (REV_005):** the deep-wood "grey hands" proof (folk + Script).
- Onn Greenward's old songs (the eastern node of the continental old-songs folk-thread; oblique REV_005).
- **Hard cap:** the Old Holds NEVER deliver REV_006/007 (beyond a far Script-fragment), the keystone, the harvest mechanism, or the Concord Deep. The presence is a forest-power, NOT the Court.

## Puzzles

**The Bargain / the Songs (social + reverence, not a lock):** the wood and its dead answer to the old bargains and grove-songs.
- **Solutions:** learn Onn Greenward's songs (Persuasion/Religion DC 15-16 to earn them); keep the bargain-marks (Survival/lore); earn Bram Hethe's leave; read the Script (Concord Script via a Remnant reader).
- **Failure:** the wood strands you; the presence and the holds turn hostile (telegraphed, not instant-death).
- **Costly bypass:** force a Remnant expedition's route (then contest the ruins).
- **Do not reveal:** any "the heart is here" implication — the Old Holds are surface ruins over an older closed grove.

## Noncombat Options

- **The songs / reverence:** pass the presence and lay the dead without a fight.
- **The holds' leave:** the lawful, far-safer path.
- **A forest-guide:** negates most navigation/misleading-wood risk.
- **Stealth:** slip the dead and the rival scholars.

## Combat Encounters

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Deep-wood failing-bargain dead** — Drowned/Drifting Remembrance, far-scaled (AC 13, ~40-56 HP; rite/parley/song-able; bound to the ruins).
- **A grove-bound presence** — treat as a high-tier Ward-Keeper/Ancient Remembrance hybrid (AC 14-15, ~80-110 HP; **reverence/flee primary; force fails and strands**; bound to the sleeping grove).
- Far NPCs (Bram Hethe, Onn Greenward) per `HETHEWALD_FREE_HOLDS_NPCS.md` — never cheap-killed.

## Retreat Options

- **Havens:** Hethemoot and Greenward are the retreat points.
- **A guide / the songs:** make withdrawal reliable.
- **The presence and dead are bound** to the ruins/grove — leaving the marked wood escapes.

## Treasure and Rewards

- Deep-wood Concord relics and root-vault salvage (tier-3).
- Onn Greenward's old songs (a lore-prize + a recurring far-ally bond).
- The free-holds' alliance (if the player guards rather than loots the ruins).
- Far REV_005 corroboration (C_HW_001).

## Consequences if Ignored

The old bargains slip further; the deep-wood dead stir worse; a Remnant expedition or the Ledger's logging breaches the ruins and rouses the presence (a far catastrophe), or the Remnant strips the REV_005 records to "prove their inheritance" eastward. The free-holds' unity frays under the pressure. The far REV_005 echo passes to a faction (REV_005 stays reachable in the NW).

- Hold/Remnant/Ledger outcomes: `FACTION_STATE.md`, `RELATIONSHIPS.md` (Bram Hethe, Onn Greenward).

## Scaling Notes

- **L9:** soften the dead (~40 HP) and the presence (~80 HP); emphasize the wood and the songs.
- **L11 (intended):** as written.
- **L13:** stronger presence (~110 HP), the root-vaults open, full faction convergence. **The grove stays a closed older heart regardless — never a path to the keystone.**
- **Solo PC:** the songs/leave/guide outs are the intended solo path; the presence is reverence-first and bound.
