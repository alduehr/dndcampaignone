## AI Use

Master index of every authored adventure site (dungeon, ruin, environmental-dungeon, heist site, gated site, endgame approach). Use to pick a site by **region, level, faction, mystery/revelation, site type, or solo danger**. Each row tells the AI DM the file, level band, solo-danger rating, secrecy, and which mysteries/factions it touches. **DM-only rows are flagged — never render `THE_UNDER_SHRINE_APPROACH.md` content to the player.**

## Status

**Stage 12 complete.** 15 Stage 3/4/7 sites + 8 new Stage 12 sites = **23 authored adventure sites**, spanning Levels 1–20 across the Reach, Ring 1, Caradril, and two far-continent anchors, plus the endgame approach (DM-only). All sites support multiple play styles (exploration / social / stealth / puzzle / faction / combat) and solo-PC safety valves. Optional far-continent sites (Saltmere/Hethewood/Marrowdowns/Karran) remain in CONTENT_GAPS as placeholders.

## Design Standards (all sites)

Every site in this index meets the Stage 12 / `DND_MECHANICS_REQUIREMENTS.md` dungeon standard: level range, solo-danger rating, zone list, trap/hazard DCs, encounter list with stat references, puzzle/clue checks, treasure, boss/climax mechanics, **noncombat options, retreat routes, solo-play safety valves, consequences-if-ignored, and state-update triggers.** No site is a pure combat gauntlet; every site has a noncombat path; every gated/lethal site is telegraphed before the player can suicide into it.
