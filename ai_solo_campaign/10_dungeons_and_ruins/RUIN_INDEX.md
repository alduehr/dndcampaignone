## Ruin Themes

**Collapsed Concord network nodes** (D01, D02, D07, D09, D14, D15, D18, D21): the harvest network's physical infrastructure — relay stations, chapels, cloister-archives, shore-shrines. Each is a fragment; no single one reveals the apex truth. Clue-trail access is gated by level and by how much the player has already pieced together.

**Pre-Concord / older-than-the-machine** (D06, D11, D12): burial barrows, sacred groves, and a presence older than the Concord's roads. These hint that the world was haunted before the harvest — deepening M5 ("the dead have always been strange here") without substituting for the Concord explanation.

**Far surface ruin** (D21): the Concord Heartlands ruin is the largest surviving above-ground Concord structure. It shows the empire's scale and generates REV_005-006 corroboration at high tier — but it is a surface layer only; the machine is not here.
