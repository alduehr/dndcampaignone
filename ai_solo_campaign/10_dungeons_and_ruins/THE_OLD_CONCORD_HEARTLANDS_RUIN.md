## AI Use

Load for far-continent / Ring 2+ play: the **surface ruins of the Concord's fallen capital country** — the Ruin'd Crown, astride the Greatspine, accessed from the scavenger-town Crownmouth and the Pilgrim Camps. A high-tier (L10-14) ruin-exploration site that delivers far **surface-proof of the Concord's true scale and sin** (REV_005/006 corroboration). **CRITICAL guardrail: this is NOT the keystone, NOT the Concord Deep, NOT where the Hollow Court sleeps.** The endgame stays vertical, beneath Hollowmere. The Heartlands corroborate *how vast and guilty* the Concord was above ground — never that the live machine or the Court is here. A late-game treasure many factions covet.

## One-Sentence Identity

The drowned-in-time capital country of the fallen Concord — a vast surface ruin of palaces, archives, and shrines that proves how enormous and how guilty the old order was, picked over by scavengers, scholars, and the desperate.

## Level Range and Solo Danger Rating

**Levels 10-14. Solo danger: high.** Far from any home ally; ancient guardians, rival expeditions (Remnant, Gravecaller, Ledger, local powers), structural collapse, and the ruin's old dead. Telegraphed and retreat-rich (Crownmouth/the Pilgrim Camps are the havens), but a genuine high-tier far-frontier site. Not required for the campaign; corroboration and treasure, not a critical path.

## Location

Far-continent, central-SE, astride the **Greatspine / Sundering Wall** cordillera (`FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`). Reached from **Crownmouth** (fortified scavenger-town, ruin-edge) and the **Pilgrim Camps** (refugee/relic-seeker shanties). Distinct from and far from the NW campaign cluster — a Ring 2+ horizon site.

## Purpose in the World

A late-game far-corroboration and treasure site that (1) proves the Concord's *true scale* (an empire, not a frontier order) and *true sin* (REV_005/006 surface-proof, via the Crown's records), (2) gives the Concord Remnant a natural Act 3-4 ambition (they covet the Crown openly), (3) offers a far high-tier ruin-crawl, and (4) deepens the "the world has fallen and drowned before" motif — **without ever implying the harvest-heart or the Court is here.**

## History (Player-Safe)

The Ruin'd Crown is the broken heart-country of the old Concord, astride the great mountains — fallen, dangerous, and coveted. Folk say it was the seat of the order that ruled before the Quietfall. Scavengers strip it; scholars dig it; pilgrims seek miracles and relics in it.

## History (DM-Only)

The true surface capital of the Concord — proof of how vast the order was *above ground*. Its archives and shrine-halls corroborate the harvest (REV_005) and the deliberate Quietfall (REV_006) in surface records that survived. It fell in the staged Quietfall like everywhere else, but harder, being the heart. **It is a fallen surface ruin, NOT a node, NOT the keystone, NOT the Court's seat** — the singular live machine remains under Hollowmere (`DM_ONLY_CANON.md`, Full-World section). The Concord Remnant covets it as the prize that "proves their inheritance"; Sabine Ferrant frames its records as "holy restoration"; Hollin Vane leads expeditions deep (apex-adjacent, but **never confirms the Court — he does not know**). The Crown corroborates the *theme and history*; it must never deliver REV_007 or the keystone mechanism.

## First Impression

The Greatspine's shoulder is crowned with ruin to the horizon — toppled colonnades, drowned plazas, a shrine-dome cracked like an egg, all on a scale the frontier has no word for. Scavenger-fires smoke at the edges; pilgrim-tents huddle in a fallen forum. Concord Script covers everything, vast and confident and dead. This was an *empire*, and it ate itself.

## Access / Entrance Routes

1. **From Crownmouth (the scavenger way):** hire a guide (Old Crown Mab), pay the war-captain's toll, brave the ruin-edge.
2. **The Pilgrim Camps (the desperate way):** join or follow a relic-pilgrimage in.
3. **A Remnant expedition (the scholarly way):** travel with Hollin Vane's expedition (access + a rival/ally dynamic).
4. **Alone over the Greatspine (the reckless way):** the most dangerous approach.

## Current Occupants and Factions

- **Crownmouth scavengers & the war-captain (Lyssa Crownmouth):** control the ruin-edge.
- **Concord Remnant expedition (Hollin Vane, Sabine Ferrant's faction):** digging for proof and inheritance.
- **Gravecaller-adjacent zealots & Father Casian Ord (heretic-priest):** seeking to "free" the Crown's dead.
- **The Crown's old dead & guardians:** ancient, powerful, ruin-bound.
- **Hollow Court:** **NOT present.** Network-touch does not reach here; this is surface history.

## Zone / Room List

### Zone 1: The Ruin-Edge (Crownmouth's reach)
- **Description:** the scavenged outer ruins; tents, fires, tolls, rival diggers.
- **Occupants:** scavengers, the war-captain's toll-takers, rival expeditions.
- **Hazards:** social (tolls, rivals); structural collapse (Perception/Dex DC 14).
- **Secrets / Clues:** the *scale* is itself the clue — this was an empire (REV_005 shape).

### Zone 2: The Fallen Forum & Pilgrim Camps
- **Description:** a drowned plaza turned refugee/relic-seeker camp.
- **Occupants:** pilgrims, Father Casian Ord, relic-hawkers.
- **Hazards:** desperation, fever, false-relic scams.
- **Secrets / Clues:** the heretic-priest's half-truths (an oblique REV_005 folk-fragment, far-capped).

### Zone 3: The Crown Archives (the surface-proof)
- **Description:** the Concord's great surviving archive-hall — the documentary heart.
- **Occupants:** the Remnant expedition (Hollin Vane); ancient archive-wards.
- **Hazards:** archive-guardians; Script-traps; the Remnant rivalry.
- **Secrets / Clues:** **C_FC_001 (REV_005):** the Crown's harvest-record — the Concord's true scale and sin, in surface records. **Capped: corroborates the harvest's history; NEVER implies the live machine/keystone is here.**

### Zone 4: The Sundered Shrine-Dome (REV_006 surface-proof)
- **Description:** the cracked great shrine-dome — where the Crown's records of the Quietfall survive.
- **Occupants:** a powerful ruin-guardian (boss); the Crown's eldest dead.
- **Hazards:** the guardian; collapse; the eldest dead's regard.
- **Secrets / Clues:** **NEW: C_FC_014 (REV_006):** the Crown's account of the *deliberate Quietfall* — surface corroboration of the planned self-destruction (far source for REV_006; never the Court's survival).

### Zone 5: The Deep Vaults (off-limits to the apex)
- **Description:** the deepest surviving vaults — treasure-dense, guardian-thick.
- **Occupants:** the strongest guardians; the best loot.
- **Hazards:** high-tier combat/traps.
- **Secrets / Clues:** Concord artifacts and a final REV_005/006 surface-record — **and an explicit DM gate: the vaults bottom out in collapse/rubble, NOT a passage to the keystone. The Crown is a dead end downward by design — the live machine is under Hollowmere.**

## Exploration Features

- **The scale-as-revelation:** standing in an *empire's* ruin recontextualizes the whole frontier (REV_005 emotional payoff).
- **Far rival dynamics:** Hollin Vane's expedition, the war-captain, the heretic-priest — a web of far powers to navigate.
- **A true ruin-crawl:** guardians, collapse, vaults, Script-traps — the campaign's big far dungeon.

## Puzzles

**The Archive Wards / the Watchword (Zones 3-4):** like the Reach Relay-Vault, the Crown's halls answer to Concord challenge-responses.
- **Solutions:** read the Script (Concord Script / a Remnant reader / Hollin Vane's expedition); name the watchword (Investigation DC 15-16); a Mourner/old-songs star-lore alternate.
- **Failure:** rouses an archive-guardian (a telegraphed high-tier fight).
- **Costly bypass:** let the Remnant open it (then contest the records).
- **Do not reveal:** any "the heart is here" implication — the Crown corroborates history, not location.

## Secrets and Clues

- **C_FC_001 (REV_005):** the Crown's harvest-record (surface-proof of the Concord's scale/sin).
- **NEW: C_FC_014 (REV_006):** the Crown's account of the deliberate Quietfall (far surface corroboration).
- Father Casian Ord's heretic half-truths (oblique REV_005 folk-fragment).
- **Hard cap:** the Crown NEVER delivers REV_007 (the Court), the keystone, or the Concord Deep. Hollin Vane is apex-adjacent but does not know the Court survives.

## Boss / Climax Mechanics

**A Crown ruin-guardian** (Concord Construct-Guardian, apex-tier; see STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES — scale up to AC 18, ~150-200 HP) OR **the Crown's eldest dead** (a powerful, ancient Remembrance; parley/rite primary).
- **Watchword/reverence (best):** read the Script or honor the eldest dead to pass and read freely (non-combat).
- **Combat:** a hard high-tier fight; guardians are ruin-bound (retreat-rich).
- **The rivalry:** the *real* climax may be Hollin Vane's expedition — beat them to the records, ally, or contest (often non-combat).
- **No cheap death:** telegraphed, bound, retreat to the havens always open.

## Adversary Profiles

Reference `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Concord Construct-Guardian (Crown ruin-guardian)** — apex-scaled (AC 18, ~150-200 HP).
- **Ancient Remembrance (the Crown's eldest dead)** — high-tier wraith/ghost (AC 15, ~90-120 HP; parley/rite-able).
- Far NPCs (Hollin Vane, Lyssa Crownmouth, Father Casian Ord, Old Crown Mab) per `CONCORD_HEARTLANDS_NPCS.md` — recurring far powers, never cheap-killed.

## Noncombat Options

- **Script-reading / watchword:** clear the archives without a fight.
- **Far diplomacy:** ally with or play off Hollin Vane, the war-captain, and the heretic-priest.
- **Reverence:** honor the Crown's eldest dead.
- **Stealth:** slip past guardians and rival expeditions.

## Solo-Play Safety Valves

- **Havens:** Crownmouth and the Pilgrim Camps are the retreat points.
- **A guide (Old Crown Mab):** negates much navigation/collapse risk.
- **Telegraphing:** guardians, collapse, and the deep vaults are all signposted.
- **Not required:** the player can walk away; nothing critical dead-ends here.

## Treasure and Rewards

- Concord **artifacts** (tier-3/4 magic items; the Crown's great prizes — see `14_treasure_and_artifacts/` when built).
- The Crown's surface-records (C_FC_001 / C_FC_014 — REV_005/006 corroboration; the scholarly prize).
- Far allies/leverage (Hollin Vane, the war-captain) or a powerful far enemy.
- Significant coin/salvage from the vaults.

## Consequences if Ignored

The Remnant (Hollin Vane) reaches the Crown's records first and uses them to "prove their inheritance" and press their Reclamation ambition at continental scale; the Gravecaller-adjacent zealots "free" the Crown's dead (a far catastrophe); the war-captain sells the ruin to the highest bidder. The far surface-proof of REV_005/006 passes to a faction (REV_005/006 remain reachable in the NW — nothing dead-ends).

## How Site Changes After Interaction

- **Records secured/read:** the player gains far REV_005/006 corroboration; the Remnant's far ambition is checked or empowered (track).
- **Allied/contested with Hollin Vane:** far faction state shifts.
- **The Crown's dead freed/honored:** a far consequence (eased or catastrophic).
