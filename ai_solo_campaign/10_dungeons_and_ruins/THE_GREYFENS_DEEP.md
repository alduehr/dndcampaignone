## AI Use

Load when the player goes deep into the Greyfens as a *site* rather than a travel zone — chasing the dead's drift, the Gravecallers, or a fen delve beyond the wilderness encounter table. The Greyfens Deep is an **environmental dungeon**: the fen itself is the dungeon, with zones, hazards, factions (the Gravecaller cell), and clues, threading between the already-authored Greyfens landmark-sites and the Barrow of Nine Doors (D06). **Guardrails:** delivers M2/M5 field-proof and M6 *fragments* via the Gravecallers/old dead (Act-paced, oblique); never the keystone interior, never the Court. Old Sashe is the chief safety valve. Use `SUNDERING_REACH_ENCOUNTERS.md` Zone 2 as the wandering layer.

## One-Sentence Identity

The drowned heart of the Greyfens, where the dead pile up against the basin's pull, the Gravecallers hide their cell, and the fen tries to keep and drown anyone who reads its signs wrong.

## Level Range and Solo Danger Rating

**Levels 3-7. Solo danger: moderate, scaling.** The chief dangers are *getting lost*, sinking ground, luring lights, and the gathered dead — not a boss gauntlet. **Far safer with Old Sashe** (the fen-guide), who negates most navigation/mire risk. Without a guide it is genuinely dangerous; the design rewards preparation and woodcraft over force.

## Location

The deep interior of the Greyfens, beyond Greywater Holm and the marked landmark-sites (the Drift-Line, the Bone Weir, the Lantern Maze, the Drowned Blind, Mother Tongue Pool — `GREYFENS_SITES.md`). The Barrow of Nine Doors (D06) sits at its dead-center as the deep climax-site.

## Purpose in the World

A repeatable, scalable exploration zone that (1) gives the clearest *field-proof* of M5 (the Drift-Line — dozens of dead all facing the basin), (2) is the home of the Reach Gravecaller cell (M2/M3/M6 forbidden-contact door), (3) demonstrates fen survival as a play pillar, and (4) funnels into the Barrow of Nine Doors for a deeper M5/M6 payoff. It makes the Greyfens a destination, not just a travel hazard.

## History (Player-Safe)

The Greyfens are a drowned, treacherous bog the fen-folk navigate by old markers; the dead "go there and can't get through." Greywater Holm is shrinking as its rites fail worst. The Gravecallers are rumored to shelter somewhere in the deep fen.

## History (DM-Only)

The Greyfens lie in the basin's pull-shadow: leaked Remembrance drifts here from across the Reach and **jams** against the pre-Concord Barrow (which the network was built over and tapped, D06). The Drift-Line is the visible front of that jam — the single best M5 field-proof in the campaign. The Gravecaller cell (Sister Knell, Cole Ashby's radicals, the Tallow Man at Mother Tongue Pool) shelters here because the deep dead *answer* readily — a forbidden M2/M3/M6 fragment source. As the harvest reawakens, the drift thickens, the cell grows bolder toward "the Breaking," and the fen grows more dangerous. The Greyfens corroborate the harvest from the field and the forbidden margins — never the keystone, never the Court.

## First Impression

Past the last marker the fen closes in: black water, reed-walls taller than a rider, fog that swallows sound. Lights flicker out over the deep pools where no firm ground should be. And everywhere, drifting at the edge of sight, the dead — all of them turned the same way, west, toward the water, as if the whole bog were a slow tide of the lost.

## Access / Entrance Routes

1. **From Greywater Holm with Old Sashe:** the safe way — the guide negates most hazards.
2. **The fen-paths alone:** following the markers (and losing them); the dangerous default.
3. **The Gravecaller way:** invited or following the Knock to the cell (forbidden contact; reduces social danger, raises legal/faction risk).
4. **By punt along the drowned channels:** a water route past the worst sinking ground (a fen-craft approach).

## Current Occupants and Factions

- **Drifting/gathered Remembrances:** the jammed dead — mournful, telegraphed, parley-able.
- **The Gravecaller cell:** Sister Knell (recruiter), Cole Ashby's radicals, the Tallow Man (speaker) at Mother Tongue Pool.
- **The Mourners' Circle:** fen grave-tenders; allies for laying the dead.
- **Old Sashe:** the fen-guide (solo-safety anchor).
- **Fen predators:** mundane bog beasts (telegraphed; flee on morale).

## Zone / Room List

### Zone 1: The Drift-Line (the visible front)
- **Description:** an open stretch where dozens of drifting dead converge, all facing the basin — the great M5 field-proof.
- **Occupants:** many weak drifting Remembrances (non-hostile; a mass-rite scene).
- **Hazards:** the dread of the sight (Wis DC 13); provoking the drift = a dangerous, telegraphed swarm.
- **Secrets / Clues:** C-M5 field-proof (C_SR_005 / REV_001) — the dead are pulled basin-ward, visibly, at scale.

### Zone 2: The Lantern Maze (luring lights)
- **Description:** deep pools where will-o'-wisp-like lights lure travelers off firm ground.
- **Occupants:** will-o'-wisp-like lights (a hazard, not a boss).
- **Hazards:** Wis DC 13 to disbelieve the lights; sinking ground (Survival DC 13 / Athletics DC 15); getting lost (Survival DC 14, cumulative — Sashe negates).
- **Secrets / Clues:** the lights cluster thickest *basin-ward* (a navigation clue that doubles as M5 corroboration).

### Zone 3: The Bone Weir (the snag)
- **Description:** a drowned debris-snag where the drift jams against fallen timber — grave-goods, salvage, a smuggling cache.
- **Occupants:** a fen predator possible; a smuggler (Gull's line) possible.
- **Hazards:** deep water (Athletics DC 13); foul air (Con DC 12).
- **Secrets / Clues:** Investigation/Survival DC 13 — drowned grave-goods and a minor relic (M3); a smuggling link (Saltmargin thread).

### Zone 4: The Drowned Blind & Mother Tongue Pool (the Gravecaller cell)
- **Description:** the cell's hidden camp (the Drowned Blind) and the sacred pool where the dead answer (Mother Tongue Pool).
- **Occupants:** Sister Knell, recruits, the Tallow Man (speaker); Cole Ashby's radicals may be present.
- **Hazards:** social, not combat — the cell wants a witness, not a fight; turns dangerous only if attacked/betrayed.
- **Secrets / Clues:** the cell's teaching (C-M3-2 / C_SR_012, REV_002) and the channeled dead (oblique M5/M6 fragment, C_SR_023 capped to Act 2-3); the Tallow Man channels a real Quietfall-era witness (a gated REV_005 path — fragment only here).

### Zone 5: The Sinking Reach (the deep mire)
- **Description:** the worst of the fen between the cell and the Barrow — open sinking ground and gathered dead.
- **Occupants:** wrathful drifting dead possible (telegraphed); Cole's radicals moving toward the shrine.
- **Hazards:** sinking ground (the signature danger; Survival DC 14 / Athletics DC 15, staged sinking — Sashe negates); cold; getting lost (DC 15).
- **Secrets / Clues:** the path to the Barrow of Nine Doors (D06) — the deep climax-site beyond this dungeon.

## Exploration Features

- **Reading the drift:** mapping the dead's direction (Survival/Investigation DC 13) is itself the M5 proof and a navigation aid.
- **Fen-craft:** poling, marker-reading, and weather-reading are the play pillars; clever fen survival bypasses most combat.
- **The cell as a knowledge hub:** the Gravecallers are a recurring forbidden-contact source, not a one-off.

## Hazards and Traps

| Trap / Hazard | DC | Effect | Bypass |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sinking ground (Zones 2, 5) | Survival 13-14 / Athletics 15 | Staged sinking; mire; lost gear | Old Sashe; markers; a punt; rope |
| Getting lost / the misleading fen | Survival 14-15 (cumulative) | Strand the player deeper; time loss | Sashe (negates); the markers; landmarks |
| Luring lights (Zone 2) | Wis 13 | Drawn toward deep water/sinking | Disbelieve; ignore; Sashe |
| Drift-dread (Zone 1) | Wis 13 | Shaken; provoked drift = swarm | Don't provoke; mass-rite |
| Deep water / cold | Athletics 13 / Con 12 | Exhaustion; drowning risk | Punt; rope; firm ground |
| Fen predators | telegraphed | Mundane beast; flees on morale | Avoid; scare off |

No lethal mechanical traps; the fen is the danger.

## Puzzles

**The Markers (navigation puzzle):** the safe path is read from old fen-markers and the drift's direction. Solutions: Survival DC 14; Old Sashe; the Gravecaller Knock (the cell knows the way); a Mourner's grave-lore. **The fen is fair** — there is always a readable path; failure costs time and risk, never an instant death.

## Secrets and Clues

- **C-M5 / C_SR_005 (REV_001):** the Drift-Line — the campaign's clearest M5 field-proof.
- **C-M3-2 / C_SR_012 (REV_002):** the Gravecallers' teaching (forbidden contact).
- **C_SR_023 (REV_005/006 fragment, gated):** the Tallow Man's channeled Quietfall-era witness — Act 2-3 fragment only here; the full confession is gated.
- **NEW: C_SR_040 (REV_001/005, fragment):** the drift's *convergence pattern* across the whole fen (mapped) proves a single basin-ward pull — a strong inference clue (still not the mechanism/Court).
- **M3 relic** at the Bone Weir; a smuggling link.

## Boss / Climax (variable)

No fixed monster boss — the Greyfens Deep funnels into the **Barrow of Nine Doors (D06)** for its true climax (the barrow-lord parley-boss, M5/M6). Within this dungeon, the "climax" is one of:
- **The Gravecaller cell scene** (parley/forbidden-knowledge; the Tallow Man's witness).
- **A mass-rite at the Drift-Line** (lay the gathered dead; ease the local drift — a visible win).
- **A wrathful gathered-dead** if the drift is provoked (telegraphed; layable/escapable).

## Adversary Profiles

Reference `SUNDERING_REACH_ENCOUNTERS.md` (Zone 2 Greyfens) and `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md`:
- **Drifting Remembrance** (specter-like, AC 12, ~17-22 HP; many but weak; mass-rite).
- **Wrathful gathered-dead** (wraith-like, AC 13, ~36 HP; provoked only).
- **Will-o'-wisp-like light** (hazard).
- Gravecaller cell (Sister Knell — Acolyte-like; the Tallow Man — Cult Fanatic/Warlock-like; Cole Ashby — per NPC entry; **recurring, never cheap-killed**).
- Fen predators (CR 1/4-1).

## Noncombat Options

- **Fen-craft/exploration:** navigate, map the drift, pole past danger — the core loop.
- **Social/forbidden:** parley the Gravecaller cell for knowledge.
- **Ritual:** lay the gathered dead (Mourner/Warden aid).
- **Stealth:** slip past predators and radicals.

## Solo-Play Safety Valves

- **Old Sashe** is the dedicated safety anchor — recruiting him negates most hazards.
- **The drift points the way:** the dead's direction is always a navigation clue back toward the basin/Holm.
- **Retreat:** the markers and Sashe lead out; Greywater Holm is the recovery point.
- **The cell parleys:** the Gravecallers default to talk, not ambush.
- **Telegraphing:** sinking ground, luring lights, and dread are all signposted.

## Treasure and Rewards

- A minor Remembrance relic (M3) and drowned grave-goods at the Bone Weir (~60-100 gp value).
- A smuggling cache (Gull's line; coin/goods + a contact).
- The Gravecallers' forbidden knowledge (the real prize — M2/M3/M6 fragments).
- Sashe's/Mourners' goodwill and eased local hauntings if the drift is laid.

## Consequences if Ignored

The drift thickens; Greywater Holm dies faster; the Gravecaller cell grows bolder toward "the Breaking" (a catastrophic ending-cost the player could prevent); the Barrow's jam worsens. The Reach's clearest M5 field-proof and a key forbidden-contact source go unused — but M5/M2/M3 remain reachable elsewhere (nothing dead-ends).

## How Site Changes After Interaction

- **Drift laid (mass-rite):** the Greyfens ease visibly (a player-win); the Court counters over time.
- **Cell befriended/opposed:** `FACTION_STATE.md` (Gravecallers ±); Cole's radicalization slowed or hastened (the Breaking clock).
- **Bone Weir looted / smugglers met:** the Saltmargin smuggling thread advances.

## Scaling Notes

- **L3:** weak/few drifting dead; gentle hazards; Sashe strongly encouraged.
- **L5 (intended):** as written.
- **L7:** thicker drift, wrathful dead more common, harder sinking ground, Cole's radicals active; the Barrow becomes the natural next step.
- **Solo PC:** never field the gathered dead as a true mob without the mass-rite/retreat outs; Sashe is the intended counterweight.
