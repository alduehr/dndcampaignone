## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY — DM-facing arc spine for levels 5–10 (Act 2 + lower Act 3).** Load when planning a Tier 2 session, choosing what to surface, or checking pacing. **Revelation ceiling for Tier 2:** REV_002 (substance collected), REV_003 (Reke steers), REV_004 (you were steered) are the *core* tier reveals; a hard-pushing player may begin REV_005 (the Concord harvested) at L9–10. **Never** surface REV_007 (the Hollow Court), REV_006-as-intent beyond a "stranger than the histories say" hint before its gate, or the keystone's true nature. The conspiracy in Tier 2 reads as **contemporary and human**, not yet a century-old machine.

This is an **open-world kit, not a track.**

## Expected Campaign State at Entry (Level 5)

- **Player knows:** REV_001 — the failures have a source/pattern at the basin. Possibly an Act 1 faction stance.
- **Reputation:** known in Hollowmere; a rising name in the Reach.
- **Clocks:** Stage 1, or Stage 2 for the Basin (#2) / Wardens (#3) if the player was passive in Act 1.
- **Geography:** the full Sundering Reach is open; roads run to Ring 1 (Vale S, Tollwood E, Coast W) and toward Caradril (SE).
- **Open threads:** Wren resolved or her consequence live; basin stance chosen or forced; Sefra still a presence.

## The One-Paragraph Shape (DM)

The Reach is no longer a single town's problem. As the player ranges wider, the factions stop being friendly local faces and start *acting*: recruiting the player, opposing them, deceiving them, and — if crossed — retaliating. The player learns the uncanny relics actually **hold the dead** and that someone is **collecting them on purpose** (REV_002); that the basin "water-rights" scheme is a **cover to reopen the drowned shrine** pushed by Councilor Reke for a hidden master (REV_003); and, as a personal gut-punch, that **they were steered to Hollowmere** as an unwitting probe (REV_004). The drowned shrine becomes the undeniable center. Caradril opens as the mid-game hub where the conspiracy lives in daylight — the Ledger's bank, the Remnant's archive, the city's denial. At the top of the tier, a player who reaches the Sealed Archive or breaks Othetha's oath begins to confirm the unthinkable: **the Concord itself harvested the dead** (REV_005). Tier 2 ends with the conspiracy fully exposed as contemporary — and the first crack in the *historical* truth opening beneath it.

## Main Pressures (Tier 2)

1. **The basin is being reopened.** The Basin clock (#2) is the master infrastructure driver; if it reaches Stage 3–4 the upper shrine surfaces and the master harvest clock (#1) accelerates. This is the single most important world-state lever of the tier.
2. **The factions go regional.** Each major faction runs its Stage 8 quest chain (Q*1→Q*4) through this band, ending at a decision point (Q*4) that sets its endgame posture. See `FACTION_ESCALATION_PATHS.md`.
3. **The relic trade swells.** The Ledger's Monopoly (#6) and the Remnant's Reclamation (#7) clocks rise; the Vyre–Quorrin deal (Caradril C1) is negotiated. Remembrance becomes a market.
4. **The player becomes a target.** Once the player is a consequential actor (~L7+), crossed factions retaliate — legally (Compact/Tide-Watch), economically (Ledger debt), criminally (Salt Syndicate/the Hush), or violently (Gravecaller radicals, cutout-hired hands). Always telegraphed, never cheap death.
5. **The steering unravels** (#8). Sefra's unease grows; the cutout patron may move to "tidy up" — the trust shock of REV_004.

## Likely Regions (Tier 2)

- **Sundering Reach (full):** all 8 settlements; the basin shore; the Greyfens; the Sunder Heights (Harrowgast, the Deep Adit). The home board for the tier.
- **Ring 1:** **Ashgarden Vale** (S; the Vale's denial; Saint Veddow's capped node), **Tollwood** (E; the gated Old Mast; the toll-war), **Pale Coast** (W; the maritime nodes; the drowned-tide). Each has an *honest window* confirming the failures radiate outward from the Reach (REV_001 corroboration; M5).
- **Caradril (SE):** the mid-game library/bank/battlefield. Reachable ~L5–6; the clearest mid-game M3 (commercial) and, at the top of the tier, M6/M9 (the Sealed Archive, act-paced L9–12).
- **Verdance Reaches (Ring 2 corridor entry):** the natural inland corridor opens at the top of the tier (L8+); the Nine Locks Sunken Stair (D34) is the first far node a player meets.

Full per-region change detail: `REGIONAL_ESCALATION_PATHS.md`.

## Failure States (Tier 2)

Summary — full logic in `FAILURE_STATES_AND_WORLD_CONSEQUENCES.md`:

- **Ignore the basin scheme:** the vote goes Reke's way; drainage begins; the upper shrine surfaces; master clock (#1) jumps. The world-state hardens against the player but the campaign continues.
- **Ignore the factions:** their Q*4 decision points resolve *off-screen* in the default (worst-for-the-player) direction — the Monopoly closes, the Reclamation advances, the Wardens fracture, the Circle/Gravecallers turn militant.
- **Antagonize a faction:** you make an enemy and open its rivals; retaliation is telegraphed and survivable (jail-arc, debt-arc, bounty-arc), never campaign-ending.
- **Fail a dungeon:** retreat costs time and a clue, not the run (every D-site has a retreat route).
- **Ignore the steering:** Sefra's clock (#8) runs; she nearly confesses or vanishes; the patron may strike (a telegraphed Tier 2 shock).

> **No ignored thread ends the game.** Each evolves into a new situation and new hooks.

## World-Clock Changes (Tier 2)

- **Basin (#2):** the tier's pivot. Player action can hold it at Stage 1–2; passivity pushes it to Stage 3 (upper shrine surfaces).
- **Master (#1):** rises one stage when the Basin reaches Stage 3; otherwise creeps on inaction.
- **Monopoly (#6) / Reclamation (#7):** rise to Stage 2–3; the Vyre–Quorrin deal (C1) closes if Caradril is ignored.
- **Wardens (#3) / Circle (#4) / Breaking (#5):** rise toward Stage 2–3 based on neglect; faction Q*4 capstones set their endgame posture.
- **Steering (#8):** resolves into REV_004 or goes cold.
- **Thinning (#10):** creeps in the background; visible via Harrowgast "thin" miners and Caradril's Sill refugees.
- **Caradril C1/C2/C3:** advance toward Stage 2–3 as the player visits or ignores the city.

## Solo-Play Balance Notes (Tier 2)

- **Power spike at L5:** the PC gains tier-2 features; encounters can ask harder questions, but node-diving still needs the **Drowned Flame / Warden's Lantern** survivability tech — hand these as faction rewards so a lone PC isn't blind.
- **Allies on tap:** Brother Hale (rites/companion), honest officials (Coalmont, Kade), faction contacts the player has cultivated, hireling guides (Brann in Tollwood, Wenna Roke at sea, Old Sashe in the fens).
- **Fallbacks for missing tools:** no *remove curse*? A Mourner/Warden rite clears Thin-touch and the Gravecaller's-Knock curse. No *teleport*? Travel is meaningful but never a dead end; the Verdance is a water-route shortcut. No reliable AoE? Faction muscle, terrain choke-points, and stealth bypasses substitute.
- Detail: `SOLO_CAMPAIGN_BALANCE_GUIDE.md`.

## Guidance for Off-Path Players (Tier 2)

- **The faction-avoider:** delivers REV_002/003/004 through exploration (node ruins), documents (filings, the Archive), and the fixer (Sefra) — no faction membership required.
- **The exploration-only player:** the node sites (D05/D14/D15/D16/D18/D20) carry M2/M3/M5/M6 fragments; the Custodian's Compass turns the whole region into a treasure-map of truth.
- **The Caradril-skipper:** M3, M6, and M9 are *also* reachable in the Reach (Othetha, the Tallow Man, the Deep Adit, the Relay-Vault, decoded songs) — the city is the clearest path, never the only one (`MYSTERY_WEB.md` anti-railroad note).
- **The far-rusher (heads to the Verdance/ports early):** the corridor is L8–12; telegraph the danger, let them go, and remember **far sources never deliver the apex** — they corroborate the theme and point home.
- **The passive player:** the world clocks deliver REV_002/003 via the surfacing shrine and active factions; the truth comes to them, slower and costlier.

## Tier Exit — What Unlocks Tier 3 (Level 11)

Tier 3 begins when **any** of these crystallizes (use whichever the player's path produces):

1. The player **confirms REV_005** (the Concord harvested the dead) via the Sealed Archive, Othetha, the dead, or decoded songs — the historical truth lands.
2. The player **travels to the far continent** (the Verdance corridor, the ports, or toward the Concord Heartlands) to confirm the Concord's true scale.
3. **Clocks force it:** the Basin reaches Stage 3–4 and the surfacing shrine + continental faction war make the historical truth undeniable.
4. The player **commits hard against (or with) a faction's endgame posture** at a Q*4 decision point, escalating the conflict to continental scale.

> Tier 3 content lives in `TIER_3_LEVELS_11_16.md`. Tier 2 only needs to deliver the player to the *threshold of the historical truth*, with the contemporary conspiracy fully exposed.
