## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY.** How each major faction *escalates, diverges, and reacts to the player* from local pressure (Tier 1) to regional (Tier 2) to continental (Tier 3) to the keystone (Tier 4). Use with `../07_factions/FACTION_TURN_RULES.md` to run faction turns and with `../02_runtime_state/FACTION_STATE.md` to track posture. **DM-only agendas are flagged; never narrate them.** Each faction's full file is in `../07_factions/major_factions/`. The Hollow Court's escalation has its own DM-only file: `VILLAIN_AND_APEX_THREAT_ESCALATION.md`.

> **Divergence principle:** a faction's posture at the endgame is set by its **Stage 8 Q*4 decision point** and the player's Tier 2–3 choices. Each major faction can end as the player's ally, enemy, or a third-party claimant. No faction is locked.

## Reachward Compact — local council → infiltrated infrastructure → keystone access

- **Tier 2:** pushes the basin vote (Reke); honest officials (Coalmont, Sela Coalmont) resist. **Q chain:** `QC1`→`QC4_THE_BASIN_VOTE`. **Clock:** #2 Basin Drains → Stage 2–3.
- **Tier 3:** the basin is breached/breaching; Reke is exposed (REV_003) or entrenched; the Compact becomes the *access pawn* for whoever controls the basin.
- **Tier 4:** the basin is the keystone's door; the Compact (Reke or a successor) is the infrastructure everyone fights over.
- **Divergence:** *Reke exposed and Coalmont empowered* → the Compact slows the basin, a player ally. *Reke entrenched* → the Compact opens the keystone for the Court.
- **DM-only:** infiltrated by the Hollow Court via Reke; the basin scheme is the Court's plan executed through the council.
- **Reacts to player:** lawful retaliation if crossed (Tide-Watch-style warrants); honest gratitude if the player defends the Reach.

---

## Cinder Ledger — frontier lender → continental monopolist → commodifier of the dead

- **Tier 2:** out-buys salvage; offers paid deniable work; courts the Remnant. **Q chain:** `QL1`→`QL4_THE_FACTORS_CONSCIENCE`. **Clock:** #6 Monopoly → Stage 2; Caradril C1 (the deal) advances.
- **Tier 3:** brokers the dead as commodity; the continental arm (Glassmere; Wessel Crane, Oren Glass, rival Sefwy Holt) activates; Vyre may betray Quorrin. **Clock:** #6 → Stage 3–4 (immortality sold).
- **Tier 4:** backs whoever lets it sell immortality; the keystone is the ultimate inventory.
- **Divergence:** *deal exposed/shattered* (turn Coll Riis, reach the Archive) → the Ledger weakened or split (Domic's conscience). *deal closed* → the Ledger and Remnant co-own the dead.
- **DM-only:** wants to monopolize and sell Remembrance; holds a vault relic that "remembers" the harvest (the Ledger of Quiet Debts).
- **Reacts to player:** debt-grip, enforcers (Mauld Tallow, Tallytooth Ren) if crossed; lucrative patronage if useful.

---

## Concord Remnant — scholars → would-be heirs → keystone racers

- **Tier 2:** sends agents into the Reach; races for nodes; teaches Script (Briss). **Q chain:** `QR1`→`QR4_THE_RECLAIMERS_GAMBIT`. **Clock:** #7 Reclamation → Stage 1–2.
- **Tier 3:** the inner circle (Quorrin, Sabine Ferrant, Hollin Vane) races for the Heartlands and the keystone; idealists may break from Quorrin. **Clock:** #7 → Stage 3–4. The Sealed Archive (REV_005/006) is their truth-cache.
- **Tier 4:** races the player and the Court to seize the keystone for a restored Concord — a *living tyranny*.
- **Divergence:** *idealists turned against Quorrin* (Briss, Tamsin Orr, Wenna Stone) → a reform-Remnant ally. *inner circle wins* → a third-party claimant racing the Court.
- **DM-only:** knows the harvest is real and wants to control it — repeating the original sin on purpose. Rank-and-file are idealistic dupes.
- **Reacts to player:** recruits a useful relic-finder; ambushes a rival once the player nears the truth.

---

## Ashen Wardens — overwhelmed rite-keepers → fracturing guardians → keepers of the seal

- **Tier 2:** strain under the crisis; Othetha guards the seal-truth; the Lantern relic recruits the player as a deniable agent. **Q chain:** `QW1`→`QW4_THE_OATH_AND_THE_DOUBT`. **Clock:** #3 Wardens Break → Stage 2.
- **Tier 3:** Othetha breaks her oath (REV_005/006 source); the order fractures or rallies. **Clock:** #3 → Stage 3.
- **Tier 4:** the Wardens guard or restore the seal — the backbone of the *seal* ending.
- **Divergence:** *player helps with rites / protects Othetha* → the Wardens rally, a seal ally. *neglected* → the order scatters, the seal weakens (accelerates #1).
- **DM-only:** unknowingly founded by Quietfall survivors to keep the harvest paused; Othetha is the sole local keeper of the true purpose.
- **Reacts to player:** warm to anyone who honors the dead; the most natural early ally; heresy-anxious about the Lantern relic.

---

## Mourners' Circle — folk grief-faith → militant grief → seal/destroy backbone

- **Tier 2:** furious at relic-buying and the basin; grief curdling to anger. **Q chain:** `QM1`→`QM4_SALT_OIL_AND_IRON`. **Clock:** #4 Circle Turns Militant → Stage 2.
- **Tier 3:** the old-songs truth confirmed (REV_005 folk-fragment via Wend, Ezrith Combe, Bryd, Sennet, far anchors); the Circle moves toward destroying a shrine. **Clock:** #4 → Stage 3.
- **Tier 4:** rallies to re-seal and let the dead rest — or, with the Gravecallers, to destroy.
- **Divergence:** *player honors the dead / earns Wend's trust* → a seal/synthesis ally. *neglected/angered* → the Circle radicalizes (overlaps the Gravecallers; a shrine shattered violently).
- **DM-only:** closest to the moral truth; some elders stockpile tools to destroy a shrine.
- **Reacts to player:** tests outsiders by how they treat the dead; deepest trust if the player passes.

---

## Gravecallers — feared cult → radicalizing fragment-knowers → the tragic mirror

- **Tier 2:** recruit the bereaved; radicals (Cole Ashby) inch toward the shrine; the Tallow Man teaches (M3/M5). **Q chain:** `QG1`→`QG4_THE_BREAKING_TOOLS`. **Clock:** #5 The Breaking → Stage 2.
- **Tier 3:** cells acquire breaking-tools + a shrine map; a breach attempt looms. **Clock:** #5 → Stage 3.
- **Tier 4:** Cole Ashby's cell attempts to shatter the keystone — the *destroy* ending's catastrophe risk.
- **Divergence:** *player learns from them / steers them* → the Tallow Man's dead-witness (REV_005/006) and a route to the *synthesis* (break it *safely*). *radicals unchecked* → a catastrophic solo breach (the dead freed destructively).
- **DM-only:** right about the harvest; would break it catastrophically; underestimate the disaster total destruction causes.
- **Reacts to player:** welcome a curious newcomer; persecuted and desperate; sometimes right.

---

## Hollow Court — dormant apex (full detail: VILLAIN_AND_APEX_THREAT_ESCALATION.md)

- **Tier 2 (DM-only):** dormant beneath the basin; acts only through Reke and the steering of Sefra; the player is an unwitting probe. **Clock:** #1 Harvest Restarts (MASTER) → Stage 2.
- **Tier 3 (DM-only):** as the basin breaches, the Court manifests to those who enter; Veyl prepares to make its case. **Clock:** #1 → Stage 3. **Apex gate (REV_007) opens at L13+.**
- **Tier 4:** Veyl bargains/recruits; Orre fights if pressed; Maire may defect (REV_010). The endgame is the Court's case vs. the player's choice. **Clock:** #1 → Stage 4 → resolved.
- **Never narrate the Court's existence/agenda before REV_007.** See `VILLAIN_AND_APEX_THREAT_ESCALATION.md` and `SECRET_PROTECTION_MATRIX.md`.

## Caradril City Blocs (Tier 2–4)

- **Tidewater Council / Charter Houses:** complacent (C2); resolve the open charter (Ledger/old-House default, or Dann-reform if the player backs `Q_THE_OPEN_CHARTER`); an endgame asset (reformed) or obstacle (complacent).
- **Tide-Watch:** Kade is a turnable lawful ally; enforces charter law; weighs the Syndicate problem.
- **Salt Syndicate / the Hush:** the quiet-coin trade and information-brokering; fair, dangerous shortcuts to mid-arc clues; feed C1/C3.

## Endgame Posture Matrix (set by Q*4 + player choices)

| Faction | If allied | If opposed | If ignored (default) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compact | slows the basin; keystone defended | basin opened against the player | vote goes Reke's way; keystone opens |
| Ledger | weakened/split; an asset | enforcer war; a hindrance | deal closes; commodifies the dead |
| Remnant | reform-Remnant ally | keystone racer/enemy | seizes the keystone path |
| Wardens | rally; seal backbone | scatter; seal weakens | order fractures |
| Mourners | seal/synthesis ally | radicalize | turn militant |
| Gravecallers | safe-break / synthesis route | purged/hostile | catastrophic solo breach |
| Hollow Court | (joined — a dark ending) | confronted at the keystone | restart succeeds (worst default) |
