## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY — how the world changes when the player ignores the main arc and chases side content instead.** The campaign is open-world: the player is *never* forced onto the main track. This file gives the AI DM the concrete, named world-state at each stage of delay, techniques to reintroduce arc pressure without railroading, and reassurance that side content still feeds the main arc. **The world clocks deliver the truth to a passive player anyway** (`REVELATION_MAP.md`: no REV strictly requires the player to chase it) — just slower, costlier, and with fewer endgame options. **DM-only causes stay DM-only; only surface consequences and rumors are player-facing.**

---

## After 1 Arc Cycle of Delay — Player Finishes Tier 2 (L10) Without Engaging the Main Arc

The player has done faction work, regional quests, and dungeon runs, but never chased the basin/shrine/Reke truth.

**What faction moves happen (named):**
- **Reke** wins the basin vote; **Pevin Oss**'s forged filings stand; drainage begins (Clock #2→2–3). The Reed Holms families are evicted.
- **Domic Sael** and **Mauld Tallow** corner Reach salvage (Clock #6→2); **Veska Dunn** takes the Deep Adit for the Remnant (#7→2).
- In Caradril, the **Vyre–Quorrin deal** forms; **Coll Riis** copies the proof in fear (C1→2); the Council sleeps (C2→2).

**What regions change:**
- The Reach openly debates and begins the drainage; the upper shrine starts to surface.
- The Vale closes its eyes (Tindle's cover-up, V1→2); the Coast's drowned-tide rises (C-PC1→2).

**What rumors now circulate:**
- *"They've started draining the old basin at Hollowmere."*
- *"The Ledger and the Lamplighter scholars have some deal nobody's supposed to know."*

**What is harder to do later:**
- The basin is partly drained — re-sealing it later costs more (the player must reverse drainage, not just prevent it).
- The player banked fewer faction allies; the endgame coalition starts thinner.
- REV_004 (the steering) may go cold if **Sefra Quick** vanished (Clock #8); the personal beat is missed but not blocking.

## After 2 Arc Cycles of Delay — Player Reaches Tier 3 (L11–16) Without Engaging the Main Arc

The player has gone far-continent, done political/dungeon work, but still hasn't chased the historical truth.

**Escalated faction moves (named):**
- The Hollowmere shrine **surfaces** (Clock #2→3–4; #1 MASTER→3); the first deliberate local harvest occurs (DM-only); wrong-come-backs appear near the basin.
- **Othetha** breaks her oath as the Wardens fracture (#3→3) — the REV_005/006 source now leaks regardless.
- The **Vyre–Quorrin deal closes** (C1→3); the Ledger sells death-cheating to Caradril's rich (#6→3); **Hollin Vane** secures the Ruin'd Crown and the Ash-Crown (#7→3–4).
- **Cole Ashby**'s cell gets breaking-tools (#5→3); **Wend**'s Circle turns militant (#4→3).
- The **thinning** becomes a regional pattern (#10→2–3); **Onaia Veen** and **Ysarra** document it.

**What regions change:**
- The Reach is openly afraid; the basin is a no-go zone of failing rites.
- Caradril's denial cracks (C3→3); the Sill's thin-born refugees swell.
- The far continent's Reclamation race (Heartlands) and continental Monopoly (Glassmere) are in full swing.

**What rumors now circulate:**
- *"The old shrine's come up out of the basin — the dead won't lie quiet near it."*
- *"The Wardens are tearing themselves apart over something the old order did."*
- *"That magister means to raise the Concord again in the far Heartlands."*

**What is harder to do later:**
- REV_005/006 are now delivered *to* the player by the fracturing Wardens and the surfacing shrine — but the player arrives at the apex gate under-informed about *who* benefits, having skipped the convergence (REV_003+005+006), so REV_007 lands later and harder.
- The endgame rivals (Remnant, Ledger) are far stronger and better-positioned for the keystone race.
- The basin is breaching — the seal ending is harder; the Gravecaller breach risk is live.

## After Full Tier Delay — Player Reaches Tier 4 (L17–20) Without Engaging the Main Arc

The player has ignored the arc into the endgame window. The world has nearly resolved without them.

**Crisis-level world-state (named):**
- The basin is **breached** (Clock #2→keystone reopened; #1 MASTER→4); the keystone is accessible and the harvest can restart at scale (DM-only).
- The rival claimants **converge on the basin**: **Hollin Vane**'s Remnant, **Vyre**'s Ledger, and **Cole Ashby**'s Gravecaller cell race for the keystone; **Othetha** and **Wend** move to defend the seal — *without the player having organized any of them.*
- The **Hollow Court** (Veyl, Maire, Orre) is awake and acting; the master clock is one step from completing.

**What is locked off / impossible:**
- **The Transform (synthesis) ending is effectively unreachable** — without having met **Maire** (REV_010), banked the **Sunmark** model (Sael Sunmark), and built a coalition, the player cannot end the Court from within. Seal/Destroy/Seize/Restart remain.
- The cleanest **Seal** is hard — the basin is already breached, so the player must re-seal an open shrine against active claimants rather than hold a sealed one.
- The player arrives **alone or nearly so** — few banked allies, no artifacts earned in Tier 3, no Ash-Crown legitimacy.

**What is still possible:**
- The player can still **descend, confront Veyl, and choose** (Path 8 Partial/Bittersweet, or Path 1 Confrontation) — the endgame always delivers itself to the player's doorstep.
- A late, desperate Alliance (Path 2) can still be scrambled by exposing the truth to the converging factions.
- Even total failure produces a **playable bleak epilogue** (Path 9), not a dead screen.

## Reintroduction Techniques (no railroading)

The AI DM reintroduces arc pressure through the *world acting*, never by forcing the player:

1. **Rumor escalation.** Each clock stage gives a louder, more specific rumor (see `CONTINENTAL_PRESSURE_TIMELINE.md`). The player overhears them organically — at inns, from NPCs they already know, in markets.
2. **A known NPC brings the arc to the player.** **Sefra Quick** confesses her unease (REV_004); **Reeve Coalmont** begs help against the basin vote; **Iola Wend** carries a Mourner plea; **Warden Hale** asks for aid with failing rites. These are people the player already trusts.
3. **Side content surfaces an arc clue.** A dungeon the player dives for treasure contains a Concord Script fragment; a faction job exposes a relic that "remembers" the dead (REV_002). The arc finds the player through what they *already* want to do.
4. **A consequence reaches the player's home/allies.** The basin drainage floods a place the player cares about; a wrong-come-back appears in a settlement they protected; an ally is hurt by a faction move. The arc becomes personal.
5. **A faction recruits the player against the arc-villain (unknowingly).** The Remnant (Veska Dunn), the Wardens (Othetha), or even the Ledger hires the player for a job that turns out to touch the basin/shrine — pulling them in through paid work.
6. **The endgame comes home.** At Tier 4, if all else is ignored, the harvest reaches the player's region (`CONTINENTAL_PRESSURE_TIMELINE.md` T4-3); the player must respond or watch the world fall.

> **The rule:** the world *acts*, and the player *reacts on their own terms*. The DM never says "you must go to the basin." The DM makes the basin matter to something the player already cares about.

## What Side Content Still Accomplishes (even when the player doesn't know it)

A player who only does side content is *still* advancing the main arc, because the arc is woven into everything:

- **Faction quest chains (Q*1–Q*4)** set each faction's endgame posture (`FACTION_ESCALATION_PATHS.md`) — so even "just" helping the Wardens banks a seal ally.
- **Regional dungeons** (D05–D36) each carry an arc clue fragment (REV_002/005 echoes, Concord Script) — so "just" delving for treasure surfaces the truth.
- **Far-continent allies** (Sefwy Holt, Senna Crale, Mira Cindra, Bryd/Tess Brackhold, Tamur Wend-Khar, Sael Sunmark, Wenna Stone) are banked by regional play — so "just" exploring builds the endgame coalition.
- **Artifacts** (the Drowned Flame, the Ash-Crown, the Custodian's Compass, the Sealed Archive Key) are earned through regional/dungeon play — so "just" adventuring assembles the endgame levers.
- **Survivability tech** (the Drowned Flame for Thin-touch immunity) comes from Ring 1 side content — so side content makes the endgame *survivable*.

The DM should *quietly* credit this: when the player finally engages the arc, their accumulated side-work pays off as allies, levers, and knowledge. Nothing was wasted.

## Failure States That Are Serious But Not Campaign-Ending

If the arc is very badly delayed, the world reaches sober-but-playable states (full logic in `FAILURE_STATES_AND_WORLD_CONSEQUENCES.md`):

- **The Drained Reach:** the basin is dry, the shrine surfaced, the Reach gripped by failing rites and fear — but the player can still descend and act. The home region is harder, not lost.
- **The Sold Dead:** the Vyre–Quorrin deal closed; Caradril's rich buy death-cheating; the Ledger and Remnant co-own the dead — a well-resourced endgame obstacle, not a wall.
- **The Militant Circle:** the Mourners and Gravecallers move to shatter a shrine; the player may have to *prevent a catastrophe* (a bad mid-tier ending to scramble against), not just stop the Court.
- **The Strong Rivals:** Hollin Vane holds the Ash-Crown and the Heartlands; the endgame keystone race is genuinely contested — the player arrives as one claimant among several.
- **The Lonely Descent:** the player reaches the apex with few allies and no synthesis path — they can still seal, destroy, seize, or live with a bittersweet partial victory (Path 8).

None of these ends the campaign. Each is a *harder, sadder* version of the endgame the player still gets to play.
