## AI Use

**DM-ONLY.** How the AI DM keeps continuity and the main arc intact when the player moves through levels 5–20 **out of order, partially, or in unexpected directions**. The campaign is open-world by mandate: the player may join no faction, visit regions in any order, skip the main arc, rush the apex, or solve mysteries by unplanned means. This file is the playbook for absorbing all of that without breaking canon, secrecy, or pacing. **The convergence is geographic and cosmological, not scripted — let the world pull the player home, don't push.**

---

## The Five Continuity Anchors (What Stays Fixed)

No matter the player's path, these never change:

1. **The keystone is the center.** The Hollowmere basin is the lowest point and the harvest's heart; the dead drift there; the failures radiate outward. The player always ends up at the center because the center comes to them.
2. **The revelation order holds.** REV_001 → 002/003/004 → 005/006/009 → 007/010 → 008. A clue may *hint* up a layer but never *delivers* a higher truth out of phase (`MAIN_ARC_REVELATION_SEQUENCE.md`).
3. **The apex is gated and protected.** REV_007 (the Court lives) is never named before its L13+ convergence/descent/heretic gate (`SECRET_PROTECTION_MATRIX.md`).
4. **The clocks advance regardless.** The world moves whether the player engages or not (`CAMPAIGN_ESCALATION_TIMELINE.md`).
5. **The endgame is a choice, vertical, under Hollowmere.** No far land is the keystone; no second Court; no single correct ending.

Everything else — which factions, which regions, which order, which approach — is the player's.

## Handling Non-Linear Movement

### The player visits regions out of order
- **Fine.** Each region carries REV-fragments and corroboration; none gates a revelation. Track which fragments the player has (`KNOWN_CLUES.md`) and surface the *next-layer* clue when prerequisites are met, wherever the player is.
- **Far before near:** if the player rushes the far continent at L11+, let them — far sources corroborate the theme and build leverage, and the Compass/convergence pull them home for the apex.

### The player skips the main arc for side content
- **Side content is the arc.** Let the master clocks advance in the background and periodically *show* the world moving (a surfacing shrine, a faction-war headline, a thin-born tide). The side-content player accumulates fragments and is delivered the truth by the world.

### The player rushes the apex early
- **Gate it, don't dump it.** The upper shrine is dangerous; the Court is dormant/sealed; the player gets *fragments and lethal danger*, not REV_007, until the convergence/level gate. Telegraph the lethality so they withdraw and prepare.

### The player solves a mystery by an unplanned method
- **Honor it.** If the player reasons out REV_003 (Reke) from a clue you didn't plan, confirm what they can prove. If they reach a true answer cleverly, award it — the three-clue rule means many valid paths exist (`AI_DM_CORE_RULES.md` roll/clue policy).

### The player ignores or destroys a faction the arc "expected"
- **Reroute.** A broken Wardens can't seal; a broken Remnant can't seize — narrowing the player's own endgame options, which is a *consequence*, not a contradiction. Power vacuums get filled by rivals (`FAILURE_STATES_AND_WORLD_CONSEQUENCES.md`).

## The "Where Is the Player in the Arc?" Checklist (Run at Session Start)

1. **What REVs are confirmed?** (`KNOWN_CLUES.md` / mystery-state tracker.) This sets the *phase*, not the level.
2. **What clocks are at what stage?** (`WORLD_CLOCKS.md`.) This sets the world-state.
3. **Which factions are allied/opposed/ignored?** (`FACTION_STATE.md`.) This sets who acts and how.
4. **Which regions are stabilized/fallen/etc.?** (`REGIONAL_ESCALATION_PATHS.md` outcome logic.)
5. **What is the player pulling toward right now?** Lean into it; surface the next-layer hook on that thread.
6. **What hasn't moved in a while?** Advance an ignored clock off-screen and show a consequence.

---

## Surfacing the Next Hook (Anti-Stall)

If the player is between beats or unsure where to go, surface a hook toward the *next revelation layer they're eligible for* — drawn from a thread they've already touched:

- **Eligible for REV_002 but stalled?** A relic acts up; a Gravecaller reaches out; the Ledger offers a salvage job.
- **Eligible for REV_005 but stalled?** Othetha's strain shows; a Sealed Archive rumor; a decoded song fragment.
- **Eligible for REV_007 but stalled?** The convergence "clicks" via a heretic contact; the shrine breaches; Maire reaches out.

> Never push a *specific* faction or region — push the *layer*, via whatever thread the player likes. The player chooses the door; the DM ensures a door is always visible.

---

## Reconciling Player Choices With Prepared Content

- **Use prepared content first** (`AI_DM_CORE_RULES.md` prime directive). When the player goes somewhere prepared, run the authored material; when they go somewhere unprepared, improvise *connective tissue* consistent with canon and **record durable improvisation** into state/canon.
- **Never improvise major content** (a new faction, a new node, a second Court, a far keystone). If the player demands a major answer the world doesn't have, route it back to the authored truth (the keystone is under Hollowmere; the conspiracy is local and singular).
- **Keep the secrecy spine** no matter what the player does — the apex stays protected even if the player's path is wildly off-track.

## Continuity Failure Modes to Avoid

| Anti-pattern | Why it breaks the campaign | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Forcing the player onto the "main" faction/region | railroad; kills agency | lean into the player's chosen thread; the truth has many paths |
| Dumping REV_007 because the player reached the shrine early | spoils the keystone reveal | gate it; give fragments + lethal danger |
| Letting a far clue name the Court | breaks apex protection | far sources corroborate, never name |
| Freezing the world while the player ignores it | the world feels dead; consequences vanish | advance clocks; show faction moves |
| Inventing a second keystone/Court for an off-track player | contradicts canon | the conspiracy is singular and vertical |
| Blocking a clever player solution | breaks fairness | honor valid solutions; award the truth |

---

## State Discipline (Every Session End)

To keep a non-linear campaign coherent, update at session end (`SESSION_END_UPDATE_CHECKLIST.md`):
- `KNOWN_CLUES.md` / `HIDDEN_CLUES.md` (what the player actually knows — never assume connections they haven't made).
- `WORLD_CLOCKS.md` (advance/slow clocks).
- `FACTION_STATE.md`, `RELATIONSHIPS.md`, `NPC_MEMORY.md`, `CONSEQUENCES.md`.
- The mystery-state tracker (which REVs are confirmed/misunderstood/locked).

> In a non-linear campaign, **the state files are the source of truth for where the player is** — not the level, not the "expected" act. Run the player from their state, not from a script.
