## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY.** The structure of the Act 5 / Tier 4 endgame: the **player approaches** (how the player gets there), the **five canonical outcomes** (what the player decides about the Remembrance), and the **resolution/epilogue logic** (what the world becomes). The *choice* and its costs (REV_008) become player-known in Act 5; learn the costs through play. **There is no "correct" ending** — each has a real price. DM-only mechanism detail lives in `../10_dungeons_and_ruins/THE_UNDER_SHRINE_APPROACH.md` (D23). Reach this only past the REV_007 gate.

> **Open-world rule:** the player chooses the *approach* (the verb) and the *outcome* (the destination) somewhat independently. Many approaches can reach the same outcome; one approach (synthesis) can only reach one outcome. The DM honors clever player solutions even if unplanned.

---

## The Decision (M10 / REV_008)

> **What should be done with the Remembrance — the harvestable substance of every soul?**

The player has learned (or can learn) that **every ending has a terrible price**, and that a **synthesis** is possible but hard. The endgame is the *enacting* of a decision, reachable many ways.

---

## Part 1 — The Nine Approaches (How the Player Gets There)

These are the *verbs* — the methods a player uses at the keystone. Each maps to one or more outcomes (Part 2). The DM should support whichever the player builds toward across Tiers 2–4.

| # | Approach | What it looks like | Reaches outcome(s) | Solo-supportable via |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Direct confrontation** | Fight through to the keystone; face Orre/Veyl | seal, destroy, seize (by force) | terrain, allies, Veyl's built-in weaknesses |
| 2 | **Alliance-building** | Forge a faction coalition against the Court | seal, transform, seize | banked Tier 2–3 allies |
| 3 | **Ritual / counter-ritual** | Use the Concord's own seal-rite/harvest mechanism | seal, restart-controlled, transform | Warden seal-knowledge, the Vessel |
| 4 | **Artifact strategy** | Deploy the Vessel / Harvest Engine Shard / Last Voice | any (depends on artifact) | Tier 3 gated artifacts |
| 5 | **Political / faction victory** | Dismantle the Court's proxy control (Reke, the basin, the deal) | seal (by denial), seize | exposing Reke, holding the basin |
| 6 | **Sacrifice / costly containment** | Pay a personal/cosmic price to contain the harvest | seal, transform (partial) | the Vessel; the player's own Remembrance |
| 7 | **Mystery-resolution / exposure** | Make the truth public; let the world decide | seize (by the people), transform | the Sealed Archive, the Last Voice, allies |
| 8 | **Partial / bittersweet** | Achieve a flawed version of an outcome | any (degraded) | whatever the player has |
| 9 | **Failure & aftermath** | The player loses the race / does nothing | restart (default), destroy (Gravecaller), seize (rival) | — |

## Part 2 — The Five Canonical Outcomes (What the Player Decides)

Each is the player's decision about the Remembrance, with its real cost. No outcome is "correct."

### Outcome A — SEAL IT *(Wardens / Mourners path)*
- **What:** Re-seal the shrine; let the dead rest; the harvest stops.
- **Cost:** Resurrection mostly ends; civilization loses a crutch; the Quiet Country survives but the living lose easy hope against death. Quiet, costly, humane.
- **Reached by:** confrontation (force the seal), ritual (the Warden seal-rite), alliance (Wardens + Mourners), political (deny the Court the basin), sacrifice.
- **Requires:** Warden seal-knowledge (Othetha) or the keystone severed; allies to hold off rival claimants.
- **World after:** the thinning slows but is not healed; the Wardens are vindicated; resurrection-dependent powers (Ledger, some clergy) lose their footing.

### Outcome B — RESTART IT CONTROLLED *(Hollow Court path)*
- **What:** Let the Court resume a "gentler" harvest; the player joins, allows, or fails to stop them.
- **Cost:** Stability and immortality for some; the dead consumed forever; the Court rules as immortal stewards. Powerful, monstrous, tempting.
- **Reached by:** agreeing with Veyl; failing to stop the master clock; joining the Court (a dark patron ending).
- **Requires:** nothing the player must *build* — it is the default if the player does nothing, or a deliberate choice if persuaded.
- **World after:** an age of order under the Court; the thinning addressed by consumption; a slow horror beneath a calm surface.

### Outcome C — SEIZE IT *(Remnant or Ledger path)*
- **What:** Hand the machine to a single living faction (a restored Concord, or the Ledger's monopoly), or take it yourself.
- **Cost:** A new tyranny or a market in souls; the original sin repeated by living hands.
- **Reached by:** alliance with the Remnant/Ledger; confrontation (take it by force); exposure (the people demand a "responsible" living owner).
- **Requires:** a faction claimant who wins the keystone race (their Tier 2–3 posture).
- **World after:** empire or commerce built on the dead; the player as kingmaker or new tyrant.

### Outcome D — DESTROY IT *(Gravecaller path)*
- **What:** Shatter the harvest; free the dead; resurrection ends entirely.
- **Cost:** Chaotic liberation; the living/dead boundary destabilizes; possible catastrophe (the Gravecallers underestimate it). The freed dead may not all pass cleanly.
- **Reached by:** the Gravecaller breaking-tools; the Harvest Engine Shard misused; sabotage; alliance with Cole Ashby.
- **Requires:** breaking-tools + a shrine map, or the Shard; ideally guidance to break it *safely* (the synthesis-adjacent variant).
- **World after:** the dead freed; resurrection gone; a destabilized but *honest* world; the Quiet Country's fate uncertain.

### Outcome E — TRANSFORM IT *(the synthesis — hardest, best, not guaranteed)*
- **What:** Invent/enable a *consensual* system where the dead choose whether to be remembered.
- **Cost:** The hardest path; requires uniting unlikely allies, turning Maire against Veyl (REV_010), and often a key artifact (the Vessel / Last Voice). It is not guaranteed and demands deep play.
- **Reached by:** alliance (a broad coalition), ritual/artifact (a new consensual rite), Maire's defection, the Sunmark grove-faith model.
- **Requires:** REV_010 confirmed; Maire turned; the Last Voice or the Vessel; a coalition; the Sunmark contrast as a working model.
- **World after:** the only outcome that *addresses the thinning without consuming the dead*; the Court ends itself; a new, fragile, just settlement between the living and the dead. The "best" ending — earned, never handed.

## Part 4 — Resolution & Epilogue Logic

At the climax, resolve in this order:

1. **Determine the outcome** (A–E) from the player's enacted decision.
2. **Resolve the master clock (#1)** accordingly (sealed / restarted / seized / destroyed / transformed).
3. **Resolve the Thinning (#10):** only Transform (E) addresses it without consuming the dead; Seal (A) slows it; Restart (B) "manages" it by consumption; Seize (C) mirrors B under living owners; Destroy (D) frees the dead but leaves the Quiet Country's fate uncertain.
4. **Resolve each faction** per its endgame posture (`FACTION_ESCALATION_PATHS.md` matrix) and whether it stood with/against the player.
5. **Resolve each region** per its escalation state (`REGIONAL_ESCALATION_PATHS.md`: stabilize/fall/revolt/ally/corrupt).
6. **Resolve the Custodians:** Veyl (agreed-with / outmaneuvered / destroyed / transformed), Maire (defected/saved/lost), Orre (fought/bypassed).
7. **Resolve major NPCs** per their banked relationships (`NPC_MEMORY.md`, `RELATIONSHIPS.md`).
8. **Resolve the player's personal threads** (REV_004 steering; whether the PC is thin-born; artifacts claimed/lost/corrupted).

## Part 5 — Artifact Fates (Claimed / Lost / Destroyed / Corrupted)

- **The Harvest Engine Shard** — used to enact the chosen outcome (any), destroyed (Destroy), or claimed by the Court/a rival (a corrupted ending).
- **The Quiet Country Vessel** — the containment/sacrifice lever (Seal/Transform); can hold a Custodian (a late synthesis option); misused → a wrong-come-back catastrophe.
- **The Last Voice** — the dead's witness and synthesis ally (Transform/Exposure); claimed by the Quiet Country's envoy in the epilogue.
- **The Custodian's Compass / Ash-Crown** — political legitimacy levers (Seize/Exposure); inert after the harvest's fate is decided.

## Part 6 — Solo-Play Guarantees (Endgame)

- **At least one non-combat win condition is always live** for the player's path.
- **No outcome requires winning a fight** — even Confrontation has weaknesses, terrain, and Maire's lever.
- **The player arrives with allies and levers**, not alone — banked across Tiers 2–3.
- **Death is meaningful, never cheap;** retreat exists; the Vessel/Last Voice/Maire can resolve the climax without a damage race.
