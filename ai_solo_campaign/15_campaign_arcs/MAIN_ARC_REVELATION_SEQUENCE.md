## AI Use

**DM-ONLY.** The arc-level companion to `../11_mysteries_and_secrets/REVELATION_MAP.md`: it shows, tier by tier, **how each main-arc secret is earned and when it may be revealed**, and it re-states the **redundant (3+) discovery routes** so no single path gates the truth. This file does not redefine the REVs — it sequences them for the level 5–20 arc and enforces the apex protection. Always defer to `REVELATION_MAP.md` and `MYSTERY_WEB.md` for the authoritative clue lists. **The Hollow Court (REV_007) is the keystone reveal — never surface it before its gate.**

## Tier 2 Reveals — Earned How, Revealed When

### REV_002 — Remembrance is a substance someone collects *(M3; gate L5–8)*
**Earned by any of (need ~1 demonstration + context):**
1. **Magic** — study a Remembrance relic (Arcana/Religion DC 15): it *stores a person* (the Grief-Glass, "old glass", a vault relic).
2. **Teaching** — the Gravecallers (Tallow Man) explain what the dead taught them.
3. **Mercantile** — the Ledger vault relic / Ashmarket quiet-coin / Crucible smelting "incidents" show someone *collecting on purpose*.
4. **Scholarly** — Concord Script texts (Briss/Remnant) describe "the keeping of the dead's pattern."
5. **Far echo (oblique)** — "old glass" (Emberfell) / "humming iron" (Karran) prove the substance exists continent-wide.
**Reveal ceiling:** keep the machine *contemporary* — never its age, never the Court.

### REV_003 — A trusted authority steers the basin reopening *(M4/Reke; gate L5–8)*
**Earned by any of:** Coalmont's votes (Investigation/Insight DC 13); the fabricated water-rights filings (Pevin Oss) + a Caradril misfiled charter record; tailing Reke to the shore at night; the Ledger factor's grumbling; Sela Coalmont's suspicion that the Compact is steered against the Reach.
**Reveal ceiling:** reveal only "someone above him" — **never** that Reke's master is the Court.

### REV_004 — You were steered here *(M8; gate L5–12, flexible)*
**Earned by any of:** pressing Sefra (she confesses she doesn't know whom she served); following the patron's money to a cutout; the player noticing the pattern of convenient leads; the Hush selling a fragment of the correspondent thread.
**Reveal ceiling:** the cutout stays Reke-adjacent and murky — never traces to the Court.

### REV_005 *(may begin late Tier 2)* — see Tier 3.

## Tier 3 Reveals — Earned How, Revealed When

### REV_005 — The Concord harvested the dead *(M6; gate L9–12)*
**Earned by any of (3+ independent):**
1. **Archives** — the Sealed Archive (Caradril; clearest written proof; Script-gated).
2. **Confession** — Warden Othetha breaks her oath (trust-gated).
3. **The dead testify** — a Quietfall-era Remembrance via the Tallow Man.
4. **Song/folk** — Wend's decoded old songs ("the Concord ate the dead"), corroborated by the far old-songs network.
5. **Far surface-proof** — the Ruin'd Crown (Heartlands) / the Highmark Frozen Works' cold-preserved records.
**Reveal ceiling:** the Court's *survival* stays only hinted.

### REV_006 — The Quietfall was deliberate *(M9; gate L9–12)*
**Earned by any of:** the Sealed Archive's planned, signed Quietfall; Othetha's confession; the Tallow Man's witness; Concord Script inscriptions at shrine/node-sites.
**Reveal ceiling:** that the Custodians who did it *still exist* (REV_007) stays hidden. Do not separate REV_005 and REV_006 by more than an act.

### REV_009 — The afterlife is finite and was nearly drained *(supporting cosmological; gate L9–14)*
**Earned by any of:** deep study of resurrection failure + relic analysis; the thin-born (the Sill refugees, "thin" Harrowgast miners); the dead/Court testimony ("the empty country"); far cosmic echoes (Esha's "the Sky has gone thin," Doll's "a far root aches" — deeply oblique).
**Reveal ceiling:** that the Court caused it deliberately folds into REV_005/007 — not stated here.

### REV_007 — THE HOLLOW COURT LIVES *(M7; APEX; gate L13–16)*
**The campaign's keystone reveal. No single-source path.** Earned only by:
1. **Physical** — descend to the under-shrine and meet them (late-arc, lethal-telegraphed).
2. **Convergence** — assemble REV_003 (Reke serves someone) + REV_005 (surviving sin) + REV_006 (deliberate Quietfall) into the only explanation that fits (inference; DM confirms).
3. **Heretic/forbidden** — Custodian Maire reaches out, OR the Tallow Man's dead name "the ones who would not pass."
**Apex protection (absolute):** before L13, the Court is a *legend*; no clue, NPC, or site names it as real; far-continent sources never deliver it. The player's likely Act 3 guess (Quorrin/the Remnant is the apex; Reke is the top) is deliberately fostered — the Court is the true apex *above* them. See `SECRET_PROTECTION_MATRIX.md`.

### REV_010 — Maire's heresy: the Court could end itself *(synthesis key; gate L14+)*
**Earned by any of:** Maire's direct contact in the under-shrine; perceiving Orre's hawkishness vs. Maire's doubt; the far hopeful contrast (Sunmark's working grove-faith) + Gravecaller fragments suggesting a consensual system is conceivable.
**Reveal ceiling:** endgame-only; it unlocks the hardest, best-outcome ending.

## Tier 4 Reveals — Earned How, Revealed When

### REV_008 — The choice and its costs *(M10; gate L17–20)*
**Not a truth-clue — consequence-knowledge.** The player should learn the *costs* before choosing:
1. **Gravecaller warning** — destroying the shrine = catastrophe.
2. **Warden warning** — sealing = the end of resurrection.
3. **The Court's case** — Veyl's controlled restart = stability + horror.
4. **The heretic's synthesis** — Maire's case that the dead could *choose*, corroborated by the Sunmark contrast.
**Reveal:** the *choice* becomes player-known in Act 5; the full cost-set is the final layer. Endings: seal / restart-controlled / seize / destroy / transform (`ENDGAME_STRUCTURE.md`).

## Redundancy Verification (Three-Clue Rule)

Every REV above is served by **≥3 independent clue sources** spanning different regions, NPCs, factions, and approaches (social / exploration / ritual / scholarly / mercantile / forbidden). This means:

- **No single faction is a chokepoint.** Avoid the Remnant? Othetha + the Tallow Man + decoded songs still deliver REV_005/006. Avoid the Gravecallers? Relics + archives still deliver REV_002.
- **No single region is a chokepoint.** Skip Caradril? The Reach (Othetha, the Relay-Vault, the Deep Adit, the Keystone Approach) still delivers M3/M6/M9. Skip the far continent? It is corroboration, never the only path.
- **No single roll gates the story.** Failed checks cost time/partial info, never the truth.

## Pacing Guard Rails (Re-Stated for the Arc)

- **Tier 2 = conspiratorial & contemporary.** REV_002/003/004; the machine is *current*, not ancient; the Court is unnamed.
- **Tier 3 lower = historical & shattering.** REV_005/006/009; the Concord's sin; the Court still a legend.
- **Tier 3 upper / Tier 4 = apex & choice.** REV_007/010/008; the Court named only at the gate, only by convergence/descent/heretic.
- **If the player rushes the shrine early:** do not dump REV_007. The upper shrine is dangerous, the Court dormant/sealed; the player gets *fragments and lethal danger*, then more clues — never the apex.
- **The world clocks deliver the truth to a passive player** at a slower pace; no REV strictly *requires* the player to chase it.
