## AI Use

**DM-ONLY. Do not summarize, quote, or paraphrase to the player.** The escalation of the campaign's antagonist hierarchy across levels 5–20. The antagonist **escalates by revelation, not by power level alone**: faceless dread → human conspiracy → historical sin → named apex → a person with an argument. Use this to pace the threat and to protect the apex secret (`../11_mysteries_and_secrets/SECRET_PROTECTION_MATRIX.md`). Stat references are in `../13_encounters_and_bestiary/BOSS_AND_APEX_THREATS.md` and `../07_factions/major_factions/HOLLOW_COURT.md`. **The Hollow Court must never be a "you must out-damage me" wall.**

---

## The Antagonist Hierarchy (each "right" from their own view)

1. **The Hollow Court (apex hidden power)** — surviving Custodians persisting as preserved Remembrance beneath Hollowmere; restarting the harvest "to save everyone." Leader: **Custodian Veyl.** Members: **Orre** (hardliner), **Maire** (doubter; the synthesis lever). Cannot easily act in the living world; works through agents.
2. **Surface agents (the Court's hands):** **Councilor Reke** (knowing agent), cutout-hired deniable hands, leaked Remembrances (unwitting scouts).
3. **Rival claimant — Concord Remnant inner circle (Quorrin Vane, Sabine Ferrant, Hollin Vane):** want to seize the harvest to rebuild the Concord's empire; think they are the heroes.
4. **Rival claimant — Cinder Ledger (Mother Calla Vyre):** want to monopolize and sell immortality.
5. **The tragic mirror — Gravecallers (Cole Ashby, the Tallow Man):** want to *free* the dead by shattering the shrines; would cause catastrophe.

The player can ally with, oppose, expose, or replace any of these.

## Escalation by Revelation, Not by Hit-Points

| Tier | What the player perceives as "the threat" | What is actually escalating |
|---|---|---|
| **1 (1–4)** | Faceless dread (the leakage) + a smooth politician (Reke) | The master clock creeping; the steering placing the player |
| **2 (5–10)** | A human conspiracy — Reke steers, someone collects the dead, the player was used | REV_002/003/004; the rival claimants (Remnant/Ledger) rising; the Court still unseen |
| **3 lower (11–14)** | The Concord's historical sin; the rival claimants racing for the keystone | REV_005/006/009; the Reclamation peaking; the basin breaching |
| **3 upper (13–16)** | The apex named: the Hollow Court lives | REV_007; Veyl prepares to make the Court's case |
| **4 (17–20)** | A person with an argument — Veyl — and the weight of the choice | REV_008/010; the Court's case vs. the player's decision |

> The "boss" of the campaign is a **moral argument made flesh**, not a damage check. The final confrontation can be combat, debate, sabotage, or persuasion.

## Tier 2 — The Court Stays a Legend (DM-only)

- **Court activity:** dormant beneath the basin. Acts *only* through Reke (steering the Compact) and the cutout's steering of Sefra. Leaked Remembrances drift toward the warming shrine as unwitting scouts.
- **Threat to the player:** indirect. If the player crosses the Court's interests (opposing the basin, nearing the shrine, pressing Sefra), a **cutout-hired agent** (Spy/Assassin-lite; AC 14, ~40 HP; prefers to vanish) may "tidy up" loose ends — telegraphed, never cheap-death. Reke (Mage/Spy-like; AC 12, ~45 HP; flees not dies) maneuvers politically.
- **Protection:** the player's clues point at **Reke / the Remnant / the Ledger** — never the Court. The Court is a ghost story (`HOLLOW_COURT.md` Public Face). The Compact Seal relic, if held, quietly leashes the player to the Court's cutout (DM-only surveillance — never named).
- **Rival claimants this tier:** the Remnant (locating the shrine via archives) and the Ledger (cornering relics) are the *visible* antagonists; the Vyre–Quorrin deal forms.

---

## Tier 3 — The Sin Surfaces, the Apex Gate Opens (DM-only)

- **Court activity:** as the basin breaches (master clock → Stage 3), the Court can **manifest as compelling Remembrance-apparitions** to those who enter the shrine. The first *deliberate local harvest* may occur. The Court still prefers proxies and persuasion.
- **Threat escalation:** Reclaimer champions and Ledger enforcers hunt the player at continental scale; the deepest harvest horror (Soul Monger CR 11, Bodak, Callers in Darkness) guards the deep nodes; Concord Construct-Guardians/Relay-Wardens (CR 7–17) defend the network. **The Custodians themselves remain Under-Shrine-only.**
- **The apex gate (REV_007, L13+):** confirmable only by **convergence** (REV_003+005+006), a **lethal-telegraphed descent**, or **heretic contact** (Maire / the Tallow Man's dead). Never one source. Before this, the player's likely guess (Quorrin/the Remnant is the apex) is *deliberately* fostered.
- **First Veyl contact (late Tier 3):** Veyl makes the Court's case — *persuasive, not cartoonish*. He is a grieving, certain, century-old idealist who believes consuming the dead is the only mercy left against the slow thinning. **Maire's doubt (REV_010)** becomes perceptible.

---

## Tier 4 — Veyl, the Final Voice (DM-only)

- **Custodian Veyl (Tier-4 solo-boss; Ghost/Wraith/Lich-like Remembrance-being, scaled L17–20):** the campaign's final *voice*. **Built-in weaknesses and non-combat win conditions:** sever the keystone, persuade Maire, deploy the player's chosen artifact/ally. **Never an out-damage wall** — he would far rather the player *agree* with him.
- **Custodian Orre (the hardliner; Wraith/Lich-like):** the fight, if there is one. The most combat-prone Custodian; bypassable via Maire or Veyl.
- **Custodian Maire (the doubter; Ghost-like; ally-capable):** a **non-combat lever**, not a fight. Turn her against Veyl to unlock the synthesis (REV_010).
- **Rival claimants at the keystone:** depending on Tier 2–3 outcomes, the Remnant (seize), the Ledger (commodify), and/or the Gravecallers (destroy) may be physically present, racing or fighting for the keystone.
- **The threat is the choice.** The master clock is near completion; doing nothing lets the harvest restart under the Court's rule (worst default). The pressure is moral, existential, and time-bound — not a hit-point race.

## Boss/Scenario Solo-Adjustments (DM)

Per `../13_encounters_and_bestiary/BOSS_AND_APEX_THREATS.md` and the solo balance guide:

- **Always at least one live non-combat win condition** for the player's path (sever the keystone, turn Maire, the right artifact, a faction coalition, public exposure).
- **Terrain matters:** the under-shrine provides cover, alternatives, and the keystone-mechanism as an interactable objective — the player can *act on the place*, not just the boss.
- **Allies present:** the player should reach the keystone with banked allies (turned factions, Maire, the Last Voice). A solo PC is never expected to out-fight an apex alone.
- **Telegraph lethality** at every prior level (drowning, cold, dread) so a PC never suicides into the descent early.
- **Death is meaningful, never cheap:** retreat exists from D20/D23; the Vessel/Last Voice/Maire are levers that can resolve the climax without a damage race.

## Apex Secret Protection (Re-Stated — Absolute)

- Before REV_007 (L13+ gate), **no player-safe text, NPC line, artifact description, or quest summary names the Hollow Court as real**, locates it beneath Hollowmere, or describes it as surviving Custodians.
- **Far-continent sources never deliver the apex** — they corroborate the theme (REV_002/005 obliquely, REV_009 deeply obliquely) and point home.
- **Reke's master stays "someone above him"** until the apex gate.
- The **Harvest Engine Shard's** true nature stays DM-only until M8.
- See `../11_mysteries_and_secrets/SECRET_PROTECTION_MATRIX.md` for safe/unsafe wording.
