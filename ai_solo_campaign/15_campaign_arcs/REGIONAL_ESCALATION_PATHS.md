## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY.** How each region's *state, danger, and role* changes across levels 5–20, so the AI DM can show the world moving whether or not the player visits. Player-safe surface changes are usable narration; **DM-only causes (warming nodes, the harvest, the Court) are flagged and never narrated as such.** Each region's full file lives in `../05_regions/` and `../06_settlements/`; this is the arc-level escalation overlay. **The endgame is vertical under Hollowmere — no region here is the keystone or the Court's seat.**

## Sundering Reach (Starting Region — the spine of the whole arc)

| Tier | Player-safe state | DM-only escalation |
|---|---|---|
| **2 (5–10)** | The basin reopening advances; relic-buying spikes; failed rites spread; the player is a regional name. | Basin clock → Stage 2–3; the upper shrine surfaces; secondary nodes (Deep Adit, Sunken Tollhouse) warm; master clock → Stage 2. |
| **3 (11–16)** | The basin is drained/breaching; the Reach is a faction battleground; the dead drift worse; the Wardens fracture. | Shrine breached; Court manifests to those who enter; first deliberate local harvest; Othetha breaks her oath; Reclaimers race for the keystone. |
| **4 (17–20)** | The basin is the center of everything; the under-shrine accessible; rival claimants converge. | The vertical descent (D20→D23) opens; the keystone reopening is imminent; the endgame is enacted here. |

**Role:** the home board, the corridor everything funnels back to, and the site of the climax. A player who never leaves the Reach can still reach every revelation and the endgame.

## Ashgarden Vale (Ring 1, South — the region in denial)

| Tier | Player-safe state | DM-only escalation |
|---|---|---|
| **2 (5–10)** | The Vale insists its failures are isolated; Saint Veddow's relic-trade grows; the Harvest-Moot covers up. | V1 (Moot's Silence) / V2 (Relics Sold) → Stage 2; Saint Veddow's Tomb is a capped node leaking; M3/M5 corroboration radiating from the Reach. |
| **3 (11–16)** | The denial cracks; the Vale's failures match the Reach's; relic-buyers swarm. | The capped node stirs as the master clock rises; the Vale becomes a Remnant/Ledger supply ground. |
| **4 (17–20)** | The Vale either rallies to the Mourners (rest) or is hollowed by the trade. | Its grief-keepers (Ezrith Combe) are a banked Mourner ally for the seal/synthesis endings. |

**Role:** the honest-window region (Tilbrook) for REV_001/REV_005 folk-fragment; a Mourner power-base. **Never the keystone.**

## Tollwood (Ring 1, East — the gated old presence)

| Tier | Player-safe state | DM-only escalation |
|---|---|---|
| **2 (5–10)** | The toll-war flares (Renn vs. Skell); the deep wood "goes wrong"; forest-bargains slip. | T1 (Old Mast Stirs) / T2 (Toll Turns to Plunder) → Stage 2; the Old Mast is a *pre-Concord* landmark-power, NOT a node — an oblique M5/M6 witness. |
| **3 (11–16)** | The East Road to Caradril chokes or reopens; the deep wood is dangerous; the Old Mast stirs. | The Old Mast (D12) is gated, high-tier; it corroborates "an older fall" without ever being the harvest-heart. |
| **4 (17–20)** | The wood is a refuge or a barrier depending on the toll-war's outcome. | Its grey-lords folk-memory feeds the destroy/free debate, never the apex. |

**Role:** pre-Concord echo; a route-control region (the East Road); never a node. **Never the keystone or the Court.**

## Pale Coast (Ring 1, West — the maritime harvest echo)

| Tier | Player-safe state | DM-only escalation |
|---|---|---|
| **2 (5–10)** | The "drowned-tide" rises; sea-relics traded; wreckers active; the Lamp shrine stirs. | C-PC1 (Drowned Tide) / C-PC2 (Salt Price) → Stage 2; the sea-lighthouses (Drowned Lamp, Skerry Shrine) are *maritime nodes* warming — M2/M3/M5/M6 fragments. |
| **3 (11–16)** | The sea returns its dead worse; the Skerry Shrine is dangerous; the salt-trade monopolizes. | Coastal nodes warm with the master clock; the Drowned Flame relic (Thin-touch immunity) is recoverable here — key node-diving tech. |
| **4 (17–20)** | The Coast either settles its dead (salt-and-tide rite) or drowns in the tide. | Salt-Mother Bryd is a banked Mourner/seal ally; the maritime echo never becomes the keystone. |

**Role:** maritime corroboration; the survivability-relic source (Drowned Flame). **Never the keystone.**

## Caradril (First Major City — the mid-game library, bank, and battlefield)

| Tier | Player-safe state | DM-only escalation |
|---|---|---|
| **2 (5–10)** | A prosperous city in denial; an open charter contested; quiet-coin and smelting "incidents"; the Sill fills with refugees. | C1 (Deal Closes) → Stage 2/3 (Vyre–Quorrin); C2 (Council Sleeps); C3 (Denial Cracks) → Stage 2; the Sunken Wards warm (continental-network proof). The Sealed Archive (M6/M9) is the clearest mid-game truth-cache. |
| **3 (11–16)** | The denial cracks; the relic-trade swells; the deal closes; the city is a faction war-front. | C1 → Stage 3–4 (the two richest factions co-own the dead unless the player intervened); C3 → Stage 3 (wrong-come-backs surface). |
| **4 (17–20)** | The city faces the truth — reckoning if the player prepared it, panic if not; an asset or an obstacle at the endgame. | A reformed Caradril (Dann/Kade) is a banked political ally; a complacent one is a dangerous, well-resourced endgame obstacle. |

**Role:** the mid-game hub; the clearest M3 (commercial) and M6/M9 (the Archive). **The Sunken Wards are a minor dormant cousin of the network — never the keystone.**

## The Far Continent (Tier 3 breadth — corroboration, never the keystone)

All twelve far regions + the Highmark Passes open in Tier 3. They **corroborate the theme and build leverage**, but the apex stays vertical under Hollowmere. DM-only hard ceilings per `MYSTERY_WEB.md`'s far-continent cross-reference.

| Region | Tier 3 role (player-safe) | DM-only ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| **Concord Heartlands / Ruin'd Crown** | The Concord's fallen surface capital; faction-contested treasure; the Ash-Crown | Surface scale/sin of the Concord (REV_005/006); **NEVER the live machine, keystone, or Court seat.** |
| **Glassmere League** | The Ledger's heartland + Remnant grand chapter; banking/archive intrigue | Continental relic-trade (REV_002); never the meaning's origin. |
| **Hethewald Free Holds** | Forest free-holds; gated deep-wood Old Holds | Oblique M5/M6; pre-Concord grove-presence echo. |
| **Hollow Gulf Ports** | Rival port city-states; maritime salvage | Faint REV_002 echo; an overseas world-horizon (Hadiz). |
| **Saltmere Reaches** | Salt-clan holds; drowned towns | Pre-Concord "older fall" echo; never the keystone. |
| **Emberfell Theocracy** | Volcanic fire-theocracy; "old glass" relic-source; very lethal | REV_002 echo; a *thematic mirror* (a fire-cult that also tends the dead), never the conspiracy. |
| **Sallowmarch Protectorate** | Plague delta; the Drowned Steps causeway | Pre-Concord echo; deep aberrations. |
| **Marrowdowns** | Chalk-downs barrows; manorial holds | Pre-Concord barrow echo (REV_001/005). |
| **Wender Steppe** | Nomad confederacy; the non-Concord Sky-Stones | REV_009 cosmic echo ("the Sky has gone thin"); never the Quiet Country/harvest. |
| **Karran Marches** | Warlord holds; Old Iron forts (far peripheral node) | M2/M5 fragment; "humming iron." |
| **Sunmark** | Grove-tribes; the living-faith Great Grove | The **hopeful contrast** (a faith that holds the dead consensually; REV_008/010 seed); never a "second keystone." |
| **Highmark Passes** | Frozen far-N; the cold-preserved Frozen Works | The clearest *cold-preserved* far surface-proof of REV_005/006; never the machine. |

**Universal far-region rule:** no far NPC knows the harvest mechanism, the Court, the surviving Custodians, the deliberate-Quietfall *intent*, or the Hollowmere keystone. Hollin Vane (Heartlands) is apex-*adjacent* but holds a *record of what the Concord did*, never the live machine.

## Region Fall / Stabilize / Revolt / Ally / Corrupt — Outcome Logic

Each region resolves into one of these states by the endgame, driven by clocks and player action:

- **Stabilize:** the player slowed the relevant clocks / backed the honest faction (e.g. a reformed Caradril, a Vale rallied to the Mourners).
- **Fall:** clocks ran unopposed (e.g. the Coast drowned by the tide, the Vale hollowed by the relic-trade).
- **Revolt:** a faction-turn the player triggered or ignored (e.g. the Circle turns militant, the Tollwood toll-war erupts).
- **Ally:** the player earned a regional power-base (Mourner Vale, Warden waystations, a turned Reclaimer in Karran) that stands with them at the keystone.
- **Corrupt:** the harvest/relic-trade took the region (e.g. a Caradril co-owned by the deal, an Emberfell heresy-deepened).

The endgame epilogue (`ENDGAME_STRUCTURE.md`) reflects each region's resolved state.
