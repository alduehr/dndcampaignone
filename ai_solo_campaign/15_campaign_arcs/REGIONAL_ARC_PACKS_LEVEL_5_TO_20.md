## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY — the Stage 15B regional play kit.** One concrete arc pack per region, levels 5–20. When the player is *in* a region, this tells you exactly what is happening, who is involved, where, and what changes — using only existing registered NPCs, factions, dungeons, quests, clues, and threats. **Apex content (REV_007/Hollow Court/Custodians/harvest/keystone) is DM-only and gated to L13+; the "Connection to Main Arc" sections separate player-safe surface from DM-only truth.** Never narrate DM-only escalation as such.

**Open-world, not a track.** Every pack offers multiple situations; the player pulls any thread or none. Clocks advance off-screen regardless (`../02_runtime_state/WORLD_CLOCKS.md`).

# §1 — Sundering Reach (Starting Region; the spine of the whole arc)

**Level Band:** Tier 2 primary (5–10) / Tier 3 primary (11–16) / Tier 4 primary (17–20, the descent)
**Recommended Entry Level:** 1–5 (home from level 1; the tier-2 fill begins at 5)
**Files:** `../05_regions/SUNDERING_REACH.md`; settlements `HOLLOWMERE`, `KETTLE_BRIDGE`, `SALTMARGIN`, `CANDLEWICK`, `GREYWATER_HOLM`, `HARROWGAST`, `REEDFORD`, `THE_ASHWALK_REST`.

### Arrival Situation
The Reach is no longer one town's problem. At Hollowmere, the Reachward Compact is openly debating Councilor **Aldous Reke**'s basin "water-rights" drainage scheme over Reeve **Marda Coalmont**'s objection; **Cinder Ledger** factor **Domic Sael** is buying every scrap of "old Concord salvage" that comes off the Heights; and rites are failing worse the nearer the drowned basin (Greywater Holm worst). The fixer **Sefra Quick** is still around, and still oddly eager to point the player at the shrine.

### Current Regional Pressure
Reke (DM: Hollow Court agent) drives the basin drainage; **Pevin Oss** fabricates the water-rights filings to cover it. Domic Sael and enforcer **Tallytooth Ren** out-buy and intimidate independent salvagers; **Collector-Captain Mauld Tallow** roams as the Ledger's muscle. **Warden Sister Othetha** quietly strains to keep the seal-truth while the order overloads (Hospitaller **Sirrin** at the Ashwalk Rest is swamped). The **Mourners' Circle** under **Grandmother Wend** seethes at the relic trade. In the Greyfens, the **Gravecallers** (the **Tallow Man**, **Sister Knell**, radical **Cole Ashby**, cell-mother **Mother Osset**) recruit the bereaved, and fen-warlord **Hewett Drane** ("the Reedwarden") preys on travelers.

### Playable Situations (6)
1. **The Basin Vote** — Hollowmere / the Compact moot. The player can sway **Coalmont** and **Magister-Reeve Sela Coalmont** against Reke, expose Pevin Oss's forged filings, or rally the Mourners. *Changes:* holds or advances Clock #2 (Basin Drains). Quest: `QC4_THE_BASIN_VOTE`, `Q_THE_REED_HOLMS`.
2. **The Salvage Squeeze** — Saltmargin / Harrowgast. Domic Sael and Mauld Tallow are pricing out independents and seizing "old glass" from the Deep Adit. The player can break the squeeze, expose **Iss Marlow**'s skim, or supply the Ledger. *Changes:* Clock #6 (Monopoly). Quests: `Q_LIGHT_ON_THE_SCALE`, `Q_RACE_NORTH`.
3. **The Reclaimers Race** — Sunder Heights / the Deep Adit (D05). Remnant agent **Veska Dunn** is racing to claim the secondary node for relic-supply. The player can beat them, sabotage, or join. *Changes:* Clock #7 (Reclamation). Quest: `Q_RACE_NORTH`.
4. **The Fen Cult** — Greyfens / the Drowned Blind, Mother Tongue Pool. Sister Knell and Cole Ashby work the Drowned Blind cell; the Tallow Man will *teach* a curious newcomer what the dead told him (M3/M5). *Changes:* Clock #5 (Breaking). Quest: `Q_THE_SECOND_MARK`.
5. **The Warden Overload** — the Ashwalk Rest / road-rites. Helping Sirrin and Warden **Pell**/**Hale** with failing road-funerals earns Othetha's trust (the path to her later confession). *Changes:* Clock #3 (Wardens Break, slows). Quests: `Q_THE_FAILING_FUNERAL`, `Q_PELLS_DOUBT`.
6. **The Reedwarden** — Greyfens. Hewett Drane is an independent fen-warlord antagonist (early Reach combat) extorting fen-folk and salvage routes. The player can break, bargain with, or recruit him. *Changes:* opens safe fen passage; banks a rough ally or a cleared threat.

### Escalation Events (4)
1. If the player ignores the basin within ~3 sessions, **Reke** wins the Compact vote; **drainage begins**, the upper shrine starts to surface (Clock #2→Stage 2–3; master Clock #1→Stage 2).
2. If the relic squeeze is ignored, **Domic Sael** corners Saltmargin/Harrowgast salvage; **Mauld Tallow** jails or ruins an independent the player knows (Clock #6→2).
3. If the Reclaimers are ignored, **Veska Dunn** claims the Deep Adit and ships "old glass" to Caradril (Clock #7→2; feeds C1).
4. If the Gravecallers are ignored, **Cole Ashby** acquires breaking-tools and inches toward the shrine (Clock #5→2); a botched rite at Greywater Holm produces a wrong-come-back.

### Key NPCs (from codex)
Sefra Quick, Aldous Reke, Marda Coalmont, Sela Coalmont, Pevin Oss, Domic Sael, Tallytooth Ren, Mauld Tallow, Othetha, Hale, Pell, Halloran Voss (Warden-Confessor), Grandmother Wend, Iola Wend, Bann Oester, the Tallow Man, Sister Knell, Cole Ashby, Mother Osset, Hewett Drane, Sirrin, Veska Dunn, Old Sashe, Halla Coalmont, Osrin Vole (Harrowgast), Mahalf Dunmore (Kettle Bridge), Onaia Veen.

### Active Factions
- **Reachward Compact:** pushing the basin drainage (Reke); honest officials resist. *(DM: infiltrated by the Court via Reke.)*
- **Cinder Ledger:** cornering salvage; enforcers active.
- **Concord Remnant:** racing for the Deep Adit (Veska Dunn).
- **Ashen Wardens:** overwhelmed; Othetha guards the seal-truth.
- **Mourners' Circle:** furious at the relic trade.
- **Gravecallers:** recruiting; radicals inching toward the shrine.
- *(DM: **Hollow Court** dormant beneath the basin, acting only through Reke and Sefra.)*

### Relevant Dungeons/Adventure Sites
- **D05 Deep Adit** (L3–5) — secondary node; "old glass" demonstrates M3; Reclamation race.
- **D06 Barrow of Nine Doors** (L3–5) — pre-Concord echo; parley-boss; M5/M6 fragment.
- **D03 Sunken Tollhouse** (L2–4) — minor relay-node under Kettle Bridge.
- **D04 Ledger Vault** (L4–6) — Hollowmere heist; the Ledger of Quiet Debts.
- **D18 Concord Relay-Vault / Signal-Cairn** (L7–10) — buried relay; M2/M6/M9 fragment; the Custodian's Compass; the Relay-Warden construct.
- **D19 Greyfens Deep** (L3–7) — scales with the player; M2/M5/M6.
- **D20 Basin Keystone Approach** (L5–9) — the keystone's surface works; **deep stair below is telegraphed lethal**; REV_001/003/005 fragments only.
- *(DM: D23 Under-Shrine Approach below D20 — endgame only, L16–20.)*

### Relevant Quest Chains
Major spine: `Q_MAJOR_001` (the failing rite), `Q_MAJOR_002` (basin culmination, campaign-long), `Q_MAJOR_003`/`Q_MAJOR_004` (Remembrance/relic truth), `Q_MAJOR_007` (the steering). Faction chains all begin here: `QC1→QC4`, `QL1→QL4`, `QR1→QR4`, `QW1→QW4`, `QM1→QM4`, `QG1→QG4`.

### Clues/Revelations Possible Here
- **REV_001** (source/pattern — confirmed in Act 1): Wren, Old Sashe's drift-map, Warden rite-observations, grave-soil drift.
- **REV_002** (substance): study a relic; the Tallow Man's teaching; "old glass" at the Deep Adit.
- **REV_003** (Reke steers): Coalmont's suspicions; Pevin Oss's filings; tail Reke to the shore at night.
- **REV_004** (you were steered): press Sefra; follow the patron's money.
- **REV_005/006** (Concord harvested / deliberate Quietfall): Othetha's confession (trust-gated, L9+); the Tallow Man's channeled witness; decoded Mourner songs; the Relay-Vault Script.
- *(DM: **REV_007** reachable ONLY here, late-arc, via the D20→D23 descent or convergence — never before L13.)*

### Rewards/Artifacts Available
The Warden's Unlit Lantern (Warden Rank 3; node detector), The Ledger of Quiet Debts (D04/D16), The Grief-Glass (Mourner/Gravecaller contest), The Compact Seal (Compact Rank 3; DM-only surveillance trap), The Custodian's Compass (D18; node-finder), The Drowned Flame (recoverable; Thin-touch immunity — also on the Coast).

### Encounter Profile
L1–10 (home) rising to L16–20 at the descent. Threats: Drowned/Drifting Remembrances (CR 1–3), wights, the Relay-Warden construct (D18, CR ~7), green hags and Mire-Hounds in the fens, Hewett Drane's fen-raiders, Ledger enforcers. **Thin-touch** is the signature environmental hazard near raw relics. The descent's apex (Custodians, CR 16–20) is DM-only, Under-Shrine only.

### If Player Helps
Holds the basin (Clock #2 low → master Clock #1 low); banks Othetha/Wend/Coalmont as seal/synthesis allies for the endgame; the Reach becomes a defensible home board.

### If Player Ignores
Reke wins the vote; the basin drains; the upper shrine surfaces; the Reclaimers take the Deep Adit; the master clock creeps. The Reach hardens against the player but the campaign continues — and the endgame still happens here.

### If Player Fails
A botched intervention (lost vote, failed dungeon) costs time and a clue, never the run. Worst case: the shrine surfaces early, the Court's reach grows, and the player faces a harder, lonelier descent later.

### Connection to Main Arc
**Player-safe:** the Reach is the center of the failures; the basin is the heart of the crisis. **DM-only:** the drowned shrine beneath Hollowmere is the **keystone** of the harvest network; Reke serves the Hollow Court; the endgame is straight down beneath this town. Every other region radiates from or corroborates this one.

### Routes Out
- **Caradril (SE):** the mid-game hub — follow the salvage trade and Reke's correspondent thread (`REGION_TO_REGION_TRANSITION_GUIDE` §Reach→Caradril).
- **Ring 1 (Vale S / Tollwood E / Coast W):** the honest-window regions corroborating REV_001/005.

### Solo-Play Notes
Allies on tap: Hale (rites/companion), Coalmont/Kade (lawful), Old Sashe (fen-guide), Brann (Tollwood), Wenna Roke (sea). No *remove curse*? A Warden/Mourner rite clears Thin-touch. The Reach never requires winning a fight to progress any REV.

# §2 — Ashgarden Vale (Ring 1, South — the region in denial)

**Level Band:** Tier 2 primary (5–10) / Tier 3 available
**Recommended Entry Level:** 5–8
**Files:** `../05_regions/ASHGARDEN_VALE.md`; `ORCHARDMERE`, `SAINT_VEDDOWS_REST`, `TILBROOK`.

### Arrival Situation
The Vale is warm, orchard-rich, and *insistently* fine. At Orchardmere, **Moot-Reeve Halsa Tindle** runs the Harvest-Moot and waves off the failures as "Reach trouble." At Saint Veddow's Rest, pilgrims buy "saint's-bones" relics by the cartload, and **Factor Wymar Pell** keeps the Ledger's relic-trade humming. But in Tilbrook, the miller's people whisper that the dead don't settle anymore.

### Current Regional Pressure
Tindle (cover-up) suppresses the truth to protect the Vale's harvest and pilgrimage economy (Clock V1, the Moot's Silence). Wymar Pell (DM: the "saint's-bones" are raw Remembrance relics; M3) feeds the Ledger Monopoly (Clock V2). Mourner elder **Mother Ezrith Combe** carries the old-songs folk-truth and resists the trade. Warden **Sister Adwen** quietly keeps Saint Veddow's capped node; shrine-keeper **Mother Sennet** and her conscience-stricken deputy **Lay-Sister Petronel** know the rest-rite has been failing.

### Playable Situations (4)
1. **The Cover-Up** — Orchardmere / the Harvest-Moot. Expose Tindle's suppression of the failures; rally the Moot or **Sergeant Idony Frost**. *Changes:* Clock V1; feeds #10 Thinning. Quest: `Q_THE_FUNERAL_DONE_TWICE`.
2. **Saint's-Bones** — Saint Veddow's Rest. Trace the relic trade; demonstrate the "saint's-bones" hold the dead (a clean M3/REV_002 proof). *Changes:* Clock V2; feeds #6 Monopoly. Quest: `Q_SAINTS_BONES`.
3. **The Capped Shrine** — Saint Veddow's Tomb (D09). With Sister Adwen's leave or by stealth, descend the capped Concord node (gated deep). *Changes:* M2/M3/M6 fragment; corroborates REV_005. Site: D09.
4. **The Honest Window** — Tilbrook. Old Mam **Tace**, the grave-singer, gives an honest M5 witness: the failures *radiate outward from the Reach*. *Changes:* REV_001 corroboration; a folk-fragment of REV_005.

### Escalation Events (3)
1. If the cover-up is ignored, Tindle's silence holds until a public wrong-come-back forces panic (V1→Stage 2–3); the Vale loses time to organize.
2. If the relic trade is ignored, Wymar Pell consolidates the "saint's-bones" pipeline for the Ledger (V2→2; feeds #6).
3. If the capped node is left, it *stirs* as the master clock rises; Remnant/Ledger buyers swarm Saint Veddow's.

### Key NPCs
Halsa Tindle, Wymar Pell, Mother Ezrith Combe, Sister Adwen, Mother Sennet, Lay-Sister Petronel, Idony Frost, Mam Tace, Sael Brunt (Tilbrook), Ser Aldous Penhew (cross-pressured land-magnate), Oneth Vael (Remnant pilgrim).

### Active Factions
Reachward Compact (Vale governance, cover-up); Cinder Ledger (saint's-bones trade); Mourners' Circle (Ezrith Combe's power-base; resists); Ashen Wardens (Adwen, the capped node); Concord Remnant (relic-buyers at Saint Veddow's). *(DM: the Vale is a cousin-site, never the keystone.)*

### Relevant Dungeons/Adventure Sites
- **D07 Buried Cloister** (L5–7) — Concord ruin.
- **D08 Pellow Grange** (L5–7) — Ledger relic-quarry.
- **D09 Saint Veddow's Tomb / Inner Shrine** (L4–6, gated deep) — capped node; M2/M3/M6.

### Relevant Quest Chains
`Q_THE_FUNERAL_DONE_TWICE`, `Q_SAINTS_BONES`, `Q_THE_MARKS_ON_THE_DOORS` (V-clocks), plus the Stage 7 Vale regional library.

### Clues/Revelations Possible Here
REV_001 (Tilbrook honest window); REV_002 (saint's-bones demonstration); REV_005 folk-fragment (Ezrith Combe's old songs). **Never REV_007 here.**

### Rewards/Artifacts Available
Level-appropriate magic items; relic salvage; **Ezrith Combe as a banked Mourner ally** (seal/synthesis endgame).

### Encounter Profile
L4–8. Threats: Drifting Remembrances, barrow-wights at the Tomb, Ledger guards, relic-leak hazards (Thin-touch at the capped node). Solo-safe; the Tomb's deep is gated/telegraphed.

### If Player Helps
The Vale rallies to the Mourners; Ezrith Combe and a grief-keeper base stand with the player at the endgame.

### If Player Ignores
The denial holds until it shatters; the Vale is hollowed by the relic-trade (V1/V2 run); it becomes a Remnant/Ledger supply ground.

### If Player Fails
A failed exposure entrenches Tindle; a failed Tomb delve costs a clue and Thin-touch, never the run.

### Connection to Main Arc
**Player-safe:** the Vale's failures match the Reach's, proving the crisis isn't local. **DM-only:** Saint Veddow's Tomb is a capped *cousin-node* of the harvest network corroborating M2/M3/M5 from outside the Reach — never the keystone, never the Court.

### Routes Out
- **Caradril (SE):** the saint's-bones trade leads to the Ledger's city HQ.
- **Sundering Reach (N):** home, and the source the Vale's failures radiate from.

### Solo-Play Notes
Ezrith Combe's Mourners are a noncombat ally network; the Vale's threats are low-tier and telegraphed; the Tomb has a retreat route.

# §3 — Tollwood (Ring 1, East — the gated old presence)

**Level Band:** Tier 2 primary (5–10) / Tier 3 available (Old Mast gated)
**Recommended Entry Level:** 5–8 (Old Mast deep: L11+)
**Files:** `../05_regions/TOLLWOOD.md`; `HARTFELL`, `COLDHEARTH`, `TOLLSTONE_CROSS`.

### Arrival Situation
The East Road to Caradril runs through deep old-growth and a bandit toll. At Tollstone Cross, the **Tollmen** under **Toll-Boss Cady Renn** shake down travelers — and Renn's lieutenant **Skell** wants to escalate from tolls to murder. Deeper in, at Coldhearth, the charcoal-burners keep "the forest-rules," and the elder **Goodwife Sennet** warns that the deep wood is "going wrong."

### Current Regional Pressure
Ledger logging-driver **Factor Hesk Dorr** is deep-cutting marked trees to feed the relic-and-timber trade, slipping the woodfolk's bargains (Clock T1, the Old Mast Stirs). The toll-war simmers: Renn (turnable) vs. Skell (murderous) (Clock T2). Road-Warden **Captain Brannoch Vey** (turnable) tries to keep the East Road open. *(DM: the Old Mast is a pre-Concord landmark-power — NOT a node — an oblique M5/M6 witness via the "grey lords" folk-memory.)*

### Playable Situations (4)
1. **The Toll-War** — Tollstone Cross. Turn Renn, stop Skell, or break the Tollmen; keep the East Road open. *Changes:* Clock T2; East Road to Caradril stays open or chokes. Quest: `Q_THE_TOLL_WAR`.
2. **The Marked Oaks** — deep wood / the Charcoal Burns. Dorr's deep-cutting breaks the forest-bargain; the player can stop it or expose it. *Changes:* Clock T1. Quests: `Q_THE_MARKED_OAKS`, `Q_THE_BARGAIN`.
3. **The Lost Crew** — toward the Sentinel Oaks / the Old Mast (D12). A logging crew vanished near the Old Mast; the player can recover them (gated; the deep Mast is L11+). *Changes:* oblique M5/M6. Quest: `Q_THE_LOST_CREW`, `Q_THE_HANGING_OAKS`.
4. **The Grey-Lords' Memory** — Coldhearth. Goodwife Sennet (deep secret, gated) and the old songs carry an oblique pre-Concord half-memory of "whoever took the dead before the roads fell." *Changes:* a gated M5/M6 fragment feeding REV_005.

### Escalation Events (3)
1. If the toll-war is ignored, **Skell** ousts Renn; the Tollmen turn to plunder; **the East Road to Caradril chokes** (T2→2–3) — a meaningful logistics cost.
2. If Dorr's cutting is ignored, the forest-bargain fully breaks and the Old Mast *stirs* (T1→2–3); the deep wood grows dangerous.
3. If the Old Mast is provoked early (a low-level dive), it is gated, lethal-telegraphed, and yields fragments and danger only.

### Key NPCs
Cady Renn, Skell, Hesk Dorr, Brannoch Vey, Goodwife Sennet, Brann the Guide, Goodwife Edda Sorrel, Holm Dunnal (Ledger camp-guard, turnable), Warden Brother Ošric (gated M5/M6), Wren-of-the-Wood (minor guide).

### Active Factions
The Tollmen (minor regional faction; toll-war); Cinder Ledger (Dorr, logging/relic trade); Ashen Wardens (Ošric, gated); Mourners' Circle (Coldhearth folk-faith); *(DM: a pre-Concord landmark-power in the Old Mast — not the Court.)*

### Relevant Dungeons/Adventure Sites
- **D10 Greenward Toll-Station / Drowned Vault** (L5–7) — Concord road-node.
- **D11 Hanging Oaks** (L4–6) — pre-Concord grove; Gravecaller cell.
- **D12 Old Mast** (L6–10 outer, **L11+ deep, gated**) — pre-Concord presence; oblique M5/M6.

### Relevant Quest Chains
`Q_THE_TOLL_WAR`, `Q_THE_MARKED_OAKS`, `Q_THE_BARGAIN`, `Q_THE_LOST_CREW`, `Q_THE_HANGING_OAKS`, plus the Stage 7 Tollwood library.

### Clues/Revelations Possible Here
REV_001 corroboration (the wood's honest window); oblique REV_005/006 via the grey-lords memory and the Greenward Toll-Station Script. **Never REV_007.**

### Rewards/Artifacts Available
Level-appropriate items; **Brann the Guide** as a hired solo-safety ally; a cleared/kept East Road.

### Encounter Profile
L4–10. Threats: Mast-Beasts (deep-wood predators), Tollmen bandits, Gravecaller cultists at the Hanging Oaks, the Greenward Toll-Station's guardians. The Old Mast's deep is *very* dangerous and gated.

### If Player Helps
The East Road stays open (a logistics asset to Caradril); Renn becomes a rough ally; the wood is a refuge.

### If Player Ignores
Skell's plunder chokes the road; the Old Mast stirs; the wood becomes a barrier.

### If Player Fails
A failed toll-war hands the road to Skell; a botched Old Mast dive yields danger and fragments, never death-by-ambush (telegraphed).

### Connection to Main Arc
**Player-safe:** even the deep wood "goes wrong," proving the crisis is wide. **DM-only:** the Old Mast is a *pre-Concord* power the harvest network bent around — an "older fall" echo and an oblique witness to the harvest, never the keystone or the Court.

### Routes Out
- **Caradril (SE):** the East Road is the direct route.
- **Sundering Reach (W):** home.

### Solo-Play Notes
Brann guides solo travelers safely; the Old Mast is gated so a lone low-level PC can't suicide into it; the toll-war has noncombat (negotiation) routes.

# §4 — Pale Coast (Ring 1, West — the maritime harvest echo)

**Level Band:** Tier 2 primary (5–10) / Tier 3 available (Skerry gated)
**Recommended Entry Level:** 5–9
**Files:** `../05_regions/PALE_COAST.md`; `WRACKMOUTH`, `COBBLE_STRAND`.

### Arrival Situation
The "drowned-tide" is rising — the sea returns its dead worse than before. At Wrackmouth, the rough harbor (the Wrack) hums with the sea-relic trade; Ledger **Factor Maris Cole** runs it and the wreckers are bold. At Cobble Strand, **Salt-Mother Bryd** performs the salt-and-tide rite to settle the drowned dead, and it is starting to fail.

### Current Regional Pressure
Maris Cole (DM: sea-relics are Remembrance relics salvaged from the old Concord sea-lighthouses; M3) drives the salt-trade monopoly with the wreckers (Clock C-PC2). The Lamp and sea-shrines are *warming maritime nodes* (Clock C-PC1, the Drowned Tide; feeds #1/#10). Harbor-Reeve **Ond Falk** governs; honest skipper **Wenna Roke** and tide-reader **Mabon Crale** offer solo-safety and an M5 witness; wandering Warden **Anchorite Sef** keeps the coastal nodes (gated).

### Playable Situations (4)
1. **The Drowned Tide** — Cobble Strand. Help Salt-Mother Bryd settle the dead as the rite fails; learn the drowned-tide is the maritime harvest leaking (oblique M5). *Changes:* Clock C-PC1. Quests: `Q_THE_DROWNED_BELL`, `Q_THE_SALT_MOTHERS_BURDEN`.
2. **The Salt Price** — Wrackmouth / the Wrack. Break or expose Maris Cole's salt-monopoly and the wreckers; demonstrate the sea-relics hold the dead (M3/REV_002). *Changes:* Clock C-PC2; feeds #6. Quests: `Q_THE_SALT_PRICE`, `Q_THE_WRECKERS`.
3. **The Drowned Lamp** — coastal headland (D14). With Provost **Anselm Verge**'s leave or by stealth, dive the coastal Concord node. *Changes:* M2/M3/M5/M6; **the Drowned Flame relic (Thin-touch immunity)** is recoverable here. Site: D14.
4. **The Skerries** — offshore (D15, gated, L6–9+). The largest coastal node; a REV_005 fragment. *Changes:* gated proof; high danger telegraphed.

### Escalation Events (3)
1. If the drowned-tide is ignored, the sea returns its dead worse; Cobble Strand's rite fully fails; the Coast slides toward "drowning in the tide" (C-PC1→2–3).
2. If the salt-trade is ignored, Maris Cole monopolizes; the wreckers grow bolder and deadlier (C-PC2→2).
3. If the nodes are left, the Lamp and Skerry Shrine warm with the master clock; the Drowned Flame may fall to a relic-buyer first.

### Key NPCs
Salt-Mother Bryd, Maris Cole, Ond Falk, Wenna Roke, Mabon Crale, Anchorite Sef, Anselm Verge, Wreck-Master Tomas Quint (grey M3 chain), Maren Voll (Remnant node-mapper), Corl Strand (eldest fisher).

### Active Factions
Cinder Ledger (Maris Cole, sea-relic trade); Mourners' Circle (Bryd's salt-and-tide rite; banked seal ally); Ashen Wardens (Sef, Verge; coastal nodes); wreckers/Salt-adjacent criminals (Quint). *(DM: maritime cousin-nodes, never the keystone.)*

### Relevant Dungeons/Adventure Sites
- **D13 Wreckers' Caves** (L5–7) — sea-caves; wreckers/cult.
- **D14 Drowned Lamp** (L4–6) — coastal node; the Drowned Flame.
- **D15 Skerry Shrine** (L6–9, gated) — largest coastal node; REV_005 fragment.

### Relevant Quest Chains
`Q_THE_DROWNED_BELL`, `Q_THE_EMPTY_HEARTH`, `Q_THE_SALT_MOTHERS_BURDEN`, `Q_THE_SALT_PRICE`, `Q_THE_SKIPPERS_DEBT`, `Q_THE_WRECKERS`.

### Clues/Revelations Possible Here
REV_001 corroboration (Cobble Strand honest window); REV_002 (sea-relic demonstration); REV_005 fragment (Skerry Shrine; Bryd's old songs). **Never REV_007.**

### Rewards/Artifacts Available
**The Drowned Flame** (Thin-touch immunity — key node-diving tech for the whole campaign); **Salt-Mother Bryd** as a banked Mourner/seal ally; a water-route to Caradril via the Verdance Mouth (Wenna Roke).

### Encounter Profile
L4–9. Threats: Drowned Remembrances, Salt-Stalkers, wreckers, sea-cave hazards, the Skerry Shrine's Ward-Keeper. Drowning/cold are environmental hazards; Wenna Roke makes sea-travel safe.

### If Player Helps
The Coast settles its dead; Bryd stands with the player; the Drowned Flame makes the whole campaign's node-diving survivable.

### If Player Ignores
The Coast drowns in the tide; Maris Cole monopolizes; the Drowned Flame is lost to a relic-buyer.

### If Player Fails

# §5 — Caradril (First Major City — the mid-game library, bank, and battlefield)

**Files:** `../06_settlements/CARADRIL.md` + 8 district files in `caradril_districts/`.

A warm southern land of sacred groves and grove-keeper tribes. At Sunhollow, grove-faith head **Grove-Keeper Sael Sunmark** tends the Great Grove; seer **Sun-Singer Doll** reads a hopeful sign; plains-factor **Doss Sunward** trades along the Green Roads.

### Current Regional Pressure
A gentler, warmer "other" south with a *living old faith* — the grove-tribes **hold the dead consensually**, a working model of the synthesis the campaign's best ending requires. *(DM: the **hopeful contrast** — a faith that holds the dead by consent; REV_008/010 seed; NEVER a "second keystone.")*

### Playable Situations (3)
1. **The Great Grove** — D33 / Sunhollow. Learn the grove-faith's consensual way with the dead from Sael Sunmark — the synthesis model. *Changes:* REV_008/010 seed; banks the synthesis path. Site: D33. Quest: `Q_MAJOR_026` (the grove that will not harvest).
2. **The Hopeful Sign** — Sun-Singer Doll. A hopeful cosmic echo (the contrast to Esha's "thin Sky") — that the dead *can* be held without consuming them.
3. **The Green Roads** — Doss Sunward. Trade and travel through the groves; a southern ally and route.

### Escalation Events (2)
1. If the grove is ignored, the grove-faith remains an unconnected curiosity — the player reaches the endgame without the synthesis model (no REV_010 deepening from here).
2. If the Green Roads are left, relic-buyers and outsiders pressure the groves.

### Key NPCs
Sael Sunmark, Sun-Singer Doll, Doss Sunward, Tamsin Greenway (road-captain).

### Active Factions
Grove-tribes (Sael Sunmark); plains-traders (Sunward). *(DM: the hopeful contrast; no apex; no second keystone.)*

### Relevant Dungeons/Adventure Sites
- **D33 Sunhollow Great Grove** (L8–13) — living-faith site; REV_008/010 hope-seed.

### Relevant Quest Chains
`Q_SUN_*` (Sunmark far library); `Q_MAJOR_026` (the grove that will not harvest).

### Clues/Revelations Possible Here
**REV_008/010 seed** (a working consensual model — the synthesis is *conceivable*); a hopeful REV_009 contrast. **Never REV_007; never a second keystone.**

### Rewards/Artifacts Available
Grove-craft items; **Sael Sunmark as the banked synthesis ally**; the grove-faith model (a Transform-ending requirement).

### Encounter Profile
L8–13 (gentler). Threats: grove-guardians, southern predators, outsiders. Solo-safe with a grove-guide.

### If Player Helps
The synthesis model and Sael Sunmark are banked — a *requirement* for the best (Transform) ending; a southern ally.

### If Player Ignores
The grove-faith stays an unconnected curiosity; the player may reach the endgame unable to model the synthesis.

### If Player Fails
A failed grove-visit costs the synthesis deepening — never the apex.

### Connection to Main Arc
**Player-safe:** a land whose living faith holds its dead in peace. **DM-only:** the grove-faith is the **hopeful contrast** — a working consensual model that seeds REV_008/010 and the Transform ending; it is NEVER a second keystone, node, or the Court.

### Routes Out
- **Sallowmarch / Hollow Gulf (S/E):** the delta and ports.
- **Marrowdowns / Hethewald (N):** the downs and the forest.

### Solo-Play Notes
Sunmark is the *gentle* far region and the key to the best ending — make Sael Sunmark reachable and memorable; the grove is a noncombat site.

# §18 — Highmark Passes (the frozen far north; cold-preserved proof)

**Level Band:** Tier 3 primary (L12–16)
**Recommended Entry Level:** 12–16
**Files:** `FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md` (region 18); dungeon `THE_HIGHMARK_FROZEN_WORKS.md` (D35); far NPCs as referenced.

### Arrival Situation
The cold passes of the Highmark Spine, where the oldest Concord works and the most isolated survivors lie frozen. Scattered isolated holds cling to the passes; the ice-locked Frozen Works hold the best-preserved Concord records on the continent.

### Current Regional Pressure
An optional late frontier; a barrier, not arc-required. The Frozen Works are the clearest *cold-preserved* far surface-proof of REV_005/006 — and relic-buyers and Remnant expeditions covet them. *(DM: the clearest cold-preserved far surface-proof; never the machine.)*

### Playable Situations (3)
1. **The Frozen Works** — D35. An ice-locked Concord station with the best-preserved records — far surface-proof of REV_005/006. *Changes:* confirms/deepens REV_005/006. Site: D35.
2. **The Isolated Holds** — the passes. Win or relieve a frozen hold cut off by the winter; a cold-north ally and a guide through the passes. *Changes:* a banked cold-north ally; safe passage. The frozen holds cling to the oldest, most isolated survivors of the Concord's fall and remember scraps of the "old order" (oblique REV_005/006 folk-fragment).
3. **The Remnant Expedition Race** — the route to D35. A Concord Remnant cold-expedition (apex-adjacent scholars, often Reclaimers loyal to **Hollin Vane** / **Sabine Ferrant**, or a crew the player can split off like **Wenna Stone**'s) is racing for the Frozen Works' cold-preserved records. The player can beat them, sabotage their camp, or turn an idealist among them. *Changes:* feeds #7 Reclamation; banks a turned-Reclaimer ally or denies the Remnant the clearest cold proof. Site approach: D35.

### Escalation Events (2)
1. If the Frozen Works are ignored, the **Concord Remnant** expedition claims the cold-preserved records and ships them to the Heartlands dig, strengthening Hollin Vane's legitimacy (#7→3).
2. If the holds are ignored, they freeze out and the passes close; a cold-north ally and the oblique folk-fragment are lost.

### Key NPCs
Frozen-hold leaders and far NPCs as referenced in the far rosters (referenced, not invented here).

### Active Factions
Isolated holds; Concord Remnant expeditions; relic-buyers. *(DM: far surface-proof; no apex.)*

### Relevant Dungeons/Adventure Sites
- **D35 Highmark Frozen Works** (L12–16) — cold-preserved far surface-proof; REV_005/006.

### Relevant Quest Chains
Highmark far library quests as referenced.

### Clues/Revelations Possible Here
**REV_005/006** (the clearest cold-preserved far surface-proof). **Never REV_007; never the machine.**

### Rewards/Artifacts Available
Cold-preserved Concord records and relics; a cold-north guide/ally.

### Encounter Profile
L12–16, high environmental hazard (cold, ice, altitude). Threats: frozen dead, ice-guardians, cold hazards. Require cold protection and retreat.

### If Player Helps
The cold-preserved records (REV_005/006 deepening) and a cold-north ally.

### If Player Ignores
The Remnant claims the records; the holds freeze out.

### If Player Fails
A failed expedition costs a clue and cold damage; never the apex.

### Connection to Main Arc
**Player-safe:** the oldest, most isolated Concord works lie frozen here. **DM-only:** the Frozen Works are the clearest cold-preserved far surface-proof of REV_005/006 — never the machine, never the keystone, never the Court.

### Routes Out
- **Karran Marches (SE):** the warlord frontier — the most natural exit route south from the Spine; the Frozen Works' records can be a bargaining chip with Warlord "Iron" Brask.
- **Wender Steppe (S):** the steppe clans — Wind-Singer Esha can guide the player from the steppe's far edge to the Spine-Foot trade-meet, which accesses the Highmark Passes from the south; a Wender escort avoids the worst of the cold.

### Solo-Play Notes
Cold protection and a pass-guide are required; the Frozen Works are gated with retreat; a barrier region, never arc-mandatory.

# §19 — The Vertical Descent (Concord Deep / Under-Shrine — DM-ONLY endgame zone)

**Level Band:** Tier 4 primary (17–20); surface (D20) reachable Tier 2–3 (lethal-telegraphed)
**Recommended Entry Level:** 16–20 (deep); D20 surface L5–9
**Files:** `../10_dungeons_and_ruins/THE_BASIN_KEYSTONE_APPROACH.md` (D20); **`THE_UNDER_SHRINE_APPROACH.md` (D23, DM-ONLY)**.

> **DM-ONLY SECTION. The apex truth (REV_007), the harvest mechanism, the Custodians' nature, and the endgame choice (REV_008) live only in D23 and the DM-only arc files. Never render D23's content, name-truth, or location-truth to the player. The Court is a legend until REV_007's gate (L13+).**

### Arrival Situation (DM)
Beneath Hollowmere, straight down through the drowned shrine, lies the keystone — the hub of the Concord Deep (the buried node-network) and the seat of the Hollow Court. The Basin Keystone Approach (D20) is the surface/upper works; the gated stair below it descends (D23) to where the surviving Custodians — **Veyl**, **Maire**, **Orre** — persist as preserved Remembrance and are restarting the harvest.

### Current Pressure (DM)
The master clock (#1) drives toward a full restart. Rival claimants (Remnant, Ledger, Gravecallers) race the player to the keystone. Veyl prepares to make the Court's case; Orre will fight if pressed; Maire secretly doubts (REV_010). Reke is the surface link.

### Playable Situations (DM, endgame)
1. **The Approach** — D20→D23. The vertical descent: drowning, cold, dread, Concord guardians at full power. Telegraphed lethal at every prior level.
2. **The Court's Case** — D23. Veyl bargains/recruits; the moral/existential confrontation. *Changes:* REV_007 owned; the choice (REV_008) framed.
3. **Maire's Heresy** — D23. Maire can be turned against Veyl (REV_010) — the synthesis key.
4. **The Enacted Decision** — D23. Seal / restart / seize / destroy / transform, per the player's approach and means.

### Key NPCs (DM)
Custodian Veyl (final voice), Custodian Maire (doubter/synthesis ally), Custodian Orre (hardliner/fight), Councilor Reke (surface link), the Last Voice (sentient artifact witness).

### Active Factions (DM)
The Hollow Court (Veyl, Maire, Orre); rival claimants present per their Tier 2–3 postures (Remnant/Ledger/Gravecallers); the player's banked allies (Wardens/Mourners/turned factions).

### Relevant Dungeons/Adventure Sites
- **D20 Basin Keystone Approach** (L5–9 surface, revisit/breach Tier 4) — surface works; fragments only until the gate.
- **D23 Under-Shrine Approach** (L16–20, **DM-ONLY APEX**) — the only place the apex truth and the choice are enacted.

### Relevant Quest Chains
`Q_MAJOR_002` (basin culmination), `Q_MAJOR_014` (Hollow Court, DM-only), `Q_MAJOR_015` (Fate of the Remembrance), `QH3`/`QH4` (DM-only), `Q_MAJOR_028` (faction war converges).

### Clues/Revelations Possible Here
**REV_007** (the Court lives — confirmed here via descent/convergence/Maire); **REV_008** (the choice and costs); **REV_010** (Maire). This is the only place REV_007 is *physically* confirmed.

### Rewards/Artifacts (DM)
The Harvest Engine Shard (plot detonator), The Quiet Country Vessel (choice/containment engine), The Last Voice (the dead's witness/synthesis ally), the Custodian's Compass (points down). All M6–M9 gated.

### Encounter Profile (DM)
L16–20, apex. The Custodians (CR 16–20, Under-Shrine only): Veyl as a solo-boss *with built-in weaknesses and non-combat win conditions*; Orre the most combat-prone (bypassable via Maire/Veyl); Maire an ally-capable lever. Plus the deepest harvest horror (Soul Monger, Bodak, Callers in Darkness) and Concord guardians.

### If Player Helps / Acts
The decision is enacted (one of five outcomes); the epilogue reflects the accumulated path.

### If Player Ignores / Fails
Doing nothing completes the master clock — the harvest restarts under the Court (worst default, a playable bleak epilogue). Losing the race hands the ending to a rival. A solo Gravecaller breach shatters the keystone catastrophically.

### Connection to Main Arc
**This IS the main arc's climax** — vertical, under Hollowmere. The whole campaign converges here. **Never narrate any of this to the player before REV_007's gate.**

### Routes Out
The descent is one-way toward the climax; the player can retreat from D20/D23 to prepare (the descent is never an unwinnable wall), but the endgame resolves here.

### Solo-Play Notes (absolute)
Veyl is **never** a "you must out-damage me" wall. At least one non-combat win condition is always live (sever the keystone, persuade Maire, deploy the player's chosen artifact/ally). The player arrives with banked allies, artifacts, and knowledge. Telegraph everything; provide retreat; death is meaningful, never cheap. Full detail: `ENDGAME_STRUCTURE.md`, `TIER_4_PLAYABLE_PATHS_LEVELS_17_20.md`, `SOLO_CAMPAIGN_BALANCE_GUIDE.md`.
