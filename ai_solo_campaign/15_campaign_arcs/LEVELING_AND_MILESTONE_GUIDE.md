## AI Use

**DM-ONLY.** Milestone-leveling guidance for levels 5–20. Extends Act 1's `../12_campaign_arc/ACT_1_MILESTONES.md` and the framework in `../12_campaign_arc/LEVEL_1_TO_20_PROGRESSION.md`. **Milestone, not XP-grind:** the player levels by *achieving things that matter to the arc*, earnable by **multiple approaches** (combat is never required). Use these as defaults, not a rigid script; a fast player may outpace them, a thorough player may linger — both are fine.

> **No-grind rule:** never gate a level behind "kill N monsters." Every trigger below is reachable by combat **or** social **or** stealth **or** investigation **or** travel. If the player solves a milestone cleverly without the "expected" scene, award the level anyway.

## Tier 2 — Levels 5 → 10

| To Level | Earned when (any qualifying path) | Arc beat |
|---|---|---|
| **5 → 6** | Establish a base of operations or a faction standing in the Reach (a safehouse, a patron, a rank, a regional reputation). | The player becomes a regional force. |
| **6 → 7** | Achieve the **"Remembrance is collected" revelation** (REV_002) — by relic study, Gravecaller teaching, mercantile evidence, or Script. | Horror becomes conspiracy. |
| **7 → 8** | **Expose a Hollow Court agent** (REV_003 — Reke or a cutout), *or* survive a first major faction reprisal for crossing one. | The player is a consequential actor. |
| **8 → 9** | **Travel beyond the Reach toward the larger truth** (reach Caradril in earnest, the Verdance corridor, or a Ring 1 deep site) *or* resolve a faction Q*4 decision point. | The world opens. |
| **9 → 10** | Begin/confirm **"The Concord harvested the dead" (REV_005)** — the Sealed Archive, Othetha, the dead, or decoded songs. | The historical truth cracks open. |

**Tier 2 pacing note:** expect ~1–2 substantial sessions per level. If the player rushes Caradril, REV_005 may land at L9; if they linger in Ring 1, they may reach L10 still in the conspiracy phase — both are valid.

## Tier 3 — Levels 11 → 16

| To Level | Earned when (any qualifying path) | Arc beat |
|---|---|---|
| **10 → 11** | **Survive a major faction reprisal** at regional/continental scale (an assassination attempt, political ruin survived, a sabotage thwarted) *or* commit hard to a faction's endgame posture. | Enemies escalate. |
| **11 → 12** | Confirm **"The Quietfall was deliberate" (REV_006)** and/or **REV_009** (the finite afterlife) — archives, Othetha, the dead, inscriptions, or the cosmic echo. | The world's founding trauma is reframed. |
| **12 → 13** | **Locate the path to the Hollow Court** — assemble the convergence, reach the under-shrine's approach (D20 deep), or be contacted by a heretic. | The apex gate. |
| **13 → 14** | **First contact with the Hollow Court / Veyl** (REV_007 owned) — by descent, convergence-confirmation, or Maire/Tallow Man contact. | The apex named. |
| **14 → 15** | **Commit to (or be forced toward) an endgame branch** *or* confirm **REV_010** (Maire's heresy / the synthesis key). | The choice crystallizes. |
| **15 → 16** | **Gather the means to enact the chosen ending** — a key artifact, a faction coalition, the seal-knowledge, the breaking-tools, or the synthesis model. | The endgame is armed. |

**Tier 3 pacing note:** the apex gate (L13) is the campaign's most important pacing beat. Do not rush a player into REV_007 before the convergence; do not stall a prepared player past it. Expect ~2 sessions per level here (the stakes and travel are larger).

## Tier 4 — Levels 17 → 20

| To Level | Earned when (any qualifying path) | Arc beat |
|---|---|---|
| **16 → 17** | **Breach the under-shrine's defenses** (begin the vertical descent past D20 toward D23). | The endgame begins. |
| **17 → 18** | **Defeat/outmaneuver the last obstacle between the player and the keystone** (a rival claimant, a guardian, a defense — by force, stealth, or guile). | The keystone is in reach. |
| **18 → 19** | **The penultimate confrontation** — Veyl, a rival claimant, or Orre (resolved by combat, debate, sabotage, or persuasion). | The final voice. |
| **19 → 20** | **Enact the decision about the Remembrance** — the campaign climax. | The fate of the Remembrance. |

**Tier 4 pacing note:** levels 17–20 are the densest, most consequential sessions. Award the final level at the moment the player *enacts* the decision, so the epilogue plays at level 20.

## Solo Milestone Principles

- **Reward the approach, not the body count.** A player who *talks* Veyl down, *exposes* Reke, or *steals* the proof levels exactly as fast as one who fights.
- **Don't punish thoroughness or speed.** A thorough player who hits every milestone slowly is fine; a fast player who skips to the apex via convergence is fine (gate REV_007 by prerequisites, not by level).
- **Keep the solo PC tier-appropriate at the apex.** If a side-content-only player reaches Tier 4 under-leveled, award catch-up milestones for the means-gathering excursions so they enter the keystone at ~L17–18.
- **Never strand the player.** If the player is stuck between milestones, surface a hook toward the next beat (`OPEN_WORLD_CONTINUITY_GUIDE.md`).

## Passive-Player Leveling (the Floor)

A passive player still levels: the world delivers milestone-worthy events to them (a faction reprisal arrives, the shrine surfaces, the harvest reaches their region, Veyl makes contact). Award the level when the *event* resolves, however the player handles it. The passive player reaches L20 and the choice — slower, with fewer allies, but they get there.
