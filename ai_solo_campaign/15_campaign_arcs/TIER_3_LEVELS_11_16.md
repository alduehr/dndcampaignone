## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY — DM-facing arc spine for levels 11–16 (Act 3 upper + Act 4).** Load when planning a Tier 3 session, calibrating the historical reveals, or approaching the apex. **Revelation arc for Tier 3:** confirm REV_005 (the Concord harvested), REV_006 (the Quietfall was deliberate), REV_009 (the afterlife is finite). The **apex gate opens at L13+**: REV_007 (the Hollow Court lives) and REV_010 (Maire's heresy) become reachable — but **only** through convergence, a lethal-telegraphed descent, or heretic contact, never one NPC/document/roll. **Before L13 the Court is still a legend; far-continent sources NEVER name it.**

This is an **open-world kit, not a track.**

## Expected Campaign State at Entry (Level 11)

- **Player knows:** REV_002/003/004 confirmed; REV_005 begun or confirmed (the Concord harvested the dead).
- **Reputation:** a regional power with banked Tier 2 allies and enemies; factions react to the player by name.
- **Clocks:** master (#1) and faction clocks at Stage 2–3; the Basin (#2) may have surfaced the upper shrine; Reclamation (#7) rising hard; Caradril clocks advancing.
- **Geography:** the far continent is reachable via the Verdance corridor (SE) and the southern ports; the Concord Heartlands beckons.
- **Open threads:** which factions the player aligned with/against; whether Reke is exposed; whether Caradril is an asset or obstacle.

## The One-Paragraph Shape (DM)

The truth turns *historical and shattering*. The player confirms that the Concord's "keeping the dead at peace" was a **harvest** that powered its golden age (REV_005), and that the Quietfall was not a disaster but a **deliberate, controlled self-destruction** to pause the harvest and let the drained afterlife refill (REV_006). The cosmological floor drops out: the Quiet Country is **finite and was nearly emptied** (REV_009), which is why resurrection is dangerous and "thin-born" people exist. The continent opens: the player can travel to the far regions, where the Concord's true *surface* scale (the Ruin'd Crown), continent-wide relic-trade, and the old-songs network all corroborate the sin — without ever holding the keystone. Faction war goes continental as the Remnant races for the Heartlands and the keystone, the Ledger brokers the dead as a commodity, and the Mourners/Gravecallers move to act. At the top of the tier the apex gate opens: assembling REV_003 + REV_005 + REV_006, or descending into the under-shrine's reach, or being contacted by a heretic, the player learns the keystone reveal — **the surviving Custodians live as the Hollow Court and are restarting the harvest** (REV_007) — and meets Veyl, who is not a monster but a grieving, certain, century-old idealist. Custodian Maire's secret doubt (REV_010) opens the hardest road forward.

## Main Pressures (Tier 3)

1. **The historical truth detonates.** REV_005/006 recontextualize all history; the noble-fallen-order myth dies. This reshapes every faction relationship.
2. **The continental faction war.** The Reclamation (#7) and Monopoly (#6) clocks peak; the Remnant covets the Heartlands and races for the keystone; the Ledger's continental arm (Glassmere) backs them or betrays them. See `FACTION_ESCALATION_PATHS.md`.
3. **The basin nears breach.** If the Basin clock (#2) reached Stage 3–4 in Tier 2, the upper shrine is exposed and the master clock (#1) is at Stage 3 — the Court can manifest to those who enter; the first deliberate local harvest may occur.
4. **The player is hunted at continental scale.** Reclaimer expeditions, Ledger enforcers, and — once the player nears the truth — cutout-hired agents and (late) the Court's manifestations. Telegraphed; the apex is choice-built, never a damage race.
5. **The thinning becomes visible** (#10). Failed resurrections and thin-born births form a pattern; even the Court's argument starts to feel tempting — the campaign's central moral engine.

## Likely Regions (Tier 3)

All 12 far regions plus the Highmark Passes open in this tier. Use the player's chosen route; **none is required** (the endgame is vertical under Hollowmere). Highlights:

- **Concord Heartlands / the Ruin'd Crown (central-SE):** the Concord's fallen *surface* capital; the clearest far surface-proof of REV_005/006 (the Ash-Crown Fragment; Hollin Vane's expedition). **NOT the keystone, never the Court's seat.**
- **Glassmere League (central):** the Ledger's true heartland and the Remnant's grand chapter (the Reliquary); continental relic-trade and banking intrigue.
- **Verdance Reaches (Ring 2):** the corridor; the Nine Locks Sunken Stair (D34) points home.
- **Saltmere Reaches / Marrowdowns / Sallowmarch:** pre-Concord "older fall" echoes — the world has drowned before (REV_001/005 echoes, never the keystone).
- **Emberfell Theocracy / Highmark Passes:** the continent's most lethal far sites (volcanic, frozen); "old glass" relic-source (REV_002 echo); the Highmark Frozen Works is the clearest *cold-preserved* far surface-proof of REV_005/006.
- **Wender Steppe / Sunmark:** the cosmic echo (the afterlife thins; REV_009 oblique) and the hopeful contrast (a living grove-faith that *holds the dead* consensually; REV_008/010 seed — never a "second keystone").

Full per-region change detail: `REGIONAL_ESCALATION_PATHS.md`.

## Failure States (Tier 3)

Summary — full logic in `FAILURE_STATES_AND_WORLD_CONSEQUENCES.md`:

- **Ignore the historical truth:** the world clocks deliver it anyway via the surfacing shrine and the faction war; the player arrives at the endgame under-informed (fewer endgame options — the synthesis becomes unreachable without REV_010).
- **Let the Reclamation win:** the Remnant secures the keystone path and Ledger backing; the endgame antagonist shifts toward a *living* tyranny racing the Court.
- **Let the Gravecallers breach early:** Cole Ashby's cell attempts to shatter a shrine; if they succeed alone, catastrophe (the dead freed destructively) — a possible bad mid-tier ending the player must scramble to prevent or redirect.
- **Fail at a far site:** costs time, resources, and a corroboration clue — never the apex (far sites are never the only path to any REV).
- **Reach the apex too hot/under-prepared:** the Court is dormant/defended; the player gets fragments and lethal danger, not a free REV_007 — then must withdraw and prepare.

## Solo-Play Balance Notes (Tier 3)

- **The far continent is lethal.** Give the player the Drowned Flame (Thin-touch immunity) and a route to faction/ally support before any very-high site (Emberfell/Highmark/Cindern). Telegraph; allow retreat; the corridor is never a one-way door.
- **Allies scale up:** turned factions field real support (Warden rite-circles, Mourner mobs, Remnant idealists, Gravecaller cells, even a turned Reclaimer like Wenna Stone). A solo PC should reach the apex with *people*, not alone.
- **Fallbacks for missing tools:** no *teleport/flight*? Water-routes, sky-stone guides, and faction transport substitute. No *resurrection*? The setting makes it dangerous *on purpose* — death is meaningful, and the Quiet Country Vessel / Last Voice are the lore-correct levers. No *dispel/remove curse*? Othetha, Mother Ysarra, or a far grove-keeper can break harvest curses.

## Guidance for Off-Path Players (Tier 3)

- **The homebody (never leaves the Reach):** REV_005/006/007 are all reachable in the Reach (Othetha, the Tallow Man, the Relay-Vault, the Basin Keystone Approach, decoded songs). The far continent is *breadth*, not a gate.
- **The far-explorer (lives on the continent):** far sites corroborate the theme and build political leverage (the Ash-Crown), but the apex stays vertical under Hollowmere — the Compass and the convergence pull the player home.
- **The faction-loyalist:** their chosen faction's Q*4 posture shapes the apex confrontation; the DM should make their allies matter at the keystone.
- **The apex-rusher:** if they reach D23's approach before the convergence, gate it (lethal, dormant Court, fragments only) and let them retreat with more clues, not the reveal.
- **The passive player:** the surfacing shrine, the breached basin, and the faction war deliver REV_005/006 and, at the gate, REV_007 — slower, costlier, with fewer endgame options.

## Tier Exit — What Unlocks Tier 4 (Level 17)

Tier 4 begins when the player **owns REV_007** (the Court lives) and commits to — or is forced toward — gathering the means to enact a decision about the Remembrance:

1. The player **confirms REV_007** via convergence, the lethal descent, or Maire's contact, and **meets Veyl** (or learns the Court is real and located beneath Hollowmere).
2. The player **commits to an endgame approach** (`ENDGAME_STRUCTURE.md`) — or rival claimants force the timeline by racing to the keystone.
3. **Clocks force it:** the master clock reaches Stage 4 (harvest at scale); the basin is breachable; the endgame can no longer be deferred.

> Tier 4 content lives in `TIER_4_LEVELS_17_20.md`. Tier 3 ends with the apex named, Veyl met, and the choice (REV_008) coming into view.
