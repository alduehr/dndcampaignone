## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY — DM-facing arc spine for levels 5–20.** Load this first when planning any session past level 5, when the player levels into a new tier, or when calibrating how far the truth has come. It is the **master map** for the three Stage-15 tier files; it does not replace them. Player-safe surface framing is marked; everything under **DM-Only** must be earned through play per `../11_mysteries_and_secrets/REVELATION_MAP.md`. **Never name the Hollow Court, the harvest mechanism, the Custodians, or the keystone's true nature to the player before REV_007 (Tier 3–4 gate).**

This is an **open-world kit, not a track.** It tells the DM what is prepared and how the mid-and-late game can branch so the player can pull any thread (or none) and still reach authored content. The world clocks advance the truth toward a passive player regardless (`../02_runtime_state/WORLD_CLOCKS.md`).

## The Story So Far (Entering Level 5)

Act 1 (`../12_campaign_arc/ACT_1_LEVELS_1_4.md`) ends with the player understanding **REV_001**: the death-rite failures have a *source and a pattern*, centered on the Hollowmere basin. The player has chosen — or been forced toward — a stance on the basin scheme, met the factions' local faces, and reached a first Concord site. Nothing of the substance (REV_002), the Concord's sin (REV_005), the deliberate Quietfall (REV_006), or the Hollow Court (REV_007) has been confirmed.

Levels 5–20 are the journey from *"the failures have a source"* to *"who gets to decide the fate of every soul that has ever died?"* — and the enacting of that decision.

## Tier ↔ Act Crosswalk (BINDING)

Stage 15 uses **tier** language (the user's framing). The existing canon (`MAIN_ARC_OVERVIEW.md`, `REVELATION_MAP.md`) uses **act** language. They map as follows and must stay consistent:

| Tier | Levels | Acts covered | Phase (REVELATION_MAP) | Headline truth the player may confirm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | 1–4 | Act 1 | Early | REV_001 — the failures have a source/pattern (the basin). |
| **Tier 2** | 5–10 | Act 2 (full) + Act 3 lower | Mid → into Deep | REV_002 (substance collected), REV_003 (Reke steers), REV_004 (you were steered); then **into** REV_005/REV_006 at L9–10. |
| **Tier 3** | 11–16 | Act 3 (upper) + Act 4 | Deep → Apex | REV_005/REV_006 confirmed, REV_009 (finite afterlife); the **REV_007 apex gate opens at L13+**; REV_010 (Maire). |
| **Tier 4** | 17–20 | Act 5 | Choice | REV_007 fully owned; REV_008 (the choice and its costs); the decision is enacted. |

> **Tier 2 spans an act-and-a-half on purpose.** Tier 2 is where the campaign opens widest (Ring 1, Caradril, the Verdance corridor) and where the *conspiratorial* truth (Act 2) gives way to the first *historical* truth (Act 3 lower) for a player who pushes hard. A slow player stays in Act 2 longer; a fast player tips into REV_005 by level 9–10. **Phase is gated by revelation progress, not strictly by level** (`REVELATION_MAP.md`).

## One-Paragraph Shape Per Tier (DM)

- **Tier 2 (5–10):** The world opens outward. The player becomes a regional force, then a consequential actor. Factions stop being local faces and start recruiting, opposing, deceiving, and retaliating at *regional* scale. The player learns Remembrance is a **substance someone collects** (REV_002), that a trusted authority is **steering the basin reopening** (REV_003/Reke), and that they themselves **were steered here** (REV_004). The drowned shrine becomes the obvious center of everything. By the top of the tier, a hard-pushing player begins to confirm the Concord's true sin (REV_005). Caradril is the mid-game library/bank/battlefield. See `TIER_2_LEVELS_5_10.md`.
- **Tier 3 (11–16):** The continent opens. The historical truth lands and shatters the noble-fallen-order myth: **the Concord harvested the dead** (REV_005) and **the Quietfall was deliberate** (REV_006); the afterlife is **finite and was nearly drained** (REV_009). Faction war goes continental. Far regions corroborate the *theme* without ever holding the keystone. At the top of the tier the apex gate opens: the player can confirm **the Hollow Court lives and is restarting the harvest** (REV_007) and meet Veyl — and learn of **Maire's heresy** (REV_010). See `TIER_3_LEVELS_11_16.md`.
- **Tier 4 (17–20):** The endgame. The full truth is owned. The player gathers the means to enact a decision about the Remembrance and descends (vertically, under Hollowmere) toward the keystone. **REV_008** — every ending has a terrible price; a synthesis is possible but hard. The climax is a *choice*, reachable by combat, alliance, ritual, artifact, politics, sacrifice, exposure, or synthesis. Epilogues reflect the accumulated path. See `TIER_4_LEVELS_17_20.md`.

## Per-Tier Entry-State Summary (Quick Reference)

### Tier 2 entry (level 5)
- **Knows:** REV_001 (source/pattern at the basin). Maybe a faction stance from Act 1.
- **Reputation:** known across Hollowmere; a rising name in the Reach.
- **Clocks:** all at Stage 1–2 (the Basin and Wardens clocks may be at Stage 2 if the player was passive).
- **Open:** Wren's fate resolved or its consequence live; basin stance chosen or forced.
- **Geography unlocked:** the full Sundering Reach; the roads to Ring 1 and toward Caradril.

### Tier 3 entry (level 11)
- **Knows:** REV_002/003/004; likely REV_005 begun (the Concord harvested) — confirmed or nearly so.
- **Reputation:** a regional power; allies and enemies banked from Tier 2 choices.
- **Clocks:** master clock and faction clocks at Stage 2–3; Caradril clocks advancing; Reclamation clock rising hard.
- **Open:** which factions the player has aligned with/against; whether Reke is exposed; whether Caradril is an asset or obstacle.
- **Geography unlocked:** the far continent via the Verdance corridor and the ports; the Concord Heartlands beckons.

### Tier 4 entry (level 17)
- **Knows:** REV_007 (the Court lives) — confirmed or about to be; REV_005/006/009 owned; REV_010 (Maire) available.
- **Reputation:** a continental player; faction postures locked toward the endgame.
- **Clocks:** master clock at Stage 3–4; the basin breachable or breached; rival claimants racing to the keystone.
- **Open:** which endgame approach the player commits to; which allies/artifacts they bring.
- **Geography unlocked:** the vertical descent — Basin Keystone Approach (D20) → the Under-Shrine (D23, DM-only).

## Open-World Spine (Always On, Levels 5–20)

1. **No single mandatory quest sequence.** Every tier offers faction-heavy, exploration-heavy, dungeon-heavy, investigation-heavy, social-heavy, and travel-heavy paths to the same revelations.
2. **No assumed faction.** The player may join none, one, several, or oppose all. Each path reaches the truth (every REV has 3+ independent clue sources spanning regions/NPCs/approaches).
3. **No fixed region order.** Ring 1 and the far continent are explorable in any order; the corridor logic (everything funnels back to the basin) is *geographic and cosmological*, not scripted.
4. **No single correct ending.** Five+ endgame branches, each with real costs (`ENDGAME_STRUCTURE.md`).
5. **Ignored threats evolve.** Clocks advance off-screen; a passive player still gets the truth and the consequences delivered by the world (`FAILURE_STATES_AND_WORLD_CONSEQUENCES.md`).
6. **Factions act without the player** (`../07_factions/FACTION_TURN_RULES.md`).
7. **Player choices change the endgame.** Tier 2–3 alignments set who stands with or against the player at the keystone.

## Secrecy Spine (Always On)

- **REV_007 (the Hollow Court lives) is the keystone reveal** — highest protection (`../11_mysteries_and_secrets/SECRET_PROTECTION_MATRIX.md`). Before its Tier 3–4 gate, the Court is a legend; all clues point at Reke / the Remnant / the Ledger first.
- **Player-safe arc summaries** (anything the DM might paraphrase aloud) never name the Court, the harvest mechanism, the surviving Custodians, the deliberate-Quietfall *intent*, or the under-shrine location.
- **Far-continent sources never deliver the apex.** They corroborate REV_002/REV_005 obliquely and REV_009 deeply obliquely — never the Court, the keystone, or the Concord Deep.
- **The endgame is vertical, under Hollowmere.** No far land is the keystone; no second Court exists.

## Solo-Play Spine (Always On)

- Telegraph lethal danger at every tier; no ambush ends a run before a meaningful choice.
- Every revelation is reachable without winning a fight.
- Allies, hirelings, faction support, terrain, retreat, and noncombat resolution scale with the player (`SOLO_CAMPAIGN_BALANCE_GUIDE.md`).
- Fallbacks exist when the solo PC lacks key tools (resurrection, teleport, flight, dispel, remove curse, restoration, reliable AoE) — see `SOLO_CAMPAIGN_BALANCE_GUIDE.md`.
- Bosses (including Veyl) have weaknesses, terrain, and non-combat win conditions; never a forced damage race.
