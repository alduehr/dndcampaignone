## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY — five concrete, named, step-by-step paths through Tier 2 (levels 5–10).** Each path is a runnable spine the AI DM can follow without inventing structure. They are **not exclusive or mandatory** — the player can mix them, switch between them, or follow none (the world clocks deliver the truth anyway). All five reach the tier's core revelations (REV_002/003/004) by different routes. **Apex (REV_007/Court) is never reached in Tier 2 — keep it a legend.**

The five paths cover the open-world spine: **Faction**, **Mystery**, **Dungeon**, **City**, **Travel**.

---

## Path 1: The Faction Path — "Throw In With (or Against) a Faction"

**Levels:** 5–10
**Entry Point:** any Reach settlement where a faction has a face (Hollowmere, the Ashwalk Rest, the Greyfens, Saltmargin).

### Step 1 (Levels 5–6)
The player runs a faction's Stage 8 introductory chain. **Named example (Wardens):** Hospitaller **Sirrin** at the Ashwalk Rest is overwhelmed; the player helps with failing road-rites (`QW1`, `Q_THE_FAILING_FUNERAL`), earning Warden trust and, eventually, the **Warden's Unlit Lantern** (Rank 3 reward). *Changes:* slows Clock #3 (Wardens Break); banks the Wardens as a seal ally. *(Substitute any faction: Ledger `QL1` via Domic Sael; Mourners `QM1` via Grandmother Wend; Gravecallers `QG1` via the Tallow Man; Compact `QC1` via Coalmont; Remnant `QR1` via Veska Dunn/Briss.)*

### Step 2 (Levels 7–8)
The chain hits its **moral complication and rival conflict**. **Named example (Wardens):** the player learns the Wardens "keep something sealed" and meets resistance from relic-buyers and Gravecallers (`QW2`/`QW3`). Cross-faction friction goes hot: the Compact pushes the basin, the Ledger out-buys salvage, the Gravecallers inch toward the shrine. The player is now a target — a crossed faction retaliates (telegraphed: a jail-arc, debt-arc, or bounty-arc). *Changes:* the player's faction posture hardens; rival clocks (#5/#6) advance if neglected. **REV_002** surfaces here via the faction's relic-knowledge (a Warden's relic, the Tallow Man's teaching, the Ledger's vault).

### Step 3 (Levels 9–10)
The chain reaches its **Q*4 decision point**, setting the faction's endgame posture. **Named example (Wardens):** `QW4_THE_OATH_AND_THE_DOUBT` — the player learns Othetha guards a true purpose, and a hard-pushing player earns her **first confession** toward **REV_005** (the Concord harvested). *Changes:* the Wardens rally or fracture (Clock #3); the seal stance is set for the endgame. **Transition to Tier 3:** Othetha's confession (or the Sealed Archive) tips the player into the historical truth.

### Milestone Guidance
- L5: completing the faction's intro chain (Q*1).
- L6: surviving first faction friction; the Lantern/relic reward.
- L7: the moral complication (Q*2/Q*3); becoming a target.
- L8: confirming REV_002 through faction knowledge.
- L9: reaching the Q*4 decision point.
- L10: setting the faction's endgame posture; Othetha's first confession.

### Failure State
Abandoning the faction lets its Q*4 resolve off-screen in its default (worst-for-player) direction — the Wardens scatter, the Monopoly closes, the Circle turns militant, etc. The player loses that faction as an endgame ally but the campaign continues.

### Transition Hook to Tier 3
**Warden Othetha** (or the player's chosen faction's truth-holder) points toward the historical truth: "The seal keeps something older than the Quietfall." This opens REV_005 and the Tier 3 continental war.

## Path 2: The Mystery Path — "Follow the Failures to Their Source"

**Levels:** 5–10
**Entry Point:** Hollowmere, after Act 1's REV_001 (the failures center on the basin).

### Step 1 (Levels 5–6)
The player investigates *what the relics are*. **Named:** study a Remembrance relic (the Peat Chapel altar relic, or "old glass" from the Deep Adit, D05); or accept the **Tallow Man**'s teaching in the Greyfens — the dead taught him the lingering is a *substance*. *Changes:* **REV_002** confirmed (Remembrance is collected on purpose). Quests: `Q_MAJOR_004`, `Q_THE_SECOND_MARK`.

### Step 2 (Levels 7–8)
The player investigates *who steers the basin*. **Named:** Reeve **Coalmont**'s suspicions + the council votes; **Pevin Oss**'s forged water-rights filings; tail **Reke** to the basin shore at night (C-M4-3). *Changes:* **REV_003** confirmed (Reke steers the basin reopening for a hidden master — but his master stays murky). Quest: `Q_MAJOR_002`, `Q_THE_REED_HOLMS`.

### Step 3 (Levels 9–10)
The player turns the lens on *themselves*. **Named:** press **Sefra Quick** about her odd job and "wrong money"; follow the patron's payment trail to a cutout (in Hollowmere or via the **Bellman** in Caradril). *Changes:* **REV_004** confirmed (the player was steered here as an unwitting probe — a personal shock; the ultimate employer stays hidden). Quest: `Q_MAJOR_007`, `Q_THE_BELLMANS_PRICE`. **Transition:** the basin and the shrine are now the undeniable center; the player begins to suspect a deeper, older truth (REV_005).

### Milestone Guidance
- L5: confirming a relic holds a person.
- L6: REV_002 (substance collected).
- L7: suspecting Reke.
- L8: REV_003 (Reke steers).
- L9: pressing Sefra.
- L10: REV_004 (you were steered).

### Failure State
If the player drops the investigation, the world clocks deliver REV_002/003 via the surfacing shrine and active factions — slower, with fewer leads. REV_004 may go cold (Sefra vanishes) — a missed personal beat, not a block.

### Transition Hook to Tier 3
Reke's "hidden master" and the relics' true age point toward the Concord itself. The **Sealed Archive** in Caradril (`Q_THE_SEALED_ARCHIVE`) or **Othetha**'s confession opens REV_005.

## Path 3: The Dungeon Path — "Read the Truth in the Ruins"

**Levels:** 5–10
**Entry Point:** any Concord node site; the Deep Adit (D05) is the natural first.

### Step 1 (Levels 5–6)
The player dives the **Deep Adit** (D05) or the **Greyfens Deep** (D19). "Old glass" demonstrates the substance (REV_002); the **Reclaimers** (Veska Dunn) are racing for it. *Changes:* REV_002 fragment; Clock #7 (Reclamation) if the player beats or joins them. **Thin-touch** is the signature hazard — get the **Drowned Flame** (from the Pale Coast, D14) to dive safely.

### Step 2 (Levels 7–8)
The player dives the **Concord Relay-Vault / Signal-Cairn** (D18) or the **Sunken Wards Deep** (D16, Caradril). The Relay-Warden construct guards Concord Script that describes "the keeping of the dead's pattern" (M2/M6/M9 fragment); the player can recover the **Custodian's Compass** (node-finder). *Changes:* REV_002 deepened; the Compass turns the region into a map of truth. The Sunken Wards prove the network is *continental*.

### Step 3 (Levels 9–10)
The player reaches the **Basin Keystone Approach** (D20) — the keystone's surface/upper works. REV_001/003/005 *fragments* surface; the deep stair below is **telegraphed lethal** and the Court is dormant/sealed. *Changes:* the player sees the center but cannot yet breach it. **Guard rail:** a player who dives early gets fragments and danger, **never REV_007**. **Transition:** the Compass and the surface fragments pull the player toward the historical truth (REV_005) — found fully via Script (Caradril Archive) or confession (Othetha).

### Milestone Guidance
- L5: clearing the Deep Adit / Greyfens Deep.
- L6: REV_002 via "old glass"; securing the Drowned Flame.
- L7: the Relay-Vault; the Custodian's Compass.
- L8: the Sunken Wards (continental network proof).
- L9: reaching D20.
- L10: surface fragments at the keystone; gated from the deep.

### Failure State
A failed dive costs time, a clue, and Thin-touch — never the run (every D-site has a retreat route). Diving D20's deep stair under-leveled yields fragments and lethal danger; the player must withdraw and prepare.

### Transition Hook to Tier 3
The Custodian's Compass points ever down and home; the surface fragments demand the full history. The far nodes (Nine Locks, D34) and the Sealed Archive open REV_005/006 and Tier 3.

## Path 4: The City Path — "Caradril, Where the Conspiracy Lives in Daylight"

**Levels:** 5–10
**Entry Point:** Caradril, ~10–12 days SE of Hollowmere (via the Verdance, the East Road, or Wenna Roke's water-route).

### Step 1 (Levels 5–6)
The player arrives and reads the city's denial. **Named:** the quiet-coin trade in the Ashmarket and **Beck Harrow**'s relic-smelting in the Crucible demonstrate the substance commercially and by accident (REV_002); thin-born refugees gather in the Sill under **Renna Sill** (REV_009 local). *Changes:* REV_002 (clearest commercial proof); Clock C3 (the city's denial). Quests: `Q_QUIET_COIN`, `Q_THE_SMELTING`.

### Step 2 (Levels 7–8)
The player works the city's power game. **Named:** back **Sefa Dann**'s reform against the Ledger/old Houses (`Q_THE_OPEN_CHARTER`); turn Ledger clerk **Coll Riis** to copy proof of the Vyre–Quorrin deal; buy the Reke correspondent fragment from the **Bellman** (`Q_THE_SEALED_LETTER`, REV_004 lead). *Changes:* Clocks C1/C2; REV_004 deepened. The player makes city allies (Dann, Kade) or enemies (Vyre, the Syndicate).

### Step 3 (Levels 9–10)
The player reaches the **Sealed Archive** (the Lantern Reach, Script-gated, L9–12). With the **Sealed Archive Key**, **Tamsin Orr**/**Briss** for Script, past **Wessel Dree** — the clearest written proof of **REV_005** (the Concord harvested) and **REV_006** (the deliberate Quietfall). *Changes:* REV_005/006 confirmed (the historical truth lands). Quest: `Q_THE_SEALED_ARCHIVE`. **Transition:** the historical truth opens Tier 3 and the continental war.

### Milestone Guidance
- L5: arriving; reading the denial.
- L6: REV_002 via quiet-coin/smelting.
- L7: the open charter / turning Coll Riis.
- L8: the correspondent thread (REV_004 lead).
- L9: reaching the Sealed Archive (Script-gated).
- L10: REV_005/006 confirmed.

### Failure State
Ignoring the city lets the Vyre–Quorrin deal close (C1), the Council sleep (C2), and the denial crack into panic (C3) — a complacent, well-resourced endgame obstacle. **The Archive is the clearest REV_005/006 path but never the only one** (Othetha and the Tallow Man deliver it in the Reach), so a Caradril-skipper is never blocked.

### Transition Hook to Tier 3
The Sealed Archive's proof of the deliberate Quietfall (REV_006) demands the Concord's true scale — found at the **Concord Heartlands** (the Ruin'd Crown, D21) and far surface-proofs. Tier 3's continental war opens.

## Path 5: The Travel Path — "Open the World Outward"

**Levels:** 5–10
**Entry Point:** the Reach, ranging into Ring 1 and toward the Verdance corridor.

### Step 1 (Levels 5–6)
The player visits Ring 1 and finds the **honest windows**. **Named:** Old Mam **Tace** in Tilbrook (Vale), **Goodwife Sennet** in Coldhearth (Tollwood), **Salt-Mother Bryd** in Cobble Strand (Coast) each give an honest M5 witness — the failures *radiate outward from the Reach*. *Changes:* REV_001 corroboration; a folk-fragment of REV_005. Banks regional Mourner allies.

### Step 2 (Levels 7–8)
The player engages a Ring 1 crisis and earns survivability tech. **Named:** the Pale Coast's drowned-tide (`Q_THE_SALT_PRICE`) yields the **Drowned Flame** (Thin-touch immunity); the Tollwood toll-war (`Q_THE_TOLL_WAR`) keeps the East Road open; the Vale's saint's-bones (`Q_SAINTS_BONES`) prove REV_002 from outside the Reach. *Changes:* Ring 1 clocks; REV_002 corroboration; a key survivability reward.

### Step 3 (Levels 9–10)
The player opens the **Verdance corridor** (L8+). **Named:** up the Verdance to Marrowfen Stair, where **Factor-General Wessel Crane** builds the Ledger's inland pipeline; the **Nine Locks Sunken Stair** (D34) is a relay-vault that *points home* (M2/M5 — the network is continental). *Changes:* the far continent opens; REV_002 deepened. **Guard rail:** far sources corroborate the theme and point home — **never the apex**. **Transition:** the corridor leads to Glassmere and the Heartlands (Tier 3).

### Milestone Guidance
- L5: visiting Ring 1; the honest windows.
- L6: REV_001 corroboration; regional allies.
- L7: a Ring 1 crisis (Drowned Flame).
- L8: REV_002 corroboration from Ring 1.
- L9: opening the Verdance corridor.
- L10: the Nine Locks node points home.

### Failure State
Ignoring Ring 1 lets its clocks run (the Coast drowns, the Vale hollows, the Tollwood road chokes) and costs regional allies and the Drowned Flame. The far corridor opening is optional — the endgame is vertical under Hollowmere, so a homebody is never blocked.

### Transition Hook to Tier 3
The Nine Locks node "points home" and the corridor opens to **Glassmere** and the **Concord Heartlands** — where the Concord's true scale (REV_005/006) is corroborated and the continental faction war begins.

## Cross-Path Convergence (DM)

All five paths converge on the same Tier-2 truths by L10: **REV_002** (substance collected), **REV_003** (Reke steers), **REV_004** (you were steered), and the *threshold* of **REV_005** (the Concord harvested). They differ only in route and in which allies/enemies/rewards the player banks. **None reaches REV_007** — the Court stays a legend until L13+. Mix freely; the world clocks fill any gaps.
