## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY — what each endgame-relevant region looks like at Tier 4, and the role it plays in the climax.** Load when the player is in the endgame window and you need a region's Tier-4 state (what changed, who's there, what it offers, what endings it supports). Use with `TIER_4_PLAYABLE_PATHS_LEVELS_17_20.md` (the approaches) and `ENDGAME_STRUCTURE.md` (the outcomes). **The endgame is vertical, under Hollowmere; far regions are means-gathering, refuge, evidence, or battlefields — never the keystone.** **DM-only apex content stays DM-only; the Court/keystone/harvest are never named to the player before REV_007.**

Each region's Tier-4 state assumes the default trajectory (clocks advanced if the player didn't hold them); adjust for what the player actually banked.

## Sundering Reach — Endgame State (the home region; the descent)

Role: **the battlefield and the climax** — the keystone is under Hollowmere.

### What Has Changed By Tier 4
The basin is breached or breaching (Clock #2→keystone reopened); the upper shrine surfaced; the Reach is gripped by failing rites, wrong-come-backs, and fear (#1 MASTER→3–4). Rival claimants converge on the basin. **Reke** (or a successor) holds basin access.

### Active Factions
- **Reachward Compact:** the basin's gatekeeper — Reke (Court pawn) or a reformed Compact under **Coalmont** (if the player exposed Reke) holding it shut.
- **Ashen Wardens / Mourners' Circle:** **Othetha** and **Wend** rally to defend/restore the seal — the seal-coalition backbone.
- **Gravecallers:** **Cole Ashby**'s cell races to shatter the keystone.
- *(DM: the **Hollow Court** — Veyl/Maire/Orre — awake beneath the basin, acting through Reke.)*

### Key NPCs
Othetha (seal-rite), Wend (Mourner numbers), Coalmont (holds the basin), Reke (surface link), Cole Ashby (the breach), Sefra Quick (the steering, if unresolved). *(DM: Veyl, Maire, Orre at the keystone.)*

### Key Sites
**D20 Basin Keystone Approach** → **D23 Under-Shrine Approach** (DM-only apex). The basin itself, held or breached, above.

### What Endings This Region Can Support
**All of them** — the keystone is here. Seal (Wardens' rite), Restart (default/Veyl), Seize (a rival wins the race), Destroy (Gravecaller/Shard), Transform (Maire + coalition + Last Voice).

### If Player Never Visited
Impossible to skip — the endgame *is* here. If the player ignored the Reach, the DM brings the endgame to them (the harvest reaches their region; `CONTINENTAL_PRESSURE_TIMELINE.md` T4-3) and provides the exposition the player missed (who Reke is, what the basin holds — revealed at the gate, never before).

## Caradril — Endgame State (the political hub)

Role: **ally source / Seize-Exposure lever / obstacle.**

### What Has Changed By Tier 4
The Vyre–Quorrin deal closed or shattered (C1); the Council is reformed (Dann/Kade) or complacent (C2); the denial cracked into reckoning or panic (C3); the Sill's thin-born refugees swelled.

### Active Factions
- **Cinder Ledger:** **Vyre**/**Anneth Vell** — a continental power backing whoever lets it sell immortality, or weakened if the player split it.
- **Concord Remnant:** **Quorrin**/**Sabine Ferrant** — racing for the keystone, or fractured if the player turned the idealists.
- **Tidewater Council:** reformed (Dann/Kade — an endgame asset) or complacent (an obstacle).

### Key NPCs
Sefa Dann / Yorell Kade (reformed-city allies), Calla Vyre / Quorrin Vane (rival claimants), the Bellman (information), Renna Sill (thin-born advocate; REV_009 moral weight).

### Key Sites
The Sealed Archive (REV_005/006 — the public-truth evidence for Exposure); the Ledger Keep (the deal's heart); the Sunken Wards (a dormant cousin-node).

### What Endings This Region Can Support
**Seize** (a reformed Council or the Ledger/Remnant as the "responsible" living owner), **Exposure/Transform** (the Sealed Archive as public proof + the Ash-Crown legitimacy), **Alliance** (reformed Caradril's political/military backing for the seal-coalition).

### If Player Never Visited
The deal closed unopposed; Caradril is a complacent, well-resourced endgame obstacle. The DM provides the REV_005/006 proof through the Reach (Othetha, the Tallow Man) and far sources instead — the Archive was the clearest path, never the only one.

## Concord Heartlands / Ruin'd Crown — Endgame State (the primary apex *approach*)

Role: **evidence / legitimacy / rival-stronghold** — the imperial proof and the Ash-Crown.

### What Has Changed By Tier 4
**Hollin Vane** secured the Ruin'd Crown and the Ash-Crown (or the player took it); the Reclamation peaked (#7→3–4); the Remnant has imperial legitimacy and races for the keystone.

### Active Factions
- **Concord Remnant:** **Hollin Vane**/**Sabine Ferrant** — the strongest living claimant, a tyranny-in-waiting; **Wenna Stone** turnable to split them.
- **Cinder Ledger:** backing or betraying the dig.
- **Crownmouth / Pilgrim factions:** **Lyssa Crownmouth** vs. the Camps.

### Key NPCs
Hollin Vane (apex-adjacent rival; never confirms the Court), Lyssa Crownmouth, "Old Crown" Mab (guide), Casian Ord (folk-truth), Wenna Stone (the Remnant-split lever).

### Key Sites
**D21 Old Concord Heartlands Ruin / Ruin'd Crown** — the imperial REV_005/006 proof; the Ash-Crown Fragment.

### What Endings This Region Can Support
**Seize** (the Ash-Crown legitimacy for the player or a reformed Remnant), **Exposure** (the imperial records as public proof), **Alliance** (a split, reform-Remnant joins the player). **Never the keystone** — the climax is under Hollowmere; the Heartlands feed the *approach*.

### If Player Never Visited
Hollin Vane holds the Ash-Crown and imperial legitimacy uncontested — the strongest endgame rival, racing the player to the keystone. The player lacks the Seize/Exposure legitimacy lever (gettable, weaker, elsewhere).

## Emberfell — Endgame State (the artifact/wildcard mirror)

Role: **artifact source / thematic wildcard.**

### What Has Changed By Tier 4
The theocracy consolidated the "old glass" supply (or the player broke it); **Mira Cindra**'s heresy is ascendant (if shielded) or silenced (if not); the Caldera works are a relic-source rivals covet.

### Active Factions
- **Emberfell Theocracy:** **Vole Cindra**/**Sef Embren** — a distant power; a reformed theocracy (via Mira) can be an Alliance/Transform contributor.
- **Cinder Ledger:** relic-buyers competing for the "old glass."

### Key NPCs
Mira Cindra (turnable scholar; synthesis-mirror), Vole Cindra, Doss Ashfast.

### Key Sites
**D22 Emberfell Caldera Descent** — a source of fire-tempered relics and possibly an artifact lever; REV_002 echo.

### What Endings This Region Can Support
**Transform** (the public, consensual fire-faith as a working synthesis-mirror — rhetorical/model support), **Artifact** (fire-tempered relics, "old glass" for the Shard). A distant Alliance contributor (a reformed theocracy).

### If Player Never Visited
The theocracy consolidated the relic-supply; the synthesis-mirror argument is unavailable to the player's endgame case; a far artifact-source is lost.

## Glassmere League — Endgame State (the political/faction endgame)

Role: **ally source / Ledger-split lever / Exposure venue.**

### What Has Changed By Tier 4
**Oren Glass** corners the continental relic-trade (#6→4) or the player split the Ledger (via **Sefwy Holt**); **Mareth Senn**'s Remnant chapter backs the Heartlands race or was fractured.

### Active Factions
- **Cinder Ledger:** the continental heartland — an obstacle, or split if the player turned Holt.
- **Concord Remnant:** Mareth Senn's grand chapter — a rival, or fractured.
- **The living powers:** the League can be a venue for Exposure (a continental audience for the truth).

### Key NPCs
Sefwy Holt (Ledger-split ally), Oren Glass, Mareth Senn, Wenna Lowwater (old-songs/moral weight), "the Glass Ear."

### Key Sites
The Reliquary (far Concord records — Exposure evidence); the Three Bridges (banking leverage).

### What Endings This Region Can Support
**Exposure** (a continental audience + far records), **Seize** (banking/political leverage), **Alliance** (a split Ledger weakens the commodify-the-dead path; Holt and the League back the player).

### If Player Never Visited
The Ledger consolidates the Monopoly continentally; immortality is sold; Mareth Senn's chapter backs the Heartlands race. The endgame faces a richer, unified Ledger.

## Hollow Gulf Ports — Endgame State (maritime logistics)

Role: **logistics / transport / refuge / world-horizon.**

### What Has Changed By Tier 4
One port consolidated the southern trade (or the player brokered a balance); the Ledger's southern reach grew; **Hadiz of Surren** hints the wider world watches.

### Active Factions
- **Rival port city-states:** **Ive Calder** (Calderport) vs. **Doss Saltgate** — a transport/logistics ally if the player engaged them.
- **Privateers:** **Roke Mallin** — naval muscle, hired or hostile.

### Key NPCs
Ive Calder (port-ruler; transport), Roke Mallin (privateer; naval muscle), Sera Mardenmouth (sea-Mourner; old-songs), Hadiz of Surren (world-horizon).

### Key Sites
The Wreck-Reef (D31); the ports as transport hubs.

### What Endings This Region Can Support
**Logistics for any approach** (moving a coalition, an artifact, or the player by sea); a **refuge** in a bleak aftermath; a **world-horizon** epilogue note (the wider world reacts).

### If Player Never Visited
A southern port consolidated; the player lacks maritime transport/naval support for the endgame; the world-horizon stays distant.

## Saltmere Reaches — Endgame State (the DM-only echo region)

Role: **older-fall evidence / Destroy-Transform debate corroboration** (DM-only thematic).

### What Has Changed By Tier 4
The salt-realm fractured further (or the player banked **Bryd Saltmere**'s clans); the Drowned Towns were stripped (or preserved); **Tess Brackhold**'s old-songs node corroborates REV_005.

### Active Factions
- **Salt-clan holds:** Bryd Saltmere — a far Alliance contributor and the old-songs network.
- *(DM: the Drowned Towns are a pre-Concord "older fall" echo — never the keystone.)*

### Key NPCs
Bryd Saltmere (far ally), Tess Brackhold (old-songs node), Oss (delver).

### Key Sites
**D24 Saltmere Deep Towns** — older-fall corroboration (the world has drowned its dead before).

### What Endings This Region Can Support
**Transform/Destroy debate weight** (the older-fall lore: the harvest was a pattern, not a unique sin — strengthening the case that the world can find another way); a far Alliance contributor; the old-songs moral network.

### If Player Never Visited
The older-fall corroboration is missing from the endgame moral case; a far ally and the salt-grief synthesis-model are unbanked. **DM-only:** this never affects the keystone — Saltmere is a thematic echo, not a node.

## Supporting Endgame Regions (not primary endgame sites — banked ally/evidence/logistics sources)

These regions are **not primary Tier-4 operational areas**. The player does not need to travel to them at levels 17–20. They are **banked asset sources** — the value of having visited them earlier (allies recruited, artifacts retrieved, clues confirmed) flows into the endgame. Each entry describes that value and what the AI DM should infer if the region was skipped.

### Sunmark — the synthesis model (Transform's requirement)
Role: **the working consensual model.** **Sael Sunmark** and the Sunhollow Great Grove (D33) are the proof that holding the dead by consent is possible — a **requirement for the Transform ending**. If the player never visited, the Transform synthesis is much harder to model (though REV_010/Maire can still carry it). A banked Sael Sunmark is a far Alliance/Transform contributor.

### Pale Coast — the Drowned Flame (survivability)
Role: **survivability tech.** The **Drowned Flame** (Thin-touch immunity) from the Drowned Lamp/Skerry Shrine makes the lethal descent survivable; **Salt-Mother Bryd** is a banked seal/synthesis ally. If skipped, the player needs the Flame retrieved late or risks Thin-touch in the descent.

### Karran Marches — the Remnant-split lever
Role: **ally source.** **Wenna Stone** (turnable Reclaimer) is the player's best far lever to split the Remnant, banking a reform-Remnant ally for the Alliance/Seize endings. If skipped, the Remnant stays unified behind Quorrin/Hollin Vane.

### Wender Steppe / Highmark — the cosmic frame and cold proof
Role: **evidence / existential stakes.** **Wind-Singer Esha**'s REV_009 echo and the **Highmark Frozen Works** (D35) REV_005/006 cold-proof give the player the full cosmic stakes that make Veyl's argument frighteningly reasonable and the Transform ending the only one that addresses the thinning. If skipped, the player reaches the apex without the full cosmic frame (REV_009 stays shallow).

### Ashgarden Vale / Tollwood — Ring 1 allies and refuge
Role: **ally source / refuge.** **Ezrith Combe** (Vale Mourner) and **Goodwife Sennet**/**Brann** (Tollwood) are banked allies and the wood/vale as refuge or route-control. If skipped, the seal-coalition is thinner and the East Road (logistics to Caradril) may be choked.
