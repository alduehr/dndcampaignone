# Part 1 — The Ten Revelations (Three Route-Independent Paths Each)

## REV_001 — The Failures Have a Source and a Pattern
Secrecy: discoverable (the Act 1 frame).

### Discovery Path A
Region: Sundering Reach / Hollowmere. Source: **Wren Hollowmere** herself. Method: social/personal. What the player learns: the dead are not lingering at random — Wren remembers dying and "something held her"; the crisis has a center.

### Discovery Path B
Region: Sundering Reach / Greyfens. Source: **Old Sashe**'s drift-map. Method: exploration/testimony. What the player learns: the dead consistently drift *toward* the basin, for years — a pattern.

### Discovery Path C
Region: Ring 1 (Vale/Tollwood/Coast). Source: **Old Mam Tace** / **Goodwife Sennet** / **Salt-Mother Bryd**. Method: social (the honest M5 windows). What the player learns: the failures *radiate outward from the Reach* — the basin is the center.

### What Unlocks After This Revelation
The basin/shrine become the obvious investigative center; Act 2's threads (REV_002/003/004) open.

### What Changes in the World
Horror becomes a solvable mystery; the player can act on a center, not just react to scattered failures.

---

## REV_002 — Remembrance Is a Substance Someone Collects
Secrecy: dm-only until earned, then player-known.

### Discovery Path A
Region: Reach or Caradril. Source: **studying a Remembrance relic** (the Peat Chapel altar relic; Caradril quiet-coin). Method: magic/investigation. What the player learns: a relic *stores a person* — identity is a substance.

### Discovery Path B
Region: Reach / Greyfens. Source: **the Tallow Man**'s teaching. Method: forbidden/social. What the player learns: the dead taught him the lingering is a substance that can be gathered.

### Discovery Path C
Region: Caradril (Ashmarket/Crucible) or far (Emberfell/Karran). Source: **Beck Harrow**'s relic-smelting / **Doss Ashfast**'s "old glass" / **Mully Karr**'s "humming iron." Method: mercantile. What the player learns: someone is collecting the substance on purpose, continent-wide.

### What Unlocks After This Revelation
Reframes the crisis as a profit/power conspiracy; implicates the Ledger and Remnant; gates toward REV_005.

### What Changes in the World
The relic-trade becomes sinister; the player can follow the money to the conspiracy.

---

## REV_003 — A Trusted Authority Is Steering the Basin (Reke)
Secrecy: dm-only until earned.

### Discovery Path A
Region: Reach. Source: **Reeve Marda Coalmont**'s suspicions + the council votes. Method: political. What the player learns: the basin scheme doesn't add up; someone steers it.

### Discovery Path B
Region: Reach/Caradril. Source: **Pevin Oss**'s forged water-rights filings + a misfiled Caradril charter record. Method: paper trail. What the player learns: the scheme is a cover to reopen the shrine.

### Discovery Path C
Region: Reach. Source: **tail Reke to the basin shore at night**. Method: surveillance. What the player learns: Reke serves a hidden master.

### What Unlocks After This Revelation
Names a human antagonist (Reke); raises the basin-clock stakes; a prerequisite for REV_007.

### What Changes in the World
The player can expose Reke (slowing the basin) or watch him entrench (opening the keystone).

---

## REV_004 — You Were Steered Here
Secrecy: dm-only until earned, then a personal shock.

### Discovery Path A
Region: Reach. Source: **press Sefra Quick**. Method: social/confession. What the player learns: Sefra was paid by a "wrong-money" patron to point them at the shrine.

### Discovery Path B
Region: Reach/Caradril. Source: **trace the patron's payments** to a cutout. Method: follow-the-money. What the player learns: the steering traces to a hidden employer (kept murky).

### Discovery Path C
Region: Caradril. Source: **the Bellman** sells a correspondent-thread fragment. Method: broker. What the player learns: the steering connects to Reke's circle (never a named second Court agent).

### What Unlocks After This Revelation
A trust crisis; personal stakes; the player questions every "lucky" lead.

### What Changes in the World
The player realizes they are a piece on someone's board — a personal motive for the endgame.

---

## REV_005 — The Concord Harvested the Dead
Secrecy: dm-only (apex-historical truth).

### Discovery Path A
Region: Caradril. Source: **the Sealed Archive** (Script-gated; Tamsin Orr/Briss; past Wessel Dree). Method: archives/documentary. What the player learns: the clearest written proof — the Concord's golden age was powered by consuming the dead.

### Discovery Path B
Region: Reach. Source: **Warden Othetha**'s confession (trust-gated). Method: confession. What the player learns: the Wardens were seeded to keep the harvest paused.

### Discovery Path C
Region: far surface-proof (**Concord Heartlands** D21 / **Highmark Frozen Works** D35) OR the **old-songs network** (Wend, Ezrith Combe, Bryd, Sennet, far anchors). Method: exploration / folk-song. What the player learns: the Concord's true imperial scale and sin — never the live machine/Court.

### What Unlocks After This Revelation
Destroys the noble-fallen-order myth; recontextualizes all history; a prerequisite for REV_007.

### What Changes in the World
The Wardens (unwitting guardians) and the Remnant (would-be heirs) are recast; the player understands the *crime*.

---

## REV_006 — The Quietfall Was Deliberate
Secrecy: dm-only (pairs with REV_005).

### Discovery Path A
Region: Caradril. Source: **the Sealed Archive**'s signed, planned Quietfall. Method: archives. What the player learns: the inner Custodians destroyed their own order on purpose.

### Discovery Path B
Region: Reach. Source: **Othetha**'s confession / the **Tallow Man**'s Quietfall-era witness. Method: confession/channeling. What the player learns: the Quietfall was a controlled pause to let the drained afterlife refill.

### Discovery Path C
Region: Reach/Caradril/far. Source: **Concord Script inscriptions** at the shrine, node-sites, or the Frozen Works (D35). Method: inscription (Script-gated). What the player learns: the pause was intentional, stated in the Concord's own hand.

### What Unlocks After This Revelation
Reframes the world's founding trauma; explains the "age of healing"; directly sets up REV_007.

### What Changes in the World
The Quietfall is no longer an accident; the player asks *where are the ones who did it?* — the convergence toward the Court.

---

## REV_007 — The Hollow Court Lives and Is Restarting the Harvest (APEX)
Secrecy: **dm-only — the campaign keystone reveal. Highest protection. L13+ only. NEVER from a far source.**

### Discovery Path A
Region: Sundering Reach / Under-Shrine. Source: **descend to the under-shrine and meet them** (D20→D23). Method: physical (lethal-telegraphed). What the player learns: the original Custodians survive as the Hollow Court beneath the basin.

### Discovery Path B
Region: cross-region. Source: **convergence of REV_003 + REV_005 + REV_006**. Method: inference (DM confirms). What the player learns: the only explanation that fits all the proof — the architects survive and are restarting the harvest.

### Discovery Path C
Region: Reach. Source: **Custodian Maire reaches out** OR the **Tallow Man**'s dead name "the ones who would not pass." Method: heretic/forbidden contact. What the player learns: a Custodian voice confirms the Court is real.

### What Unlocks After This Revelation
Names the apex; makes the antagonist a person with an argument (Veyl); opens the endgame choice (REV_008) and Maire's heresy (REV_010); begins Tier 4.

### What Changes in the World
The campaign's true shape is revealed; the player can move to enact a decision about the Remembrance.

> **REV_007 has no single-source path** — convergence (three prior REVs), OR a lethal physical descent, OR heretic contact. Never one NPC, document, or roll. Before L13 the Court is a legend; the player's likely guesses (Quorrin/the Remnant; Reke) are *deliberately* fostered.

## REV_008 — The Choice and Its Costs
Secrecy: dm-only until apex; the *choice* becomes player-known in Act 4–5.

### Discovery Path A (consequence-knowledge)
Region: Reach. Source: the **Gravecaller warning** (Cole Ashby — destroy = catastrophe) + the **Warden warning** (Othetha — seal = end of resurrection). Method: faction testimony. What the player learns: two endings and their costs.

### Discovery Path B
Region: Under-Shrine. Source: **Veyl's case** (control = stability + horror). Method: the Court's argument. What the player learns: the restart's seductive, monstrous logic.

### Discovery Path C
Region: Under-Shrine + Sunmark. Source: **Maire's heresy** + the **Sunmark grove-faith** (Sael Sunmark). Method: heretic + far model. What the player learns: a synthesis (consensual system) is conceivable.

### What Unlocks After This Revelation
Turns the climax into an informed moral decision; frames all five endings.

### What Changes in the World
The player chooses the fate of the Remembrance knowing every price.

---

## REV_009 — The Afterlife Is Finite and Was Nearly Drained
Secrecy: dm-only (cosmological floor).

### Discovery Path A
Region: Caradril/Reach. Source: **deep resurrection-failure study** (Onaia Veen / Mother Ysarra) + relic analysis. Method: research. What the player learns: the Quiet Country is finite and nearly emptied.

### Discovery Path B
Region: Caradril/Reach. Source: **the thin-born** (Renna Sill's refugees; "thin" Harrowgast miners). Method: living evidence. What the player learns: the thinning is real and demographic.

### Discovery Path C
Region: far (**Wender Steppe** / **Sunmark**). Source: **Wind-Singer Esha**'s "the Sky has gone thin" / **Sun-Singer Doll**'s "a far root aches." Method: cosmic echo (deeply oblique). What the player learns: the wrongness is cosmic — never names the Quiet Country/harvest.

### What Unlocks After This Revelation
Raises the stakes of every ending (sealing/restarting both touch the afterlife's survival); makes Veyl's argument reasonable.

### What Changes in the World
The player understands *why* the harvest exists — and why only Transform addresses the thinning without consuming the dead.

---

## REV_010 — Maire's Heresy: The Court Could End Itself (synthesis key)
Secrecy: dm-only; endgame only.

### Discovery Path A
Region: Under-Shrine. Source: **Maire reaches out directly**. Method: apex contact. What the player learns: a Custodian secretly believes the Court should end and the dead should choose.

### Discovery Path B
Region: Under-Shrine. Source: **perceiving Orre's hawkishness vs. Maire's doubt**. Method: reading the Court's internal tension. What the player learns: the Court is divided; Maire is the crack.

### Discovery Path C
Region: cross-region. Source: the **Sunmark model** + **Gravecaller fragments** (a consensual system is conceivable). Method: synthesis evidence. What the player learns: Maire's heresy is *achievable*, not just wished.

### What Unlocks After This Revelation
The hardest, best ending (Transform); requires uniting unlikely allies and turning Maire against Veyl.

### What Changes in the World
The synthesis becomes reachable — the only ending that ends the Court from within.

# Part 2 — The Ending Paths

The five canonical outcomes (`ENDGAME_STRUCTURE.md`), each as a runnable ending. Approaches that reach each are in `TIER_4_PLAYABLE_PATHS_LEVELS_17_20.md`.

## Ending: SEAL IT (Outcome A)
Requirements: Warden seal-knowledge (**Othetha** / QW4) or the keystone severed; allies to hold off rival claimants.
Key Revelation Required: REV_007 (the Court lives) — to know what is being sealed.
Final Site: the Under-Shrine (D23) — or the basin held shut above (political seal).
Final Boss / Obstacle: **Custodian Orre** (if force) / rival claimants / the basin's lethality — or no boss (political/ritual seal).
Resolution: re-seal the shrine via the Warden rite; the harvest stops; resurrection mostly ends.
World-State After: the thinning slows but isn't healed; the Wardens are vindicated; resurrection-dependent powers (Ledger, some clergy) lose footing; civilization loses its crutch against death.
NPC Fates: **Othetha** vindicated; **Veyl** sealed away (not destroyed); **Wend** and the Mourners at peace; **Maire** sealed with the Court (unless saved).
Faction Fates: Wardens vindicated; Mourners vindicated; Ledger impoverished; Remnant thwarted; Gravecallers suppressed; Hollow Court sealed away.

## Ending: RESTART IT CONTROLLED (Outcome B)
Requirements: nothing the player must build — the *default* if the player does nothing, or a deliberate choice if persuaded by **Veyl**.
Key Revelation Required: REV_007 + REV_008 (to choose it deliberately) — or none (the default).
Final Site: the Under-Shrine (D23).
Final Boss / Obstacle: the player's own conscience; Veyl's argument; the rivals (who want it for themselves).
Resolution: the Court resumes a "gentle" harvest; the player joins, allows, or fails to stop them.
World-State After: an age of order under the Court; the dead consumed forever; the thinning addressed by consumption; a slow horror beneath a calm surface.
NPC Fates: **Veyl** rules as immortal steward; **Maire** silenced/sidelined; **Orre** enforces; the player a dark patron's agent or a failed hero.
Faction Fates: Hollow Court rules; Remnant absorbed; Ledger become the Court's bankers; Wardens obsolete; Mourners a grieving resistance.

## Ending: SEIZE IT (Outcome C)
Requirements: a living claimant who wins the keystone race (their Tier 2–3 posture) — or the player takes it; the **Ash-Crown** legitimacy helps.
Key Revelation Required: REV_007 (the machine exists to be taken).
Final Site: the Under-Shrine (D23) — or a public forum (Exposure → "responsible" living owner).
Final Boss / Obstacle: the rival claimants (**Hollin Vane**/**Quorrin** or **Vyre**) and/or the Court.
Resolution: the machine is handed to a single living faction (a restored Concord or the Ledger's monopoly) — or taken by the player.
World-State After: a new tyranny or a market in souls; the original sin repeated by living hands.
NPC Fates: **Hollin Vane**/**Quorrin** or **Vyre** the new tyrant/monopolist; **Veyl** displaced; the player a kingmaker or new tyrant.
Faction Fates: the winning faction triumphant; the others sidelined/ruined; Hollow Court displaced by rivals.

## Ending: DESTROY IT (Outcome D)
Requirements: Gravecaller breaking-tools + shrine map (**Cole Ashby**) or the **Harvest Engine Shard**; ideally guidance to break it *safely* (the synthesis-adjacent variant).
Key Revelation Required: REV_007 (what is being destroyed) + ideally REV_008 (the cost of catastrophe).
Final Site: the Under-Shrine (D23) — the keystone shattered.
Final Boss / Obstacle: **Custodian Orre** (defends to destruction); the catastrophe risk itself.
Resolution: the harvest is shattered; the dead freed; resurrection ends entirely.
World-State After: chaotic liberation; the living/dead boundary destabilizes; possible catastrophe (the freed dead may not pass cleanly); a destabilized but honest world; the Quiet Country's fate uncertain.
NPC Fates: **Cole Ashby** triumphant/horrified; **Veyl** destroyed; the player a liberator or a catastrophe-bringer.
Faction Fates: Gravecallers triumphant/horrified; Wardens purposeless; Mourners freed but uneasy; Hollow Court destroyed.

## Ending: TRANSFORM IT (Outcome E — the synthesis; hardest, best, not guaranteed)
Requirements: **REV_010 confirmed; Maire turned**; the **Last Voice** or the **Quiet Country Vessel**; a coalition; the **Sunmark** model (Sael Sunmark) as a working contrast.
Key Revelation Required: REV_007 + REV_008 + REV_010 + REV_009 (the cosmic stakes).
Final Site: the Under-Shrine (D23) — the consensual rite anchored there.
Final Boss / Obstacle: **Veyl** (out-argued/out-maneuvered, not just fought); **Orre** (stood down or bypassed via Maire); the fragility of the coalition.
Resolution: invent/enable a consensual system where the dead choose whether to be remembered; the Court ends itself.
World-State After: the only outcome that addresses the thinning without consuming the dead; a new, fragile, just settlement between the living and the dead; the "best" ending — earned, never handed.
NPC Fates: **Maire** redeemed (defects, may share the cost); **Veyl** ends himself or is persuaded; **Orre** stands down or falls; **Sael Sunmark**/the Last Voice central to the new rite.
Faction Fates: Wardens re-founded around consent; Mourners central to the new rite; Gravecallers partners in the safe-break; Remnant reformed or broken; Hollow Court ends itself.

## Endgame Approaches → Endings (the nine approaches reach these)

Per `ENDGAME_STRUCTURE.md` Part 3 and `TIER_4_PLAYABLE_PATHS_LEVELS_17_20.md`:

| Approach (playbook) | Reaches |
|---|---|
| 1 Direct Confrontation | Seal, Restart (yield), Seize, Destroy |
| 2 Alliance-Building | Seal, Transform, Seize |
| 3 Artifact Strategy | any |
| 4 Ritual/Counter-Ritual | Seal, Restart, Destroy (safe), Transform |
| 5 Political/Faction Victory | Seal (denial), Seize |
| 6 Mystery-Resolution/Exposure | Seize (people), Transform |
| 7 Sacrifice/Containment | Seal, Transform (partial) |
| 8 Partial/Bittersweet | any (degraded) |
| 9 Failure/Aftermath | Restart (default), Destroy (cult), Seize (rival) |

> **Only Transform (E) is locked** behind REV_010 + Maire + allies/artifact. Every other outcome is reachable by multiple approaches — open-world agency preserved. No outcome is "correct."
