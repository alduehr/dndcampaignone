## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY — the inter-region movement kit.** When the player wants to leave a region (or asks "where can I go?"), this gives the concrete route, level band, travel time, a named hook that motivates the move, a named complication en route, and a clue that can surface during travel. Use with the region arc packs (`REGIONAL_ARC_PACKS_LEVEL_5_TO_20.md`) for what waits at the destination, and `PLAYER_GOES_ANYWHERE_GUIDE.md` for early/late arrival handling. **Travel is decisions, not filler** (per `WORLDBUILDING_STANDARDS.md`). **Far-continent travel never delivers REV_007; the apex is vertical under Hollowmere.**

Travel-time estimates assume foot/cart pace unless a water/mount route is noted; halve for river/sea passage with a banked transport ally.

## Ring 1 Internal Transitions (the home cluster)

### Sundering Reach → Caradril
Level Range: 5–12 (the mid-game move)
Route Type: river (the Verdance) / road (East Road via Tollwood) / sea (Wenna Roke's water-route via the Verdance Mouth)
Travel Time: ~10–12 days overland; ~6–8 days by Wenna Roke's boat
Common Hook: follow the salvage trade Domic Sael ships south, or chase Reke's Caradril correspondent thread (REV_004 lead via the Bellman).
Complication: the East Road runs through Tollwood, where Skell's Tollmen extort or ambush travelers (T2); the river passage risks Ledger toll-farmers (Quenna Bly).
Clue Possible En Route: a Ledger caravan's manifest naming "Reach water-rights works" (C_CAR_008, REV_003 corroboration).

### Sundering Reach ↔ Ashgarden Vale
Level Range: 5–8
Route Type: road (the South Road, a Concord causeway)
Travel Time: ~3–4 days
Common Hook: Old Mam Tace's honest M5 window (the failures radiate from the Reach), or Mother Ezrith Combe's call through the Mourner network.
Complication: the Drowned Lane ford floods (a Concord causeway sinking as the basin shifts); gibbet-crossroads bandits at Marrow Cross.
Clue Possible En Route: grave-soil drifting waterward even on the road (REV_001 corroboration).

### Sundering Reach ↔ Tollwood
Level Range: 5–8 (Old Mast deep L11+)
Route Type: road (the East Road, a Concord toll-causeway)
Travel Time: ~3–4 days to Hartfell
Common Hook: Goodwife Sennet's warning that the deep wood "goes wrong," or the East Road must be kept open to reach Caradril.
Complication: the Tollmen's toll-shakedown at Tollstone Cross (Cady Renn turnable, Skell murderous, T2); deep-wood Mast-Beasts off the road.
Clue Possible En Route: the grey-lords folk-memory at the Coppice Shrines (oblique pre-Concord M5/M6).

### Sundering Reach ↔ Pale Coast
Level Range: 5–9
Route Type: road (the Pale Road via Saltmargin) / sea (coastal passage)
Travel Time: ~4–5 days to Wrackmouth
Common Hook: Salt-Mother Bryd's failing salt-and-tide rite, or the Drowned Flame (Thin-touch immunity) at the Drowned Lamp.
Complication: the drowned-tide makes the coast road treacherous; wreckers (Tomas Quint) prey on the shore near the Lantern Stacks.
Clue Possible En Route: drowned-cairns on the tide-line giving an honest M5 maritime window (REV_001/005 fragment).

### Caradril ↔ Pale Coast (water-route)
Level Range: 5–10
Route Type: sea/river (Wenna Roke via the Verdance Mouth)
Travel Time: ~5–6 days by boat
Common Hook: the sea-relic trade (Maris Cole) feeds the Caradril Ashmarket; follow it either direction.
Complication: privateer/wrecker interception; storms off the Verdance Mouth (Mabon Crale's tide-reading mitigates).
Clue Possible En Route: a sea-relic cargo that "remembers" the dead (REV_002 maritime proof).

### Caradril ↔ Tollwood / Vale (the city's feeders)
Level Range: 5–10
Route Type: road (East Road from Tollwood; South Road from the Vale)
Travel Time: ~5–7 days
Common Hook: the saint's-bones trade (Wymar Pell) and the timber/relic trade (Hesk Dorr) both flow to Caradril.
Complication: Tollmen on the East Road; flooded causeways on the South Road.
Clue Possible En Route: a "saint's-bones" shipment proving the relics hold the dead (REV_002).

## Ring 1 → Ring 2 (opening the wider world)

### Caradril → Verdance Reaches
Level Range: 8–12 (the corridor entry)
Route Type: road/river (the Verdance Road up the Verdance)
Travel Time: ~6–8 days to Marrowfen Stair
Common Hook: Factor-General Wessel Crane's inland Ledger pipeline (break/expose/exploit it), or Mossa Drenn's offer of honest barge passage upriver.
Complication: Lord Ennis Marrow's toll at the Nine Locks; Ledger pipeline-guards; the Nine Locks relay-vault (D34) stirs.
Clue Possible En Route: the Nine Locks Sunken Stair "points home" — the harvest network was continental (M2/M5 fragment).

### Sundering Reach → Verdance Reaches (direct, bypassing Caradril)
Level Range: 8–12
Route Type: river (the Verdance, joining above Caradril) / road
Travel Time: ~12–14 days (longer without the Caradril stop)
Common Hook: a player who skips Caradril can still reach the corridor up the Verdance; the Nine Locks node pulls them on.
Complication: no Caradril resupply (see `PLAYER_GOES_ANYWHERE_GUIDE.md` §Skips Caradril); river hazards.
Clue Possible En Route: the same Nine Locks fragment (the network is continental).

## Ring 2 → Far Continent (the deep corridor)

### Verdance Reaches → Glassmere League
Level Range: 9–13
Route Type: road/river (the Verdance Road continues SE to the Glasswater)
Travel Time: ~8–10 days to Glassmere city
Common Hook: League-Factor Oren Glass's relic-contracts on the Floor, or Sefwy Holt's hire to dig dirt on the Ledger.
Complication: Wessel Crane's pipeline-guards follow; the Glass Ear's rivals; river-city customs.
Clue Possible En Route: continental relic-ledgers (REV_002 at scale).

### Verdance Reaches → Concord Heartlands
Level Range: 11–14 (L13+ for the apex-adjacent depth)
Route Type: road (branching off the Verdance Road toward the Greatspine passes)
Travel Time: ~12–15 days to Crownmouth
Common Hook: Crown-Reclaimer Hollin Vane's expedition recruits relic-finders, or Lyssa Crownmouth needs the Reclaimers checked.
Complication: the Greatspine passes (high hazard); Reclaimer expedition-guards; Pilgrim Camp lawlessness.
Clue Possible En Route: imperial-scale Concord ruins corroborating REV_005/006 (never the keystone).

### Glassmere League → Marrowdowns
Level Range: 9–13 (gentler; playable from L6)
Route Type: road (south from Glassmere into the chalk downs)
Travel Time: ~6–8 days to Marrowmoot
Common Hook: Shire-Reeve Aldous Penmark's bounty on barrow-robbers, or Senna Crale's old-songs call ("the downs remember an older drowning").
Complication: barrow-robbers (Doss Wether's rivals); downs weather; manorial toll-disputes.
Clue Possible En Route: pre-Concord barrow glyphs (the "older fall"; REV_001/005 echo).

### Glassmere League → Hethewald Free Holds
Level Range: 7–12
Route Type: river (down the Hethe) / road (east into the great forest)
Travel Time: ~7–9 days to Hethemoot
Common Hook: Hold-Speaker Bram Hethe's confederacy as an ally/refuge, or Onn Greenward's old-songs grove.
Complication: "Greenfinger" Maddoc's outlaw river-tolls on the Hethe; deep-wood predators; the forbidden Old Holds.
Clue Possible En Route: deep-wood Concord ruins (the Old Holds; REV_005 oblique).

### Concord Heartlands → Emberfell Theocracy
Level Range: 12–16
Route Type: road (south through the Greatspine into the volcanic highlands)
Travel Time: ~8–10 days to Ashfast
Common Hook: Ember-Sage Mira Cindra's smuggled plea, or Doss Ashfast's "old glass" fortune at Cinderhold.
Complication: very high volcanic hazard (lava, ash); Pyre-Warden patrols (Sef Embren); the Caldera's heat.
Clue Possible En Route: "old glass" relic-sources (REV_002 echo).

### Emberfell → Cindern Waste
Level Range: 13–16
Route Type: road/trail (SE into the ash badland)
Travel Time: ~4–6 days (a hostile crossing)
Common Hook: the Cindern Waste Buried Works (D36) hold an ash-drowned Concord works (REV_005/006 fragment); relic-buyers race for them.
Complication: ash storms, no water, the Forbidden Waste's hazards (DC 15+ Constitution saves); ash-bound dead.
Clue Possible En Route: ash-preserved Concord Script (REV_005/006 fragment).

### Hollow Gulf Ports → Saltmere Reaches
Level Range: 11–15
Route Type: road/river (N up from the Gulf into the salt-sea country)
Travel Time: ~7–9 days to Brackhold
Common Hook: Salt-Clan Matriarch Bryd Saltmere's plea as the realm fractures, or Drowned-Town Delver Oss's salvage offer.
Complication: salt-flats and receding-lake hazards; raiders; Marek Bonepan's caravan routes contested.
Clue Possible En Route: surfacing pre-Concord ruins (the "older fall"; REV_001/005 echo).

### Hollow Gulf Ports → Sallowmarch Protectorate
Level Range: 10–14
Route Type: sea/road (W along the coast into the fever delta)
Travel Time: ~5–7 days to Fenward/Reedmouth
Common Hook: Marsh-Healer Doll Fenn (the delta's true leader) needs help against fever and the absent crown, or "Heron" Maddox's smuggling routes.
Complication: fever (DC 13+ Constitution saves); the Fever Channels' aberrations; crocodilians; the absent crown's neglect.
Clue Possible En Route: the pre-Concord Drowned Steps causeway (REV_001/005 echo; deep aberrations).

### Karran Marches ↔ Wender Steppe
Level Range: 8–14
Route Type: road/trail (W from the marches into the grass-sea)
Travel Time: ~6–8 days to Cold Springs
Common Hook: Clan-Speaker Tamur Wend-Khar's guest-right, or Wind-Singer Esha's "thin Sky" warning (REV_009 oblique).
Complication: Borr of the Black Horse's reavers; "Iron" Brask's marcher tolls; open-steppe weather.
Clue Possible En Route: the Sky-Stones' cosmic echo (REV_009 oblique — never the Quiet Country).

### Wender Steppe → Highmark Passes
Level Range: 12–16
Route Type: trail (N into the frozen passes, often via Karran)
Travel Time: ~8–10 days (a cold, dangerous crossing)
Common Hook: the Highmark Frozen Works (D35) hold the clearest cold-preserved REV_005/006 proof; a Remnant expedition races for them.
Complication: cold/ice/altitude (DC 15+ Constitution saves); frozen dead; isolated holds wary of outsiders; pass-closures.
Clue Possible En Route: cold-preserved Concord records (REV_005/006).

### Highmark Passes → Karran Marches
Level Range: 12–16
Route Type: trail (S back down out of the passes)
Travel Time: ~8–10 days
Common Hook: a turned Wenna Stone (reform-Remnant ally) waits at the Old Iron forts; ore-trade with Mully Karr.
Complication: the same cold-pass hazards reversed; "Iron" Brask's hold blocks the easy route.
Clue Possible En Route: "humming iron" relic-source at the Old Iron forts (REV_002/005 fragment).

### Sunmark ↔ Hollow Gulf Ports
Level Range: 8–15
Route Type: road (the Green Roads) / coast
Travel Time: ~5–7 days
Common Hook: Grove-Keeper Sael Sunmark's consensual grove-faith (the synthesis model; REV_008/010 seed), or Doss Sunward's plains-trade.
Complication: outsiders pressuring the groves; coastal privateers near the Gulf.
Clue Possible En Route: a hopeful sign from Sun-Singer Doll (the dead can be held without consuming them; REV_008/010 seed).

## Endgame Transition (Tier 4 only)

### Any Region → The Under-Shrine Approach (the descent)
Level Range: 17–20 (surface D20 reachable Tier 2–3, lethal-telegraphed)
Route Type: vertical — return to Hollowmere, breach the basin, descend D20 → D23
Travel Time: the descent itself is a dungeon (D20→D23), not overland travel; getting *to* Hollowmere uses the routes above
Common Hook: REV_007 owned (the Court lives beneath Hollowmere); the master clock forcing the endgame; rival claimants racing to the keystone.
Complication: the basin's lethal environment (drowning, cold, dread); Concord guardians at full power; rival claimants; Reke the surface link. **DM: telegraph lethality at every prior level; gate before REV_007.**
Clue Possible En Route: this is the **only** place REV_007 is physically confirmed (DM-only; the Court, the harvest mechanism, the endgame choice). **Never narrate before the gate.**

> **The endgame is vertical, under Hollowmere — never a far region.** Every far journey loops back here. See `TIER_4_PLAYABLE_PATHS_LEVELS_17_20.md` and `../10_dungeons_and_ruins/THE_UNDER_SHRINE_APPROACH.md` (D23, DM-only).

## "Where Can I Go Next?" — Router by Tier

When the player asks with no context, offer by their level band:

### Tier 1 (Levels 1–4)
Stay in the **Sundering Reach** (home). Offer: deeper into Hollowmere/the Greyfens, or short trips to Kettle Bridge, Saltmargin, Candlewick, Harrowgast. The wider world is *rumored*, not yet open.

### Tier 2 (Levels 5–10)
Offer: **Ring 1** (Vale S / Tollwood E / Coast W — honest M5 windows, faction chains), then **Caradril** (SE, the mid-game hub, REV_005/006 via the Archive), then the **Verdance corridor** (L8+, the door to the far continent). Gentle far regions (Marrowdowns L6+, Sunmark/Wender L8+) are reachable on the high end.

### Tier 3 (Levels 11–16)
Offer: the **far continent** via the player's chosen route — Glassmere (intrigue/banks), Concord Heartlands (apex-adjacent imperial proof, L13+), Emberfell/Highmark (lethal far proof), Saltmere/Marrowdowns/Sallowmarch (older-fall echoes), Wender/Sunmark (cosmic echo / synthesis model). **None is required** — the apex is vertical under Hollowmere. Pull the player home as the convergence completes.

### Tier 4 (Levels 17–20)
Offer: the **vertical descent** under Hollowmere (D20→D23), plus optional means-gathering excursions (the Ash-Crown in the Heartlands, an artifact at a far node, the Sunmark synthesis model). The endgame resolves at the keystone. See `TIER_4_PLAYABLE_PATHS_LEVELS_17_20.md`.

> **Off-route arrivals** (too early/too late, skipping a region) are handled in `PLAYER_GOES_ANYWHERE_GUIDE.md`. **Ignored main arc** consequences are in `PLAYER_IGNORES_MAIN_ARC_GUIDE.md` and `CONTINENTAL_PRESSURE_TIMELINE.md`.
