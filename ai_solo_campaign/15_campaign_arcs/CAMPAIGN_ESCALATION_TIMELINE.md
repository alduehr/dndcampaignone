## AI Use

**DM-ONLY.** The chronological escalation of the campaign's clocks and world-state across levels 5–20. Use this to know, at any point, *how far the world has moved* and what advances next — independent of which threads the player chases. It is a **default trajectory for a moderately-engaged player**; a fast player outpaces it, a passive player is pushed along it by the clocks. Never narrate clock causes (the harvest) to the player. Clock definitions live in `../02_runtime_state/WORLD_CLOCKS.md`; this file shows their *tier-by-tier* movement together.

> **Time is not the only driver.** Clocks advance on time, on player inaction toward a threat, and on specific triggers (votes, breaches, deals). Phase is gated by *revelation progress*, not strictly by level (`../11_mysteries_and_secrets/REVELATION_MAP.md`). This timeline is the scaffolding, not a script.

## Tier 2 (Levels 5–10) — The Conspiracy Hardens

**Headline world-state:** the relic trade swells; the basin reopening advances; the factions go regional; the steering resolves.

- **Early Tier 2 (L5–6):** Factions begin their Stage 8 chains in earnest. The Basin clock advances toward Stage 2 (debate → work begins). Caradril opens; C1/C2/C3 leave Stage 1. The Monopoly and Reclamation clocks creep toward Stage 2.
- **Mid Tier 2 (L7–8):** A Hollow Court *agent* is exposed (Reke or a cutout) — REV_003. The player is now a target; crossed factions retaliate. The Steering clock resolves into REV_004. The Vyre–Quorrin deal (C1) nears terms. Possible first travel beyond the Reach.
- **Late Tier 2 (L9–10):** The Basin reaches Stage 3 (upper shrine surfaces) on a passive or losing basin track, pushing the master clock to Stage 2. The Sealed Archive becomes act-available (L9–12); a hard-pushing player confirms REV_005 (the Concord harvested). The Verdance corridor opens (L8+).

**Key triggers this tier:** the basin vote (`QC4`); the Vyre–Quorrin deal closing (C1); the exposure of Reke (REV_003); pressing Sefra (REV_004); reaching the Sealed Archive (REV_005 begins).

**If ignored:** the vote goes Reke's way; the deal closes; the upper shrine surfaces on its own; the factions' Q*4 postures resolve in their default (worst-for-player) directions. See `FAILURE_STATES_AND_WORLD_CONSEQUENCES.md`.

## Tier 3 (Levels 11–16) — The Truth Detonates and the War Goes Continental

**Headline world-state:** the historical truth lands; the faction war goes continental; the basin nears breach; the apex gate opens at the top.

- **Early Tier 3 (L11–12):** REV_005/006 confirmed (the harvest; the deliberate Quietfall). The Wardens clock reaches Stage 3 (Othetha breaks her oath). REV_009 (finite afterlife) lands. The Reclamation clock peaks toward Stage 3 (agents in the Reach; race for the keystone). The Heartlands and far regions open.
- **Mid Tier 3 (L13–14):** The **apex gate opens.** Convergence of REV_003+005+006, or a lethal descent, or Maire's contact, can confirm REV_007 (the Court lives). The Basin reaches Stage 4 (breachable); the master clock reaches Stage 3 (the Court manifests to those who enter; first deliberate local harvest). The Gravecaller Breaking clock nears a breach attempt (Stage 3).
- **Late Tier 3 (L15–16):** Veyl met; the Court's case made. REV_010 (Maire) reachable. The Monopoly clock reaches Stage 4 (immortality sold) if the Ledger won; the Thinning clock reaches a regional pattern (Stage 3). Rival claimants close on the keystone. The endgame choice (REV_008) comes into view.

**Key triggers this tier:** confirming REV_005/006; the apex convergence (REV_007); the basin breaching; a Gravecaller breach attempt; the Reclamation reaching the keystone path.

**If ignored:** the surfacing/breached shrine and the faction war deliver REV_005/006 anyway; the master clock reaches Stage 4 (harvest at scale) on continued inaction; the player arrives at the endgame under-informed (no REV_010 → no synthesis).

## Tier 4 (Levels 17–20) — The Keystone and the Choice

**Headline world-state:** every clock converges on the keystone; the decision is enacted; the world-state resolves into an epilogue.

- **Early Tier 4 (L17–18):** The player descends (D20 → D23). The master clock is at Stage 3–4; the basin is breachable or breached. Rival claimants race; their Tier 2–3 postures determine whether they help or hinder. Veyl bargains/recruits.
- **Mid Tier 4 (L18–19):** The penultimate confrontation (Veyl, or a rival claimant, or Orre). The synthesis becomes live if REV_010 + Maire's turn + the right allies/artifacts are in hand. The player gathers the final means (artifact, coalition, ritual, sabotage).
- **Climax (L19–20):** The decision is enacted. The master clock (#1) resolves — sealed, restarted, seized, destroyed, or transformed. The Thinning clock's fate is decided. All faction clocks resolve into epilogue outcomes (`ENDGAME_STRUCTURE.md` → faction-outcome matrix).

**Key triggers this tier:** reaching the keystone; turning (or failing to turn) Maire; the rival-claimant race; the enacted decision.

**If ignored / failed:** doing nothing completes the master clock — the harvest restarts under the Court's rule (worst default). Losing the keystone race hands the ending to a rival. A solo Gravecaller breach shatters the keystone catastrophically. All are *playable, bleak epilogues*, never a "game over."

## Cross-Tier Clock Interaction Rules (DM)

1. **#2 Basin drives #1 Master.** Each time the Basin clock advances a stage, check whether the master clock should follow. The basin is the player's single most important lever on the harvest.
2. **#6 Monopoly + #7 Reclamation feed each other via C1.** The Caradril deal (C1) is the hinge; closing it accelerates both. Exposing/shattering the deal slows both.
3. **#3 Wardens and #4/#5 Mourners/Gravecallers are the *counter-clocks*.** A player who strengthens the Wardens/Mourners slows #1 (keep it sealed); a player who arms the Gravecallers risks a catastrophic ending but can *also* be steered toward the synthesis.
4. **#10 Thinning is the moral pressure.** It cannot be directly stopped except by resolving #1. Its visible rise makes the Court's argument more tempting each tier — by design.
5. **Ring 1 and far clocks are regional feeders** — they corroborate and pressure the master clocks but never replace them; far events never advance the apex (REV_007) out of phase.

## Passive-Player Failsafe (DM)

If the player chases *no* main-arc thread, run the default trajectory above at roughly **one master-clock stage per tier**, delivering REV_002/003 (Tier 2), REV_005/006 (Tier 3), and — at the apex gate — REV_007 (late Tier 3 / Tier 4) through the *world acting on them*: the surfacing shrine, faction agents arriving, the harvest reaching their region. The player always reaches a choice; passivity only costs them allies, options, and the synthesis. See `FAILURE_STATES_AND_WORLD_CONSEQUENCES.md`.
