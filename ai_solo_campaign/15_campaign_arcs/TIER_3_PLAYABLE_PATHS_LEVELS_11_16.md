## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY — six concrete, named, step-by-step paths through Tier 3 (levels 11–16).** Each path is a runnable spine the AI DM can follow without inventing structure. They are **not exclusive or mandatory** — the player can mix them, switch, or follow none (the world clocks deliver the truth regardless). All six confirm the tier's historical reveals (REV_005/006/009) by different routes; the **apex gate (REV_007/REV_010) opens at L13+** and is reached only via convergence, the lethal-telegraphed descent under Hollowmere, or heretic contact — **never** through a far-continent source. **Before L13 the Court is a legend; far sources NEVER name it.**

The six paths are organized by far region but each loops the player back toward the vertical endgame under Hollowmere.

## Path 1: The Glassmere Path — "Trade, Banks, and Mirror Politics"

Entry Level: 11–12
Entry Region: Glassmere League (down the Verdance corridor from Caradril)
Entry Hook: **League-Factor Oren Glass** invites the player to the Floor (the merchant-exchange) to broker continental relic-contracts — or **Banker-Magistra Sefwy Holt** quietly hires the player to find dirt on the Ledger's continental arm.

### Levels 11–12: The Floor and the Counting-Deep
The Cinder Ledger's true heartland runs on relic-money. **Oren Glass** and (arriving from the Verdance) **Factor-General Wessel Crane** are assembling the continental Monopoly; **Sefwy Holt** is a rival banker who can be turned against them. The player can break a relic-contract on the Floor, or run the **Three Bridges Counting-Deep** (D29) — a Ledger bank-vault heist that recovers ledgers proving the dead are being trafficked continent-wide (REV_002 confirmed at continental scale). *Changes:* feeds Clock #6 (Monopoly); turning Holt splits the Ledger; Wessel Crane marks the player as a threat to the pipeline. **Spy-broker "the Glass Ear"** sells leads (costly).

### Levels 13–14: The Reliquary and the Mirror of Caradril
Glassmere is Caradril's larger, colder reflection — the same factions, higher stakes. **Reliquary-Lector Mareth Senn** heads the Concord Remnant's grand chapter at the Reliquary and hoards the far Concord records; the player infiltrates the **Glassmere Reliquary Vaults** (D28) for a far surface-fragment of REV_005 (the Concord harvested). In **Lowwater**, Mourner eldest **Wenna Lowwater** keeps an urban old-songs node that corroborates REV_005 as folk-memory. Politically, the player plays Oren Glass, Mareth Senn, and Sefwy Holt against one another — the same triangle as Vyre/Quorrin/Tolm in Caradril, now continental. *Changes:* REV_005 corroborated from the far continent (never the origin/Court); the player can fracture either the Ledger (via Holt) or the Remnant (by exposing Mareth Senn's hoarding to idealists).

### Levels 15–16: Toward the Heartlands Race (transition to Tier 4)
The Reliquary records and the relic-money trail both point at the Concord's fallen capital. **Mareth Senn** dispatches a chapter-expedition to back **Crown-Reclaimer Magister Hollin Vane** at the Ruin'd Crown (Path 6); **Oren Glass** finances whoever wins the keystone race. The player leaves Glassmere with banking leverage, a possible Ledger-split (Holt), and the knowledge that the relic-money runs *home* — to a buried network whose hub is not in Glassmere at all. *Changes:* the player is now a continental power; the Monopoly (#6) and Reclamation (#7) clocks are entwined and racing.

### Key NPCs (from codex — no new ones)
Oren Glass (relic-broker; the Floor), Sefwy Holt (rival banker; turnable Ledger-splitter), Mareth Senn (Remnant grand-chapter head; hoards records), Wenna Lowwater (old-songs node; REV_005 folk-fragment), "the Glass Ear" (information-broker), Wessel Crane (Ledger pipeline-builder, from the Verdance).

### Active Factions Here
- **Cinder Ledger:** Oren Glass + Wessel Crane build the continental Monopoly; the heist and Holt's defection are the levers against it.
- **Concord Remnant:** Mareth Senn's grand chapter races for records and backs the Heartlands dig.
- **Mourners' Circle:** Wenna Lowwater's Lowwater quarter — a banked far seal/synthesis ally.
- *(DM: the relic-money corroborates the harvest's commercial reality; never names the Court.)*

### Dungeons/Sites
- **D29 Three Bridges Counting-Deep** (L9–12) — Ledger bank-vault heist; REV_002 continental proof.
- **D28 Glassmere Reliquary Vaults** (L9–13) — Remnant chapter-vault; REV_005 far fragment; social/archive infiltration.

### Clues/Revelations
- **REV_002** confirmed continental (the Counting-Deep ledgers; the Floor's contracts).
- **REV_005** far surface-fragment (the Reliquary Vaults; Wenna Lowwater's songs) — **never the origin, never the Court.**

### Rewards/Artifacts
High-tier relics from the Vaults; banking leverage; **Sefwy Holt** as a banked Ledger-splitting ally; far Concord records that strengthen the Sealed Archive's REV_005/006 case.

### Encounter Profile
L11–16. Mostly heist/intrigue: Ledger enforcers, Remnant chapter-guards, vault construct-guardians (Concord Construct-Guardian, CR 7–11), urban assassins/spies (the Glass Ear's rivals). Combat survivable and telegraphed; noncombat solutions everywhere.

### Milestone Guidance
Level 11: arriving in Glassmere; reading the Floor.
Level 12: the Counting-Deep heist (REV_002 continental).
Level 13: the Reliquary infiltration (REV_005 far fragment).
Level 14: turning Holt or splitting the Remnant's chapter.
Level 15: the Heartlands race opens; banking leverage banked.
Level 16: leaving for the Crown or home, a continental power.

### Failure State
A botched heist makes the Ledger a continental enemy (Oren Glass sets bounty-prices on the Floor) and costs a REV_002 proof — never the apex. If the player ignores Glassmere entirely, the Ledger consolidates the Monopoly (#6→3–4; immortality begins to be sold) and Mareth Senn's chapter backs Hollin Vane unopposed.

### If Player Ignores
**Oren Glass** corners the continental relic-trade; **Wessel Crane** completes the inland pipeline; **Mareth Senn** ships the Reliquary's records to the Heartlands dig, giving the Remnant uncontested far legitimacy. Clock #6→Stage 3–4; #7 accelerates. The player reaches the endgame facing a richer, better-organized Ledger and Remnant.

### Transition to Tier 4
The relic-money "points home." Holt's defection, the Reliquary records, and the Floor's contracts all corroborate that the harvest network is continental — but the hub is under Hollowmere. The player's banking leverage and Ledger-split become endgame levers (Seize/Exposure approaches in `ENDGAME_STRUCTURE.md`).

### Solo-Play Notes
Glassmere is an intrigue/heist hub — every objective has a noncombat route; the Glass Ear sells shortcuts; Holt and Wenna Lowwater are noncombat allies. No REV here requires winning a fight.

## Path 2: The Marrowdowns Path — "Ancestors, Barrows, and Pre-Concord Memory"

Entry Level: 11–12 (playable as low as L6 on the gentler end)
Entry Region: Marrowdowns (mid-continent chalk downs, reachable via the Verdance/Glassmere or from Hethewald)
Entry Hook: **Shire-Reeve Aldous Penmark** posts a bounty on barrow-robbers stripping the Barrow-Fields, or **Moot-Mother Senna Crale** sends word through the Mourner old-songs network that "the downs remember an older drowning."

### Levels 11–12: The Shire-Moot and the Robbers
The downs are pastoral, lawful, and old. **Aldous Penmark** keeps order from Penmark Hold but cannot stop **Barrow-Master Doss Wether** and outside relic-buyers from looting the pre-Concord Barrow-Fields. The player can deputize under Penmark (with **Horse-Reeve Edony Marrow**'s mounted patrols), or hire Wether as a barrow-guide — Wether knows the safe ways into the burials but sells to relic-buyers. *Changes:* who controls the Barrow-Fields; banks Penmark's law or Wether's guidance.

### Levels 13–14: The Barrow Complex and the Older Fall
The player descends the **Marrowdowns Barrow Complex** (D26) — pre-Concord burials that predate the harvest network and "rhyme" with the Reach's Barrow of Nine Doors. The dead here were laid to rest by a *different, older* custom; the glyphs and grave-goods testify that **the world has drowned its dead before the Concord ever existed** (REV_001/005 echo — a pre-Concord "older fall"). **Senna Crale**'s fully decoded old songs at Marrowmoot corroborate REV_005 as folk-memory: "the grey ones took the dead before the roads were laid." *Changes:* REV_005 corroborated and *deepened* — the harvest was not the first time the dead were taken, which feeds the endgame destroy/transform debate (was the Concord's sin unique, or a pattern?).

### Levels 15–16: The Downs as a Synthesis Witness (transition)
Senna Crale's grief-keeping and the consensual ancestor-rites of the downs are a *second hopeful model* alongside Sunmark — the downs hold their ancestors by memory and consent, not consumption. The player can bank **Senna Crale** as a far Mourner/synthesis ally and carry the "older fall" lore home. *Changes:* deepens the moral frame of REV_008 (the synthesis has precedent); Senna Crale joins the old-songs ally network for the endgame.

### Key NPCs (from codex — no new ones)
Aldous Penmark (shire-reeve; downs law), Senna Crale (Moot-Mother; old-songs node; banked synthesis-adjacent ally), Doss Wether (barrow-guide; sells to relic-buyers; turnable), Edony Marrow (horse-reeve; mounted patrols).

### Active Factions Here
- **Mourners' Circle:** Senna Crale's downs old-songs node — REV_005 folk-fragment; a banked seal/synthesis ally.
- **Manorial holds:** Penmark's law vs. barrow-robbers.
- **Cinder Ledger / Concord Remnant:** relic-buyers funding the barrow-looting (the harvest trade reaching even the downs).
- *(DM: a pre-Concord barrow echo — "older fall"; never the keystone.)*

### Dungeons/Sites
- **D26 Marrowdowns Barrow Complex** (L6–10) — pre-Concord barrows; REV_001/005 echo; gentler far site, good for lower-Tier-3 play.

### Clues/Revelations
- **REV_001/005 echo** (the older fall) — the Barrow Complex glyphs + Senna Crale's decoded songs.
- A **REV_008 deepening** (the synthesis has historical precedent in the downs' consensual ancestor-rites).

### Rewards/Artifacts
Barrow-relics; a downs ally network; **Senna Crale** as a banked old-songs/synthesis ally; "older fall" lore that strengthens the Destroy/Transform endgame debate.

### Encounter Profile
L6–14 (a gentler far region; scale up the deeper barrows). Threats: barrow-wights, barrow-shades, robbers, downs predators. Doss Wether's guidance makes the fields navigable; retreat routes throughout.

### Milestone Guidance
Level 11: the shire-moot; the robber problem.
Level 12: choosing Penmark's law or Wether's guidance.
Level 13: the Barrow Complex descent (older-fall echo).
Level 14: REV_005 corroborated via Senna Crale's songs.
Level 15: banking Senna Crale; the synthesis-precedent insight.
Level 16: carrying the "older fall" lore toward the endgame.

### Failure State
If the barrows are stripped, the older-fall lore is lost to relic-buyers and the player loses a REV_005 corroboration and a synthesis-precedent — never the apex. A failed barrow-dive costs a clue and a wight-fight, never the run.

### If Player Ignores
**Doss Wether** and outside relic-buyers strip the Barrow-Fields for the Ledger/Remnant trade; **Aldous Penmark**'s law is overwhelmed and the downs turn inward and wary of outsiders; **Senna Crale** stops trusting the player, removing a far synthesis ally.

### Transition to Tier 4
The "older fall" lore reframes the endgame choice: the Concord's harvest was a pattern, not a one-time sin, which makes the Transform ending's consensual model (Sunmark + the downs' ancestor-rites) more compelling. Senna Crale joins the coalition for an Alliance or Transform approach.

### Solo-Play Notes
A gentle far region with a guide (Wether) and mounted allies (Edony Marrow); the barrows are telegraphed and retreat-rich. Senna Crale is a noncombat ally; no REV requires a fight.

## Path 3: The Emberfell Path — "Fire, Ash, and the Mirror Theocracy"

Entry Level: 12–13
Entry Region: Emberfell Theocracy / the Ashfast (volcanic highlands, SE-central)
Entry Hook: **Ember-Sage Mira Cindra** smuggles a plea to the player for protection from the orthodoxy, or **Cinder-Master Doss Ashfast** offers a fortune in "old glass" from Cinderhold's mines.

### Levels 12–13: The Heretic-Sage and the Old Glass
Emberfell reads its volcanoes as divine and mines "old glass" (raw Remembrance relics; REV_002 echo) from buried Concord works. **Ash-Hierophant Vole Cindra** rules from Ashfast; enforcer **Pyre-Warden Sef Embren** burns dissent; heretic-scholar **Mira Cindra** secretly questions the faith. The player can break or exploit **Doss Ashfast**'s "old glass" trade at Cinderhold (proving the substance exists continent-wide, REV_002), and/or shield Mira Cindra from Sef Embren. *Changes:* feeds REV_002 corroboration; banks Mira Cindra as a turnable scholar-ally; marks the player to the Pyre-Wardens.

### Levels 13–14: The Caldera Descent
The player descends the **Emberfell Caldera Descent / Ashfast Deep** (D22) — a volcanic Concord ruin of *very high* hazard (lava, ash, the Caldera Guardian elemental). Its records confirm the substance (REV_002) and reveal that Emberfell's fire-faith is a **thematic mirror** of the harvest conspiracy: the theocracy *also* tends and "keeps" its dead, but through fire and consent rather than secret consumption. *Changes:* REV_002 deepened; a thematic REV_008/010 contrast (a different, public way of holding the dead). **Requires the Drowned Flame** (Thin-touch immunity) and fire protection.

### Levels 15–16: The Theocracy's Choice (transition)
Mira Cindra's heresy and the Caldera records together make Emberfell a *mirror* the player can hold up to the Hollow Court: here is a faith that holds its dead openly, with cost and consent, and it does not consume them. The player can spark a reform (Mira vs. Vole Cindra) or simply carry the model home. *Changes:* banks a thematic model for the synthesis debate; the player may have a reformed theocracy as a distant ally.

### Key NPCs (from codex — no new ones)
Vole Cindra (Ash-Hierophant; ruler), Sef Embren (Pyre-Warden; enforcer), Mira Cindra (Ember-Sage; heretic-scholar; turnable ally), Doss Ashfast (Cinder-Master; "old glass" mining-lord).

### Active Factions Here
- **Emberfell Theocracy:** Vole Cindra + Sef Embren enforce orthodoxy and guard the old works.
- **Heretics:** Mira Cindra's faction — a turnable lever and a thematic synthesis-contrast.
- **Cinder Ledger:** relic-buyers competing for the "old glass" supply.
- *(DM: a thematic mirror, NOT the conspiracy; never a second keystone, never the Court.)*

### Dungeons/Sites
- **D22 Emberfell Caldera Descent / Ashfast Deep** (L12–16) — volcanic Concord ruin; REV_002 echo; **very high** environmental hazard.

### Clues/Revelations
- **REV_002** echo ("old glass"; the Caldera records).
- A **thematic REV_008/010 contrast** (a fire-faith that holds its dead openly and consensually). **Never REV_007; the fire-cult is NOT the conspiracy.**

### Rewards/Artifacts
Fire-tempered relics; "old glass" supply leverage; **Mira Cindra** as a turnable scholar-ally; a thematic synthesis model.

### Encounter Profile
L12–16, **very high** environmental hazard. Threats: the Caldera Guardian (fire elemental, CR 11+), ash-bound dead, Pyre-Wardens (veteran/zealot profiles), lava/heat hazards (DC 15–18 saves). **Gate it hard** — require the Drowned Flame, fire protection, faction support, and telegraphed retreat.

### Milestone Guidance
Level 12: arriving; the heretic-sage's plea / the old-glass offer.
Level 13: breaking/exploiting Doss Ashfast's trade (REV_002).
Level 14: the Caldera Descent (very high hazard; REV_002 deepened).
Level 15: sparking or aiding Mira Cindra's reform.
Level 16: carrying the mirror-faith model home.

### Failure State
A failed descent costs serious hazard damage and a clue (never the apex). If Mira Cindra is left unprotected, **Sef Embren** burns her and the heresy is silenced — the player loses a turnable ally and a synthesis-contrast.

### If Player Ignores
**Vole Cindra** consolidates the "old glass" supply for the theocracy; **Doss Ashfast**'s mines and the Caldera works fall to Ledger/Remnant relic-buyers; **Mira Cindra** is silenced. The thematic mirror is lost to the player's endgame argument.

### Transition to Tier 4
The Emberfell mirror is rhetorical ammunition for the Transform ending and a warning for the Restart ending (a public, consensual fire-faith vs. the Court's secret consumption). Mira Cindra and a reformed theocracy can be a distant Alliance/Transform contributor.

### Solo-Play Notes
The most lethal far region — gate it, require the Drowned Flame and fire protection, telegraph every hazard, provide retreat from the Caldera. Mira Cindra and reform are noncombat objectives; Vole Cindra can be out-argued via Mira, not just fought.

## Path 4: The Saltmere Path — "The Drowned Towns and the Salt-Clans' Grief"

Entry Level: 11–13
Entry Region: Saltmere Reaches (dying brackish inland sea, S-central)
Entry Hook: **Salt-Clan Matriarch Bryd Saltmere** seeks outside help as the salt-realm fractures, or **Drowned-Town Delver Oss** offers to guide the player into the receding-lake ruins for a cut of the salvage.

### Levels 11–12: The Salt-Clan Moot
The salt-clans cling to a fallen realm as the Saltmere recedes and exposes pre-Concord ruins. **Bryd Saltmere** rules from Brackhold but the clans splinter and raiders circle; **Marek Bonepan** runs the salt-caravans through dangerous country. The player can win Bryd's clans as allies/a route by mediating the holds or breaking a raider band. *Changes:* a salt-clan ally; safe passage across the receding lake.

### Levels 13–14: The Drowned Towns
The player dives the **Saltmere Deep Towns** (D24) — submerged pre-Concord towns surfacing as the lake dies. These towns drowned long before the Concord; their dead were laid to rest by an older custom and the ruins testify to an "older fall" (REV_001/005 echo). **Salt-Mother Tess Brackhold**'s old songs corroborate REV_005 as salt-clan folk-memory. *Changes:* REV_005 corroborated and deepened (the older fall, again — a continental pattern); a pre-Concord echo that thematically rhymes with the Reach's Barrow and the Marrowdowns barrows.

### Levels 15–16: The Salt-Grief Model (transition)
Tess Brackhold's salt-and-grief rites are another consensual model of holding the dead — the salt-clans mourn and remember without consuming. The player banks **Tess Brackhold** as a far old-songs/synthesis ally and carries the "older fall" corroboration home. *Changes:* the destroy/transform debate deepens (the world has drowned and re-drowned its dead; the harvest is the worst, but not the only, way).

### Key NPCs (from codex — no new ones)
Bryd Saltmere (Salt-Clan Matriarch; ruler; banked ally), Oss (Drowned-Town Delver; guide), Tess Brackhold (Salt-Mother; old-songs node; banked synthesis-adjacent ally), Marek Bonepan (salt-caravan master).

### Active Factions Here
- **Salt-clan holds:** Bryd Saltmere's confederacy — a banked far ally.
- **Mourners' Circle:** Tess Brackhold's salt-grief node — REV_005 folk-fragment.
- **Delver bands / raiders:** competing over the surfacing Drowned Towns.
- *(DM: a pre-Concord "older fall" echo; never the keystone.)*

### Dungeons/Sites
- **D24 Saltmere Deep Towns** (L11–15) — drowned-town dive; pre-Concord echo.

### Clues/Revelations
- **REV_001/005 echo** (the older fall; the Drowned Towns + Tess Brackhold's songs).
- A **REV_008 deepening** (salt-grief as a consensual model). **Never REV_007.**

### Rewards/Artifacts
Pre-Concord relics; a salt-clan ally; "older fall" lore; **Tess Brackhold** as a banked synthesis-adjacent ally.

### Encounter Profile
L11–15. Threats: salt-mummies, salt-stalkers, drowned dead, raiders, receding-lake hazards (mud, salt-flats, sudden channels). Telegraph; Bryd's guides and retreat routes essential.

### Milestone Guidance
Level 11: the salt-clan moot; the fracturing realm.
Level 12: winning Bryd's clans / safe passage.
Level 13: the Drowned Towns dive (older-fall echo).
Level 14: REV_005 corroborated via Tess Brackhold.
Level 15: banking the salt-grief synthesis model.
Level 16: carrying the corroboration home.

### Failure State
A failed dive costs a clue and a salt-mummy fight; if the clans are ignored, the realm fractures and raiders take the holds — the player loses a far ally and the older-fall lore, never the apex.

### If Player Ignores
The salt-realm fractures further; raiders consolidate the holds; **Oss** and delver bands strip the Drowned Towns and the "older fall" lore is lost; **Tess Brackhold** is displaced. A far ally and a REV_005 corroboration vanish.

### Transition to Tier 4
The Saltmere "older fall" (third such echo after the Reach barrows and Marrowdowns) cements that the harvest was a pattern — strengthening the Transform/Destroy debate. Bryd and Tess Brackhold join the old-songs coalition.

### Solo-Play Notes
A salt-clan guide makes the lake safe; the Drowned Towns are telegraphed lethal with retreat routes; Bryd and Tess Brackhold are noncombat allies.

## Path 5: The Highmark / Steppe Path — "Cold Proof and the Thinning Sky"

Entry Level: 12–13 (steppe playable from L8)
Entry Region: Wender Steppe → Highmark Passes (far north, via Karran Marches)
Entry Hook: **Clan-Speaker Tamur Wend-Khar** grants the player guest-right at the Winter-Camp at Cold Springs, or **Wind-Singer Esha** sends word that "the Sky has gone thin in the south" and a stranger must hear it.

### Levels 12–13: The Steppe and the Thinning Sky
The Wender Steppe is grass-sea ridden by clans the Concord never tamed. **Tamur Wend-Khar** gathers the clans at Cold Springs; **Wind-Singer Esha** reads the heavens at the Sky-Stones (an *independent*, non-Concord holy site) and senses a cosmic wrongness — a deeply oblique **REV_009 echo** (the afterlife thins). The player navigates clan politics, can win a horse-clan ally and mounts, and hears Esha's warning. Raid-lord **Borr of the Black Horse** reaves the far steppe and must be broken, turned, or bypassed. *Changes:* a horse-clan ally and mounts; the first oblique REV_009 cosmic echo.

### Levels 13–14: The Frozen Works
The player crosses into the **Highmark Passes** (via Karran) and reaches the ice-locked **Highmark Frozen Works** (D35) — the oldest Concord works and the most isolated survivors, frozen and best-preserved on the continent. Its records are the **clearest cold-preserved far surface-proof of REV_005/006** (the Concord harvested; the Quietfall was deliberate). *Changes:* REV_005/006 confirmed/deepened from the far north; relic-buyers and a Remnant expedition covet the records. **Requires cold protection and a pass-guide.**

### Levels 15–16: The Cosmic Frame (transition)
Esha's "thin Sky" (REV_009 oblique) and the Frozen Works' planned-Quietfall records (REV_005/006) together give the player the full cosmic stakes: the afterlife is finite, it was nearly drained, and the Concord drained it on purpose. This is the cosmological floor of the endgame — why every ending touches the survival of the dead. *Changes:* REV_009 deepened; the player understands the *why* behind the harvest before reaching the apex.

### Key NPCs (from codex — no new ones)
Tamur Wend-Khar (Clan-Speaker; banked ally), Wind-Singer Esha (steppe seer; REV_009 oblique cosmic echo), Borr of the Black Horse (raid-lord; obstacle), Tamur-Sai (clan-matriarch; distinct from Tamur Wend-Khar), Hessa Teeth (Karran pass-warlord, en route).

### Active Factions Here
- **Steppe clans:** Tamur Wend-Khar's confederacy — a banked ally and mounts.
- **The Sky-Stones' seers:** Esha — REV_009 oblique echo (independent, NOT a Concord node).
- **Concord Remnant / relic-buyers:** coveting the Frozen Works' records.
- *(DM: REV_009 cosmic echo; the Frozen Works corroborate REV_005/006 but are NOT the machine; the Sky-Stones are not a second keystone.)*

### Dungeons/Sites
- **D32 Wender Sky-Stones** (L8–13) — non-Concord shrine; REV_009 oblique.
- **D35 Highmark Frozen Works** (L12–16) — cold-preserved far surface-proof of REV_005/006.

### Clues/Revelations
- **REV_009 oblique** (Esha's "thin Sky" — never names the Quiet Country/harvest).
- **REV_005/006** (the Frozen Works records — clearest cold-preserved far proof). **Never REV_007; never the machine.**

### Rewards/Artifacts
Steppe-craft items and mounts; cold-preserved Concord records (REV_005/006 deepening); a horse-clan and a cold-north ally.

### Encounter Profile
L8–16; high environmental hazard in the Highmark (cold, ice, altitude; DC 15+ Constitution saves). Threats: frozen dead, ice-guardians, the Sky-Stones' guardian-spirits, reavers, weather. Require cold protection and a guide; telegraph and provide retreat.

### Milestone Guidance
Level 12: the steppe; Esha's thin-Sky warning.
Level 13: crossing into the Highmark.
Level 14: the Frozen Works (REV_005/006 cold proof).
Level 15: assembling the cosmic frame (REV_009 + REV_005/006).
Level 16: carrying the full cosmic stakes home.

### Failure State
A failed Frozen Works expedition costs cold damage and a clue (never the apex). If the steppe is ignored, **Borr** spreads his raiding and the passes close; Esha's warning goes unheeded (a missed REV_009 deepening).

### If Player Ignores
**Borr of the Black Horse** consolidates the far steppe and the passes close; a **Concord Remnant** expedition claims the Frozen Works' cold-preserved records uncontested; Esha's "thin Sky" warning is never heard, so the player reaches the endgame without the full cosmic frame (REV_009 stays shallow).

### Transition to Tier 4
The cosmic frame (finite afterlife, deliberately drained) is the existential stakes of the endgame — it makes Veyl's argument frighteningly reasonable and makes the Transform ending the only one that addresses the thinning without consuming the dead. Tamur Wend-Khar's clans can be a distant Alliance contributor.

### Solo-Play Notes
A clan-guide and mounts make the steppe safe; cold protection and a pass-guide are required in the Highmark; the Sky-Stones are a noncombat seer-site; Borr has a negotiation route. The far north is a barrier, never arc-mandatory.

## Path 6: The Concord Heartlands Path — "Imperial Ruin and the Apex Approach"

Entry Level: 13–14 (the apex-adjacent path; L13+ only)
Entry Region: Concord Heartlands / the Ruin'd Crown (the Concord's fallen surface capital, astride the Greatspine)
Entry Hook: **Crown-Reclaimer Magister Hollin Vane** recruits the player as a relic-finder for his Remnant expedition, or **Warden Lyssa Crownmouth** hires the player to check the Reclaimers and hold her scavenger-town.

### Levels 13–14: The Reclaimer's Dig
Astride the Greatspine lies the shattered seat of the old Custodian Concord. **Hollin Vane** (apex-*adjacent* — he holds *a record of what the Concord did*, never the live machine) races to seize the Heartlands and the **Ash-Crown Fragment** for a restored Concord; **Lyssa Crownmouth** holds fortified Crownmouth against the Pilgrim Camps; heretic-priest **Father Casian Ord** preaches a half-true heresy about the Concord's sin; deep-ruin guide **"Old Crown" Mab** knows the dangerous ways in. The player can beat, sabotage, or join Hollin Vane's dig into the **Old Concord Heartlands Ruin / Ruin'd Crown** (D21) and recover the Ash-Crown Fragment. *Changes:* the Reclamation (#7) peaks; the player gains political legitimacy in the Concord-succession game, or hands it to the Remnant.

### Levels 14–15: The Crown's Records
The Ruin'd Crown's record-vault is the **clearest far surface-proof of REV_005/006** — the Concord's true scale and sin, in imperial writing and stone. **Casian Ord**'s heresy and **"Old Crown" Mab**'s lore corroborate it as folk-memory. *Changes:* REV_005/006 confirmed/deepened; the player learns the Concord was a continent-spanning power that harvested at imperial scale — and that *the histories lie about how it fell.* **DM hard ceiling: the records NEVER imply the live machine, the keystone, or the Court is here.**

### Levels 15–16: The Convergence Toward the Apex (transition to Tier 4)
By now the player should hold REV_003 (Reke serves someone), REV_005 (the Concord harvested), and REV_006 (deliberate Quietfall). The Heartlands provide the imperial-scale confirmation that makes the convergence inevitable: *if the Concord deliberately paused the harvest, where are the ones who did it?* Hollin Vane is apex-adjacent but **never confirms the Court** — he believes the harvest is a thing to be *restored*, not that its architects survive. The player leaves the Heartlands with the Ash-Crown (legitimacy), the imperial proof, and the question that opens REV_007 — answerable only by the lethal-telegraphed descent under Hollowmere, by full convergence, or by Maire's heretic contact. *Changes:* the apex gate (REV_007) is now reachable; the player heads home, to the vertical endgame.

### Key NPCs (from codex — no new ones)
Hollin Vane (Crown-Reclaimer; apex-adjacent; never confirms the Court), Lyssa Crownmouth (war-captain; scavenger-town), Casian Ord (heretic-priest; folk REV_005 corroboration), "Old Crown" Mab (deep-ruin guide), Sabine Ferrant (Remnant true believer, may be present), Wenna Stone (turnable Reclaimer, may cross over from Karran — Path 5/§16).

### Active Factions Here
- **Concord Remnant:** Hollin Vane's expedition + Sabine Ferrant — racing for the Heartlands and the keystone (a *living* tyranny in the making).
- **Cinder Ledger:** backing or betraying the dig.
- **Scavenger-town / pilgrim factions:** Lyssa Crownmouth vs. the Camps.
- *(DM: the **surface** Concord heart — NOT the underground keystone, NEVER the Court's seat.)*

### Dungeons/Sites
- **D21 Old Concord Heartlands Ruin / Ruin'd Crown** (L10–14) — far surface-proof of REV_005/006; faction-contested; the Ash-Crown Fragment.

### Clues/Revelations
- **REV_005/006** (far surface-proof — the Concord's imperial scale and sin).
- The **convergence threshold of REV_007** — the Heartlands raise the question (where are the architects?) but **never answer it**; the answer is only under Hollowmere. **DM hard ceiling: never imply the live machine/keystone/Court is here.**

### Rewards/Artifacts
**The Ash-Crown Fragment** (political legitimacy lever for the Seize/Exposure endings); high-tier Concord relics; **Wenna Stone** as a banked reform-Remnant ally if turned (here or in Karran).

### Encounter Profile
L13–17, high to very high. Threats: Concord Construct-Guardians at full power (CR 11–17), ash-bound dead, Reclaimer champions and the Remnant hierarchy (assassin/mage/knight profiles), far apex predators (chimera, manticore, young dragon). Telegraph lethality; allies and retreat essential. **DM-gated horror** (Soul Monger CR 11, Bodak) only in the deepest harvest-scarred sub-vaults.

### Milestone Guidance
Level 13: arriving; the Reclaimer's dig.
Level 14: the Ruin'd Crown descent; the Ash-Crown.
Level 15: the Crown's records (REV_005/006 imperial proof).
Level 16: the convergence question; the apex gate opens.

### Failure State
A failed dig hands the Ash-Crown and the Heartlands' legitimacy to **Hollin Vane** and the Remnant — a stronger endgame rival — and costs a corroboration clue (never the apex; the Heartlands are never the only path to REV_005/006). If Crownmouth falls, the Camps riot and the surface ruin becomes a lawless death-trap.

### If Player Ignores
**Hollin Vane** and the inner Remnant secure the Heartlands and the Ash-Crown with full legitimacy and Ledger backing, then race the player to the keystone (#7→3–4); **Lyssa Crownmouth**'s town falls; the clearest imperial proof of REV_005/006 stays in Remnant hands. The endgame rival is far stronger.

### Transition to Tier 4
The Heartlands are the **primary apex approach** in spirit — they pose the question REV_007 answers, hand the player the Ash-Crown (a Seize/Exposure lever), and can split the Remnant (turning Wenna Stone/idealists against Quorrin and Hollin Vane). The player carries all of this *home*, to the vertical descent under Hollowmere — the only place the Court is real. See `TIER_4_PLAYABLE_PATHS_LEVELS_17_20.md`.

### Solo-Play Notes
A Tier 3 lethal far site — require the Drowned Flame, faction support, and a retreat route before the Crown. Hollin Vane can be out-talked or split, not just out-fought; "Old Crown" Mab is a noncombat guide; the apex truth is never dumped here even if the player rushes the dig.

## Cross-Path Convergence (DM)

All six paths confirm the Tier-3 historical truths — **REV_005** (the Concord harvested), **REV_006** (the Quietfall was deliberate), and **REV_009** (the afterlife is finite) — by different routes, and several bank far allies (Holt, Senna Crale, Mira Cindra, Bryd/Tess Brackhold, Tamur Wend-Khar, Wenna Stone) and levers (the Ash-Crown, banking leverage, the synthesis models). **None of the far paths delivers REV_007** — the Court stays a legend until the player returns to Hollowmere and either descends (D20→D23), converges REV_003+005+006, or is contacted by Maire. The far continent is **breadth, corroboration, allies, and the cosmic frame**; the apex is **vertical, under Hollowmere.** Mix freely; the world clocks fill any gaps.
