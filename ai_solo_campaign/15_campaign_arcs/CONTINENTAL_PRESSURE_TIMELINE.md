## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY — the concrete event calendar the world follows when the player does nothing.** Each event names an NPC, a faction, a location, a concrete action, the concrete consequence, the rumor the player can hear, and the clock it advances. Fire these between sessions when the player lingers or ignores a thread; they make the world consequential and give the player rumors that pull them back toward play. **Order is the default trajectory, not a script** — a player who engages a thread preempts its event. **DM-only causes (the harvest, the Court, the keystone) must never be narrated as such; only the surface action and rumor are player-facing.**

Calendar baseline: campaign start is Greyfall AQ 101 (`../02_runtime_state/WORLD_CLOCKS.md`). Tiers map to roughly: Tier 2 ≈ AQ 101–103, Tier 3 ≈ AQ 103–105, Tier 4 ≈ AQ 105–106.

## Tier 2 Period (Levels 5–10 equivalent, AQ 101–103)

### Event T2-1: The Basin Vote Carries
Trigger: ~3 sessions of player inaction on Hollowmere politics, or time alone.
Action: **Councilor Aldous Reke** wins the Reachward Compact vote over **Reeve Marda Coalmont**'s objection; **Pevin Oss** files the forged water-rights; drainage of the Hollowmere basin begins.
Result: the upper drowned shrine starts to surface; the Reed Holms families are evicted; the Reach's heart of the crisis is laid bare.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"They've started draining the old basin at Hollowmere — turned a family of fen-folk out of the Reed Holms to do it."*
Clock Advanced: #2 Basin Drains → Stage 2; #1 Harvest Restarts (MASTER) → Stage 2.

### Event T2-2: The Salvage Corner
Trigger: player ignores the Saltmargin/Harrowgast relic squeeze.
Action: **Factor Domic Sael** and enforcer **Collector-Captain Mauld Tallow** price out independent salvagers and seize the "old glass" coming off the Sunder Heights; an independent the player knows (e.g. **Decca Rull** or a Harrowgast digger) is ruined or jailed.
Result: the Cinder Ledger corners Reach salvage; the relic-trade consolidates.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"The Ledger's bought up every scrap of old Concord glass on the Heights — and broke the ones who wouldn't sell."*
Clock Advanced: #6 The Monopoly Closes → Stage 2.

### Event T2-3: The Reclaimers Take the Adit
Trigger: player ignores the Sunder Heights node race.
Action: Remnant agent **Veska Dunn** claims the **Deep Adit** (D05) and ships "old glass" south to Caradril for the Vyre–Quorrin pipeline.
Result: the Concord Remnant secures a relic-supply node; the Reclamation accelerates.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"Some scholar's crew has taken over the Deep Adit up in the Heights — digging for the old glass, shipping it to the city."*
Clock Advanced: #7 The Reclamation → Stage 2; feeds C1 (the Caradril deal).

### Event T2-4: The Vale Closes Its Eyes
Trigger: player ignores Ashgarden Vale.
Action: **Moot-Reeve Halsa Tindle** suppresses the Vale's failures to protect the harvest/pilgrimage economy; **Factor Wymar Pell** consolidates the "saint's-bones" relic pipeline at Saint Veddow's Rest.
Result: the Vale becomes a quiet Ledger/Remnant relic-supply ground in denial; Mourner elder **Ezrith Combe** is sidelined.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"Down in the Vale they'll tell you everything's fine — and sell you a saint's-bone relic while they say it."*
Clock Advanced: V1 The Harvest-Moot's Silence → Stage 2; V2 The Saint's Relics Sold → Stage 2 (feeds #6).

### Event T2-5: The Drowned Tide Rises
Trigger: player ignores the Pale Coast.
Action: the maritime nodes (the Drowned Lamp, the sea-shrines) warm as the master clock turns; **Salt-Mother Bryd**'s salt-and-tide rite fails at Cobble Strand; **Factor Maris Cole** tightens the sea-relic monopoly with the wreckers.
Result: the sea returns its dead worse than before; the Coast slides toward "drowning in the tide"; the **Drowned Flame** relic risks falling to a Ledger relic-buyer.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"The sea's giving its dead back wrong down the Pale Coast — and the rites at Cobble Strand have stopped taking."*
Clock Advanced: C-PC1 The Drowned Tide Rises → Stage 2 (feeds #1/#10); C-PC2 The Salt Price Closes → Stage 2.

### Event T2-6: The Caradril Deal Forms
Trigger: player ignores Caradril through Tier 2.
Action: **Mother Calla Vyre** (Cinder Ledger) and **Archivist Quorrin Vane** (Concord Remnant) strike the terms of their secret Remembrance monopoly; clerk **Coll Riis** copies the proof in fear; the Tidewater Council under **Halloran Voss** shelves the Reach's warnings.
Result: the two richest factions begin to co-own the dead; reformer **Sefa Dann** is sidelined; the city sleeps in denial.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"The Ledger and those Lamplighter scholars are thick as thieves in Caradril lately — some deal nobody's supposed to know about."*
Clock Advanced: C1 The Deal Closes → Stage 2; C2 The Council Sleeps → Stage 2.

## Tier 3 Period (Levels 11–16 equivalent, AQ 103–105)

### Event T3-1: The Shrine Surfaces
Trigger: the Basin clock reaching Stage 3–4 without intervention.
Action: the Hollowmere drainage exposes the upper drowned shrine; leakage spikes across the Reach; the first deliberate local harvest of a death occurs (DM-only cause).
Result: rites fail catastrophically near the basin; wrong-come-backs appear; the Reach is openly afraid.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"The old shrine under the basin's come up out of the water at Hollowmere — and the dead won't lie quiet anywhere near it."*
Clock Advanced: #2 Basin Drains → Stage 3–4; #1 MASTER → Stage 3 (DM-only: the Court can now manifest to those who enter).

### Event T3-2: Othetha Breaks Her Oath
Trigger: the Wardens reaching crisis-load (Clock #3) without the player slowing it.
Action: **Warden Sister Othetha**, overwhelmed and unable to keep the seal-truth alone, reveals the historical truth (REV_005/006) to a trusted few Wardens; the order fractures between keepers and doubters.
Result: the seal weakens as the Wardens split; the true purpose leaks (a REV_005/006 source the player can still reach).
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"The Ashen Wardens are tearing themselves apart — some of them are saying the old order did something terrible, and the rest won't hear it."*
Clock Advanced: #3 The Wardens Break → Stage 3.

### Event T3-3: The Deal Closes (and the Ledger Sells Death)
Trigger: the Caradril deal (C1) reaching Stage 3 without exposure.
Action: **Vyre** and **Quorrin** finalize their monopoly; the Cinder Ledger begins selling small death-cheating to Caradril's rich; **Provost Anneth Vell** runs the trade from the Ledger Keep.
Result: immortality begins to be a purchasable commodity; the dead become inventory; the Remnant secures its relic-pipeline.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"The very rich in Caradril have stopped dying on schedule — the Ledger's selling something under the counter, and it isn't medicine."*
Clock Advanced: C1 → Stage 3; #6 The Monopoly Closes → Stage 3.

### Event T3-4: The Reclamation Races for the Crown
Trigger: player ignores the Concord Heartlands / Remnant.
Action: **Crown-Reclaimer Magister Hollin Vane** secures the Ruin'd Crown and the **Ash-Crown Fragment** for a restored Concord, backed by **Sabine Ferrant** and Ledger money; **Lyssa Crownmouth**'s scavenger-town is pressured.
Result: the Remnant gains imperial legitimacy and races the player (and, unknowingly, the Court) toward the keystone.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"That scholar-magister has taken the old Concord crown-seat in the far Heartlands — they say he means to raise the Concord again."*
Clock Advanced: #7 The Reclamation → Stage 3–4.

### Event T3-5: The Gravecallers Get Their Tools
Trigger: player ignores the Greyfens cult.
Action: **Cole Ashby**'s radical cell acquires Concord breaking-tools and a shrine map (with **Sister Knell** and **Mother Osset**'s help); the cell moves toward the basin to shatter the shrine.
Result: a breach attempt looms — the destroy-ending's catastrophe risk goes live.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"The grave-speakers out in the Greyfens have got hold of something old and dangerous — and they're headed for the basin with it."*
Clock Advanced: #5 The Breaking → Stage 3.

### Event T3-6: The Circle Turns Militant
Trigger: player ignores the Mourners through the relic crisis.
Action: **Grandmother Wend**'s Circle, enraged by the relic-trade and the surfacing shrine, blocks relic-caravans and moves (with old-songs confirmation of REV_005) toward destroying a shrine; radicals overlap the Gravecallers.
Result: the Mourners radicalize; a shrine may be shattered violently.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"The Mourners aren't just grieving anymore — they're stopping the relic-wagons on the roads, and worse is coming."*
Clock Advanced: #4 The Circle Turns Militant → Stage 3.

### Event T3-7: The Ledger Goes Continental
Trigger: player ignores Glassmere/the Verdance.
Action: **Factor-General Wessel Crane** completes the inland pipeline and **League-Factor Oren Glass** corners the Glassmere relic-trade; rival banker **Sefwy Holt** is outmaneuvered (if not turned by the player).
Result: the Cinder Ledger controls the continental relic-trade; the Monopoly peaks.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"The Ledger's bought the whole river-trade from the Verdance down to Glassmere — there's no relic moves on the continent they don't tax."*
Clock Advanced: #6 The Monopoly Closes → Stage 4.

### Event T3-8: The Thinning Becomes a Pattern
Trigger: the master clock at Stage 3+ with the player not slowing it.
Action: failed resurrections and thin-born births form a noticeable regional pattern; **Doctor Onaia Veen** and Caradril's **Mother Ysarra** document the clinical alarm; the Sill's thin-born refugees swell under **Renna Sill**.
Result: even the Court's argument starts to feel tempting (REV_009 visible); regional dread spreads.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"More and more children are born 'thin' — empty-eyed, the priests say — and the dead they try to raise come back wrong. Something's gone out of the world."*
Clock Advanced: #10 The Thinning Spreads → Stage 2–3; C3 The City's Denial Cracks → Stage 3.

## Tier 4 Period (Levels 17–20 equivalent, AQ 105–106)

### Event T4-1: The Basin Is Breached
Trigger: the Basin clock reaching Stage 4 without the player holding it.
Action: drainage (or a rival/Gravecaller breach) opens the drowned shrine fully; the keystone becomes accessible; **Reke** (or his successor) hands the basin to whoever controls it.
Result: the Hollow Court gains direct keystone access; the harvest can restart at scale (DM-only).
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"The basin's dry now, and there's a stair down into the old shrine that nobody should walk — the air comes up cold and hungry."*
Clock Advanced: #2 → keystone reopened; #1 MASTER → Stage 4.

### Event T4-2: The Keystone Race Converges
Trigger: the endgame approaching with multiple factions un-neutralized.
Action: the rival claimants converge on the basin — **Hollin Vane**'s Remnant, **Vyre**'s Ledger, and **Cole Ashby**'s Gravecaller cell each race for the keystone; **Othetha**'s Wardens and **Wend**'s Mourners move to defend the seal.
Result: a multi-way standoff at the basin; whoever reaches and controls the keystone decides the harvest's fate.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"Every power in Orrun is sending people to that dry basin at Hollowmere — scholars, Ledger-men, grave-speakers, Wardens. Something's about to happen there."*
Clock Advanced: #5/#6/#7 converge on the keystone.

### Event T4-3: The Harvest Restarts (default catastrophe)
Trigger: total player inaction at the endgame.
Action: the master clock completes; the **Hollow Court** (Veyl, Maire, Orre) resumes the harvest; the Court emerges as immortal stewards (DM-only).
Result: the worst default outcome — an age of order under the Court, the dead consumed forever (a playable bleak epilogue; see `TIER_4_PLAYABLE_PATHS_LEVELS_17_20.md` Path 9).
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *(player-safe surface only)* *"The dying has changed. The sick linger past their time, the grieving are comforted by things wearing familiar faces, and a cold calm has settled over the Reach. No one will say what walks up from the basin at night."*
Clock Advanced: #1 MASTER → resolved (Restart-default).

### Event T4-4: The Gravecaller Breach (alternate catastrophe)
Trigger: the Gravecaller cell reaching the keystone alone (Clock #5 unchecked).
Action: **Cole Ashby**'s cell shatters the keystone with the breaking-tools, alone and unguided.
Result: the dead are freed catastrophically; resurrection ends; the living/dead boundary destabilizes (an honest but broken world; `TIER_4_PLAYABLE_PATHS_LEVELS_17_20.md` Path 9).
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"The grave-speakers did something at the basin — and now the dead are everywhere, all at once, all leaving. Nobody can raise the fallen anymore."*
Clock Advanced: #5 → resolved (uncontrolled Destroy).

### Event T4-5: The Reclamation Seizes (alternate catastrophe)
Trigger: the player loses the keystone race to the Remnant or Ledger.
Action: **Hollin Vane**/**Quorrin** seize the keystone for a restored Concord (a living tyranny), OR **Vyre** seizes it for a market in souls.
Result: the original sin repeated by living hands; the player is sidelined or hunted.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"The Concord's risen again — or the Ledger has — and they've got the old machine under Hollowmere. The dead serve the living now, and the price is paid by the gone."*
Clock Advanced: #7 (or #6) → resolved (Seize).

### Event T4-6: The Synthesis Holds (best outcome, if earned)
Trigger: the player enacts the Transform ending (REV_010 + Maire + coalition/artifact).
Action: with **Custodian Maire** turned against **Veyl**, the **Last Voice** testifying, and the **Sunmark** model (**Sael Sunmark**), the player anchors a consensual system; the Hollow Court ends itself.
Result: the only outcome that addresses the thinning without consuming the dead; a fragile, just new settlement between the living and the dead.
Rumor the Player Can Hear: *"They say the dead get to choose now — to be remembered or to rest — and the cold thing under Hollowmere has gone quiet at last. It cost more than anyone will ever know."*
Clock Advanced: #1 MASTER → resolved (Transform); #10 Thinning → addressed.
