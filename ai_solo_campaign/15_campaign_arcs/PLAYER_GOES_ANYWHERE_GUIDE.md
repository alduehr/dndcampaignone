## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY — how to handle the player going somewhere too early, too late, or skipping a region.** The campaign is open-world: the player can walk anywhere. This file gives concrete, named handling so the AI DM never has to improvise a region's danger-gating, partial content, or substitute hooks. **Core principle: warn, gate, offer partial content, provide retreat — never block and never spring cheap death** (per `SOLO_CAMPAIGN_BALANCE_GUIDE.md` and `AI_DM_CORE_RULES.md`). **The apex (REV_007/Court/keystone) stays gated regardless of where the player goes; far sources never deliver it.**

---

## Player Arrives in a Region Too Early (Below Recommended Level)

For each region, the AI gives a danger warning, the partial content that *is* playable low-level, named NPCs available even then, a retreat option, and a hook toward somewhere more appropriate.

### Concord Heartlands (rec. L13–17) arrived under L13
- **Danger warning:** Concord Construct-Guardians at full power, ash-bound dead, Reclaimer champions — *"the Ruin'd Crown is a death-ground for the unprepared; even the scavengers of Crownmouth won't go deep."*
- **Partial content:** the surface scavenger-town and Pilgrim Camps are playable (social/intrigue); **Lyssa Crownmouth**'s town-defense and **Father Casian Ord**'s heresy-preaching are low-risk; the **D21** deep is gated/lethal-telegraphed.
- **NPCs available low-level:** Lyssa Crownmouth, Casian Ord, "Old Crown" Mab (guide).
- **Retreat option:** Crownmouth's gates; the Greatspine road back to Glassmere/Verdance.
- **Hook elsewhere:** Mab warns the player they need the Drowned Flame and more strength — sending them to the **Pale Coast** (the Flame) or back to **Caradril** (the Archive).

### Emberfell Theocracy (rec. L12–16) arrived under L12
- **Danger warning:** *"the Caldera will cook you; the Pyre-Wardens burn outsiders who pry."* Very high environmental hazard.
- **Partial content:** Ashfast's politics and **Mira Cindra**'s heresy (social); Cinderhold's "old glass" trade (mercantile); the **D22** descent is gated.
- **NPCs available low-level:** Mira Cindra, Doss Ashfast.
- **Retreat option:** the Ash Roads out; the route to Saltmere/Cindern.
- **Hook elsewhere:** Mira Cindra notes the player isn't ready for the deep works — pointing them to gentler far regions (Marrowdowns, Sunmark).

### Highmark Passes (rec. L12–16) arrived under L12
- **Danger warning:** *"the cold alone will kill you; the Frozen Works are older and deadlier than anything you've faced."*
- **Partial content:** the isolated holds (survival/social); the **D35** Frozen Works deep is gated/lethal.
- **NPCs available low-level:** frozen-hold leaders (referenced far rosters).
- **Retreat option:** back down the passes to Karran/Wender.
- **Hook elsewhere:** a hold-elder sends the player to the **Wender Steppe** (Esha, gentler) to learn the cold country first.

### Saltmere / Sallowmarch / Karran (rec. L9–15) arrived under L9
- **Danger warning:** salt-mummies, deep aberrations (Sallowmarch), warlord bands (Karran), fever (DC 13+ saves).
- **Partial content:** the surface holds and politics (social); the deep dives (D24/D30/D27) are gated.
- **NPCs available low-level:** Bryd Saltmere, Doll Fenn, "Iron" Brask, Mully Karr.
- **Retreat option:** the safe holds; the routes back toward the Gulf/Glassmere.
- **Hook elsewhere:** a local guide sends the player to **Marrowdowns** (the gentler far region, L6+) or back to **Caradril**.

### Glassmere / Hethewald / Hollow Gulf (rec. L7–15) arrived under L7
- **Danger warning:** moderate — these are reachable on the low end; the *deep* sites (D28/D29/D25/D31) are gated.
- **Partial content:** the cities, ports, and free-holds are largely social/intrigue and playable low; the vault/forbidden-ruin deeps are gated.
- **NPCs available low-level:** Oren Glass, Sefwy Holt, Bram Hethe, Ive Calder.
- **Retreat option:** the river/sea routes home.
- **Hook elsewhere:** a faction contact points the player to level-appropriate work or back up the Verdance.

### Gentle far regions — Marrowdowns (L6+), Sunmark (L8+), Wender (L8+)
- **Danger warning:** light — these are *meant* for lower-Tier-3 play; warn only on the deep barrows/works.
- **Partial content:** most of each region is playable; gate only the deepest sub-sites.
- **NPCs available low-level:** Senna Crale, Sael Sunmark, Tamur Wend-Khar.
- **Retreat option:** guides (Doss Wether, grove-guides, clan-guides) make these safe.
- **Hook elsewhere:** these are good *destinations* for an over-eager low-level traveler — let them play.

## Player Arrives in a Region Too Late (Above Recommended Level)

For each, the AI notes what changed from delay, named consequences, what's still available, and how the region now connects to higher-tier play.

### A region the player ignored through Tier 2 (e.g. the Vale, Coast, Tollwood)
- **What changed:** the regional clock ran (V1/V2, C-PC1/C-PC2, T1/T2). The Vale hollowed (Tindle's cover-up held; Wymar Pell consolidated the relic-trade); the Coast drowned in the tide (Bryd's rite failed; Maris Cole monopolized); the Tollwood road choked (Skell ousted Renn).
- **Named consequences:** the **Drowned Flame** may already be lost to a relic-buyer; **Ezrith Combe**/**Bryd** are sidelined and less willing to ally; the honest M5 windows are now grief, not warning.
- **Still available:** the player can still recover allies (harder), dive the gated nodes (now stirred and more dangerous, scaled up), and gather corroboration.
- **Higher-tier connection:** the hollowed region is now a Ledger/Remnant supply ground — engaging it becomes about *breaking* an established pipeline, not preventing one.

### Caradril arrived late (after the deal closed)
- **What changed:** C1 closed — **Vyre** and **Quorrin** co-own the dead; the Ledger sells death-cheating; the Council slept (C2); the denial cracked (C3).
- **Named consequences:** the **Sealed Archive** is still reachable (REV_005/006) but the city is a complacent, well-resourced endgame obstacle; **Sefa Dann**'s reform is sidelined.
- **Still available:** the player can expose the closed deal (harder, but it shatters the Ledger/Remnant alliance late); reach the Archive; protect **Renna Sill**'s refugees.
- **Higher-tier connection:** Caradril becomes an endgame Seize/Exposure lever (the Ash-Crown + the Archive + a late reform).

### A far region arrived late (Heartlands, Glassmere, etc.)
- **What changed:** the Reclamation/Monopoly ran — **Hollin Vane** holds the Ash-Crown and the Heartlands; **Oren Glass** corners the continental trade.
- **Named consequences:** the player faces stronger, entrenched rivals; the records/Ash-Crown are in Remnant hands.
- **Still available:** the player can contest, sabotage, or split the rivals (turn **Wenna Stone**/**Sefwy Holt**) — the corroboration (REV_005/006) is still gettable elsewhere.
- **Higher-tier connection:** the late far region is now about *contesting the keystone race*, feeding directly into Tier 4.

## Player Skips Caradril

- **Faction services unavailable:** the city's deep services (the **Sealed Archive** as the clearest REV_005/006 path; the **Bellman**/Hush information; reformed-Caradril political allies Dann/Kade; Ledger-splitting via Coll Riis) are not picked up.
- **Quest chains that "break":** `Q_THE_SEALED_ARCHIVE`, `Q_THE_OPEN_CHARTER`, `Q_THE_QUAY_CHARTER`, `Q_THE_SEALED_LETTER` are skipped.
- **Substitute sources (the arc is never blocked):** REV_005/006 also come from **Othetha**'s confession and the **Tallow Man**'s channeled witness in the Reach, and from the far surface-proofs (Heartlands D21, Highmark D35); REV_004 (steering) comes from pressing **Sefra Quick** directly; political endgame allies can be banked from far factions (Holt, Wenna Stone) instead. The Archive is the *clearest* REV_005/006 path but **never the only one** (`REVELATION_MAP.md` three-clue rule).
- **Consequence:** without engaging Caradril, the **Vyre–Quorrin deal closes** unopposed (C1→3–4) — a stronger endgame obstacle. The player reaches the endgame with less city leverage.

## Player Skips Ring 1 (Vale / Tollwood / Coast)

- **What's missed:** the honest M5 windows (Tace, Sennet, Bryd — REV_001/005 corroboration); the regional allies (Ezrith Combe, Bryd, Brann); and critically the **Drowned Flame** (Thin-touch immunity from the Pale Coast).
- **Substitute sources:** REV_001/005 corroboration is also in the Reach (Old Sashe, the Tallow Man, Othetha) and the far continent (the old-songs network, the older-fall echoes); the **Drowned Flame** can also be recovered later (it surfaces at the Drowned Lamp/Skerry Shrine and can be retrieved in Tier 3) — but a player who skips the Coast should be **warned that node-diving without Thin-touch immunity is dangerous** (disadvantage on Death saves near raw relics until a true rite/shrine long-rest).
- **Consequence:** Ring 1's clocks run (the Coast drowns, the Vale hollows, the road chokes); the player loses regional allies and survivability tech. The endgame is still reachable (it's vertical under Hollowmere), so a homebody-who-skips-Ring-1 is never blocked — just under-equipped.

## Player Goes Straight to the Far Continent at Low Level

- **What happens:** the Verdance corridor (L8+) is the gate; below L8 the player can *attempt* it but the corridor's hazards (Lord Ennis Marrow's toll, Ledger pipeline-guards, the Nine Locks relay-guardian) and the far regions' deep sites are lethal-telegraphed.
- **How to handle it:** **Mossa Drenn** (honest barge-captain) offers safe passage and *warns* the player the far continent is beyond them; the gentle far regions (Marrowdowns L6+, Sunmark/Wender L8+) absorb an over-eager traveler safely; the lethal far regions (Emberfell, Highmark, Heartlands deep) are gated with warnings and retreat.
- **The arc is unaffected:** far sources never deliver REV_007 (the apex is vertical under Hollowmere), so a low-level far-explorer corroborates the theme and banks allies/levers without ever breaking the mystery's pacing.
- **Consequence:** the player risks death at gated deep sites if they ignore the warnings — but the DM telegraphs every lethal site and provides retreat, so this is the player's informed choice, never a cheap ambush.

## Player Tries to Enter the Under-Shrine Too Early

- **What blocks access:** the **Basin Keystone Approach** (D20) is reachable as low as L5, but the deep stair to the **Under-Shrine** (D23) is **gated and lethal-telegraphed**; before the Basin clock breaches and before REV_007's gate (L13+), the Court is **dormant/sealed** and the deep is barred (flooded, warded, guarded by Concord constructs).
- **What danger warnings exist:** *"the air comes up cold and hungry from that stair; nothing that goes down comes back the same"*; Concord Construct-Guardians at the threshold; the Relay-Warden; drowning/cold/dread.
- **What they can learn at low level:** **fragments only** — REV_001/003/005 surface fragments at D20, never REV_007. A player who dives early gets clues and danger, withdraws, and prepares (`REVELATION_MAP.md` pacing guard rail: "if the player rushes, do not dump REV_007 — gate it").
- **How to handle it:** telegraph the lethal danger, let the player take fragments and a hard lesson, provide retreat from D20/D23, and **never render D23's apex content**. The Court stays a legend until the gate.

---

## General Principle

The AI DM treats the world as *real and indifferent to the player's level* — danger exists where it exists — but **fair**: every lethal place is telegraphed before the player commits, every region has partial content and named NPCs playable on arrival, every dead-end has a substitute path (the three-clue rule guarantees no single point of failure), and retreat is always available. The player can go anywhere; the DM's job is to make "anywhere" *consequential and survivable-by-clever-play*, never *blocked or cheaply lethal*. The apex stays gated by *narrative readiness* (REV_007's prerequisites), not by walls — so the player's freedom never breaks the mystery.
