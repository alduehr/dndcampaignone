## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Marrowdowns** — settled chalk-down sheep country and ancient barrow-fields, the agrarian heart of old Orrun. Biome: pastoral downs + barrow-undead overlay. Danger: low pastoral / high at the barrows (Lvl 6–10). The barrow-dead are a **pre-Concord echo** of the campaign's horror (older, separate fall). Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
Rolling chalk downs, shire-moots, and old barrow-fields where the very old dead don't lie quiet — Lvl 6–10, barrow-danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A Remnant party surveying the barrows (M6) · a blood-feud between holds (pick a side or pass) · a ghoul-warren in an old crypt · a banshee in a ruined hall · a barrow disturbed by treasure-seekers (the dead rousing).

## Rare Encounters
- The Gathered Barrow mass-undead set-piece (HORROR #8) · a barrow-wight commanding lesser dead · a pre-Concord truth surfacing (older fall, oblique M6 far-echo).

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Barrow-Wight / Barrow-Shade** (`HORROR_AND_CURSE_THREATS.md` #4; bound to its barrow, grave-rite out) · a hold-lord boss (Knight + levy) · the Barrow Complex's gathered-dead climax (mass-undead with a mass-rite lever).

## Noncombat Encounters
- Grave-goods laid out wrong (a clue/dilemma — restore or rob) · a flock-loss the shepherds blame on the barrows · two predators contesting a carcass · a wounded warhorse · a moot-debate the PC can sway · a barrow that "watches."

## Social Encounters
- Shire-reeves, shepherds, wool-traders, barrow-robbers, moot-mothers (Mourners), hold-levies, mounted lawkeepers, treasure-seekers.

## Environmental Hazards
- Sinkholes/collapsing barrows, downs mist, fast moorland streams, exposure on the open downs at night, an unstable barrow-roof.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** barrow-dead most active; mist + getting-lost. **Storm:** exposed downs, no cover. **Season:** lambing-spring (shepherd density), winter (exposure, short days).

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- The *downs* are safe; the *barrows* are the danger — and they're avoidable and bound. Barrow-dead can be laid by grave-rite (Religion DC 14–15) or by restoring stolen grave-goods. Holds prefer toll/levy to a fight. Always a shepherd's croft or hold to retreat to.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **6–8:** pastoral common rows; single barrow-shades; light hold-politics. **9–10:** the Barrow Complex; mass-undead; hold-feud stakes.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Mourners' Circle** (downs grief-keepers; M5; old grave-songs — strong here). **Manorial holds / shire-moots** (levies, tolls, feuds — regional minor power). **Concord Remnant** (barrow-surveying; M6). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M5** (the down-dead won't rest; old songs), **M6** (pre-Concord echo — an *older, separate* fall; far-echo, gated). Fragments only; `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`.
- **Horror additions (RtHW pass 2026-07-07):** a **Gallows Speaker** (RtHW) at a gibbet-crossroads or hanged-man's barrow — a talking M5/M6 fragment-vector (gated wording only; `HORROR #H10`); a **Carrion Stalker** (VRGtR/RtHW) laired in a robbed barrow; a **Dullahan** (VRGtR/RtHW) as a rare down-roads death-omen.
