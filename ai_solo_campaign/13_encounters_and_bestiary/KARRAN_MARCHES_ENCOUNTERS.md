## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Karran Marches** — a jagged outlaw-and-mining frontier in the Karran Teeth, a harsher mirror of the Sundering Reach. Biome: mountain/pass + mine-works. Danger: high (Lvl 9–14) from giants, warlords, and terrain. Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
A lawless northeastern mountain frontier of petty warlords, deep ore-mines, and giants in the high passes — Lvl 9–14, warlord/giant danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A warlord blood-feud the PC can exploit · a hill/stone giant's raiding-ground · a deep-mine ooze/roper · an Old Iron fort node (Concord far-echo, M6) · a mercenary mutiny (morale).

## Rare Encounters
- An ettin or stone-giant boss · a wyvern's high-pass range · a chimera/manticore in the Teeth · an apex ore-construct in the deepest fort-vault.

## Elite / Boss Options
- **"Iron" Brask / warlord boss** (`BOSS_AND_APEX_THREATS.md` B11; warband + hold; single-combat & faction-play outs) · **hill/stone giant or ettin** (B12; tribute/avoidance/slowness outs) · a deep ore-construct (bound, watchword out).

## Noncombat Encounters
- A single-combat challenge instead of a battle · warlords' rival ambitions to play off · a giant's tribute-demand (pay or trick) · an unpaid mercenary willing to turn · a collapsed mine-shaft rescue · an ore-construct standing-down to the watchword.

## Social Encounters
- Warlord soldiers, ore-factors, mercenaries, miners, the Remnant expedition, rival warlords, pass-toll bands.

## Environmental Hazards
- Avalanche, rockfall, blizzard/exposure, mine collapse, fast mountain streams, thin air/cold at altitude, unstable old fortworks.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** raiders + cold; ambush in the passes. **Storm/blizzard:** the defining hazard — exposure, whiteout, avalanche. **Winter:** passes close; holds hunker; deadly travel.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- A frontier of *strength and deals* — single combat, tribute, and faction-play resolve warlord encounters without a party-mob. Giants are slow and territorial (out-think them). Bound mine-constructs don't pursue. Warlords prefer a useful living traveler to a corpse (morale/deals). Always a hold or mine to retreat to.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **9–11:** warlord tolls, single beasts/ogres, light mine-delving. **12–14:** giants, the Old Iron Forts, apex constructs, warlord-war stakes.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Petty warlord holds** (tolls, feuds, warbands — regional power). **Mercenary companies** (hire, mutiny). **Concord Remnant** (Old Iron fort expedition; M6). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M6 far-echo** at the Old Iron forts (the Concord was *continental* — peripheral works; far-proof, gated). Fragments only; `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`. (DM: a harsher *mirror* of the Reach — not the keystone.)
