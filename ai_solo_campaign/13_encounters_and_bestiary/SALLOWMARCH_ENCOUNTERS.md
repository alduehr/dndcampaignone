## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Sallowmarch Protectorate** — the fever-ridden Mardenflow delta of rice, reed, and smuggling under an absent southern crown. Biome: deltaic wetland + plague/swarm. Danger: high (Lvl 10–14) from disease, terrain, and the drowned dead. Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
A vast feverish reed-and-rice delta of smugglers, plague, and drowned causeways under a distant crown's thin hand — Lvl 10–14, wetland/disease danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A legate (Vorr Sallow) imposing the crown's claim · a smuggler turf-war · a ghast in a drowned shrine · an assassin-vine choking a channel · a plague-camp the player must navigate (moral choice).

## Rare Encounters
- A green hag's grief-bargain in the deep delta · a tidal aberration near the Drowned Steps approach · a shambling mound in the rice sallows · a banshee in a drowned hall.

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Tidal/drowned aberration** at the Sallowmarch Drowned Steps (`DROWNED_STEPS_ENCOUNTERS.md` / HORROR) · a Protectorate commander boss (Veteran/Knight + soldiers) · a delta-apex predator (giant crocodile, CR 5+).

## Noncombat Encounters
- A plague the PC can help or flee · two crocs contesting a kill · a sinking smuggler-boat (rescue) · a fever-camp's quarantine line · drowned-dead facing the sea (M5/M2 echo) · a healer begging medicine.

## Social Encounters
- Garrison soldiers, smugglers, marsh-healers, rice-villagers, the crown's legate, fen-witches (Mourners), foreign factors at the delta ports.

## Environmental Hazards
- **Disease** (the defining hazard — Con saves, exhaustion, fever), floods, sinking ground, fast channels, fog, heat, leeches/insects.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** drowned dead + fever-mist; navigation by water perilous. **Storm/flood:** the delta drowns; channels shift. **Heat/wet season:** disease spikes; the worst travel season.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- The delta itself is the antagonist — **disease, terrain, and getting lost** outrank monsters. Give the PC water-routes, local guides (Heron, Doll Fenn), and clear disease-saves with telegraphed onset. Drowned dead are bound to their water; predators flee on morale.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **10–12:** common rows; disease + single drowned dead; smuggler intrigue. **13–14:** the Drowned Steps approach; aberrations; tidal set-pieces.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Protectorate garrison** (checkpoints, conscription, crackdowns — regional power). **Smuggler networks** (jobs, turf-wars). **Mourners** (fen-witches; M5). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M5** (drowned dead; fen-witch songs), **M6** (the Drowned Steps — pre-Concord echo, far-proof; gated). Fragments only; `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`.
