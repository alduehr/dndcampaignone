## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Drowned Steps** — a submerged pre-Concord causeway-ruin running out to sea off the Sallowmarch's coast, toward "nothing anyone will name." Biome: submerged ruin / tidal / drowned causeway. Danger: high→very high (Lvl 10–16); the home of the campaign's **deepest aberration horror** (the deep, gated). A pre-Concord echo, NOT the keystone. Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
A tide-swallowed ancient causeway marching out to sea, where drowned dead and deep things wait — Lvl 10–16, tidal/aberration/deep-horror danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A deep-water aberration surfacing · a sea hag's drowned-bargain · a drowned-tide mass-haunt · a tidal aberration nest · a salvage-rival's claim-dispute.

## Rare Encounters
- A **Mind-fog Lurker / Memory-Eater** off the deepest steps (the deep horror; gated) · a kraken-spawn-tier aberration in the deeps · a drowned-causeway collapse set-piece · a pre-Concord "what marched out here, and why" truth (oblique, older fall).

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Tidal/drowned aberration** (HORROR; bound to the deep water) · the **Memory-Eater / Mind-fog Lurker** (`HORROR_AND_CURSE_THREATS.md` #10; **DM-gated, high tier** — a *time/memory* threat resolved by light/anchors/exit, not HP) · a drowned-dead host climax (mass; rite out).

## Noncombat Encounters
- Reading the tides to cross/retreat (the master key) · writing things down to resist the memory-fog (a literal solo tip) · drowned dead settling to a rite · two predators clashing · a salvager's safe-route · glyphs that *point home* (DM: corroborate Hollowmere, never relocate it).

## Environmental Hazards
- **The tide** (the defining hazard — flooding the causeway, drowning the careless), deep water/current, the memory-fog, slick drowned stone, sudden depth, sea-storm.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** drowned dead + memory-fog worst; the deep "wrong silence." **Storm:** the steps vanish; deadly. **High tide:** much of the causeway impassable (timing is everything). **Fog:** the memory-fog and sea-fog blur; getting lost = the deep horror.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- A **very high, optional** site — **the tide and the memory-fog** are the real threats, not HP. The deep horror has **no morale to break and re-forms** — so it is resolved by *light, anchors, and leaving*, never a slugfest (telegraph this hard). Gate the deepest steps to high tier. Drowned dead are bound and rite-resolvable. The safe-tide window is the ever-present retreat. **Not arc-required.**

## Level-Band Guidance
- **10–12:** salvagers, drowned dead, tidal timing, shallow steps. **13–16:** the deep steps, aberrations, the memory-fog/Memory-Eater (gated), pre-Concord far-proof.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Coastal salvagers** (safe-tide lore, leads). **Concord Remnant** (charting the causeway; M6). Sallowmarch-adjacent law (light). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`, `SALLOWMARCH_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M6 pre-Concord echo (gated):** the causeway is *older* than the Concord and marches toward "something" — corroborates the theme of buried, drowned truths by contrast. The **deep horror** is the closest the far-continent comes to the keystone's dread — but it is a *separate, older* thing; **DM: never make the Drowned Steps the endgame or the Court's seat.** Fragments only; `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`, `SECRET_PROTECTION_MATRIX.md`.
- **Horror additions (RtHW pass 2026-07-07; DM-GATED, tier 3–4 only):** the deep-cosmic terrors — **Mist Horror**, **Nightgaunt**, **Gug**, **Shoggoth**, **Greater Star Spawn Emissary** (all RtHW) — live *past the deepest steps*. Set-pieces only, heavily telegraphed, retreat-rich; they are the *older, alien* thing the causeway marches toward — **never** the harvest, the Court, or the apex answer. See `HORROR #H13`.
