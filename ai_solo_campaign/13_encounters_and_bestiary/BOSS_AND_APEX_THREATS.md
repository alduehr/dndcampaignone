## AI Use

The campaign's **elite / boss / apex threats**, organized by level band and region. Each boss is built for a **solo PC**: telegraphed in advance, with a signature mechanic (not three), a non-combat resolution, an escape route, and a defeat consequence. Recurring *villains* (the named human antagonists) are profiled in their NPC files and the faction files; this file covers their **boss-fight framing** plus the non-NPC apex creatures. Convert any party-balanced creature using `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md` Rule 7.

**Secrecy:** the Hollow Court Custodians (apex) are **DM-only / gated to REV_007 / endgame**. Do not deploy or foreshadow them as a "boss" prematurely; they are persons with an argument and a *decision*, not a slugfest.

## Boss Design Rules (every boss must satisfy these)

1. **Telegraphed before confrontation** — clues, sightings, reputation, signs (a "Clues Foreshadowing Them" line below).
2. **One signature mechanic**, supported by terrain — not a legendary-action gauntlet a lone PC can't survive.
3. **A non-combat resolution exists** — parley, weakness, rite, watchword, betrayal, alternate objective.
4. **An escape route exists** — the PC can always disengage; bound bosses can't pursue.
5. **A weakness the PC can discover and exploit** — found through scouting/investigation (rewards prep).
6. **Phases are breathers, not escalations** for a solo PC — a beat to reposition, not a second monster bolted on.
7. **Defeat ≠ campaign end** — failure converts to consequence (capture, retreat, a worsened clock), not instant death.
8. **Recurring villains never die cheaply** and have escape/return logic.

## Tier 1 Bosses — Levels 1–4 (Sundering Reach + Ring 1)

### B1. Cole Ashby, the Breaking (Gravecaller radical) — *recurring villain*
- **Role:** Act 1 antagonist; would shatter shrines to "free" the dead. **Profile:** `MAJOR_NPCS.md` (T1-lite, AC 13, ~30 HP) + recruits.
- **Signature:** zealotry + a Concord breaking-tool; turns a node hostile mid-scene.
- **Weakness:** he is *sincere and grieving* — Insight/Persuasion can split him from his recruits; exposing what breaking really does (a clue) can stop him.
- **Foreshadowing:** the Knock, Sister Knell's recruiting, fen graffiti, a stirred Remembrance.
- **Non-combat / escape / defeat:** talk him down, out-rite the node, or let him flee (he **returns** if not resolved — recurring). Defeat = the Breaking clock pauses, not ends.

### B2. The Reedwarden (Hewett Drane) — fen warlord — *recurring villain*
- **Role:** independent early antagonist controlling fen passage. **Profile:** `MAJOR_NPCS.md`.
- **Signature:** terrain mastery (the fen is his weapon — ambush, mire, fog).
- **Weakness:** his men follow coin and fear, not loyalty (morale-fragile); Old Sashe knows his routes.
- **Foreshadowing:** toll-demands, missing peat-cutters, his markers.
- **Non-combat / escape / defeat:** pay, outwit, turn his men, or route around him. He **flees by water** if losing; returns as a rival.

### B3. Mast-Beast Alpha (custom monstrosity) — Tollwood deep-wood apex
- **Type:** Monstrosity, Large, CR ~4. **Role:** territorial apex predator (a *warning*, not a quest-gate).
- **Abbreviated mechanics:** AC 14, HP ~75, Speed 40; multiattack maul (~2d8+4) + a fear-roar (Wis DC 13); ambushes from cover; **does not leave its range**.
- **Signature:** terror + the deep wood; it hunts the *unprepared*, not the careful.
- **Weakness:** territorial — respects the forest-rules; will not enter warded ground; flees fire.
- **Foreshadowing:** kills, claw-marked trees, Brann the Guide's warnings, the Mast-Beasts' Range marked off-limits.
- **Non-combat / escape / defeat:** avoid its range, travel with Brann, use fire/warded ground. **Solo: do NOT fight it head-on below L4.**

### B4. Drowned Choir-Keeper (Remembrance climax-dead) — node boss
- **Profile:** `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md` #2 (Ward/Choir-Keeper, AC 13, ~52–64 HP, bound).
- **Signature:** keening wail + summons one drifting dead; **bound to its relay**.
- **Non-combat / escape / defeat:** true rite (Religion DC 15–16), parley, the closing word; or simply leave (it can't follow). The first true "boss-feel" fight that is *also* fully avoidable.

## Tier 2 Bosses — Levels 5–8 (Caradril + Ring 1 deep, early Ring 2)

### B5. Quorrin Vane / Reclaimer leadership (Concord Remnant) — *recurring villain*
- **Role:** mid-game intellectual antagonist; wants the truth *for the Remnant*, not the player. **Profile:** `MAJOR_NPCS.md` (Tier-1 Mage/Archmage-like; never a cheap ambush).
- **Signature:** control magic + a Remembrance one-off + institutional power (he sends agents, not himself).
- **Weakness:** internal split (Lector Briss's conscience; Sub-Lectors who'd talk); he *can be allied with* — opposition is a choice.
- **Foreshadowing:** Reclaimer agents, the Sealed Archive, scholarly rivals, leaked Remembrances.
- **Non-combat / escape / defeat:** ally, out-maneuver, expose, or turn his subordinates. He **retreats to the Archive** and escalates institutionally if beaten.

### B6. Salt Syndicate boss ("Mother" Silque / Sorrel) — *recurring villain*
- **Role:** Caradril underworld apex. **Profile:** Caradril NPC files (Spy/Assassin-like; leverage over blades).
- **Signature:** the city itself — informants, ambush turf, the Sunken Wards; she fights with *leverage*, not a stand-up brawl.
- **Weakness:** she values the deal over the kill; rivals (the Hush, the Ledger) can be turned against her.
- **Non-combat / escape / defeat:** out-deal, out-inform, ally with a rival. Cornered, she **vanishes into the Wards** and returns.

### B7. Caradril Sunken Wards apex (custom) — Otyugh-/aberration-led flooded deep
- **Type:** Aberration/Construct mix by room; CR ~5–7. **Role:** dungeon climax of the city undercity.
- **Abbreviated mechanics:** the Wards' guardian-construct (Track-B, AC 16, ~95 HP, bound) or a flooded-deep aberration (AC 14, ~80 HP, grapple-drag); **bound to the deep**.
- **Signature:** the flooded dark + one big guardian; the *environment* (water, collapse, dark) is half the fight.
- **Non-combat / escape / defeat:** the watchword, Old Pater Dunk's route, retreat up the Sealgate. Bound — never pursues.

### B8. Wyvern / lesser drake (Track A, CR 6) — Highmark/Karran/Greatspine elite
- **Reskin notes:** a *territorial* aerial predator; telegraphed by kills and circling; flees when hurt (animal morale).
- **Signature:** flight + sting; the PC needs cover/terrain. **Non-combat:** avoid its hunting ground, travel under cover, drive it off rather than kill. **Escape:** it won't pursue far from its eyrie.

## Tier 3 Bosses — Levels 9–12 (Ring 2 / mid-continent)

### B9. Concord Construct-Guardian / Relay-Warden (custom construct) — node-vault boss
- **Profile:** `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md` #3 (AC 17, ~95–120 HP, bound, *watchword* stand-down).
- **Signature:** relay-blast + thinning aura; demands the watchword. **Two non-combat outs** (watchword; a Warden's stand-down). Bound to its vault. The model "fair solo boss": a postured sentinel, not a berserker.

### B10. Stone/Iron Golem-like deep guardian (Track A, CR 10–11) — Concord deep vaults
- **Reskin notes:** a Concord-built guardian of a sealed vault (Glassmere Reliquary, deep Heartlands).
- **Signature:** slow, immense, **bound**; the PC must out-think it (terrain, the closing word, an alternate route) more than out-hit it.
- **Non-combat / escape:** the watchword/closing word, route around it, leave (it won't follow).

### B11. Warlord "Iron" Brask / petty-warlord bosses (Karran) — *recurring regional villains*
- **Role:** Karran Marches frontier antagonists. **Profile:** `KARRAN_MARCHES_NPCS.md` (Veteran/Knight-like + warband).
- **Signature:** his warband (use the faction-encounter tables) + a fortified hold; he fights as a *commander*.
- **Weakness:** rival warlords, unpaid mercenaries (morale), the player playing factions off each other.
- **Non-combat / escape / defeat:** ally, bribe, turn his men, beat a champion in single combat (steppe/marches custom). Beaten, he **withdraws to a deeper hold**.

### B12. Hill/Stone giant or Ettin (Track A, CR 4–7) — Karran/Highmark/Heights elite
- **Reskin notes:** a *territorial* or *raiding* giant; telegraphed by tracks, smashed stone, local fear.
- **Signature:** reach + thrown rocks; needs cover. **Non-combat:** tribute, avoidance, a single-combat challenge, exploiting its slowness. **Escape:** giants tire of chasing.

## Tier 4 Bosses — Levels 13–16 (far-continent apex)

### B13. Caldera Guardian / Fire-Remembrance (custom) — Emberfell apex
- **Profile:** `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md` #7 (AC 15–17, ~80–140 HP, immune fire, **bound to the vault**).
- **Signature:** heat aura + fire-blast; the **descent's heat/gas/lava** is the real boss. **Non-combat:** quench/read the fire-wards (DC 15–16), the heretic-sage's aid, reverence. Gear/water-gated, telegraphed.

### B14. Concord Heartlands Ruin-Guardian (apex construct) — the Ruin'd Crown
- **Profile:** `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md` #3 apex-scaled (AC 18, ~150–200 HP, bound).
- **Signature:** apex relay-blast + bound-operator summons; demands the watchword. **Non-combat:** the watchword, a Warden/Remnant stand-down, an alternate route through the ruin. The surface-Concord's grandest guardian — **not** the keystone (do not let it pull the climax off Hollowmere).

### B15. Banshee / Wraith-lord haunt (Track A, CR 4–5 scaled) — Heartlands/old-hall apex
- **Reskin notes:** a Concord-era noble dead whose Remembrance soured; a *grief-boss* (HORROR roster).
- **Signature:** wail + drain; **resolvable by rite/answering the grievance**. The high-tier echo of the campaign's signature horror.

### B16. White / chromatic dragon, young (Track A, CR 8–10) — Highmark apex (rare)
- **Reskin notes:** the continent's rare true-dragon presence; a *legendary*-feel solo boss delivered as **one big creature** (per scaling Rule 2).
- **Signature:** breath + flight + frightful presence; lair effects stand in for "a party's worth" of pressure. **Non-combat:** tribute, riddle/bargain, avoid its peak. **Escape:** flee its territory; it defends, rarely pursues to the lowlands. **Convert HP down ~25–40% for solo (Rule 7).**

## Tier 5 — Levels 17–20 (Endgame)

### B17. Ancient dragon / continental apex creature (Track A, CR 16–20) — optional far apex
- **Reskin notes:** the optional non-arc apex (deep Highmark, the far north). One legendary creature = the whole boss (Rule 2). **Convert HP down for solo; keep ONE signature + lair effects.** Always a non-combat out (bargain, riddle, tribute, a shared enemy) and an escape.

### B18. Hollow Court Custodians (APEX — **DM-ONLY, GATED**)
- **Profile:** `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md` #8. **Never deploy before REV_007 / the endgame gate.**
- **The boss IS a decision, not a slugfest.** Veyl argues for a controlled restart; Maire offers the synthesis (REV_010); Orre fights. The **keystone heart** is the lever for every ending (seal / restart / seize / destroy / transform).
- **They persist** (standard death doesn't remove them) — *intentional and thematic*: you cannot simply kill the afterlife's accountants. The player should **always reach the decision if they reach the room**.
- **Non-combat spine:** the entire climax resolves by argument, allies, and discovered truths. Combat profiles exist (AC 16–18, ~150–220 HP, scale L16 vs L20) but are the *failure* path, not the intended one.
- **Defeat consequences / endings:** see `12_campaign_arc/` endgame states — seal (status quo held), restart (the harvest resumes controlled), seize (the player takes the lever), destroy (the afterlife breaks — dark), transform (Maire's synthesis — the hopeful path). **Every ending flows from the choices, not the dice.**

## Recurring-Villain Escape & Return Logic (summary)

| Villain | Returns if not resolved? | How they escape | How they escalate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cole Ashby | yes | flees the fen | the Breaking clock advances; more radical |
| The Reedwarden | yes | by water | tightens fen control; raids |
| Quorrin Vane / Reclaimers | yes | to the Sealed Archive | institutional pressure, agents, Archive lockdown |
| Salt Syndicate boss | yes | into the Sunken Wards | underworld reprisal, blackmail |
| "Iron" Brask / warlords | yes | to a deeper hold | warband raids, alliances |
| Hollow Court Custodians | **persist** (gated) | re-form unless keystone addressed | the harvest itself — the endgame |

Always record a recurring villain's escape and changed state in `FACTION_STATE.md` / `WORLD_CLOCKS.md` / `CONSEQUENCES.md`.
