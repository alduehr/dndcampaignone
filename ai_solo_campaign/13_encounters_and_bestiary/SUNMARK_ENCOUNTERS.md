## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Sunmark** — a warm southern land of sacred groves and grove-keeper tribes. Biome: warm forest / sacred grove. Danger: moderate (Lvl 8–13), gentler than most — **the campaign's hope-mirror**: living faith that keeps its dead well, the contrast to the harvest. Benign fey/celestial here. Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
Warm sacred groves where a living faith still tends the dead rightly — Lvl 8–13, gentle danger; the hopeful counter-image to the harvest.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A couatl-like grove guardian (benign warden) · a dryad's grove-bargain · a grove-tribe dispute (mediate) · a shambling mound in deep canopy · a pilgrim seeking the living faith (M5 contrast).

## Rare Encounters
- A Grove-Light / living-faith echo (the **hope-mirror** to the harvest dead — benign) · a celestial guardian (very rare) · an outsider's relic-raid the grove resists · a great awakened-tree set-piece.

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Grove-Warden Spirit** (`BESTIARY_INDEX.md`/SUNMARK; a *test of worth*, nonlethal — the boss you don't kill) · a couatl-like celestial guardian (benign unless the grove is violated) · a jungle-apex predator (CR 5+). **Note:** Sunmark "bosses" are mostly *trials and guardians*, not slaughters — the region's whole point.

## Noncombat Encounters
- A grove-warden's test (prove respect, not might) · the living faith's rite (the contrast clue — the dead *can* rest well) · two predators clashing · a pilgrim's hope · a celestial omen (atmosphere) · a grove-bargain (fair fey).

## Social Encounters
- Grove-keepers, sun-singers, plains-factors, grove-tribes, pilgrims, benign fey, festival-gatherers, outsiders seeking (or threatening) the groves.

## Environmental Hazards
- Heat/humidity, canopy-mires, river-fords/floods, venomous flora, getting lost in dense canopy, sudden monsoon.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** predators active; the groves "wake" (benign-strange). **Storm/monsoon:** floods, dense rain. **Festival season:** gatherings = welcome + opportunity. **Dry season:** easier travel.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- The **gentle region** — danger is mostly *tests, predators, and terrain*, not slaughter. Grove-wardens and celestials are *benign unless provoked*; respect is the key, not combat. This is a deliberate **rest-and-hope beat** in a grim campaign — let the player breathe here, and seed the synthesis (the dead *can* be kept well). Always a grove-camp to retreat to.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **8–10:** grove-keepers, single predators, benign tests, the green roads. **11–13:** the Great Grove, grove-wardens, celestial omens, the living-faith node (synthesis-seed).

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Grove-keeper tribes / living-faith circles** (the region's whole society — hospitable, protective of the groves). No harvest-factions hold ground here (a contrast). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M5 contrast + REV_010 synthesis-seed (DM-gated):** the Sunmark's living faith keeps its dead *well* — proof the harvest is not the only way; the seed of the *hopeful ending*. The Great Grove is a **living-faith node**, not a Concord node, not the keystone. Hopeful-echo fragments; `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`. (DM: this region argues *for* the synthesis ending — handle with care, don't over-explain.)
