## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Highmark Passes** — the cold far-northern barrier range where the oldest Concord works and the most isolated survivors lie. Biome: high mountain / frozen pass. Danger: high (Lvl 12–16), the continent's rare **dragon and frost-giant** country. The Frozen Works hold the **clearest far-proof** of the harvest (gated). Optional late frontier — barrier, not arc-required. Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
The cold roof of the continent — frozen passes, the oldest Concord works, frost giants, and rare dragons — Lvl 12–16, frost/giant/dragon danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A frost giant's territory (warning) · an ice-construct guardian (Frozen Works) · a chimera/manticore in the peaks · a snowbound hold under siege (rescue) · a high-pass toll by survivor-folk.

## Rare Encounters
- A young white/chromatic dragon's range (rare apex) · a frost-giant raid · the Frozen Works' clearest-proof inscription (huge M6; DM-gate apex implications) · an ancient creature stirring (optional far apex).

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Frost giant** (`BOSS_AND_APEX_THREATS.md` B12-scaled; tribute/terrain/single-combat outs) · **young dragon** (B16; convert HP down for solo, ONE signature + lair, bargain/avoid outs) · the Frozen Works ice-construct (bound, watchword) · optional **ancient dragon** (B17) for L17+ far-apex.

## Noncombat Encounters
- Reading the weather to dodge a blizzard · a frost-giant's tribute-demand (pay/trick) · a dragon's bargain or riddle · a survivor's lore (the *clearest* far-proof — DM points it home) · two predators clashing · an ice-construct standing-down to the watchword.

## Environmental Hazards
- **Killing cold, blizzard, avalanche, crevasse, whiteout, thin air** (the defining hazards), ice-collapse, frozen ruin-traps, no shelter/fuel.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** lethal cold + predators. **Blizzard:** the defining hazard — exposure, whiteout, avalanche, getting lost. **Winter:** passes effectively closed; suicide travel. **Clear days only** offer real movement.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- An *optional high-tier frontier* — **cold and altitude** are the primary killers; gate the deep passes behind cold-protection and timing (telegraphed). Giants are slow (out-think); dragons get the solo-boss treatment (one big creature, HP cut, non-combat out — Rule 7/Rule 2). Bound constructs don't pursue. Always a hold or cave-shelter as a retreat. **Not arc-required** — the player can skip the whole region.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **12–14:** scouts, single predators/wyverns, the Frozen Works approach, frost-dead. **15–16:** frost giants, young dragons, the Works' deep proof. **17+:** optional ancient-creature far apex.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Scattered isolated holds** (wary, toll, survival-folk). **Concord Remnant** (Frozen Works expedition — the clearest far-proof; M6). **Far survivors** (old strange knowledge). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.
