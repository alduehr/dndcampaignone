## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Cindern Waste** — an ash-and-stone badland in the Emberfells' rain-shadow, with old buried Concord works beneath and the fire-priests forbidding its depths. Biome: ash badland / waste. Danger: high (Lvl 13–16) from heat, ash, and ash-dead. A desolate **M6 far-proof** site (buried works). Closely tied to the Emberfell; this file covers the *waste itself*. Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
A baked ash-and-stone waste over buried Concord works, forbidden by the fire-priests — Lvl 13–16, heat/ash/undead danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- An ash-fiend (cult-summoned heresy) · a fire elemental loosed from below · a desiccated/ash-dead host · a forbidden-waste cult-cell · a buried-cache worth a fortune (and a curse).

## Rare Encounters
- The Cindern Buried Works guardian roused · an ash-bound mass-undead · a great fire/ash-storm set-piece · a deep ash-horror (rare) · the clearest *Emberfell-region* far-proof of the works (gated).

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Cindern Buried Works guardian** (`STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md` #3/#7-style, bound; ward-reading out) · an ash-bound dead host (mass; rite + dousing) · the ash-storm/heat as an **environmental boss** (the waste itself).

## Noncombat Encounters
- Timing/routing around ashfall and heat · reading the buried-works wards (Arcana/Concord Script) · ash-dead settling to a rite + water · a scavenger's map to the works · a pyre-warden turned aside by piety/the heretic-sage · a mirage to see through.

## Environmental Hazards
- **Extreme heat, ashfall, ash-storms, no water, burning ground, gas pockets, sinkholes in ash, mirage** — the defining and constant threat. Gate the deep waste hard behind water + heat-protection (telegraphed).

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** ash-dead active; sharp cold-after-heat; navigation by stars. **Ash-storm:** the defining hazard — lungs, vision, getting lost. **Midday:** killing heat + glare; undead amplified. **Dry always:** water is the campaign here.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- The **waste is the antagonist** — heat, ash, thirst, and getting lost outrank monsters. Telegraph hard; gate the buried works behind survival resources. Ash-dead are slow and rite-resolvable; the theocracy forbids the depths (a *reason* to be careful, and a non-combat out via piety/the heretic-sage). Bound guardians don't pursue. Few retreats — make the scavenger-camps and ash-road edges count.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **13–14:** scavengers, pyre-wardens, single ash-dead, the waste-edge. **15–16:** the Buried Works, ash-bound hosts, guardians, mass-undead, ash-storm set-pieces.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Emberfell theocracy** (forbids/patrols the deep waste — the *reason* it's dangerous-and-secret). **Scavengers** (salvage, leads). **Concord Remnant** (chasing the buried works; M6). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`, `EMBERFELL_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M6 far-proof (gated):** old buried Concord works lie beneath the ash — corroboration of the harvest's continental reach. **DM:** a desolate far-echo, NOT the keystone; the theocracy's forbidding *masks* pre-Concord/Concord works, not the endgame. Fragments only; `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`.
