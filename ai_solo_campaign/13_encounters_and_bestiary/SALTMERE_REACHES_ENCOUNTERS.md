## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Saltmere Reaches** — a dying brackish inland-sea basin of salt-clans and drowned towns, an isolating fallen realm. Biome: salt sea / salt flats (Bonepan). Danger: high (Lvl 11–15) from heat, desiccated dead, and clan feuds. The drowned towns are a **pre-Concord echo** (an older drowning). Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
A shrinking brackish sea ringed by salt-flats, salt-clans, and drowned towns where the dry dead wake — Lvl 11–15, heat/undead danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A salt-clan blood-feud (mediate/blood-price) · a salt-mummy host in a drowned town · a buried salvage-cache · a brine-flooded ruin (ooze) · a mirage that lures off the safe path.

## Rare Encounters
- A drowned-town mass-haunt (HORROR #8) · a salt aberration in the deepest brine-ruin · a great salt-wind elemental · a "what went down here" pre-Concord truth (older fall; oblique).

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Desiccated-dead / salt-mummy set-piece** (`HORROR_AND_CURSE_THREATS.md` #5/#8; rite + fresh-water outs) · a **Salt-Stalker** apex (monstrosity, CR 5; avoidance/terrain) · a salt-clan war-leader boss (Veteran + clan; single-combat/blood-price outs).

## Noncombat Encounters
- Guest-right at a hold (custom is law) · two scavengers fighting over salvage · a mirage to see through (Wis) · desiccated dead settling to a rite + water-offering · a clan-feud to mediate · a drowned-town surfacing (a clue, not a fight).

## Social Encounters
- Salt-clan matriarchs, drowned-town delvers, salt-caravan masters, salt-mothers (Mourners), feuding clans, salvagers.

## Environmental Hazards
- **Heat, thirst, salt-glare** (the defining hazard), mirage, brine-quicksand, sinkholes in dried lakebed, salt-storms, no fresh water.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** desiccated dead active; cold-after-heat; navigation by stars. **Midday:** glare + heat peak; undead amplified. **Salt-storm:** vision + skin/lung hazard. **Dry season:** the sea recedes, exposing more drowned towns (and more dead).

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- **Water and heat** are the real antagonists — gate the flats behind water-management (telegraphed). Desiccated dead are slow and rite-resolvable; salt-clans run on guest-right/blood-price/single-combat (non-combat spine). Mirages and the salt-stalker reward caution. Always a hold or oasis-spring to retreat to.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **11–13:** clan-tolls, single desiccated dead, flats-crossing. **14–15:** the Deep Towns, salt-mummy hosts, the salt-stalker, mass-haunts.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Salt-clan holds** (tolls, feuds, guest-right, blood-price — regional power). **Mourners** (salt-mothers; M5). **Salvage networks** (drowned-town delving). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M5** (the dry dead won't rest; salt-songs), **M6 pre-Concord echo** (the drowned towns "went down in an older fall" — corroborates the theme by contrast; far/older, NOT the keystone). Fragments only; `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`.
