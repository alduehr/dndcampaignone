## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Concord Heartlands / the Ruin'd Crown** — the shattered surface seat of the old Custodian Concord, now contested ruins everyone covets. Biome: vast ruin-field astride the Greatspine. Danger: very high (Lvl 13–17). The richest **M6 far-proof** in the world — *and the place most likely to wrongly pull the climax off Hollowmere.* Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

**DM CRITICAL:** the Heartlands are the **surface** Concord heart — **NOT** the keystone, the Concord Deep, or the Hollow Court's seat (those are *beneath Hollowmere*). The Heartlands corroborate the truth; they do not contain the endgame. Never relocate the climax here. See `FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md` §11.

## Region Identity
The Concord's fallen capital country — endless contested ruins, postured guardians, and every faction's covetous expedition — Lvl 13–17, ruin/construct/faction danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A Ledger-vs-Remnant grab over a great relic · a banshee/grief-haunt in a Concord noble hall · a golem-like deep guardian · a pilgrim-camp schism (moral choice) · a Pass-Warden (Brask Tarn) toll on the Crown Road.

## Rare Encounters
- The apex Heartlands Ruin-Guardian roused (`BOSS_AND_APEX_THREATS.md` B14) · a chimera/manticore in the broken passes · a Custodian-era memory-echo replaying the Concord's fall (huge M6; DM-gate the *apex* implications) · a Reclaimer breakthrough that reframes the mystery.

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Apex Concord Construct-Guardian / Ruin-Guardian** (`STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md` #3 apex; AC 18, ~150–200 HP, bound, watchword out — B14) · a golem-like vault guardian (B10) · Hollin Vane / Reclaimer leadership as an *intrigue boss* (the truth, on their terms — ally or oppose, not a default fight).

## Noncombat Encounters
- A watchword that stands a guardian down · the deep-ruin guide's safe route · a memory-echo replaying the fall (witness/record — M6) · rival expeditions to play off · a pilgrim's plea · an inscription that *corroborates Hollowmere* (DM: points home, never relocates the climax).

## Social Encounters
- Reclaimer lords, scavenger war-captains, heretic-priests, deep-ruin guides, pilgrim-camp factions, rival expeditions, Pass-Wardens, Ledger agents.

## Environmental Hazards
- Ruin collapse, Greatspine pass weather/avalanche, unstable vaults, trap-warded chambers, thinning-aura zones (thin-touch), dead-ground where rites fail.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** Concord dead + constructs active; ruin-cold. **Storm:** Greatspine passes deadly. **Winter:** passes close; expeditions trap. **Fog:** lost in the endless ruin-field; ambush.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- A *very high* tier — gate the deep ruins behind language/watchword/allies (telegraphed). Bound guardians never pursue; the watchword is the master non-combat key. Factions prefer the player as ally/rival to a corpse. Pilgrim camps and scavenger-towns are rest-points. The horror is **bound, retreat-rich, and resolvable** — never a no-out gauntlet.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **13–15:** expeditions, single guardians/dead, ruin-crawl, Crown Road politics. **16–17:** the apex Ruin-Guardian, the Ruin'd Crown delve, mystery-climax-scale M6 far-proof.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Concord Remnant / Reclaimers** (expedition-lords; the truth-race — strongest here; M6/M9-adjacent). **Scavenger-towns** (terms, war-captains). **Cinder Ledger** (relic-grabs). Pilgrim/heretic camps. See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M6 (primary, climax-scale far-proof):** the Concord spanned the continent; its guardians, script, and stored Remembrance are everywhere here. **M9-adjacent (DM-ONLY, gated):** Reclaimer lords chase the apex truth — but the *answer* is at Hollowmere, not here. **NEVER** surface the Court/keystone/Under-Shrine as Heartlands content.
