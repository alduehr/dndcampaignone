## AI Use

Abbreviated, D&D 5e/2024-compatible adversary profiles for the **Stage 12 dungeons** (`DUNGEON_INDEX.md`). These extend the regional encounter tables (`SUNDERING_REACH_ENCOUNTERS.md`, `CARADRIL_ENCOUNTERS.md`) with the new dungeon-specific foes. Per `RULESET_ASSUMPTIONS.md`, these are **reference-style profiles** ("use a [baseline]-like creature, modified as follows") — not copied stat blocks. All are **solo-tuned**: single foes or small groups, telegraphed, with morale, escape, and non-combat outs. Scale by PC level per each profile's notes. Where an adversary already exists in the regional tables, that entry is referenced, not duplicated.

## Solo-Play Reminders (apply to all)

- Telegraph every dangerous foe before initiative.
- Most "dead" foes resolve by **true rite (Religion DC 13-16)** or **parley (Insight/Persuasion DC 12-16)** — combat is optional.
- Single foes or small groups vs. a solo PC; never a party-mob without allies/terrain/escape.
- Bound guardians **cannot pursue** beyond their chamber — retreat is always an out.
- Recurring named NPCs (Veska Dunn, Cole Ashby, Hollin Vane, etc.) never die cheaply.

## 1. Drowned / Drifting Remembrance (the woken dead)

*Used in: Sunken Wards Deep (D16), Basin Keystone Approach (D20), Greyfens Deep (D19), and most node sites.* The campaign's signature undead — leakage of the reawakening harvest, drawn to the nodes. **Not evil; mournful, drawn, dangerous in number.**

- **Type/Size:** Undead, Medium (incorporeal). **Role:** clue-bearer / mournful threat. **CR-equivalent:** ~1-3 (scales).
- **Baseline:** use a **Specter-like** (weak) to **Wraith-like** (strong) profile.
- **AC** 12-13 · **HP** 17 (weak) / 22-30 (standard) / 36-40 (wrathful) · **Speed** fly/hover, move through water and stone.
- **Saves/Skills:** Wis-leaning; Stealth.
- **Resist/Immune:** resistant to nonmagical physical; immune poison, exhaustion; the usual incorporeal-undead conditions.
- **Senses:** darkvision. **Languages:** understands Reach Common (rarely speaks).
- **Actions:** *Chill/Life-drain Touch* (1d6+2 to 2d6 necrotic; standard has no multiattack); a strong one may *drain Remembrance* (recharge).
- **Traits:** *Bound to the node/water* (cannot leave its site); *Telegraphed* (cold, weeping, grey light, dimming lanterns before it acts).
- **Tactics:** drifts, grieves, defends; attacks if attacked or if its relic/node is taken.
- **Morale:** begs release when reduced or out-argued.
- **Non-combat outs:** true rite (Religion DC 13-14); parley (Insight/Persuasion DC 12-13); naming the dead; simply withdrawing.
- **Solo danger:** low (single) to moderate (provoked group). **Scaling:** L3 ~17 HP; L5-7 wraith-like ~36 HP; groups only with a mass-rite/retreat out.

## 2. Ward-Keeper / Choir-Keeper Remembrance (node climax-dead)

*Used in: Sunken Wards Deep Cistern (D16), Basin Keystone upper-works (D20).* A stronger Remembrance bound to a warming relay — a *guardian*, not a slayer.

- **Type/Size:** Undead, Medium (incorporeal). **CR-equivalent:** ~5-8 (scales).
- **Baseline:** **Wraith/Ghost-like**, upgraded.
- **AC** 13 · **HP** 52-64 (D16 ~52-60; D20 choir-keeper ~64) · **Speed** fly/hover, move through water/stone.
- **Actions:** *Life-drain Touch* (2d6 necrotic + drain); *Frightful Presence* (Wis DC 14); *Withering/Keening Wail* (Con DC 14, 3d8 necrotic/cold, recharge); *Summon 1 drifting dead* (1/short).
- **Traits:** *Bound to the relay/choir* (no pursuit); resistant to nonmagical physical; *Telegraphed*.
- **Tactics:** challenges intruders; defends the relay; summons when pressed.
- **Morale:** released gratefully if rituals'd/parleyed; cannot leave.
- **Non-combat outs:** true rite (Religion DC 15-16); parley (Insight/Persuasion DC 15-16); the "closing word" (Concord Script DC 16, D20).
- **Solo danger:** high; bound and retreat-rich. **Scaling:** L6 ~44-48 HP; L8 as written; L10 ~64-72 HP + summon each round.

## 3. Concord Construct-Guardian (Relay-Warden / Crown Ruin-Guardian)

*Used in: Concord Relay-Vault (D18), Old Concord Heartlands Ruin (D21 — apex-scaled).* An animated Concord guardian of stone and stored Remembrance — a **postured sentinel** demanding the watchword, not a berserker.

- **Type/Size:** Construct, Large. **CR-equivalent:** ~7-11 (D18) / ~14-17 (D21 apex).
- **Baseline:** use a **Stone Golem-like** profile with a Remembrance-relay twist (lower HP, ranged blast, an aura).
- **AC** 17 (D18) / 18 (D21) · **HP** 95-120 (D18) / 150-200 (D21) · **Speed** 30 (cannot leave its vault).
- **Saves/Skills:** Str/Con-leaning; immune to most mind-effects (construct).
- **Resist/Immune:** resistant to nonmagical physical; immune poison, psychic, charm, fright, exhaustion.
- **Senses:** darkvision, tremorsense in its vault. **Languages:** understands Concord Script/Old Custodial; responds to the watchword.
- **Actions:** *Slam* (multiattack, ~2d8+5 bludgeoning); *Relay-Blast* (ranged, ~6d6 force/necrotic, recharge 5-6).
- **Traits:** *Thinning Aura* (Con DC 14-15 within 10 ft or thin-touch); *Bound to the vault* (no pursuit); *Watchword* (stands down if the correct Concord challenge-response is spoken).
- **Tactics:** challenges; demands the watchword; attacks only the forceful or the silent; defends the inner vault.
- **Morale:** halts when the intruder leaves or speaks the watchword (not "morale" so much as programming).
- **Non-combat outs:** *Watchword* (Concord Script DC 15); a Warden's "stand-down" (Religion/faction DC 14); a Mourner's star-lore (D18).
- **Solo danger:** high (D18) / very high (D21); bound and retreat-rich; two non-combat outs. **Scaling:** L7 ~80 HP, no first-round blast; L10 ~120 HP + bound-operator summons; D21 apex ~150-200 HP.

## 4. Concord Sentinel-Scholar / Reclaimer Agent (living Remnant)

*Used in: Sunken Wards Deep (D16), and Remnant-contested sites generally.* Remnant agents guarding their secret works — **prefer to turn intruders back, not kill.**

- **Type/Size:** Humanoid, Medium. **CR-equivalent:** ~3-6.
- **Baseline:** **Spy-like** (the muscle) and **Mage-like** (the scholar), modified.
- **AC** 13-14 · **HP** 40-60 · **Speed** 30.
- **Saves/Skills:** Int/Dex-leaning; Arcana, Investigation, Stealth, Deception.
- **Actions:** weapon (~1d8+2); the mage-type casts *Hold Person, Counterspell, Mage Armor, Misty Step*, and a **Remembrance one-off** (a relic-powered single effect — a fear-burst, a binding, a memory-strike).
- **Traits:** *Turn-back protocol* (they warn, then escalate; they want the player gone, not dead); *Concord Script reader*.
- **Tactics:** warn -> Hold/control -> withdraw the player; lethal only if cornered.
- **Morale:** disengage and report if outmatched.
- **Non-combat outs:** social (turn, bribe, talk past); faction standing; the player offering to *help* the Remnant.
- **Solo danger:** moderate. **Scaling:** L6 single Spy-type; L10 Spy + Mage pair with a Remembrance one-off.

## 5. Salt Syndicate Runner / Strong-Arm (living criminal)

*Used in: Sunken Wards Deep (D16), Ashmarket Undercroft (D17).* City underworld muscle — **catch-and-intimidate, not kill.**

- **Type/Size:** Humanoid, Medium. **CR-equivalent:** ~1/2-3.
- **Baseline:** **Thug-like / Bandit-like** (runners), **Spy-like** (a lieutenant).
- **AC** 13-14 · **HP** 16-45 · **Speed** 30.
- **Actions:** weapon (~1d6+2 to 2d6+3); grapple + threats.
- **Traits:** *Catch-and-eject* (prefer to march intruders to the boss/Watch over killing in the city).
- **Morale:** break/flee at half HP or when their goal fails.
- **Non-combat outs:** bribe, avoid, social, the Hush's intel.
- **Solo danger:** low-moderate. **Scaling:** more bodies / a Spy lieutenant at higher levels.

## 6. Will-o'-Wisp-like Luring Light (fen hazard)

*Used in: Greyfens Deep (D19).* A luring light over deadly ground — a **hazard**, not a boss.

- **Type/Size:** treat as a **Will-o'-Wisp-like** hazard. **CR-equivalent:** ~2 (rarely fought).
- **Effect:** Wis DC 13 to disbelieve/ignore; following it leads to sinking ground/deep water (Survival DC 13 / Athletics DC 15).
- **Non-combat outs:** disbelieve, ignore, Old Sashe negates.
- **Solo danger:** moderate (the *ground* it lures you onto, not its attacks). Treat as environmental.

## 7. Caldera Guardian / Fire-touched Remembrance (volcanic apex)

*Used in: Emberfell Caldera Descent (D22).* The fire-warded vault's keeper — fire-elemental or fire-touched dead.

- **Type/Size:** Elemental or Undead, Large/Medium. **CR-equivalent:** ~11-15.
- **Baseline:** **Fire Elemental-like** (guardian) or **Wraith-like with fire** (Remembrance), modified.
- **AC** 16-17 (guardian) / 15 (Remembrance) · **HP** 110-140 (guardian) / 80-100 (Remembrance) · **Speed** 40 / fly-hover.
- **Resist/Immune:** immune fire; the guardian is vulnerable to cold; resistant to nonmagical physical.
- **Actions:** *Magma-Grip / Burning Touch* (fire damage + ongoing); *Heat Aura* (Con DC 15 within 10 ft, fire damage); *Fire-Blast* (ranged, recharge).
- **Traits:** *Bound to the vault* (no pursuit); the descent's **heat/gas/lava** is the real threat (see D22 hazards).
- **Non-combat outs:** quench/read the fire-wards (Survival/Arcana/Concord Script DC 15-16); the heretic-sage's aid; reverence (Remembrance).
- **Solo danger:** very high (environmental + guardian); gear-gated, telegraphed, bound. **Scaling:** L12 ~90 HP; L16 ~140 HP + summons.

## 8. Hollow Court Custodian (APEX — DM-ONLY)

*Used ONLY in: The Under-Shrine Approach (D23).* **DM-ONLY. Never deploy before the Act 4-5 endgame gate (REV_007).** The surviving Custodians, persisting as preserved Remembrance — the campaign's apex antagonists, **persons with an argument**, not monsters.

- **Type/Size:** Undead (preserved Remembrance), Medium. **CR-equivalent:** apex (~16-20).
- **Baseline:** a unique **Lich-like / high Wraith-like** profile, heavily modified — but **the climax is a decision, not a slugfest** (see D23).
- **AC** 16-18 · **HP** 150-220 (Orre the hawk highest; Veyl high; Maire avoids combat) · **Speed** fly/hover, move through stone/water.
- **Resist/Immune:** **standard "death" does not remove them** — they persist as Remembrance; resistant/immune to most physical; high mental resilience. *(This is intentional and thematic: you cannot simply kill the afterlife's accountants — a clue and a problem.)*
- **Actions:** *Memory-Strike* (psychic + memory loss flavor); *Unmaking Touch* (high necrotic); *Frightful Presence* (Wis DC 17); *Command the gathered dead* (summon); Veyl/Orre have apex control/area effects.
- **Traits:** *Cannot easily act in the living world* (why they used cutouts — relevant to how the player reached them); *Persistence* (defeated in "combat," they re-form unless the keystone is dealt with).
- **The real mechanics — the choice:** Veyl argues for a controlled restart; Maire offers the synthesis (REV_010); Orre fights. The keystone heart is the lever for every ending (seal/restart/seize/destroy/transform). **The player should always reach the decision if they reach the room.**
- **Non-combat outs:** the entire climax can be resolved by argument, allies, and the discovered truths — the intended spine.
- **Solo danger:** lethal (endgame); but decision-centered, with the gate above preventing premature arrival. **Scaling:** adjust Custodian combat profiles for L16 vs L20; the decision-spine is constant.
