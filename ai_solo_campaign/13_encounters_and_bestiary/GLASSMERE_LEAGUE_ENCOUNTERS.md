## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Glassmere League** — the great river-cities on the Glasswater, the "real" urban Orrun past Caradril and the **Ledger's true heartland**. Biome: city/urban + great river. Danger: moderate, but mostly *social/intrigue/financial* (Lvl 9–13). Violence is leverage and the underworld, not random. Roll d10 or pick; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
Wealthy free-city league on the continent's main river — banks, guilds, spies, and Remnant's grandest chapter — Lvl 9–13, social/intrigue danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A Ledger-vs-Holt bank-war maneuver · a Reclaimer trying to acquire a continental relic · a poor-district (Lowwater) unrest beat · an assassin retained by a rival house · a Concord-script broker (M6 fragment).

## Rare Encounters
- A Reliquary archive break (Remnant secrets at stake) · a vault construct roused under the Three Bridges · a banshee/grief-haunt in an old guild-hall · a continental power's envoy.

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Reliquary golem-like guardian** (`BOSS_AND_APEX_THREATS.md` B10; bound) · a Ledger bank-heist vault construct (Three Bridges Counting-Deep) · the Glass Ear's spy-network as an "intrigue boss" (no fight; leverage).

## Noncombat Encounters
- Reading the market floor (insight) · a clerk who'll leak ledgers · two spy-networks shadowing each other (play them off) · a Mourner procession (M5) · a relic that "remembers" on an auction block (M3) · a debtor begging mediation.

## Social Encounters
- Factors, bankers, spies, guild-masters, Reclaimer scholars, urban Mourners, foreign traders, debtors and reformers (Lowwater).

## Environmental Hazards
- River flood at the wharves, a counting-house fire, a crowd-crush, a collapsing old bridge, a poisoned cup (intrigue).

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** the Glass Ear and Syndicate-adjacent crime active; ambush in alleys. **Storm/flood:** the Glasswater rises into Lowwater. **Festival season:** crowds = both cover and risk.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- Glassmere is a **safe-rest hub** like Caradril: danger is exposure, debt, and the underworld, not random violence. A lone PC thrives on social/stealth play; combat is telegraphed reprisal. Allies are everywhere for hire/alliance.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **9–11:** social/intrigue common rows; light underworld. **12–13:** the Reliquary/Three Bridges delves; elite constructs; bank-war stakes.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Cinder Ledger** (true heartland — strongest presence; banks, directorate, rivals). **Concord Remnant** (grand Reliquary chapter; M6). **The Hush**-style brokers (the Glass Ear). **Mourners** (urban grief, M5). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M3** (the Ledger's continental scale; relics that remember), **M6** (Reliquary archive; the Concord was continental — far-echo), **M5** (urban Mourner songs). Fragments only; `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`.
