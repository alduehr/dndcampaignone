## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Wender Steppe** — a cold open grass-sea ridden by horse-clans the Concord never tamed. Biome: cold steppe/grassland. Danger: moderate (Lvl 8–13) from raiders, wind, and exposure. Note: the steppe is **outside the Concord's whole history** — its sites (Sky-Stones) give an *outside view* / cosmic-echo, not a Concord node. Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
A wide cold grass-sea of horse-clans, raiders, and ancient non-Concord shrines under an enormous sky — Lvl 8–13, raider/exposure danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A clan blood-feud (mediate or pass) · a kin-matriarch's hospitality-test · a harpy-like cliff-lurer at a rock outcrop · a buried raider-cache · a steppe-monstrosity's range (warning).

## Rare Encounters
- A great wind-elemental set-piece · a sky-omen creature (rare) · the Sky-Stones' guardian-spirit (an *outside-view* echo, strange not hostile) · a unifying raid-host (Summer-Riding).

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Raid-Lord Borr / a raid-host boss** (`BOSS_AND_APEX_THREATS.md`; Veteran/Knight + warband; single-combat out) · a great air-elemental (CR 5+, bound to a storm) · the Sky-Stones set-piece (non-combat cosmic-echo, a *test* not a kill).

## Noncombat Encounters
- A hospitality-test (guest-right is law) · a wind-singer's vision (a clue about the dead/the world — an *outside* perspective) · two raid-bands clashing (slip past) · a foundered traveler in a storm · a horse worth winning/racing · a single-combat challenge instead of a battle.

## Social Encounters
- Clan outriders, speakers, wind-singers, raiders, kin-matriarchs, trade-meet settlers, horse-traders. **Guest-right and single combat are the cultural keys** — use them as the primary non-combat outs.

## Environmental Hazards
- Steppe storms, whiteout, exposure (cold + wind), prairie fire, getting lost on the featureless plain, sudden flash-floods in gullies.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** raiders strike; exposure deepens; vast dark. **Storm:** whiteout/exposure — the defining hazard. **Winter:** the clans gather (Cold Springs); travel deadly. **Wind always:** ranged combat and navigation penalties.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- The steppe is governed by **custom, not law** — guest-right, gifts, and single combat resolve most encounters without a battle. Raiders want horses/tribute, not a fight to the death (morale). The *weather and distance* are the real threats. Always a clan-camp or trade-meet within reach.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **8–10:** outriders, single beasts, hospitality-tests. **11–13:** raid-hosts, great elementals, the Sky-Stones, Summer-Riding stakes.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Steppe clan confederacy** (guest-right, tolls, single combat — regional power). **Raid-bands** (tribute, horses). No Concord factions hold ground here (the steppe was never tamed). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M5/M6 outside-view echo** only: the Sky-Stones and wind-singers offer a *non-Concord perspective* on the dead and the world — a thematic mirror, **not** a Concord node and **not** the keystone. Cosmic-echo fragments; `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`. (DM: keep the steppe genuinely *outside* the harvest — it corroborates by contrast.)
