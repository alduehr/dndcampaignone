## AI Use

Encounter types and creatures that **carry mystery clues** — the encounters that, when they happen, deliver a breadcrumb toward the campaign's mysteries (the legacy M1–M9 set and the REV_001–REV_010 revelation map). Use this so a "random" encounter can also *advance an investigation*. Every entry says which mystery it feeds and what fragment it delivers — **the fragment, never the whole truth**.

**Secrecy:** the apex truth (the harvest, the Hollow Court, the keystone, the Under-Shrine) is **DM-only** and gated. These encounters deliver *oblique, partial* clues; the AI DM must not connect the dots for the player or reveal the answer before its REV gate. Cross-check `SECRET_PROTECTION_MATRIX.md` for safe vs unsafe wording.

## Mystery-Encounter Tables

### M5 — "Why the dead won't rest" (the most common; Tiers 1–2)

The signature horror encounters *are* the M5 clue stream. Every Remembrance-dead encounter can carry one of these fragments:

| d6 | Encounter | Fragment delivered (oblique) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A lone Remembrance facing the basin/keystone | "they all face the same way" (direction = a clue) |
| 2 | The Drift-Line Chorus | many dead, one direction; mass-grief (C-M5 field-proof) |
| 3 | A dead person "returned wrong" (Wrong-Come-Back) | the dead come back *incomplete* — something is missing/taken |
| 4 | A grieving family + a dead who won't settle | the rites used to work; lately they fail more |
| 5 | An eldest Mourner's old song | a half-memory of "the grey lords who took the dead before the roads fell" |
| 6 | A memory-echo haunt replaying a death | a glimpse of *the moment something was taken* |

- **Solo:** all resolvable by rite/parley; deliver one fragment, don't lecture. **Feeds:** REV_001.

### M2 — "Something under the basin" (Tiers 1–3; place-based)

| d6 | Encounter | Fragment delivered |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Surfacing shapes through thinning fog (basin shore) | there are *structures* down there |
| 2 | Concord Script on a surfacing ruin | language-gated fragment (C-M2-1) |
| 3 | Lantern Shallows / leakage glow at night | something *active* leaks from below |
| 4 | A node site warming/relay-humming | the network is *waking* |
| 5 | A Warden refusing access "it's not for the living" | the seal protects something deliberate |
| 6 | Dread from the deep water (the Court stirs) | **atmosphere only — never the Court at the shore** |

- **Solo:** investigation/observation, no combat required. **Feeds:** REV_002. **Gate:** keep the *what* hidden; deliver only the *that something is there*.

### M3 — "Who profits from the relics" (Tiers 1–3; faction-based)

| d6 | Encounter | Fragment delivered |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A Ledger bidding war vs the Remnant over a relic | relics are worth *killing-level* money |
| 2 | "Quiet-coin" / "saint's-bones" / "sea-relics" trade | the relics are sold as something they're not |
| 3 | A turnable Ledger clerk + ledgers | a paper trail of the relic monopoly (C, M3/M6) |
| 4 | A relic that "remembers" (the vault relic) | the relics *hold* the dead |
| 5 | A caravan guarding a strange cargo | the trade is organized, continental (Glassmere heartland) |
| 6 | An eviction/drainage crew at a node | someone is *clearing the way* to the relics |

- **Solo:** social/investigation. **Feeds:** REV_003. **Gate:** the player can learn the *monopoly*; the *harvest* behind it is later.

### M4 — "Who really runs the frontier" (Tiers 1–2; intrigue)

| d6 | Encounter | Fragment delivered |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Councilor Reke steering the player wrong | a "helpful" official whose advice always protects the basin |
| 2 | A corrupt ruling favoring the Ledger | the council is *bought* somewhere |
| 3 | A records-clerk's paper-trail anomaly | decisions trace to one hidden hand |
| 4 | An honest reeve frustrated by interference | someone above keeps quashing inquiries into the basin |
| 5 | A cutout passing instructions | orders come from someone *off-stage* (never named as the Court) |
| 6 | Sefra Quick relaying a "client's" job | the fixer doesn't know who she really works for |

- **Solo:** social/intrigue. **Feeds:** REV_004. **Gate:** the *infiltration* can surface; the *Court* cannot, yet.

### M6 — "What the Concord really was" (Tiers 2–4; research/ruins)

| d6 | Encounter | Fragment delivered |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Concord Script / Old Custodial inscription | language-gated history fragment (C-M6) |
| 2 | A Construct-Guardian demanding the watchword | the Concord built *guardians* to keep people out |
| 3 | The Sealed Archive gate (Remnant) | the Remnant *hides* the worst of the truth |
| 4 | A Warden seal-rite witnessed | the order's whole purpose is *holding something paused* |
| 5 | A far ruin (Heartlands/Reliquary/Highmark) corroborating | the Concord spanned the *whole continent* |
| 6 | A node's relay-choir / stored Remembrance | the Concord *stored people* — the heart of M6 |

- **Solo:** research/exploration, language-gated. **Feeds:** REV_005/006. **Gate:** the *order's purpose* surfaces over the mid-game; the *Court still living* is endgame.

### M9 — The endgame truth (Tier 5; **DM-ONLY, GATED to REV_007**)

- **No random encounters deliver M9.** It is reached only through the gated dungeon spine (Basin Keystone Approach → Under-Shrine) and the Custodian decision (`BOSS_AND_APEX_THREATS.md` B18). Any earlier "endgame" foreshadowing is **atmosphere only** (the deep dread, the Court stirring) and must never name the Court, the keystone-as-machine, or the harvest's full mechanism.
- **Feeds:** REV_007/010. **Never deploy before its gate.**

## Mystery-Encounter Solo Rules

- **Deliver one fragment per encounter**, in the fiction, then move on. Don't stack revelations.
- **Three-clue redundancy:** every mystery has ≥3 independent paths (`REVELATION_MAP.md`); a missed encounter never blocks the mystery.
- **Never connect the dots for the player.** Present the clue; let them reason (`CLUE_DELIVERY_PROTOCOL.md`).
- **Update `KNOWN_CLUES.md`** when a fragment is genuinely discovered; keep undiscovered fragments in `HIDDEN_CLUES.md`.
- **Gate the apex.** M9 and the Court are off-limits until REV_007. Earlier "endgame" beats are dread-atmosphere only.
