## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Emberfell Theocracy / the Ashfast** — a fire-and-ash theocracy reading the volcanoes as divine, guarding old buried works. Biome: volcanic highland. Danger: high (Lvl 12–16) from fire, the theocracy, and ash-dead. The Emberfell has its own pre-Concord secrets (a thematic mirror, not the keystone). Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
A volcanic theocracy of fire-priests, ash-roads, and buried works — Lvl 12–16, fire/theocracy danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A fire elemental loosed from a vent · an ash-fiend (cult-summoned heresy) · a theocracy purge of heretics (moral choice) · a Remnant agent chasing the buried works (M6) · a magma mephit swarm.

## Rare Encounters
- A red dragon's range (rare apex) · the Caldera Guardian roused · a great fire-elemental set-piece · an ash-bound dead host (mass-undead).

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Caldera Guardian / Fire-Remembrance** (`STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md` #7; bound, fire-ward outs) · the Cindern Buried Works guardian · the Ash-Hierophant (Vole Cindra) as a *theocratic* boss (Priest/cult-army; piety/heretic-sage outs, not just a fight) · a young red dragon (rare).

## Noncombat Encounters
- Reading fire-wards to pass a vault (Arcana/Concord Script) · the heretic-sage's aid against the dogma · a lava flow to time/route around · a heresy-victim to save · ash-dead settling to a rite + dousing · a pilgrim's vision of the fire-god (M6 mirror).

## Social Encounters
- Pyre-wardens, ember-sages (heretics), pilgrims, cinder-masters, the Ash-Hierophant's court, mining crews, heresy-cells.

## Environmental Hazards
- **Fire/heat everywhere** — lava, gas vents, ashfall, ash-storms, eruption tremors, no shade/water, burning ground. This is the defining threat; gear/water-gate the deep areas.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** ash-dead active; vents glow; cold-after-heat exposure. **Ash-storm:** the defining hazard — vision, lungs, navigation. **Eruption season:** tremors, gas, closed roads. **Heat always:** Con saves, water as a resource.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- The *environment* is the antagonist — fire, gas, ash, heat outrank monsters. Gate the deep works behind fire-protection gear/lore (telegraphed). The heretic-sage is a key ally; the theocracy prefers conversion/trial to slaughter (a non-combat spine). Bound guardians don't pursue. Always an ash-road waystation to retreat to.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **12–14:** pyre-wardens, single fire-dead, ash-roads, heresy intrigue. **15–16:** the Caldera Descent / Cindern Buried Works, the Hierophant, red dragon, apex guardians.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Emberfell theocracy** (pyre-wardens, pilgrim-tolls, heresy-trials, fire-rites — regional power). **Heretic-scholars** (forbidden lore; M6 allies). **Concord Remnant** (chasing the buried works). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M6 mirror/far-echo:** the Emberfell guards its *own* pre-Concord buried works and reads death/fire its own way — a **thematic mirror** of the harvest, not the keystone. Cindern Buried Works = far-proof of the Concord's continental reach (gated). Fragments only; `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`.
