## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Hollow Gulf Ports** — rival warm-water southern port city-states facing the Calm Reach, gateway to the wider world (Surren). Biome: open sea/gulf + bustling ports. Danger: moderate-high (Lvl 10–15) from sea, pirates, and coastal dead. Mix of maritime combat and port intrigue. Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
A string of rich, rival warm-water ports and the pirate-shadowed sea-lanes between them — Lvl 10–15, maritime danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A Ledger maritime arm muscling the relic-trade · a port-lord (Doss Saltgate) rivalry the PC can exploit · a wreck-salvage dispute · a water elemental in a squall · a letter-of-marque legal tangle.

## Rare Encounters
- A drowned-tide mass-haunt off a wreck-reef · a deep-water aberration in the gulf deeps · a pirate-fleet blockade · a foreign warship (world-horizon set-piece).

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Sea aberration** at the Wreck-Reef (`DUNGEON_ENCOUNTER_SUPPORT.md`; HORROR) · a pirate-lord boss (Veteran/Assassin + ship + crew) · a sea-storm/shipwreck set-piece (environmental boss).

## Noncombat Encounters
- Reading the weather to dodge a storm · a foundering ship to rescue · two pirate crews fighting (slip past) · drowned dead facing seaward (M5/M2 echo) · a foreign sailor's tale of distant lands (world-horizon clue) · a smuggler's offer.

## Social Encounters
- Port-mistresses, privateers, foreign factors, sea-Mourners, rival port-lords, dockworkers, smugglers, sailors with world-rumors.

## Environmental Hazards
- Sea-storms, drowning, reef-wrecks, ship fires, false-light wrecking, gulf currents, tropical heat in port.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** wreckers' false lights; drowned dead; navigation risk. **Storm:** the defining sea hazard — shipwreck, drowning. **Fair season:** open lanes; storm season closes them. **Fog:** reef-collision + ambush.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- At sea, the PC needs a ship/crew (an ally-resource) — frame pirate/sea encounters around the *vessel*, with parley/bribe/port-law outs. Drowned dead bound to wrecks; predators flee on morale; storms are *decisions* (run, ride out, reroute). Ports are safe-rest hubs.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **10–12:** port intrigue + single sea threats. **13–15:** pirate-fleet stakes; the Wreck-Reef; deep-water aberrations; world-horizon (Surren) opens.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Port city-states** (rival officials, law). **Privateers/pirates** (tolls, boardings, hire — regional power). **Cinder Ledger** (maritime relic-trade). **Mourners** (sea-Mourners; M5). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M5** (drowned dead; sea-songs), **M3** (maritime relic-trade), **M6** faint far-echo at the Wreck-Reef; plus **world-horizon** (Surren tales). Fragments only; `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`.
