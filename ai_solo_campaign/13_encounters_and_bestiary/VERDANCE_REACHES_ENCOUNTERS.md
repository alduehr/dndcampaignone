## AI Use
Solo-tuned encounter tables for the **Verdance Reaches** — the river-road corridor SE of the campaign cluster, the player's *first step into the wider continent*. Biome: river/wetland + settled river-towns. Danger: moderate (Lvl 8–12). Mostly social/trade/intrigue with river hazards; the first Ring-2 Concord node (Nine Locks) points *home* to the keystone. Roll d10 or pick; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`; always offer a non-combat path.

## Region Identity
River-corridor of locks, barge-towns, and petty lords up the Verdance toward richer Orrun — Lvl 8–12, moderate danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- A Factor-General Crane (Ledger) proposition with strings · a rival barge-line feud · a wight in a flooded riverside crypt · a ferry-wreck salvage (clue) · a Compact-militia muster against river-bandits.

## Rare Encounters
- The Nine Locks node "warming" (Concord network waking; M2 far-echo) · a banshee in a drowned hall · a water elemental at a ruined weir · a Reclaimer grand-expedition passing up-river.

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Lesser Construct-Guardian** at the Nine Locks Sunken Stair (`STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md` #3; bound, watchword out) · a river-warlord boss (Veteran/Knight + barge-crew) · a flooded-deep aberration in the deepest lock-vault.

## Noncombat Encounters
- Drowned-tide signs on a falling lock · two predators fighting over a kill (bypass) · a wounded barge-horse/ox (dilemma) · a territorial croc's warning · a node's relay-hum heard from the water · a lock-keeper's family in debt to the Ledger (moral choice).

## Social Encounters
- Ledger factors, river-captains, petty-lord soldiers, pilgrims to the interior, smugglers running the locks, a Mourner eldest with old river-songs (M5).

## Environmental Hazards
- Spring floods, broken locks, fast current (drown risk), river-fog, fever near stagnant backwaters, a collapsing tow-path.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** river-dead active; current harder to read. **Storm:** flood + lightning on open water. **Fog:** lost on the locks; ambush risk. **Season:** spring flood is the defining hazard.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- River travel favors *avoidance* — the PC can often pass a danger by water. Predators flee on morale; lords/factions prefer toll/escort to a fight. Bound node-guardians never pursue. Always a barge or bank to retreat to.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **8–10:** common rows; single foes; node is "warming," not yet hostile. **11–12:** uncommon/rare; the Nine Locks delve; elite construct.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Cinder Ledger** (heartland-adjacent; strong here — convoys, debt, directorate). **Concord Remnant** (node-mapping). **Reachward Compact** (thin frontier law). **Mourners** (river grief-songs, M5). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M2** (Nine Locks node points home to the keystone — far-echo, gated), **M5** (river-dead), **M6** (Remnant node-mapping). Deliver fragments only; see `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`.
