> **Secrecy note (DM):** This file is **mixed** — the biome ecology is player-safe, but the gated "presences," "deep horror," and "harvest-leak / harvest-touched" overlays are DM-side placement guidance. Keep gated apex creatures and the harvest term off the player's table until earned in play.

## AI Use

A fast **biome → creature** lookup so the AI DM never places an ecologically wrong creature. Find the biome the PC is in, get the creature types that belong there, then pull the specific creature from `BESTIARY_INDEX.md` and the tactics/solo notes from the regional file. This enforces the campaign's ecology rules. Creatures appear where they make sense; the Concord/Remembrance dead and constructs overlay *any* biome that holds a Concord site.

## How To Use

1. Identify biome (terrain the PC is actually in, not just the region label).
2. Read the **Typical**, **Uncommon**, and **Rare/Apex** columns.
3. Note the **always-possible overlay**: Concord constructs + Remembrance dead at any Concord-touched site; humanoid faction agents on any travelled route.
4. Pull specifics from `BESTIARY_INDEX.md`; tune for solo with `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Always-Possible Overlays (any biome)

- **Concord overlay:** at any Concord-touched site (roads, nodes, ruins, shrines — and these thread the *whole* continent), add **Remembrance dead** and **Concord constructs** regardless of biome. This is the campaign's unifying ecology.
- **Faction overlay:** on any travelled route or near any settlement, **humanoid faction agents** (patrols, enforcers, scouts, cultists, smugglers) are always plausible — see `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.
- **Curse overlay:** near any harvest-leak (nodes, drift-lines, failed funerals), add the curses/afflictions from `HORROR_AND_CURSE_THREATS.md`.

## Ecology Don'ts (quick rejects)

- No dragons/giants in the lowland fen/farmland/city (they live in mountains/wastes).
- No fire elementals outside volcanic/waste biomes (and the rare summoned one).
- No celestials outside Sunmark (and the very rare omen).
- No fey in cities or open salt flats (they belong to old forests/groves/strange fen).
- No "deep horror" aberrations outside the Drowned Steps and deepest nodes (gate them).
- No undead in living farmland/grove *except* at burial/cursed/harvest-touched sites.
