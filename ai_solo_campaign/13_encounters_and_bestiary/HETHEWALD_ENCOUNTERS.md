## AI Use
Solo-tuned tables for the **Hethewald Free Holds** — reclusive forest-folk and outlaw holds along the eastern Hethe river; the Tollwood writ large. Biome: vast old-growth forest + river. Danger: moderate (Lvl 7–12) from predators, fey, outlaws, and deep-wood "presences." Deep-wood ruins (Old Holds) are gated. Roll d10; tune per `SOLO_ENCOUNTER_SCALING.md`.

## Region Identity
A vast eastern old-growth forest of free-holds, river-tollers, fey, and deep Concord ruins — Lvl 7–12, forest/outlaw/fey danger.

## Uncommon Encounters
- An owlbear-like deep-wood apex (warning) · a green hag's grief-bargain in a fen-grove · an outlaw-hold blood-feud · a Remnant party probing the Old Holds (M6) · a will-cluster luring off-path.

## Rare Encounters
- The Old-Wood Presence stirring (a deep-wood landmark-power; gated, NOT a god) · a banshee in a drowned Concord hall · a displacer/blink-dog fey hunt · a hold-war erupting.

## Elite / Boss Options
- **Old-Wood Presence / deep-wood apex** (`HORROR_AND_CURSE_THREATS.md` fey-presence + `BOSS_AND_APEX_THREATS.md` B3-style; gated, avoidance via forest-custom) · an outlaw river-lord boss (Bandit-captain + crew) · an Old Holds construct/dead climax.

## Noncombat Encounters
- Forest-custom (offer at the shrines, never cut marked trees — keeps the presence asleep) · two predators contesting a kill · a fey bargain (fair) · a lost child/hold-feud to resolve · Concord dead facing one way (M5) · a guide (forest-elder) offering safe passage (solo-safety).

## Social Encounters
- Hold-speakers, forest-mothers (Mourners), outlaw tollers, reclusive woodfolk, Remnant probers, fey (bargains), river-traders.

## Environmental Hazards
- Getting lost in the deep wood, river fords/floods, deadfalls, mires, fey-touched paths that mislead, deep-wood cold/dark, wolf/bear country.

## Travel Variants
- **Night:** predators + fey + Concord dead; getting lost. **Storm:** river floods, deadfall risk. **Fog:** the wood "moves"; ambush + fey lures. **Deep season (winter):** holds close; the wood is hungry.

## Solo-Play Adjustments
- The **deep wood itself** is the danger — getting lost, fey misdirection, and the gated Old-Wood Presence. **Forest-custom is the master non-combat key** (respect it, keep the presence asleep, travel with a guide). Outlaws prefer toll to a fight; fey want bargains, not blood; predators flee on morale. Always a hold or shrine to retreat to.

## Level-Band Guidance
- **7–9:** holds, tollers, single predators/fey, shallow wood. **10–12:** the Old Holds, deep-wood presence (gated), Concord dead, hold-war stakes.

## Faction-Linked Encounters
- **Forest free-holds** (passage, custom, feuds — regional power). **Outlaw river-tollers** (Hethe tolls). **Mourners** (forest-mothers; M5). Fey (bargains). See `FACTION_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Mystery-Linked Encounters
- **M5** (Concord dead at ruin-edges; forest-mother songs), **M6 far-echo** (the Old Holds — deep Concord ruins; the Concord reached the eastern forests; gated). Deep-wood "presences" are **landmark-powers, NOT the Hollow Court or new gods.** Fragments only; `MYSTERY_ENCOUNTERS.md`.
- **Horror additions (RtHW pass 2026-07-07):** a **Bodytaker Plant** and its **Podlings** (RtHW) in the deepest wood near the Old Holds — the "wrong returned" as flora; a woodcutter who came back *not quite right* is the telegraph. See `HORROR #H12`.
