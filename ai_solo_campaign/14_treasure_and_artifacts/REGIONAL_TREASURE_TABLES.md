## AI Use

The **ambient treasure layer**: what a solo PC plausibly finds, loots, or buys in each of Orrun's 20 regions, by level band. Use to dress hoards, fill pockets, and give each region a *material identity*. Coin/gem values follow `ECONOMY_AND_PRICING_GUIDE.md`; pacing follows `SOLO_REWARD_BALANCE.md`. Named magic items referenced here are detailed in `MAGIC_ITEM_INDEX.md`; consumables in `CONSUMABLES_AND_MINOR_MAGIC.md`.

## How to Roll a Regional Hoard (quick method)

1. Pick the region's flavor row (below) for *what the treasure looks like*.
2. Pick coin/gems by **level band** (the universal bands, next section).
3. On a notable site/boss, add the region's signature minor-magic or a consumable.
4. Reskin generic value into regional flavor (a "100 gp gem" in the Marrowdowns is a chalk-carved ancestor-token; in Glassmere it's a cut-glass bead).

## Universal Level-Band Bands (coin, gems, magic frequency)

| Band | Loose coin per notable hoard | Gem/art objects | Magic frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1–4** | 1–50 gp (often cp/sp) | 0–2 × 10–25 gp | Common items + 1 consumable per dungeon; 1 Remembrance-relic *fragment* as plot |
| **5–8** | 50–500 gp | 1–3 × 25–250 gp | Uncommon per site; 1–2 Rare by band's end; faction-favor tokens |
| **9–12** | 500–5,000 gp; letters of credit | 2–4 × 100–500 gp | Rare/Very Rare at apexes; first artifact fragments/cursed examples |
| **13–16** | 5,000–50,000 gp equiv.; imperial relics | 3–5 × 250–1,000 gp | Very Rare common; 1–2 Legendary gated; faction-defining relics |
| **17–20** | massive historical hoards | art-object hoards | Legendary multiples; endgame artifacts (M6–M9 gated) |

**Remembrance relics** (whole, verified) are worth 2,500 gp+ to the right buyer (Ledger, Remnant, Gravecallers) — but *selling* them is a faction-charged choice, never a clean payday (`ECONOMY_AND_PRICING_GUIDE.md`, `FACTION_REWARDS.md`).

## The 20 Regional Flavor Rows

> Each row: **band tilt** (where the region sits on the level curve) · **mundane look** · **signature gems/art** · **signature minor-magic/consumable** · **special relic note.**

### Home Region + Ring 1 (levels 1–7)

**1. Sundering Reach** (L1–6; keystone region)
- *Mundane:* broken heirlooms, frontier tools, old road relics, Warden rite-tools, peat-cutter gear, Concord road-tokens.
- *Gems/art:* memory-touched "old glass" (10–50 gp; some are raw Remembrance relics in disguise), pewter pilgrim-tokens.
- *Signature magic:* Warden's Road-Token, Mourner's Quiet Bell, The Reedwalker's Pole, Coldhearth Charcoal-Ward (Tollwood edge). Consumables: shrine-water, Warden salve.
- *Relic note:* the Reach is where Remembrance-relic *fragments* first appear as plot items — handle per `SOLO_REWARD_BALANCE.md` (clue, not power; Thin-touch risk).

**2. Ashgarden Vale** (L1–6; south)
- *Mundane:* pressed-flower grave-goods, orchard-shrine tokens, agricultural relics, hedge-charm pouches, cider-seals.
- *Gems/art:* carved-wood saint-tokens (10–25 gp), "saint's-bone" reliquaries (really raw relics — see `CURSED_ITEMS.md` #5).
- *Signature magic:* Pilgrim's Dry-Boots, Orchardmere Wake-Garland; Whisper-Stone (Candlewick edge). Consumables: pilgrim's-blessing draught, hedge-poultice.
- *Relic note:* the Vale's denial means relics are sold *as faith* — a recurring M3 temptation/trap.

**3. Tollwood** (L2–7; east)
- *Mundane:* toll-charms, road-tokens, forest contraband, bandit caches (Tollmen), bark-script messages, charcoal-burner tools.
- *Gems/art:* fey-touched carved wood (25–100 gp), antler-and-amber pieces.
- *Signature magic:* Tollwood Bark-Charm, Coldhearth Charcoal-Ward, The Toll-Keeper's Chain (deep-wood, gated). Consumables: forest-rules offering-bundle, hunter's tincture.
- *Relic note:* deep-wood treasure is *gated* behind the Old Mast "bargain" — taking it carelessly stirs the presence.

**4. Pale Coast** (L2–7; west)
- *Mundane:* salt-silver coin (salt-marks), shipwreck salvage, drowned-shrine offerings, sea-cursed jewelry, net-and-tackle.
- *Gems/art:* stormglass (volcanic sea-floor glass; 25–100 gp), pearls, coral.
- *Signature magic:* Salt-Marked Cloak-Pin, Wrackmouth Diving-Mask, Lantern of the Held Breath (Rare), The Drowned Flame (relic). Consumables: salt-and-tide rite-kit, breath-draught.
- *Relic note:* sea-relics are the Ledger's coastal monopoly arm (M3); the drowned-tide makes wreck salvage dangerous.

### Caradril (levels 5–12)

**5. Caradril (city-state)** (L5–12; mid-game hub)
- *Mundane:* guild charter-marks, noble-house seals, legal writs, civic medals, black-market relic fragments, Ledger letters of credit.
- *Gems/art:* Caradril civic medals (250 gp), Glassmere-imported glasswork (500 gp), quiet-coin relics (black market).
- *Signature magic:* Ledger Clerk's Tally-Glass, Compact Writ-Cylinder, The Honest Coat (Rare), Custodian's Quiet-Step Boots (Very Rare, Sunken Wards). Consumables: city apothecary potions, Ashmarket scrolls.
- *Relic note:* the city is where relics are *traded in daylight* — the densest legal-and-illegal relic market; selling here pulls Ledger/Remnant/Hush attention.

### Ring 2 + Far Continent (levels 8–20)

**6. Verdance Reaches** (L8–12; SE corridor)
- *Mundane:* living-wood carvings, bloomcraft items, lock-stair iron, river-trade ledgers, waterproof cases.
- *Gems/art:* bloom-amber (100–500 gp), river-pearl.
- *Signature magic:* Verdance Bloom-Splint (Rare; recovery). Consumables: bloom-tonic, lock-oil.

**7. Glassmere League** (L8–13; rich river-cities)
- *Mundane:* glasswork tools, mirror-magic items, trade-ledger codes, water-clarity instruments, lens arrays.
- *Gems/art:* cut Glassmere glass (250–1,000 gp), banking-seal cameos.
- *Signature magic:* Glassmere Clarity-Lens, Glasswater Mirror-Shard (Very Rare). Consumables: clarity-draught, scrying-ink.

**8. Marrowdowns** (L8–14; chalk downs)
- *Mundane:* barrow grave-goods, ancestor-tokens, chalk-carved bone, warding-mark stones, burial-mound silver.
- *Gems/art:* carved ancestor-bone (50–250 gp), barrow-silver torcs.
- *Signature magic:* Marrowdowns Ancestor-Ring (Rare; anti-death). Consumables: barrow-ward chalk, grave-bread.
- *Relic note:* pre-Concord barrows — treasure here is *old*, sometimes guarded by barrow-dead (a pre-Concord echo, not a node).

**9. Sallowmarch** (L9–14; deltaic wetland)
- *Mundane:* plague-ward charms, marsh-medicine vials, quarantine seals, delta-reed instruments, bog-preserved leather.
- *Gems/art:* bog-amber with preserved things inside (100–500 gp).
- *Signature magic:* (Coast-class: Saltmere Desiccant Case fits; marsh-healer kits). Consumables: fever-draught, quarantine-incense, antitoxin (plentiful here).

**10. Hollow Gulf Ports** (L9–15; rival sea city-states)
- *Mundane:* maritime charts, salvage-permits, pearl/shell/reef items, letters of marque, naval signal-codes.
- *Gems/art:* deep-sea pearls (250–1,000 gp), reef-coral art.
- *Signature magic:* Hollow Gulf Reef-Charts (Rare). Consumables: privateer's tonic, signal-flares.

**11. Wender Steppe** (L9–15; nomad confederacy)
- *Mundane:* horse-culture tack, sky-reading tokens, nomad bone-craft, clan-seals.
- *Gems/art:* sky-stone beads (50–250 gp), worked-bone clan-art.
- *Signature magic:* Steppe Wind-Chime Compass. Consumables: ride-tonic, wind-reader's salt. (The Sky-Stones are a *non-Concord* holy site — its "treasures" are sacred, not for sale.)

**12. Karran Marches** (L10–16; warlord marches)
- *Mundane:* old iron weapons, fort relics, military heraldry, siege-craft tools, border-lord trophies, ore-samples.
- *Gems/art:* warlord trophies (100–500 gp), Old Iron heraldic plate.
- *Signature magic:* Karran Border-Iron Brand (Uncommon), Highmark-class martial gear. Consumables: battle-stimulant, whetstone-oil.
- *Relic note:* the Old Iron forts are peripheral Concord ruins (echoes, not nodes) — relic salvage draws far-region Reclaimers.

**13. Emberfell / Ashfast** (L11–16; volcanic theocracy)
- *Mundane:* fire-hardened tools, ash-glass items, theocracy penance-tokens, sun-disk medals, ember-relics, volcanic obsidian.
- *Gems/art:* fire-opal and obsidian art (250–1,000 gp), penance-medals.
- *Signature magic:* Emberglass Brand-Bottle (Rare). Consumables: fire-resist unguent, ember-coal (everlasting heat).

**14. Saltmere Reaches** (L10–16; salt-clan holds / fallen realm)
- *Mundane:* salt-preserved goods, drowned-town salvage, desiccation-resistant cases, clan-saltmark seals.
- *Gems/art:* salt-crystal art (50–250 gp), drowned-town silver.
- *Signature magic:* Saltmere Desiccant Case (Rare; relic-transport). Consumables: salt-ward, preserved rations.
- *Relic note:* the Drowned Towns are *pre-Concord* submerged ruins — old salvage, a "the world drowned before" echo (never the keystone).

**15. Concord Heartlands / Ruin'd Crown** (L11–17; fallen imperial ruin)
- *Mundane:* imperial Concord relics, Script-carved stones, memory-artifact fragments, Reclaimer expedition gear.
- *Gems/art:* imperial Concord regalia (500–2,500 gp), Script-cameos.
- *Signature magic:* The Unforgotten Mail (Very Rare), The Ash-Crown Fragment (Very Rare relic). Consumables: Reclaimer's preservative, Script-ink.
- *Relic note:* **surface** imperial treasure — corroborates the Concord's scale (M6/M9), **never** the harvest-heart. The richest mundane-relic region; the most faction-contested.

**16. Hethewald** (L10–16; forest free-holds)
- *Mundane:* old hold treasures, clan-oath rings, carved standing-stone fragments, forest-pact tokens, deep-wood amber.
- *Gems/art:* deep-wood amber (100–500 gp), oath-ring silver.
- *Signature magic:* (Tollwood-class wood-charms scaled up). Consumables: hold-mead (heroism-flavor), forest-poultice.
- *Relic note:* the Old Holds are deep-wood Concord ruins (forbidden by the free-holds) — gated, echo-only.

**17. Sunmark** (L9–15; sacred grove tribes)
- *Mundane:* bright-metal solar items, grove-pilgrim tokens, living-faith charms, jungle-amber, sun-singer instruments.
- *Gems/art:* sun-bright bronze art (100–500 gp), jungle-amber.
- *Signature magic:* Sunmark Sun-Disk Pendant (Uncommon; anti-undead). Consumables: sun-draught, grove-balm. (The Great Grove's "treasures" are sacred/living-faith — a hopeful contrast; not for plunder.)

**18. Highmark Passes** (L12–16; far-north mountains)
- *Mundane:* mountain-iron (superior), giant-made tools, frost-preserved relics, dragon-adjacent trophies, pass-toll tokens.
- *Gems/art:* frost-clear gems (250–1,000 gp), giant-worked silver.
- *Signature magic:* Highmark Giant-Forged Maul (Very Rare). Consumables: cold-ward draught, climbing-oil.

**19. Cindern Waste** (L11–16; ash badland)
- *Mundane:* ash-glass survival gear, ember-lanterns, ruin-salvage tools, fire-resist charms, cinder-black iron.
- *Gems/art:* ash-glass curios (50–250 gp), buried-works relic fragments.
- *Signature magic:* (Emberfell-class fire gear). Consumables: ash-mask (breathe in ashfall), fire-resist unguent.
- *Relic note:* the Buried Works are ash-drowned Concord echoes — corroboration only.

**20. Drowned Steps** (L13–17; submerged sea-causeway)
- *Mundane:* aquatic salvage, storm-glass, drowned-offering urns, tidal-clock instruments, ruin-diving gear.
- *Gems/art:* deep-pearl and drowned-silver (250–1,000 gp).
- *Signature magic:* The Drowned Crown-Arc (Very Rare). Consumables: deep-breath draught, pressure-ward.
- *Relic note:* a *pre-Concord/echo* submerged ruin — the deepest "the world drowned before" motif; high-tier diving treasure, never the keystone.

## Selling Treasure (regional buyers, quick table)

| Treasure type | Best buyer | Catch |
|---|---|---|
| Mundane salvage/gems | local merchants, Glassmere/Caradril markets | poor regions pay little |
| Concord salvage (mundane) | Cinder Ledger, Concord Remnant | Ledger lowballs; Remnant wants *records* not coin |
| Remembrance relics (raw/whole) | Ledger, Remnant, Gravecallers, Ashmarket fences | **faction-charged**; Mourners/Wardens disapprove; can mark you |
| Imperial Concord regalia | Remnant, ambitious magisters, far collectors | draws Reclaimer/Court attention |
| Pre-Concord barrow/drowned goods | collectors, scholars, museums | sometimes cursed/haunted |

See `ECONOMY_AND_PRICING_GUIDE.md` for prices and `FACTION_REWARDS.md` for who pays in *favor* rather than coin.
