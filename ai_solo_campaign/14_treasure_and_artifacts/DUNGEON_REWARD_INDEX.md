## AI Use

For each of the **36 authored adventure sites** in `../10_dungeons_and_ruins/`, this file names: a **signature reward** (the notable item/cache that makes the site worth delving), the **typical mundane treasure by level**, any **special quest-reward trigger**, and any **cursed/dangerous item** present. **MIXED SECRECY** — DM-only relic truths are flagged. Pull the full site (occupants, hazards, retreat options) from its own file; this is the treasure layer only.

**Solo rule:** every site offers a notable reward, but **retreat with partial reward is always possible** — a lone PC who scouts, grabs the prize, and leaves should be rewarded for prudence (`SOLO_REWARD_BALANCE.md`). Not every room is loot.

## Legend

`Site (L band)` — **Signature:** the headline reward · **Mundane:** band treasure · **Trigger:** special reward condition · **Danger-item:** cursed/risky loot present (if any).

## Sundering Reach (home region, L1–9)

- **The Peat Chapel (L1–3)** — **Signature:** the **Grief-Glass** may be placed here (Rare relic; M2/M5) *or* a relic fragment + Mourner favor. **Mundane:** broken grave-goods, 1–25 gp, shrine tokens. **Trigger:** completing the rite cleanly → Mourner standing. **Danger-item:** raw relic fragment (Thin-touch risk).
- **The Sunken Tollhouse (L2–4)** — **Signature:** a Concord road-token cache + a **Whisper-Stone**-class clue relic (M2 node-proof). **Mundane:** drowned boatmen's coin, 5–50 gp, toll-ledgers. **Danger-item:** leaking relic (Thin-touch).
- **The Deep Adit (Harrowgast, L3–6)** — **Signature:** verified **"old glass"** Remembrance-relic samples (M3 commercial proof) + Heights gems. **Mundane:** mining silver, 25–250 gp, ore. **Trigger:** beating the Remnant (Veska Dunn) to it → Remnant rivalry or a deal. **Danger-item:** secondary-node relics (Thin-touch; the node "warms").
- **The Whispering Cairn (L2–5)** — **Signature:** a Concord archive-relic + Script-fragment (M2/M6 lead); a **Script-Reader's Chalk** cache. **Mundane:** cairn grave-silver, 25–100 gp. **Danger-item:** a clinging Remembrance keepsake (minor curse possible).
- **The Barrow of Nine Doors (Greyfens, L3–6)** — **Signature:** **pre-Concord** barrow treasure (the network bends around it) — a **Marrowdowns Ancestor-Ring**-class anti-death relic fits thematically here. **Mundane:** ancient torcs/bone-art, 50–250 gp. **Danger-item:** barrow-dead guard a cursed grave-good (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #10 Keening Crown-Shard fits).
- **The Ledger Vault (Hollowmere heist, L3–6)** — **Signature:** **The Ledger of Quiet Debts** (Rare relic; M3) — the heist's prize. **Mundane:** Ledger coin, letters of credit, 100–500 gp. **Trigger:** clean heist → the relic + Ledger as enemy; loud heist → collectors hunt you. **Danger-item:** the **Ledger's Favor** trap-letter (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #3).
- **The Concord Relay-Vault / Signal-Cairn (Heights, L7–10)** — **Signature:** **The Custodian's Compass** (Very Rare; M6/M7 node-finder) deep within; guarded by the **Relay-Warden** construct. **Mundane:** Concord salvage, 250–1,000 gp. **Trigger:** disabling the Relay-Warden nonviolently (watchword) → the Compass + a network clue.
- **The Greyfens Deep (environmental, L3–7)** — **Signature:** drift-caught relics + **The Drift-Line** salvage (M5); a **Reedwalker's Pole**-class fen item. **Mundane:** lost travelers' goods, 25–250 gp. **Danger-item:** Gravecaller relics near the Drowned Blind.
- **The Basin Keystone Approach (L5–9)** — **Signature:** Concord upper-works salvage + a major M2/M6 clue; a **Warden's relic** (toward the First Seal late). **Mundane:** 250–1,000 gp Concord salvage. **DM-Only:** the deep stair down is gated (endgame); never reward keystone-core access here. **Danger-item:** heavy Thin-touch zone (telegraphed).
- **The Under-Shrine Approach (DM-ONLY, L16–20)** — **Signature:** endgame artifacts — **Quiet Country Vessel**, **Harvest Engine Shard**, **Last Voice** (all M6–M9 gated; `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md`). **DM-ONLY ENTIRELY.** Rewards here are the endgame *choice*, not loot. Never surface before the apex is earned.

## Ashgarden Vale (S, L1–6)

- **The Pellow Grange (L2–4)** — **Signature:** Ledger relic-quarry haul (M3) + a **Tally-Glass**. **Mundane:** quarry silver, 25–100 gp. **Danger-item:** "saint's-bone" raw relics (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #5).
- **The Buried Cloister (L3–5)** — **Signature:** Concord cloister relic + Script-fragment (M2/M6). **Mundane:** chapel silver, art objects, 50–250 gp. **Danger-item:** a Remembrance-corruption relic (the Quiet Coat fits, `CURSED_ITEMS.md` #2).
- **Saint Veddow's Tomb / Inner Shrine (L4–7, gated deep)** — **Signature:** a capped-node relic + strong M2/M6 corroboration; an **Orchardmere Wake-Garland**-class anti-fear item. **Mundane:** pilgrim offerings, reliquaries, 100–500 gp. **DM-Only:** secondary node — deep level gated. **Danger-item:** the **Saint's-Bone Reliquary** (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #5).

## Tollwood (E, L2–7)

- **The Old Mast (deep wood, gated, L5–8)** — **Signature:** **pre-Concord** presence-relic (oblique M5/M6; "the grey lords") + **The Toll-Keeper's Chain** (Rare). **Mundane:** fey-touched wood, amber, 100–500 gp. **DM-Only:** a landmark-power, NOT a node/Court. **Danger-item:** taking treasure carelessly stirs the presence (the "bargain").
- **The Hanging Oaks (L3–6)** — **Signature:** Gravecaller cell cache + **The Gravecaller's Knock** (cursed; M5). **Mundane:** offerings, 25–100 gp. **Danger-item:** the Knock itself (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #1).
- **The Greenward Toll-Station / Drowned Vault (L3–6)** — **Signature:** Concord road-node salvage + M2 network clue. **Mundane:** toll-coin, road-tokens, 50–250 gp. **Danger-item:** drowned-vault relic (Thin-touch).

## Pale Coast (W, L2–7)

- **The Drowned Lamp (L3–6)** — **Signature:** **The Drowned Flame** (Rare relic; Thin-touch immunity; M5) *or* the **Lantern of the Held Breath** (Rare). **Mundane:** wreck salvage, salt-silver, 50–250 gp. **DM-Only:** maritime node. **Danger-item:** sea-cursed jewelry (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #6 Drowned Wedding-Band).
- **The Skerry Shrine (offshore, gated, L5–8)** — **Signature:** largest coastal-node relic + strong M2/M5/M6 corroboration; **The Beacon Unbroken** lens late (Legendary). **Mundane:** drowned-shrine offerings, pearls, 100–500 gp. **Danger-item:** drowned-tide haunt relics.
- **The Wreckers' Caves (L3–6)** — **Signature:** wreckers' plunder hoard + **Stormglass** cache. **Mundane:** ship-salvage, sea-jewelry, 50–250 gp. **Trigger:** sparing/turning the wreckers (Wreck-Master Quint) → Coast standing + the hoard. **Danger-item:** a cursed salvage piece (DM choice).

## Caradril (mid-game, L5–12)

- **The Sunken Wards Deep (L6–10)** — **Signature:** **Custodian's Quiet-Step Boots** (Very Rare) + a dormant-node M2 fragment (the network is continental). **Mundane:** flooded-undercity Concord salvage, 250–1,000 gp. **Danger-item:** quiet-coin relics (Thin-touch).
- **The Caradril Ashmarket Undercroft (L6–9)** — **Signature:** black-market relic cache (quiet-coin) + the **Hush-Knife** (Rare cursed). **Mundane:** fenced goods, gems, 100–500 gp. **Danger-item:** the Hush-Knife (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #8) + a Court-plant item possible (`SENTIENT_ITEMS.md` #4 / `CURSED_ITEMS.md` #9).

## Far Continent (L8–20)

- **The Old Concord Heartlands Ruin / Ruin'd Crown (L10–14)** — **Signature:** **The Ash-Crown Fragment** (Very Rare relic; M9) + **The Unforgotten Mail** (Very Rare). **Mundane:** imperial Concord regalia, 500–2,500 gp. **DM-Only:** surface imperial ruin, NOT the harvest-heart. **Danger-item:** the **Reclaimer's Bright Astrolabe** (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #7).
- **The Emberfell Caldera Descent (L12–16)** — **Signature:** **Emberglass Brand-Bottle** + ember-relic hoard. **Mundane:** obsidian/fire-opal, penance-relics, 250–1,000 gp. **Danger-item:** a fire-cursed relic (DM choice).
- **The Saltmere Deep Towns (L10–14)** — **Signature:** **Saltmere Desiccant Case** (Rare; relic-transport) + drowned-town silver. **Mundane:** salt-preserved goods, 100–500 gp. **DM-Only:** pre-Concord echo. **Danger-item:** a preserved-dead relic.
- **The Hethewald Old Holds (L10–14)** — **Signature:** old-hold treasure + clan-oath rings; a Hethewald-amber relic. **Mundane:** deep-wood amber, oath-silver, 100–500 gp. **DM-Only:** forbidden deep-wood Concord ruin (echo). **Danger-item:** a forest-pact cursed token.
- **The Marrowdowns Barrow Complex (L8–14)** — **Signature:** the **Marrowdowns Ancestor-Ring** (Rare; anti-death) + barrow regalia. **Mundane:** ancestor-bone, barrow-silver, 50–250 gp. **DM-Only:** pre-Concord. **Danger-item:** the **Keening Crown-Shard** (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #10).
- **The Karran Old Iron Forts (L10–16)** — **Signature:** **Karran Border-Iron Brand** + Old Iron weaponry; a Concord-echo relic. **Mundane:** military trophies, ore, 100–500 gp. **DM-Only:** peripheral Concord ruin (echo). **Danger-item:** a war-cursed weapon.
- **The Glassmere Reliquary Vaults (L8–13)** — **Signature:** **Glasswater Mirror-Shard** (Very Rare) + Remnant relic-hoard. **Mundane:** Glassmere glasswork, 250–1,000 gp. **Trigger:** Remnant standing → the **Custodian's Compass** here as an alternative. **Danger-item:** a scrying-cursed mirror.
- **The Three Bridges Counting-Deep (L8–12)** — **Signature:** banking vault haul + the **Honest Coat** (Rare) or a Ledger-relic. **Mundane:** coin, gems, letters of credit, 250–1,000 gp. **Danger-item:** a debt-trap ledger (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #3 variant).
- **The Sallowmarch Drowned Steps (L9–14)** — **Signature:** delta-preserved relic + plague-ward gear; ties to the Drowned Steps causeway. **Mundane:** bog-amber, marsh medicine, 100–500 gp. **Danger-item:** a fever-cursed relic.
- **The Hollow Gulf Wreck-Reef (L9–15)** — **Signature:** **Hollow Gulf Reef-Charts** (Rare) + drowned-harbor salvage. **Mundane:** pearls, salvage, letters of marque, 250–1,000 gp. **Danger-item:** a drowned-harbor curse.
- **The Wender Sky-Stones (NON-Concord, L9–15)** — **Signature:** **Steppe Wind-Chime Compass** + sky-stone sacred relic (treat reverently — not for plunder). **Mundane:** sky-stone beads, clan-art, 50–250 gp. **DM-Only:** independent steppe holy site, NOT Concord. **Danger-item:** sacrilege consequence if looted.
- **The Sunhollow Great Grove (living-faith, L9–15)** — **Signature:** **Sunmark Sun-Disk Pendant** + a living-faith blessing (a *hopeful* reward). **Mundane:** sun-bright bronze, jungle-amber, 100–500 gp. **DM-Only:** hopeful contrast site — rewards are gifts, not plunder.
- **The Nine Locks Sunken Stair (Ring-2, L8–12)** — **Signature:** **Verdance Bloom-Splint** (Rare; recovery) + lock-stair relic; M2 corridor clue. **Mundane:** river-trade goods, bloom-amber, 100–500 gp. **Danger-item:** a relay-vault relic (Thin-touch).
- **The Highmark Frozen Works (L12–16)** — **Signature:** **Highmark Giant-Forged Maul** (Very Rare) + frost-preserved relics. **Mundane:** mountain-iron, frost-gems, 250–1,000 gp. **Danger-item:** a frost-cursed relic.
- **The Cindern Waste Buried Works (L11–16)** — **Signature:** ash-glass survival relic + Concord-echo salvage. **Mundane:** ash-glass curios, cinder-iron, 50–250 gp. **DM-Only:** ash-drowned echo. **Danger-item:** an ash-cursed relic.
