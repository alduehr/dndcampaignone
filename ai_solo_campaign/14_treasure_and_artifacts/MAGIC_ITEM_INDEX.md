## Reading the Entries

`Name (Rarity, type, attunement) — Region/origin.` Then **P:** player-facing, **E:** effect, **L:** limitation/cost, **>** links + solo note.

## Common (levels 1–4; minor utility)

1. **Warden's Road-Token** (Common, wondrous, no attune) — *Ashen Wardens / Reach.*
   **P:** A pewter pilgrim-token stamped with the Warden's open-door sigil. **E:** Once per long rest, press it and speak a rite-word to shed dim light and 30 min of gentle warmth in a 10-ft radius (a campfire that needs no fuel; soothes the restless dead enough to deter weak Remembrances). **L:** The light is cold blue; it marks you as Warden-friendly, which not everyone likes. **>** Common Warden Rank-1 gift; superb solo cold-weather/morale utility.

2. **Mourner's Quiet Bell** (Common, wondrous, no attune) — *Mourners' Circle / Reach.*
   **P:** A small hand-bell with a muffled tongue. **E:** Rung over a grave or corpse, grants advantage on the next check to perform or assist a death-rite, and a fresh Remembrance is 1 step less likely to rise that night. **L:** Single soft ring per long rest; useless against already-risen dead. **>** Mourner Rank-1; ties to M1/M5 calming.

3. **Frontier Everlamp** (Common, wondrous, no attune) — *Reach / Caradril craft.*
   **P:** A hooded tin lantern that needs no oil. **E:** Bright light 20 ft / dim 20 more, on command, no fuel; weatherproof. **L:** Mundane brightness; can be seen by enemies. **>** Cheap, common; the solo explorer's friend.

4. **Salt-Marked Cloak-Pin** (Common, wondrous, no attune) — *Pale Coast / Saltmargin.*
   **P:** A pin of salt-silver shaped like a wave. **E:** Advantage on saves vs natural sea/fog cold and on Constitution checks to endure exposure on the coast/fens. **L:** Only against environmental cold/damp. **>** Coast/fen survival; minor.

5. **Tollwood Bark-Charm** (Common, wondrous, no attune) — *Tollwood.*
   **P:** A disk of carved heartwood on a thong. **E:** While worn in the deep wood, you have advantage on Survival to avoid getting lost and the wood's lesser hazards leave you be (honors the "forest-rules"). **L:** Works only in Tollwood; meaningless elsewhere. **>** Ties to the Old Mast "bargain"; avoidance tool.

6. **Pilgrim's Dry-Boots** (Common, wondrous, no attune) — *Ashgarden Vale.*
   **P:** Worn shrine-pilgrim boots, always dry inside. **E:** Ignore difficult terrain from mud/shallow water/bog at a walk; never get trench-foot or wet-cold from your feet. **L:** Cosmetic in dry land. **>** Fen/marsh mobility; common.

## Uncommon (levels 1–8; meaningful utility)

7. **The Reedwalker's Pole** (Uncommon, wondrous, no attune) — *Greyfens / Reach.*
   **P:** A fen-guide's quarterstaff, balanced and salt-stained. **E:** As a quarterstaff (+stats). Also: 1/short rest, vault across up to 20 ft of water/mire as a single move without a check; advantage on saves vs being grappled/restrained by terrain or fen creatures. **L:** No magic bonus to attack/damage. **>** Old Sashe's gift path; mobility for a lone fen-delver. Situational, not a +1 — fits the "no early flat bonus" rule.

8. **Gravesinger's Censer** (Uncommon, wondrous, requires attune) — *Mourners' Circle.*
   **P:** A swinging brass censer of grey incense. **E:** Burn a charge (3, recharge at dawn) to fill 15 ft with rite-smoke: restless/undead in it have disadvantage on attacks vs you; allies' death-rites auto-succeed on routine cases. **L:** Smoke is obvious; doesn't harm the living. **>** Mourner Rank-2; solo defensive vs the dead.

9. **Ledger Clerk's Tally-Glass** (Uncommon, wondrous, no attune) — *Cinder Ledger / Caradril.*
   **P:** A jeweler's loupe in a Ledger-stamped case. **E:** Advantage on Investigation to appraise goods, spot forged documents/coin, and read account-books; instantly know the fair market price of an item. **L:** Knowledge only. **>** Ledger Rank-1/2; investigation + economy tool (`ECONOMY_AND_PRICING_GUIDE.md`).

10. **Coldhearth Charcoal-Ward** (Uncommon, wondrous, no attune) — *Tollwood / Coldhearth.*
    **P:** A lump of consecrated charcoal in a pouch. **E:** 1/long rest, crush it to draw a 10-ft ward-circle; restless dead and beasts of the wood will not cross it for 1 hour (a safe-rest bubble). **L:** Single use per rest; a determined boss can still reach in with ranged/effects. **>** Solo safe-rest enabler — key for a lone PC.

11. **Wrackmouth Diving-Mask** (Uncommon, wondrous, requires attune) — *Pale Coast.*
    **P:** A salvager's mask of glass and gut. **E:** See clearly underwater and in fog; hold breath 10 min; advantage on Athletics to swim. **L:** No water-breathing (10-min cap). **>** Drowned-site delving (`THE_DROWNED_LAMP`, `THE_SKERRY_SHRINE`).

12. **Compact Writ-Cylinder** (Uncommon, wondrous, no attune) — *Reachward Compact.*
    **P:** A bronze message-tube with a Compact seal-clasp. **E:** Documents inside are tamper-evident and weatherproof; presenting it grants advantage on social checks to be believed as an official courier. **L:** Lesser than the Compact Seal artifact; no real legal power. **>** Compact Rank-1/2; access/social.

13. **Whisper-Stone of Candlewick** (Uncommon, wondrous, requires attune) — *Reach / Candlewick.*
    **P:** A pebble of pale Concord stone that hums faintly near old script. **E:** 1/day, touch Concord-marked stone to "hear" a fragment of what was carved there (clue delivery); advantage on checks to find hidden Concord mechanisms. **L:** Fragments only; never the whole truth. **>** M2 clue tool; pairs with Concord Script training.

14. **Orchardmere Wake-Garland** (Uncommon, wondrous, no attune) — *Ashgarden Vale.*
    **P:** A dried-flower circlet from a Vale wake-orchard. **E:** Worn, you have advantage on Wisdom saves vs fear and despair effects (the Vale's "we don't speak of it" comfort, made real). **L:** Crumbles after 7 uses, then mundane. **>** Vale flavor; anti-fear for solo horror beats.

15. **Steppe Wind-Chime Compass** (Uncommon, wondrous, no attune) — *Wender Steppe.*
    **P:** A traveler's chime of bone and bronze. **E:** Always know true north and the direction of the nearest fresh water; advantage on Survival to navigate open plains/snow. **L:** Open terrain only. **>** Far-region travel; Wender clan trade-good.

16. **Karran Border-Iron Brand** (Uncommon, weapon [any metal weapon], requires attune) — *Karran Marches.*
    **P:** A weapon reforged in old fort-iron, marked with warlord heraldry. **E:** 1/short rest, your next hit also frightens the target (Wis save DC 13) — a soldier's killing presence. **L:** No flat bonus; the fear is once per rest. **>** Karran martial flavor; situational, not a +1.

17. **Glassmere Clarity-Lens** (Uncommon, wondrous, no attune) — *Glassmere League.*
    **P:** A ground-glass monocle in a fine frame. **E:** Advantage on Perception to spot illusions, false coin, hidden compartments, and forgeries; can read tiny/distant writing. **L:** No magic-detection beyond seeing-through. **>** Investigation/intrigue tool; League craft.

18. **Sunmark Sun-Disk Pendant** (Uncommon, wondrous, no attune) — *Sunmark.*
    **P:** A bright bronze disk on a cord, warm in sunlight. **E:** Stores 1 hour of daylight; release it (action) to shed sunlight (10-ft bright) for 1 minute — true sunlight vs creatures that dread it. **L:** Must recharge in real sun; 1 use stored. **>** Anti-undead/horror utility; hopeful far-region flavor.

## Rare (levels 5–12; significant power — earned, not bought cheap)

19. **The Restful Edge** (Rare, weapon [bladed], requires attune) — *Ashen Wardens / Reach.*
    **P:** A plain Warden blade honed for "laying to rest." **E:** +1 to attack/damage; **vs undead and Remembrance-touched creatures**, deals an extra 1d8 and a slain target cannot rise again from that wound. **L:** The +1 and bonus die apply in full only vs the dead; vs the living it is merely a fine +1 blade with a heavier conscience. **>** First "real" weapon, gated to Rare per balance rules; perfect against the campaign's signature foes.

20. **Lantern of the Held Breath** (Rare, wondrous, requires attune) — *Pale Coast / Concord salvage.*
    **P:** A storm-lantern whose flame turns silver underwater. **E:** Grants water-breathing and 60-ft sight in water/fog to you and allies within 10 ft while lit; advantage on Constitution saves vs drowning/cold-water shock. **L:** Snuffs if forcibly extinguished; relight = action. **>** Drowned-site capstone; pairs with the Drowned Flame.

21. **The Honest Coat** (Rare, armor [light/medium], requires attune) — *Cinder Ledger / Caradril.*
    **P:** A magister's fine coat woven with truth-thread. **E:** +1 AC; advantage on saves vs being charmed or magically deceived; you always know if someone is lying *to* you in conversation (not their full intent — just the lie). **L:** It does not tell you the truth, only the lie; clever half-truths slip past. **>** Intrigue armor; solo social survival.

22. **Marrowdowns Ancestor-Ring** (Rare, ring, requires attune) — *Marrowdowns.*
    **P:** A bone ring carved with a barrow-line of tiny figures. **E:** 1/long rest, when you drop to 0 HP, instead drop to 1 HP and stand (the "ancestors hold you up") — once, then the ring is silent until next long rest. **L:** Once per long rest; cosmetic otherwise. **>** Premier **solo anti-death** item; barrow grave-good. Keeps a lone PC out of cheap death.

23. **The Toll-Keeper's Chain** (Rare, wondrous [neck], requires attune) — *Tollwood.*
    **P:** A heavy iron chain-of-office taken from a Concord toll-node. **E:** 1/day, command a single restless dead or beast within 30 ft to *let you pass* (Wis save DC 14; on success it ignores you and your group for 10 min). **L:** Pass, not control — it won't fight for you; bosses save with advantage. **>** Avoidance over combat; very solo-friendly.

24. **Emberglass Brand-Bottle** (Rare, wondrous, no attune) — *Emberfell / Ashfast.*
    **P:** A flask of black volcanic glass holding a coal that never dies. **E:** 3 charges (recharge in lava/forge-fire): pour to make a 10-ft line of flame (2d6 fire, Dex DC 14 half) or warm a camp against any cold. **L:** Recharges only near true fire/lava. **>** Far-region; the warmth use is the real solo value in cold zones.

25. **Saltmere Desiccant Case** (Rare, wondrous, no attune) — *Saltmere Reaches.*
    **P:** A salt-lined coffer that preserves what's inside. **E:** Anything sealed in it (food, relics, even a wounded creature) is held in stasis — no decay, no spoilage, gentle preservation; a Remembrance relic inside is "quieted" (won't leak/Thin-touch). **L:** Holds one helm-sized object; opening resets it. **>** Safely transport dangerous relics — a key support item for the harvest plot.

26. **Verdance Bloom-Splint** (Rare, wondrous, requires attune) — *Verdance Reaches.*
    **P:** A living-wood brace that grows to fit a limb. **E:** Strap it on during a short rest to mend a wound: regain 4d4+4 HP and remove one level of exhaustion, 1/long rest; the splint then "rests" (flowers and is inert) until next long rest. **L:** Once per long rest; slow (needs a short rest). **>** Solo recovery — exactly the support a lone PC needs.

27. **Hollow Gulf Reef-Charts** (Rare, wondrous, no attune) — *Hollow Gulf Ports.*
    **P:** A privateer's set of self-correcting sea-charts. **E:** While at sea, advantage on navigation and to predict weather 24 hrs out; reveals safe channels and hidden reefs/wrecks (and the salvage on them). **L:** Sea/coast only. **>** Maritime exploration + treasure-finding.

## Very Rare (levels 9–16; major power)

28. **The Unforgotten Mail** (Very Rare, armor [medium/heavy], requires attune) — *Concord Heartlands.*
    **P:** Concord battle-mail, every ring etched with a name. **E:** +2 AC; you cannot be reduced below 1 HP by Remembrance/necrotic effects; advantage on Death saves. **L:** Heavy; the etched names whisper at night (no mechanical penalty, strong flavor). **>** Capstone defense vs the campaign's death-themed threats; far-region imperial loot.

29. **Custodian's Quiet-Step Boots** (Very Rare, wondrous [feet], requires attune) — *Concord salvage.*
    **P:** Soft grey Concord boots that make no sound. **E:** You are silent when you wish; 1/short rest, take a 30-ft *misty step* between shadows or still water. **L:** The teleport needs shadow/water at both ends (the Reach has plenty; bright open plains, less so). **>** Premier solo stealth/escape — a lone PC's lifeline.

30. **The Mercy-Lamp** (Very Rare, wondrous, requires attune) — *Mourners' Circle / Concord.*
    **P:** A grief-lamp of grey glass and silver. **E:** 1/day, "lay to rest" a single restless dead/undead of CR ≤ 6 within 30 ft with no combat (Wis save DC 16; a true peace, not destruction); also functions as the Mercy charm for harder cases at DM discretion. **L:** One use per day; mightier dead save with advantage or are immune. **>** Turns a fight into a release — deeply on-theme, and solo-merciful.

31. **Glasswater Mirror-Shard** (Very Rare, wondrous, requires attune) — *Glassmere League.*
    **P:** A shard of enchanted mirror in a velvet sleeve. **E:** 1/day, scry a place or person you've seen (as the spell, save DC 16); also reflects one targeted spell back at its caster as a reaction, 1/long rest. **L:** Scrying can be detected/blocked; the reflect is once per long rest. **>** Intrigue + a defensive trump; League magnate treasure.

32. **Highmark Giant-Forged Maul** (Very Rare, weapon [maul/greatweapon], requires attune) — *Highmark Passes.*
    **P:** A mountain-iron greatweapon sized by giant smiths. **E:** +2; 1/short rest, a hit shoves the target 15 ft and knocks prone (Str DC 16 negates). **L:** Heavy; the shove is once per rest. **>** High-tier martial; far-north loot.

33. **The Drowned Crown-Arc** (Very Rare, wondrous, requires attune) — *Drowned Steps.*
    **P:** A second crown-arc, salvaged from the submerged sea-causeway (a cousin to the Ash-Crown Fragment). **E:** Underwater you act unimpeded (no penalties, swim speed = walk speed); 1/day command the drowned dead to part before you (no combat). **L:** Its power wanes far from water. **>** Drowned-Steps capstone; ties M9 imagery (do not equate to the Ash-Crown — see DM note).
    **DM-Only:** A surface-imperial relic like the Ash-Crown — corroborates Concord scale, never the harvest-heart.

## Legendary (levels 13–20; campaign-defining)

34. **The Wardens' First Seal** (Legendary, wondrous, requires attune) — *Ashen Wardens / Concord.*
    **P:** The original keystone-seal carried by the Wardens' founders; cold as deep water. **E:** Once per long rest, *seal* a stirring node or restless-dead surge within sight for 1 hour (suppresses harvest effects, calms the dead in 60 ft); advantage on all checks to perform Warden seal-rites. **L:** Suppression, not cure; the node wakes again. **>** A literal lever on the campaign's central machine — gate to late game.
    **DM-Only:** This is a true Concord seal-key. Othetha's order was founded to guard such things. It can buy time at the keystone but cannot end the harvest — that is the endgame *choice*, not an item.

35. **The Beacon Unbroken** (Legendary, wondrous, requires attune) — *Pale Coast / Concord lighthouse.*
    **P:** The intact lens-heart of a Concord sea-lighthouse. **E:** Project a beam of true daylight up to 120 ft (sunlight; devastating to light-dreading dead); 1/day, the beam can also *call back* a wrong-come-back to a moment of clarity (a clue/closure, or a chance to end it peacefully). **L:** Recharges only at dawn; the daylight beam is concentration. **>** Anti-horror capstone + a mercy tool.

36. **The Ledger Eternal** (Legendary, wondrous, requires attune) — *Cinder Ledger / Concord.*
    **P:** A master account-book that reckons debts beyond money — favors, oaths, even the dead's unfinished business. **E:** Once per long rest, "call in a debt": an NPC who genuinely owes you (life, favor, oath) is compelled to aid you once (no save for ordinary folk; powerful NPCs get a DC 18 Wis save). Records any unfinished business of the dead present. **L:** Only *real* debts; inventing one fails. **>** A social/leverage Legendary — fitting for a solo intrigue endgame; never a combat I-win.
    **DM-Only:** Its deepest pages reckon the Concord's debt to the dead — a thematic mirror of the harvest's true cost (M9).
