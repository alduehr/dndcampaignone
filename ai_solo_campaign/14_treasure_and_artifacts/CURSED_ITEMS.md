## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY.** Player-facing description blocks are safe; **DM-Only blocks (especially Hollow Court gift-curses) are never revealed early** — a leaked one would expose M7. Load when placing a tempting-but-dangerous reward, when a Compact agent "rewards" the PC, or when a Remembrance relic is mishandled.

## Design Rules for Cursed Items (solo-fair)

1. **Always telegraphed.** A cursed item gives at least one warning sign (a chill, a rumor, an NPC's reluctance, a failed Identify, a too-good gift). No gotcha curses.
2. **Recoverable, never campaign-ending.** Every curse has a known or discoverable lift (a rite, a return-to-site, a faction, a sacrifice). Curses cost and complicate; they don't brick the character.
3. **A real temptation.** A good cursed item offers something genuinely useful, so removing it is a *choice*, not an obvious dump.
4. **Curses are clues.** Remembrance-curses point at M3/M5/M6. The harvest's fingerprints are on everything that drains the self.
5. **No cheap death.** A curse may endanger, weaken, or expose the PC; it should not silently kill them. Solo PCs especially get warning and an out.

## Curse Class Legend

- **Remembrance-corruption** — slowly drains the user's sense of self (M3/M6).
- **Hollow Court gift-curse** — given as a "reward" by a Compact agent; DM-only true nature (M7).
- **Gravecaller-origin** — attracts the wrong dead (M5).
- **Beautiful boon** — seems like a major prize, extracts a hidden price.
- **Site-bound** — only liftable by returning to a specific dungeon.

## 1. The Gravecaller's Knock — *Uncommon (Gravecaller-origin)*

> Also in `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md` #4.
- **P:** A Concord-stone door-knocker that calls the dead when struck thrice at a shrine.
- **Curse:** Each use summons a Wrong-come-back that may not leave; after 3 uses the bearer is **marked** — leaked Remembrances seek them out 1/night (a haunting random encounter; Wis save DC 13 to rest properly).
- **Telegraph:** Cold to the touch; Gravecaller graffiti near where it's found; an NPC warns "don't knock more than you must."
- **Lift:** A true Mourner or Warden rite over the knocker (or destroying it at a sealed node).
- **Link:** M5. Found in `THE_DROWNED_BLIND` / `THE_HANGING_OAKS`.
- **Solo note:** Tempting (a free *Speak with Dead* engine) but escalates nightly danger — the bearer chooses safety vs answers.

## 2. The Quiet Coat — *Rare (Remembrance-corruption)*

- **P:** A fine grey coat lined with "old glass" thread; whoever wears it is calm, unbothered, never afraid. Looted from an Ashmarket relic-dealer.
- **Curse:** It dampens fear by **eating memory.** Each long rest worn, make a DC 12 Wis save; on a fail, lose one specific personal memory (DM picks a small one, then larger). The wearer doesn't notice the loss (that's the horror); allies do. Advantage on fear saves is real and seductive.
- **Telegraph:** Identify reveals "muffles the mind"; an NPC who knew the previous owner says they "got so calm, then so empty." It's literally lined with quiet-coin.
- **Lift:** Remove and undergo a Mourner restoration-rite (recovers recent memories; old losses may be permanent) **or** burn it at a shrine.
- **Class:** Remembrance-corruption. **Link:** M3/M6 — direct proof relics eat the self.
- **Solo note:** Especially dangerous solo (no party to notice the player forgetting). The DM must voice the warning signs since there's no party to do it.

## 3. The Ledger's Favor — *Uncommon (Beautiful boon / Hollow Court-adjacent)*

- **P:** A sealed letter of credit "gifted" by a generous Ledger factor: spend it for up to 500 gp of goods/services anywhere the Ledger reaches, no payment now.
- **Curse:** It is a **debt-hook.** The moment it's spent, the bearer owes the Ledger a favor *of the Ledger's choosing* — and the Ledger's choosing trends toward "go to this old shrine and bring us what you find." Refusing the called favor turns the Ledger hostile (collectors dispatched).
- **Telegraph:** "Too generous"; the factor smiles too much; the fine print is in tiny Ledger cant.
- **Lift:** Repay the 500 gp in coin + interest, or complete one (escapable) favor.
- **Class:** Beautiful boon. **Link:** M3/Monopoly clock (#6).
- **Solo note:** Pure social entanglement, not a stat trap — fits intrigue play.

## 4. The Compact's Commendation Ring — *Rare (Hollow Court gift-curse) — DM-ONLY TRUE NATURE*

- **P:** A handsome signet "rewarded" to the PC for service to the Reachward Compact, pinned on by a senior councilor. Grants advantage on Persuasion with Compact folk and a stipend.
- **Player-perceived:** A genuine honor and a useful social/economic perk.
- **DM-Only Curse:** The councilor is **Aldous Reke**, a knowing Hollow Court agent. The ring is a **tracking and influence relic**: while worn, the Court's cutout always knows the PC's general location, and 1/long rest the ring nudges the wearer toward the basin/shrine (DM may impose a faint "pull" — a dream, a restlessness; never compulsion, per the anti-railroad rule). It is otherwise exactly as helpful as it appears.
- **Telegraph (fair):** Reke is oily; a paranoid NPC (the Hush, a Gravecaller) warns "Compact gifts have strings"; the ring is faintly warm near old water.
- **Lift:** Stop wearing it (ends tracking) once suspected; a Warden/Remnant ward can "deafen" it; destroying it at a node alerts the Court.
- **Class:** Hollow Court gift-curse. **Link:** M4 → M7 (Reke's exposure).
- **Solo note:** The trap is *information*, not damage — it makes the Court's surveillance concrete without ambushing the lone PC. **Never reveal the DM-Only block until the player investigates Reke.**

## 5. The Saint's-Bone Reliquary — *Uncommon (Remembrance-corruption / Beautiful boon)*

- **P:** A gilt reliquary of "Saint Veddow's finger-bone," sold at Saint Veddow's Rest as a healing pilgrim-relic. Holding it during a short rest seems to ease wounds.
- **Curse:** The "saint's bone" is a raw Remembrance relic. The healing is real but it heals by **borrowing** — each use inflicts Thin-touch (disadvantage on Death saves until a true rite) and, on repeat use within a day, a point of exhaustion. Pilgrims who lean on it grow grey and listless.
- **Telegraph:** Folk call them "saint's-bones"; a Warden or honest Mourner recoils; Identify shows "stores the dead."
- **Lift:** Stop using it; a shrine long-rest clears Thin-touch. The reliquary itself is a clue, not a keeper.
- **Class:** Remembrance-corruption. **Link:** M2/M3 (the Vale's denial; relics sold as faith).
- **Solo note:** A trap that masquerades as the healing a lone PC craves — the lesson is "the Reach's easy magic always costs."

## 6. The Drowned Wedding-Band — *Rare (Gravecaller-origin / site-bound)*

- **P:** A simple gold band recovered from the Drowned Mile, warm and humming with old love. Wearing it grants advantage on Insight (you "feel" what others feel).
- **Curse:** It carries a drowned woman's clinging Remembrance. While worn, 1/night you dream her death and wake to find water pooled by your bed; restless dead near water treat you as kin and *follow* you (a recurring haunt). Removing it normally doesn't work — it "won't come off" until her grievance is addressed.
- **Telegraph:** The water; the dreams; an old-song about "the bride the river kept."
- **Lift:** Return to her drowning-site (the Drowned Mile / a Coast node) and perform her death-rite, or deliver her last message (a small quest). Then it slips off freely and becomes a mundane, sad keepsake.
- **Class:** Gravecaller-origin / site-bound. **Link:** M5.
- **Solo note:** A self-contained mini-quest curse — the empathy boon is a fair trade for the obligation.

## 7. The Reclaimer's Bright Astrolabe — *Very Rare (Beautiful boon)*

- **P:** A gorgeous Concord star-instrument, prize of a Remnant dig. It grants advantage on all Concord-lore and navigation checks and can pinpoint Concord ruins for miles — a researcher's dream.
- **Curse:** It is *too* eager to find ruins. Once attuned, 1/long rest it "shows" the bearer the nearest node so vividly they feel compelled to seek it (DC 14 Wis save to set it aside that day; on a fail, disadvantage on rests until they at least travel toward it). It leads the bearer, over time, ever inward toward the keystone.
- **Telegraph:** The previous owner "walked into the fens and never came back"; it pulls the needle toward water.
- **Lift:** End attunement and let it cool a tenday; a Warden ward calms it.
- **Class:** Beautiful boon. **Link:** M6/M7 — it wants to find the machine.
- **Solo note:** Powerful exploration value vs a compulsion clock — a real choice.

## 8. The Hush-Knife — *Rare (Remembrance-corruption)*

- **P:** A black-glass dagger from the Caradril underworld that "kills quiet" — a creature slain by it makes no sound and leaves no Remembrance behind (it cannot rise, cannot be Spoken With, cannot haunt). +1 dagger.
- **Curse:** Each kill with it **feeds the blade a sliver of the wielder's own self** instead. After a number of kills equal to the wielder's level, the wielder begins to "go quiet" — losing the ability to be moved by grief or joy (DC 13 Wis save per kill thereafter; on fail, gain a point of a custom "Hollowing" track; at 5, the wielder is becoming thin-souled — a horror beat, reversible).
- **Telegraph:** Assassins who used it "lost their hearts"; it's cold; Identify warns "it pays itself from you."
- **Lift:** Stop killing with it and undergo a Mourner restoration; the Hollowing track recovers slowly.
- **Class:** Remembrance-corruption. **Link:** M3 (the self as fuel). Cross-ref `../13_encounters_and_bestiary/HORROR_AND_CURSE_THREATS.md`.
- **Solo note:** A genuinely strong assassin's tool with a self-erasing price — never a free upgrade.

## 9. The Compass That Lies — *Uncommon (Hollow Court gift-curse) — DM-ONLY TRUE NATURE*

- **P:** A "Custodian's Compass" the PC may acquire from a too-helpful stranger — looks like the genuine artifact (ARTIFACT_INDEX #7).
- **DM-Only Curse:** A Court-made forgery. It points *away* from the keystone and toward dead ends/danger, gently steering the PC off the trail (or into a trap) while feeling authoritative.
- **Telegraph (fair):** Its needle disagrees with the genuine Compass, the Warden's Lantern, and the drift-evidence; a real scholar notices the Script is subtly wrong (Investigation DC 16).
- **Lift:** Recognize the forgery (cross-check clues) and discard it; no lingering effect once distrusted.
- **Class:** Hollow Court gift-curse. **Link:** M7 (Court misdirection). **Never reveal DM-Only until cross-checked.**
- **Solo note:** A misdirection trap, not a damage trap — fair because multiple other clues contradict it (three-clue rule protects the player).

## 10. The Keening Crown-Shard — *Very Rare (site-bound / Remembrance-corruption)*

- **P:** A shard of a Concord crown found deep in a node (a corrupted cousin of the Ash-Crown Fragment). It grants real authority over the restless dead (1/day command undead, no save vs CR ≤ 5).
- **Curse:** Power over the dead draws the dead *to* the bearer. While carried, the bearer accrues a growing escort of unseen Remembrances; at intervals the DM triggers a swelling haunt, and in a node the shard tries to **wake** what sleeps there. It cannot be discarded normally (it "follows you home").
- **Telegraph:** The escort (cold spots, whispers); it hums louder the deeper you go; a Warden flatly refuses to touch it.
- **Lift:** Return it to the node it came from and re-seal it (a Warden rite / a small dungeon return), or surrender it to the Wardens.
- **Class:** Site-bound / Remembrance-corruption. **Link:** M6/M9 (Concord scale + the harvest's pull).
- **Solo note:** Strong command-power vs escalating haunt — the kind of "should I keep this?" the campaign wants. Site-bound lift gives the lone PC a clear path out.
