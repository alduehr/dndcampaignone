> **Secrecy note (DM):** This file is **mixed** — the consumables catalog is player-safe, but the resurrection-caution framing ("the harvest's danger," "the broken Last Door," scrolls as "clues to the broken Last Door") is DM-side guidance referencing DM-only truth. Present the *availability and risk* of resurrection to the player; do not surface the harvest or the broken-Last-Door truth until earned.

## AI Use

The **gap-filler layer.** Consumables keep a solo PC alive and flexible without permanent power creep — be generous here (see `SOLO_REWARD_BALANCE.md` § Consumable Pacing). Load when stocking a shop, dressing a hoard, or when the player wants to prepare before a delve. Official potions/scrolls (Track A: *Potion of Healing*, spell scrolls) are referenced by name only — pull exact numbers from the 2024 DMG. Original campaign consumables (Track B) are given abbreviated prose below.

## Two Tracks

- **Track A (official, reference-only):** *Potion of Healing* (and Greater/Superior/Supreme), *Antitoxin*, *Potion of Climbing/Water Breathing/Fire Resistance*, *Oil of Slipperiness*, *Spell Scrolls* (cantrip–9th), *Alchemist's Fire*, *Holy Water*. Name + 2024 DMG. **No copied text.**
- **Track B (ORIGINAL CAMPAIGN consumables):** below. Each: **P** (player-facing), **E** (effect), **rarity/level**, region.

## Healing & Recovery (weight UP for solo)

1. **Shrine-Water Vial** (Common; L1+; Reach/Vale shrines) — **P:** blessed water from a Three-Thresholds shrine. **E:** as a half-strength *Potion of Healing* (regain 1d4+1 HP) **and** ends Thin-touch on the drinker. **The cheapest reliable Thin-touch cure** — keep these available in shrine-towns.
2. **Mourner's Grave-Bread** (Common; L1+; Mourners' Circle) — **E:** eaten during a short rest, regain 1d6+2 HP and shrug off one level of fatigue-flavor weariness; 1/short rest.
3. **Veen's Field-Draught** (Uncommon; L3+; Doctor Onaia Veen / frontier physicians) — **E:** as *Potion of Healing* (2d4+2) plus removes one disease or poison; a frontier all-purpose.
4. **Salt-and-Tide Tonic** (Uncommon; L3+; Pale Coast) — **E:** regain 2d4+2 HP and ignore the next exhaustion from cold/wet exposure for 8 hrs.
5. **Bloom-Tonic** (Rare; L7+; Verdance) — **E:** as *Greater Potion of Healing* (4d4+4) and regrow superficial wounds (cosmetic scars fade); 1 use.

## Anti-Death / Stabilization (solo lifelines)

6. **Wake-Candle** (Common; L1+; Mourners) — **E:** lit beside a dying creature, grants advantage on their Death saves while it burns (10 min); a solo PC's best friend when alone and bleeding.
7. **Ancestor's Last Breath** (Uncommon; L4+; Marrowdowns) — **E:** crush under the nose of a creature at 0 HP to stabilize them instantly and grant 1 HP; single use.
8. **Warden's Steadying Salve** (Uncommon; L3+; Ashen Wardens) — **E:** applied during a short rest, your next failed Death save this day counts as a success instead; 1 use.

## Defense vs the Dead / Horror (campaign-signature)

9. **Grave-Incense Stick** (Common; L1+; Mourners) — **E:** burn for 10 min to ward a 10-ft space; weak restless dead won't enter; advantage on the next save vs Remembrance-fear.
10. **Salt-Circle Pouch** (Common; L1+; Coast/fens) — **E:** pour to make a 5-ft circle the wrong-come-backs won't cross for 1 min; one pour.
11. **Sun-Draught** (Uncommon; L4+; Sunmark) — **E:** drink to shed true sunlight (5-ft) from your skin for 1 min; potent vs light-dreading dead; 1 use.
12. **Mercy-Oil** (Rare; L7+; Mourners/Concord) — **E:** anoint a weapon; the next creature you reduce to 0 with it is *laid to rest* (cannot rise, no Remembrance left) and you may choose to deal nonlethal "release" instead; 1 use.

## Mobility / Escape / Utility (weight UP for solo)

13. **Reed-Skipper's Wax** (Common; L1+; Greyfens) — **E:** smear on boots to walk on water/mire for 10 min as if firm ground; 1 use.
14. **Fog-Step Token** (Uncommon; L4+; Coast/Concord) — **E:** snap it to *misty step* 30 ft (once); a clutch escape.
15. **Climber's Resin** (Common; L1+; Highmark/Karran) — **E:** as *Potion of Climbing* (climb speed for 1 hr).
16. **Smuggler's Quiet-Dust** (Uncommon; L3+; Salt Syndicate/the Hush) — **E:** toss to silence a 15-ft area for 1 min (no sound in or out); great for stealth escapes.
17. **Lantern-Oil of Long Burning** (Common; L1+; anywhere) — **E:** a flask that burns 24 hrs and can't be doused by wind/rain.

## Information / Clue (solo investigation aids)

18. **Script-Reader's Chalk** (Uncommon; L3+; Concord Remnant) — **E:** rub over Concord Script to render one short passage legible without *Comprehend Languages*; 3 uses. A gentle gate-bypass for solo players without the language.
19. **Truth-Salt** (Uncommon; L4+; the Hush/Mourners) — **E:** sprinkle in a drink; the next single statement the drinker makes is true or they cannot speak it (consent/ethics flavor; not mind-control combat); 1 use.
20. **Memory-Smoke** (Rare; L7+; Gravecaller-made — mild) — **E:** inhale beside a corpse/relic to glimpse one vivid memory of the dead (clue); careless use risks Thin-touch; 1 use. **(Gravecaller origin — telegraph the cost.)**

## Offense (use sparingly; Track-A alchemy preferred)

21. **Stormglass Flask** (Uncommon; L4+; Coast) — **E:** thrown, 2d6 cold + slick ice in 5-ft (Dex DC 13 or prone); 1 use.
22. **Ember-Vial** (Uncommon; L4+; Emberfell) — **E:** as *Alchemist's Fire* but with a 5-ft splash; 1 use. (Reference Track-A alchemist's fire for base numbers.)

## Faction-Favor Tokens (spend for access, not power)

23. **Warden Passage-Token** — spend to claim Warden hospitality/safe-rest at any waystation; 1 use.
24. **Ledger Chit** — a small bearer-credit (worth 10–50 gp at any Ledger counter); see `ECONOMY_AND_PRICING_GUIDE.md`.
25. **Mourner's Welcome-Knot** — spend for a Mourner rite, lodging, or grief-lore without question; 1 use.
26. **Compact Courier-Seal** — spend to pass one Compact checkpoint/gate as an official courier; 1 use.

> Faction tokens are detailed as *standing rewards* in `FACTION_REWARDS.md`. They are deliberately access/social, not combat.

## Spell Scrolls by Band (Track A, reference)

| Band | Typical scrolls available to find/buy |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | cantrips, 1st–2nd (utility-leaning: *Cure Wounds, Detect Magic, Comprehend Languages, Feather Fall*) |
| 5–8 | up to 3rd (*Revivify* exists but is **Reach-risky** — see `MAGIC_RULES.md`; *Speak with Dead, Water Breathing, Protection from Energy*) |
| 9–12 | up to 5th (*Greater Restoration, Raise Dead* [risky], *Dispel Magic*) |
| 13–16 | up to 7th |
| 17–20 | up to 9th |

**Campaign caution:** resurrection scrolls function but carry the harvest's danger (DC check, wrong-come-back risk per `MAGIC_RULES.md`). The DM should make these *available but never trivial* — they are clues to the broken Last Door, not a safety net. Do not flood the market with them.

## Pricing & Availability

Prices and where-to-buy are in `ECONOMY_AND_PRICING_GUIDE.md`. Quick rule: the Reach is scarce (consumables are precious, often barter-only); Caradril and Glassmere have real apothecaries and scroll-shops; far regions have region-specific stock (Sallowmarch = abundant antitoxin/fever-cures; Emberfell = fire gear; Coast = breath/cold gear).

## Solo Stocking Guidance

Before a known dangerous delve, a prepared solo PC should be able to assemble: **2–4 healing**, **1 anti-death** (Wake-Candle / Ancestor's Last Breath), **1 escape** (Fog-Step / smuggler's dust), **1 horror-defense** (grave-incense / mercy-oil), and **1 region-appropriate environmental** (breath, cold, fire). If they can't, surface a fair shop/rite/cache before the lethal beat (`SOLO_REWARD_BALANCE.md` § Insurance Floor).
