## At a Glance

The campaign's complete reward layer for solo play, levels 1–20. Built in **Stage 14** (2026-06-14). Treasure here is **solo-tuned** (breadth over spikes, recovery and utility over flat bonuses, no solve-everything item), **Remembrance-bound** (death-touching items carry the harvest's fingerprints; no easy resurrection; apex truth never spelled out in an item), and **intentionally placed** (no random artifact drops; every relic has an owner and an interested faction).

**Start at `TREASURE_INDEX.md`** — the master file that routes every reward need to the right file.

## Load Guide for the AI DM

1. **Player finds/earns/buys/sells anything** → `TREASURE_INDEX.md` (routes you).
2. **How much to give a solo PC** → `SOLO_REWARD_BALANCE.md`.
3. **A specific item** → `MAGIC_ITEM_INDEX.md` (custom) / `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md` (major) / `CONSUMABLES_AND_MINOR_MAGIC.md`.
4. **A faction / quest / dungeon reward** → `FACTION_REWARDS.md` / `QUEST_REWARD_INDEX.md` / `DUNGEON_REWARD_INDEX.md`.
5. **A nonviolent or social reward** → `NONCOMBAT_REWARDS.md`.
6. **Prices, selling, hiring** → `ECONOMY_AND_PRICING_GUIDE.md`.
7. **A tempting-but-dangerous reward** → `CURSED_ITEMS.md` / `SENTIENT_ITEMS.md`.

## Hard Secrecy Rules

- **DM-Only blocks are never surfaced to the player.** They sit in `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md`, `CURSED_ITEMS.md`, `SENTIENT_ITEMS.md`, and `FACTION_REWARDS.md` §7.
- **The Hollow Court gives no clean rewards** — every Court-traced gift is a trap, curse, or leash.
- **No item names or locates the keystone, the harvest, the Hollow Court, or the Custodians** before those are earned through play. Endgame artifacts require M6–M9 to understand/use.
- **Remembrance relics are plot before power**; careless contact risks Thin-touch.

## Source Handling

- **Track A (official D&D items):** reference only — name + source shorthand (2024 DMG, etc.) + fit + placement. No copied text/stats.
- **Track B (custom items):** original, tagged **ORIGINAL CAMPAIGN ITEM**, abbreviated D&D-compatible prose effects.
