## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY.** Standing rewards are player-facing; **Hollow Court "rewards" and the hidden costs of certain gifts are DM-only.** Load when the player advances with a faction, completes a faction quest (`../09_quests/faction_quests/`), or asks "what do I get for helping them?" Each faction has a **3-rank reward track** + an **opposition/betrayal** entry. Ranks roughly map to the 4-quest chains from Stage 8.

## Reward-Track Shape (per faction)

- **Rank 1 — Neutral contact:** a minor reward, information, or token. (You've been useful once.)
- **Rank 2 — Trusted agent:** a meaningful reward, access, or tool. (You're relied upon.)
- **Rank 3 — Faction champion:** a major reward, relic, or title. (You are one of them, or their chosen.)
- **Opposition / Betrayal:** what they take back, deny, or turn on you.

Rewards are weighted toward **access, tools, and standing** over raw combat power (`SOLO_REWARD_BALANCE.md`). Faction relics live in `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md`.

## 1. The Ashen Wardens — *the seal-guardians*

- **Rank 1:** Warden hospitality (free safe-rest at any waystation; a **Warden Passage-Token**), a **Warden's Road-Token** (Common), and honest road-lore (which routes the dead haunt).
- **Rank 2:** Training in a seal/death-rite (advantage on those checks); a **Gravesinger's Censer** or **Warden's Steadying Salve** supply; a Warden ally (Brother Hale) who may travel a leg with you.
- **Rank 3:** **The Warden's Unlit Lantern** (Uncommon relic — node-detector; `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md` #2) from Warden Sister Othetha as a mark of deepest trust; late-game, access to **The Restful Edge** (Rare) and ultimately knowledge of **The Wardens' First Seal** (Legendary). Title: *Sworn of the Ash.*
- **Opposition/Betrayal:** the Wardens withdraw hospitality, the Lantern goes dark for you (they reclaim it), and they quietly warn other Wardens. They do not hunt you — they *grieve* you. (DM-Only: Othetha may still feed you breadcrumbs if you oppose the Court, even while officially cold.)

## 2. The Cinder Ledger — *the merchant-bank monopoly*

- **Rank 1:** a **Ledger Chit** (10–50 gp bearer-credit), fair-market appraisal, and a **Ledger Clerk's Tally-Glass** (Uncommon). Access to Ledger counters continent-wide.
- **Rank 2:** a **letter of credit** (spend now, settle later — see `CURSED_ITEMS.md` #3 for the *gift* version's strings), warehouse/transport access, the **Honest Coat** (Rare) for sale at a "friend's price," and trade-route intelligence.
- **Rank 3:** a **share in a trade route** (recurring income; `NONCOMBAT_REWARDS.md`), a Ledger factorship (authority over local commerce), and — for a true insider — a line on **quiet-coin** relics. Title: *Factor of the Ledger.* Late-game whispers of **The Ledger Eternal** (Legendary).
- **Opposition/Betrayal:** the Ledger calls your debts, freezes your credit, dispatches **collectors** (Collector-Captain Mauld Tallow, Tallytooth Ren), and blacklists you from markets. (DM-Only: Vyre would rather *own* you than kill you — betrayal becomes leverage, not just violence.)

## 3. The Mourners' Circle — *the grief-keepers*

- **Rank 1:** a **Mourner's Quiet Bell** (Common), grave-rites performed for you/your dead, and old-songs lore (oblique M5 truth). A **Mourner's Welcome-Knot**.
- **Rank 2:** rare herb/incense supply (**Grave-Incense**, **Mercy-Oil**), a **Mourner's Mute Lyre** (Rare sentient; `SENTIENT_ITEMS.md` #6) for the worthy, and spirit-lore that calms haunts.
- **Rank 3:** **The Grief-Glass** (Rare relic; `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md` #1) entrusted to lay to rest, **The Mercy-Lamp** (Very Rare), and the elders' deepest grief-truth ("the Concord ate the dead" — a major M6 corroboration, gated). Title: *Keeper of the Quiet.*
- **Opposition/Betrayal:** the Circle closes its doors, denies you rites (a real cost in the Reach — your dead won't rest), and the elders speak against you. If you *restart* the harvest, some will violently resist (overlap with Gravecallers).

## 4. The Reachward Compact — *the (infiltrated) government*

- **Rank 1:** a **Compact Courier-Seal**, a small stipend, watch cooperation, and legal standing as a recognized agent.
- **Rank 2:** a **Compact Writ-Cylinder** (Uncommon), a minor commission (a title with real local authority), warrant-writing power, and gate/road access.
- **Rank 3:** **The Compact Seal** (Rare; `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md` #5) and a true office (magistrate, reeve, commissioner) with land/property rights. Title: *Commissioner of the Reach.*
- **Opposition/Betrayal:** warrants for *your* arrest, revoked standing, watch turned against you.
- **DM-ONLY:** Compact rewards are the Hollow Court's cleanest leash. **The Compact Seal and the "Commendation Ring" (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #4) carry hidden surveillance** via Councilor Reke. The Compact's help is real and watched. Never reveal until Reke is investigated. An *honest* Compact patron (Magister-Reeve Sela Coalmont) can give the same ranks **clean** — reward players who route around Reke.

## 5. The Gravecallers — *the outlawed cult (dangerous rewards)*

- **Rank 1:** a Gravecaller **cult-mark** (safe passage among cells; danger if found by the Compact), forbidden fen-lore, and a **Memory-Smoke** stick (clue, with cost).
- **Rank 2:** **Greyspeak** the skull-bead (Rare sentient; `SENTIENT_ITEMS.md` #2), Concord **breaking-tools** (genuinely dangerous), and a real fragment of the truth (M5/M6 — "the dead were eaten; the shrines must break").
- **Rank 3:** **The Gravecaller's Knock** (Uncommon cursed; `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md` #4 / `CURSED_ITEMS.md` #1), the Tallow Man's channeled testimony (a deep M6 witness), and standing to **lead a breaking**. Title: *Voice of the Drowned.*
- **Opposition/Betrayal:** the cult curses you (a marked-by-the-dead hex), and the Tallow Man's followers harry you in the fens.
- **Caution:** every Gravecaller reward is double-edged — knowledge that is *true* but a method that is *catastrophic*. Telegraph this. The cult are tragic, sometimes-right, never safe.

## 6. The Concord Remnant — *the scholar-zealots*

- **Rank 1:** archive reading-access (lower stacks), **Script-Reader's Chalk**, a **Glassmere Clarity-Lens** (Uncommon), and Concord history lore.
- **Rank 2:** **Concord Script training** (a major access reward — unlocks Script-gated clues across the campaign; `NONCOMBAT_REWARDS.md`), research tools, maps of node-sites (the **Custodian's Compass**, Very Rare, for the trusted), and a Remnant patron (Lector Briss).
- **Rank 3:** **The Sealed Archive Key** (Very Rare; `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md` #6) — the truth-cache of M6/M9 — and standing among the Reclaimers; late-game, a claim on **The Ash-Crown Fragment**. Title: *Reclaimer-Scholar.*
- **Opposition/Betrayal:** the Archive is sealed against you, Reclaimers (Wessel Dree, far-region expedition captains) move to take back relics, and the Remnant brands you a vandal of history.
- **DM-Only:** the inner circle (Quorrin Vane, Lector-General Sabine Ferrant) wants to *control* the harvest. High-rank rewards come with pressure to help them — a moral trap dressed as scholarship. The rank-and-file are idealistic dupes; reward players who exploit that split.

## 7. The Hollow Court — *the apex hidden power (NO OPEN REWARDS)*

> **DM-ONLY in its entirety as a "reward track." Never present the Court as a faction the player can openly join and farm rewards from.**

- The Court does not give open rewards. **Every "gift" that traces back to the Court is a trap, a curse, a leash, or a compromise:** the Commendation Ring (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #4), the Helpful Stranger's Ring (`SENTIENT_ITEMS.md` #4), the Compass That Lies (`CURSED_ITEMS.md` #9), the Compact Seal's surveillance (#5).
- **If the player late-game *allies* with the Court** (a valid endgame path), "rewards" are power-with-a-price: a measure of control over the harvest, unnatural longevity, authority — all costing the player's freedom, conscience, or humanity. These are **endgame choices** (M10), not loot.
- **Never** hand the player a clean, beneficial Court item before the apex is earned. The Court's currency is corruption, not generosity.

## City Factions (Caradril) — secondary reward tracks

### The Salt Syndicate (smuggling / relic black market)
- **R1:** safe-passage through the Sill, fence access (sell anything, no questions). **R2:** **Smuggler's Quiet-Dust**, smuggler routes (`NONCOMBAT_REWARDS.md`), the **Hush-Knife** (Rare cursed; `CURSED_ITEMS.md` #8) for sale. **R3:** a cut of the relic black market (quiet-coin access), a safe-house. **Betrayal:** the docks turn deadly; "Mother" Silque sends knives.

### The Hush (information / blackmail brokers)
- **R1:** a single bought secret, **Truth-Salt**. **R2:** the Bellman's broker-access (buy/sell information; `NONCOMBAT_REWARDS.md`), surveillance favors. **R3:** a standing line of intelligence, leverage on a Charter House. **Betrayal:** your secrets become the city's gossip.

### The Charter Houses / Tidewater Council (legitimate power)
- **R1:** an introduction, a small civic favor. **R2:** a Charter House patron (Imris Vane-Tolm), legal protection, a civic medal. **R3:** a **charter share / trade-house seat**, property in Caradril, a council swing-vote's ear (`NONCOMBAT_REWARDS.md`). **Betrayal:** charters revoked, Tide-Watch attention.

### The Tide-Watch (city law)
- **R1:** watch goodwill, a deputization token. **R2:** Captain Kade's cooperation, access to the Watch's records. **R3:** a true commission (lawful authority in Caradril). **Betrayal:** wanted in the city.

## Cross-Faction Reward Rules

1. **Helping rivals costs you.** Major rewards from one faction can lower standing with its enemies (`../07_factions/FACTION_RELATIONSHIP_MAP.md`). The solo PC can't please everyone — that's the point.
2. **Relics are loyalty tests.** A faction relic is given when you're trusted and *taken back* (or hunted) when you betray.
3. **Noncombat parity.** Faction rewards are mostly access/standing/tools — see `NONCOMBAT_REWARDS.md` for the political/social/economic detail.
4. **The Court is never a vendor.** Re-read §7 every time the player is "rewarded" by a Compact agent.
