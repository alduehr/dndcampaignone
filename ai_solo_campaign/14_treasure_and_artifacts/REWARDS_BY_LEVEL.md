## Reward Richness By Tier

| Tier | Levels | What rewards should feel like |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 1–4 | Small and grounded. Coin in silver/gold, a few consumables, one or two uncommon items across the tier, favors and information that open doors. A single early magic item feels significant. Solo survivability aids (healing, a defensive trinket) are the most valuable finds. |
| Tier 2 | 5–8 | Regional stakes. Uncommon items become normal; the first rare item appears. Faction standing, property, and named favors matter as much as gear. Caradril unlocks paid services and the city market. Relics tied to factions (Warden/Mourner/Ledger) can enter play. |
| Tier 3 | 9–12 | Real power. Rare items expected, the first very rare item. Rewards begin carrying lore weight (Concord relics, Sealed Archive access). Money matters less than access, leverage, and answers. |
| Tier 4a | 13–16 | World-scale. Very rare items, the first legendary. Endgame artifacts begin to appear (gated). Rewards reshape the player's options and the world's reaction to them. |
| Tier 4b | 17–20 | Legendary and final. Legendary items and the apex artifacts that determine the ending. Rewards are decisions as much as prizes — what the player does with the harvest-class relics shapes the endgame. |

## Artifact Gating (summary; authority is `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md`)

Endgame artifacts are level- and revelation-gated. In particular:
- **The Harvest Engine Shard** — L16+, M8 (apex) revelation required; DM-only.
- **The Quiet Country Vessel** — L13+, needs M8 to use safely; DM-gated.
- **The Last Voice** — L17+; DM-gated.
- **The Custodian's Compass** / **Sealed Archive Key** — L9+, M6/M7-gated.

Full gates, access conditions, and mechanics are defined in `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md`. Do not surface gated apex artifacts (or their true nature) before the player has earned the corresponding revelation.
