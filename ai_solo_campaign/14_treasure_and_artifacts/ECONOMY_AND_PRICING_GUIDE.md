## AI Use

Load when the player buys, sells, bribes, hires, or values something. Keeps prices consistent and regionally flavored. Standard 5e/2024 coin and goods pricing applies as the baseline; this file layers Orrun's **regional economies, coinage variants, gem/art bands, and services** on top. For *what's available where*, see `REGIONAL_TREASURE_TABLES.md`.

## Design Note (campaign economy)

Orrun runs on a **NW(cold-poor) → SE(warm-rich) axis** (per `../03_canon/CANON.md`). The Reach is genuinely poor — a single gp matters there. Caradril and the Glassmere League are wealthy and bank-driven. The far regions vary (steppe barter, theocratic tithe, port-state coin). The Cinder Ledger is the closest thing to a continental currency authority, which is itself a plot pressure (the Monopoly clock).

## Coinage

- **Standard denominations:** cp, sp, ep (rare/old), gp, pp. Use normal 5e exchange (10 cp = 1 sp, 10 sp = 1 gp, 10 gp = 1 pp; ep = 5 sp).
- **Ledger-stamped coin:** Caradril and the trade-cities prefer Cinder Ledger-stamped gp/pp — guaranteed weight, accepted everywhere the Ledger reaches. Unstamped coin may be weighed/discounted in Ledger markets.
- **Salt-marks (Pale Coast):** salt-silver tokens used as currency on the Coast; ~1 sp each; outsiders' coin is fine but salt-marks get local goodwill.
- **Barter (Wender Steppe, deep frontier, Sunmark groves):** coin is secondary to goods, horses, hospitality, and favors. Flashing gold can offend or mark you.
- **Tithe-script (Emberfell/Ashfast):** the theocracy runs on penance-tokens and tithe; pilgrims pay in service as much as coin.
- **Concord road-tokens:** ancient Concord coins still circulate as curiosities. **Worth ~1 gp each as currency**, but **worth more to Concord Remnant scholars** (5–25 gp, sometimes more for rare mintings) and a clue-flag for the harvest network. A handful is pocket-change; a hoard is a research lead.

> **Remembrance relics are special.** A verified whole relic that demonstrably "holds the dead" is worth 2,500 gp+ to the Ledger, Remnant, or Gravecallers — but **selling one is a faction-charged choice**, not a clean payday (Mourners/Wardens disapprove; it can mark the seller; the Ledger may try to *own* the seller). ## Magic Item Pricing (rough, for buying/selling where allowed)

Most magic items are **not freely for sale** — they are found, earned, or faction-granted (`SOLO_REWARD_BALANCE.md`). Where a market exists (Caradril Ashmarket, Glassmere), use these *rough* bands as a ceiling, and remember the campaign deliberately gates flat-bonus gear:

| Rarity | Rough value range | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 50–100 gp | apothecaries, shrine-towns, markets |
| Uncommon | 101–500 gp | Caradril, Glassmere, faction grants |
| Rare | 501–5,000 gp | rarely sold; mostly earned |
| Very Rare | 5,001–50,000 gp | almost never sold; quest/dungeon/faction only |
| Legendary | 50,000 gp+ | never for sale; placed intentionally |
| Artifact | priceless / cannot be bought | M6–M9 gated; never a market item |

Consumable prices: see the band table below and `CONSUMABLES_AND_MINOR_MAGIC.md`.

| Consumable | Rough price |
|---|---|
| Common potion/charm (healing vial, shrine-water, grave-incense) | 25–50 gp |
| Uncommon potion/scroll (greater healing, 2nd-level scroll, tonic) | 100–300 gp |
| Rare consumable (superior healing, 3rd-level scroll, mercy-oil) | 300–1,200 gp |
| Resurrection scroll (Reach-risky) | scarce; 1,000 gp+ and a hard search |

## Regional Economy Snapshots

- **Sundering Reach:** poor, barter-heavy, peat/salt/mining; coin is precious; Ledger toll-pressure; "old glass" relic trade lurks beneath honest commerce.
- **Ashgarden Vale:** agrarian, cider/grain; pilgrim economy at Saint Veddow's (where "saint's-bones" relics are sold as faith — a trap market).
- **Tollwood:** timber, charcoal, toll-roads (Tollmen banditry); forest-rules barter; contraband.
- **Pale Coast:** fishing, salt, salvage; salt-marks; the Ledger's sea-relic monopoly; wreck salvage (dangerous).
- **Caradril:** banking capital of the NW; charters, guilds, the Ashmarket relic-trade (legal and quiet-coin); the richest hub the early-mid player will see.
- **Glassmere League:** mercantile city-league; glasswork, banking; the Ledger's true heartland; high prices, high intrigue.
- **Verdance Reaches / Marrowdowns / Sallowmarch / Hethewald / Sunmark:** mixed agrarian/forest/delta/grove economies; regional goods; modest coin.
- **Hollow Gulf Ports:** rival port city-states; sea-trade, salvage, privateering; letters of marque as currency-of-violence.
- **Wender Steppe:** pastoral barter; horses, hides, sky-stone craft; coin is for outsiders.
- **Karran Marches / Highmark Passes:** ore, iron, mercenary pay; warlord tribute; superior mountain-metal commands premiums.
- **Emberfell/Ashfast / Cindern Waste:** tithe-and-penance economy; obsidian, fire-glass, ember-relics.
- **Saltmere Reaches / Drowned Steps:** salt-clan goods, drowned-town/causeway salvage; preservation a prized craft.
- **Concord Heartlands:** no living economy — a *ruin economy* of expedition salvage and relic-trade; the most relic-rich, most contested ground (Reclaimers, Court agents).

## DM-Only Economic Notes

- The **Ledger's continental reach** lets it lowball relic-sellers and trace coin (the Ledger of Quiet Debts proves this). Selling relics through the Ledger feeds the Monopoly clock (#6).
- **Quiet-coin** (black-market relics rumored to hold the dead) trades in the Ashmarket and Glassmere underworld — buying/selling it draws the Hush, the Syndicate, and eventually the Court's notice.
- The Reach's poverty is partly the harvest's long shadow (the region drains toward the keystone) — a thematic, not just economic, fact. Do not over-explain in early play.
