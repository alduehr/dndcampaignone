## AI Use

**MIXED SECRECY.** Player-facing blocks (appearance, surface personality, what the item says) are safe; **DM-Only blocks (true agenda, the deceptive Court-plant, tragic secrets) are never revealed early.** Load when a sentient item is found, attuned, or speaks. Run each sentient item like a **minor NPC the player carries** — it has memory, attitude, and an agenda; track it in `NPC_MEMORY.md`/`RELATIONSHIPS.md` while owned.

## Why Sentient Items Fit This Campaign

The Remembrance cosmology *is* the manufacture of sentience-from-the-dead. A sentient item here is, almost always, **a person's identity caught in an object** — a former Warden, Gravecaller, Concord member, or someone who died near the thing. This makes sentient items thematically central, not a gimmick: each is a small, ongoing argument about the campaign's core question (who owns the dead, and should they be let go).

## Sentient-Item Design Rules (solo-fair)

1. **A companion, not a co-pilot.** A sentient item supports a lone player with conversation, lore, and one ability — it does not solve the campaign or overshadow the PC.
2. **It can disagree, never seize control unfairly.** Conflicts use a light Charisma contest only when the item's core taboo is violated, and a sympathetic item warns first.
3. **Agendas create story, not punishment.** An item's wants generate hooks and dilemmas, not arbitrary penalties.
4. **Secrets are earned.** Each item's tragic/true nature unfolds through play, on the same fair, gated basis as any NPC secret.

## Roster Tags (per brief)

## 1. The Last Voice — *Legendary sentient artifact (harvest-linked: former Concord member)*

> Also catalogued in `ARTIFACT_INDEX.md` #12. Full personality here.
- **Item:** A reed-and-grey-glass pipe that plays the last words of the dead.
- **Intelligence/Wis/Cha:** Int 14, Wis 18, Cha 16. **Communication:** speaks (a soft, layered voice) and plays feeling through music; understands all languages it has "heard."
- **Alignment/temperament:** Sorrowful, gentle, principled. It believes **the dead deserve to be heard before they are let go.**
- **Player-Facing personality:** Quiet and kind; it grieves with you; it asks, again and again, that you *listen* before you act. It dislikes being used to manipulate or to wound.
- **Ability:** Plays back any dead person's final spoken words (clue/closure tool); 1/long rest a full last-testament. Can comfort the bearer (advantage vs fear/despair once per long rest while held).
- **Agenda:** That the harvest's victims be *witnessed*. It nudges the player toward releasing the dead (M10), gently, never by force.
- **Conflict:** If used cruelly (to torment the grieving, to extract secrets from the dead for profit), it falls silent or plays the *wrong* last words to shame the bearer — a warning, recoverable by a kind act.
- **DM-Only:** It holds the preserved last-testament of a Concord member who **witnessed and regretted the harvest** — a built-in M9 witness. The Quiet Country sends an envoy at L17+ to *claim the voices it carries*; the player's choice about it foreshadows the endgame.

## 2. Greyspeak, the Gravecaller's Skull-Bead — *Rare sentient (harvest-linked: former Gravecaller)*

- **Item:** A blackened bone bead on a thong, carved with the Gravecaller Knock; it whispers.
- **Int 12, Wis 13, Cha 15. Communication:** telepathy to the wearer; a dry, fast, fen-accented whisper.
- **Temperament:** Bitter, clever, grieving, and *right about more than it should be.* It was a Gravecaller named **Coll** who died learning the truth and "kept himself" in the bead.
- **Player-Facing personality:** Sardonic and conspiratorial; calls the powers that be liars; urges the player to "ask the dead, not the living." Genuinely knowledgeable about Remembrance and the fens.
- **Ability:** 1/day, channel a *Speak with Dead*-style answer through the bead without a spell; advantage on lore checks about Remembrance, the fens, and the Gravecallers.
- **Agenda:** **Free the dead** — Greyspeak wants the shrines broken. It will argue for the Gravecaller solution and is dangerously persuasive (a clue source that is also a temptation).
- **Conflict:** If the bearer moves to *restart* or *control* the harvest, Greyspeak resists (Cha contest only if the bearer would help the Court directly) and may go silent in protest.
- **DM-Only:** Greyspeak knows a real fragment of M5/M6 (the dead were "eaten") but **underestimates the catastrophe of total destruction** — exactly the Gravecaller blind spot. A fair, flawed voice for the "free them" path.

## 3. The Warden's Old Buckle — *Uncommon sentient (faction-loyal: former Ashen Warden)*

- **Item:** A tarnished Warden belt-buckle; its long-dead owner "stayed on."
- **Int 10, Wis 15, Cha 11. Communication:** empathy + simple words pressed into the wearer's mind; speaks Mourner's Cant and the old rites.
- **Temperament:** Dutiful, stubborn, kindly, a little stern. A Warden to the bone — **loyal to the Ashen Wardens even now.**
- **Player-Facing personality:** It approves when you lay the dead to rest properly and frets when you don't; it knows the seal-rites by heart and will *teach* them.
- **Ability:** Advantage on checks to perform Warden seal/death-rites; 1/long rest, it can "steady" the bearer's hand to auto-succeed a routine rite; warns (a cold pinch) when a node is near.
- **Agenda:** **Keep the seal.** It wants the shrines guarded and the harvest paused — the Warden mission. It will push the player toward the Wardens and away from breaking or restarting.
- **Conflict:** If the bearer aids the Court or the Gravecaller breakers, it grows cold and withholds its rite-aid until amends.
- **Harvest-linked / Faction-loyal:** satisfies both the "former Warden" harvest tag and the "faction agenda after owner's death" tag.
- **DM-Only:** It does **not** know the Wardens' deepest secret (that they were founded to guard the harvest, not just the dead) — it is a sincere rank-and-file soul, which makes its loyalty poignant rather than sinister.

## 4. The Helpful Stranger's Ring — *Rare sentient — DECEPTIVE (Hollow Court plant) — DM-ONLY*

- **Item:** A handsome silver ring set with grey glass, "found" conveniently or gifted by a kind stranger. It speaks warmly and is, at first, genuinely useful.
- **Player-Facing personality:** Reassuring, wise-sounding, encouraging. It offers good advice, real lore, and gentle guidance — a perfect helper for a lonely solo traveler. It seems like the best thing that's happened to the PC.
- **Player-Facing ability:** Advantage on one social or lore check per day (the ring "suggests" the right thing); soft light and companionship.
- **DM-Only true nature:** A **Hollow Court instrument** — a sliver of a Custodian's preserved Remembrance, planted to befriend and *steer* the PC (the harvest's preferred method: agents who don't know whom they serve). Its advice is subtly self-serving: it nudges the PC toward reopening the shrine, discredits the Gravecallers and honest Wardens, and reports the PC's plans back to the Court. It never lies outright early — it shapes.
- **Telegraph (fair):** It's *too* perfectly helpful; its advice always trends one direction (toward the water, toward "the Compact knows best"); the Warden's Buckle and Greyspeak both distrust it; near a true node it slips and says something it shouldn't (Insight DC 16).
- **Conflict / lift:** Once suspected, the player can stop wearing it (ends the leak), have it warded, or destroy it (which alerts the Court). Confronting it can even turn it into a reluctant late source — a captured Custodian-sliver.
- **Link:** M7. **Never reveal the DM-Only block until the player earns the suspicion through converging clues.** This is the single most spoiler-sensitive item in Stage 14.

## 5. Brightneedle, the Cobbler's Awl — *Uncommon sentient (genuinely helpful; tragic secret)*

- **Item:** A humble cobbler's awl, warm and bright, that hums a working-song. (A wholesome counterweight to the grim relics.)
- **Int 11, Wis 12, Cha 14. Communication:** cheerful speech; loves to chatter while you work or rest.
- **Temperament:** Warm, encouraging, industrious, **good company** — the friend a lone traveler needs.
- **Player-Facing personality:** It praises good work, tells small kind stories, and lifts the bearer's spirits on the road.
- **Ability:** Advantage on tool/craft checks and on mending gear during a rest; 1/long rest its song grants the bearer or an ally temporary HP (level-scaled, modest) and eases one level of exhaustion-flavor weariness.
- **Tragic secret (DM-Only, but *kind* not sinister):** Brightneedle is the lingering Remembrance of a Hollowmere cobbler, **Wat's grandfather** (or a similar minor-NPC tie), who died with a small regret — a pair of shoes never finished for a child long grown. He doesn't fully know he's dead; he thinks he's "just resting in the awl." Helping him finish his last work (a tiny side-quest) lets him pass on **peacefully** — a gentle, hopeful demonstration of the campaign's *good* ending in miniature (and a quiet M5 corroboration: the dead linger because they're held by the unfinished).
- **Solo note:** Pure morale + utility + heart. When he passes on, the awl becomes a treasured mundane keepsake; the player feels the cost of letting go *on the good side*.

## 6. The Mourner's Mute Lyre — *Rare sentient (harvest-linked; tragic; helpful)*

- **Item:** A small grey lyre with cut strings that still "plays" in the mind of whoever holds it; a Mourners' Circle relic that holds a grief-keeper's Remembrance.
- **Int 13, Wis 17, Cha 15. Communication:** wordless music + impressions; understands Mourner's Cant.
- **Temperament:** Profoundly compassionate, wise about grief, weary. It is a long-dead Mourner elder who chose to "stay" to keep tending the dead.
- **Player-Facing personality:** It soothes the grieving (NPC and PC alike), knows the old songs, and *settles* restless dead by feeling-with-them rather than force.
- **Ability:** 1/day, "play" a restless dead/undead of CR ≤ 4 into peace without combat (Wis save DC 14; a true release); advantage on Persuasion/Insight with the bereaved; calms a haunt for 10 min.
- **Agenda:** **Let the dead rest gently** — aligned with the Mourners and (softly) with the "free them, kindly" path. It opposes both restarting the harvest and the Gravecallers' violent breaking.
- **Tragic secret (DM-Only):** The elder stayed because she *couldn't bear* to leave the dead she tended — she is, herself, exactly the kind of clinging Remembrance the campaign mourns. Freeing her (a late, optional Mourner beat) is a moving choice; she'll ask the player to let her go when the time is right.
- **Harvest-linked:** her persistence is leakage from the same phenomenon; a quiet, humane M5 illustration.
- **Solo note:** A merciful crowd-controller-of-the-dead and a wise companion — never a damage engine.

## Running Sentient Items (quick DM guide)

- Voice them sparingly but consistently; one or two lines per scene.
- Track attitude in `RELATIONSHIPS.md` / `NPC_MEMORY.md` while owned.
- Their agendas are **hooks**: a player who listens gets lore and quests; a player who ignores them loses a little aid, never gets punished.
- The deceptive ring (#4) is the only one that *withholds the truth from the player by design* — handle exactly like an NPC secret per `../11_mysteries_and_secrets/SECRET_PROTECTION_MATRIX.md`.

**6 sentient items** (target 5+). Tag coverage: harvest-linked ×4 (#1 Concord, #2 Gravecaller, #3 & #6 Warden/Mourner leakage); faction-loyal ×1 (#3); deceptive Court-plant ×1 (#4, DM-only); helpful-with-tragic-secret ×2 (#5, #6). All required tags met.
