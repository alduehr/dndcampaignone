## AI Use

**Check the authored roster first** — `../08_npcs/MINOR_NPCS.md` and the settlement's own file almost always have someone who fits (500+ minor NPCs exist so you rarely need this). Use these tables only when play genuinely demands a *new* incidental figure. Then: give them the standard Tier-3 fields (name, role, location, hook, useful detail, **combat relevance tag**) and, if they mattered, record them per `../16_ai_session_packs/SESSION_END_UPDATE_CHECKLIST.md` §14 and check `../00_control/NAMING_REGISTRY.md` before promoting them.

## d12 — Manner (how they carry themselves)

1 Talks to fill silence · 2 Answers in as few words as the law allows · 3 Warm to strangers, wary of neighbors · 4 Wary of strangers, warm to neighbors · 5 Precise, corrects small errors · 6 Deflects with jokes · 7 Devout — invokes the Thresholds constantly · 8 Tired in the bones; honest about it · 9 Ambitious and bad at hiding it · 10 Kind by reflex, flustered by thanks · 11 Suspicious of paper, contracts, and clerks · 12 Old-fashioned courtesy, ironclad

## d12 — Concern (what's on their mind today)

1 A debt (owed or owing) · 2 A child/parent/sibling worry · 3 The weather vs. their livelihood · 4 A neighbor-feud, years deep · 5 The rites — a funeral coming, or one that went wrong (NW cluster: the campaign's pulse) · 6 A faction's local pressure (pick the one with stakes here) · 7 Their health, minimized aloud · 8 Money for a specific small dream · 9 A rumor that scared them (roll `RUMOR_SURFACES.md`) · 10 Work piling past bearing · 11 Something they saw and wish they hadn't (fringe-clue at DM discretion, gated) · 12 Nothing — today is a good day, infectious

## d10 — Useful Detail (why the PC remembers them)

1 Knows every face that passed this week · 2 Kin in a neighboring settlement (name it from the authored roster) · 3 Skilled hands (name a trade tool proficiency) · 4 Keeps the local custom exactly (rites, tolls, forest-rules) · 5 Owes/is owed a favor by someone notable (authored NPC) · 6 Remembers the old songs (M5-fringe at DM discretion) · 7 Feeds travelers; hears everything (rumor node) · 8 Watch-adjacent — deputized in trouble · 9 Distrusts a specific faction, with a story · 10 Has exactly the mundane item/knowledge the scene needs, for a fair price

## d8 — Voice Tic (per `NPC_VOICE_GUIDE.md` style — short and usable)

1 Ends statements as questions? · 2 Names the listener often ("friend," "goodman," the PC's name once learned) · 3 Trails off before anything important · 4 Repeats the last word said to them, thinking · 5 Swears by a threshold ("by the Door") · 6 Speaks of themselves in the plural ("we manage") · 7 Pauses before names, checking they're safe to say · 8 Everything is a comparison to weather, stock, or tools

## Combat Relevance (required Tier-3 tag; per `NPC_STANDARDS.md`)

d10 — 1–6: **none** (Commoner, 2024 MM) · 7–8: **commoner-like** (will swing something once) · 9: **guard-like** (Guard, 2024 MM; militia past or watch-adjacent) · 10: **specialist** (one notable proficiency — hunter's bow, barge-pole, smith's hammer; still not a fighter)

## Names

Reuse the campaign's established name-stock and surname families (Pell, Wend, Sael, Coalmont, Tull, Strand, Brunt, Marrin…) per `NAMING_REGISTRY.md` conventions — **check the registry's disambiguation notes before reusing a loaded surname**, and never coin a new *major*-styled name on the fly.
