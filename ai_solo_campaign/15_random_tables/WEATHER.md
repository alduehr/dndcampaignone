## AI Use

Roll when a scene or travel leg needs weather. Weather is a **decision-maker, not just flavor** (`TRAVEL_PROTOCOL.md`): fog hides routes and the dead, floods close causeways, cold forces shelter choices. The campaign opens in **Greyfall (mid-autumn), AQ 101** — start with the Autumn column. Seasons per `../03_canon/CALENDAR.md`: autumn = Reaping/Greyfall/Hollowing; winter follows with Firstfrost.

## Zone Notes (apply on top of the master roll)

- **Cold-wet fen:** all rain results also raise water — causeway flooding on a second consecutive wet day (`The Drowned Mile`-style closures); fog results are **the Reach's signature** — visibility ~60 ft, navigation Survival DC +2, and the dead walk oftener in fog (flavor, not mechanics).
- **Deep forest:** halve wind results (canopy); double fog/mist duration in hollows; falling-branch hazard in storms (Dex DC 12 if camped badly).
- **Storm coast:** results 5–10 can become **gales** — no small-boat travel; wreck-chance on the reefs (wreckers get busy; links: the Lantern Stacks); the salt-spray cold soaks gear.
- **Temperate vale:** kindest zone; weather is mood more than hazard except harvest-rain (roads to mud) and the rare downs-storm (no cover on the open downs).
- **Warm river/south:** rain is warm but the **rivers answer it** — flood stretch DCs +2 the day after hard rain; Sallowmarch: every wet result also feeds fever-season (see region file DCs).
- **Cold steppe/mountain:** wind is the killer — add a Con check (DC 12–15) for a full day's travel in results 5, 7, 10; whiteouts stop travel; pass-closures in winter are absolute (Highmark).
- **Ash highland:** replace rain results with **ash-fall** (visibility, breathing — Con DC 12–14 without cover), and storm results with **pyroclastic weather** near the caldera (region file rules override).
- **Salt flat:** replace rain with **glare** (Wis (Perception) disadvantage at distance; mirage) and **heat/thirst** pressure (water supplies per `TRAVEL_PROTOCOL.md`); the rare true rain briefly floods the pans.

## Weather As Decision (reminders)

- Offer the choice: push through (checks), shelter (time), or reroute (distance). Never just narrate suffering.
- Fog and night multiply *encounter ambiguity* — telegraphs shorten; let cautious players slow down instead of rolling more.
- A storm is a scene: where to shelter, with whom, what's overheard (roll `RUMOR_SURFACES.md` at any shared shelter).
- Season turns are campaign texture: Greyfall → Hollowing (the Nameday of the Dead, tense in AQ 101) → winter's travel constraints are real planning pressure.
