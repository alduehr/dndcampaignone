## AI Use

Roll **once per travel leg** (or once per day on multi-day journeys) when you want texture beyond the encounter check. These are **events and complications, not fights** — for creature encounters use `../13_encounters_and_bestiary/TRAVEL_ENCOUNTERS.md` and the regional `*_ENCOUNTERS.md` tables. Pick the table matching the route class (route classes per `../04_world_atlas/TRAVEL_ROUTES_RING1.md` and the region map packets). Results marked **[Clue]** deliver only gated fragments per `../11_mysteries_and_secrets/` rules.

## How Events And Encounters Fit Together

Per `TRAVEL_PROTOCOL.md`: each leg gets **one** meaningful beat unless the fiction demands more. Roll d6 first — 1–2: weather beat (see `WEATHER.md`), 3–4: event (below), 5–6: encounter check (`TRAVEL_ENCOUNTERS.md`).

## Table A — Concord Roads (NW cluster: South/East/Pale roads, Mirewend causeways; Lvl 1–6)

| d12 | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 | A funeral procession bound for the nearest Mourner's ground — walking with it earns folk trust (a `RUMOR_SURFACES.md` roll from the mourners) |
| 2 | A Compact toll-post or road-levy; papers, questions, a bored clerk who talks (Persuasion DC 12 for road-news) |
| 3 | A Ledger caravan — guarded, hurried, low on patience; a factor who might buy, sell, or hire (links: `../07_factions/major_factions/CINDER_LEDGER.md`) |
| 4 | Road damage — a flooded stretch or slumped causeway-stone; detour (half-day) or ford (per `TRAVEL_PROTOCOL.md` DCs) |
| 5 | A Warden walking the road alone; courteous, watchful, bound for a shrine (links: Ashen Wardens; honest travel-aid) |
| 6 | Pilgrims bound for Saint Veddow's Rest — gossip, small needs, one carrying something they shouldn't (Insight DC 13) **[Clue: M3 fringe]** |
| 7 | A carter with a broken axle; an hour's help earns a ride, a meal, and a `RUMOR_SURFACES.md` roll |
| 8 | A star-stone or field-shrine by the road, recently visited — fresh offering, fresh footprints (Survival DC 12 to read them) |
| 9 | Peddler with mixed wares — mundane goods, one oddity (roll `../14_treasure_and_artifacts/CONSUMABLES_AND_MINOR_MAGIC.md` for a common item at fair price) |
| 10 | Weather turns — roll on `WEATHER.md` for the current season and region |
| 11 | A place where the road remembers: cold air, lanterns dimming at dusk — atmosphere only, no threat **[Clue: M5 atmosphere]** |
| 12 | Another traveler in genuine trouble (mired, robbed, sick) — helping is a hook-door; roll `RUMOR_SURFACES.md` for what they know |

## Table B — Wild Country (fens, deep wood, downs, heights; any region's off-road)

| d12 | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 | Ground trouble — sinking peat / bramble ravine / scree slope (per region file hazard DCs) |
| 2 | A local guide's marker (Sashe's poles, Brann's blazes, shepherd cairns) — following it is safe; ignoring it invites row 1 |
| 3 | Game trail and clean water — good camp; restful night (no rest interruption) |
| 4 | Signs of a predator's range — old kills, claw-marks (telegraph for the regional apex; see the region's `*_ENCOUNTERS.md`) |
| 5 | A charcoal-burners' / peat-cutters' / shepherds' camp — fire, food, talk (roll `RUMOR_SURFACES.md`) |
| 6 | An old boundary-stone or offering-place, still kept (Religion DC 12: whose custom this is) **[Clue: forest-rules / folk-rites]** |
| 7 | Lost — fog, canopy, or featureless downs (Survival DC 13–15 by region; failure costs hours, not progress) |
| 8 | A ruin-edge not on your route — visible, dark, older than the road (link the nearest authored site from `../10_dungeons_and_ruins/DUNGEON_INDEX.md`; do not invent a new one) |
| 9 | Weather turns hard — roll `WEATHER.md`; wild-country weather bites (shelter check per `TRAVEL_PROTOCOL.md`) |
| 10 | Someone else's camp, cold — a day old; what they left says who they were (Investigation DC 13) |
| 11 | The dead, distant — a drifting figure at the edge of sight, facing away (toward the basin, if NW cluster) **[Clue: M5; C-M5 corroboration]** |
| 12 | Small mercy — berries in season, a dry hollow, a view worth the climb; grant advantage on the next travel check |

## Table C — River & Barge (the Mirewend, the Verdance, the Glasswater, the Hethe)

| d10 | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 | Lock/weir queue (Verdance: the Nine Locks) — hours of waiting among talkative bargefolk (roll `RUMOR_SURFACES.md` twice) |
| 2 | Snag or shallows — Vehicles (water) DC 12–15 by river; failure costs time or a soaking, not the cargo |
| 3 | A toll-boat (lawful or otherwise) — Compact levy on the Mirewend; outlaw tolls on the Hethe (links: Tollmen-style river toll-lords) |
| 4 | Barge in distress — holed, grounded, or short-handed; help earns passage and standing |
| 5 | Fog on the water — navigation slows; sounds carry wrong; the river feels *watched* (atmosphere; Greyfens/basin: **[Clue: M5]**) |
| 6 | Cargo inspection — someone official (or dressed official) wants a look below-decks |
| 7 | River-trade gossip — prices, closures, a route gone quiet (roll `RUMOR_SURFACES.md`) |
| 8 | "Bad water" ahead — bargefolk refuse a stretch; the long way costs a day, the short way is theirs to refuse (links: Verdance node-stretches) **[Clue: M2 oblique]** |
| 9 | Weather on the water — roll `WEATHER.md`; wind and flood change the river's DCs |
| 10 | A drowned thing surfaces — debris, grave-goods, or worse; salvage at your own judgement (Investigation DC 13; see the region's encounter file if it *moves*) |

## Table D — Sea Lanes (Pale Coast, Hollow Gulf, the Reach Lanes)

| d10 | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 | Weather-sign — the tide-readers call a blow; sail now or wait a day (see `WEATHER.md` coastal column) |
| 2 | Sail on the horizon — trader, privateer, or crown cutter (reaction per region; Hollow Gulf: links Roke Mallin's lanes) |
| 3 | Wreck-sign — floating spar, cargo, a name-board (salvage law differs by port; Investigation DC 12) |
| 4 | Port-officialdom — harbor dues, quarantine flags, a factor's queue (social beat; roll `RUMOR_SURFACES.md` at the harbor inn) |
| 5 | The salt-bell tolls / sea-Mourners at the tide-line — a drowned-rite in progress; respect earns trust **[Clue: drowned-tide, M5 oblique]** |
| 6 | Becalmed or headwinds — a day lost; crew talk (roll `RUMOR_SURFACES.md`) |
| 7 | Fishing fleet with news — catches, sightings, a stretch of sea "gone wrong" **[Clue: M5 maritime fringe]** |
| 8 | Stowaway, debtor, or runaway aboard — a small human problem with a faction edge |
| 9 | Sea-hazard — reef, race, or fog-bank; Vehicles (water) DC 13–16 by waters (the Wreck-Reef approaches are their own site rules) |
| 10 | Blue day — fair wind, good speed; arrive early and rested |

## Far-Continent Roads

For the far regions (Lvl 8+), use Table A for settled corridors (Glassmere roads, the Ash Roads, downs-roads) and Table B for wild country (steppe, Karran, deep Hethewald), then flavor by the region's map packet ("Encounter And Hazard Mechanics" section). Region-specific dangers (fever channels, ash-storms, barrow-fields) are in each region's `*_ENCOUNTERS.md` — this file never overrides them.
