## AI Use

Roll **once per downtime day or street scene** for living-settlement texture. Use the tier matching where the player is. Social *encounter* tables (with stat references) live in `../13_encounters_and_bestiary/CARADRIL_ENCOUNTERS.md` and the city map packets — this file is the connective color and small-event layer. Results marked **[Hook-door]** should route into an authored hook (`RUMOR_SURFACES.md` → the settlement's hooks file), never into invented content.

## Table A — Villages & Small Towns (Hollowmere-tier; any region)

| d12 | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 | Market day (or its eve) — stalls, strangers, prices; every settlement's hooks file has a market row **[Hook-door]** |
| 2 | A funeral — and in AQ 101, every funeral is watched with held breath (Reach/Ring 1: the rites' failure is the campaign's pulse) **[Hook-door]** |
| 3 | Public dispute — boundary, debt, water, or beast; the reeve/moot wants no trouble; a fair witness is welcome |
| 4 | The watch asks questions — someone new (the PC?) matches someone's description; a chance to make a friend or a file |
| 5 | Faction presence, low-key — a Ledger factor collecting, a Warden passing, a Mourner keeping (pick the faction with local stakes; see the settlement file) |
| 6 | Small kindness needed — a lost child, a stuck cart, a roof before the rain; folk remember help |
| 7 | Inn night — music, dice, and talk (roll `RUMOR_SURFACES.md` twice at the inn) |
| 8 | Petty crime — a cut purse, a pilfered smokehouse; small, but the accused matters to someone |
| 9 | A traveler arrives with news — roll `RUMOR_SURFACES.md` for a *neighboring* region's rumor |
| 10 | Work offered — honest, paid, local (roll the settlement's jobs table via `../09_quests/HOOKS_JOBS_RUMORS_INDEX.md`) **[Hook-door]** |
| 11 | An omen read aloud — weather-sign, bird-sign, or a Mourner's unease; folk-mood shifts (atmosphere; M5-adjacent in the NW cluster) |
| 12 | Festival or rite-day per `../03_canon/CALENDAR.md` (Greyfall: the Door's Opening; Hollowing 15: Nameday of the Dead) — the whole settlement is out |

## Table C — Far Cities (Glassmere / Calderport / Ashfast; Lvl 8+)

| d10 | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 | Exchange-floor day (Glassmere: the Floor) — fortunes move; the player's coin/credit is noticed |
| 2 | Port muster (Calderport: the Mardenmouth) — a fleet readies; press of work, watchers, and foreign faces (the Foreign Quarter) |
| 3 | Procession of the fire-faith (Ashfast) — ash-marked pilgrims; piety enforced in public (theocracy rules per region file) |
| 4 | A bank calls a debt (Glassmere: Three Bridges) — someone's ruin is public theatre; the Ledger–League war in miniature |
| 5 | City festival per the local calendar — league-fair, tide-blessing, or fire-rite (region packet flavor) |
| 6 | Law, foreign-flavored — the city's own watch works differently than home; a small compliance scene teaches the rules |
| 7 | News from home — a NW-cluster rumor arrives *distorted* (roll `RUMOR_SURFACES.md`; add drift) |
| 8 | The Reliquary opens its doors (Glassmere) — a public reading; Remnant faces; M6-adjacent scholarship on display (gated) |
| 9 | Underworld brush — Lowwater rackets / wreck-salvage courts / ash-market smuggling touch the player's business lightly |
| 10 | An audience is possible — a factor, port-mistress, or hierophant's aide can be reached today (route to the region's authored quests) **[Hook-door]** |

## Downtime Day (any settlement, quick)

d6 — 1: quiet day (rest counts, prices fair); 2: roll Table A/B/C; 3: a letter or message finds the player (faction or personal thread); 4: a follow-up from a past scene (an NPC from `../02_runtime_state/NPC_MEMORY.md` resurfaces); 5: faction clock tick becomes *visible* locally (see `../02_runtime_state/WORLD_CLOCKS.md`); 6: roll `RUMOR_SURFACES.md`.
