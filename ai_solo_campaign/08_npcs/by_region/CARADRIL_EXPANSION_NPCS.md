## Secondary NPCs (Tier 2 — competence, leverage, fallback profile)

### The Magisterium

**Clerk-Advocate Wystan Mool** — petitions clerk of Magisters' Hall; every suit, license, and appeal crosses his desk first. Motive: an orderly city and a quiet pension. Complication: he has noticed a pattern in whose petitions "get lost" (a thread toward the Reke-correspondent lead — DM: he holds *pattern*, not names; gated). Knows: procedure, precedent, which magister owes whom. Enters play: any council business. **Combat relevance:** none — Commoner; will not fight. Skills: bureaucratic mastery (Investigation/History on city records +6-equivalent), passive Insight 14.

**Under-Magister Corvin Dail** — junior magistrate handling the harbor courts. Motive: rise without owing the Charter Houses. Complication: Ledger credit paid for his robes; Provost Vell holds the note. Knows: harbor-court dockets, smuggling verdicts, which cases died quietly. Enters play: any legal trouble at the Stillwater. **Combat relevance:** noncombatant — Noble-like social profile; DC 14 Persuasion moves him if the law can plausibly follow.

### The Counting-Quays

**Weighmaster Essa Corve** — mistress of the public weigh-house; her stamp makes cargo legal. Motive: her stamp staying worth something. Complication: she has twice been offered triple wages to mis-weigh Ledger salvage crates and is running out of polite refusals. Knows: true cargo flows vs. declared ones (M3-fringe: the relic-crates weigh wrong). Enters play: any cargo, tariff, or smuggling scene. **Combat relevance:** commoner-like; keeps a quay-hook (club) behind the counter. Passive Perception 15 for weights, seals, and lies about either.

**Purser Jonet Vasser** — freelance ships' purser between contracts; hires onto whichever house pays. Motive: a berth that doesn't cheat her. Complication: she kept a copy of a manifest she was paid to burn (Syndicate-adjacent leverage). Knows: crew gossip on every quay. Enters play: hiring, passage, or dockside information. **Combat relevance:** Spy-like fallback (AC 12, ~22 HP) if cornered; prefers gangplanks and crowds.

### The Lantern Reach

**Lector Abbas Prine** — Collegium teaching-lector for first-year scripts; genial, overworked, beloved. Motive: students who stay curious and alive. Complication: he recognizes Concord Script better than his rank should allow and underplays it (he fears the Reclaimers' notice — DM: honest, not hidden-agenda). Knows: which students/scholars asked dangerous questions lately. Enters play: any Script or research scene below Archive tier. **Combat relevance:** noncombatant — Acolyte-like; one defensive cantrip-equivalent flourish at best.

**Bookbinder-Mistress Hava Lorn** — binds for the Collegium and privately for collectors. Motive: craft, and her daughter's apprenticeship fees. Complication: she rebound a water-damaged Concord ledger last winter and remembers the page she was told to pretend she didn't see (M6-fringe fragment; TRUST-gated, oblique). Knows: who collects what, and who pays for silence. Enters play: documents, forgeries (she refuses, but knows who wouldn't). **Combat relevance:** none — Commoner.

### The Ashmarket

**Provenancer Osma Kesh** — freelance authenticator of salvage pedigrees; the honest one (mostly). Motive: reputation — her word *is* her trade. Complication: she quietly flags "humming" pieces and sells the flag to the Wardens' friends, at cost, out of conscience (M3 lever; the Syndicate suspects a leak). Knows: real vs. forged provenance, which lots moved through quiet-coin channels. Enters play: any relic purchase, sale, or fraud. **Combat relevance:** noncombatant — Spy-like Insight/Investigation, no fight in her; runs to Watch-friends.

### Highmourn

**Sister-Almoner Bess Vray** — Threshold Cathedral's almoner; runs the death-dole for paupers' funerals. Motive: no one buried unnamed. Complication: the dole's ledger shows more paupers' rites *failing* this year — she has started keeping a private count (M5 corroboration, player-safe as far as her count goes). Knows: every poor family's dead for a decade; the Quiet Houses' schedules. Enters play: funerals, charity, the city's grief. **Combat relevance:** none — Commoner; the crowd protects her.

### The Crucible

**Pattern-Master Ulric Gorse** — senior pattern-maker of the foundry guilds; Grandmaster Cole's steadiest rival-in-craft. Motive: the guild's standards outliving Cole's politics. Complication: he was asked to cast a "bell that doesn't ring" from Ashmarket bronze and declined — but kept the drawings (a relic-smelting thread; feeds the Foundress Harrow arc). Knows: what the foundries are *actually* pouring. Enters play: metalwork, guild politics, sabotage. **Combat relevance:** commoner-like; foundry hammer, foundry arms — one good swing (treat Thug-like STR, no tactics).

### The Sill

**Dockmother Reyna Marsh** — unofficial mother-of-the-rafts; settles Sill disputes before they reach knife or Watch. Motive: the Sill surviving everyone's plans for it. Complication: both Renna Sill (openly) and "Tidewife" Sorrel (quietly) court her voice; she trusts neither entirely. Knows: everything the Sill sees, which is everything the river carries. Enters play: any Sill scene; the human door to the district. **Combat relevance:** noncombatant — but ten raft-folk (Commoner/Thug-like) appear if she raises her voice. Passive Insight 15.

### The Sunken Wards (edge)

**Sump-Warden Cobb** — the Sealgate's semi-official watchman; paid in coin by the Council and in silence by delvers. Motive: no one dying on his stair (his ledger of who went down is the district's grim joke — and accurate). Complication: he lets Old Pater Dunk's people through and reports fewer descents than occur. Knows: who went down, who came up, and the difference. Enters play: any Sunken Wards approach. **Combat relevance:** guard-like (Guard, AC 16, ~11 HP); fights only to hold the gate, shouts for the Watch otherwise.

### Citywide

**Watch-Sergeant Pia Rusk** — Tide-Watch sergeant on the rotating district beat; Captain Kade's best sergeant and Sister-Commander Ansele's least favorite. Motive: the law meaning the same thing in the Sill as in the Magisterium. Complication: her honesty is being *managed* — transfers keep landing her wherever nothing is happening. Knows: patrol patterns, which posts are bought. Enters play: any Watch encounter where the player deserves fairness. **Combat relevance:** Veteran-like (AC 17, ~58 HP) — dangerous, disciplined, morale-solid; never fights dirty.

**Harbormaster's Deputy Coram Tench** — deputy at the Stillwater; runs berthing when his master runs politics. Motive: the harbor moving on time. Complication: he tracks a private tally of hulls the Ledger berths "empty" that ride low in the water (M3-fringe). Knows: arrivals, departures, crews, cargoes — the harbor's memory. Enters play: any waterborne arrival/departure. **Combat relevance:** noncombatant — Commoner; whistle brings Watch boats.
