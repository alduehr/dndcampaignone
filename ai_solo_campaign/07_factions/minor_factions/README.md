## AI Use

Standalone files for minor factions — groups with real agency but no continental reach, no 4-quest chain, and (usually) a clock owned by their region rather than themselves. Most minor power blocs remain authored **inline** where they live (the design default); a group gets a standalone file here only when it has enough moving parts (leader, internal conflict, clock lever, multi-quest presence) that a consolidated view helps the AI DM. Do not create standalone files for placeholder political forms (far-region holds, moots, clans) until play deepens them.
