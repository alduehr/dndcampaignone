## AI Use

Load with `TOLLSTONE_CROSS.md` whenever the player travels the East Road between Kettle Bridge and Hartfell, or engages the toll-war (TW-H2/H4/H5, `Q_THE_TOLL_WAR.md`). This file **consolidates the Tollmen canon already established in Stage 7** (settlement file, region clocks, NPC entries) into the standard faction format — it introduces no new facts, names, or clocks. The Tollmen are a **minor regional faction**: no continental reach, no chain of their own, one clock (the region's T2).

## One-Sentence Identity

The East Road's bandit toll-keepers — half racket, half the only protection three days of haunted forest road actually has.

## Public Face

Road-bandits squatting in the ruined Concord toll-station at Tollstone Cross, levying a "toll" on every cart under a painted board and calling it protection. The Compact can't dislodge them; the Ledger finds paying cheaper than fighting; travelers grumble and pay.

## True Agenda (DM-Only)

Survival — and the faction is **divided about how**. Toll-Boss Cady Renn (ex-Compact road-watch, deserted when the Compact abandoned the road) runs a hard but real protection racket and genuinely keeps worse predators — and the drifting dead — off the stretch. Her lieutenant Skell wants outright robbery and murder. Renn is one bad season from losing the men to him. The Tollmen also sit on top of something they refuse to face: the Drowned Vault below the arch (a leaking minor Concord road-node, M2/M5 fragment), which they fear and forbid.

## Founding / History

A generation old: when the Compact's road-watch failed and withdrew, deserters and desperate woodfolk took the chokepoint at the ruined toll-station and made the toll their living. In a bitter sense they *re-founded* the site's original function — the Concord tolled this pinch of road a century before them.

## Leader

**Toll-Boss Cady Renn** (secondary, elevated to a full major profile in Stage 9 pass-2 — see `../../08_npcs/by_region/STAGE_9_MAJOR_NPCS.md`). Hard, grudgingly fair, turnable — she would quit the toll for a way back to honest work.

## Important Members

- **Skell** (secondary) — Renn's lieutenant; wants blood and plunder; the faction's real danger.
- Rank-and-file: **6–10 toll-bandits** (see Combat Capability below).
- Adjacent, not members: the Honest Corner residents (Smith Doune, Ale-wife Pell, a Mourner) who live under the Tollmen's tolerance.

## Resources

The chokepoint itself (bog and bramble allow no bypass for carts); the Broken Arch lookout; toll-coin; road-information (they see everything that passes); grudging local knowledge of the wood's edges.

## Methods

The chain and the painted toll-board; escort-for-coin; intimidation calibrated to avoid real fights (under Renn); ambush from the Arch (under Skell, or if pushed).

## Territory / Influence

The East Road pinch at Tollstone Cross and roughly a day's road either side. No reach beyond the Tollwood; no urban presence.

## Internal Conflict

**Renn vs. Skell** — the campaign-relevant powder keg. Renn holds the men by competence and fear of the alternative; Skell agitates after every thin week. The player can tip this either way (TW-H4 "Renn or Skell").

## Allies

None formal. The Ledger pays (and resents it); Hartfell tolerates them as the price of an open road; the Honest Corner depends on their order while hating it.

## Enemies

The Reachward Compact (wants them cleared; can't afford to); rival wood-bandits (kept out by the Tollmen — a fact travelers rarely credit); Road-Warden Captain Brannoch Vey (Hartfell) is their most direct adversary and a lever in the toll-war.

## Secrets

- Renn's road-watch past and her standing offer-shaped exit (DM: she is a grey ally in waiting, not a simple villain).
- Skell's coup plot.
- The Drowned Vault below — the leaking node they fear (M2/M5 fragment; exploitable leverage: DC 13 to learn their fear).

## Current Activity

Holding the toll through a lean season; Skell's faction growing louder; the dead drifting nearer the stretch than anyone likes (the node, though they don't know it).

## Escalation Clock

**Clock T2 — The Toll Turns to Plunder** (owned by the region: `../../05_regions/TOLLWOOD.md` §Regional Clocks; tracked in `../../02_runtime_state/WORLD_CLOCKS.md`). Advances when the toll-war is ignored, when Renn is weakened, or when Skell gains standing; final consequence: Skell ousts (or kills) Renn and the Tollmen become murderous road-pirates choking the East Road. The player can slow, stop, redirect, or exploit it via `Q_THE_TOLL_WAR.md`.

## Quests Offered By This Faction

None formally offered — but Renn can *hire* discreet help (against rival bandits, against Skell, or to look at "the stair below" she won't descend), and turning her IS the quest opportunity.

## Quests Against This Faction

`Q_THE_TOLL_WAR.md` (clear/broker/co-opt, via Brannoch Vey or Hartfell); TW-H4 (tip the Renn–Skell struggle); TW-H5 (the Drowned Vault below their gate).

## How They React To The Player

Default: a toll, sized to the player's apparent wealth, with watchful courtesy (Renn's rule: no fights the toll can avoid). Repeat crossings earn recognition and slightly better terms — or Skell's attention if the player humiliated anyone.

## What They Do If Ignored

Clock T2 runs: Renn loses her grip, Skell takes the toll to plunder, the East Road strangles, Hartfell weakens, and the vacuum (if they're later destroyed) fills with worse. See `TOLLSTONE_CROSS.md` §Consequences If Ignored.

## How They Can Help A Solo Player

Paid escort through the worst stretch; road-intelligence (who passed, when, carrying what); rough shelter; Renn as a turnable grey ally — and, once turned, a hard-handed friend on the road.

## How They Can Threaten A Solo Player Without Cheap Death

The toll is the threat that isn't a fight; numbers are telegraphed from the chain onward (bows visible in the Arch); Renn's Tollmen break on morale and prefer robbery to murder; even under Skell, ambushes are foreshadowed (the wood's silence, the missing lookout). Retreat back down the road is always open — the Tollmen defend the pinch; they don't pursue.

## Combat Capability And Stat References

Per `TOLLSTONE_CROSS.md` §Law And Threat Mechanics: **6–10 bandits** (**Bandit/Scout-like**, AC 12–13, ~11–16 HP); **Renn** — **Veteran/Spy-like** leader (AC 15, ~45 HP); **Skell** — **Thug/Berserker-like** (AC 13, ~40 HP). Morale breaks at half strength unless Skell commands. Solo danger: **moderate**, heavily weighted to social outcomes; lethal mainly if Skell rules or the player forces it. Scaling: at L2 keep engagements to 2–3 bandits + terrain; at L5+ the Tollmen avoid open battle entirely and turn to negotiation or night-theft.
