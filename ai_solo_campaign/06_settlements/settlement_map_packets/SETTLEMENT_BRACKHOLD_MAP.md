## Settlement: Brackhold (Saltmere Reaches hub — light anchor)

- **Local grid:** simple 0–100 (light anchor).
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (57,68) — on land NW of the Saltmere water body.
- **Water/road relationship:** the largest surviving salt-clan hold; on the **Salt Road**; on the NW shore-land of the **Saltmere** inland sea (Brackhold is on land; the Drowned Towns are submerged ruins at 60,71).

## Notable Areas (from SALTMERE_REACHES_NPCS.md)

- Salt-works and clan quarters.
- The shore road toward the Drowned Towns and Saltcairn.

## Entrances / Exits

- Salt Road N → Marrowdowns; shore road E → Saltcairn (63,73).

## Landmarks
