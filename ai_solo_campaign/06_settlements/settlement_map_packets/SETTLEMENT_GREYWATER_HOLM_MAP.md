> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The hamlet is player-safe. Elder Mabb Tay's knowledge of the M5 drift-line phenomenon is DM-only until the player asks the right questions.

## Settlement: Greywater Holm (Sundering Reach — basin-side hamlet)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. A loose cluster of farmsteads and reed-thatched cottages on low ground at the basin's NE edge, where the fen-drainage channels meet the Mirewend's shallows.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (21,24).
- **Population / scale:** ~80–110. Fisherfolk, peat-cutters, and small-plot farmers. The hamlet feels damp, quiet, and overlooked.
- **Water/road relationship:** sits on a fen-drainage channel off the Mirewend; the **Concord road** passes ~1 mile north; the **Greyfens edge** is 30 minutes S; the **basin shore** is 45 minutes W.

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering the hamlet:** no law here; the Compact barely notices. Village suspicion (strangers = trouble) — Persuasion DC 11 to be treated as welcome.
- **Asking about the Greyfens:** Mabb Tay answers cautiously (DC 12 to get useful directions; DC 15 to get her real fears about the Drift-Line).
- **Threatened by fen-beast or Gravecaller scout:** no guards; hamlet flees indoors. Den Hask may help if present (Guard-like; DC 13).
- **Staying overnight during a Greyfens event (wailing, lights):** Wisdom DC 12 (fright save); Mabb Tay calls for a Mourners' vigil.

## Quest Hooks

- **Greyfens rumors:** Mabb Tay tells the player that "the weeping gets closer each year" (M5 oblique; L1–2 on-ramp to Greyfens exploration).
- **Den Hask's favor:** Hask will guide the player safely to the Drift-Line edge in exchange for a service — he needs something retrieved from a flooded croft before the Compact's levy-man notices it (L2; DC 13).
- **Fen-Watch Post:** the rotting platform is the hamlet's only "defense"; someone has left a Gravecaller second-mark on the post's base (M1 clue C_SR_004 or adjacent; L1–2 discovery).

## Encounter / Treasure References

- Encounter: Greyfens edge wildlife (Giant Frog/Frog-swarm, CR 1/4; Mire-Hound variant, CR 2–3; see `SUNDERING_REACH_ENCOUNTERS.md`); Gravecaller scouts if the Drift-Line is approached (Cultist-like, AC 11, ~9 HP).
- Treasure: `14_treasure_and_artifacts/REWARDS_BY_LEVEL.md` Tier 1 (L1–5) — peat-cutter coin (1d6 sp), Mabb Tay's dried-herb bundle (Mourners' folk remedy; minor restorative), Den Hask's fen-guide favor.

## Player-Safe Layer

Low, wet, fog-touched hamlet on the fen-edge. The Mourners' elder watches the ground; the fowler watches the water. The Greyfens are close enough to see the lights on a clear night.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on player map)

- Mabb Tay has noticed the Drift-Line's wailing is louder than it was ten years ago. She doesn't know why; it feeds M5 (Quiet Country thinning) obliquely.
- Den Hask has seen something in the fen that frightened him — he won't name it (Gravecaller "Mother Osset" moving through; gated M4/M5).
- The Fen-Watch Post's second-mark is Gravecaller — placed by Sister Knell's cell on a scouting pass.
