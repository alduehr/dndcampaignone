> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The logging push toward the Old Mast and the Gravecaller courier line are DM-only. Never hand directly to the player.

## Settlement: Hartfell (Tollwood hub)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south). Stockaded clearing on the East Road.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (33,22); campaign-grid (78,40).
- **Water/road relationship:** the only town in the forest; the **East Road** runs through (W→Tollstone Cross/Kettle Bridge, E→Caradril). A log stockade faces *inward* toward the deep wood; the barred E gate is offering-marked.

## Bridges / Fords / Ferries

- None (forest road-town). The Green Mile drowned-road stretch lies W on the East Road.

## Landmarks

The Antlers (inn, hart-skulls); the Timber-Weigh (Ledger sawmill); the Coppice Shrine (Mourners'); the stockade + barred offering-marked East Gate.

## Player-Safe Layer

The stockaded town; the East Road through; the barred E gate (visibly offering-marked); all named areas; "deep wood — do not enter" beyond the E gate.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The Ledger's **logging push** deeper toward the Old Mast (D12) each season (the Tollwood master tension).
- A **Gravecaller courier line** passing through to the deep cells.
- Dorr's suppression of the woodfolk's warnings (commercial denial, not Court).

## Unresolved Map Gaps

- The deep-wood approach beyond the E gate is intentionally gated/vague (the Old Mast is a gated site).
