> **Secrecy classification:** Player-safe. Cresswater is a small barge-village on a Verdance backwater: rustic, independent, River-Captain Drenn's home-port. No apex content.

## Settlement: Cresswater (Verdance Reaches — barge-village)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. A loose cluster of buildings on a backwater bend; the main river channel is nearby but not the village's exact bank; jetties on the S face.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (55,46).
- **Population / scale:** ~150–250; a small barge-village; River-Captain Drenn's home base and turnaround point.
- **Water/road relationship:** on a **Verdance backwater channel** (slower current; not the main towpath); the **Fen Track** leads E (to Marrowfen Stair overland; days); south channel connects to the main Verdance (hours by flat-bottomed barge).

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering Cresswater:** completely open; no law beyond community expectation.
- **Threatening Drenn or her crew:** the village turns out (20 bargemen and fisherfolk; improvised-weapon commoners but numerous).
- **Trying to confiscate Drenn's cargo:** Drenn's response depends on what the cargo is; always Persuasion DC 16 first.

## Quest Hooks

- **Q_VR_003 — Mossa's Cargo:** Drenn has cargo she won't declare; she needs discreet escort past the Compact checkpoint (L9; Cresswater is the departure point).
- **Water-route transit:** Drenn offers barge passage Verdance-route to Glassmere or downstream to Caradril; the fastest way on the river (Q_VR_007; L8+).

## Encounter / Treasure References

- Encounter: river wildlife (giant-pike equivalent, CR 3; Water Weird in the backwater fen, CR 3); Ledger river-patrol (if Drenn's cargo is hot, CR 4–6 intercept); see L8+ river lines.
- Treasure: Drenn's payment (coin and river-goods); Cresswater has no significant treasure.
