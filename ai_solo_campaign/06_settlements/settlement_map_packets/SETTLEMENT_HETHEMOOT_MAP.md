> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The Old Holds (D25) are forbidden deep-wood Concord ruins — M5/M6-oblique, never the keystone. Hethemoot is the confederacy's political heart; wood's law governs everything. Never hand directly to the player.

## Settlement: Hethemoot (Hethewald Free Holds — great gathering free-hold)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. A large forest clearing; the Moot-Hall at the center; forest gates at the edges. Not a dense urban settlement.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (71,39).
- **Population / scale:** ~300–500 permanent (the Speaker's household and support); up to 2,000 during full moots; the confederacy's political center.
- **Water/road relationship:** within the **Hethewood** forest; no significant water feature in the clearing itself; the **Hethe river** is nearby (E, Tollreach 75,37); forest roads lead to Greenward (68,43) and the Hold-Road (W, 66,37.6).

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering at Forest Gate (without wood's-law welcome):** challenged; Q_HFH_001 is the formal entry; Persuasion DC 16 to waive the challenge (Bram's discretion).
- **Moving toward the River Road head without telling Bram:** watched; Persuasion DC 14 to proceed without appearing suspicious (Maddoc's territory begins past the gate).
- **Approaching the Old Holds track:** openly told it's forbidden; Persuasion DC 17 to get Bram's reluctant approval for a guided run; anything else is treated as a serious breach.
- **Disturbing the Moot-Circle during session:** the full moot stops; judgment from Bram (Persuasion DC 18 to escape censure); the confederacy disperses hostile.
- **Violent act in the Hethemoot:** overwhelming response — 50+ warriors emerge from the tree-line (**Tribal Warrior / Veteran-like**, AC 13–15); Survival DC 17 to escape the clearing.

## Quest Hooks

- **Q_HFH_001 — The Wood's Law:** the mandatory entry hook; Bram sets a wood's-law welcome challenge for outsiders; earning it unlocks safe movement and named paths (L7; entry arc).
- **Q_HFH_002 — The River-Toll Crisis:** Maddoc's extortion is cutting the trade-ground's income and Bram needs outside leverage; a political mission more than a fight (L8+).
- **Q_HFH_006 — The Moot's Vote:** the confederacy must vote on its response to a crisis (failing bargains, Maddoc, or Old Holds); the player can attend and tip the balance (L10+; see `HETHEWALD_FREE_HOLDS_QUESTS.md`).

## Encounter / Treasure References

- Encounter: free-hold warriors (**Tribal Warrior / Veteran-like**, AC 13–15, ~22–52 HP; overwhelming numbers if provoked); Maddoc's river-bandits (**Bandit-like**, off-site at Tollreach); Old Holds undead at D25 (**Wight / Revenant-like**); see `13_encounters_and_bestiary/` forest lines at L7+.
- Treasure: `14_treasure_and_artifacts/REWARDS_BY_LEVEL.md` Tier 2 (L6–11) forest confederation rewards; Bram's formal gift (fine-crafted wood-work, rare forest goods, passage-right); Old Holds salvage (D25; Remembrance-adjacent relics; Thin-touch risk).

## Player-Safe Layer

The moot-hall, the speaker's longhouse, the moot-circle clearing, the trade-ground, the two forest gates. The settlement reads as politically alive and deeply traditional — everything is governed by wood's law, and the wood itself is watching. The Old Holds are mentioned as "the forbidden interior" without explanation.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The Old Holds (D25) are **surface-Concord ruins** in the deep forest; their stirring dead and "wrong" resonance are M2/M5-oblique echoes; NEVER the keystone.
- Forest-Mother Onn Greenward's "failing bargains" concern (the groves are quiet; the old rites are weak) is a pagan rendering of the Quiet Country thinning — NEVER name the harvest or Hollow Court; keep as forest-faith.
- No apex geometry here. The endgame is vertical beneath Hollowmere (NW only).

## Unresolved Map Gaps

- The forest's interior track-network is intentionally unmapped; guides are required for sub-destinations.
- Individual free-hold villages feed into Hethemoot at moot-time but are not separately mapped here.
- Greenward and Tollreach are separate settlement packets (to be created) within half-day to multi-day distance.
