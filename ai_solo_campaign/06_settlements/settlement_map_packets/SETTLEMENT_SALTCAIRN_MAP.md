> **Secrecy classification:** Player-safe. Saltcairn is a Ghostmark-edge clan-town: a secondary salt-clan hold on the edge of the Ghostmark Range. No apex content. An alternate approach to the Drowned Towns via E shore.

## Settlement: Saltcairn (Saltmere Reaches — Ghostmark-edge clan-town)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. A fortified clan-town at the Saltmere's E shore, where the Ghostmark foothills meet the lake-edge. Brackhold is W (57,68; days).
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (63,73).
- **Population / scale:** ~400–700; a secondary salt-clan hold; smaller than Brackhold and more militarized (Ghostmark threats from the N).
- **Water/road relationship:** on the **Saltmere's E shore**; the **Shore Road** leads W (to Brackhold, 57,68; days); the **Ghostmark Track** leads N (toward the Ghostmark Range passes; dangerous); the lake provides E-side barge access to the Drowned Towns offshore.

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering Saltcairn:** clan-toll (1gp or equivalence); Persuasion DC 14 to waive.
- **Ghostmark Track access:** clan-captain's permit; DC 15 and a known reason.
- **Violent act:** clan-garrison (15 fighters, **Berserker-like**, AC 13; faster response than Brackhold due to military culture).

## Quest Hooks

- **Q_SALT_002 — The E-Shore Approach:** Oss or the player discovers that the Drowned Towns are also accessible from the E side via Saltcairn; a tactical advantage (L11+; D24 access).
- **Ghostmark trouble:** something is coming down from the Ghostmark foothills more often than usual; the Clan-Captain wants it investigated before it reaches Saltcairn's walls (L12+).
