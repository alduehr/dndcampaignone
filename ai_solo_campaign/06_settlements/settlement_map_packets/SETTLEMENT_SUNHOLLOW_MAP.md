> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. D33 (the Sunhollow Great Grove) is a living faith site — the grove's rites *work* because they were never inside the Concord's reach. This is the campaign's hopeful contrast; keep EXTREMELY oblique; never explain the mechanism or connect to the keystone. Never hand directly to the player.

## Settlement: The Great Grove at Sunhollow (Sunmark — sacred grove-gathering heart)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. An open grove-gathering, not a walled town; the Heart-Tree at center; paths radiate outward; the Sunhollow Pool in the SE.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (44,80).
- **Population / scale:** ~150–250 permanent grove-keepers in residence; hundreds more in the dispersed Grove-Camps; draws 1,000+ at major rites.
- **Water/road relationship:** the **Sunhollow Pool** (sacred spring-fed pool) is the Grove's heart; the **Green Roads** pilgrimage paths connect to the Grove-Camps (40,77) and the wider Sunmark Wilds; no road in the settled-world sense.

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering the grove:** respectful approach required; weapons sheathed; Persuasion/Charisma DC 13 (friendly tone, no hostility shown); grove-keepers greet all who arrive respectfully.
- **Disrespecting grove customs (weapons drawn, threatening, loud aggression):** keepers withdraw and send for help; DC 15 Persuasion to smooth over before the grove mobilizes.
- **Threatening or attacking a grove-keeper:** the tribes mobilize (hundreds of warriors from the dispersed camps, **Tribal Warrior-like**, AC 13–15, significant numbers); Survival DC 18 to escape the Sunmark Wilds.
- **Logging or desecrating the grove:** a clock triggers (Q_SUN_007 — the grove-defense; Ledger version of this already uses this clock).
- **Accessing the Heart-Tree directly:** only grove-keepers permitted; Sael's escort required; DC 17 to ask and have it granted.

## Quest Hooks

- **Q_SUN_001 — The Saw at the Grove:** the inciting event; Ledger logging-scouts appear at the grove-edge (or Doss's camp is seen from the Pilgrim-Path); Sael asks for help understanding the threat (L8).
- **Q_SUN_003 — What the Pool Knows:** a pilgrim approaches the Sunhollow Pool and something unexpected happens; Sael invites an outsider to witness and carry the truth back to the world (L10+; oblique M5 hint).
- **Q_SUN_007 — The Grove-Defense clock:** if Ledger logging is active, the grove-keeper tribes mobilize; the player can help, hinder, or negotiate (L12+; see `SUNMARK_QUESTS.md`).

## Encounter / Treasure References

- Encounter: grove-keeper warriors if threatened (**Tribal Warrior / Druid-like**, AC 13–15, ~22–37 HP; significant numbers; defensive only); Ledger guards at Q_SUN_001 (**Guard / Bandit-like**, AC 13–14); see `13_encounters_and_bestiary/` forest encounter lines at L8+.
- Treasure: `14_treasure_and_artifacts/REWARDS_BY_LEVEL.md` Tier 2 (L6–11) — grove-faith rewards are non-monetary: sacred healing herbs (equivalent to healing potions), blessed wood items, grove-keeper escort (safe passage through the Sunmark Wilds). Sael can offer a nature-blessing (advantage on one Wisdom saving throw; one use).

## Player-Safe Layer

The Heart-Tree grove (lush, ancient, warm), the gathering-ground where travelers are welcomed, the sacred Sunhollow Pool, Sael's hut, Doll's storytellers' circle, the pilgrims' arrival-path. The setting reads as genuinely peaceful and alive — a place where death-rites still work, presented as beautiful fact with no explanation offered.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- D33 (The Sunhollow Great Grove) is a **living faith site** — the grove's rites settle the dead because this faith was never compromised by the Concord's harvest. The mechanism is never explained; the contrast is EXTREMELY oblique.
- Sun-Singer Doll perceives a **"far root-ache" in the southern sky** — an oblique, deniable far-echo of the Quiet Country thinning; keep as mystical sensitivity, never named as the harvest.
- The Sunhollow Pool's healing quality is real (a genuine grove-blessing, not a Concord node). The pool is NOT a Concord relic and NOT the keystone's echo. It works for an entirely different reason.
- No apex geometry here. The endgame is vertical beneath Hollowmere (NW only).
