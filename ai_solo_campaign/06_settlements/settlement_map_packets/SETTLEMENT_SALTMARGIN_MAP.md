> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The wharf-factor's salvage-skimming and the smuggler ring are DM-only. Never hand directly to the player.

## Settlement: Saltmargin (Reach W gateway / salt-trade town)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south). On a low salt-grass shelf between fen and coast.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (20,22); campaign-grid (36,42).
- **Water/road relationship:** the Reach's W gateway, where Pale Coast salt-carts meet the peat-roads; the **Pale Road** runs through (E→Hollowmere, W→Cobble Strand/Wrackmouth). Tidal salt-pans; the Tar Wharf takes Coast carts.

## Bridges / Fords / Ferries

- Salt-pan causeways across the tidal flats. The Tar Wharf is a cart-unloading wharf, not a river crossing.

## Landmarks

The Knotted Hand (inn); the Weigh-House (Ledger salt-scale); the Brine Chapel (Mourners'); the salt-pans/drying-sheds; the Tar Wharf.

## Player-Safe Layer

The salt-town; the salt-pans; the Pale Road through; all named areas; the Tar Wharf.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The **wharf-factor's salvage-skimming** of Concord relics before they reach Hollowmere (M3; exposes the Ledger's relic-hunger / Vyre's Monopoly clock).
- The **smuggler ring** (Gull Heddwyn) moving "grave-goods."
- The salt shortage is secondary — rites fail from the reawakening harvest (M5), not salt scarcity.
