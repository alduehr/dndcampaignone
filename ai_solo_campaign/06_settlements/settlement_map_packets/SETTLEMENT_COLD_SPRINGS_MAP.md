## Settlement: The Winter-Camp at Cold Springs (Wender Steppe — seasonal clan-gathering)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. A large felt-tent camp in the open steppe; no permanent structures except the Cold Springs themselves (a spring-cluster); positions are cold-season illustrative anchors.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (45,16).
- **Population / scale:** ~400–800 during cold-season gathering (multiple clans); ~50–100 in warmer months (the spring's year-round watchers); the steppe's largest seasonal meeting.
- **Water/road relationship:** the **Cold Springs** (a cluster of cold-season springs, frozen-edge in winter) are the permanent feature; **Steppe Tracks** (seasonal horse-trails) lead toward central Orrun and the Spine-Foot trade-meet; the **Sky-Stones** (D32) are hours away at (42,14).

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering the camp:** formal approach to the clan-speaker required; Persuasion DC 14 (respectful, mounted or walking slowly) or DC 16 (riding in fast or with weapons drawn).
- **Violating guest-right (attacking a host or fellow guest):** the full camp mobilizes (hundreds of riders, **Berserker-like**, AC 13, ~67 HP); Survival DC 18 to escape on horseback; Survival DC 20 on foot.
- **Trying to buy or trade without the Trade-Ring protocols:** social snub (all prices double); Persuasion DC 14 to acknowledge the custom and start over.
- **Borr's riders harassing camp-visitors:** between camps, not at the camp itself; DC 16 Perception for early warning; Persuasion DC 15 to negotiate past them (they respect strength or cold calculation).
- **Asking Esha for a vision:** DC 16 Persuasion or Insight; she does not refuse sincere seekers, but her answers come in steppe-image language.

## Quest Hooks

- **Q_WS_001 — The Confederacy's Crisis:** Tamur needs outside witnesses to a truth the confederacy's internal politics can't acknowledge; Borr's raids are forcing a choice (L8).
- **Q_WS_002 — Borr's Raids:** a contract (from Tamur or a frightened settled-world merchant at Spine-Foot) to handle what the clans cannot fight directly (L10+).
- **Sky-Stones pilgrimage:** Esha guides willing travelers to the Sky-Stones (D32) for a vision or a relic-question; she names it as "the place where the sky used to speak" (L8+; oblique M5 hint).

## Player-Safe Layer

The cold-season felt-tent camp around the springs, the clan-speaker's great tent, the horse-lines, the trade-ring, the seer's circle (permanent stones in the moving camp), the path toward the Sky-Stones. The camp reads as vibrantly alive — hundreds of people, thousands of horses, the smell of felt and dung-fire — and absolutely governed by guest-right.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The Sky-Stones (D32) are **NON-CONCORD** — a pre-existing steppe holy site that senses cosmological stress ("the sky has gone thin"). This is M5-oblique cosmic echo from an independent source; NEVER named as the harvest or the Hollow Court.
- Wind-Singer Esha's visions are genuine but translated through steppe cosmology — she perceives what she perceives in terms of "the herd-sky" and "the cold that won't end." This is a far echo of the Quiet Country thinning; keep as seer-imagery only.
- No Concord infrastructure on the steppe; no apex geometry here. The endgame is vertical beneath Hollowmere (NW only).

## Unresolved Map Gaps

- The camp shifts position slightly each cold-season; the Seer's Circle (permanent stones) and the Cold Springs (permanent water) are the fixed landmarks.
- Borr's raid-lord territory in the deep steppe E is not mapped; it's an encounter zone, not a settlement.
- The Spine-Foot trade-meet is a separate settlement packet (to be created) at a day's ride.
