> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The village is player-safe. Old Mam Tace's knowledge of the Ammet floods and the "old songs" about the drowned road is DM-only until questions are asked.

## Settlement: Tilbrook (Ashgarden Vale — mill village on the Ammet)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. A working mill village where the Ammet River can be forded; grain and apple-press commerce; flat Vale farmland surrounds it. Compact road access via the South Road.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (31,38).
- **Population / scale:** ~180–220. Millers, farmers, orchard-hands, a handful of tradespeople. Miller Sael Brunt is the de facto authority.
- **Water/road relationship:** the **Ammet River** runs through the village at the mill-ford; the **South Road** (Concord causeway) passes through from Orchardmere (1 hr N) toward Nettlecombe/Marrow Cross (2 hrs S); the **Buried Cloister** is ~3 hrs SE in rough country.

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering the village:** no formal law; Compact visits monthly. Persuasion DC 10 for welcome.
- **Asking Miller Brunt about the Ledger levy:** he'll answer with frustration (DC 11; hook into Ledger conflict).
- **Crossing the Ammet ford in spring flood:** Athletics DC 14 or swim; Strength DC 12 if swept.
- **Armed confrontation in the village:** Brunt and three stout millers respond (Guard-like; AC 11; +3 attack).
- **Asking about the Drowned Lane / south road flooding:** Mam Tace has stories (DC 13; M5-oblique "old dead walk the flood-road at midsummer").

## Quest Hooks

- **The Ledger levy:** Miller Brunt is being squeezed by the Cinder Ledger's milling license; he wants help contesting it or pressuring the local factor (L2–3; Ledger conflict; feeds Q_LEDGER_003 or adjacent).
- **Old Mam Tace's songs:** she knows "the flood dead walk the Drowned Lane when the Ammet runs wrong." Investigation reveals the lane floods near old Concord road-work (M5 oblique; L2; DC 13 to prompt story; DC 15 to map the old road stretch).
- **The Buried Cloister:** Brunt has heard that "strangers with Remnant marks" were seen on the track toward the Cloister last week (L3 on-ramp to THE_BURIED_CLOISTER dungeon; feeds M2/M6 chain).

## Encounter / Treasure References

- Encounter: Bandits on the South Road S of Tilbrook (Bandit-like, CR 1/8–1/4); Ammet flood-animal (Giant Frog/Crocodile near ford, CR 1); Ledger collection thugs (Guard-like, CR 1/8, 2–3 of them) if conflict with Brunt escalates.
- Treasure: `14_treasure_and_artifacts/REWARDS_BY_LEVEL.md` Tier 1 — miller's coin-pouch (2d6 sp reward from Brunt), Mam Tace's dried apple-garland (Mourners' folk-token; earns trust with any Mourners' elder).

## Player-Safe Layer

Working mill village on the Ammet ford; Brunt's mill grinding; Genna's alehouse fire; the old Mourners' graveyard; South Road traffic. A functional, workaday Vale settlement with visible Ledger pressure.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on player map)

- Old Mam Tace's "flood-songs" are a genuine M5 echo: the Ammet floods bring dead near an old Concord road-channel whose sealwork is cracked. She doesn't know what she's describing — she learned it from her grandmother.
- The "strangers with Remnant marks" are Sub-Lector Oneth Vael and a Remnant party mapping the Buried Cloister approach (M2/M6 chain; gated).
- Miller Brunt has an old bill of lading from two years ago with a Ledger vault seal he doesn't understand — it could be evidence of the Ledger's relic trade (M3 clue; he'll hand it over if helped).
