> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The Drowned Steps (D30) are offshore pre-Concord tidal-causeway ruins — surface/echo, never the keystone. The real governance here is the plague-doctor, not the crown; that gap is a source of political play, not apex information. Never hand directly to the player.

## Settlement: Fenward (Sallowmarch Protectorate — garrison-capital)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. Garrison on the higher ground NW; delta wharves at the S/SE waterline; sprawling over raised ground in a wetland basin.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (57,82).
- **Population / scale:** ~700–1,000 residents; a garrison-capital that governs by military presence and the plague-doctor's practical medicine.
- **Water/road relationship:** in the **Mardenflow delta** / Sallow Marches; the **Mardenflow river** runs N (from Marrowdowns); delta channels snake SE (to Reedmouth, 60,85); boat is the primary transport.

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering through the Garrison gate:** state purpose; Persuasion DC 15 (crown-affiliated) or DC 17 (foreign without papers).
- **Approaching the Smuggler's Dock:** Deception/Persuasion DC 15 (Heron's people); failure means watched, not arrested.
- **Requesting the Plague-Doctor's attention:** Persuasion DC 15 (genuine medical need); DC 17 (political access).
- **Violent act near the Garrison:** immediate military response — 30 soldiers (**Guard-like**, AC 14, ~11 HP each, numbers overwhelming); Survival DC 17 to escape by boat.
- **Contraband (relic trade):** Vorr turns a blind eye (he knows and doesn't want to know); Persuasion DC 13 to keep it that way; failing that, DC 17 to explain.

## Quest Hooks

- **Q_SP_001 — The Delta Plague (inciting event):** Doll Fenn's diagnosis of a new sickness moving up from the Rice Sallows; she needs outside help because Vorr's garrison is useless (L10).
- **Q_SP_002 — Heron's Compromised Route:** the Smuggler's Dock's back-canal has been blocked by something in the flooded channels (L11+); Heron offers good pay to clear it.
- **The crown's assessment:** Vorr asks outside visitors for an "unbiased" report on the delta's stability — he needs to send something to the distant crown; Doll's truth vs. Vorr's preferred fiction.

## Encounter / Treasure References

- Encounter: garrison soldiers (**Guard-like**, AC 14, overwhelming numbers); delta smugglers (**Bandit / Scout-like**, AC 12–13); delta hazards (crocodile/giant-lizard-equivalent, CR 4–7); see `13_encounters_and_bestiary/` swamp/delta lines at L10+.
- Treasure: `14_treasure_and_artifacts/REWARDS_BY_LEVEL.md` Tier 2–3 (L9–14) delta trade rewards; crown-seal letters as faction currency; Heron's trade-goods (rare spices, foreign cloth, occasionally relics).

## Player-Safe Layer

The garrison, the Protectorate Hall, the delta wharves, the plague-doctor's compound, the smuggler's dock, the fen-witch's grove outside the walls. The settlement reads as a place where the formal power and the real power are completely different people — the crown's soldier and the doctor who keeps everyone alive.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The Drowned Steps (D30; offshore at 54,90) are pre-Concord tidal-causeway ruins that corroborate "an older fall" — M2/M5-oblique, never the keystone.
- Fen-Witch Sela Reed's grief-rite knowledge is M2/M5-oblique folk-memory of the delta's dead "going wrong" — she names it as "the channels keeping the dead angry" without understanding the harvest.
- No apex geometry here. The endgame is vertical beneath Hollowmere (NW only).

## Unresolved Map Gaps

- Delta channels are diffuse and shift seasonally; the precise water-positions are illustrative terrain texture.
- The Fen-Witch's Grove position is outside the formal town; exact grove layout is improvisational.
