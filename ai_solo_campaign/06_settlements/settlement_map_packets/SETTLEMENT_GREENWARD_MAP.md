> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. Greenward is Forest-Mother Onn's grove-village and the Hethewald free-holds' faith-center. The "failing bargains" are M5/M6-oblique (pagan rendering of Quiet Country thinning) — never name the harvest. Never hand directly to the player.

## Settlement: Greenward (Hethewald Free Holds — grove-village; old-faith heart)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. A cleared grove-village in the deep Hethewood; large old-growth trees at the perimeter; Forest-Mother Onn's long-hall at the center.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (68,43).
- **Population / scale:** ~200–350 permanent grove-keepers and their families; the Hethewood's spiritual and old-faith center; draws more during the faith's seasonal rites.
- **Water/road relationship:** deep in the **Hethewood**; no significant water feature except a spring-pool (forest-sacred); the **guided Greenward Path** leads N (to Hethemoot, 71,39; 2–3 days guided); the Old Holds track splits SE from the deep forest (DO NOT ADVERTISE).

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering Greenward:** guides only (from Hethemoot); arriving unguided triggers grove-keeper challenge; Persuasion DC 15 to be treated as welcome vs. intruder.
- **Approaching the Old Holds cairn:** Onn stops you; "that path is sealed by the moot's decree." Persuasion DC 18 to even discuss it; she won't approve without Bram.
- **Threatening Onn or the grove-keepers:** hundreds of Hethewood warriors mobilize within hours; Survival DC 18 to navigate out of the deep wood.
- **Respectfully asking about the "failing bargains":** Onn shares obliquely (Insight DC 15 to sense the scope of what she knows but won't say).

## Quest Hooks

- **Q_HFH_003 — The Failing Bargains:** Onn asks the player to carry a message to Bram at Hethemoot that "the trees are asking wrong questions" — she needs someone who isn't bound by wood's-law silence (L8+; M5/M6-oblique).
- **Q_HFH_005 — The Grove Rite:** an important seasonal rite is failing; Onn needs something recovered from the forest (likely from a minor ruined Concord node — surface only; L8+).
- **Spring-Pool healing:** Greenward is the best healing in the Hethewood; Onn's grove-faith care is the region's closest thing to a temple (L7+; requires respectful approach).

## Encounter / Treasure References

- Encounter: Hethewood wildlife (Owlbear, CR 3; Giant Spider, CR 1; forest guardian-construct at D25 echo, CR 6–10 if the Old Holds track is approached); free-hold warriors (**Tribal Warrior-like**) if mobilized.
- Treasure: `14_treasure_and_artifacts/REWARDS_BY_LEVEL.md` Tier 2 (L6–11) old-faith rewards (non-monetary: forest medicines, blessed bow-string, a grove-keeper's escort through the deep wood).

## Player-Safe Layer

Onn's long-hall, the spring-pool, the grove-ring, the skill-houses, the path N toward Hethemoot. The village reads as ancient, quiet, and deeply faithful — the old faith works here, and the trees are watched. The cairn at the SE edge is visible without being explained.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on player map)

- The "failing bargains" Onn senses are M5/M6-oblique: the forest's old-faith rites are losing effectiveness as the Quiet Country thins. She perceives this as "the trees hearing wrong" — never named as the harvest.
- The Old Holds track SE leads to D25 (surface Concord ruins); NEVER the keystone.
- No apex geometry here.
