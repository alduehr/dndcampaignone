## Settlement: Candlewick (Reach S gateway / candle-and-archive village)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south). Among hedged fields on firm Vale-edge ground.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (24,26); campaign-grid (50,54).
- **Water/road relationship:** the Reach's S gateway toward the Vale; the **South Road** runs through (N→Hollowmere, S→Tilbrook/Orchardmere). Hedged fields and rush-ponds; a roofed Concord archive-stone on the green.

## Player-Safe Layer

The village; the hedged fields; the South Road through; the roofed archive-stone (a protected curiosity of carved old script); all named areas.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The archive-stone is a genuine **Concord record-marker**; one panel is a truth-fragment behind a **Concord Script** language gate (M6/M9 seed; the player's natural place to begin learning Script — Hook 8).
- Candlewick's rites "hold" because it sits at the **outer edge of the shrine's pull** — geographic proof of M5 (read the right way).
- The Remnant's offer to "purchase and protect" the archive-stone.
