> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The Ashwalk Rest is a Warden waystation and **solo-safety sanctuary** — a guaranteed safe rest point for a vulnerable PC in the Reach. Its role as a seal-guardian station is DM-only; the player sees a fortified waystation with Warden hospitality.

## Settlement: The Ashwalk Rest (Sundering Reach — Ashen Warden waystation)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. A solid stone-and-timber waystation on a slight rise above the bogland, flanked by two carved Warden ash-posts. It reads as a fortified traveler's rest; the Wardens' true function as seal-guardians is not visible.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (26,19).
- **Population / scale:** 3–6 Wardens resident; capacity for ~15 guests. Occupancy varies with road traffic and season.
- **Water/road relationship:** on the **Concord road** (the main Reach transit route) between Hollowmere (1.5 hrs W) and the Sunder Heights road junction (2 hrs NE); a minor track leads S toward the Greyfens margin.

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering the waystation:** Wardens welcome all travelers; no toll. Persuasion DC 10 for a warm reception. DC 14 if you're visibly affiliated with the Gravecallers or the Ledger.
- **Asking about the Wardens' true purpose:** Sirrin deflects (DC 16 Insight to sense she's hiding something; DC 17 Persuasion to get a partial answer about "old road-duties").
- **Threatening a Warden on the premises:** 3–5 Wardens respond immediately (Veteran-like, AC 14, +5 attack); Sirrin calls for restraint but will defend the Rest.
- **Camping south of the waystation toward the Greyfens:** Wardens will ask where you're going; they warn sincerely about the south track's dangers.

## Quest Hooks

- **Safe harbor:** the Rest is the solo PC's best guaranteed safe rest in the Reach (no ambush, no interruption); Sirrin is a reliable expedition-planner for any Reach/Heights delve (L1+).
- **Warden message:** Sirrin asks the player to carry a sealed message to Hollowmere — marked with Warden ash-sigil — for Warden Sister Othetha. She doesn't explain what it says (L2; DC 13 Insight to sense urgency; M3 oblique connection).
- **South track warning:** Doune saw movement on the south track that he doesn't recognize — "not fen-beasts." He'd like a skilled traveler to investigate (L2; Gravecaller scout activity near the Greyfens approach; feeds M4).

## Encounter / Treasure References

- Encounter: no hostile encounters inside the Rest (solo-safety guaranteed). South track: Gravecaller scouts (Cultist-like, AC 11); Greyfens predators (Giant Rat swarms, Mire-Hound, CR 1–3); see `SUNDERING_REACH_ENCOUNTERS.md` L1–4.
- Treasure: `14_treasure_and_artifacts/REWARDS_BY_LEVEL.md` Tier 1 — Warden supply kit (donated; rope, torches, 3 days rations; given to travelers in need); Sirrin's sealed message (quest item; no monetary value).

## Player-Safe Layer

Stone waystation on the road; the carved ash-posts; the Guest Hall fire; Hospitaller Sirrin's no-nonsense welcome; the signal post; Doune at the south watch. The safest night's sleep in the Reach.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on player map)

- The Ashwalk Rest is a Warden seal-monitoring station. Sirrin logs any unusual activity near the Greyfens margin and the Sunder Heights road — she's tracking signs of shrine-instability (M3, gated).
- The South Watch was added after Doune reported "voices from the fen" during a storm. Neither Doune nor Sirrin knows it's Gravecaller activity.
- The "sealed message to Othetha" carries Sirrin's weekly seal-status report — player-safe on the outside; DM-only in its function.
