> **Secrecy classification:** Player-safe. Saltgate is a rival Gulf port city-state competing with Calderport for the Gulf trade. Doss Saltgate's port-lord politics are entirely player-safe. No apex content.

## Settlement: Saltgate (Hollow Gulf Ports — rival port city-state)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. A walled harbor-city on the Gulf's E shore; the harbor to the S; the city climbs NW up the rocky headland.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (46,82).
- **Population / scale:** ~8,000–12,000; a significant port city-state; smaller than Calderport but with a stronger claim to the E Gulf lanes.
- **Water/road relationship:** on the **Hollow Gulf** E coast; harbor faces S/SW (toward the Gulf sea-lanes); the **Coastal Road** runs N (toward the Sunmark approach; days) and W (toward Calderport, 44,80; days by sea or road).

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering Saltgate:** Customs Gate inspection; 1gp toll or Persuasion DC 13.
- **Carrying undeclared cargo:** Customs Gate catch DC 15; fine (10gp) or seizure.
- **Competing with Calderport publicly:** neutral in Saltgate; Persuasion DC 14 to navigate Doss's political sensitivities.
- **Violent act in the city:** Saltgate garrison (50 soldiers, **Guard/Veteran-like**, AC 14–16); serious response.

## Quest Hooks

- **Q_HGP_003 — The Port War:** the Calderport/Saltgate rivalry heats into an actual blockade of one another's ships; outside mediators or privateers are hired (L11+).
- **Roke Mallin's commission:** Mallin needs a cargo (or a mission) run without either port's oversight; he offers good pay for discretion (L10+; see `CALDERPORT_CITY_MAP.md` for full Mallin profile).
