> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The fishing village is player-safe. Salt-Mother Bryd's knowledge of the drowned-tide (M5 oblique) and the coast's relic salvage are DM-only until discovered.

## Settlement: Cobble Strand (Pale Coast — shingle-cove fishing village)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. A compact fishing village on a shingle cove sheltered from the open Pale Sea by a headland arm. The beach is black cobble-stone; the sea is cold grey-green. The village smells of salt, tar, and drying fish.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (18,31).
- **Population / scale:** ~120–160. Fisher-folk, net-menders, salt-rakers, a small Mourners' chapter. Headman Corl Strand leads the fishing families.
- **Water/road relationship:** directly on the **Pale Sea** coast; the **Pale Road** (coast road) passes behind the headland (~30 min N to Wrackmouth; the road is exposed and storm-prone); the **salt-pan flats** are 1 hr S; the **Weeping Light headland** is 45 min N (dangerous).

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering the village:** fishing families are clannish but not hostile to respectful strangers. Persuasion DC 11 for genuine welcome; DC 13 for access to boat hire.
- **Asking about the Skerries or the Weeping Light:** Corl Strand warns strongly (DC 12; he'll describe wrecks); Salt-Mother Bryd will warn ritually (DC 14; she speaks about "what the sea gives back wrong").
- **Trying to buy sea-relics or salvage:** Corl Strand goes quiet (DC 15 Insight to catch his hesitation; he knows Factor Cole's relic trade but won't say so directly).
- **Armed threat in the village:** Corl + 4 fisher-folk (Guard-like; AC 11; +3 attack; Corl has a boat-hook and will call the cove's tidal conditions as a tactical threat — they know the water).

## Quest Hooks

- **The drowned-tide:** Salt-Mother Bryd will tell the player that a drowned fisherman came back to the Tide-Shrine at midnight — wrong. She wants someone to investigate the Drowned-Cairns stretch (M5 oblique; L2–3; DC 14 to get full story from Bryd).
- **The Ledger's sea interest:** Corl Strand reluctantly admits that Factor Maris Cole has been paying well for "odd salvage from the coast-shrines." He's worried this is attracting the wrong attention (L3; M3; leads to Wrackmouth / THE_DROWNED_LAMP dungeon chain).
- **Young Ferec's scheme:** Ferec wants to sell something he found on the Drowned-Cairns strand — a piece of worked stone with Concord Script. He doesn't know what it is (M2 clue; L2; DC 12 to get him talking).

## Encounter / Treasure References

- Encounter: Shore-prowler (Mire-Hound coastal variant, CR 2; nocturnal beach approach); Wreck salvager rivals (Bandit-like, CR 1/4, 2–4 of them; hired by Ledger to intimidate); Salt-Stalker (sea-creature variant, CR 3–4; if the Skerries approach is taken); see `PALE_COAST_ENCOUNTERS.md`.
- Treasure: `14_treasure_and_artifacts/REWARDS_BY_LEVEL.md` Tier 1–2 — dried fish and coastal rope (practical); Ferec's fragment (Concord Script stone; quest item; non-monetary value); the Drowned Flame (relic; found in THE_DROWNED_LAMP dungeon, not in the village).

## Player-Safe Layer

Black cobble beach; grey-green Pale Sea; drying nets; the salt-smell; Corl's large-handed welcome; Bryd's Tide-Shrine with its wreath of dried kelp; Senna gossiping over net-work; the sea at the cove mouth going dark at evening.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on player map)

- Salt-Mother Bryd's "drowned-tide" is a real M5 phenomenon: the Pale Coast's Concord shrine network (especially the Drowned Lamp) has cracked seals that cause Remembrance leak into the sea — the dead return wrong. She has no name for it.
- Ferec's stone fragment is a broken piece of a Concord relay-marker — Concord Script, M2 oblique, links to THE_DROWNED_LAMP. He found it on the beach after a storm uncovered something below the Drowned-Cairns.
- Corl Strand knows more than he admits about the Ledger's sea-relic operation (Factor Cole's boats at night; he's been paid to stay quiet).
