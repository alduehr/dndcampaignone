> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The village itself is player-safe. Goodwife Sennet's deep-wood secrets (the forest-rules, the Old Mast's "presence") are DM-only and gated until M5/M6 progress. The player sees a quiet charcoal-burners' village; the DM knows it sits at the edge of something old.

## Settlement: Coldhearth (Tollwood — deep-forest charcoal-burners' village)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. A small, smoke-darkened village in a clearing half a day into the Tollwood, where charcoal-burning and wood-working support a close-knit community. The trees press in on three sides; the East Road is 2 hrs W.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (37,27).
- **Population / scale:** ~90–130. Charcoal-burners, woodcutters, a single bone-set healer, a few farming households on a small cleared patch. Goodwife Sennet is the true elder; Brann the Guide is the traveler's face.
- **Water/road relationship:** no road access directly — only wood-tracks. The **East Road** is 2 hrs W toward Hartfell. A wood-track leads N toward **Tollstone Cross** (1.5 hrs, dangerous). The **Old Mast** is ~3 hrs deeper into the wood (gated, dangerous; DO NOT send low-level PC without scouting).

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering the village:** village is wary of outsiders but not hostile. Persuasion DC 12 for genuine welcome; DC 14 if arriving from the N track (Tollstone Cross direction = bandit-associated).
- **Asking about the deep wood or the Old Mast:** Brann refuses flatly (DC 20 to push him; see guide notes above). Sennet speaks carefully in forest-rule terms (DC 14; M5 oblique — the "grey lords" took the dead before the roads fell).
- **Breaking the forest-rules (cutting a marked tree, ignoring the Mark-Stone):** the village turns hostile; Sennet calls for the intruder to leave (unanimous village pressure; if ignored, the Old Mast's "presence" stirs — see DM-Only Layer; treat as a curse escalation mechanic).
- **Armed threat in the village:** Brann + 4 woodcutters (Guard-like; AC 12; +3 attack; they fight in the tree-line and know the ground).

## Quest Hooks

- **The Hanging Oaks:** Brann will guide to the Hanging Oaks — a pre-Concord offering-grove turned Gravecaller cell. Investigation there leads to M4/M5 (L3–4; DC 14 Survival or Brann to navigate; links to THE_HANGING_OAKS dungeon).
- **The forest-rules:** Sennet can explain the "bargain" — village keeps the forest-rules (offerings at the Mark-Stone; never cut marked trees) and the "presence" in the deep wood sleeps. Violating the rules is a gated M5/M6 escalation trigger (L4+).
- **Old Gethin's names:** Gethin calls the Old Mast "the Mast of the Grey Lords." If the player asks what that means, he says "they took the dead before the roads fell." DC 15 to get coherent directions (M5 clue; gated).

## Encounter / Treasure References

- Encounter: Mast-Beast (see `TOLLWOOD_ENCOUNTERS.md`; CR 4–6; deep wood only; not in the village); Gravecaller scouts (Cultist-like, AC 11; operating from the Hanging Oaks direction); Bandit looters from Tollstone Cross (Bandit, CR 1/4; 2–4 of them; avoid the village but prey on the logging camps).
- Treasure: `14_treasure_and_artifacts/REWARDS_BY_LEVEL.md` Tier 1–2 — charcoal-burner coin (1d6 sp); Brann's guide-fee; Sennet's favor (access to forest-rule lore; earns trust with Mourners' Circle network).

## Player-Safe Layer

Smoke-darkened village in a clearing; the Hearth-Stone cold and ash-stained; bone-chimes at Sennet's door; the burn-pit smoke hanging low; Brann's careful watchfulness; the Mark-Stone at the south edge with a fresh leaf-offering on it. The wood presses close; the light in the clearing is always a little grey.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on player map)

- Goodwife Sennet knows the forest-rules as a *grief-keeper's duty*, not as superstition. The "grey lords" who "took the dead before the roads fell" is an oblique pre-Concord half-memory of the Custodian harvest (M5/M6 gated; she has no name for it beyond the old song).
- The Mark-Stone offering tradition actually maintains a latent seal on the Old Mast's "presence" — a pre-Concord wood-power that the Custodians never fully contained. Breaking the forest-rules awakens it incrementally. This is distinct from the central Concord harvest; the Old Mast is a regional echo, not the keystone.
- Wren-of-the-Wood knows a path to within 30 min of the Old Mast. She doesn't understand the danger. She is a minor color figure (NOT Wren Brevin).
