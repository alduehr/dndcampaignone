## Settlement: Saint Veddow's Rest (Vale pilgrimage shrine-town)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south). Town wraps a green hill crowned by the shrine.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (23,33); campaign-grid (48,74).
- **Water/road relationship:** a hill-shrine town half a day's climb above Orchardmere; the pilgrim-track switchbacks up; reached by the South Road / Orchardmere lake pilgrim-boats.

## Bridges / Fords / Ferries

- Pilgrim-boats on Orchardmere lake (to the Vale hub). The shrine-hill is a climb, not a crossing.

## Landmarks

The Shrine Crown (the pale hill-tomb); the Warden House; the Relic Row; the Pilgrim Hostels; the sealed inner-tomb door.

## Player-Safe Layer

The town; the green shrine-hill; the pale shrine (the oldest, finest in the region); the Warden House; the Relic Row; the pilgrim-track and lake-boats. The shrine is a beloved saint's tomb.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The inner tomb (D09) is a **major capped Concord shrine — a secondary node**, the largest harvest installation outside the Reach, dormant but warming (M2/M6 fragments).
- The resident Wardens **unknowingly guard a seal** (Adwen holds a fragment of the truth).
- The "saint's relics" are Remembrance relics quarried from the shrine's outer chambers (M3).
- "Saint Veddow" is a composite legend grown over the cap.

## Unresolved Map Gaps

- The inner-tomb descent geometry is in the dungeon file (D09); the surface town is fully mappable.
