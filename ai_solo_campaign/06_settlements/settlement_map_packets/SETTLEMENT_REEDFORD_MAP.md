> **Secrecy classification:** Player-safe. Reedford is a minor Mirewend ford hamlet with no hidden secrets of its own; it serves as a travel node and resupply stop.

## Settlement: Reedford (Sundering Reach — ford hamlet on the Mirewend)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. A handful of cottages and a single inn-shed at the ford where the Concord road crosses the upper Mirewend. The ford is shallow but wide; the banks are reedy and boggy in spring.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (22,21).
- **Population / scale:** ~30–45. A ferryman family, a widow-mourner, a few farming households. No permanent authority.
- **Water/road relationship:** the **Mirewend River** runs E–W here, crossable at the ford (waist-deep in summer, chest-deep in spring); the **Concord road** crosses N–S; Hollowmere is 1 hr N; Greywater Holm is 45 min SE.

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Crossing the ford in flood season:** Athletics DC 13 or use Dob's punt. Failure = swept 20 ft downstream, Strength DC 12 to grab bank.
- **Entering the hamlet:** completely open; no authority. Persuasion DC 10 for a welcome fire.
- **Trouble at the ford (ambush, fen creature):** no guards; flee or fight unaided. Dob will help if the water is involved (Guard-like; DC 12).

## Quest Hooks

- **Ford blocked by flood or fen-beast:** the ford is impassable; Dob's punt is damaged; someone needs to cross urgently (L1; improvised; DC 12 Athletics or repair the punt).
- **Widow Sel's worry:** a traveler she witnessed at the ford three days ago matched the description of a missing person from Candlewick — she'll tell anyone who listens (L1 rumor hook to Candlewick/Reach missing-person thread).
- **Road message:** Goodwife Nan is holding a message-package left by a traveler going the other direction; it has an unfamiliar seal (minor intrigue hook; L1).
