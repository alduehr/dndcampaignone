> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The drowned shrine / keystone / Hollow Court beneath the basin is DM-only. Never hand directly to the player.

## Settlement: Hollowmere (the starting hub)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south). (50,50) = town center on the basin rim.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (24,23); campaign-grid (50,45).
- **Water/road relationship:** rings the **Hollowmere basin** (dark central water); the Mirewend feeds the basin; in-Reach Concord roads radiate out (S→Candlewick, E→Kettle Bridge, W→Saltmargin).

## Bridges / Fords / Ferries

- Plank walkways cross the boggy basin edges (the Boards). No true bridge; the basin is a dead-end dark water.

## Landmarks (inn / temple / faction / cemetery / market)

The Drowned Lantern (inn); Mourner's Green (cemetery + Wend's cottage); the Counting-House (Ledger); the Warden Rest; the Rim market/council hall.

## Player-Safe Layer

The town rim; the basin (dark water, unexplained); the four road exits; all named areas above; the Brevin cottage (the opening crisis).

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The **drowned shrine / Under-Shrine / Drowned Keystone** beneath the basin (D23, vertical, deepest Z).
- The **Hollow Court** seat below.
- The **Concord Deep** hub under the basin.
- The Ledger vault relic (D04) that "remembers" the harvest.
- Reke's true allegiance; the drainage scheme's real purpose.

## Unresolved Map Gaps

- Stilt-house micro-layout is illustrative; the Boards' exact plank-path geometry is improv-safe.
