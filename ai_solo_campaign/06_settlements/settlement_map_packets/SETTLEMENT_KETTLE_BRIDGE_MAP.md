> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The Sunken Tollhouse node beneath the broken arch is DM-only. Never hand directly to the player.

## Settlement: Kettle Bridge (the Mirewend crossing / E gateway)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south). Center = the bridge.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (27,23); campaign-grid (60,44).
- **Water/road relationship:** astride the only good **Mirewend** crossing for days; the East Road lifts onto a Concord causeway and crosses on the great old bridge (three Concord arches + a patched fourth). Town clusters at both bridge-ends.

## Landmarks

The Drowned Kettle (inn); the Toll-house; the grain-mill; the Compact House (lockup); the Warden rest + Mourners' house (Two Houses Lane).

## Player-Safe Layer

The bridge + toll-chain; the market; the riverbank slum; the Compact House; the inn; the road exits; river boat-hire.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The **Sunken Tollhouse** (D03) — a drowned Concord **node** in the river-mud beneath the broken fourth arch (the harvest-conduit that cracked at the Quietfall; M2/M5).
- The river-drownings are Remembrance leakage (M5).
- Bly's quiet relic-salvage trade.
