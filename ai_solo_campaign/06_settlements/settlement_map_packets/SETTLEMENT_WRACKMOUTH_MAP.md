> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The Drowned Lamp coastal node and Cole's sea-relic trade are DM-only. Never hand directly to the player.

## Settlement: Wrackmouth (Coast hub/port)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south). Harbor at the S/W; town climbs the cliff.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (12,23); campaign-grid (14,44).
- **Water/road relationship:** a steep cliff-town in a deep cove; the **Pale Sea** is W/S; the Pale Road climbs in from the E; the **Pale Coast Sea-Route** departs from the harbor (down-coast → Verdance mouth → Caradril). The broken Drowned Lamp lighthouse stands on the far headland.

## Bridges / Fords / Ferries

- Harbor quays and the salt-hulk moorings; skipper-hire (Wenna Roke) for the sea-route. No bridges.

## Landmarks

The Gull & Anchor (inn); the Weigh-House (Ledger); the Tide-Bell (Mourners' shrine); the Moot-Stair (harbor-moot); the broken Drowned Lamp on the far headland (visible from town).

## Player-Safe Layer

The harbor; the cliff-town; the Pale Sea; the Pale Road in; the sea-route out; all named areas; the Drowned Lamp as a visible "ruined lighthouse" landmark.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The **Drowned Lamp** (D14) is a coastal Concord **node**, warming (M2 fragment) — surface = "ruined lighthouse" only.
- Cole's **sea-relic** (Remembrance relic) trade (M3; the Monopoly clock's coastal arm).
- The Gravecaller current through the Wrack/wreckers.
- The drowned-tide (the maritime harvest leaking; M5).

## Unresolved Map Gaps

- The wild-coast run S toward the Verdance mouth is illustrative (the sea-route bends S then inland).
