> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The Barrow Complex (D26) is a pre-Concord echo of an older fall — surface/echo, never the keystone. Marrowmoot is an honest chalk-downs shire-town: open, prosperous, politically engaged by moot. Never hand directly to the player.

## Settlement: Marrowmoot (Marrowdowns — shire-moot town)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south); (0,0) = NW corner. Open chalk-downs town; moot-ground at the center; market and inn SE; Barrow Road leads NE.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (50,66).
- **Population / scale:** ~500–800 permanent residents; a prosperous chalk-downs shire-town; the regional political hub; swells to 1,500+ during moot-days.
- **Water/road relationship:** the **Mardenflow** rises nearby (52,64) — small chalk stream flows through the town; the **Salt Road** (caravan track) passes through E–W; roads connect to Penmark Hold (47,63) and Wether (53,68).

## Law and Threat DCs

- **Entering Marrowmoot:** no gate or fee; the downs are open. Simply arrive.
- **Disturbing a moot in session:** Moot-Mother intervenes formally; Persuasion DC 14 to explain; DC 16 to override her intervention.
- **Carrying Barrow salvage:** Shire-Reeve Penmark enforces the moot's rule (no Barrow grave-robbing); Persuasion DC 16 to explain a legitimate purpose.
- **Violent act in town:** small watch (8 guards, **Guard-like**, AC 13, ~11 HP) responds; Shire-Reeve Penmark imposes downs-style justice (public reprimand, compensation, or exile).
- **Approaching the Barrow-Fields (D26):** allowed but strongly warned; Doss Wether offers to guide; the walking dead are a known hazard.

## Quest Hooks

- **Q_MD_001 — The Barrow Problem:** Doss Wether comes in from the Barrow-Fields white-faced; something changed at D26 (the wrong kind of dead); Senna wants to know; Penmark wants it suppressed (L6).
- **Q_MD_002 — The Moot's Truth:** Senna calls a special moot on the question of "what the barrows keep" — she needs someone who can speak to the moot from outside the shire politics (L8+).
- **Barrow escort:** Wether hires an escort for a Barrow-Fields recovery run; he knows where something valuable is buried and needs company (L6+; leads to D26).

## Player-Safe Layer

The open moot-ground, the grief-keeper's hall, the wool-market with its seasonal buyers, the shire-reeve's circuit-office, the warm inn, and the Barrow Road pointing NE. The town reads as an honest, open shire-community — the most "normal" far-region settlement, except for the Barrow-Fields and what Wether saw there.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The Barrow Complex (D26) is **pre-Concord chalk-downs burial-grounds** — their stirring dead and Senna's oblique old-songs are M2/M5-oblique echoes. NEVER the keystone.
- Moot-Mother Senna Crale's grief-lore is the faintest oblique hint in the southlands: "the barrows are giving back the wrong dead." She is not connected to the Concord; this is folk-wisdom of an older disaster.
- No apex geometry here. The endgame is vertical beneath Hollowmere (NW only).

## Unresolved Map Gaps

- The chalk-stream through town is small and not navigationally significant.
- Penmark Hold and Wether are separate settlement packets (to be created); they are day-trip distance from Marrowmoot.
