> **Secrecy classification:** Mixed. The Deep Adit (secondary node) and the Reclamation race are DM-only. Never hand directly to the player.

## Settlement: Harrowgast (Sunder Heights mining town)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south). Clings to a scree slope in the Heights.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (25,17); campaign-grid (52,26).
- **Water/road relationship:** a half-ruined Concord mining town in the **Sunder Heights**, N of Hollowmere (~2.5 days); the Concord road climbs out of the fens to it; black adit-mouths cut the mountain. Staging point for the Heights ruins.

## Bridges / Fords / Ferries

- None (highland mining town). Adit-mouths and spoil-heaps define the terrain.

## Landmarks

The Cold Drum (tavern); the Ledger weigh-post (Spoil-Heaps); the Open Adits; the Stilled Quarter (haunted ruins); the barred Deep Mouth.

## Player-Safe Layer

The mining town on its scree; the road in; the adit-mouths; the spoil-heaps; the weigh-post; the Stilled Quarter ruins; the barred Deep Mouth (a sealed, feared tunnel).

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The **Deep Adit** (D05) is a **secondary Concord node** — a deep harvest-adit the miners have unknowingly dug toward (M3/M6).
- The deep tunnels leak raw Remembrance ("old glass"; the rock "remembers"); the reversible **thin-touch** risk.
- The **Concord Remnant Reclamation race** (Veska Dunn / Quorrin's agents) to reach the Deep Adit first (M6/M7 foreshadow).

## Unresolved Map Gaps

- The deep-tunnel network beyond the Deep Mouth is in the dungeon file (D05); the surface town is fully mappable.
