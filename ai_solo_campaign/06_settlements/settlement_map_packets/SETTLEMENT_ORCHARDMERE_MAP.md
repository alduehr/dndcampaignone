## Settlement: Orchardmere (Vale hub)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south). Town on the N shore of Orchardmere lake.
- **Full-continent position:** render-grid (25,32); campaign-grid (52,70).
- **Water/road relationship:** orchard-and-cider town on **Orchardmere lake**, fed by the **Ammet**; the **South Road** runs through (N→Candlewick/the Reach, S→the downs/Caradril). Pilgrim-boats ferry to the Saint Veddow's track.
