## Population and Scale

Calderport is the greatest city-state on the Hollow Gulf — the continent's southern maritime gateway and the door to Surren overseas. Population 30,000–50,000 within the city proper; the harbor swells with seasonal sailors and foreign traders. Scale: a warm, crowded, cosmopolitan port; the south's version of Caradril but sea-facing and international. Wealth concentrated in the upper city and harbor merchants; the docks are rough and polyglot.
