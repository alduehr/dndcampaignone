## Population and Scale

Ashfast is the fortress-temple-city of the Emberfell Theocracy — population 15,000–25,000 (the closed theocracy limits outside settlement). Every resident is a subject of the faith; foreign visitors are unusual and watched. Scale: a fortress-city, not a commercial hub; imposing, fire-imaged, self-sufficient. Wealth flows through the theocracy's officials, not free markets. The Permitted Market is the only sanctioned commerce; everything else is gray or forbidden.

## Quest Hooks (3)

1. **Q_ET_001 — The Ash Roads Passage** (Lvl 12–14): To enter the closed theocracy at all, the player must win safe-conduct from Pyre-Warden Sef Embren on the Ash Roads — proving they are no heretic and no Waste-breacher. Gateway quest to all Emberfell content. (`09_quests/by_region/EMBERFELL_THEOCRACY_QUESTS.md`)

2. **Q_ET_002 — The Heretic's Question** (Lvl 13–15): Ember-Sage Mira Cindra watches a northern outsider with the hunger of forbidden questions — "what do your northern dead *do*?" Exchanging frontier knowledge for Ashfast lore risks exposing her heresy to the Pyre-Confessor. (`09_quests/by_region/EMBERFELL_THEOCRACY_QUESTS.md`)

3. **Q_ET_003 — The Forbidden Salvage** (Lvl 12–15): Cinder-Master Doss Ashfast needs a discreet buyer and muscle for "old glass" (forbidden Remembrance relics) dug near the Waste — and his deep crews are coming back "ash-touched and wrong." (`09_quests/by_region/EMBERFELL_THEOCRACY_QUESTS.md`)
