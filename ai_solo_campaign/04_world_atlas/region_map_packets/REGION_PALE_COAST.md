## Region: Pale Coast (MF-004)

- **Local grid:** 0–100 (X east, Y south). (0,0) = NW of the region frame.
- **Full-continent bounds:** render-grid **X 10–18, Y 17–26** (W of the Reach). Local (50,50) ≈ full (14,21).
- **Campaign-grid anchor:** Wrackmouth = campaign (14,44); the Coast runs N–S along the W edge.
- **Orientation note:** the **Pale Sea is to the W (local X<30 = open water)**; land rises E toward the Reach.

## Local Labels (player map)

Pale Coast; Wrackmouth; Cobble Strand; the Pale Sea; the Skerries ("wrecking reef"); the Pale Road; "ruined lighthouse" (Drowned Lamp); the Verdance mouth.

## Player-Safe Layer

Settlements, the Pale Sea, the cliff-road, the sea-route, the Verdance mouth, and label-only hazard markers (Skerries, Drowned Lamp, Wreckers' Caves). Coastal nodes are "ruined lighthouse"/"wrecking reef," never nodes.

## DM-Only Layer (NEVER on the player map)

- The Drowned Lamp and Skerry Shrine are coastal Concord **nodes** (the network's W reach; M2/M5/M6).
- The wreckers' cove Gravecaller current.
- The "drowned-tide" (the maritime harvest leaking) — never as a map fact.

## Unresolved Map Gaps

- The exact run of the wild coast S toward the Verdance mouth is LOW; the sea-route bends S then inland.
- Offshore Skerries/stack positions are illustrative.
