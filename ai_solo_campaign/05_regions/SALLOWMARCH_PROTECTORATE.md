## AI Use

Load when the player reaches the Mardenflow delta on the south coast. Narrative/political anchor (Phase 5 consolidation, 2026-07-07; **no new canon**); cartography/mechanics in the map packet (which also documents the level-range decision: authored content sets **10–14**); people/quests in the by_region files. The delta itself is the antagonist as much as any faction.

## One-Sentence Identity

A fever-ridden delta of rice, reed, and smuggling at the Mardenflow's mouth, held under the cold, tolerated rule of an absent southern crown — foreign occupation laid over a marsh-people who endure it, smuggle around it, and outlast it.

## Player-Facing Overview

The Mardenflow splays into reed-country and fever-mist. Protector-Legate Vorr Sallow governs for a crown across the sea from the drained garrison-shelf at Fenward; Reed-Boss "Heron" Maddox runs everything the crown can't tax through the Fever Channels from Reedmouth; Marsh-Healer Doll Fenn — the delta's true leader — fights the fevers; and Fen-Witch Mother Sela Reed keeps the water-shrines where the delta's drowned are sung down. The paddy-folk of the Rice Sallows feed everyone and are asked by no one.

## DM-Only Truth

The "new sickness" (Q_SP_002) and the deep "drowned grounds" (Q_SP_006) are the crisis's far-south face — rites failing in the wettest country on the continent (M5-oblique). The Drowned Steps offshore (D30, shared landmark with the Hollow Gulf) are a **pre-Concord/Concord sea-causeway echo** marching toward something *older and separate*. **Guardrail:** far-south corroboration only; never the keystone/Deep/Court; the fen-witches' warnings stay folk-true, not explanatory.

## Major Settlements

- **Fenward** — garrison-capital on the delta's driest ground; the crown's gate and causeway.
- **Reedmouth** — the smuggling-village; Maddox's hidden channel-ferries.

## Minor Settlements

- **The Rice Sallows** — paddy-country villages and dyke-works; the Rice Moot (Q_SP_007; Speaker Pell Sallows).

## Wilderness Zones

The Fever Channels (deep shifting backwaters — smuggler country, crown boats can't follow, fen-witch-warned); the deep-delta "drowned grounds"; the tidal flats.

## Factions Present

The **crown's protectorate** (Vorr Sallow's fever-thinned garrison — the occupying power); **Maddox's smuggling network**; the **fen-Mourners** (Sela Reed's water-shrines); **Doll Fenn's healers** (the delta's real authority in a plague year); **Cinder Ledger** (rice-and-revenue factors at the edges).

## Regional Conflicts

- **Occupation** (Q_SP_001/005): the crown's peace vs. the delta's patience; the vanishing revenue.
- **The new sickness** (Q_SP_002/004): plague on top of fever; Fenn vs. the impossible.
- **The channels** (Q_SP_003): Maddox's smuggler-kingdom vs. everyone's law.
- **The drowned ground** (Q_SP_006): what the deep delta gives back.

## Regional Secrets (DM-Only)

The "drowned grounds" hold raw Remembrance exposure (Thin-touch); the crown's revenue vanishes into more pockets than Maddox's; Sela Reed's rites still *mostly* work — which is itself a datum (the failure gradient runs N→S).

## Recurring Threats

Fever (the region's true monster); crown soldiers (Guard/Veteran-like, fever-weakened); delta smugglers (Bandit/Scout-like; Maddox Scout/Assassin-like); marsh predators; drowned-dead/Memory-Echo profiles at the deep ruins (L11–14). Horror additions (2026-07-07): see `SALLOWMARCH_ENCOUNTERS.md`. Stat references: encounter file + map packet.

## Quest Hooks

Seven developed quests — Q_SP_001 The Crown's Peace · 002 The New Sickness · 003 The Heron and the Channels · 004 The Fever and the Fen · 005 The Revenue That Vanished · 006 The Drowned Ground · 007 The Rice Moot (`../09_quests/by_region/SALLOWMARCH_PROTECTORATE_QUESTS.md`).

## Encounter Themes

Environment-first (fever, flood, shifting water), occupation-politics second, smuggling third, the drowned dead last and worst.

## Level Range And Solo Danger

**Lvl 10–14.** Moderate–high: the delta itself is the danger. Lethal-at-level (telegraphed): the Fever Channels and the drowned grounds — fen-witch warnings, shifting water, the fever's toll; travel the Channels only with Maddox's pilots. The garrison is slow and fever-weakened: bargain or vanish into the marsh. Details: map packet §Level Range.

## Encounter And Hazard Mechanics

Authoritative in the map packet: marsh-fever Con save DC 13–15 (Fenn's craft mitigates); Fever Channels Survival DC 16–17 unpiloted; tidal/flood windows; drowned-ground Thin-touch. Scaling: L10 village dispute + contained outbreak → L14 occupation flashpoint + delta-wide plague + the deep drowned ground at full danger.

## What Changes If Ignored

The sickness spreads beyond the delta (a continental plague-thread); the crown answers vanishing revenue with a crackdown the delta answers in kind; Maddox's kingdom becomes the only law; the drowned grounds widen.
