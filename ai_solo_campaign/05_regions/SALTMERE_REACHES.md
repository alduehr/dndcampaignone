## AI Use

Load when the player crosses the s-central interior to the dying salt sea. Narrative/political anchor (Phase 5 consolidation, 2026-07-07; **no new canon**); cartography/mechanics in the map packet; people/quests in the by_region files. Tone: elegy — a people watching their world literally recede.

## One-Sentence Identity

A dying inland salt sea ringed by salt-clan holds and the bone-white Bonepan Flats — where the receding water uncovers whole drowned towns, proving that catastrophe and troubled dead are older than the Concord.

## Player-Facing Overview

The Saltmere has been going down for generations. Salt-Clan Matriarch Bryd Saltmere holds the confederacy together from Brackhold's harbor-mole; Saltcairn works the Ghostmark edge; the clans harvest the pans, run their barge-lanes, and sing the going-down song. Where the water has fled, the Drowned Towns stand up out of the brine — older than any road the Concord built — and Delver Oss brings things up from them that Salt-Mother Tess Brackhold says should be sung down again. East and south, the Bonepan Flats kill the unprepared by glare and thirst.

## DM-Only Truth

The Drowned Towns are **pre-Concord ruins** — the campaign's strongest "older, wider fall" corroboration (M6-by-contrast; C_SR2_001). Their walking dead are salt-preserved, not harvested. The Ledger's salt-monopoly play (Q_SALT_003) is the Vyre pattern applied to a dying economy. Tess's old songs are genuine M2/M5-oblique folk-memory. **Guardrail:** the "going-down" is a pre-Concord catastrophe-echo, never the harvest mechanism; keystone/Deep/Court stay NW.

## Geography

Full physical geography, climate, and culture: `vael/orrun/regions/saltmere-reaches`. Coordinate geometry and mechanical cartography: map packet.

## Major Settlements

- **Brackhold** — the largest surviving salt-clan hold; the matriarch's harbor.
- **Saltcairn** — Ghostmark-edge clan-town; terminus of the Bonepan caravan-track.

## Minor Settlements

None living — **the Drowned Towns** are a pre-Concord ruin-cluster (D24), not a settlement.

## Wilderness Zones

The Bonepan Flats (glare, mirage, thirst — the region's environmental killer); the receding shore (emerging ruins, wrong-coming dead); the deep Saltmere (brine-squalls, clan barge-lanes); the Ghostmark foothills (rockfall, stirring waystation dead).

## Travel Routes

Full travel-time detail: `vael/orrun/regions/saltmere-reaches` (Travel Routes section). Campaign-specific: the Bonepan Track requires dry-season water discipline; clan-barge lanes require a pilot. Mechanical/DM route authority: map packet §Routes.

## Factions Present

The **salt-clan confederacy** (Bryd's fraying authority — the regional power); the **salt-Mourners** (Tess Brackhold's rites); **Cinder Ledger** (salt-factors circling the dying economy — Q_SALT_003); **salvage networks** (Oss's delvers); **Marek Bonepan's** caravans (the Flats' one reliable crossing).

## Regional Conflicts

- **The dying sea**: every year less water, less salt, less clan.
- **The dying clan** (Q_SALT_007): a hold facing its end; the confederacy's future.
- **The Ledger's salt** (Q_SALT_003): monopoly money offering to "save" what it will own.
- **What comes up** (Q_SALT_001/002): the towns the lake gives back, and what Oss found below.

## Regional Secrets (DM-Only)

The Drowned Towns' deepest streets predate even the clans' songs; the going-down song (Q_SALT_004) preserves a real memory of the older fall; the Listener at the Water (Q_SALT_006) hears more than superstition allows.

## Recurring Threats

Salt-preserved dead (Salt-Mummy/Desiccated Dead, CR 3–6, chassis: Mummy 2024 MM; Jiangshi VRGtR/RtHW as published kin); the Salt-Stalker (custom, chassis: Bulette); Bonepan mirage-spirits (Air-elemental chassis); clan feuds; Ledger enforcers. Stat references: `SALTMERE_REACHES_ENCOUNTERS.md` + map packet.

Seven developed quests — Q_SALT_001 The Town the Lake Gave Back · 002 What Oss Found Below · 003 The Ledger's Salt · 004 The Going-Down Song · 005 The White Flats · 006 The Listener at the Water · 007 The Dying Clan (`../09_quests/by_region/SALTMERE_REACHES_QUESTS.md`).

## Encounter Themes

Environment-first (glare, thirst, brine-squall); the preserved dead at the ruins; clan-politics ashore; the Ledger circling.

## Level Range And Solo Danger

**Lvl 11–15.** Moderate ashore, high at the ruins and on the Flats — both heavily telegraphed (salt-white standing figures, the clans' refusals, Marek Bonepan's water-discipline). The environment amplifies everything: heat, thirst, and mirage are the real killers. Details: map packet §Level Range.

## Encounter And Hazard Mechanics

Authoritative in the map packet + encounter file: Bonepan heat/thirst and glare rules; desiccation-curse saves (Con DC 13–15); ruin Thin-touch; barge-pilot requirements. Scaling: L11 a single emerged street + clan dispute → L15 the deep towns open + the confederacy's crisis.

## What Changes If Ignored

The confederacy fractures hold by hold; the Ledger buys the salt-economy's corpse; the Drowned Towns' dead spread along the retreating shore; the going-down song is forgotten a verse at a time. Arc consequences: `../15_campaign_arcs/REGIONAL_ARC_PACKS_LEVEL_5_TO_20.md`, `CONTINENTAL_PRESSURE_TIMELINE.md`.
