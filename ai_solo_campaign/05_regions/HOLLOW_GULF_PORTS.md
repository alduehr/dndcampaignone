## AI Use

Load when the player reaches the warm southern ports (down the Glasswater Run or along the south coast). Narrative/political anchor (Phase 5 consolidation, 2026-07-07; **no new canon**); cartography/mechanics in the map packet; Calderport's interior in `CALDERPORT_CITY_MAP.md`. This is the campaign's most *normal* region — proof most of the world neither knows nor cares about the frontier crisis — and the **gateway overseas** (Surren; world-horizon).

## One-Sentence Identity

A string of great warm-water port city-states ringing the Hollow Gulf — rival merchant ports, the gate to Surren and the wider world, safe ashore and dangerous at sea.

## Player-Facing Overview

Calderport, greatest of the Gulf ports, rises in counting-house terraces over its crescent harbor under Port-Mistress Ive Calder; Saltgate glowers across the Gulf under Port-Lord Doss Saltgate; the Mardenmouth wharves work the delta-mouth between them. Privateer-Captain Roke Mallin's licensed raiders prowl the Reach Lanes; Foreign-Factor Hadiz of Surren keeps the overseas door in Calderport's Foreign Quarter; and at the tide-line, Tide-Mother Sera Mardenmouth's sea-Mourners sing down the drowned — more often, lately, than the ports care to notice.

## DM-Only Truth

The Gulf's normalcy is the point: the harvest's failure gradient barely reaches here (M5 maritime fringe: "the sea's own dead," Q_HGP_006). The Wreck-Reef (D31) and the shared Drowned Steps (D30) hold only **faint far-south relic-echoes**. Hadiz and the overseas door are the campaign's world-horizon, not a mystery thread. **Guardrail:** never the keystone/Deep/Court; sailors' dread stays folk-true.

## Geography

The warm crescent of the Hollow Gulf: Calderport's harbor (W-central), Saltgate's headland (E), the Mardenmouth delta-wharves (W), the open Gulf and Reach Lanes running south to the Calm Reach and overseas. The Glasswater/Mardenflow deltas empty here. Full geometry: map packet.

## Major Settlements

- **Calderport** — greatest Gulf port; the Foreign Quarter (overseas traders) and the Mardenmouth wharves; city packet `CALDERPORT_CITY_MAP.md`.
- **Saltgate** — the rival port city-state on its own headland.

## Minor Settlements

- **The Mardenmouth** — delta-mouth wharf-district and sea-shrines (Sera Mardenmouth's ground).

## Wilderness Zones

The open Gulf and Reach Lanes (privateers, storms); the Wreck-Reef (offshore salvage-ground/dungeon); the Gulf-mouth approaches (lighthouse-marked).

## Travel Routes

The **Glasswater Run** in from Glassmere (~3–4 weeks by river); the **Reach Lanes** (coastal: Calderport ↔ Saltgate ↔ Mardenmouth, hours–days by ship); the **South Sea Lanes** overseas to Surren (weeks); the delta-road W into the Sallowmarch. Map packet §Routes.

## Factions Present

The **port city-states** themselves (Calder vs. Saltgate — the region's great rivalry); **Mallin's privateers** (licensed, leashed, slipping the leash); the **sea-Mourners** (Sera's tide-rites); the **overseas factors** (Hadiz; Surren trade); **Cinder Ledger** (sea-relic and cargo interests at the margins).

## Regional Conflicts

- **Two harbors, one knife** (Q_HGP_002): Calderport vs. Saltgate, fought in charters, spies, and the occasional hull.
- **The privateer's leash** (Q_HGP_005): Mallin serves whoever holds it — until he doesn't.
- **The crown's cut** (Q_HGP_008): the Sallowmarch crown's tariff-reach into Gulf trade.
- **The sea's own dead** (Q_HGP_006): the drowned-tide arriving in a region that has no frame for it.
- **The door overseas** (Q_HGP_004): who controls the Surren trade.

## Regional Secrets (DM-Only)

The Wreck-Reef's drowned harbour-quarter holds relic-salvage with a faint Remembrance echo; one of the two ports is quietly buying wreck-salvage through a cut-out; Mallin holds a letter that would embarrass whichever port didn't write it.

## Recurring Threats

Privateer/pirate crews (Mallin: Pirate-Captain/Veteran-like AC 16 ~75 HP; Bandit/Veteran-like crews); port-guards and dock-toughs; fever-thinned crown customs; sea-drowned guardians at the Reef/Steps (drowned-dead profiles L12–15, telegraphed). Stat references: `HOLLOW_GULF_ENCOUNTERS.md` + map packet.

## Quest Hooks

Eight developed quests — Q_HGP_001 The Port-Mistress's Favor · 002 Two Harbors, One Knife · 003 The Salt Crow's Wager · 004 The Door Overseas · 005 The Privateer's Leash · 006 The Sea's Own Dead · 007 The Drowned Steps · 008 The Crown's Cut (`../09_quests/by_region/HOLLOW_GULF_PORTS_QUESTS.md`).

## Encounter Themes

Ashore: intrigue, trade, port-politics. At sea: privateers, storms, and the deadly dives. The split is the region's design.

## Level Range And Solo Danger

**Lvl 10–15.** Low ashore; **high at sea** — sea-battles, and the Wreck-Reef/Drowned Steps dives are heavily telegraphed (sailors' refusals, the sea-Mourners' warnings, the wreck-strewn approach). The dive aborts at each "step"; ship-fights are escapable by speed and guile. Details: map packet §Level Range.

## Encounter And Hazard Mechanics

Authoritative in the map packet: the dive (Vehicles(water)/Survival DC 16–17, Athletics DC 16, tidal windows); reef wreck-hazards; deep-ruin Thin-touch; storm-season pressure on the lanes; social DCs 16–18 (Calder, Saltgate, Hadiz, Mallin).

## What Changes If Ignored

The port rivalry sharpens toward open letters-of-marque war; Mallin slips the leash and the lanes grow lawless; the drowned-tide worsens unanswered at the Mardenmouth; the overseas door swings on someone else's hinge. Arc consequences: `../15_campaign_arcs/REGIONAL_ARC_PACKS_LEVEL_5_TO_20.md`, `CONTINENTAL_PRESSURE_TIMELINE.md`.
