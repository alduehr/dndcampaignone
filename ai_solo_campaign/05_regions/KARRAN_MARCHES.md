## AI Use

Load when the player reaches the cold NE mining frontier. Narrative/political anchor (Phase 5 consolidation, 2026-07-07; **no new canon**); cartography/mechanics in the map packet. Design note: this is a **dark mirror of the Sundering Reach** — the same frontier bones under the rule of the strong instead of the Compact's law.

## One-Sentence Identity

A second, harsher frontier — cold stone, mines, petty-warlord holds, the rule of the strong, and peripheral Concord ruins whose stirring dead the warlords simply call "bad ground."

## Player-Facing Overview

Below the Karran Teeth, might makes law. Warlord "Iron" Brask holds the largest fortress-town and tolls the Iron Road; Karran-Gate, the mining-and-mercenary town, is the closest thing to neutral ground, run in practice by Ore-Factor Mully Karr's ledgers; Pass-Toll Warlord Hessa Teeth taxes the mountain approaches. The Deep Cuts — the richest mines in the Marches — have lately "gone wrong," and on the NE fringe the Old Iron forts crumble, where even Brask's men won't camp.

## DM-Only Truth

The Deep Cuts struck **something Concord** (Q_KM_001: the deep that went wrong; Q_KM_004: the humming iron), and Reclaimer-Captain Wenna Stone's Remnant expedition is racing to reach the Old Iron forts (D27) before anyone understands what the "old iron" is (M6 far-echo; C_KM_001). The warlords' "bad ground" talk is honest ignorance. **Guardrail:** peripheral ruins and echoes only; never the keystone/Deep/Court. Wenna Stone is turnable.

## Geography

Cold stone country under the Karran Teeth (the near-impassable NE barrier); Brask's Hold territory (W); Karran-Gate's mining basin (central); the Deep Cuts ore-complex (SW); the scattered Old Iron forts on the NE fringe. Mountain melt-streams; brine-tainted mine-sump drainage that "tasted wrong" before the ground went wrong. Full geometry: map packet.

## Major Settlements

- **Brask's Hold** — the warlord's fortress-town; toll, inspection, iron discipline.
- **Karran-Gate** — mining-and-mercenary town; the market, the muster-ground, and Mully Karr's toll-post (bribable).

## Minor Settlements

None — **the Deep Cuts** (mine-complex) and **the Old Iron forts** (ruin-cluster, D27) are work-camps and dead ground, not towns.

## Wilderness Zones

The Karran Teeth (extreme cold and altitude; the campaign's NE limit; the Sunder Ocean beyond); the Old Forts track (the dead thicken as you approach); the mine-country between holds.

## Travel Routes

The Iron Road S–N (weeks; Brask's toll; partly Concord-paved); the Deep Cuts access road (permit-gated); the Old Forts track NE (weeks, dangerous); the Teeth Pass N (near-impassable). W lies the Wender Steppe; S/SW the Hethewald. Map packet §Routes.

## Factions Present

**The warlords** (Brask the strongest; Hessa Teeth on the passes; lesser holds between — the regional power is exactly as stable as Brask's health); **Mully Karr's ore-trade** (money as the second law); **Concord Remnant** (Wenna Stone's Reclaimer expedition — the truth-race's NE finger); **Cinder Ledger** (ore-factors, arms-length); mercenary companies for hire at Karran-Gate.

## Regional Conflicts

- **The strong hand** (Q_KM_003): Brask's order vs. what replaces it.
- **The deep that went wrong** (Q_KM_001) and **the humming iron** (Q_KM_004): the mines' find and the race it started.
- **The gate with no law** (Q_KM_005): Karran-Gate's neutrality fraying.
- **The pass out** (Q_KM_007): who controls leaving.

## Regional Secrets (DM-Only)

The Old Iron forts were Concord *road-forts* — the network's NE periphery (relay-echoes, not nodes); Wenna Stone's orders exceed what Ferrant's institution would sanction; Brask knows exactly one true thing about the forts and trades on never saying it.

## Recurring Threats

Warlord muscle and mercenaries (Bandit/Veteran/Berserker-like); mine hazards (collapse, bad air, the wrong-tasting sump); the forts' stirring dead (Wight/Specter chassis + Relay-Warden construct profiles scaled L9–14, gated); mountain predators and giants (Hill/Stone giant, ettin — Track A); the cold. Stat references: `KARRAN_MARCHES_ENCOUNTERS.md` + map packet.

## Quest Hooks

Seven developed quests — Q_KM_001 The Deep That Went Wrong · 002 The Old Iron Fort · 003 The Strong Hand · 004 The Humming Iron · 005 The Gate With No Law · 006 The Deep Listener · 007 The Pass Out (`../09_quests/by_region/KARRAN_MARCHES_QUESTS.md`).

## Encounter Themes

Warlord politics and toll-muscle; mine-delving hazards; gated Concord-periphery dead; high-mountain survival.

## Level Range And Solo Danger

**Lvl 9–14.** Moderate in the holds (pay, bow, or be useful); high in the mines and at the forts — both telegraphed (the miners' refusals, Brask's own prohibition, the dead visibly thickening up the Old Forts track). The Teeth are an environment-lethality wall, not an encounter. Details: map packet §Level Range.

## Encounter And Hazard Mechanics

Authoritative in the map packet + encounter file: mine collapse/air hazards; cold/altitude Con checks; fort Thin-touch and gated fragments; hold-law social DCs (Brask inspects notable outsiders personally).

## What Changes If Ignored

The Deep Cuts' find reaches the wrong hands (Reclaimer or Ledger); Brask consolidates the Marches — or dies and the holds burn each other; the forts' dead walk down the Iron Road; Karran-Gate's neutrality ends.
