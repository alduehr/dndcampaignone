## AI Use

Load when the player travels up-river past Caradril toward inland Orrun (Ring 2; the first true step into the wider continent). This file is the region's **narrative/political anchor**, consolidating the authored layers built in Stages 9.5–15 — cartography and hazard mechanics live in the **map packet** (`REGION_VERDANCE_REACHES.md`, authoritative for geometry, DCs, and encounter stat references); people in the NPC file; quests in the quest file. Created 2026-07-07 (Phase 5 consolidation); **no new canon** — every name herein is already registered.

## One-Sentence Identity

The river-road corridor up the Verdance past Caradril — locks, wharves, and barge-villages where the cold frontier gives way to older, richer, settled inland Orrun, and where the death-rite crisis is *just beginning* to be noticed up-river.

## Player-Facing Overview

Barges climb the Verdance from Caradril through a settled corridor of terraced farms, lock-towns, and toll-points. Everything moves by water and everything pays: Lord Ennis Marrow taxes the Nine Locks, the Cinder Ledger's inland directorate (Factor-General Wessel Crane at Marrowfen Stair) squeezes the debt-side, and independent barge-captains like Mossa Drenn thread between them. Up-river, folk speak of "bad water" — stretches where rites fail and the drowned come up wrong — but call it river-superstition.

## DM-Only Truth

The corridor's drowned Concord lock-works are **waking minor nodes** — the crisis spreading inland (M2/M5 corroboration, never the keystone). The Nine Locks Sunken Stair (D34) is a relay-vault that *points home* toward the NW. The Ledger's inland push and the Remnant's quiet survey (Sub-Lector Hadwin Vael) are both circling the same truth without naming it. **Guardrail:** everything here is echo/fragment; the keystone, Concord Deep, and Hollow Court stay vertical beneath Hollowmere.

## Major Settlements

- **Marrowfen Stair** — lock-town corridor hub; the Ledger's inland toll-house; Wessel Crane's seat. (Settlement packet exists.)
- **Lord's Wend** — Lord Ennis Marrow's hall-town on the bluffs; the river-gate and the lord's debt (Q_VR_002).

## Minor Settlements

- **Cresswater** — barge-village on the fen margin; Mab Cress's free ferry, which the toll-lords want taxed (Q_VR_007).

## Wilderness Zones

The drowned reaches (flooded Concord works; "wrong" still water); the Marrowfen edge (sink-fen toward the Mirewend Sinks); the Wend uplands (hunting-wood, vineyards). None is deep wilderness — danger here is *water, works, and men*.

## Travel Routes

Full travel-time detail: `vael/orrun/regions/verdance-reaches` (Travel Routes section). Campaign-specific: barge passage depends on the locks behaving and the lord being paid (Lord Marrow's toll — see Regional Conflicts). Mechanical/DM route authority: map packet §Routes.

## Factions Present

**Cinder Ledger** (inland directorate — its true expansion arm; Crane); **Concord Remnant** (quiet corridor survey; Hadwin Vael); **Mourners' Circle** (river-rite keepers, alarmed by the bad water); Lord Marrow's petty river-lordship and the barge-folk's informal brotherhood (Drenn) as local powers.

## Regional Conflicts

- The **Ledger–League squeeze**: Caradril money pushing up-river into Glassmere's sphere; the corridor is the rope in the tug-of-war.
- **The lord's debt** (Q_VR_002): Marrow mortgaged the Nine Locks; who holds the paper holds the corridor.
- **The bad water** (Q_VR_001/004): failing rites and wrong-come-up drowned spreading stretch by stretch.
- **The free ferry** (Q_VR_007): the poor's crossing vs. the toll-lords.

## Regional Secrets (DM-Only)

The drowned lock-chambers' node-hum (M2 inland); Lord Marrow's hall-archive holds pre-corridor records Hadwin Vael covets (Q_VR_001 reward); the Ledger's debt-book on the corridor is a map of leverage the player can steal or turn.

## Recurring Threats

River-pirates (Eel Maddox's toll-not-murder crews); Ledger enforcers/auditors; waking node-manifestations at drowned works (Memory-Echo/drowned-dead profiles scaled L8–12). Stat references: `VERDANCE_REACHES_ENCOUNTERS.md` + map packet §Encounter And Hazard Mechanics.

## Encounter Themes

Settled-corridor threats: social/political pressure first, river hazards second, the waking dead third. See `VERDANCE_REACHES_ENCOUNTERS.md`.

## Level Range And Solo Danger

**Lvl 8–12.** Low–moderate overall; the corridor is settled. Lethal-at-level zones (telegraphed): manifesting lock-chambers (don't enter under ~L8; humming works, wrong still water, fish leaving). River-pirate ambushes are toll-by-nature and avoidable. Full solo-danger notes and DCs: map packet §Level Range / §Encounter And Hazard Mechanics.

## Encounter And Hazard Mechanics

Authoritative in the map packet: Thin-touch exposure at raw works; flood/current Athletics/Vehicles DC 15; lock-passage DC 15; investigation/social DC bands 16–17. Scaling: L8 single contained node + debt-politics → L11–12 corridor-wide barge crisis + Ledger–League proxy conflict.

## What Changes If Ignored

The Ledger closes its grip on the corridor (Monopoly clock pressure feeds inland); the bad-water stretches multiply and barge-traffic chokes; Lord Marrow's debt is called and the Nine Locks change hands; the free ferry is taxed or burned.
