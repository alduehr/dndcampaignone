## AI Use

Load for tier-3 play when the truth-race reaches the Concord's fallen capital. Narrative/political anchor (Phase 5 consolidation, 2026-07-07; **no new canon**); cartography/mechanics in the map packet. **CRITICAL secrecy guardrail (from the packet):** this is the Concord's *surface* fallen-capital country — NOT the Concord Deep, NOT the Under-Shrine/keystone, NOT the Hollow Court's seat. The Crown's deepest secret is a *record of what the Concord did*, not the living machine. Hollin Vane is a dupe — never let him confirm the Court or keystone. The endgame is vertical beneath Hollowmere, NW only.

## One-Sentence Identity

The shattered surface heart of the old Custodian Concord — an enormous contested ruin astride the Greatspine, where every faction scrambles for records and relics, and no one knows what the Concord *actually did*.

## Player-Facing Overview

The Ruin'd Crown: collapsed halls, broken roads, and the Concord Spire-stump visible for miles. Crownmouth — Warden Lyssa Crownmouth's fortified scavenger-town — is the only defended ground; the Pilgrim Camps sprawl at the southern fringe under Father Casian Ord's heretic preaching; "Old Crown" Mab guides the only reliable way into the interior; and Crown-Reclaimer Magister Hollin Vane's Remnant expedition digs, with Ledger relic-agents and everyone else's spies digging behind him.

## DM-Only Truth

The Crown is the campaign's greatest **M6 far-proof**: the Concord's grandeur, reach, and fall written in stone. Its deepest prize (Q_CH_003/004) is a *record* of the harvest — climax-scale corroboration that still cannot name the living mechanism. Vane believes the answer is here; it is not, and his ambition (Q_CH_003) is the region's engine. **Guardrail:** records and echoes only; the answer is at Hollowmere.

- **Crownmouth** — fortified scavenger-town; the only functioning settlement; Lyssa Crownmouth's gate-law.

## Minor Settlements

- **The Pilgrim Camps** — sprawling temporary shanty-camps of relic-seekers and the desperate; Ord's congregation; dangerous.

## Wilderness Zones

The Crown's deep interior (the region's true dungeon-country); the Greatspine ridge (altitude, cold, passes); the Pilgrim wastelands; "Old Crown" Mab's Waypoint as the only interior base.

## Factions Present

**Concord Remnant** (strongest here — Vane's Reclaimer expedition; the truth-race's spearhead); **Cinder Ledger** (relic-grabs); **Mourners' Circle** (a thin, appalled presence among the pilgrims); scavenger-town law (Lyssa's war-captains); Ord's heretic congregation.

## Regional Conflicts

- **The truth-race** (Q_CH_002/003): who reaches the Crown's records first, and what they do with them.
- **Crownmouth's survival** (Q_CH_001): the last safe town against the interior and the influx.
- **The Pilgrim question**: Ord's heresy feeding on real wonders and real hunger.
- **The contested pass** (Q_CH_005): the Greatspine route as a chokepoint everyone wants.

## Regional Secrets (DM-Only)

The Sealed Road-Hall (a relay-hall last seen sealed — Q_CH_003); the grave that won't keep (Q_CH_006); the Spire-stump's interior; Mab knows a route to the record-halls she has never sold.

## Recurring Threats

Concord Construct-Guardians at apex scale (Relay-Warden profile, CR ~14–17 — Stone-Golem chassis); the ruin's dead (Ward/Choir-Keeper Remembrances, banshees, Allip at the worst halls); Reclaimer field-teams and Ledger agents; the interior itself (collapse, dry cisterns, altitude). Horror additions (2026-07-07): Gallows Speaker / Dullahan — see `CONCORD_HEARTLANDS_ENCOUNTERS.md`. Stat references: encounter file + map packet.

## Quest Hooks

Six developed quests — Q_CH_001 The Last Safe Town · 002 Into the Crown · 003 The Reclaimer's Ambition · 004 The Door That Sticks · 005 The Contested Pass · 006 The Grave That Won't Keep (`../09_quests/by_region/CONCORD_HEARTLANDS_QUESTS.md`).

## Encounter Themes

Ruin-delving at continental scale; faction field-war; guardian-constructs and the unquiet dead; survival logistics (water, altitude, collapse).

## Level Range And Solo Danger

**Lvl 13–17.** High to very high — this is tier-3/4 country. Crownmouth and the Camps are safe-ish; the interior is telegraphed as lethal at every step (Mab's refusals, the war-captains' hard rules, the dead thickening). Retreat to the margin is always possible; the deep interior is set-piece country, not wandering-monster country. Details: map packet §Level Range.

## Encounter And Hazard Mechanics

Authoritative in the map packet + encounter file: construct watchwords (Concord Script DC-gated stand-downs), dry-cistern water pressure, altitude on the passes, collapse hazards, apex-scale guardian scaling.

## What Changes If Ignored

Vane's expedition reaches the record-halls and the Remnant schism sharpens continent-wide (REV-adjacent pressure, still never the apex); the Camps starve or explode; Crownmouth's law thins; the Ledger strips what the Reclaimers don't. Arc consequences: `../15_campaign_arcs/REGIONAL_ARC_PACKS_LEVEL_5_TO_20.md`, `FACTION_ESCALATION_PATHS.md`.
