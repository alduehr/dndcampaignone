## AI Use

Load when the player reaches the great river-cities up the Verdance corridor. This is the region's narrative/political anchor (Phase 5 consolidation, 2026-07-07; **no new canon**); cartography and mechanics live in the map packet, the city in `GLASSMERE_CITY_MAP.md`, people and quests in the by_region files. This is a **city-world**: danger is intrigue, finance, and espionage, not monsters.

## One-Sentence Identity

A confederacy of free river-cities grown rich on the Glasswater — the Cinder Ledger's true rival-heartland and the "real" urban Orrun, a city-world of banks, guilds, and old wealth that denies the frontier crisis by sheer prosperity.

## Player-Facing Overview

Glassmere, greatest river-city of the continent, rules a league of sister-cities (Sennfort up-river, Cairnwater down) from the Floor — the famous merchant-exchange — and the Three Bridges banking quarter. League-Factor Oren Glass speaks for the league's trade; Banker-Magistra Sefwy Holt's house is the Ledger's true continental rival; the Reliquary, the Concord Remnant's grand chapter-house, holds the greatest relic-collection anywhere; and everything anyone says is for sale to the Spy-Broker they call the Glass Ear. In Lowwater, the poor district, Mother Wenna Lowwater keeps the old grief-rites — and lately the graves there won't stay quiet either.

## DM-Only Truth

The League is where the campaign's **economic war** is decided: Holt's bank vs. the Ledger (Q_GL_002/008) is the continental version of the Vyre monopoly scheme. The Reliquary's collection contains genuine Concord material the Reclaimers covet (Q_GL_004; Mareth Senn's chapter is contested ground between honest scholarship and Ferrant's institution). The "humming cabinet" (Q_GL_007) and Lowwater's drifting graves (Q_GL_006) are the crisis arriving in the city that refuses to see it. **Guardrail:** urban relic/vault sites only; never the keystone/Deep/Court.

## Wilderness Zones

Effectively none — canal-country and settled terraces. The "wilds" here are institutional: vaults, archives, and the river itself.

## Factions Present

**Cinder Ledger** (expanding in; Heartland-Provost Iss Vyre's bank-guards); **Holt's merchant-bank** (the league's native counterweight); **Concord Remnant** (the Reliquary — its grandest chapter); **Mourners' Circle** (urban, Lowwater; Mother Wenna); **the Glass Ear's web** (information-market analogous to Caradril's Hush); league city-governments (Oren Glass's trade-diplomacy).

## Regional Conflicts

- **The bank war** (Q_GL_002/008): Holt vs. the Ledger for the continent's credit — the player's biggest economic lever anywhere.
- **The Reliquary's judgement** (Q_GL_004): what the Remnant's greatest chapter does with what it holds.
- **The Floor and the forgotten** (Q_GL_005/006): prosperity's blind spot — Lowwater's dead and the league's conscience.
- **The Ear** (Q_GL_003): everyone's secrets, one broker.

## Regional Secrets (DM-Only)

The Counting-Deep (D29) beneath the Three Bridges holds instruments of the old Concord economy; the Reliquary Vaults (D28) hold cataloged Remembrance material no one has correctly identified; the Glass Ear knows a NW-frontier rumor is worth more than its buyers realize (gated M-fringe).

## Recurring Threats

Bank-guards and agents (Guard/Spy-like; Veteran-like captains), hired Lowwater toughs, a minor waking-relic manifestation (Memory-Echo scaled L10–13). Institutional pressure — credit frozen, agents tailing, wards raised — is the region's signature "encounter." Stat references: `GLASSMERE_LEAGUE_ENCOUNTERS.md` + map packet.

## Quest Hooks

Eight developed quests — Q_GL_001 The League's Overture · 002 The Bank and the Ledger · 003 The Face of the Ear · 004 The Reliquary's Judgement · 005 The Floor and the Forgotten · 006 The Drifting Graves of Lowwater · 007 The Humming Cabinet · 008 Good Paper, Bad Debt (`../09_quests/by_region/GLASSMERE_LEAGUE_QUESTS.md`).

## Encounter Themes

Intrigue, finance, espionage, vault-security; a single telegraphed relic-manifestation. Most quests have no required fight.

## Level Range And Solo Danger

**Lvl 9–13.** Low physical danger; the risks are political and financial, and a blown infiltration draws Guard/Spy-like security, not lethal ambush. Escalation is signaled institutionally, giving room to retreat or change tack. Details: map packet §Level Range / §Encounter And Hazard Mechanics (Reliquary wards DC 18; vault Stealth DC 16–18; social DC 15–18).

## Encounter And Hazard Mechanics

Authoritative in the map packet. Scaling: L9 pure social intrigue → L13 the bank-war and Remnant schism become continental levers.

## What Changes If Ignored

The Ledger–League bank war escalates and continental credit becomes a weapon (feeds the Monopoly clock); the Reliquary tilts toward the Reclaimers; Lowwater's dead worsen unmourned; the Ear sells what the player left lying around.
