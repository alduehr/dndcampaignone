## AI Use

Load when the player crosses the mid-continent chalk-downs (the gentlest far region and the natural first far-region step at Lvl 6+, and the route south). Narrative/political anchor (Phase 5 consolidation, 2026-07-07; **no new canon**); cartography/mechanics in the map packet; people/quests in the by_region files.

## One-Sentence Identity

Settled chalk-down sheep-country and ancient barrow-fields — a gentle, orderly, custom-bound pastoral mid-continent of manorial holds and shire-moots, where the barrow-dead are growing restless.

## Player-Facing Overview

Green sheep-walks, hedged manor-fields, dry-stone folds, and shire-law kept at Marrowmoot's moot-stone. Shire-Reeve Aldous Penmark holds the gentry's order from Penmark Hold; Horse-Reeve Edony Marrow's mounted troop patrols the down-roads; wool-wealth flows through Wether toward the Ledger's buyers. And on the eastern downs stand the Barrow-Fields — older than any law here — which every shepherd knows "must lie quiet," and lately do not.

## DM-Only Truth

The barrows are **pre-Concord** — an older-fall echo (M6-fringe by contrast, never the keystone). Their stirring corroborates the continental pattern without touching the mechanism. The Mardenflow rises among the barrows and carries the region's unease literally downstream (to the Sallowmarch). Moot-Mother Senna Crale's old grave-songs are honest M5 folk-memory. **Guardrail:** older, separate, echo only.

## Major Settlements

- **Marrowmoot** — shire-town hub; the moot-stone; Senna Crale's seat.
- **Penmark Hold** — the shire-reeve's manor-town; gentry law.

## Minor Settlements

- **Wether** — wool-trade village; Barrow-Master Doss Wether guides (and robs) the mounds.

## Wilderness Zones

The Barrow-Fields (sacred old ground, now stirring — the region's one true danger zone); the open downs (safe, exposed to weather); the Mardenflow head-valley.

## Travel Routes

Full travel-time detail: `vael/orrun/regions/marrowdowns` (Travel Routes section). Campaign-specific: the Road South's wool-drive traffic is Q_MD_005. Mechanical/DM route authority: map packet §Routes.

## Factions Present

**Mourners' Circle** (strong — downs grief-keepers, the old grave-songs); manorial holds / shire-moots (the regional minor power: levies, tolls, feuds); **Cinder Ledger** (wool-buyers tightening credit — Q_MD_003); **Concord Remnant** (barrow-surveying, politely unwelcome).

## Regional Conflicts

- **The restless barrows** (Q_MD_001/002): custom says let them lie; something isn't.
- **The wool and the Ledger** (Q_MD_003): credit-vise closing on the fleece-economy.
- **The old songs** (Q_MD_004): what the grave-songs actually preserve, and who wants them.
- **The road south** (Q_MD_005): keeping the drive-route open through banditry and rustling.

## Regional Secrets (DM-Only)

The deep barrow ("the Barrow That Bites," Q_MD_002) predates everything and its dread/air clock forbids resting inside; Doss Wether's barrow-craft maps more mounds than he admits; C_MD_001 (pre-Concord echo clue) lives here.

## Recurring Threats

Waking barrow-dead (Skeleton/Zombie/Wight/Specter-like scaled L6–10; slow, telegraphed, thinnable by barrow-custom rite); barrow-robbers and down-road rustlers (Bandit/Scout-like); the horse-reeve's troop as **allies** (Guard/Veteran-like). Since 2026-07-07 the horror layer may also field a Gallows Speaker / Carrion Stalker / Dullahan (VRGtR/RtHW; see `MARROWDOWNS_ENCOUNTERS.md` horror additions). Stat references: encounter file + map packet.

## Quest Hooks

Five developed quests — Q_MD_001 The Barrows Gone Restless · 002 The Barrow That Bites · 003 The Wool and the Ledger · 004 The Old Songs of the Downs · 005 The Road South (`../09_quests/by_region/MARROWDOWNS_QUESTS.md`).

## Encounter Themes

Pastoral-social rows by default; barrow-undead at the fields; road-banditry on the drives. The *downs* are safe; the *barrows* are the danger — bound and avoidable.

## Level Range And Solo Danger

**Lvl 6–10.** Low–moderate; the gentlest far region. Lethal-at-level (telegraphed): the deep barrows — animal-omens, folk-song warnings, and Senna Crale's counsel precede any barrow fight; the open downs always allow retreat. Details: map packet §Level Range.

## Encounter And Hazard Mechanics

Authoritative in the map packet: barrow-traps (Thieves' tools/Investigation DC 16); deep-barrow dread/air clock (no safe rest inside); "wrong" pre-Concord relic Thin-touch risk; Survival DC 15 down-roads; Religion DC 15 barrow-custom. Scaling: L6 one stirring barrow → L10 a waking barrow-field + organized robber-ring.

## What Changes If Ignored

The barrow-field wakes wider and the shire's custom-order buckles into fear; the Ledger's wool-vise closes (holds mortgaged, moots bought); the road south grows dangerous enough to choke the wool-drives and the player's own route onward. Arc consequences: `../15_campaign_arcs/REGIONAL_ARC_PACKS_LEVEL_5_TO_20.md`, `CONTINENTAL_PRESSURE_TIMELINE.md`.
