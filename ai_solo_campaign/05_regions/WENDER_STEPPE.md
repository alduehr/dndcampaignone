## AI Use

Load when the player rides the cold northern grass-sea. Narrative/political anchor (Phase 5 consolidation, 2026-07-07; **no new canon**); cartography/mechanics in the map packet (which documents the authored level decision: **8–13**). **Secrecy note (from the packet):** the Sky-Stones are an independent steppe holy site, **NOT Concord** — the seers' "thinning sky" is an extremely oblique cosmic far-echo, never a node or the machine.

## One-Sentence Identity

A cold open grass-sea ridden by free horse-clans the Concord never tamed — a people outside the Concord's whole history, to whom the death-rite crisis is a "settled-folk's sickness" and a "thinning of the southern Sky" their seers read with dread but no understanding.

## Player-Facing Overview

No roads, no crowns: the clans migrate with the grass, winter at Cold Springs under Clan-Speaker Tamur Wend-Khar's guest-law, and trade with the settled world only at the Spine-Foot meet. Kin-Mother Tamur-Sai keeps the clans' kin-law; Wind-Singer Esha keeps vigil at the Sky-Stones, the steppe's own ancient shrine-circle; and in the far grass, Raid-Lord Borr of the Black Horse takes what the guest-law would have given.

## DM-Only Truth

The steppe is the campaign's **outside view**: a whole people with working death-ways of their own (sky-rites, not Concord rites) whose seers nonetheless perceive the southern "thinning" (REV_009-adjacent oblique echo). The Sky-Stones hum by their own old nature — an independent pre-Concord holy site, positively **not** part of the network. **Guardrail:** the steppe never confirms, locates, or explains anything southern; its dread is real and uncomprehending.

## Geography

The long grass (trackless open steppe); the Cold Springs winter-basin; the Sky-Stones rise (NW upland shrine-circle); the Spine-Foot margin where grass meets the Greatspine's foot (S); the far steppe (Black Horse country). No major river; seasonal melt and dowsed water-holes. Full geometry: map packet.

## Major Settlements

- **The Winter-Camp at Cold Springs** — the great cold-season gathering; the closest thing to a capital, three months a year.

## Minor Settlements

- **The Spine-Foot trade-meet** — the neutral ground where two worlds trade (Q_WS_005). The **Summer-Riding** is the clan-host itself, on the move — a "settlement" with hooves.

## Wilderness Zones

Everything. The long grass without a clan-guide is the region's chief hazard; the far steppe is raider-country; the Sky-Stones rise is holy ground (seer-permission expected).

## Travel Routes

Steppe tracks (migration and trade lines, seasonal, ungoverned); the Summer-Riding's circuit; the Spine-Foot trade-road S to the settled lands. The Highmark Passes lie W; the Karran Marches E. Map packet §Routes.

## Factions Present

The **clan confederacy** (Tamur Wend-Khar's Speaker-ship; guest-law, kin-law); the **seers** (Esha's vigil); **Borr's Black Horse** (the raiding counter-power); at the Spine-Foot only: settled-world traders (Ledger money among them, on sufferance). No southern faction holds ground here.

## Regional Conflicts

- **The guest-law** (Q_WS_001): an outsider living (or dying) by the steppe's one absolute rule.
- **The knotted kin** (Q_WS_002): a kin-law crisis binding two clans.
- **The thinning sky** (Q_WS_003): the seers' dread and what it costs them.
- **The Black Horse** (Q_WS_004): Borr's raids against the confederacy's restraint.
- **The edge of two worlds** (Q_WS_005): the trade-meet's fragile truce.

## Regional Secrets (DM-Only)

The Sky-Stones' hum has *changed pitch* within Esha's lifetime (oblique, cosmic, unexplained — REV_009 echo); the Black Horse rides harder because the far grass is emptier than it was; the trade-meet has a settled-world spy problem no clan cares to solve.

## Recurring Threats

The steppe itself (cold, distance, water); raiders (Borr's riders — Berserker/Scout-like, mounted); steppe predators (wolves, giant eagles — Track A); sky-omens and seer-tests (social/spiritual, not combat). Stat references: `WENDER_STEPPE_ENCOUNTERS.md` + map packet.

## Quest Hooks

Five developed quests — Q_WS_001 The Guest-Law · 002 The Knotted Kin · 003 The Thinning Sky · 004 The Black Horse · 005 The Edge of Two Worlds (`../09_quests/by_region/WENDER_STEPPE_QUESTS.md`).

## Encounter Themes

Survival and hospitality; mounted raiding; clan-law social play; the numinous at the Stones. Almost nothing here is a dungeon.

## Level Range And Solo Danger

**Lvl 8–13.** Low under guest-law; **high** without it (the unguided grass, winter, the far steppe). The steppe telegraphs by custom: break no law, refuse no host, and the land's dangers announce themselves by horizon. Details: map packet §Level Range.

## Encounter And Hazard Mechanics

Authoritative in the map packet + encounter file: exposure/wind Con checks (DC 12–15) on hard-weather days; unguided navigation; water-hole dowsing; mounted-combat notes; guest-law social DCs.

## What Changes If Ignored

Borr's raids grow until the confederacy must answer in blood; the trade-meet truce breaks and the steppe closes to the settled world; the seers' dread curdles into policy no southerner will like. Arc consequences: `../15_campaign_arcs/REGIONAL_ARC_PACKS_LEVEL_5_TO_20.md`, `CONTINENTAL_PRESSURE_TIMELINE.md`.
