## AI Use

Load when the player approaches the closed volcanic fire-theocracy (SE-central Orrun). Narrative/political anchor (Phase 5 consolidation, 2026-07-07; **no new canon**); cartography/mechanics in the map packet; Ashfast's interior in `ASHFAST_CITY_MAP.md`. **Secrecy note (from the packet):** the fire-faith's harvest of the dead — pyres, ash-absorption — is a **thematic mirror** of the main crisis, never the same machine; the closed theocracy makes the DM-only layer especially important.

## One-Sentence Identity

A closed volcanic fire-theocracy built on a pre-Concord faith that harvests the dead through fire — alien, self-sufficient, and sitting on the oldest pre-Concord ruins the campaign contains.

## Player-Facing Overview

Above the Glass Coast the Emberfells smoke, and on the terrace below the caldera stands Ashfast, fortress-temple-city of the fire-faith, ruled by Ash-Hierophant Vole Cindra and policed by Pyre-Warden Sef Embren's soldiers. Outsiders enter only by the Ash Roads pilgrim-way, warden-post by warden-post, to the Pilgrim Gate. Cinderhold mines obsidian and ore under Cinder-Master Doss Ashfast; the Cindern Waste — the ash desert downwind — is warden-forbidden; and in Ashfast's shadow, Ember-Sage Mira Cindra asks the heretic's question: *what did the fire-faith build on?*

## DM-Only Truth

The theocracy is the campaign's great **thematic mirror**: a rite-system that consumes its dead *openly and devoutly* — showing the player what the Concord's sin looks like when a culture calls it holy. Beneath it lie the oldest pre-Concord ruins in the campaign (the Caldera Descent D22; the Cindern Waste Buried Works D36; C_FC_016). Mira Cindra's heresy circles the truth of the faith's foundations, not the NW mechanism. **Guardrail:** mirror and echo only — never the harvest itself; no one here knows the apex.

## Geography

The volcanic caldera highland (active; lava-vent country); the Ashfast terrace; the Cindern Waste ash-badland (SE, rain-shadow); the Ash Roads pilgrim-highland (W approach); the Glass Coast obsidian strip (S). Hot springs sacred to the pyre-faith; the pilgrim-road's cold springs are the only reliable water on approach. Full geometry: map packet.

## Major Settlements

- **Ashfast** — fortress-temple-city; the theocracy's capital and the campaign's most alien polity; city packet `ASHFAST_CITY_MAP.md`.

## Minor Settlements

- **Cinderhold** — volcanic mining town on the Cinder Road.
- **The Ash Roads** — the warden-post string of the pilgrim approach (route, not a town).

## Wilderness Zones

The caldera rim (patrolled; safe-conduct required; one wrong step); the Cindern Waste (forbidden; lethal without water and protection; D36 lies buried there); the Glass Coast margin (obsidian beaches, hot pools).

## Travel Routes

The **Ash Roads** (W; the only sanctioned approach — Q_ET_001); the Cinder Road E to Cinderhold (2–3 days); the Glass Coast Road S toward the sea; the forbidden Cindern Waste Track. Map packet §Routes.

## Factions Present

The **fire-theocracy** itself (hierophant, pyre-wardens, ash-priests — the total power); **Mira Cindra's heretics** (quiet, curious, endangered); **Cinderhold's mining interest** (the closest thing to a secular power); **Concord Remnant** (the Reliquary's reach — Q_ET_007 — politely refused, persistently returning); **Mourners' Circle** (a horrified rumor of the pyre-rites, no presence).

## Regional Conflicts

- **The heretic's question** (Q_ET_002): Mira's inquiry vs. the faith's foundations.
- **The forbidden salvage** (Q_ET_003) and **the market beneath the ash** (Q_ET_008): what comes out of ground no one may enter.
- **The waste that wakes** (Q_ET_005): the ash-dead stirring in the Cindern.
- **The mountain's wrath** (Q_ET_006): eruption-omens as theology and politics.
- **The Reliquary's reach** (Q_ET_007): the Remnant probing a closed door.

## Regional Secrets (DM-Only)

The faith's oldest crypts predate the faith; the Buried Works (D36) are pre-Concord works the ash entombed; the hierophancy knows its own succession-rite involves the pyres' "keeping" and does not ask further.

## Recurring Threats

Pyre-soldiers (Guard/Veteran-like; doctrinaire, not cruel); Ash-Bound Dead (custom, CR 4–7 — Wight chassis + heat aura); Ash-Fiends/Cinder-Wretches at heresy-sites (Barbed devil/Salamander chassis); fire elementals and magma mephits; the Caldera Guardian (D22 apex; Salamander→Efreeti chassis, CR 11–15); the environment (ash-fall, heat, the caldera). Stat references: `EMBERFELL_ENCOUNTERS.md` + map packet.

## Quest Hooks

Eight developed quests — Q_ET_001 The Ash Roads Passage · 002 The Heretic's Question · 003 The Forbidden Salvage · 004 The Ash-Touched · 005 The Waste That Wakes · 006 The Mountain's Wrath · 007 The Reliquary's Reach · 008 The Market Beneath the Ash (`../09_quests/by_region/EMBERFELL_THEOCRACY_QUESTS.md`).

## Encounter Themes

Theocratic law and closed-city intrigue; volcanic environment; the fire-preserved dead; gated pre-Concord depths.

## Level Range And Solo Danger

**Lvl 12–16.** Moderate inside the law (the theocracy is orderly to those who keep its forms); **very high** environmentally — the caldera, the Waste, and D22/D36 are among the campaign's harshest gear-and-water-gated environments, all warden-telegraphed. Crossing the faith is signaled long before it is punished. Details: map packet §Level Range.

## Encounter And Hazard Mechanics

Authoritative in the map packet + encounter file: ash-fall Con DC 12–14 without cover; caldera and Waste heat/water rules; fire-ward readings (Arcana/Concord Script DC 15); theocratic law DCs at the gates.

## What Changes If Ignored

The heresy is purged and the question dies for a generation; the Waste's dead spread toward Cinderhold; an eruption-omen becomes policy; the Remnant finds another way in. Arc consequences: `../15_campaign_arcs/REGIONAL_ARC_PACKS_LEVEL_5_TO_20.md`, `CONTINENTAL_PRESSURE_TIMELINE.md`.
