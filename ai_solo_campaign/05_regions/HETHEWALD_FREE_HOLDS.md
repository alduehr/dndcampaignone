## AI Use

Load when the player enters the great eastern forest. Narrative/political anchor (Phase 5 consolidation, 2026-07-07; **no new canon**); cartography/mechanics in the map packet. Design note: this is **Tollwood writ large** — the player's Ring-1 forest lessons scale up here.

## One-Sentence Identity

A great dark forest of free-holds, river-tollers, and outlaws, where old pagan bargains with the wood are failing and deep Concord ruins fester in the forbidden interior.

## Player-Facing Overview

The Hethewood is the continent's largest forest, held by a confederacy of free-holds that answers to no crown — only to the moot at Hethemoot, where Hold-Speaker Bram Hethe keeps the peace between proud holds. Forest-Mother Onn Greenward keeps the old faith at the sacred grove of Greenward, where the wood's bargains are made — and lately fail. On the Hethe river, "Greenfinger" Maddoc's outlaw tollers choke the water-road from Tollreach. And in the deep interior stand the Old Holds, Concord ruins the free-holds have forbidden for a generation, because the dead there do not stay down.

## DM-Only Truth

The "failing bargains" (Q_HFH_002) are the crisis read through a pagan frame — the forest-faith's rites weakening on the same gradient as everyone else's (M5 far-echo; the wood's own presences are landmark-powers, **not** the Court or new gods). The Old Holds (D25) are surface-Concord ruins whose stirring feeds M6 obliquely. The Ledger's logging push (Q_HFH_004) would cut into ground the bargains protect. **Guardrail:** never name the harvest; the wood's trouble stays folk-framed.

## Geography

Old-growth forest heart, barely mapped inside; the Hethemoot clearing (the confederacy's one open ground); the sacred Greenward grove (SW); Tollreach's river-cut on the Hethe (NE); the forbidden Old Holds cluster (deep SE); thinning western edge toward road-country. The **Hethe** flows S through it all. Full geometry: map packet.

## Major Settlements

- **Hethemoot** — the great gathering free-hold; the moot's neutral ground; Bram Hethe's seat.
- **Greenward** — the sacred grove-village; Onn Greenward's seat; wood's-law welcome required.

## Minor Settlements

- **Tollreach** — Maddoc's outlaw river-toll camp (a route-waypoint danger marker, not a service stop).

## Wilderness Zones

The Hethewood heart (guided travel only); the Old Holds (forbidden; D25); the forest-tarns (navigation marks; the dead stir near several); the western thinning edge.

## Travel Routes

The Hold-Road in from the west (2–4 days to Hethemoot; toller-contested at the edge); the River-Road down the Hethe (fast N–S; Maddoc's toll blocks it — toll, fight, or a deep-wood bypass ford, Survival DC 16); the Greenward Path (guided, welcome-gated); the forbidden Old Holds track. Map packet §Routes.

## Factions Present

The **free-holds confederacy** (the regional power; proud, feuding, moot-bound); the **forest-faith** (Onn's grove-keepers; the failing bargains); **Maddoc's tollers** (outlaw power on the river); **Cinder Ledger** (the logging push); **Concord Remnant** (quiet interest in the Old Holds); **Mourners' Circle** (kin-craft with the forest-mothers).

## Regional Conflicts

- **The wood's law** (Q_HFH_001): an outsider earning (or breaking) the forest's terms.
- **The failing bargains** (Q_HFH_002): the old faith's rites no longer holding; Greenward's crisis.
- **The toll of the Hethe** (Q_HFH_003): Maddoc's chokehold on the river-road.
- **The logger's push** (Q_HFH_004) and **the feud of the holds** (Q_HFH_005): money and pride splitting the moot.
- **The Free Holds vote** (Q_HFH_007): the confederacy deciding what it is.

## Regional Secrets (DM-Only)

The Old Holds' interior (D25) holds a Concord record-fragment (M6 far-echo); the deepest bargain-places map onto pre-Concord sites the faith no longer remembers as such; Maddoc has been paid — once, anonymously — to keep a particular stretch of river empty.

## Recurring Threats

Forest predators and fey (dryads, hags, blights — Track A); outlaw tollers (Bandit/Scout-like; Maddoc Veteran-like); the Old Holds' dead (Barrow-Wight/Specter chassis scaled L7–12; gated); the wood itself (getting lost, the deep interior's hostility without a guide). Horror addition (2026-07-07): a Bodytaker Plant (RtHW) in the deepest wood — see `HETHEWALD_ENCOUNTERS.md`. Stat references: encounter file + map packet.

## Quest Hooks

Seven developed quests — Q_HFH_001 The Wood's Law · 002 The Failing Bargains · 003 The Toll of the Hethe · 004 The Logger's Push · 005 The Feud of the Holds · 006 The Old Holds · 007 The Free Holds Vote (`../09_quests/by_region/HETHEWALD_FREE_HOLDS_QUESTS.md`).

## Encounter Themes

Forest-custom and hold-politics ashore; river-toll conflict on the Hethe; fey-bargain strangeness in the groves; gated Concord-dead at the Old Holds.

## Level Range And Solo Danger

**Lvl 7–12.** Moderate under the wood's law; high off it (the deep wood without a guide, the Old Holds at any time). All lethal zones are telegraphed by the free-holds' own prohibitions — the forest's rules, honestly kept, are the solo-safety system. Details: map packet §Level Range.

## Encounter And Hazard Mechanics

Authoritative in the map packet + encounter file: deep-wood navigation and welcome-gates; the Tollreach bypass ford (Survival DC 16); Old Holds Thin-touch and gated fragments; fey-bargain social DCs.

## What Changes If Ignored

The bargains fail wider and the wood's presences wake hungrier; Maddoc's toll strangles the river-trade and the moot splits over the answer; the Ledger's saws bite into protected ground; the Old Holds' dead begin to walk out.
