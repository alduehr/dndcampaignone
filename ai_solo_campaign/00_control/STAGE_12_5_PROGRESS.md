## Purpose

Working-state record for Stage 12.5 (Continental Adventure-Site Coverage) — an interstitial pass between Stage 12 and Stage 13. Records what was built and the guardrails applied. **Stage 12.5 is complete.**

## Stage Goal

Improve dungeon/adventure-site spread so **every map-authoritative far-continent region has at least one authored explorable site**, with the likely mid/late-game regions having multiples. Build the 4 deferred optional far sites first, then 1–2 per undercovered region. Target ~15–20 new sites.

## Secrecy Discipline Applied (non-negotiable)

- **No apex truth in any player-safe section.** Every far site is `secrecy: mixed` with DM-Only caps; the harvest mechanism, the keystone, the Concord Deep, and the Hollow Court are NEVER named as present, confirmed, or relocated. Each file carries an explicit hard-cap DM gate ("the X bottoms out in rubble/ash/silt/ice — NOT a passage to the keystone").
- `THE_UNDER_SHRINE_APPROACH.md` (D23) remains the ONLY file describing the apex; it was not touched.
- Far pre-Concord echoes (Saltmere/Marrowdowns/Sallowmarch) are framed as *older, separate* falls — resonance, never the campaign secret.
- Wender Sky-Stones is explicitly NON-Concord (the outside view); its REV_009 echo never names the Quiet Country/harvest.
- Sunmark grove is a living faith *informing* the endgame synthesis option, never the apex lever or a Concord site.

## Guardrails Honored

- No new mystery, faction, god, cosmology, or artifact.
- No new NPC or adversary proper nouns (existing Stage 9.5 far rosters + Stage 12 adversary profiles reused/scaled).
- Site names reuse already-registered far anchors/map-features (one new label: the Hollow Gulf Wreck-Reef).
- No four-PC assumptions; every site solo-tuned, telegraphed, retreat-rich, with noncombat paths and safety valves.
- Three-clue rule preserved: all far clues are *additional* independent sources; none is required; a player who never leaves the NW reaches every revelation.

## Remaining Gaps (handed to later stages)

- Far regions remain deep-build placeholders for region/settlement gazetteers and NPC density (Stage 15 / Ring 2+ as play warrants) — *site coverage is now complete.*
- Overseas Vael landmasses (Surren, Iron Skards, Sundered Isles) remain 1-line placeholders (far-future).
- Stage 13 (Encounter and Bestiary Expansion) is next: fold `STAGE_12_ADVERSARIES.md` (now serving all 36 sites) into the full bestiary.
