## AI Use

Load to date events, describe seasons, or give any scene set in Orrun a sense of time passing. Usable for any campaign, one-shot, or NPC set in this world — not specific to any one storyline.

## Summary

Most of Orrun counts years from a single historical turning point, the **Quietfall**, roughly a century ago. The calendar itself — months, week, and holidays — is ordinary and stable; it is the *reckoning* (what year it is and why) that carries the world's weight.

## Calendar System

- **Reckoning:** Years count forward from the Quietfall: **After Quietfall (AQ)**. Years before it are counted backward as **Concord Reckoning (CR)**.
- **Year length:** 360 days, 12 months of 30 days, four seasons of three months each.
- **Week:** 6 days — Sunsday, Loomsday, Hearthsday, Marketday, Wardsday, Restday.

|---|---|---|
| Greyfall 1 | The Door's Opening | The death-rite season begins; towns hold collective funerals for the year's dead. |

| Lastlight 30 | The Long Vigil | Year-end vigil for the departed. |

## Using This Elsewhere

This calendar works for any story set in Orrun, not only a specific frontier campaign. The "current year" of any given game is a matter for that game to set — pick an AQ year that suits the tone you want (early AQ for a raw, resettling frontier; later AQ for a more recovered world) and use the months and holidays as-is.
