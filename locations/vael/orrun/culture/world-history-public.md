## AI Use

Load when a character researches history, consults a sage or archive, visits old ruins, or asks what happened roughly a century ago. This is the public record and common knowledge — the story anyone in Orrun could tell you, whether or not it's the whole truth. Usable for any story set in this world.

## Summary

Orrun's recorded history is split by the **Quietfall** (roughly a century ago). Before it: the age of the **Custodian Concord**, a continent-spanning order that tended great shrines and was said to keep the dead at peace. After it: the frontier age, a patchwork of independent regions and towns living among Concord ruins. Publicly, the Quietfall is remembered as a story of glory followed by sudden, inexplicable catastrophe — what really caused it has never been officially explained, and remains one of the great open mysteries of the age.

## Calendar Note

Dates use **After Quietfall (AQ)**; the Quietfall itself is AQ 0. Pre-Quietfall dates use **Concord Reckoning (CR)**; the Quietfall falls at roughly CR 900 / AQ 0. See `CALENDAR.md`.

## Public Timeline

| Era | Date | Event |
|---|---|---|
| Concord Age | CR 1 (legendary) | Founding of the Custodian Concord, an order said to tend the Last Door and keep the dead at peace. |
| Concord Age | CR 200–800 | A golden age. The Concord builds great shrines, roads, and archives across Orrun. Long lives, stable harvests, little plague. |
| Concord Age | CR ~880 | A faint later memory of "thin years" — poor crops, strange births. Mostly forgotten by the present day. |
| The Quietfall | AQ 0 | In a single season the Concord collapses. Shrines fall silent. Fires, floods, and the deaths of nearly all Custodians. The cause has never been officially explained. |
| Frontier Age | AQ 0–40 | Resettlement. Independent towns and councils form across the ruins. |
| Frontier Age | AQ ~60 | Death-rites begin failing more often in various places. The first widely reported "Remembrances." |
| Frontier Age | AQ 80–100 | Failures spread further. New folk faiths and orders rise up to deal with the restless dead; scattered scholarly groups form hoping to rebuild what the Concord once knew. |

Storytellers using this world for their own campaign can set their "present day" anywhere from the early frontier age onward — the later the date, the more settled (but also the more strained) the world has become.

## Old Concord Ruins (What Remains)

- **Shrine-stones** — standing markers scattered across old Concord territory, now cold and silent.
- **Drowned and buried shrines** — the great central shrines of the Concord's network; some flooded, some collapsed, some simply abandoned when the Quietfall came. Frontier towns are sometimes built on their shores or over their foundations. Publicly, these are known as curiosities and local hazards — folk say something vast rests beneath the flood or the ruin, glimpsed on quiet days, but no one agrees on what it once did.
- **Old Concord roads** — well-built, overgrown, and still the safest routes through wild country.
- **Archive cairns** — sealed stone vaults of Concord records. Most have been looted or found empty; a few remain intact, and dangerous. A natural source of historical clues for any story that wants one.
