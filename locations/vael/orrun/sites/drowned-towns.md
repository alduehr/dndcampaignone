# The Drowned Towns

**Type** submerged ruin-dive · **Region** the receding Saltmere, out from Brackhold · **Level** 11–15 · **Danger** high (cold brine, salt-collapse, blackout)

**What It Is.** The inland salt sea is shrinking, and as it goes down it is uncovering towns. Whole streets, salt-crusted and brine-preserved, with the people still in them — cured hard by the salt in the postures they drowned in. The salt-clans hold the drowned towns sacred and forbid the dive. Scavengers do it anyway. Lately the drowned dead have been stirring, which everyone has an opinion about and nobody can explain.

**Approach and Entrance.** With a diver who knows the safe streets (fast, and it makes clan enemies). By the clans' leave, earned by recovering a lost kinsman or settling the stirring dead (slow, socially safe). At the worst-receded season, which bares the most ruin and rouses the most dead. Or across the salt flats alone, which the mirage-country punishes.

**Layout.**
1. **The Lake-Edge and Bared Streets** — the receded shore where the streets come out of the crust. Scavenger camps, clan-watchers.
2. **The Brine-Drowned Concourse** — the main street, half-flooded, salt-stiffened doors and stalls. Household dead posed where they stopped.
3. **The Salt-Choir** — a drowned temple-square where the town's dead have gathered, facing inward. The eerie heart of the dive.
4. **The Clan-Vaults** — the old salt-merchants' sunken strongrooms.
5. **The Deep Drowned Quarter** — the never-receded streets, in pitch brine. Best salvage, worst dead, **bottoming out in silt and salt-rock.**

**Hazards.** Salt-collapse footing (**DC 14 Dex/Athletics**, 3d6 and a fall into brine); cold-brine immersion (**DC 14–15 Con**, exhaustion or 2d6 cold); silt blackout (Perception at disadvantage — run a guide-line); flooded vault locks (**DC 16 Thieves' Tools**); mirage-country on the flats (**DC 15 Survival**).

**Inhabitants.** Salt-cured drowned dead — **Wraith-like**, AC 13, ~40–60 HP, bound to the drowned streets, rite- or parley-able, and at the Salt-Choir best passed by reverence (**DC 15 Religion or Insight**) rather than fought. One vault-bound guardian dead (AC 13, ~64–72 HP). Living: rival scavengers, clan-watchers, and relic buyers on the shore.

**Salvage.** Salt-cured old glass and brine-preserved valuables; lost clan heirlooms, which are the socially valuable haul; a possible salt-warded minor item.

**Getting Out.** Brackhold and the lake-edge camps are havens. **The dive self-limits** — cold and air force you up, which is the built-in valve. Surfacing escapes everything down there.
