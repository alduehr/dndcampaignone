# The Wreck-Reef

**Type** shipwreck graveyard and drowned harbour-quarter · **Region** off Calderport, the Hollow Gulf · **Level** 10–15 · **Danger** high (the sea)

**What It Is.** The warmest, most cheerful dangerous place on the continent. A reef off the greatest southern port where a century of ships have died, tangled hull against hull, and beneath it a whole harbour-district the rising sea took generations ago — streets and warehouses, now dived professionally by crews out of Calderport who treat the whole thing as a trade rather than a haunting.

There is nothing supernatural here at all. It is a salvage adventure, and it belongs in this file precisely because most of the continent is like this: busy, sunlit, and completely unbothered.

**Approach and Entrance.** Hire a salvage crew. Take passage with a licensed privateer. Buy a sanctioned charter from the port-mistress (slower, taxed, safer, and it settles claim disputes before they start). Or hire a boat and go, which the currents punish.

**Layout.**
1. **The Wreck-Reef** — the graveyard proper. Tangled hulls, salvage-buoys, rival crews, and claim politics.
2. **The Tangled Hulls** — the diveable wrecks: cargo holds, drowned cabins, foreign goods.
3. **The Drowned Harbour-Quarter** — the sunken older district. Streets and warehouses, silt-choked, and something big living in one of them.
4. **The Deep Wrecks** — the richest and most dangerous, in the worst current.
5. **The Foreign Hold** — an overseas ship's hold: goods, curios, and charts of the world beyond Orrun.

**Hazards.** Currents and deep water (**DC 14–15 Athletics**); wreck-tangle (**DC 14 Athletics/Acrobatics** — being trapped underwater is the real killer here); air and cold on long dives (**DC 13 Con**); and salvage-claim disputes, which are a social hazard with real teeth in a port town.

**Inhabitants.** A reef predator — a great eel or a large shark, **Giant Shark-like**, AC 13–15, ~70–120 HP, sea-bound and thoroughly avoidable with a lookout. Rival salvage crews and privateers (**Bandit/Veteran-like**), who fight over claims and flee at half HP. **No undead. No guardians. Nothing old.**

**Salvage.** Rich foreign cargo and overseas curios; a saltwater-warded minor item; and charts and lore of the lands across the water, which is the real prize for anyone whose horizon has been the continent.

**Getting Out.** Surface and sail. The dive self-limits on air and cold. The ports are all around and none of them care what you did out there as long as your charter was in order.
