# The Counting-House Vault (Hollowmere)

**Type** urban strongroom · **Region** Hollowmere, on the Rim · **Level** 2–4 · **Danger** low combat, high consequence

**What It Is.** The Cinder Ledger's Hollowmere counting-house: a solid stone building with a public counting-room at the front, a records office behind it, and a guarded corridor to a proper vault at the back. The house keeps coin, salvage stock, and — everyone in town half-knows this — one or two relics it has chosen not to put on the market.

Included here because it is the best example in the Reach of a "dungeon" made entirely of locks, patrols, and people. **Combat is the failure state.**

**Approach and Entrance.** The front door is open by day. That is the intended way in. The others are the roof of the adjoining warehouse, the strong-room corridor's service door, and the honest one: persuading somebody who works there.

**Layout.**
1. **The Counting-Room** — public, staffed by a factor and one or two clerks. Day-ledgers on the desks.
2. **The Records Office** — lightly secured. Correspondence and the house's private paperwork.
3. **The Strong-Room Corridor** — a guarded passage. One enforcer walks it on an irregular loop.
4. **The Vault** — the objective. Locked, alarmed.

**Hazards.**

| Hazard | Check | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Vault lock | DC 17 Thieves' Tools (DC 13 with a staff key) | Hard failure trips a bell-alarm |
| Bell-alarm | — | Enforcer and any guards arrive in 1 round. A chase scene, not a killing |
| Records office lock | DC 13 Thieves' Tools | — |
| Corridor patrol | DC 14 Stealth, or a distraction | Failure is detection — a social problem, not damage |

**Inhabitants.** Clerks and a factor (**Commoner-like** and **Noble-like**, noncombatants). One house enforcer on the corridor — **Thug/Spy-like**, AC 13, ~40 HP, built to grapple, threaten, and march intruders to the reeve; he does not want blood on the counting-house floor and neither does his employer. Possibly one **Guard-like** (AC 16, ~11 HP) on the door at night.

**Non-combat outs.** The cleanest route through this site is a turned clerk. Ledger staff on the frontier are underpaid, overworked, and often unhappy with what passes through their hands: **DC 14 Persuasion or Insight**, easier if the character has done them a favor. A turned clerk supplies the key, the patrol timing, and an explanation of what is in the vault.

**Salvage.** The vault relic (whichever one the house is sitting on). A coin reserve of roughly 200 gp — taking it makes the thief a hunted one, which is the point. The records-office paperwork, which in most games is worth more than the coin.

**Getting Out.** It is a building in a friendly town; walk out. The cost of a botched job is a powerful commercial enemy, a debt, or a blacklist — never a corpse.
