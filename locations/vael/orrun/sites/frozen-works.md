# The Frozen Works

**Type** ice-locked Concord station · **Region** the high passes of the Highmark Spine · **Level** 12–16 · **Danger** very high — **the cold is the dungeon**

**What It Is.** A Concord station in the high cold passes beyond the northern wall, among the oldest works anyone has found. The dry cold has preserved it in a way no southern ruin has ever been preserved: machinery unrusted, inscription unblurred, doors that still close. Scholars who know it exists talk about it the way sailors talk about a strait they have not survived yet.

The most isolated survivor-holds on the continent live within reach of it, and know things about it that nobody has written down.

**Approach and Entrance.** Over the high passes, gear-gated and prep-gated — **this is not a "you can try it and see" site, and the DM should say so before anyone commits.** With a survivor-hold guide (the only reliable route and the only reliable haven). With an **Ashen Warden** or a **Concord Remnant** high-expedition, both of which have their own stakes in the works.

**Layout.**
1. **The High Pass** — the ice-locked approach. Survivor-holds, frozen markers, whiteout.
2. **The Frozen Station-Front** — the station's intact face: machinery and inscription, all legible through clear ice.
3. **The Ice-Vault Archive** — the station's records, preserved whole. The reason to come.
4. **The Cold-Locked Heart** — the sealed inner chamber, and an intact sentry standing in it.
5. **The Deep Ice** — the deepest vaults. Best-preserved relics, worst cold, **bottoming out in ice and bedrock.**

**Hazards.** Lethal cold and exposure (**DC 15 Con**, exhaustion *plus* cold damage, stacking toward death — gear, fire, and time limits are mandatory); ice-collapse and frozen falls (**DC 15 Dex/Perception**, 4d6–6d6 and buried); whiteout navigation (**DC 16 Survival** — a survivor-guide negates it); relic-exposure at the heart (**DC 15 Con**).

**Inhabitants.** Ice-preserved lingering dead — **Wraith-like**, AC 13, ~48–64 HP, intact, aware, bound to the station, and rite- or parley-able. Concord guardian-constructs, apex-scaled and in unusually good repair (AC 17–18, ~120–180 HP; **the watchword stands them down**, and an Ashen Warden can also do it by standing).

**Salvage.** The best-preserved Concord artifacts anywhere (tier-3/4, and the far frontier's great prize). Rubbings and copies of the ice-vault archive, which scholars across the continent will bid ruinously for. The loyalty of a survivor-hold, which is a base in the least habitable country on Orrun.

**Getting Out.** **The sole haven is a survivor-hold**, and reaching it is the whole retreat plan — which is why prep is not optional here. Everything in the station is bound; the cold is what kills you, and it kills you on the way home as readily as on the way in.
