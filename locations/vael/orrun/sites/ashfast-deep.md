# The Ashfast Deep (the caldera descent)

**Type** volcanic ruin descent · **Region** beneath Cinderhold, the Emberfells · **Level** 12–16 · **Danger** very high — **the heat is the dungeon**

**What It Is.** A descent from the volcanic mining town into the fire-mountain below it. The Emberfell fire-priests read the volcanoes as divine and return their dead to the fire, rendering them into a hard glassy substance the black market buys under the same name the northern frontier uses: **old glass**. Below the rendering-deeps lie older works the Concord left, half-consumed by the mountain, which the theocracy forbids and the desperate dig anyway.

**Approach and Entrance.** With masked mining guides out of Cinderhold. Along the pilgrim ways, which buys access and scrutiny in equal measure. With the black-market diggers who follow the old glass down, which is illegal under the Pyre's law. Or over the caldera rim, which is the fastest way to die of this site.

**Layout.**
1. **The Cinder Mines** — the worked volcanic mines. Heat, masks, gas pockets, ore, and old glass in the rock.
2. **The Forbidden Stair** — the theocracy-sealed descent. A guard post, a seal, and rising heat.
3. **The Rendering-Deeps** — where the fire-cult returns its dead. Lava channels, ash, and rites in progress.
4. **The Concord-Touched Vault** — an old Concord work the mountain has half-eaten: inscription under ash, fire-warded.
5. **The Magma Throat** — the living fire below. **Instant death, explicitly telegraphed. A gate, not a challenge.**

**Hazards.** Staged lethal heat by depth (**DC 14–15 Con**, exhaustion escalating to fire damage — heat protection is the actual key to this site); toxic gas pockets (**DC 14–15 Con**, lethal in the deep pools); lava-rock and channels (**DC 15 Dex**, high lava damage); molten collapse (**DC 15**); fire-wards on the vault (**DC 16**, a fire-blast or a roused guardian).

**Inhabitants.** Fire-touched lingering dead in the rendering-deeps — use a **Wraith-like** profile with fire flavor, AC 15, ~80–100 HP, bound, and reverence works on them as it does anywhere. A fire-elemental or fire-construct guardian in the vault: AC 16–17, ~110–140 HP, heat aura, magma-grip, fire-blast, **bound to the chamber**. Living: the theocracy's Pyre-wardens (zealous, and they enforce), miners, a heretic-scholar who studies the fire-glass in secret and is the best ally available, and black-market diggers.

**Salvage.** High-tier fire-glass and Concord-volcanic relics and artifacts (tier-3/4). The heretic-scholar's alliance, or a zealot enemy. Substantial old-glass value, and a decision about what to do with it.

**Getting Out.** Cinderhold and Ashfast are havens; the guardian is vault-bound; the heat itself forces you up on a clock. **Gear up before you descend, not after** — telegraph that at the table.
