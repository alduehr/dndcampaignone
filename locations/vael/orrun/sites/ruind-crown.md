# The Ruin'd Crown

**Type** vast surface ruin-field · **Region** astride the Greatspine, reached from Crownmouth · **Level** 10–14 · **Danger** high; far from any friendly roof

**What It Is.** The broken heart-country of the old Custodian Concord, ruined to the horizon: toppled colonnades, drowned plazas, a shrine-dome cracked open like an egg, all built on a scale the frontier has no vocabulary for. It is picked over daily by scavengers out of Crownmouth, dug by scholarly expeditions, and camped in by relic-pilgrims who have nowhere better. Standing in it is the point — nothing else on the continent conveys what the Concord actually *was*.

**Approach and Entrance.** From **Crownmouth**, the fortified scavenger-town on the ruin's edge: hire a guide, pay the war-captain's toll, walk in. From the **Pilgrim Camps** in the fallen forum. With a scholarly expedition, which buys access and a rivalry at once. Or alone over the Greatspine, which is how people vanish.

**Layout.**
1. **The Ruin-Edge** — the scavenged outer city. Tents, fires, tolls, rival diggers, and unstable masonry everywhere.
2. **The Fallen Forum** — a drowned plaza turned camp. Desperation, fever, and relic-hawkers selling fakes.
3. **The Crown Archives** — the great surviving archive-hall, and the reason scholars come. Shelf on shelf of legible Concord record.
4. **The Sundered Shrine-Dome** — the cracked dome. The oldest dead of the city are in here, and something still keeps it.
5. **The Deep Vaults** — the deepest surviving strongrooms: guardian-thick, treasure-dense, and **bottoming out in rubble and collapse.** They do not go anywhere.

**Hazards.** Structural collapse (**DC 14–15 Perception/Dex**, 3d6–5d6 and a blocked path); archive script-wards (**DC 15–16**; failure is a force-blast or a roused guardian); the eldest dead's regard (**DC 15–16 Wis**, psychic strain); far-frontier exposure and navigation (**DC 14 Survival** — a Crownmouth guide negates it).

**Inhabitants.** Concord guardian-constructs, apex-scaled (AC 18, ~150–200 HP; watchword stands them down). The Crown's eldest dead — **Ancient Remembrance**, use a **Ghost/Wraith-like** profile scaled up, AC 15, ~90–120 HP, reverence and rite before violence. Living: Crownmouth's war-captain and her toll-takers, scholarly expeditions, a heretic preacher with a following in the camps, and rival diggers of every stripe. The *human* rivalry is often the real climax here.

**Salvage.** Concord artifacts of genuine power (the Crown's great prizes); significant coin and salvage from the vaults; and the archive's records, which are the reason anyone with a brain came.

**Getting Out.** Crownmouth and the Pilgrim Camps are the havens and always reachable; guardians are ruin-bound. The deep vaults dead-end downward, and should be described that way rather than left ambiguous.
