# The Cindern Waste Buried Works

**Type** ash-drowned Concord ruin · **Region** the forbidden Cindern Waste, out from Cinderhold · **Level** 13–16 · **Danger** very high — **the ash is the dungeon**

**What It Is.** Concord works buried under volcanic ash in the badland the fire-priests forbid, over which the theocracy has built its own certainties without ever going down to look. Ash preserves almost as well as ice. The orthodox ban digging; the heretic scholars want it desperately; the miners keep breaking into voids they were not looking for.

**Approach and Entrance.** With the heretic-scholar's circle, who know the safe waste-routes and want in badly (the key access, and a defiance of the Pyre). Through a mining tunnel that has already broken into the works. Straight across the forbidden waste past the wardens' patrols. Or alone into the ash, which is the same mistake as the caldera by a different route.

**Layout.**
1. **The Forbidden Waste-Edge** — ash dunes, warden patrols, and the ban's markers.
2. **The Ash-Drowned Entry** — a Concord structure half-swallowed; a miner's break or a heretic's dig-shaft.
3. **The Buried Hall** — the works' central hall, ash-preserved. Inscription, intact, extensive.
4. **The Ember-Heart** — the sealed inner chamber and its ash-and-fire-touched sentry.
5. **The Deep Ash-Vaults** — the deepest chambers: intact relics, worst air, **bottoming out in ash and bedrock.**

**Hazards.** Ash-storms (**DC 15 Con** — choking, blinding, exhausting; shelter and time them); ash-collapse and unstable ground over buried voids (**DC 14–15**, 4d6–6d6 and buried); choke-ash underground (**DC 14 Con** — ventilate, and use miner-craft); heat (**DC 14 Con**); and the Pyre-wardens, who are a faction problem, not a monster.

**Inhabitants.** Ash-locked lingering dead — **Wraith-like**, AC 13, ~52–64 HP, bound and rite-able. Ash-and-fire-touched Concord guardian-constructs (AC 17–18, ~120–160 HP, immune to fire; **the watchword stands them down**, and the heretic-scholar can read it). Living: Pyre-wardens (**Veteran/Priest-like**, AC 16, ~60–90 HP), avoidable and negotiable; heretic diggers; miners.

**Salvage.** Intact ash-preserved Concord artifacts (tier-3/4). Copies of the buried hall's inscription. A foothold in a fractured theocracy — the heretics' alliance or the orthodoxy's enmity, and it is difficult to have neither.

**Getting Out.** Cinderhold is the haven and the waste itself is hostile the whole way back. Guardians and dead are bound. Ash-storms force you out on their own schedule, which is a valve — but **preparation against the ash is mandatory and should be telegraphed before anyone sets out.**
