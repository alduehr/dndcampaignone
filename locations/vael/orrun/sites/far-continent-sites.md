# Sites Of The Wider Continent

## AI Use

Load for play anywhere on Orrun beyond the northwestern frontier and Caradril. Fifteen high-tier sites (levels 8–16), one or two per far region, each written as a physical place: how you get there, what the ground and the air and the water do to you, what lives in it, what you can carry out, and how you leave.

**These are optional destinations, not a chain.** They are scattered across a continent that takes months to cross (see [`../01_geography/TRAVEL_AND_ROUTES.md`](../travel/travel-and-routes.md)). Each stands alone.

**Two design constants across all of them.** First, **every one of them is an environmental problem before it is a combat problem** — cold, ash, brine, tide, current, gas, collapse, or simply distance from any friendly roof. Preparation, a local guide, and knowing when to turn around are the real mechanics. Second, **all guardians are bound to their sites and none of them pursue.** Leaving always works.

**Concord guardian-constructs.** Several sites hold them: figures of fitted stone and cold light, still at their posts. Use a **Stone Golem-like** profile scaled to the tier (AC 17–18, 95–180 HP), resistant to nonmagical damage, one slam, one ranged force/necrotic burst on a recharge, and a relic-exposure aura. **They are posted sentries, not monsters.** Each issues a spoken challenge and waits. Reading the challenge and speaking the correct response (**Concord Script, DC 15–16**) makes it stand aside and let you work. This is the intended solution everywhere it appears.

**Relic-exposure ("thin-touch").** **DC 14–15 Con** on prolonged handling of raw relics or long exposure in relic-dense chambers; **disadvantage on death saves** until a folk rite (**DC 13 Religion**) or a long rest at a working shrine. See [`SUNDERING_REACH_SITES.md`](sundering-reach-sites.md).
