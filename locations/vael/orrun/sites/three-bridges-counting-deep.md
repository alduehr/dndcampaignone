# The Three Bridges Counting-Deep

**Type** banking vault-complex · **Region** the Three Bridges banking quarter, Glassmere · **Level** 9–12 · **Danger** moderate; a heist

**What It Is.** The **Cinder Ledger's** continental counting-house: a grand public banking floor, a hall of underfactors, a deep strongroom-archive, a refining cellar, and the provost's own strongbox. This is where the house's paperwork actually lives, which makes it the single most informative building on the continent for anyone who wants to know how the relic trade works.

**Approach and Entrance.** Open an account. Pose as a relic-seller. Turn an underfactor. Accept funding from a rival banker who wants an audit forced. Or go in over the roof. **Combat is the failure path and the building is designed to make that obvious.**

**Layout.**
1. **The Counting-Floor** — the public bank. Tellers, clients, provost-guards, and a cover problem.
2. **The Clerks' Hall** — underfactors and day-ledgers. The turnable inside.
3. **The Counting-Deep** — the deep strongroom-archive: the master purchase-books, logging relics by the region they were bought in and where they were sent on.
4. **The Refiners' Cellar** — where raw relics are cut, reset, and graded for the market. Hot, chemical, gloved.
5. **The Provost's Strongbox** — coin, deeds, and files on people who would rather those files did not exist.

**Hazards.** The strongroom lock (**DC 16 Thieves' Tools**, **DC 13** with a clerk's key); a fine lock on the strongbox (**DC 17**); counting-house wards (**DC 14 Investigation**); refining fumes and relic-exposure (**DC 14 Con**); and the city's banking politics, which will find you whether you want them to or not.

**Inhabitants.** Provost-guards and strong-arms (**Thug/Spy-like**, AC 13–14, ~16–45 HP) whose job is to catch and eject. A vault strong-arm and a refiner's guard (**Veteran-like**, AC 14, ~45 HP). Clerks and underfactors, several of them turnable (**DC 14–15 Persuasion or Insight**). **No undead. No guardians. This one is entirely people.**

**Salvage.** Substantial coin and deeds; the provost's leverage files; and the master purchase-book, which is a map of an entire continental trade and worth more to the right person than the strongbox.

**Getting Out.** Keep your cover and walk out the front. Lose it and you are ejected, indebted, and remembered. The city absorbs you either way.
