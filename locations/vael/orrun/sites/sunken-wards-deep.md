# The Sunken Wards Deep

**Type** flooded undercity · **Region** beneath Caradril · **Level** 6–10 (core 7–9) · **Danger** moderate, telegraphed, retreat-rich; high only in the deepest chamber

**What It Is.** Caradril was built on an older city, and the older city is still down there. The dry upper galleries of the Sunken Wards are common knowledge and lightly used — delvers, drunks, and the odd cellar-extension. Below those galleries the water rises and the stonework changes: no longer city foundations but Concord work proper, vast fitted blocks, star-marks worn smooth by a century of standing water, and inscription running along the lintels well above where any lantern reaches comfortably.

It is quiet down there, and the quality of the quiet is the thing people mention afterwards.

**Approach and Entrance.** Three ways in.

1. **The Sealgate stair**, down from the Sill through the upper wards — the semi-public route. The **Salt Syndicate** watches it, because they use the upper wards for drops.
2. **A hidden stair from the Lantern Reach** kept by the **Concord Remnant**, who have quietly mapped the deep wards and would rather nobody else did. Requires their goodwill, their map, or following one of their people.
3. **The flood-channel up from the river** at the Brass Ferry — a wet smugglers' route that bypasses the upper galleries entirely.

**Layout.**
1. **The Listening Galleries** — flooded colonnades, shin- to waist-deep, where still pools hold reflections a half-second too long. Two halls fill and drain on a slow cycle.
2. **The Syndicate Drop** — a dry upper vault used for contraband and relic caches. Someone's tripline is across the entrance.
3. **The Drowned Concourse** — the deep wards' main flooded boulevard. Stone-lined channels run away into the dark. The woken dead drift here.
4. **The Archive Stair** — a hidden, dry stair climbing up out of the wards into the lowest level of the Remnant's private archive. Warded, and watched.
5. **The Cistern Vault** — the lowest chamber. A single standing Concord stone in black water, warm to the hand, and something keeping it.

**Hazards.**

| Hazard | Check | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Flooded crossings | DC 13–14 Athletics/Con | Exhaustion risk, dropped gear, forced retreat |
| Rising-water cycle (Galleries, Cistern) | DC 14–15 to read the cycle | A hall or the cistern fills on a slow schedule. Never seals the only exit |
| Collapsing Concord stone | DC 13 Perception to spot stress-cracks; DC 14 Dex save | 3d6 bludgeoning and a blocked path; reroute |
| Relic-exposure (Concourse, Cistern) | DC 14 Con | Thin-touch |
| Syndicate tripline | DC 13 Perception / DC 13 Stealth | Brings runners; a chase, not a killing |
| The archive ward-glyph | see below | A warding apparition if forced |

**The Archive Ward-Glyph (puzzle).** The hidden door is sealed by a Concord ward: a ring of star-marks and a verse. **Read it and name the stars in the old order** (Concord Script, or **DC 14 Investigation** from a rubbing). Alternates: a Remnant keeper's key, obtained honestly or otherwise; a Mourners' Circle elder's knowledge of the old star-order; brute force (**DC 17 Thieves' Tools or Strength**), which works and wakes a warding dead. A costly bypass exists too — Caradril's information brokers sell the answer, for coin and a favor.

**Inhabitants.** One to two drifting lingering dead on the Concourse — **Specter/Wraith-like**, AC 12–13, ~22–40 HP; telegraphed by cold and dimming lanterns, and avoidable, parley-able, or settled by rite (**DC 15 Religion**). In the Cistern Vault, a stronger one keeps the standing stone: **Wraith-like**, AC 13, ~52–60 HP, chill touch, frightful presence, life-drain, and it can call one drifting dead to it. **Bound to the cistern** — retreat always works. Parley or rite (**DC 15**) resolve it without a fight.

Living: **Salt Syndicate** runners at the drop (**Thug/Bandit-like**, AC 13–14) — bribable and avoidable. **Concord Remnant** keepers on the Archive Stair (**Spy/Mage-like**, AC 13–14, ~40–60 HP, with hold person, counterspell, and a relic one-off in reserve) — they warn, control, and eject; they do not kill in their own city.

**Salvage.** One or two genuine Remembrance relics, including a notably fine one in the Cistern Vault. A Syndicate cache (150–250 gp in coin and contraband; taking it makes an enemy who lives upstairs). A portable rubbing of the Concourse inscription. And **the back-way itself** — knowing the hidden stair exists is worth more than anything you can carry out of the water.

**Getting Out.** The Sealgate stair up to the Sill is always reachable and the wards never seal their only exit. Everything undead down here is bound; everything living prefers to turn you back. A hired undercity guide removes the navigation problem entirely, and one can be found in the Sill. **No rest below the dry galleries.**
