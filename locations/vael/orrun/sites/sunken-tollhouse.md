# The Sunken Tollhouse

**Type** flooded Concord toll-house · **Region** under Kettle Bridge's broken fourth arch · **Level** 2–3 · **Danger** low–moderate (cold water, current, drowning)

**What It Is.** A Concord toll-house and wayside shrine that stood where the road met the river. When the fourth arch came down, generations ago, it went into the Mirewend with it. The building is intact but sunk; boatmen have avoided the broken arch for as long as anyone can remember, and lately have been avoiding it harder.

**Approach and Entrance.** By boat from Kettle Bridge's Wet Ward, or by diving at low water from the arch footing. Any bargeman will describe the entry stair for a coin, and a few will guide you to it for more. The cold coming off the water is the warning, and it is a good one.

**Layout.**
1. **The Flooded Stair** — descends from the arch footing into chest-deep water. Current runs across it.
2. **The Toll-Hall** — a half-drowned chamber, inscription on the walls above the waterline, submerged toll-coffers underfoot. Someone has already been through the easy ones.
3. **The Inner Chamber** — a small vaulted room holding a cracked block of Concord stonework, plainly the point of the whole building, and plainly not a toll-desk. Cold, and unpleasant to stand near.
4. **The Silt Vault** — a collapsed side room. A mislabeled salvage crate, and the body of a drowned bargeman nobody recovered.

**Hazards.**

| Hazard | Check | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Current in deep rooms | DC 13 Athletics per crossing | Failure sweeps you back toward the stair — lost progress, no damage |
| Cold water | DC 12 Con on a long stay | 1 level of exhaustion |
| Rising water | The tide/flood rises a stage per ~10 min inside | After 3 stages the lower rooms fill. A clock, not a trap — the stair stays open |
| Cracked floor (Silt Vault) | DC 13 Perception; DC 12 Dex save | Drop into a silt-pit; DC 13 Athletics to climb out, non-lethal |

**Inhabitants.** Possibly one eel or water-snake disturbed on the stair (**Constrictor Snake-like**, CR 1/8), telegraphed by movement in the water. In the Inner Chamber: one drowned lingering dead, a boatman the river took — **Specter-like**, AC 12, ~22 HP, moves freely through water, weeping. **Not hostile.** It attacks only if attacked, or if someone tries to lever the cracked stonework out of the wall. Speak with it (Insight/Persuasion **DC 13**) or settle it by rite (**DC 14 Religion**); either ends the encounter, and locals downstream will notice the difference.

**Salvage.** Water-blackened Concord coin from the coffers, ~25 gp of it (finding much more than that implicates whoever has been fishing the site already). One minor Remembrance relic from the Silt Vault crate. The drowned bargeman's signet, which has a family in Kettle Bridge.

**Getting Out.** The stair stays open the whole time. Swim up, or your boat is waiting. The rising water pressures but never seals.
