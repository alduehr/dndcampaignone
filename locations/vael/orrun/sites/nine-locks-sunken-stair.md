# The Nine Locks Sunken Stair

**Type** flooded Concord lock-works · **Region** the Verdance, at the Nine Locks by Marrowfen Stair · **Level** 8–12 · **Danger** moderate–high (water, currents)

**What It Is.** The Verdance climbs into the interior by a staircase of nine locks, and the locks are Concord-built — still working, a century after the people who cut them stopped existing. Beneath them lies a set of sub-chambers that are plainly not lock machinery: dressed stone, inscription, a sealed inner room. Bargefolk have known about them forever and have no theory worth repeating.

**Approach and Entrance.** Down a maintenance shaft between the fourth and fifth locks, or through a drowned sluice at low water. The **Cinder Ledger's** inland arm runs the locks and keeps lock-keepers on them, so access is a charter, a bribe, or a quiet night.

**Layout.**
1. **The Lock-Stair** — the great works above: nine chambers, barge traffic, keepers, and slick lock walls (**DC 13 Dex**).
2. **The Flooded Sub-Locks** — half-drowned chambers below, threaded with old stone channels. Zero visibility in the silt.
3. **The Lock-Vault** — the sealed inner chamber, warded and inscribed, faintly warm.
4. **The Drowned Channel** — a stone watercourse running out of the vault and downstream, silted solid a hundred feet in. **Impassable. A gate.**
5. **The Lock-Keepers' Cache** — the old Concord lock-wardens' stores.

**Hazards.** Flooding on the sluice cycle (**DC 15 Survival** to time it, **DC 14 Athletics** if caught — a bargeman negates this entirely); currents and deep water (**DC 14–15 Athletics**); the vault ward (**Concord Script or DC 15 Investigation**; forcing it rouses the keeper); a flooded lock on the cache (**DC 15 Thieves' Tools**).

**Inhabitants.** Drifting lingering dead in the sub-locks — **Specter/Wraith-like**, AC 13, ~30–48 HP, bound to the water, parley- or rite-able (**DC 15**). One stronger keeper-dead in the Lock-Vault (**Wraith-like**, AC 13, ~52–64 HP), which stands down if the closing words are read aloud from the wall (**Concord Script or DC 15 Religion**). Living: Ledger lock-keepers and corridor guards (**Guard/Spy-like**), who eject rather than kill.

**Salvage.** Concord relics and old glass; corridor salvage; a possible water-warded minor item. A rubbing of the vault inscription. Goodwill from the bargefolk, which is worth more on this river than coin.

**Getting Out.** Marrowfen Stair is a short haul upstream. The flooding cycle physically drives you out on a schedule, which is the built-in safety valve. Everything down here is bound to the water.
