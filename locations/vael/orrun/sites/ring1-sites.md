# Sites Of The Vale, The Wood, And The Coast

## AI Use

Load when anyone in the three regions ringing the Sundering Reach — the **Ashgarden Vale** (south), the **Tollwood** (east), the **Pale Coast** (west) — enters a ruin, a grove, a sea-cave, or a tomb. Nine authored sites, described as physical places: footing, water, tide, dark, air, what lives there, and what can be carried out. Three of them are among the largest Concord works outside the Reach; three are pre-Concord and stranger; three are held by living people, where the fight is the failure state.

**Relic-exposure ("thin-touch")** applies here as in the Reach: **DC 14 Con** on prolonged handling of raw relics; **disadvantage on death saves** until a folk rite (**DC 13 Religion**) or a long rest at a working shrine. See [`SUNDERING_REACH_SITES.md`](sundering-reach-sites.md).
