# The Old Holds

**Type** forbidden deep-wood Concord ruins · **Region** deep in the Hethewood, out from Hethemoot · **Level** 9–13 · **Danger** high, gated by the forest

**What It Is.** Concord ruins swallowed by the eastern great-wood — toppled halls, root-cracked inscription, whole buildings under a century of leaf-fall. The free-holds forbid them, and have maintained, for generations, a set of bargains and offering-marks whose stated purpose is to leave what is in the deep wood asleep. The bargains have been slipping for a couple of years. The holds are frightened about it in a way that they will not discuss with outsiders until they trust you.

**Approach and Entrance.** With the Speaker's leave, granted to someone investigating why the bargains are failing (slow, far safer). By learning the grove-mother's old songs, which is the reverent key. With a scholarly expedition. Or against the holds' law, which is fast and turns both the wood and the holds against you.

**Layout.**
1. **The Marked Wood** — the forbidden forest's edge, hung with the bargain-tokens the holds maintain. Past here the wood begins misleading you.
2. **The Drowned Holds** — the ruins proper: toppled halls, root-split stone, collapse everywhere.
3. **The Grey-Hands Hall** — the central ruin-hall, which the old songs name and do not explain. The best-preserved inscription, and the gathered dead.
4. **The Sleeping Grove** — the oldest heart, where a forest-bound presence rests under the failing bargains. **A closed older heart. There is no way down and no way past it.**
5. **The Root-Vaults** — root-swallowed strongrooms. The salvage.

**Hazards.** The misleading wood (**DC 16–18 Survival**, cumulative — this is the site's signature danger, and a forest-guide or the old songs are the only reliable answer); structural collapse (**DC 15**, 3d6–5d6); the presence's regard (**DC 15–16 Wis**, psychic strain and a compulsion to leave, which is the wood pushing you out alive); root-locked vaults (**DC 16 Athletics/Thieves' Tools**); and breaking a bargain-mark, which rouses both the wood and the holds.

**Inhabitants.** Deep-wood lingering dead — **Wraith-like**, AC 13, ~40–56 HP, bound to the ruins, settled by rite, song, or parley. The **forest-bound presence** in the Sleeping Grove: use a scaled **Ancient Remembrance** or high-tier guardian profile, AC 14–15, ~80–110 HP, but understand what it is for — **reverence or withdrawal are the resolutions. Force fails and strands you.** Living: scholarly expeditions, logging crews at the wood's edge, and hold-wardens who take the bargains seriously.

**Salvage.** Deep-wood Concord relics and root-vault salvage (tier-3). The old songs, which are a lore prize and a lasting friendship. The free-holds' alliance, if you guarded the ruins rather than stripping them.

**Getting Out.** Hethemoot and the grove-village are the havens; the presence and the dead are bound to the ruins and grove; leaving the marked wood escapes everything. A guide makes withdrawal reliable, which is why you hire one.
