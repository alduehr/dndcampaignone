# The Barrow of Nine Doors

**Type** pre-Concord barrow · **Region** the heart of the Greyfens · **Level** 3–5 · **Danger** moderate–high (a real delve; hire a fen-guide)

**What It Is.** A grass-grown mound rising out of the deep fen, far older than the Concord — old enough that the Concord evidently built around it rather than through it. Its name comes from the nine carved thresholds inside; eight are sealed. Fen-folk shun it, and describe it, without elaborating, as "where the old dead go and can't get through."

**Approach and Entrance.** Deep in the Greyfens, past everything marked (see [`../01_geography/wilderness/GREYFENS.md`](../wilderness/greyfens.md) — mire rules apply on the approach, and a hired guide negates them). The mound's single open door breathes cold air you can see. The drift of lingering dead converging on the mound is visible from a long way off, which is both the landmark and the warning.

**Layout.**
1. **The Open Door** — the antechamber, crowded. Lingering dead press here, jammed against the inner thresholds, going nowhere.
2. **The Ring of Nine** — a circular chamber with nine carved door-thresholds around it: eight sealed, one leading down. Two layers of carving, one much older than the other. The puzzle hub.
3. **The Listening Hollow** — a side chamber with unusual acoustics, where the dead's voices carry. People come here to be spoken to, and leave offerings. Sometimes there is somebody in it.
4. **The True Threshold** — the ninth door: a genuine pre-Concord burial cell, and what is in it.

**Hazards.**

| Hazard | Check | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Cold dread (whole barrow) | DC 13 Wis save on entry | Shaken: disadvantage on the first fear save |
| Forcing a sealed door | DC 16 Strength | Releases one angry dead and "wrongs" the room: hostile dead have advantage for 1 minute |
| Pushing through the jammed dead | DC 14 Wis save | 1 level of fear-strain (recovers on a short rest) if you shove rather than settle or calm them |
| The approach | Mire rules, [`GREYFENS.md`](../wilderness/greyfens.md) | A guide negates it |

**The Ring of Nine (puzzle).** The nine thresholds must be named in the old order to open the true door cleanly. **Three valid paths, and any clever fourth:** read the layered carvings (Concord Script plus **DC 14 Religion or History**); use a Mourners' Circle elder's knowledge of the old star-order, which they will teach a respectful visitor; or simply ask, if the thing behind the ninth door is inclined to be helpful.

**Inhabitants.** At the Open Door, 3–5 weak lingering dead (**Specter-like**, AC 12, ~17 HP each) — **not hostile**, mournful, and jammed. A mass folk rite (**DC 14 Religion**) settles the lot of them and visibly eases the fen for a while. Provoking them turns a sad scene into a dangerous swarm; it is telegraphed.

The Listening Hollow may hold Gravecallers — 2–3 of them, or a speaker (**Cult Fanatic-like**). They came to listen, not to ambush; this is a conversation unless someone makes it otherwise.

Behind the True Threshold: **the barrow-lord**, an ancient dead of the pre-Concord downs. **Wraith/Ghost-like boss**, AC 14, ~60 HP, life-drain and a fear aura (DC 14 Wis), incorporeal, calls 1d4 of the jammed dead as reinforcements if fought. It is not evil, it is not in a hurry, and it will talk. Parley (**DC 15 Persuasion or Religion**) or offer it true release and it goes peacefully — which settles a great deal of the surrounding fen with it.

**Salvage.** Pre-Concord grave-goods in the burial cell: roughly 120 gp of old gold, and one uncursed minor magic item of a funerary character (a cloak, an amulet, a ring). One clean relic. A rubbing of the layered carvings, which scholars will fight over.

**Getting Out.** The Open Door is always open, and the barrow-lord cannot leave the mound. Withdraw to the antechamber and out. **No safe long rest inside**; short rest in the Listening Hollow if it is empty. Greywater Holm is the recovery point, a few hours out with a guide.
