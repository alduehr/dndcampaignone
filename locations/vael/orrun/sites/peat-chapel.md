# The Peat Chapel

**Type** small sunken Concord chapel · **Region** Greyfens edge, a few hours from Hollowmere · **Level** 1–2 · **Danger** low (a good first ruin)

**What It Is.** A Concord wayside chapel — one of the small houses the old order kept along its roads, where travelers who died between towns were given their rites before the road went on. The peat has taken half of it. The tilted star-spire is visible for a mile across the fen and is the landmark everyone navigates by.

**Approach and Entrance.** A firm finger of ground reaches it from the Hollowmere side; no guide is strictly needed, though any local can point out the line. The door is the original one, jammed half-open by the tilt of the building. Nothing about the approach is hidden or dangerous.

**Layout.**
1. **The Tilted Nave** — the main chapel, floor canted maybe fifteen degrees into the peat, pews rotted to fibre, a hole where the floor gave way at the low end. Dry enough to rest in.
2. **The Sunk Vestry** — a side room, knee-deep in cold water, silted. Something usually dens here.
3. **The Altar Recess** — the star-altar, intact, still holding a smooth grey relic-stone. The one thing here worth real money.
4. **The Crypt-Space** — through the floor-hole, a crawl-height void with a traveler's bones and a second, cracked relic.

**Hazards.**

| Hazard | Check | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Rotten floor | DC 12 Perception to spot the soft boards | DC 11 Dex save or fall into the crypt-space; 1d6, climb out DC 10 Athletics |
| Cold damp (vestry) | DC 10 Con after a long stay | Minor fatigue; trivial |
| Relic-exposure | DC 14 Con if the altar-stone is carried bare | Thin-touch (above) |

**Inhabitants.** One fen predator in the vestry — **Giant Frog-like** or **Swarm of Rats-like**, CR 1/8–1/4, telegraphed by tracks and disturbed water, and entirely skippable by not entering the vestry. Lifting the altar-stone stirs a faint lingering dead: a **Specter-like** profile, AC 12, ~13 HP, **not hostile**, sorrowful, asking wordlessly to be remembered or let go. Insight or Persuasion **DC 11** to understand it; folk rite **DC 12** to settle it.

**Salvage.** The altar relic (a genuine Remembrance relic; a fair market for one is 40 gp on the frontier and considerably more in a city). Loose Concord stonework and fittings, ~15 gp of it. The cracked crypt relic, worth little but readable.

**Getting Out.** Walk out. The path back is short, firm, and unguarded.
