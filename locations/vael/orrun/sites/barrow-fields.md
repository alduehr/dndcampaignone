# The Barrow-Fields

**Type** pre-Concord barrow complex · **Region** the chalk downs, reached from Marrowmoot · **Level** 8–12 · **Danger** moderate–high

**What It Is.** Mound after mound on the open chalk, far older than the Concord, holding the down-country's ancient dead. They have lain quiet for centuries, which is precisely why the shire is unsettled: over the last year or two they have grown restless. Barrow-thieves have cracked several of the nearer mounds, the shire-reeve wants that stopped, and the shire's **Mourners' Circle** elder wants the dead settled properly, in that order of urgency depending on whom you ask.

**Approach and Entrance.** The barrows are not hidden — they are open downland, walkable in an afternoon from Marrowmoot. They are forbidden by custom, not by wall. Come by the reeve's leave (the honored path), by the Mourners' Circle elder's counsel (the reverent one), with the robbers (fast and costly), or just walk out there.

**Layout.**
1. **The Open Mounds** — the field's edge, where the thieves have already dug. Loose spoil and collapsed shafts.
2. **The Door-Glyph Mound** — a great sealed barrow whose door carries two layers of carving, one much older than the other.
3. **The Gathered Barrow** — a central chamber where the down-folk's dead have collected, restless and aware of you.
4. **The Barrow-Lord's Chamber** — the complex's heart. The richest grave-goods, and their owner.
5. **The Deep Mounds** — the oldest and unrobbed. Best treasure, worst inhabitants, **bottoming out in chalk.**

**Hazards.** Dug-mound collapse (**DC 13–14 Dex/Perception**, 2d6–4d6); the glyph-door wards (**DC 14–15**; forcing rouses a guardian); the barrow-lord's regard (**DC 14–15 Wis**); and defiling a mound, which rouses the whole complex *and* brings the shire down on you.

**The Glyph-Door (puzzle) — three valid paths.** Read the layered carvings (**DC 14–15 Script/History**); the Mourners' Circle elder's star-and-grave lore opens it by song (**DC 14 Religion**), and she will teach it to a respectful visitor; or a barrow-robber's stolen key, which costs standing. Accept any clever fourth answer.

**Inhabitants.** Restless barrow-dead — **Specter/Wraith-like** scaled dry, AC 12–13, ~36–52 HP, bound to the mounds, settled by rite, song, or parley. **The barrow-lord**: an ancient chieftain-dead, **Ghost-like**, AC 14, ~80–100 HP, **parley and reverence first** — it is not malicious, it is disturbed, and it will talk to someone who is not carrying its grave-goods. Living: barrow-robbers (**Bandit/Thug-like**, AC 13, ~22–30 HP), who scatter.

**Salvage.** Pre-Concord grave-goods (tier-2/3 valuables, and a possible barrow-warded minor item). The reeve's gratitude, which in the Marrowdowns is a lawful base and a route south. The Mourners' Circle elder's old songs, which are worth learning.

**Getting Out.** Open country in every direction; Marrowmoot is the haven; the dead are bound to the mounds. Walking away from this site is the easiest of any in this file.
