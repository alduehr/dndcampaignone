# The Drowned Steps

**Type** submerged pre-Concord causeway · **Region** off the Mardenflow delta, out from Reedmouth · **Level** 10–14 · **Danger** high (tide, fever, patrols)

**What It Is.** An ancient causeway of enormous dressed blocks that strides out of the delta and under the sea, heading for open water. It is older than the Concord and older than the delta's memory, and it runs out into nothing anyone will name. The fever-folk shun it. The smugglers use its channels. The occupying garrison denies it exists, and arrests people for saying otherwise.

**Approach and Entrance.** With the delta's smugglers, who know the channel-routes. By the marsh-healer's counsel, if the stirring dead have frightened her enough to send someone. At the lowest ebb, which bares the most steps and strands the slow. Or straight past the garrison, which adds a pursuit layer to everything else.

**Layout.**
1. **The Fever Channels** — the deep backwaters threading out toward the causeway. Reed, heat, smuggler-marks, patrols.
2. **The Tidal Causeway** — the great stepped roadway itself: awash to the knee at ebb, gone at flood.
3. **The Drowned Gate-House** — a half-submerged gate structure on the causeway, where the older dead have gathered.
4. **The Sea-Vault** — a flooded chamber beneath the gate-house: the builders' drowned hoard.
5. **The Far Steps** — the seaward run, descending under deep water. **They end. In open ocean, in ruin, and in nothing. A gate, and an honest one.**

**Hazards.** The rising tide on the steps (**DC 14 Survival** to time it; being caught means stranded or swept); fever exposure in the channels (**DC 14 Con**, disease or exhaustion); getting lost in the backwaters (**DC 15 Survival** — a smuggler or marsh guide negates it); slick steps (**DC 13 Dex**); the flooded vault lock (**DC 16**); garrison patrols, best evaded or bribed.

**Inhabitants.** Older-fall drowned dead on the causeway — **Wraith-like**, AC 13, ~40–56 HP, bound to the stone, rite- and parley-able. A vault-bound drowned guardian (AC 13, ~64 HP), which a closing reverence settles. Living: smugglers (allies for a price) and imperial patrols (**Guard/Veteran-like**, and much better avoided than fought — arresting you is the least of what they can do).

**Salvage.** Pre-Concord valuables and old glass from the sea-vault; a possible tide-warded minor item; the gratitude of the delta-folk or their smugglers, either of which buys you a base in a region that has none for outsiders.

**Getting Out.** Reedmouth and the delta camps are havens; **the tide self-limits the visit**; the dead are bound to the causeway. Leave the steps and you have left them.
