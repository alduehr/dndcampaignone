# The Whispering Cairn

**Type** Concord archive-cairn · **Region** on the south road between Candlewick and Hollowmere · **Level** 1–3 · **Danger** low (a reading site, not a fight)

**What It Is.** One of the archive-cairns: a squat, turf-grown stone mound built to hold Concord records. It sits on firm road-ground and so has never flooded, which is why it is unusually intact. Scholars come from as far as Caradril to make rubbings of the walls, and the Candlewick folk have long since stopped finding this strange.

**Approach and Entrance.** A star-carved lintel half-buried in turf, open and obvious. Any Candlewick villager will walk a stranger to it. There is no lock on the outer door and never was.

**Layout.**
1. **The Lintel Hall** — the entry passage. Walls dense with inscription, floor to ceiling. Dry, level, safe.
2. **The Tally Chamber** — the main vault. One entire wall is carved edge to edge in columns — names, by the look of them, with numbers beside each. Stone record-coffers line the others, most already emptied.
3. **The Collapsed Annex** — a side room half fallen in. Loose salvage, and animals get in.
4. **The Sealed Niche** — a star-marked recess closed by a Concord lock-glyph. It holds one intact record-relic. The glyph is a genuine puzzle, not a trap.

**Hazards.**

| Hazard | Check | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Unstable annex vault | DC 13 Perception; DC 13 Dex save | 1d6 falling stone, blocks the annex — never the exit |
| The lock-glyph | Read the star-sequence in Concord Script, or DC 14 Investigation from a rubbing | Solved: opens clean. Forced (DC 15 Thieves' Tools or Strength): the niche's warding dead manifests once |
| Relic-exposure | DC 14 Con if the niche relic is carried bare | Thin-touch |

**Inhabitants.** Nothing by default. The Collapsed Annex may hold 1–2 scavengers (**Bandit-like** or **Scout-like**, CR 1/8; see [`../04_bestiary/HUMAN_HAZARDS.md`](../bestiary/human-hazards.md)) or a fen predator, telegraphed by spoor. Forcing the niche wakes a single warding lingering dead — **Specter-like**, AC 12, ~17 HP — settled by folk rite **DC 13**, and avoidable entirely by solving the glyph.

The real pressure at this site is **other people**: Concord Remnant scholars and Cinder Ledger salvagers both want the cairn, and either may arrive mid-visit with carts and a claim. Handle it socially, hide the find, or leave early with a partial rubbing.

**Salvage.** Concord metal and fittings from the coffers and annex, ~30 gp. The niche record-relic. **The rubbings themselves** are the actual prize — portable, and scholars pay well for a clean one.

**Getting Out.** Open road-side exit throughout. Rests are safe inside if no rivals are inbound; the cairn is dry and easily defended.
