# The Signal-Cairn

**Type** buried Concord station · **Region** high in the Sunder Heights above Harrowgast · **Level** 7–10 · **Danger** high (telegraphed)

**What It Is.** A sealed Concord vault high in the cold Heights, past the worked adits and the Roofless Hall. Whoever named it thought it looked like a signal-station; scholars have argued about that for eighty years and settled nothing. What is not in dispute: it is large, it is deep, its walls carry the densest run of Concord inscription anyone in the north has found, and the **Ashen Wardens** put their own seal on the door at some point after the Quietfall and have kept it shut since.

**Approach and Entrance.** Up through Harrowgast's workings and out along the high ground. The adit ends at a bronze-and-stone door graven with star-marks, with the Wardens' later seal laid over the Concord work. The cold coming through it is the first warning; the second is that the Wardens will tell you plainly not to.

Concord Remnant scholars have been trying to open it for two seasons and may hold the approach when you arrive.

**Layout.**
1. **The Sealed Approach** — the adit's end and the door. The scholars' forward camp.
2. **The Upper Hall** — beyond the door: a vaulted chamber, inscription floor to ceiling, one or two drifting dead who have been here a long time.
3. **The Humming Chamber** — the station's centre: a close-set ring of standing Concord stones, faintly warm to the hand and faintly audible. A guardian stands in it.
4. **The Inner Vault** — a sealed chamber behind a harder seal. Its walls are covered edge to edge in a single continuous inscription. Scholars come from across the continent for a rubbing of it.
5. **The Deep Shaft** — a sealed shaft descending. It is stated plainly here so the DM can state it plainly at the table: **it goes down past anything survivable, and it stays shut.** A gate, not a puzzle.

**Hazards.**

| Hazard | Check | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| The Warden seal (outer door) | Concord Script, or DC 14 Investigation with a rubbing | Sealed. A Warden's leave opens it cleanly. Forcing it (DC 17–18) wakes the guardian hostile |
| The inner vault seal | Concord Script DC 16, or DC 15 Investigation with a rubbing | Harder version of the same |
| Relic-exposure | DC 14 Con (upper hall), DC 15 (humming chamber, inner vault) | Thin-touch |
| Collapsing Concord stone | DC 14 Perception / Dex save | 3d6 and a blocked path; reroute |
| Heights cold | DC 13 Con | Exhaustion; shelter and warm gear negate |
| The deep shaft | — | Do not. Telegraph it as a gate |

**Inhabitants.** One or two lingering dead in the Upper Hall — **Wraith-like**, AC 13, ~36–45 HP, parley- or rite-able (**DC 15**), bound to the hall.

**The guardian** stands in the Humming Chamber: a Concord construct of stone and cold light, roughly humanoid, that turns its head when you enter and speaks. Use a **Stone Golem-like** profile scaled down — AC 17, ~95–120 HP at levels 8–10, resistant to nonmagical damage, a slam, a ranged force/necrotic burst on a recharge, and a relic-exposure aura within 10 ft. It is **a posted sentry, not a berserker**: it issues a challenge and waits for the correct response.

- **Answer it.** Read the challenge and speak the response (**Concord Script DC 15**). It stands aside and you may read the whole vault at leisure. This is the intended solution.
- **A Warden's leave.** A senior Ashen Warden can stand it down (**DC 14**, faction standing).
- **Fight it.** Hard but winnable at tier 3. It is **bound to the chamber** and does not pursue; retreat up the adit always works. Its morale condition is simple — it stops when you leave or when you say the right thing.

The Concord Remnant crew is the other obstacle, and the more negotiable one.

**Salvage.** A rubbing of the inner vault (the prize, and portable). Concord salvage and one high-grade relic — handle with care. A Concord script primer, which makes every future ruin easier to read. Whichever faction you did *not* sell the rubbing to will remember it.

**Getting Out.** The adit out is always open and everything here is bound. **No safe rest inside.** Harrowgast is the recovery point.
