# The Great Grove At Sunhollow

**Type** living sacred grove · **Region** the warm Sunmark Wilds, at Sunhollow · **Level** 8–13 · **Danger** moderate–high; almost none of it combat

**What It Is.** Not a ruin. A vast, lush, living holy grove, tended by grove-tribes who have kept the same faith for longer than the Concord existed and have never had much to do with it. Their burial rites are famously effective — their dead rest, reliably, and travelers who have come from the troubled north find that fact genuinely startling. The grove is a green sanctuary, and the tension in it is not undead but **people who want to take something from it**.

**Approach and Entrance.** By the grove-keeper's leave (the proper way — earn it with humility and a service). With a grove-singer as guide (the deepest access). As a pilgrim, along the Green Roads. Or by cutting your way in, which turns the wardens, the wilds, and the tribes against you at once.

**Layout.**
1. **The Green Roads** — pilgrim-and-trade paths through warm wilds toward the camps. Easy to get lost in, and cutters' camps at the edges.
2. **The Grove-Camps** — the tribes' settlements at the sacred forest's edge. Warm, peaceable, watchful of takers.
3. **The Sacred Grove** — the living heart: enormous trees, grove-shrines, and the tribes' dead lying quiet among them.
4. **The Heart-Tree** — the oldest tree, and the honored dead around it. Nothing here is hostile. That is the strange part.

**Hazards.** Getting lost in the lush forest (**DC 14 Survival** — a grove-guide negates it); wild beasts on the approach (avoidable); and **giving offense**, which is the actual danger. Cutting, taking, or trespassing turns the wardens out and gets you barred, and the tribes have long memories.

**Inhabitants.** Grove-wardens — **Druid/Scout-like**, AC 14–15, ~45–70 HP, with nature magic. **They warn, then ward, then escort you out.** They do not pursue and they do not kill if they can help it. The grove's dead are *at peace* and are not an encounter. The optional fight here is a moral one: logging crews, relic-hunters, or a zealot band who have come to take the grove's secret, and whom the tribes cannot fight alone.

**Salvage.** The grove-keepers' friendship and a grove-blessing or charm (a possible minor item). A safe southern base, if you defend the place. The living rite itself, shared only with the deeply trusted (**DC 16 Religion or Insight** and a great deal of earned goodwill), which is a genuine piece of practical knowledge worth carrying north.

**Getting Out.** The Grove-Camps are havens. There is nothing at the heart to flee from. This is the one site in the file where the correct play is to sit down and stay a while.
