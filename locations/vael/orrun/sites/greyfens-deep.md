# The Greyfens Deep

**Type** environmental site (the fen as a destination) · **Region** the deep Greyfens · **Level** 3–7 · **Danger** moderate, scaling — terrain, not teeth

**What It Is.** The interior of the Greyfens treated as a place you go *into* rather than through. The fen itself is the site: no walls, no doors, no floor plan — reed-walls taller than a rider, black water, fog that eats sound, and firm ground that is discontinuous and lies about itself.

**For the fen's full terrain, named features, hazards, fauna, and salvage, use [`../01_geography/wilderness/GREYFENS.md`](../wilderness/greyfens.md).** This entry only adds what running it as a delve requires.

**Layout (as a route, not a map).** A trip into the deep fen strings the named features together, typically in this order: the **Standing Dead** shallows → the **Lantern Maze** → the **Bone Weir** → the **Drowned Blind** and **Still Pool** → the deep mire → the **Barrow of Nine Doors** at the far end, which is where a fen expedition usually climaxes.

**Hazards.** Sinking ground (DC 13–14 Survival / DC 15 Athletics, staged), getting lost (DC 14–15 Survival, cumulative), false lights (DC 13 Wis), deep water and cold (DC 13 Athletics / DC 12 Con), foul air at the Weir (DC 12 Con). All per `GREYFENS.md`. **A hired fen-guide negates most of it, and that is the intended solution** — the fen is a preparation problem, not a combat one.

**Inhabitants.** Gathered lingering dead in unusual numbers, especially at the Standing Dead — **Specter-like**, AC 12, ~17–22 HP, **not hostile**, settled en masse by a folk rite (**DC 14**). A provoked gathering becomes a genuine swarm and is telegraphed before it does. Wrathful ones (**Wraith-like**, AC 13, ~36 HP) only if the fen is treated badly.

People: a **Gravecallers** cell shelters somewhere in the deep fen and moves its camp. They want witnesses more than they want a fight; treat first contact as a conversation. Smugglers work the channels. A guide or two live out here on purpose. Fen predators per `GREYFENS.md`.

**Salvage.** Drowned grave-goods and gear at the Bone Weir (60–100 gp of it, and grim). One minor relic. A smuggler's cache if you find it. Fen herbs (**DC 13 Survival**).

**Getting Out.** Follow the marked stakes, or follow running water downstream — it reaches the river or a village eventually. **No safe long rest in the open fen.** Greywater Holm and the guides' huts are the recovery points.
