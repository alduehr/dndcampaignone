# The Reliquary Vaults

**Type** great scholarly archive and relic strongroom · **Region** beneath the Reliquary, Glassmere · **Level** 9–13 · **Danger** moderate–high; social and legal

**What It Is.** The **Concord Remnant's** grand chapter-house in the League's greatest river-city, and the vaults under it. The public college above is a genuine institution — reading rooms, displays, lecture halls. The lower stacks are ward-locked, and the deepest strongroom holds the largest collection of Concord relics assembled anywhere. It is not a dungeon. It is a very well-defended library with a bank vault at the bottom.

**Approach and Entrance.** As a guest scholar (earn a lector's leave — slow, safe, limited). By a rival banker's key (Glassmere's banking houses feud, and one of them will fund a nuisance). By buying the layout and the ward-schedule from the city's information trade. Or by stealth, which is the riskiest and richest.

**Layout.**
1. **The Reliquary** — the public chapter-house. Scholar-guards, cover problems, and the sheer scale of the collection as your first real impression.
2. **The Reading-Vaults** — restricted lower stacks; ward-locked Concord records, and a scholar who notices things.
3. **The Relic-Vault** — the deep strongroom. Massed relics, and a guardian standing in the middle of them.
4. **The Lector's Study** — the chapter head's private room: working papers, correspondence, and the chapter's own theories about what they are collecting.
5. **The Lost Stacks** — the uncatalogued deepest shelves. Ward-trapped and rich.

**Hazards.** Ward-glyph locks (**Concord Script or DC 15 Investigation**; failure is an alarm, not a blast in the face); the relic-vault's massed relic-exposure (**DC 14 Con**); cover failure (Deception vs. scholarly Insight — these are perceptive people); the city's own intrigue, which cross-pressures anyone poking at the chapter.

**Inhabitants.** Scholar-guards and chapter agents — **Spy/Mage-like**, AC 13–14, ~40–60 HP, with hold person, counterspell, and a relic one-off at high tier. **They warn, control, and eject**; nobody kills in their own chapter-house. One Concord guardian-construct in the relic-vault (see above — the watchword stands it down).

**Salvage.** Concord relics and rare salvaged records (tier-3 value); a scholarly minor item; the chapter's correspondence, which is leverage; and standing with either the chapter or one of its rivals, depending on how you played it.

**Getting Out.** The city is the retreat — step out into Glassmere at any point. The guardian is bound to the strongroom. Failure costs you an alarm, an ejection, and a suspicious institution — not a life.
