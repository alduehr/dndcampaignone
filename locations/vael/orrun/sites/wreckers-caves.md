# The Wreckers' Caves

**Type** tidal sea-cave system · **Region** under the headland at Cobble Strand · **Level** 3–5 · **Danger** moderate; the tide is the killer

**What It Is.** A maze of sea-caves under the Drowned Lamp headland, dry at low water and drowned at high. Wreckers stash plunder here. Smugglers move relics through. And the deepest cave is a place where the drowned dead answer readily, so the grieving find their way down to it — which is why the **Gravecallers** run a current out of it.

**Approach and Entrance.** A shingle cove and a low cave-mouth, passable only at low-to-mid tide. The village warns strangers plainly. There is a lookout posted.

**Layout.**
1. **The Cove Mouth** — the lookout and the tide-gate.
2. **The Plunder Cave** — the wreckers' stash: ship-goods, and crated sea-relics labelled for a buyer up the coast.
3. **The Wreckers' Hall** — the gang's camp. Their leader and their salvage-ledger.
4. **The Tide-Maze** — flooding side-caves. The navigation problem, and a haunting.
5. **The Listening Pool** — the deepest chamber, where the Gravecaller current meets.

**Hazards.**

| Hazard | Check | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **The tide (core)** | DC 14 Survival to time entry and exit | A missed tide traps you in a flooding cave: DC 13 Athletics to reach high ground, DC 13 Con for cold immersion, then wait it out or risk drowning. Telegraphed by the tide-table and the wet walls |
| Slick rock and deep pools | DC 13 Acrobatics/Athletics | Falls into cold water |
| Wreckers' alarm | DC 13 Perception to spot / DC 13 Stealth to pass | Brings the gang |
| Relic-exposure | DC 14 Con near the crated relics | Thin-touch |

**Inhabitants.** Living, mostly. A lookout and 2–4 wreckers (**Bandit/Thug-like**, AC 12–13, ~16–32 HP) who prefer threats and ransom and break on morale. Their leader in the Hall (**Veteran/Spy-like**, AC 15, ~45 HP) fights only cornered, surrenders or flees on morale, and can be turned — the wrecking pays badly and the trade is turning against them.

In the Tide-Maze, one drowned lingering dead (**Specter-like**, AC 12, ~26 HP), sorrowful, settled by the coast's salt-and-tide rite (**DC 13 Religion**). At the Listening Pool, the Gravecaller current: a speaker (**Acolyte/Cult Fanatic-like**), one or two recruits, and usually a grieving local who should not be there. Parley first; they are not an ambush.

**Salvage.** The salvage-ledger — sea-relics logged by which wreck they came off, and where they were sent. Leverage, and the best thing in the caves. Crated relics. The wreckers' plunder, ~120 gp of ship-goods and coin, some of which belongs to the families of people who drowned. A drowned one's keepsake, which has an owner.

**Getting Out.** At the right tide, out the cove and up the cliff to Cobble Strand. The tide limits pursuit as much as it limits you. Caught by wreckers: capture and ransom. Caught by the tide: high ground and patience. **No rest in the caves.**
