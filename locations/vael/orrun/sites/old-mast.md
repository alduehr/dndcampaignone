# The Old Mast

**Type** deep-wood primeval stand · **Region** the Tollwood's forbidden heart · **Level** 6–10 · **Danger** high, gated — the wood itself

**What It Is.** The oldest stand in the Tollwood, and the reason the Concord's east road bends where it bends. There is no ruin here and no built thing at all: only trees that get older, darker, and more attentive the further in you go, and a presence beneath them that has been asleep a long time and is not entirely asleep now. The woodfolk keep rules about this place. The Gravecallers will not come here.

**Approach and Entrance.** There is no door. You walk in past the last offering-marks and keep walking. **This site telegraphs harder than anything else in the region:** the marks stop, the guide stops, the dread mounts in stages, and the wood actively turns people around. A low-level intrusion is signposted as suicidal, and should be described that way at the table.

**Layout.**
1. **The Failing Path** — past the edge. The last marks. The wood begins misleading you (**DC 16 Survival** to hold direction).
2. **The Watcher-Stands** — stands of very old trees and the things that keep them, which test intruders and turn them back rather than kill them.
3. **The Still Heart** — the deepest grove: silent, timeless, and aware of you. Where the encounter happens.
4. **The Root-Dark** — a sense of enormous depth under the Still Heart. **The wood does not allow it and no route exists.** A player who insists is not killed; they are lost, marked, and eventually turned out of the wood a long way from where they went in.

**Hazards.**

| Hazard | Check | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| The misleading wood | DC 16 Survival per leg, DC 18 near the Still Heart | Strands you deeper; cumulative. **A guide cannot help past the edge** |
| Cumulative dread | DC 14 Wis, rising to DC 16 | Frightened; on repeated failure, compelled to leave — which is the wood pushing you out alive |
| The watchers' tests | — | Entangle, fear, displacement. Non-lethal by default; they mean to turn you back |
| The price of an answer | — | A memory, a true offering, or a binding promise. Never a cheap death |

**Inhabitants.** The **watchers** at the stands: use an **Awakened Tree-like** or **Dryad-like** profile scaled up — AC 14, ~60 HP, entangle, frightful presence, slam. They warn, test, and turn people back, and fight only the violent and the stupid. Retreating ends the encounter every time.

At the Still Heart there is **nothing to kill**. The climax is an ancient awareness taking your measure and deciding whether to grant you passage, an answer, or neither. Reverence, woodcraft, and honest parley resolve it (**DC 16**). Force fails outright and gets you lost.

**Salvage.** **Nothing you can safely take.** The Old Mast does not give loot and taking from it is the gravest taboo the woodfolk have. What it can give, to someone who earns it, is favor: safe passage through the deep wood afterward, a rite-blessing, or the guidance of one of its old dead.

**Getting Out.** Following the dread outward leads out. Accepting the watchers' turn-back leads out. An offering on the way out atones. The whole design of this place is a survivable warning — but pressing past every warning has real consequences, and one of them is the wood waking up properly, which is a regional disaster.
