# The Deep Adit

**Type** deep Concord delving beneath a mine · **Region** Sunder Heights, below Harrowgast · **Level** 3–5 · **Danger** moderate (bad air, collapse, relic-exposure, the dead)

**What It Is.** The deepest and oldest working under Harrowgast: a Concord tunnel driven far below where the ore is, for reasons no mining company has ever established. Miners call it bad rock and stop at a line they will not cross. Below that line the tunnel becomes something built rather than dug — dressed stone, star-marks, and seams of grey **old glass** running through the walls, which is the reason anybody goes down at all.

**Approach and Entrance.** Through the worked upper adits from Harrowgast, down to the barred **Deep Mouth**. The fear-line is marked by an offering-coin on a ledge and a cold draught flowing *out* of the rock, which is backwards for a mine and which every miner will point out to you.

Concord Remnant scholars currently hold a forward camp at the fear-line and are digging toward the deep chamber. Getting past them is a social or stealth problem before the site proper begins.

**Layout.**
1. **The Fear-Line** — where the miners stop. Cold; the rock carries sound strangely. The scholars' camp.
2. **The Worked Drift** — the last dug tunnel. Tool-marks, abandoned gear, stress-cracks in the roof, and a body nobody brought up.
3. **The Glass Seam** — where the old glass is embedded in living rock. Prying it loose is lucrative and stupid in roughly equal measure.
4. **The Deep Chamber** — the Concord structure the whole delving evidently exists to reach: a vaulted room of fitted stone, inscription on every surface, and a cold that gets into the teeth.
5. **The Drowned Sump** — a flooded shaft going further down. Nobody has bottomed it. Treat as scenery, not a route.

**Hazards.**

| Hazard | Check | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| The talking rock | DC 13 Wis save on entry and at the Deep Chamber | Unnerved: disadvantage on the next save against fear |
| Relic-exposure | DC 14 Con at the Glass Seam or Deep Chamber, or when prying relics | Thin-touch |
| Cave-in (Worked Drift) | DC 14 Perception to spot stress-cracks; DC 13 Dex save | 3d6 bludgeoning and a blocked passage — an alternate drift always exists |
| Dark and cold | — | Standard deep-mine conditions; light is mandatory |

**Inhabitants.** At the fear-line, a scholars' camp: a lead researcher (**Scout/Mage-lite**, AC 13, ~30 HP) and two hired bravos (**Thug/Scout-like**, AC 13–14, ~16–30 HP). They would far rather hire, bribe, or warn off a newcomer than fight one.

In the Worked Drift, possibly a single cold miner's shade (**Specter/Wraith-like**, AC 13, ~30 HP). In the Deep Chamber, one or two older and angrier dead keep the room — **Wraith-like**, AC 13, ~36 HP each, life-drain touch (~2d6 necrotic, recharge), able to move through stone but **bound to the chamber**; the player can always back out of range. Non-combat outs: a full folk rite (**DC 15 Religion**) settles them, and reading the inscription aloud correctly (**Concord Script, DC 15**) makes them stand down and quiets the room.

**Salvage.** Old glass, in quantity, at real risk — the Cinder Ledger and the Concord Remnant both pay heavily and neither asks how you got it. Concord tools and metal. A clean **rubbing** of the deep chamber's inscription, which is the only thing here you can carry out without gloves.

**Getting Out.** Open tunnel back to the worked adits throughout, barring a cave-in (which has an alternate route). **No safe rest below the fear-line.** Short rest in the Worked Drift; long rest only back in Harrowgast, which has no healer worth the name — supply matters here.
