# Ashfast

the fortress-temple-city and seat of the faith (15,000–25,000). A wall rings the temple-city; the pilgrim gate admits the ash roads and the cinder gate faces the mining country. Inside: the great fortress-temple on the volcanic rise, its archives beside it, ritual pyre-grounds, warden posts around the perimeter, and a **permitted market** that is the only sanctioned commerce — obsidian goods, ash-metals, local produce, and nothing foreign without approval. Pilgrims are housed free in a monitored state waystation; recognised envoys get guest-cells in the temple complex. Healing is available through the fire-rites, to the faithful and to those holding safe-conduct.
