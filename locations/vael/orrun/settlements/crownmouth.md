# Crownmouth

a fortified scavenger-town (~700 people) at the ruin's edge, the only functioning settlement for a very long way. Walls face the supply road; the ruin-edge gate faces the other way and is controlled. Inside: a war-captain's hall that is the real government, a large pilgrim-quarter of rough lodging for relic-seekers, a salvage market selling rope, tools, provisions and buying whatever comes out of the ruin, and a healer's row that is better than the town's size deserves. Entry requires stating your business. Going through the ruin-edge gate without a guide is permitted and strongly discouraged.
