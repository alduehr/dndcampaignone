# The Great Grove at Sunhollow

the faith's holy heart and gathering-place (150–250 grove-keepers in residence; a thousand or more at major rites). An open grove, not a walled town: a great old-growth heart-tree at the centre, a gathering-ground where travellers first meet the keepers, a sacred spring-fed pool where healing rites are performed, the grove-keeper's hut serving as the faith's administrative hub, a storytellers' grove where the seers keep their fire, and a welcoming-gate of stones where the pilgrim paths arrive. Shelter and healing are freely given to respectful visitors; food and herbs are traded; no weapons or armour are sold, made, or particularly welcome.
