# Brask's Hold

the strongest warlord's fortress-town (~600), compact on a mountain shoulder: an iron-reinforced gate where every entrant is inspected and tolled, the warlord's hall, barracks holding fifty or more hired fighters at all times, ore-yards where mine output is staged and auctioned, and a market row selling weapons, tools, cold-weather gear, and rations — no luxuries, no magic.
