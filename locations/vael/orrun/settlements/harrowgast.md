# Harrowgast

**Mining village · ~400 people · the northern highland**

### First Impression
The road climbs out of the fens into bare grey highland, and Harrowgast appears clinging to a scree slope — slate roofs, spoil-heaps, and the black square mouths of old adits cut into the mountain by hands long dead. Half the town is ruins the living have re-roofed; the other half is ruins nobody will touch. The wind never stops. The folk are lean, soot-marked, and superstitious about the deep tunnels.

### Public Overview
Harrowgast was a Concord mining-delving before the collapse and a salvage town after. Its people work the abandoned upper tunnels for old stone, metal, and "old glass" — a local term for the strange glassy salvage the deep rock yields — which city buyers pay well for. The compact barely reaches here; a single overseer keeps nominal order. It is poor, hard, cold, and lawless at the edges, and it is the only place in the region where the Concord's works stand open instead of drowned.

### Districts and Notable Areas
- **The Living Half** — re-roofed Concord buildings where people actually dwell.
- **The Spoil-Heaps** — where salvage is sorted, and where the buyers keep their weigh-post.
- **The Open Adits** — the worked upper tunnels; ongoing salvage-digging.
- **The Stilled Quarter** — Concord ruins nobody will re-roof. Outside crews camp here anyway.
- **The Deep Mouth** — the barred entrance to the lower workings. Coins are left on the lintel.

### Leadership
An **overseer** of the Compact, in practice a former miner keeping order with his fists and a short rope. Beyond his authority, the miners' own code governs: theft from a dig is the one crime everyone agrees on.

### Shops and Services
**The Cold Drum** — rough tavern and bunkhouse (about 4 sp, watered ale, hot stew, occasional knife-fights). The weigh-post buys salvage and sells tools, rope, lamp-oil and cold-weather gear at a markup. A blacksmith-toolwright. No temple and no healer beyond a bonesetter. Mining and climbing gear here is the best in the region. Guides into the upper adits can be hired, expensively.

### Laws and Customs
Compact law in name; the overseer's word in fact. Never whistle in the deep tunnels. Leave a coin at the Deep Mouth. Outsiders are tolerated if they can work or pay, and disliked if they ask too many questions about how far down the workings go.

### Local Colour
Sorters picking through spoil by lamplight; rival buyers shoving each other at the weigh-post over a crate; a dig-boss losing crews because his men will not go past a certain level and will not explain.
