# Calderport

the greatest Gulf port (30,000–50,000), built around a crescent harbour: a working docks district, an overseas traders' **Foreign Quarter**, free-port markets, privateer and naval wharves, and a walled upper city behind a customs gate where the port-mistress sits. Warm, crowded, polyglot, and rich. Services: sailors' inns at every grade, ship's stores, overseas goods and exotic materials, a sea-temple of the Three Thresholds with paid healing, coastal Mourners who tend the drowned, and a harbour-master who sells shipping intelligence. Rough at the docks, orderly above the customs wall.
