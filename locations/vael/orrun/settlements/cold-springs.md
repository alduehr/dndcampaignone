# The Winter-Camp at Cold Springs

the largest cold-season gathering (400–800 during the season, a fraction of that otherwise), a great felt-tent camp around a cluster of permanent springs. The clan-speaker's tent is the political centre; the horse-lines are the heart of steppe life; a seasonal trade-ring serves as the steppe's market; and a permanent stone circle serves the clans' seers. Guest-right is absolute and taken with total seriousness — a guest is safe, fed, and not charged, and reciprocity is expected rather than demanded.
