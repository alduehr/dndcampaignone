# Hethemoot

the great gathering free-hold (300–500 permanent, up to 2,000 in full moot). Not a dense town but a large forest clearing: the speaker's permanent moot-hall at the centre, his longhouse beside it, an open moot-circle for full gatherings, a small permanent trade-ground selling forest goods, timber, furs and hunting tools, and forest gates at the edges where outsiders are scrutinised on arrival. Lodging at the trade-ground is symbolic in price and conditional on guest protocol.
