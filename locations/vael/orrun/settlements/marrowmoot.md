# Marrowmoot

the shire-town where the moots gather (~600, swelling past 1,500 on moot-days). An open chalk-marked moot-ground at the centre, a Mourner's hall that is the moral centre of the downs, a substantial wool-market, a shire-reeve's circuit office handling law and land-disputes, and **the Drover's Inn** (about 2 sp) — warm, open, and generous with news.
