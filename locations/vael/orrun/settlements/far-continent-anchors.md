# Far Continent — Settlement Anchors Index

## AI Use

Load when play moves beyond the northwestern frontier and its city, or when a traveller, merchant, or scholar refers to somewhere far away. These are **light anchors**, not deep-built settlements: enough for a scene, a route, a market, a night's lodging, or a plausible answer to "what is it like there?" Deliberately lighter than the northwestern gazetteers. Public-facing only; offices are named, officeholders are not.

## How to Use These

Orrun is large — corner to corner is two to three months' travel by road. The northwest is a cold, poor, ruin-strewn frontier; the settled centre and south are warmer, richer, and older. Everything below is at least weeks from the frontier, and most of it is a season away. Distances should feel real: reaching any of these places is a journey with a plot of its own.

Institutions recur across the continent in local forms: the folk death-faith the northwest calls the **Mourners' Circle** (present everywhere, under many local titles — salt-mothers, fen-witches, moot-mothers, sun-singers); the **Three Thresholds** temples (formal, urban, respectable); the big chartered **merchant-banks** and trade leagues, of which the northwest's **Cinder Ledger** is only one arm; and the scholarly societies that study the fallen Concord, the largest of which is the **Concord Remnant**. Concord ruins are found on every part of the continent and are uniformly old, broken, and unexplained.

## The Verdance Reaches — the river corridor inland

The long river-and-lock corridor that carries trade between the northwestern city and the central river-cities. Prosperous, watched, and heavily regulated: permits, checkpoints, tolls, and barge-traffic.

- **The Nine Locks** — the great lock-staircase itself, a landmark and a route node; an impressive piece of engineering with older stonework beneath it.

- **Marrowfen Stair** — see `marrowfen-stair.md`.
- **Lord's Wend** — see `lords-wend.md`.
- **Cresswater** — see `cresswater.md`.

## The Glassmere League — the river-cities of the centre

A confederacy of mercantile river-cities on the Glasswater, and the wealthiest region on the continent. Cosmopolitan, banking-mad, and confident to the point of complacency.

- **Glassmere** — see `glassmere.md`.
- **Sennfort** and **Cairnwater** — see `sennfort.md`, `cairnwater.md`.

## The Concord Heartlands — the ruin'd crown

The broken heart of the old Concord: a vast, unexplained field of imperial ruin, dangerous, picked over, and never fully mapped. There is no government here, only what the living hold.

- **Crownmouth** — see `crownmouth.md`.
- **The Pilgrim Camps** — see `pilgrim-camps.md`.

## The Marrowdowns — the chalk country

Rolling chalk downs, sheep, wool, and old barrows. The most peaceful-looking region on the continent, governed by shire-moots and long custom.

- **The Barrow-Fields** — ancient burial-downs northeast of the shire-town, far older than the Concord. Barrow-robbers camp at the edge and hire out as guides. Local opinion on whether robbing them is a crime or a trade depends entirely on who is asking.

- **Marrowmoot** — see `marrowmoot.md`.
- **Penmark Hold** — see `penmark-hold.md`.
- **Wether** — see `wether.md`.

## The Sallowmarch Protectorate — the fever delta

A hot, flooded river-delta claimed by a distant crown and governed, in practice, by whoever keeps people alive. Boats matter more than roads.

- **The Rice Sallows** — flooded paddy-country, worked by village communes with their own moots and their own opinion of the garrison.
- **The Fever Channels** — the deep delta backwaters. Locals will guide you in. Locals will also tell you not to go.

- **Fenward** — see `fenward.md`.
- **Reedmouth** — see `reedmouth.md`.

## The Hollow Gulf Ports — the southern sea-gate

Rival port city-states on the great southern bay, and the continent's door to the sea and to lands overseas.

- **The Mardenmouth** — the delta-mouth wharves just west of Calderport, where the river trade meets the sea trade.

- **Calderport** — see `calderport.md`.
- **Saltgate** — see `saltgate.md`.

## The Wender Steppe — the grass sea

Cold open grassland north of the settled centre, held by a confederacy of horse-clans. There are no cities; there are gatherings.

- **The Sky-Stones** — an ancient shrine-circle, older than the Concord and unconnected to it; the clans' holiest place and none of an outsider's business.

- **The Winter-Camp at Cold Springs** — see `cold-springs.md`.
- **The Spine-Foot trade-meet** — see `spine-foot.md`.

## The Karran Marches — the iron frontier

Cold mountain country in the northeast: ore, mercenaries, petty warlords, and no government worth the name.

- **The Deep Cuts** — the great ore-mines; a mining-camp complex, days out along a hard road.
- **The Old Iron forts** — scattered Concord ruins high in the mountains. Salvage camps cling to their edges. The weather kills more people out there than anything else does.

- **Brask's Hold** — see `brasks-hold.md`.
- **Karran-Gate** — see `karran-gate.md`.

## The Emberfell Theocracy — the fire-country

A closed, self-sufficient theocracy on the flanks of a volcanic highland. Foreign visitors are unusual, watched, and require permission.

- **The Ash Roads** — the pilgrim-ways up through the highland, lined with fire-shrines and statues, and the only sanctioned approach.

- **Ashfast** — see `ashfast.md`.
- **Cinderhold** — see `cinderhold.md`.

## The Saltmere Reaches — the shrinking inland sea

Clan country around a great inland salt sea that has been slowly retreating for generations, uncovering things.

- **The Drowned Towns** — settlements the lake covered long ago and is now, year by year, giving back. Delvers work them from barges. Locals will tell you plainly that the ruins are not safe and are not always empty.

- **Brackhold** — see `brackhold.md`.
- **Saltcairn** — see `saltcairn.md`.

## The Hethewald Free Holds — the forest confederacy

A confederacy of forest free-holds in the great eastern wood, governed by moot and by a wood's-law that is stricter than it first appears.

- **Tollreach** — an outlaw river-toll camp on the eastern river; not respectable, entirely functional.
- **The Old Holds** — deep-wood Concord ruins the free-holds treat as forbidden. Asking to go there is a serious request, not a casual one.

- **Hethemoot** — see `hethemoot.md`.
- **Greenward** — see `greenward.md`.

## The Sunmark — the sacred groves

Warm southern grove country, held by tribes whose faith is a living one rather than a memorial one. The most hopeful-feeling region on the continent, and the least interested in outside politics.

- **The Green Roads** — pilgrim-and-trade paths through the groves, running north toward the chalk downs and east toward the southern coast.
