# Marrowfen Stair

the corridor's chief working lock-town (~1,000 people), built around a lock-staircase that raises and lowers barges twelve metres. Lower lock-gate crowded with waiting barge-masters, a merchant directorate's hall on the high side, river wharves and cargo warehouses below, an inspection post where permits are checked, and the **Stair Inn** (about 2 sp), where river news is traded at the common table. A small scholars' reading-room keeps maps and records for anyone with a civil manner. Passage through the locks requires a permit, and carrying undeclared salvage through the checkpoint is a reliable way to lose it.
