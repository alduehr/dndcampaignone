# Saltmargin

**Town · ~500 people · the western salt gateway**

### First Impression
The road climbs out of the fens onto a low salt-grass shelf and the air changes — brine instead of peat. Saltmargin is a sprawl of drying-sheds, salt-pans, and tar-black timber halls crusted white at the eaves. Gulls. The constant grind of the salt-mill. Stacked barrels everywhere, each chalked with a buyer's mark. People here squint, bargain hard, and wear knotted salt-grass charms at the throat.

### Public Overview
Saltmargin sits where the coast's salt-carts meet the fen roads. It refines and sells the household salt the whole region uses — for preserving, for the doorsill, for the rites — and the price it sets is felt in every inland village. The town is prosperous, anxious about its own margins, and thoroughly resented by the places that have to buy from it.

### Districts and Notable Areas
- **The Weigh-House** — the scale-hall where the salt rate is set; the town's true seat of power.
- **The Pans** — tidal salt-pans and drying-sheds; hard, wet, badly paid work.
- **The Knotted Hand** — the town's only inn, named for the salt-grass charm.
- **Brine Chapel** — a small salt-blessed shrine of the Mourners' Circle; every shipment is blessed before it leaves.
- **The Tar Wharf** — where coast carts unload. Things also move here after dark.

### Leadership
No reeve of its own. The compact governs at arm's length through a **warden of weights** — a tired, honest office concerned mainly with fraud at the scale — while the Cinder Ledger's wharf-factor sets actual policy through pricing.

### Shops and Services
Lodging at the Knotted Hand (about 5 sp). The weigh-house sells salt in bulk and offers loans. A ropewalk and cooperage, a charm-maker, a hedge-surgeon who mostly treats pan injuries, carts and a guide for the coast road. No temple healer; the chapel offers folk blessing only.

### Laws and Customs
Compact law applies, lightly. Every shipment is salt-blessed before departure; breaking a salt-barrel is bad luck and a finable offence. Smuggling is an open secret that everyone tolerates so long as the customary cut is paid.

### Local Colour
Pan-crews with cracked hands demanding better wages; a chapel sister blessing barrels in the rain; an inn full of carters comparing what the price was last season.
