# Fenward

the garrison-capital (~800), sprawling over raised ground in a wetland basin: a military garrison on the high side, a formal protectorate hall that is rarely used for actual governance, working delta wharves that are the real trade and news centre, and a plague-doctor's compound that is, unofficially, where the region is run from. Lodging is cheap or free for crown-affiliated travellers. Medicine here is genuinely excellent and hard-won.
