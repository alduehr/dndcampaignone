# Saltgate

a rival port further east (~10,000), walled, climbing a rocky headland above a deep-water harbour, with a port-lord's citadel, a busy salt market, a separate wharf where Calderport ships moor apart from everyone else, and a customs gate through which every cargo passes. **The Anchor-House** (about 3 sp) is neutral ground and the best place on that coast for maritime news.
