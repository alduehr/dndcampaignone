# Karran-Gate

the Marches' largest trade-hub (1,200–2,000), sprawling and comparatively open, with an ore exchange, a whole street of competing mercenary companies and their hiring-boards, a way-station keeping records and permits for anyone who wants them, and **the Rough House**, the big tavern-inn where disputes are settled by the crowd.
