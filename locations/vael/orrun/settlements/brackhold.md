# Brackhold

the largest surviving salt-clan hold (~1,000), facing the water: a fortified quay-gate where a clan toll is collected, the matriarch's hall where confederacy politics happen, extensive salt-works of drying pans and scale-houses, a Mourner's hall offering old rites and practical healing, a harbour-mole for clan barges, and a delver-quarter of salvage guides and relic-buyers. A visiting non-kin pays a customary toll and observes clan protocol — greet at the gate, do not interrupt a rite.
