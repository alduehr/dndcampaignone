# Saint Veddow's Rest

**Town · ~700 residents plus pilgrims · the Vale's great pilgrimage**

### First Impression
The pilgrim-track switchbacks up a green hill crowned by an old, beautiful shrine of pale stone — finer and far older than anything in the fen country. Bells, incense, and the murmur of crowds. The town below is small but rich on pilgrim coin: hostels, candle-sellers, relic-stalls hawking saint's-bones and blessed earth, and lines of the faithful climbing barefoot to lay flowers at the tomb.

### Public Overview
The town venerates a Frontier-Age holy woman said to have settled the restless dead with a touch; her hill-tomb is the oldest intact Threshold shrine for a hundred miles and the Vale's great pilgrimage. The Mourners' Circle holds the shrine. Unusually, a resident chapter of the Ashen Wardens is stationed here permanently, guarding the inner tomb and aiding pilgrims — a posting the order does not explain and outsiders find odd. The whole local economy runs on the shrine's reputation.

### Districts and Notable Areas
- **The Shrine Crown** — the pale hill-shrine: pilgrim hall, flower-altar, and the sealed door of the inner tomb, which is not open to visitors.
- **The Warden House** — the resident Ashen Wardens' fortified house beside the shrine; sanctuary, aid, and a great deal of quiet watchfulness.
- **Relic Row** — stalls and shops selling saint's-bones, blessed earth, candles, tokens, and pilgrim gear. Buyer beware.
- **The Pilgrim Hostels** — lodging from bare dormitory to genuinely comfortable; where rumour and confession circulate.
- **The Lower Cells** — quiet rooms where the keepers tend pilgrims whose grief has gone badly. Visitors are not encouraged.

### Leadership
The **shrine-keeper**, an elder of the Mourners' Circle, governs shrine and town by sacred authority. The resident Warden chapter holds independent authority over the inner tomb and answers to its own order rather than to the town — a jurisdictional split that occasionally produces spectacular arguments.

### Shops and Services
Pilgrim hostels from about 2 sp (dormitory) to 8 sp (fine); plentiful and safe. Relic Row sells candles, blessed earth, relics of extremely variable authenticity, and pilgrim supplies. The Warden House offers true rite-aid, sanctuary, and minor healing magic — the best healing in the Vale. The shrine performs funeral rites and grief-blessing.

### Laws and Customs
Sacred law: on the hill, the shrine-keeper's word is final. The inner tomb is forbidden to all but the Ashen Wardens, and forcing it is sacrilege rather than mere trespass. Pilgrims climb the last furlong barefoot, lay a flower rather than a coin at the altar, and speak no ill of the dead on the hill. Relic-selling is technically frowned upon and enormously profitable — a hypocrisy that a sharp visitor will notice within the hour.

### Local Colour
A relic-monger who can produce three different saints' fingerbones before breakfast; a hosteler who has heard every family's story twice; a pilgrim crowd that goes very quiet, all at once, when a bell rings out of turn.
