# Glassmere

the continent's greatest inland city (45,000–65,000), straddling both banks of the Glasswater and joined by three great bridges. Districts: **the Floor** (the merchant exchange, and the largest trading hall anywhere in Orrun), **the Three Bridges** (the banking quarter), a civic quarter of league council halls, a river-port and warehouse district on both banks, a scholars' quarter around a great chapter-house called **the Reliquary**, and **Lowwater**, the poor riverside district — poorer than the equivalent quarter in the northwestern city, and considerably larger. Services are extensive: inns at every price, a grand Three Thresholds temple with proper healing, specialist importers dealing in overseas goods, professional information brokers, and banking on a scale the frontier cannot imagine. The city watch is professional, underfunded, and bribable at the lower ranks.
