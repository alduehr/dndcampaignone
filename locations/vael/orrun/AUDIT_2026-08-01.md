## Scope

All 44 files in `/locations/vael/orrun` (geography, settlements, culture, bestiary, timelines, index, README), audited for internal cohesion and for consistency with `/ai_solo_campaign` canon. Cross-checked against `03_canon/` (including `DM_ONLY_CANON.md`), `04_world_atlas/`, `06_settlements/`, `13_encounters_and_bestiary/`, and `00_control/NAMING_REGISTRY.md`.

## Summary

Healthy. Content quality is high and consistent across all four curation passes, travel times and geography match campaign canon exactly, and the mechanical style follows `RULESET_ASSUMPTIONS.md` throughout. The audit found **four secrecy defects and three cohesion defects**, all now fixed. No unresolved issues remain.

- **Travel times** match campaign canon exactly (Hollowmere→Saltmargin ~5d, →Caradril ~11–12d, →Kettle Bridge ~1d, →Hartfell ~4–5d).
- **Geography** matches `WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md` / `FULL_WORLD_MAP_AUTHORITY.md`: Highmark Spine as parent of the Sunder Heights, the Verdance/Stillwater/Caradril relationship, the NW-to-SE tilt, all named seas/ranges/rivers.
- **Public cosmology** correctly presents the lingering dead as genuinely unexplained, with contradictory folk theories and no authoritative answer.

- **Links:** no broken relative links.

### 2026-08-02 follow-up pass (gaps closed)

The wilderness and dungeon gaps recorded above were closed on 2026-08-02. The method: `GREYFENS.md` was hand-written first as a strict template, then both extractions were run against it with an identical, explicit naming contract — a direct correction of the process error that caused the faction-naming divergence in the original build.

**Extraction rule applied throughout: keep the observation, drop the explanation.** Where a place is eerie for a reason the campaign keeps secret, `/locations/vael/orrun` records everything a traveler could actually perceive and none of the cause. Examples:
- The Greyfens' gathered dead: kept ("they stand in the water in numbers"), dropped (that they face one direction, and why).
- The Tollwood's deepest stand: kept (the road's deliberate bends, thickening offering-marks, cumulative dread saves, and things that come out of the timber and *turn travelers back* — statted Dryad-like/Awakened Tree-like, resolved by retreat not combat), dropped (what they are and why the wood is marked).
- The Concord roads and their wayside stones: kept (safest travel, crumbling, a chill near cracked stones), dropped (what the roads and stones were built to do).
- Relic-exposure: kept as a plain reversible hazard (DC 14–15 Con, disadvantage on death saves, cleared by a DC 13 rite), dropped (the term "thin-touch" and any cause).

**Two sites were dropped entirely rather than sanitized** — `THE_UNDER_SHRINE_APPROACH.md` and `THE_BASIN_KEYSTONE_APPROACH.md`. These are not ruins that happen to contain the campaign's endgame; they *are* it, and no publishable version exists. Confirmed absent from `/locations/vael/orrun` by grep.

Also dropped: 5 campaign index/placeholder files (not sites). Two sites required reframing rather than trimming — the Reach relay-vault is now **The Signal-Cairn**, "a Concord station whose purpose has been argued over for eighty years and settled by nobody."

## Known Non-Issues (documented, not defects)

- **Settlement populations** (~60 to ~22,000) were invented by the curation pass; the campaign specifies none. They are reasonable and internally consistent. Per `README.md`, if campaign canon ever states a population, campaign wins.
- **The Highmark Passes** have no region file (uninhabited barrier country; its campaign map packet is entangled with a DM-only site). Documented in `CONTINENT_OVERVIEW.md` and `TRAVEL_AND_ROUTES.md` instead. Deliberate.
- **`04_bestiary/` links to `ai_solo_campaign/00_control/RULESET_ASSUMPTIONS.md` and `DND_MECHANICS_REQUIREMENTS.md`.** These are pure mechanics-policy files containing no secrets; the cross-link is safe and useful.
- **`LANGUAGES.md` names the Gravecaller Knock** while the settlement pass omitted the group. Not a leak — the Gravecallers' existence and reputation are public canon; only their agenda is secret. The Reach settlement file now mentions them at the same public level for consistency.

## Verdict

**COHESIVE AND CONSISTENT.** `/locations/vael/orrun` is safe to hand to a player of "The Long Remembering," and usable standalone for unrelated games in this world.
