## AI Use

Load for the warm southern ports and anything involving sea travel, overseas trade, or the wider world beyond Orrun. Ashore and at sea are two different regions; treat them separately.

## One-Sentence Identity

A string of great warm-water port city-states ringing the Hollow Gulf — rival merchant harbors, the busiest water on the continent, and Orrun's gateway to lands overseas.

## Geography

The **Hollow Gulf** is a deep, warm bay on Orrun's south coast, where two of the continent's great rivers empty. Its shores are terraced harbor-cities, delta wharves, headland forts, and lighthouse-marked approaches.

Defining features:

- **The crescent harbor** of the region's greatest port, rising in counting-house terraces above the water.
- **A rival headland port** across the Gulf, glowering back at it — the region's defining rivalry expressed in geography.
- **The Mardenmouth wharves** — delta-mouth docks where the great southern river arrives.
- **The Reach Lanes** — the busy coastal sea-lanes linking the Gulf's ports.
- **The wreck-reef** — an offshore shoal-and-wreck ground beyond the harbor approaches; a salvage-ground, and a graveyard.
- Beyond the Gulf's mouth, open sea running to the warm southeastern **Calm Reach** and, weeks further, to lands overseas.

**Borders:** the Sallowmarch delta (west), the warm grove-country (northwest), the Glasswater's course running inland (north), open sea (south and east).

## Climate

Warm, bright, and maritime. Storm season is the year's great punctuation and dictates the sailing calendar; outside it, the Gulf is one of the pleasantest places on the continent. Hurricane-grade weather is rare but not unknown, and the ports build for it.

## Culture And Livelihood

This is the most cosmopolitan corner of Orrun and, notably, the most *normal* — the far northern frontier's troubles register here as a rumor about a poor and distant place. Money, ships, charters, and gossip are the local preoccupations.

Common trades: shipping and shipbuilding, warehousing, wholesale trade in everything the continent produces, chandlery, insurance, privateering (licensed), smuggling (not), and the overseas trade with the far southern continent.

Foreign quarters exist in the larger port — traders from overseas keep houses, temples, and their own customs, tolerated with the practical tolerance of people who like money. Sea-faith here is strong: the drowned are sung down at the tide-line by shore-priests, and no captain will sail on a day the shrines advise against.

**Who holds it:** nobody holds all of it. Each port is its own city-state, run by its port-mistress or port-lord and its merchant houses. The two greatest are open rivals, and the rivalry is conducted in charters, tariffs, spies, and the occasional convenient shipwreck. Licensed privateers work for whoever holds their letter, which changes.

## Settlements

- **Calderport** — the greatest of the Gulf ports; crescent harbor, counting-house terraces, and a **Foreign Quarter** for overseas traders.
- **Saltgate** — the rival port city-state on its own headland across the Gulf.
- **The Mardenmouth** — the delta-mouth wharf-district and its sea-shrines.

## Wilderness Zones

- **The open Gulf and the Reach Lanes** — busy, patrolled in places, and prowled by privateers who are legal until someone decides otherwise.
- **The wreck-reef** — offshore shoals and the drowned quarter of some earlier harbor; salvage-divers work it and lose people doing so.
- **The Gulf-mouth approaches** — lighthouse-marked, current-swept, and unforgiving in a storm.

## Travel Routes

- **The Glasswater Run** — inland north to the great river-cities; three to four weeks upstream.
- **The Reach Lanes** — coastal hops between the Gulf ports; hours to a couple of days by ship.
- **The South Sea Lanes** — overseas to the far southern continent; weeks of open water, sailed only in fair season.
- **The delta road west** into the Sallowmarch.

## Local Name For The Last Telling

Folk here call the darkest of the alternate Tellings **the Echo Realm**. It is the same place other regions call by other names; "the Last Telling" is the scholarly term almost nobody uses aloud. See [`../../05_timelines/THE_LAST_TELLING.md`](../timelines/the-last-telling.md).

## General Danger And Tone

Safe ashore, genuinely dangerous at sea. In the ports a visitor risks theft, litigation, and a bad contract. Out on the water they risk privateers, storms, and the reef.

The region's tone is bustle and sunlight — this is the part of Orrun that is doing fine, and knows it. That contrast is worth playing: news from the northern frontier arrives here months late and is treated as somebody else's weather.
