## One-Sentence Identity

A warm southern land of sacred groves and grove-keeper tribes, whose old nature-faith is still whole and openly kept — the gentlest country in Orrun for anyone who arrives respectfully.

## Geography

The **Sunmark Wilds**: warm, broad-canopied southern forest, open beneath the crown, laced with clearings, springs, and streams. It is not jungle and not northern old-growth — it is a bright, deep, cultivated-feeling wood, more park than wilderness in the parts people use.

Defining features:

- **The Great Grove at Sunhollow** — the faith's living heart: an immense grove around a holy pool, and the gathering-place for the whole region.
- **The Grove-Camps** — dispersed tribal villages under the canopy; the region's actual daily life.
- **The Green Roads** — carved-stone pilgrim-paths linking the groves, kept clear and marked; a working network rather than a monument.
- **Sacred springs** — every major grove has one, and travelers are welcome to drink.
- **The warm coast-edge** on the southeastern sea.

**Borders:** the Marrowdowns (north), the Hollow Gulf's port-country (east along the coast), open southern plains (west), the warm sea (south).

## Climate

Warm and wet, with a distinct rainy season and a long green summer. Frost is rare. The canopy keeps the forest floor cool and shaded even in the hottest months, which is why almost everyone in the region lives under it.

## Culture And Livelihood

The grove-tribes keep an old nature-faith that never lapsed: seasonal observances, grove-tending, formal welcomes, and a set of death-rites conducted at the groves that the tribes regard as simply how it is done. It is not a secretive faith — outsiders may attend, if invited — but it is a *reverent* one, and reverence is the price of admission to almost everything here.

Common trades: forest gardening, fruit and honey, hardwood and resin (carefully, and only where sanctioned), herbcraft, and guiding pilgrims.

Hospitality is close to absolute. A traveler on the Green Roads who observes the forms — greet the grove, ask before taking, do not cut what is marked — will be fed, housed, and pointed on their way by strangers. A traveler who does not will find the region closing around them with startling speed.

**Who holds it:** the grove-tribes themselves, through their **grove-keepers**. The faith's heart at Sunhollow is tended by its own senior grove-keeper; a **greenway-warden** keeps the Green Roads safe for respectful travelers. There is no crown, no council, and no standing army — only custom, which is enough.

## Settlements

- **The Great Grove at Sunhollow** — the holy gathering-heart. A sacred place rather than a town, though thousands come for the great observances.
- **The Grove-Camps** — the dispersed grove-tribe villages; where people actually live, work, and trade.
- **The Green Roads** — the stone-marked route system between groves; not a settlement, but the region's connective tissue and the place most visitors spend their time.

## Wilderness Zones

- **The deep Wilds off the Green Roads** — trackless but not hostile; easy to get lost in, hard to get hurt in if you behave.
- **The coast-edge** — warm beaches, river-mouths, and small-boat landings.
- **The northern transition** — where forest thins toward the chalk downs; the region's main land gateway.

## Travel Routes

- **The Green Roads** — days on foot between groves, stone-marked, and safe for the respectful.
- **The north road to the Marrowdowns** — the main overland link to the settled interior.
- **The coast track east** into the Hollow Gulf's port-world.
- **Small-boat landings** on the southern coast for anyone arriving by sea.

## Local Name For The Last Telling

Folk here call the darkest of the alternate Tellings **the Waning**. It is the same place other regions call by other names; "the Last Telling" is the scholarly term almost nobody uses aloud. See [`../../05_timelines/THE_LAST_TELLING.md`](../../05_timelines/THE_LAST_TELLING.md).

## General Danger And Tone

The lowest-danger region on the continent, and it should feel that way — this is where a traveler genuinely rests. Weather is kind, food is plentiful, and the people are welcoming.

What danger exists is almost entirely imported: poachers, illicit cutters, and outside traders pressing at the edges of holy ground. The tribes handle such people themselves, and the results are not always gentle. The one warning worth heeding is the standing one about defiled ground — take from a marked grove and you will discover that the region's calm has limits.
