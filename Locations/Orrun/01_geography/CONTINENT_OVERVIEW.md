## The World At A Glance

**Vael** is the world. **Orrun** is its best-known continent and the setting of nearly everything described in this library.

Orrun is a **large continent** — two to three months corner-to-corner by road in fair season, far longer on foot through wild country. Most people live and die within a few regions of their birth, and "abroad" for a villager means the next valley.

The continent's dominant axis runs **northwest to southeast**. The cold, broken, ruin-strewn frontier sits in the **northwest**; the land warms, lowers, and grows richer and more settled toward the **south and southeast**, where great rivers empty into a warm sea. Orrun "tilts" from cold-and-poor in the northwest to warm-and-wealthy in the southeast.

Its defining political fact: **Orrun is many small powers living in the shadow of one fallen great one.** A continent-spanning order of rite-keepers, the **Custodian Concord**, collapsed roughly a century ago in the disaster called the **Quietfall**. Its roads, shrines, causeways, tollhouses, and broken halls thread the whole land, and nothing has replaced it. What exists now is a patchwork — leagues, city-states, theocracies, tribal confederacies, protectorates, warlord holds, and fallen realms — connected by old roads and rivers and divided by mountains, marsh, forest, and long-standing distrust.

## Coastlines And Shape

Orrun's coastline is highly varied:

- **Northwest and west:** rugged, fjorded, storm-battered cliff-coast facing the open ocean.
- **North:** a long mountain-walled edge with almost no harbors.
- **South:** broad, deltaic, river-mouthed coast holding the continent's great ports.
- **Southeast:** drier and island-fringed, opening onto a warm sea.

### Oceans And Seas

| Name | Type | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **The Pale Sea** | Ocean | West and northwest; cold, storm-battered, fish-rich |
| **The Sunder Ocean** | Ocean | North, beyond the mountain wall; storm-locked, ice in winter |
| **The Calm Reach** | Warm sea | Southeast; the heart of southern maritime trade |
| **The Hollow Gulf** | Major bay | South coast; ringed by the great southern ports |
| **The Saltmere** | Inland salt sea | South-central interior; brackish and visibly shrinking |
| **The Wracking Straits** | Strait | Far west; dangerous narrows leading to the open ocean |
| **The Stillwater** | Harbor-lake | Northwest quarter; the inland port of the city-state of Caradril |

### Major Rivers

| Name | Course |
|---|---|
| **The Glasswater** | The continent's largest river; rises in the central mountains, runs southeast to the Hollow Gulf; the main commercial artery |
| **The Verdance** | The northwest quarter's great river; runs from the interior uplands past Caradril and out to the Pale Sea |
| **The Mardenflow** | Rises in the south-central downs, floods seasonally, splays into a vast southern delta |
| **The Hethe** | The eastern trade river; drains the great eastern forest to the Calm Reach; heavily tolled |
| **The Mirewend** | Slow, peat-dark artery of the northwestern fen frontier |
| **The Ammet** | The southern farm-country river of the northwest quarter |

## Mountains, Forests, And Wastes

### Mountain Ranges

- **The Sundering Wall (the Greatspine)** — the continent-spanning central cordillera, running northwest to southeast. The single most important geographic divider in Orrun; whoever holds its passes controls interior trade.
- **The Highmark Spine** — the far-northern barrier wall that closes the continent's top. The Sunder Heights of the northwest frontier are its southern children. Its crossings are the **Highmark Passes**: cold, uninhabited, and attempted only by the desperate or the very well provisioned. No settlements, no permanent population, no help.
- **The Karran Teeth** — a jagged northeastern range separating the eastern forests from the northern ocean; mines and outlaw holds in its skirts.
- **The Emberfells** — an active volcanic highland spurring southeast off the Greatspine; ash-plains, vents, obsidian.
- **The Ghostmark Range** — low, eroded, ill-reputed mountains ringing the inland salt sea.

### Forests

- **The Hethewood** — the continent's largest forest, east-central, along the Hethe.
- **The Tollwood** — the Hethewood's far northwestern finger, in the northwest frontier's east.
- **The Sunmark Wilds** — warm, broad-canopied southern forest of sacred groves.
- **The Ashen Reach Woods** — fire-adapted volcanic woodland around the Emberfells; charcoal and rare resin.

### Badlands, Wetlands, Downs, And Steppe

- **The Cindern Waste** — ash-and-stone badland in the Emberfells' rain-shadow; lethal, and forbidden by the fire-priests who claim it.
- **The Bonepan Flats** — cracked white salt-pan badland ringing the Saltmere; mirage country.
- **The Sallow Marches** — the vast deltaic wetland of the Mardenflow, fringing the Hollow Gulf; rice, reed, and fever.
- **The Mirewend Sinks** — broad boglands south of the northwestern frontier.
- **The Greyfens** — the fog-bound marsh at the heart of the northwestern frontier.
- **The Marrowdowns** — rolling chalk downs and sheep-walks in the settled south-central uplands; ancient barrow-fields.
- **The Wender Steppe** — a wide, cold, roadless grass-sea in the north-central interior; horse-clan country.

### Notable Coastal And Unusual Terrain

- **The Skerries** — offshore sea-stacks and reefs off the northwest coast; wreck-grounds.
- **The Wracking Isles** — a storm-broken island chain in the far-western straits; wreckers and hermit-shrines.
- **The Glass Coast** — black volcanic-glass beaches and sea-stacks in the southeast; prized for obsidian.
- **The Drowned Steps** — a submerged ancient causeway-ruin running out to sea off the south coast; older than the Concord, and left alone.

## Beyond Orrun

The rest of Vael is real but distant, and to most people on Orrun it is the stuff of sailors' tales.

- **Surren** — a large, warmer, older continent far to the south and southwest, across the ocean. The half-legendary "first lands." Some southern ports keep foreign factors from Surren; almost nobody else has met one.
- **The Iron Skards** — a chain of cold volcanic islands far to the north, past the seaward end of the Highmark Spine. Storm-locked and barely known.
- **The Sundered Isles (the Far Wrack)** — scattered mid-ocean islands in the western Pale Sea between Orrun and Surren; way-stops and wreck-grounds for the boldest traders.

None of these is described in detail in this library. They exist so that the world feels finite but large.

## Scale And Travel Assumptions

Distances in Orrun are measured in **days**, not miles. Locals give directions by "three days to the ford, another two if the water's up."

| Working assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Foot travel on good road, fair weather | ~15–20 miles/day |
| Cart travel on good road | ~20–25 miles/day |
| Off-road in fen, forest, or marsh | Half that, plus real hazard |
| Corner-to-corner across Orrun | ~2–3 months by road in fair season |
| Across the northwestern frontier cluster | ~12 days corner to corner |
| River barge upstream, through locks | Slower than road, but cheaper and safer for cargo |
| Coastal shipping in fair season | Usually the fastest option where it exists |

Rules of thumb that hold nearly everywhere on Orrun:

- **The old Concord roads are the safest ground.** Raised stone causeways, a century unmaintained, still beat everything around them. They are also crumbling, and long stretches have drowned or vanished under forest.
- **Water beats road for cargo.** The Glasswater, the Verdance, and the Hethe carry most of the continent's freight, and everyone who owns a stretch of them tolls it.
- **Off-road doubles time and adds danger.** Fen, deep forest, marsh, high pass, and open steppe all punish travelers without a local guide.
- **Seasons matter.** Autumn floods, winter closes the northern passes and the Sunder Ocean, spring melt swells every river, and the southern delta's fever season runs through the wet months.
