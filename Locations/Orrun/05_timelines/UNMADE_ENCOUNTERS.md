## AI Use

Abbreviated, D&D 5e/2024-compatible adversary profiles for excursions into Near/Far Tellings and the Last Telling. Per `RULESET_ASSUMPTIONS.md`, these are **reference-style profiles** ("use a [baseline]-like creature, modified as follows"), not copied stat blocks. All are solo-tuned: single foes or small groups, telegraphed, with morale and non-combat outs. These are general-purpose and campaign-agnostic — usable in any excursion into the Unmade, not tied to any single campaign's plot.

## Solo-Play Reminders (apply to all)

- Telegraph every dangerous foe before initiative — grey light, silence, cold, or a change in the air are all fair warning signs native to the Unmade.
- Most encounters here resolve without combat if the traveler engages the creature's actual want, not just its threat.
- Never mob a solo PC without a clear warning and an escape route (a Hollow Mark, a retreat direction, an ally).

## 1. The Unkept (ambient dead of the Last Telling)

*Used in: anywhere in the Last Telling.* The ordinary population of the Last Telling — dead who never finished leaving, going through old routines. **Not inherently hostile; dangerous mainly in number or when their routine is disrupted.**

- **Type/Size:** Undead, Medium. **Role:** ambient hazard / atmosphere. **CR-equivalent:** ~1/2-2 (scales with number).
- **Baseline:** use a **Zombie-like** (slow, routine-bound) to **Ghost-like** (aware, communicative) profile depending on how "present" this particular Unkept still is.
- **AC** 10-12 · **HP** 11-22 · **Speed** 20-30 ft.
- **Saves/Skills:** low across the board; the more "present" ones have decent Wisdom (Insight).
- **Resist/Immune:** immune to exhaustion and poison; resistant to cold.
- **Senses:** passive Perception 9-11 (many are not paying much attention). **Languages:** understands Common (or the language they had in life); rarely initiates speech.
- **Actions:** *Slam* (1d6+1 bludgeoning) if provoked; most never attack at all.
- **Traits:** *Routine-bound* (will not deviate from whatever task they're fixed on unless directly interfered with); *Numb* (unaffected by fear effects; nothing left to lose).
- **Tactics:** ignore travelers unless blocked, robbed, or attacked. If disrupted, a small crowd (3+) can become genuinely dangerous through sheer numbers and confusion, not raw individual power.
- **Morale:** disperses or returns to routine the moment the disruption ends.
- **Non-combat outs:** simply not interrupting them; a respectful request (Persuasion/Insight DC 10-12) will often get one to answer a question or step aside.
- **Solo danger:** low individually; moderate in groups of 4+. **Scaling:** raise HP to 20-30 and add a *Grasping Chorus* (Con DC 12, restrained) for higher-level groups.

## 2. Hollow Hound

*Used in: anywhere in the Last Telling, especially open country.* Predator-things that hunt the warmth of the living — the Last Telling's most common combat threat, and the reason travelers there don't camp in the open.

- **Type/Size:** Undead (beast-shaped), Medium. **Role:** pack predator. **CR-equivalent:** ~2-4.
- **Baseline:** use a **Death Dog-like** or **Wolf-like** profile with cold/necrotic flavor.
- **AC** 13 · **HP** 26-39 · **Speed** 40 ft.
- **Saves/Skills:** Dex-leaning; Perception, Stealth.
- **Resist/Immune:** resistant to cold; immune to exhaustion, poison.
- **Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., can sense living warmth at long range. **Languages:** none.
- **Actions:** *Bite* (multiattack in packs, 1d8+2 piercing + 1d4 cold); *Howl* (calls 1d4 more within a minute, 1/short rest).
- **Traits:** *Warmth-Sense* (advantage tracking living creatures); *Pack Tactics*.
- **Tactics:** stalk, howl to summon the pack, then rush; avoid a target that's clearly stronger than the pack unless starving/cornered.
- **Morale:** breaks off if it loses half the pack or its prey reaches a Hollow Mark/warded space.
- **Non-combat outs:** fire and strong light keep them at bay (they won't cross a maintained flame); outrunning them to a Hollow Mark ends the chase.
- **Solo danger:** moderate-high in a full pack (4-6); low as a lone straggler. **Scaling:** L8 pack of 3-4 at listed stats; L12+ packs of 5-6 with a stronger *alpha* (add 15 HP, +1 to hit/damage).

## 3. A Keeper (template for named dead rulers)

*Used in: any settlement or region a party controls or contests in the Last Telling.* Boss-tier template for the individual dead who accumulated enough will to rule a layer, town, or road. **Every Keeper is unique — reskin this template with a specific unfinished want.**

- **Type/Size:** Undead, Medium (usually humanoid-shaped). **Role:** boss / gatekeeper. **CR-equivalent:** ~6-12 depending on scale of domain.
- **Baseline:** use a **Ghost-like** or **Bone Naga-like** profile (depending on whether the Keeper is more "person" or more "power"), heavily modified per the individual.
- **AC** 14-16 · **HP** 60-110 (scale to party level) · **Speed** 30 ft. (often flies or phases within their domain only).
- **Saves/Skills:** Wis/Cha-leaning; strong Insight and Persuasion or Intimidation depending on personality.
- **Resist/Immune:** resistant to nonmagical physical; immune to exhaustion, poison, frightened (within their own domain).
- **Senses:** truesight within their domain (they know their own layer intimately); blind to much outside it. **Languages:** whatever they spoke in life.
- **Actions:** *Domain Command* (forces minor environmental effects — doors seal, light dims, the Unkept nearby freeze in place); *Grasping Touch* (2d8 necrotic + 1 exhaustion level on a failed Con save, DC scales to level); a signature ability tied to their unfinished want (DM's design — a merchant-Keeper might *seize an item*, a soldier-Keeper might *command the Unkept as troops*).
- **Traits:** *Bound to the domain* (cannot leave it, ever); *Unfinished Want* (a specific, discoverable thing that would let them finally rest — see below).
- **Tactics:** rules first, fights second. Most Keepers test, bargain, or negotiate before escalating to combat; a Keeper who reaches combat is usually one who's already lost the negotiation.
- **Morale:** does not fight to the death by default — losing control of the domain (being exposed, outmaneuvered, or reminded forcefully enough of their unfinished want) usually ends the conflict before HP does.
- **Non-combat outs:** every Keeper should have a way to be resolved without violence — discovering and addressing their unfinished want (Insight/Investigation/History DC 15-18 to learn it; then a specific roleplay or item-based resolution). This should always be findable through play, never guessable from nothing.
- **Solo danger:** high; always telegraph before a domain-entry triggers a Keeper's attention. **Scaling:** L8 ~60-70 HP, single signature ability; L12+ ~90-110 HP, two signature abilities, can call 2-3 Unkept as reinforcements once.

## 4. A Might-Have-Been (Near Telling encounter)

*Used in: Near Tellings, rarely elsewhere.* Not a monster in the usual sense — an echo of a person who could have existed here but, in the Kept Telling, does not (a child never born, a person who died young at home, a path someone didn't take). **Not inherently dangerous; a roleplay/mystery encounter, not a combat one.**

- **Type/Size:** Humanoid or Fey-touched Humanoid, as appropriate. **Role:** roleplay/mystery encounter. **CR-equivalent:** N/A (noncombat by design; see Combat Relevance below).
- **Combat Relevance:** none by default. If forced into a fight, use a **Commoner-like** profile (AC 10, HP 4-9); Might-Have-Beens are not built to survive violence and this should feel wrong, not satisfying, to the party.
- **Traits:** *Incomplete* (fades if pressed too hard about the "wrongness" of their existence — a fair way to end the encounter if the party pushes); knows things about the Near Telling's divergence that nobody else does.
- **Tactics:** none — these creatures don't fight. They talk, avoid, or flee.
- **Solo danger:** none mechanically; the danger is entirely emotional/ethical (what does the party do with what they learn, or with the fact that this person exists here and shouldn't at home).
