## One-Sentence Identity

Vael is a world where the dead do not leave cleanly, and Orrun is a continent picking itself up a century after the order that "kept the dead at peace" destroyed itself.

## Overview

**Vael** is the world; **Orrun** is its known continent. Roughly a century ago, the **Custodian Concord** — a continent-spanning order of rite-keepers — collapsed in a single catastrophic season, the **Quietfall**. With it went roads, archives, long lives, and the great shrines that "kept the dead at peace."

Today Orrun is a patchwork of independent regions, towns, and councils, living among Concord ruins. The defining unease of the age is simple and widely felt: **death-rites sometimes fail.** Some of the dead will not rest. Pale, grieving figures — **Remembrances** — are seen near old shrines and graves in various places, and by most accounts it has been getting worse for decades. Resurrection, once solemn but reliable, has become rare and genuinely dangerous (see `MAGIC_AND_REMEMBRANCE.md`).

## World-Level Themes

- The spreading, unexplained trouble with death-rites, and the folk faiths and orders that have grown up around it.
- Frontier instability — no central authority has existed since the Quietfall.
- Old Concord ruins, roads, and archives scattered across the continent, some benign curiosities and some genuinely dangerous.
- A continent that tilts, culturally and geographically, from a cold, broken, ruin-strewn northwest to a warmer, richer, more settled south and southeast.

## The Shape of the Continent

Orrun is large — months of road end to end — and unevenly settled. A well-traveled merchant, sailor, or scholar would know its broad shape even without having been everywhere on it:

- **The northwest** is cold, wet, broken country: fens, moors, highlands, and a great deal of ruined Concord infrastructure. This is frontier country, resettled slowly since the Quietfall and still working out how to govern itself.
- **Following the rivers inland and south**, the land opens into a richer, more settled interior — great free trading river-cities built where major rivers meet, forming loose leagues and confederacies rather than single kingdoms.
- **A vast mountain range splits the continent** roughly down its middle; whoever holds its passes controls inland trade. Somewhere astride those mountains lie the broken ruins of the old Concord's heart-country — fallen, dangerous, and coveted by treasure-seekers and scholars alike.
- **To the south** lie chalk downs given over to sheep and shire-moots, a warm forest of sacred groves kept by tribal faiths, a great fever-ridden river-delta held by a distant crown's garrisons, and warm-water ports facing an open southern sea.
- **To the north and east, beyond the mountains,** lie a cold open steppe ridden by horse-clans, and a jagged, half-lawless mining-and-mercenary frontier.
- **To the southeast** stands a highland of fire-mountains where ash-priests read the volcanoes as divine, and a dying inland salt-sea ringed by salt-flats and drowned towns.
- **Overseas**, sailors tell of a great southern continent across the ocean, cold isles far to the north, and storm-broken islands far to the west. Few who go have returned to tell much more than that.

None of this detail is required to run a story confined to a single frontier region — it's the horizon the world opens onto, useful when a character wants to know what's "out there," or when a campaign wants to expand its scope.

## Using This World

This overview, and the rest of `/03_culture`, describes Orrun at the level any well-informed local or traveler would understand it — common knowledge, folk belief, and public history, with no hidden or campaign-specific truth attached. It is meant to be dropped into any story set in this world: a one-shot in a frontier village, an urban intrigue game in one of the river-cities, or a wilderness trek across the southern downs. Specific regions, settlements, and adventure sites (with their own local flavor and secrets) belong in `../01_geography/` and `../02_settlements/`; a specific campaign built on top of this world is free to add its own hidden truths without contradicting anything here.
